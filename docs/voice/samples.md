# The rival's samples: the script to read

> **Not used since 2026-11-07.** The user ruled that the rival gets a designed voice like everyone else (Kokoro-82M, no cloning,
> no recordings: `docs/plans/decisions/USER-DECISIONS-2026-11-07.md`). This page is kept, unused, in case that ever changes.

This is the script for the samples of the rival's voice (plan 26 · V3, `docs/plans/26-voice.md`). The rival, Barry
(or whatever the player names him), speaks in your voice: every one of his lines is generated by a voice-cloning engine
from these few samples. You read them once. Nothing is fine-tuned and no model of your voice is made or kept: the engine
listens to the samples each time it speaks a line.

How to record them is [`recording.md`](recording.md). The passages are also in
`PokemonPlatinumEngine/Data/voice/samples/rival/samples.json`, which the tools read; a test holds this page to that
file, so a passage changed in one is changed in the other.

**Before you start, please know**: the repository is public, and the samples will be in it (decision 5 of the plan).
Anyone can download them and, with the same free tools, make a voice that sounds like yours. That was your decision
on 2026-10-07; nothing here changes it, and nothing is uploaded anywhere you haven't chosen. None of the passages says
your name, where you live or anything about you. Don't add any.

## Who Barry is

Barry is impatient, loud and warm-hearted. He runs everywhere, threatens fines he never collects, and talks faster than
he thinks. Most of his lines are high energy; a few in the second half of the story (after the lakes, at Spear Pillar)
are quiet and serious. How old he sounds, how fast he talks and how big he goes is yours to decide: the clone speaks as
you did, so **the samples are where his character is made**.

Read each passage in its mood. The engine takes the delivery of a line from the sample of the matching mood (an excited
line clones from an excited sample, a quiet line from a quiet one), so each mood the game needs is here at least twice.

## How long

21 passages of a sentence or two, each 5 to 15 seconds. Together about two to three minutes of chosen takes, which is
twenty to thirty minutes at the microphone. Take as many tries as you like; keep the best of each.

## The passages

Each passage's file is its name with `.wav`: the first is `01_calm_morning.wav`. Read the words exactly as written
(the engine is handed them beside the sample, and a word left out or added confuses it).

### 01_calm_morning (calm)

> Hey, morning! You're up early. Mom says the lake's calm today, so I figured we'd walk out there together. No rush. Well, a little rush.

Friendly, easy, a smile in it. The pace of someone chatting at a door. The last two words a touch quicker, as if he
can't help it.

### 02_calm_professor (calm)

> Professor Rowan studies how Pokémon change as they grow. He's strict, sure, but he knows more about Sinnoh than anybody, from Twinleaf all the way north.

Explaining something he's proud to know. Even pace, clear names. Lean a little on "strict".

### 03_calm_thanks (calm)

> Thanks for waiting. Seriously. I know I'm always running ahead, but it's good to have someone to run back to.

Warm and a little shy. Slower than his usual. A real pause after "Seriously."

### 04_excited_briefcase (excited)

> Did you see that? Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup, all three, right there in the briefcase! I'm taking Piplup. No, wait. Okay, yes. Piplup!

Fast and bright, words tumbling over each other. The three names clear. The "No, wait" a real change of mind, then the
last "Piplup!" decided.

### 05_excited_road (excited)

> Come on, come on, come on! Sandgem Town is just past the tall grass, and then Jubilife City, and then Oreburgh, and then everywhere!

Impatient, bouncing on his toes. Speed up through the list of places and land big on "everywhere".

### 06_excited_badges (excited)

> First badge, second badge, third badge! I'm going to win all eight before lunch. Okay, before dinner. Before next week, tops!

Showing off, counting on his fingers. Each correction a step down in confidence but not in energy.

### 07_excited_promise (excited)

> You and me, we're going to be the strongest trainers in the whole region. Champion strong! Shake on it. Right now!

Fired up and sincere. Build to "Champion strong!" and hold a hand out on "Shake on it".

### 08_shouting_wait (shouting)

> Hey! Over here! Wait up! You can't just walk off while I'm in the middle of telling you something!

Calling across a street, not screaming into the microphone: step back a little or turn your head aside, and keep the
input where it was. Loud and offended.

### 09_shouting_fine (shouting)

> That's it, you're getting a fine! A thousand Poké Dollars! No, a million! Pay up the next time I see you!

Big, mock-furious, enjoying it. The number goes up and so does the voice. Same distance as the last one.

### 10_frustrated_lost (frustrated)

> What? I lost? How did I lose? I had a plan. It was a good plan. It just didn't work.

Stung, then sulking. Quick and sharp at first, slowing down and falling flat by the last sentence.

### 11_frustrated_blocked (frustrated)

> The road's blocked again? Seriously? Every single time I'm in a hurry, something is sitting right in the way.

Exasperated, through his teeth, almost a groan on "Seriously?". Not shouting.

### 12_quiet_father (quiet)

> My dad's a famous trainer, you know. Everyone expects me to be just like him. Sometimes I don't know if I can be.

Low and honest, nearly to himself. Slow. Let the last sentence trail off.

### 13_quiet_lakes (quiet)

> The lakes... something's wrong out there. I can feel it. Let's be careful, okay? Just this once, let's actually be careful.

Serious, uneasy, the energy held down. A real pause after "The lakes". Quiet but not whispered: keep your usual distance.

### 14_quiet_promise (quiet)

> I wasn't strong enough to stop them. I keep thinking about it. Next time, I won't freeze up. I promise.

Ashamed, then resolved. The last two words firm and slow.

### 15_laughing_bidoof (laughing)

> Ha! Ha ha! Did you see its face? The Bidoof just sat down in the middle of the battle! Best day ever!

Real laughter if you can find it: think of something funny first. The words come out through the laugh.

### 16_laughing_fountain (laughing)

> Okay, okay, I'll admit it, that was pretty funny. Me, falling into the fountain. Don't tell anyone. Ha!

Embarrassed giggling, laughing at himself. A short laugh to end on.

### 17_question_center (calm)

> Which way's the Pokémon Center? Left? Right? Is it the one with the red roof? Why is every roof in this town red?

Puzzled questions, each one rising at its end. Getting faster and more baffled toward the last.

### 18_question_pokedex (excited)

> So what did the Professor say? Did he give you a Pokédex too? Can I see it? Just for a second?

Eager questions, leaning in. Rising ends, and a wheedling "Just for a second?".

### 19_exclaiming_geodude (excited)

> Whoa! Look at the size of that Geodude! No way! Is that thing even allowed to be that big?

Amazed, eyes wide. Exclamations falling at their ends, the last question rising.

### 20_calm_bridge (calm)

> Past the foggy path, the judge's lodge sits by the azure bridge. Measure twice, cut once, they say. I usually measure zero times.

A tongue-twister he's reading off a sign, then his own joke. Careful and clear on every sound; the joke deadpan. (It is
there for the sounds of "path", "judge", "azure" and "measure", which the other passages have few of.)

### 21_calm_towns (calm)

> Twinleaf, Sandgem, Jubilife, Oreburgh, Floaroma, Eterna, Hearthome, Solaceon, Veilstone, Pastoria, Celestic, Canalave, Snowpoint, Sunyshore. I'm going to see every one.

A list read off a map, each name clear and separate, as the pronunciation guide says. Proud on the last sentence.

## Afterwards

When every passage has a take, run `dotnet run --project tools/VoiceTool -- check-samples` (what it checks is in
`recording.md`). Then the cloning trial of plan 26 · V3 can begin: twenty of Barry's lines generated by each engine you
approve, for you to choose by ear.
