# Plan 08 · Platinum's remaining details

Written 2026-10-06, before any session.

**Goal**: the pieces of Platinum itself that no plan names: its battle options and a quicker battle, the six pages of a Pokémon's summary, the level-up panel, the overlays' trainers read from the table plan 06 · R9 imported instead of typed by hand, Jubilife TV's programmes and the furniture that talks, the Vs. Recorder, Spiritomb's tower without the Underground's greetings, Pokédex entries written in our own words instead of generated data sheets, the Pokédex's CRY and FORMS pages with Platinum's record of the forms and genders seen, Pal Park's migration from the player's own saves, and the dual-slot encounters with a stand-in for the GBA cartridge.

## Where we are

- **Options**: `OptionRow` (`UI/OptionsScreen.cs`) has twelve rows (`TextSpeed`, `Quality`, `WindowSize`, `Fullscreen`, `VSync`, `TimeOfDay`, `Sound`, four volumes, `Speakers`), kept by `GameSettings` in `settings.json`; `SpeakerMode` is `Stereo` or `Handheld`. None of Platinum's Battle Scene, Battle Style or Sound Mono rows exist. The pace of a battle is fixed: `BattleAnimator.AttackTime` 0.65 s, `EffectTime` 1.3 s, `ImpactTime` 0.35 s, `BattleEngine.HitDelay` 0.35 s, and every line waits for a key (`waitingForMessageConfirm`); the text speed is the only control. `GameEngine.Update` hands `dt` unchanged to `battle.Update`, `UpdateDialogue`, `evolutionScreen.Update` and `UpdateTransition`.
- **The foe's next Pokémon comes in unasked**: `BattleCore.ReplaceFainted` (`Battle/Sim/BattleCore.Switch.cs`) sends the foes' from their roster first and yields a `ReplacementRequest` only for the player's empty places. `BattleRequest` has two kinds (`ActionRequest`, `ReplacementRequest`); `BattleRecord(Seed, Answers)` is every answer given from outside, and `BattleCore.Replay` plays one back.
- **Summary**: one page, `ModernUi.DrawSummary` (`UI/ModernUi.Party.cs`), reached through `PartyScreen.ShowSummary` (style guide, "Summary"), with plan 06 · R10's PKRS tag. `Pokemon` carries `Nickname`, `Friendship`, `Beauty`, `Personality`, `Ball`, `EvolutionProgress`, and since R10 (done 2026-10-06) its effort values, `Pokerus` and `OriginalTrainer` (a `TrainerMark` of name, ID and look; null for the player's own, caught, hatched or given, and for every Pokémon of a save older than R10), but no met place, level or date, and no markings; `SavedPokemonData` the same. Plan 07 · O2 owes the met data and an id for trading, and hasn't started. `PCScreen` and the battle's switch panel open no summary.
- **A level**: `BattleCore.cs` emits `ExpGained` and `LevelRose(Pokemon, Level)`; `BattleEngine.Show` levels the shown copy, plays `MusicRole.FanfareLevelUp` and remembers who grew (`LeveledUp`); `BagScreen` levels with a Rare Candy through `GainExp`. Nothing shows the six stats and their gains. Since plan 06 · R10 a level that brings a move to a Pokémon that knows four asks which to forget, inside the battle (the log's `MoveWanted`, `Battle/BattleEngine.Learning.cs`).
- **Trainers**: plan 06 · R9 (done 2026-10-06, as this plan was written) imported Platinum's trainer table. `tools/DataImporter`'s `Importer.Trainers.cs` reads `/res/trainers/data/` of the pinned commit, with the classes' prize multipliers and genders (all in `Sources.DecompFolders`), and writes `Data/trainers.json`: a `TrainerRecord` per trainer (`Data/TrainerDatabase.cs`: class, name, AI flags by name, battle items, the double flag, prize money) with a `TrainerPokemonRecord` per Pokémon (species, form, level, IV scale, personality, item, chosen moves); `docs/data-files.md` describes it. `tools/MapImporter/WorldWriter.cs` writes an object's trainer type, sight and script name (`AreaObject.Trainer`, `Sight`, `Script`: `TRAINER_YOUNGSTER_TRISTAN`), and `WorldMapBuilder.PlaceEvents` (`Data/World.cs`) fills the placed `Trainer` from the table by that script (`TrainerDatabase.Fill`): its `Ai` and `Items` always, and its team built as the original's `TrainerData_BuildParty` builds it (`TrainerDatabase.Build`: IVs from the scale, nature, gender and ability from the personality, the held item) where the overlay names the same team or none. `TrainerAi` thinks with `Trainer.Ai` and uses `Trainer.Items`; `TrainerAiTests` holds every trainer of Platinum buildable and a map's trainer taking its team and mind. What is left: all thirty overlays with trainers (the fourteen plan 01 · M7 wrote among them) repeat the table's class and name in their `trainer` blocks (`MapFile.TrainerRecord`) beside the lines, and eight of them (the south-west's, written before R9) type a whole team by hand as well (`party`, `prizeMoney`), a second copy of what the table has. Those eight also give their 27 trainers ids of their own (`trainer_tristan`, `trainer_liv_and_liz`) where the table and the later overlays use the original's (`youngster_tristan`); a save keeps a beaten trainer by that id (`StoryState.DefeatedTrainers`; `docs/data-files.md`: never change it for a trainer already in the game). `tools/DataImporter/README.md` doesn't mention the table.
- **Furniture**: `TileBehavior` has `Television`, `TownMap`, `Bookshelf` (three sizes) and `TrashCan`, and the hand-made rooms' TVs are props (`PropType.Television`; `"type": "Television"` in `Data/maps/PlayerHouse.json` and `PlayerHouse2F.json`). `GameEngine.TryInteract` reaches a person, a counter (`Map.IsCounter`), a signboard, a hidden item, then what the tile ahead is (the original's `Field_TileBehaviorToScript`: a waterfall, a rock face, water, through `FieldScripts.Waterfall`, `RockFace` and `Water`); nothing answers a TV, a shelf or a bin unless a signboard stands on its tile. Plan 02 · S4 (done) plays the opening's television special as the bedroom's `OnEnter` (`Data/scripts/PlayerHouse2F.txt`, spoken by "TV") and gives the bedroom's TV and computer signboards of their own (`PlayerHouse2F.json`); the living room's TV (`PlayerHouse.json`) and the rival's shelves (`PropType.Bookshelf` in `RivalHouse.json`) have none, and no hand-made room has a bin. Jubilife TV stands without a room (plan 01 · M4; its room is M11). `UI/Kit/NameEntry.cs` is the one GPU-free picker, used for the player's name and, since S4, the rival's.
- **Vs. Recorder**: `items.json` has it (id 465, `KeyItems`, `fieldUse` `VsRecorder`, `canBeRegistered`); `BattleCore.Record` and `Replay` work and `BattleCoreTests.ARecordedBattleReplaysTheSame` holds them; nothing writes a record to disk or shows one, and `GameEngine.EndBattle` writes nothing out (it plays evolutions, wakes the player at the last Pokémon Center after a loss since plan 06 · R10, trims models and fades). Plan 07 keeps replays on its server for bug reports and leaves the wire format (events name Pokémon by reference) to O3. Looker stands in Jubilife with stand-in lines (`overlays/jubilife_city.json`); plan 02 · S5 writes his scene.
- **Spiritomb**: `Data/WorldModels.cs` knows `r209s02` as `PropType.Cairn`, "the Hallowed Tower"; Route 209 is open since plan 01 · M6 (`overlays/route_209.json`), the tower standing on it as a prop; the original's way to it is four tile events of type 0 round the tower in `areas/route_209.json` (script `2`, `AreaSign`), which nothing reads yet: `WorldMapBuilder.PlaceEvents` reads only the hidden items (type 2), and plan 01 · M6 left the type-0 events, the Unown inscriptions' too, to plan 02 · S7; `habitats.json` lists no Spiritomb; the Odd Keystone is in `items.json`. Plan 06 rules the Underground's multiplayer out (its decision 7) and R16 builds the Underground for one player; plan 03 · D12's completeness test isn't written yet.
- **Entries**: `PokemonSpecies.DexEntry` is one sentence `Importer.DexEntry` composes from the data (name, category, types, region, height, weight, the line's evolutions); the Pokédex's INFO page (plan 03 · D10, `UI/ModernUi.Pokedex.cs`) shows it. Plan 03 · decision 1 generated entries so that no game text is used; there is no habitat word (`habitats.json` says where a species is met, for the AREA page, not Platinum's word for the kind of place it lives in).
- **The Pokédex's pages**: an entry has three (`PokedexPage { Info, Area, Size }`, the static `PokedexScreen.Pages`, `Sideways`), drawn by `ModernUi.Pokedex.cs` (`DexInfo`, `DexArea`, `DexSize`); `MenuScreenTests.APokedexEntryHasThreePagesAndStepsThroughTheSpeciesSeen` holds the count. - **Pal Park**: plan 06 · R9 built the catching show (`Overworld/CatchingShow.cs`, made from any six Pokémon), `BattleKind.PalPark` and the species' `PalParkData`; Route 221 is open since plan 01 · M7, and its two warps into `pal_park_lobby` lead nowhere. ## Design

**Platinum's defaults don't move.** Battle Scene on and Battle Style Shift, as the original ships; the speed-up, auto-advance, text frames and autorun are our own rows beside them, off unless chosen, and so is P10's offer to record a story battle. P19's FORMS page waits for Platinum's upgrade, P21's MIGRATE shows only when its folder is there, and P23's second slot holds nothing unless the player puts a game in it. Everything here keeps two harness runs drawing the same pictures: nothing new rolls outside `Dice` or the battle's generator, and nothing reads a clock but `FrameClock` and the game's own (`GameClock.Today`, which the harness fixes through `GameClock.FixedDate`).

### Options and pace

- **Rows** (`OptionRow`, `GameSettings`): `BattleScene` (on/off), `BattleStyle` (`BattleStyle.Shift`/`Set`, a new enum), `Speakers` gains `SpeakerMode.Mono` (the mixer folds its two channels in `AudioMixer.Render`), `Frame` (the text box's frame, our own six), `SpeedUp` (off, ×2, ×3, used while Run is held), `AutoAdvance` (off, slow, fast), `Autorun` (on/off). All in `settings.json`; `OptionsScreen.Describe` gets a line for each and `GameEngine.ApplySettings` pushes them. Button Mode goes with key rebinding, which plan 12 · Q4 owns as presets of its bindings; the Rumble Pak has no place here.
- **Battle Scene off** is a mode of the face, never of the rules: `BattleEngine.Show` skips `Anim.Attack` on `Lunged` and makes no `EffectCue` on `MoveShown` (the move's sound still plays), and what a line carries in `OnImpact` lands at once instead of `HitDelay` later, through the same `pendingEffects` so damage still lands in the log's order. The ball's throw and the send-outs stay, as in Platinum. The core and the log don't change, so a record replays the same with the scene on or off. The style guide's "Move effects (G8)" gets a line for the scene off before the code.
- **Battle Style Shift** is a question the rules ask: a new `SwitchOfferRequest(Place, Pokemon upcoming)` beside `ActionRequest` and `ReplacementRequest`, yielded by `ReplaceFainted` after the line "<trainer> is about to send in <X>. Will you switch?" (a `Said`, so the harness can show it) and before the foe's `Entered`, only in a single trainer battle, only when the player's Pokémon is standing and `CanSendIn` finds anyone, and only when `CoreSetup.OffersSwitch` is set. It is answered from outside with a new `BattleChoice.Stay(place)` (`BattleChoice.GoOn` is taken: the core's own answer for a Pokémon held to its move) or `BattleChoice.Switch(place, index)`, checked by `WhyNot`, and so lands in `BattleRecord.Answers`: a replay holds. `GameEngine` passes the setting to the battle through `BattleSetup` (a new `OffersSwitch`, off unless given; Set never asks), `BattleEngine` hands it to `CoreSetup`, opens the switch panel on the request (`BattleEngine.Menus.cs`), and a cancel answers Stay. The core's default is not to ask, so `CoreScenario` and every test that builds a `BattleEngine` itself keep the turns they have. The style guide's "Battle panels" says how the offer opens the switch panel before the code.
- **The speed-up** multiplies the `dt` the engine hands to the Battle, Dialogue, Evolution and Transition states while Run is held (`GameEngine.SpeedUp`, a property the key sets and the harness sets directly). Presentation is a function of time (the harness's `Skip` proves it) and cue seeds come from the animator, so no roll changes. Dialogue runs at `CharactersPerSecond` times the same factor.
- **Auto-advance** gives `waitingForMessageConfirm` a timer once the line is whole (`BattleEngine.AutoAdvanceAfter`, null when off), and `UpdateDialogue` the same for plain lines; a question (`DialogueManager.IsQuestion`) always waits.
- **Autorun**: `Player.Update` reads Run as "walk" when `Player.Autorun` is set; `Advance` is unchanged, so tests and the harness walk as before. Running needs the Running Shoes since plan 02 · S4 (`FLAG_HAS_RUNNING_SHOES`, `Player.HasRunningShoes`), and autorun obeys the same gate.
- **Frames**: `ModernUi.DrawDialogue` takes the frame's number; each frame is drawn with `UiShapes` (a double rule, a wooden edge, a stone edge, a dark glass, a ribbon), never pixel art from the games. The style guide's "Field menus and notices" gets a text-box paragraph with the six before the code.

### The summary

- **Pages** (`SummaryPage`: `Info`, `Memo`, `Skills`, `BattleMoves`, `ContestMoves`, `Ribbons`) live in a GPU-free `SummaryView` (`UI/SummaryView.cs`: `Open(party, index)`, `Move`, `Sideways`, `Confirm`, `Cancel`, a cursor over the moves on the move pages) painted by `ModernUi.Party` painters, one per page. `PartyScreen` keeps `ShowSummary` and owns a view; `PCScreen` and the battle's switch panel (`BattleMenuState.SwitchPokemon`) open the same view on the Pokémon under the cursor. Left and right turn pages, as the Pokédex's do (there are no shoulder buttons). The style guide's "Summary" is rewritten for six pages first.
- **The memo's fields** go on `Pokemon` and `SavedPokemonData` beside plan 06 · R10's `OriginalTrainer` (null for the player's own, whose memo shows the player's name and ID; the mark's name and ID otherwise): `MetLocation` (the area's `DisplayNameAt`), `MetLevel`, `MetDate` (the day, from `GameClock.Today`, which R10 added and the harness fixes to the first of June through `GameClock.FixedDate` since plan 01 · M7, so the memo's shot is the same every run), `Markings` (Platinum's six, a bit each; the summary shows and sets them, and the box's own marking menu is plan 06 · R12's, whose "Tools" row has PC storage with markings). They are set where a Pokémon becomes the player's (`BattleEngine.Show`'s `Caught`, `FieldHost.GivePokemon`, the starter's `GameState.StarterSelect`) and copied by `Pokemon.CopyStateFrom`. A Pokémon from an older save is the player's own already (R10's null mark), met at an unknown place at its current level: that is the one migration, in `SavedPokemonData.ToPokemon`. If plan 07 · O2 hasn't run, P3 takes these fields over from it; O2 then adds `Id`, `SecretId` and `IsTraded` on top (R10 brought the EVs), and `ReceiveTradedPokemon` keeps the giver's memo.
- **The characteristic** is a new `Characteristics.Of(pokemon)` (`Models/Characteristics.cs`; plan 06's "Growth" row lists characteristics, which R10 (done 2026-10-06) didn't write and nothing in battle reads, so they are written here, where they are shown): the stat of the highest IV, ties broken from the personality value, the line by that IV's remainder by five, as the original decides; the thirty lines are ours. The nature's line and the ability's text come from the data.
- **Contest Moves and Ribbons** are frames with "nothing yet" until plan 06 · R17 fills them.

### Recorded battles

- **The file** (`Battle/Sim/BattleRecordFile.cs`, GPU-free): the `BattleRecord` with everything the core needs to replay it, as plain data: both parties as `SavedPokemonData`, the trainers as `TrainerRecord`, the format, the rules preset, the `BattleConditions`, the player's name, the day (`GameClock.Today`), and a data version (a new `GameDataFiles.Version`, a hash of the five data files, which plan 07's rooms will use too). ### Written entries

- **The file** is `Data/dex-entries.json`, read by `DexEntries` (`Data/DexEntries.cs`): for each species and form name, two entries and a habitat word from Platinum's nine (grassland, forest, water's edge, sea, cave, mountain, rough terrain, urban, rare). The importer never writes it; `PokemonSpecies.DexEntry` stays as the fallback for a name the file lacks. The INFO page shows a written entry, the two in turn on each opening, and the habitat word beside the height and weight (the style guide's "Menu screens", its Pokédex entry's INFO page, first).
- **Our own words.** Plan 03 · decision 1 keeps its purpose (no game text) and changes its means: entries written by us in the manner of a Pokédex, never a close paraphrase of Platinum's or PokeAPI's flavour text. `DataFileTests` holds the shape: two entries for every species and form, 15 to 45 words each, none alike, none the generated sentence, every habitat word from the list. The words themselves are checked by the importer, whose git-ignored cache holds PokeAPI's flavour texts (`pokemon_species_flavor_text.csv`, in the CSV folder it checks out already, Platinum's own entries among them): `--check-entries` reports any run of six words shared with any of them, and the session rewrites those. It can't be a unit test, because the original's text must never enter the repository.

### The Pokédex's cry and forms pages

- **Five pages in Platinum's order.** `PokedexPage` becomes `Info, Area, Cry, Size, Forms`, and the pages an entry offers are asked of the screen (`PokedexScreen.PagesOf(entry)`, in place of the static `Pages`): CRY for every species seen, FORMS only once the Pokédex can show forms. - **The cry page** keeps Platinum's controls in our own layout: the cry's wave as a scope across the top of the entry (a GPU-free `Cries.Scope(samples, playhead, columns)`, the low and the high of each column), and under it rows like the options': PITCH in semitones (over the original's range, read from `crysub.c`), CHORUS or PAN, REVERB or FILTER (A on either row switches its mode, as the original's switch and dial do), and LOOP. LOOP plays it again as it ends, as `crysub.c`'s `LoopCry` does, until the page is left. The scope follows the screen's own playhead, advanced by `Update(dt)`, never the audio thread's position, so a shot is the same every run; it is a few hundred `UiShapes` segments and costs the frame nothing measurable.
- **The looks seen.** A look is a Pokémon's `ModelName` and its gender (a new `SeenLook(string Name, Gender Gender)`). `Pokedex` keeps them per species in the order first seen (`LooksOf(species)`), filled by new `RegisterSeen(Pokemon)` and `RegisterCaught(Pokemon)` (the number overloads stay for a save's species lists), and keeps the first Spinda's personality, as the original records them all from the first sighting whatever the Pokédex can show yet; it records every form of every species, not only the nine species whose forms Platinum records. ### Pal Park's migration

Whether to do it at all is decision 7: it reopens plan 03 · decision 4.

- **What is read, and what is never kept.** The game reads files the player puts in a `migrate/` folder beside the saves (`SavePaths.Folder` once plan 12 · Q2 lands, the working directory before it; git-ignored): a GBA save of Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed or LeafGreen (an emulator's or a dumped cartridge's 128 KB, with or without the bytes some emulators add), a Platinum save (512 KB, likewise), PKHeX's `.pk3`, `.ek3`, `.pk4` and `.ek4`, and from P22 another save of this game. A foreign file is opened read-only, read whole into memory, and never written, renamed or copied. Of a save only what tells which game wrote it, the trainer's name and ID, and the boxes are read; the bag, the map, the story and the game's text are skipped. Of each Pokémon chosen only what `SavedPokemonData` holds is kept (species, form, personality, the original trainer's name, ID and gender, the nickname, EXP, IVs, EVs, moves, item, ability, ball, Pokérus, and P3's markings and met level once they exist), and the identity remembered against a second coming is its personality and its trainer's ID; what this game has no field for (ribbons until plan 06 · R17, contest stats but beauty, the language, the game of origin) is dropped and named in the rulings. Diamond and Pearl, HeartGold and SoulSilver lay their saves out otherwise and wait for pins of their decompilations (plan 20 · J1 pins HeartGold's); their Pokémon come through `.pk4` until then. `.pk5` is left out: no pinned source describes it.
- **Numbers, never art.** The tables the conversion needs (the GBA's species and item numbers, `sGBAToDSSpecies` and `Item_FromGBAID`'s; the GBA's and the DS's character tables, `GBA_ConvertStringToDS`'s and `tools/msgenc/charmap.txt`; the HM moves and the GBA items Platinum refuses, `sGBAHMMoves` and `sInvalidGBAItems`) are imported by `tools/DataImporter` from the pinned pokeplatinum commit into a new `Data/migration.json`: numbers and letters, like the move and item tables already imported, never a sprite, an icon, a sound or a line of the games, and no second repository is pinned for them. The readers are written from the decompilation's C (`src/main_menu/`, `src/pokemon.c`, `src/savedata.c`), never from PKHeX's code, which is GPL. No save, no `.pk*` file and no part of one enters the repository: tests build their files byte by byte, as plan 18 · W6's tests build a `map.bin`.
- **Platinum's rules** (`Models/Migration.cs`, GPU-free, from `gba_migrator.c`): only with the National Pokédex; at least six Pokémon in the source's boxes; none while the last six still wait for the show (`CANNOT_MIGRATE_ALREADY_STOCKED`); a source once in 24 hours by its trainer's ID, twenty remembered and the oldest forgotten (`pal_park_transfers.c`); no egg, nothing that knows an HM (Cut, Fly, Surf, Strength, Flash, Rock Smash, Waterfall, Dive), nothing holding one of the GBA's key items or HMs, and only the GBA's real species (`IsGBASpeciesInvalid`). Ours beside them: none that came before, and none the legality check refuses. The console and clock checks (`CANNOT_MIGRATE_DIFFERENT_CONSOLE`, `INTERNAL_CLOCK_ALTERED`) have nothing to stand for and are left out, in the rulings. The time is an argument (the engine's clock, or plan 15 · E3's `WallClock` once it lands), so tests and the harness hand it in. A folder of `.pk*` files counts as one source.
- **The conversion** is the original's `BoxMonGBAToBoxMon`: friendship back to the base value of 70, the ability by the GBA's bit (a species that gained one in Generation 4 takes its first, `sSpeciesWithNewAbilities`), the item by `Item_FromGBAID`, Unown's letter from the personality, Deoxys's forme by the game it left. Nature, gender and shininess follow the personality, as Platinum's do. The original trainer is kept (`TrainerMark`), so R10's obedience and boosted EXP apply to a migrated Pokémon, as in Platinum.
- **Where they land.** Platinum's migration is a menu entry before the game starts, so ours is MIGRATE on the title's menu (a new `TitleChoice.Migrate`, `GameState.Migrate`), shown only when the `migrate/` folder holds a file the readers know, as Platinum's shows only with a cartridge in the slot: a game without the folder has the title it has today. Its screen (`MigrateScreen`, its logic GPU-free; painted in `ModernUi.Storage.cs`) lists the files, then a source's boxes as the PC draws them, each refused Pokémon greyed with its reason; six are chosen, a question confirms, and they are written into the CONTINUE save's `SaveData.PalPark` (new: the six waiting, the twenty sources with their times, the identities that came). They are caught again in Pal Park's show (R9's `CatchingShow`, made from them) once plan 06 · R16 makes the park a place, and go to the boxes met at Pal Park.
- **The legality check** is plan 07 · O5's "no impossible Pokémon" (the species in the data, EXP that fits its level, IVs and EVs in bounds, a nature, gender and ability it can have, moves its data can teach it, real PP and item): a GPU-free `Models/Legality.cs` written once by whichever of P21, plan 07 · O5 and plan 14 · B11 (whose team check plan 13 · V13 shares) lands first. Until plan 06 · R11 and R15 bring TM, tutor and egg learnsets into the data, a move outside the level-up learnset is let through, and the rulings say so.
- **Between this game's saves** (P22): another save of this game (one of plan 12 · Q2's slots, or a save in `migrate/`) is read as `SaveData` without loading it into the engine (a new `SaveManager.Read(path)`), and its six leave it: the receiving save is written first with them and a receipt naming them by identity with the source's file and checksum, then the source without them and the same receipt, then the receipt is closed; a save that loads with an open receipt finishes it, so a crash at any step leaves each Pokémon in exactly one save. It stands on plan 12 · Q1's write-and-move, with plan 07's crash-safe receipts as the model.
- **The way out** (P22): a party member or a boxed Pokémon written as a `.pk4` (Platinum's 136 bytes, decrypted, as PKHeX reads them) or as Pokémon Showdown's text (the third pinned source, MIT) into `migrate/out/`, as a copy: nothing leaves the save. It waits for plan 06 · R15, which makes nature, gender and shininess follow the personality value as Platinum's do; before it, a written `.pk4` could read as another nature in another program.

## Risks

- **The Shift prompt changes the shape of every trainer battle**: the game's and the harness's trainer battles gain a question. P1 keeps the core's and `BattleSetup`'s default silent and turns the offer on from the settings alone, so tests that drive `BattleEngine` keep their turns, and runs `all` before and after.
- **Two owners of one save field**: the memo's fields are O2's as well. Whichever session runs first adds them; the other reads them. The migration is written once.
- **Beaten trainers forgotten**: a save keeps a beaten trainer by the overlay's id, and P7 changes 27 of them. The step in `StoryMigration.Upgrade` renames them in an older save, and P7's test loads one.
- **Broadcasts without a calendar**: the lottery and the weekday programmes wait for R14's daily events; the day itself is `GameClock.Today`, which the harness fixes through `GameClock.FixedDate`. P8 writes the programmes that need no daily event.
- **A replay goes stale** with every importer run that changes a move or a species: the data version refuses it with a notice rather than playing it wrong.
- **Three thousand entries are slow to write**, and the overlap check needs the importer's cache: each batch is one session, and the check is run before the batch is called done.
- **The player's own files**: a reader that wrote back could cost someone a save kept for twenty years. Every reader opens its file read-only, nothing in the game writes into `migrate/` but P22's `out/`, and P20's tests hold a file's bytes unchanged after it is read.
- **The same Pokémon twice**: the original's cartridge lost the six it sent, and the files here stay as they are. The identities in `SaveData.PalPark` refuse a second coming, and between this game's own saves the receipts move rather than copy.
- **The second slot changes Platinum's grass**: only with the National Pokédex and a game chosen, None by default, and `diff` of `all` before and after P23.
- **`Seen` gains two fields** that every reader of the log sees: they have defaults, so the tests that build the event keep their shape.

## Decisions for the user

1. **Where recorded battles are opened from.** *Recommended:* USE on the Vs. Recorder in the bag, as in the original. The alternative is a REPLAYS entry on the title screen as well, which costs a `TitleChoice` and its tests.
2. **Spiritomb's thirty-two greetings.** *Recommended:* thirty-two different people spoken to anywhere in Sinnoh, counted once each. The alternative is plan 06 · R16's solo Underground diggers, which makes P12 wait for R16.
3. **Which forms get entries.** *Recommended:* all 541, two each, so P19's FORMS page can show them. The alternative is only the forms that are a Pokédex entry of their own (the regional forms, Rotom's appliances, the cloaks, the seas), about 60, with Megas and Gigantamax showing their species' entries.
4. **The speed-up's key.** *Recommended:* held Run, which a battle doesn't read today (it shares X and the pad's B with Cancel, whose press only reads a line on there), with the factor an options row. The alternative is a toggle row that keeps it on, which also speeds the field's text.
5. **Jubilife TV's programmes.** *Recommended:* the five P8 names (the Pokédex, the trainer survey, the clock, the interviews, the lottery's stand-in). The alternative is the interviews alone.
6. **When the FORMS page appears.** *Recommended:* as in Platinum, once Professor Rowan's aide upgrades the Pokédex on the way into Canalave City (`FLAG_POKEDEX_FORM_DETECTION`), with the looks recorded from the first sighting so nothing seen before is lost; until the gate has its room (plan 01 · M11), Canalave City's first arrival plays his lines. The alternative is the page from the moment the Pokédex is had, which leaves Platinum's upgrade out.
7. **Whether to bring Pokémon in from the player's own saves at all** (P20 to P22). It reopens plan 03 · decision 4 ("GBA migration doesn't exist here") and reads files the user owns, keeping only the numbers that make each chosen Pokémon (design, "What is read, and what is never kept"). *Recommended:* yes, as Platinum's migration: GBA saves, Platinum saves and PKHeX's `.pk3` and `.pk4` files dropped in `migrate/`, read and never written, six at a time after the National Pokédex into the stock of Pal Park's show, with plan 03 · decision 4's habitats kept for players with no files. The alternatives: (b) not at all, the show taking its six from plan 03 · D12's habitats; (c) only P22, moves between this game's own saves and the way out, with no foreign file read. Within (a), where the six land is the show (recommended, once plan 06 · R16 makes the park a place) or straight into the boxes as a gift, which needs no park.
8. **Sources played under other rules.** *Recommended:* a save of this game played under the modern rules or a randomiser (plan 13 · V6) may send Pokémon to another save only if each passes the legality check under the receiving save's data (a randomised species' moves often won't), and foreign files are checked the same way. The alternative refuses any source whose `RulesPreset` or `Run` differs from the receiving save's.
9. **What stands in for the cartridge in slot two.** *Recommended:* an options row, SLOT TWO (None, Ruby, Sapphire, Emerald, FireRed, LeafGreen; None by default), since a cartridge is the player's to put in at any start, read only once the National Pokédex is had, as in Platinum. The alternatives: (b) only the games of the regions whose Hall of Fame this save's chain has (Kanto's FireRed or LeafGreen, Hoenn's three), which offers nothing until plans 19 and 21 build those regions; (c) the player's own GBA save in `migrate/`, its game read by P20's reader, which needs a file most players don't have.
