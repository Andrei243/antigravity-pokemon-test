# What else to add, now that every generation is in

*Written 2026-11-07 at the user's request: now that the game has all the generations, what should also be added, with a new plan for each category. It led to plans 35 to 44.*

## The short answer

Almost everything the main series added is in the game already, or is held by an open plan (above all the later regions' plans 23, 33 and 34, which carry each region's own systems). A sweep listed 254 features of every generation and of the notable spin-offs; once each was checked in the code, what is left falls into ten categories, and each is now a plan:

**The cheapest and most valuable work is not a new feature at all**: it is what is built and can't be reached, or what a finished region was left without. The bag uses mints, Bottle Caps, Exp. Candies and the Ability Capsule and Patch, and nothing in the game sells or gives one; the Shiny Charm's rule is built and nobody hands it over; 103 of the 104 Mega Stones and most of Unova's form items are given by nothing; about 27 of the later moves can be taught to no Pokémon at all; a catch gives no EXP under the modern rules; Unova has no Town Map, so Fly can't be used there or to it; three finished regions (Kanto, Johto, Unova) never had the test that every species of their games can be had there, and Kanto and Johto have none of their in-game trades. Those are plan 36 · TR3 and TR9, plan 38 · EX10, and plan 40 · GE1 to GE3, GE5 and GE11, a handful of sessions in all, and they come first in the recommended order below.

Every plan takes the later games' mechanics and makes the content our own: our places, people, names, words, art and music, as CLAUDE.md's "Nothing taken from the games" asks. Where the game already has a character (Looker, the Leaders and Champions, the villain teams' leaders), a plan uses them as they stand in the game, with stories of ours.

## How it was found

1. **The sweep.** Nine agents each took a family of games (Generations 1 and 2 with their remakes and Let's Go; 3 with Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire and Colosseum; 4 with Brilliant Diamond and Shining Pearl; 5; 6; 7; 8 with Legends: Arceus; 9 with Legends: Z-A; and the whole game across its regions) and listed what those games added that a player would notice, with each one's notable spin-offs. They found 254 candidates and judged 160 partial or missing, by quick searches of the code and the plans.
2. **The writers.** One agent per category re-checked every candidate by opening the files, chose what is worth building, designed it on the code as it stands and wrote the plan. Many of the sweep's statuses were wrong both ways ("Where the sweep was wrong", below).
3. **The review.** Five agents held the plans to the code, to the project's rules and to each other, two related plans each, and fixed them in place (doors two plans had both claimed, names already in use, facts that were wrong); a sixth, the completeness critic, looked for anything a player would miss that no plan, built or new, covers, and its findings were folded into the plans ("Found after the plans were written", below).

## Where the sweep was wrong

What the writers found by opening the files, most of it the reason a plan's session exists:

- **Built and unreachable.** Mints, Exp. Candies, Bottle Caps and the Ability Capsule and Patch work from the bag (`Models/LaterItemUse.cs`) and are in no Mart, prize list or exchange; Hyper Training's rule (`Models/HyperTraining.cs`) is called by nothing. The Shiny Charm's rule (`Models/ShinyCharm.cs`) is built and no script gives it. No Tera Shard is in the data and nothing changes a Tera Type. (Plans 36 and 40.)
- **Owed by finished regions.** The test that every species of a region's games can be had there exists for Sinnoh and Hoenn only; Kanto, Johto and Unova finished without it. Kanto's and Johto's scripts have no in-game trade, and the GBA reader reads none. Diplomas have words only for Sinnoh, Kanto, the National Pokédex and the research certificate. (Plan 40.)
- **Rematches.** FireRed's Kanto Leaders and Unova's Leaders battle once; Johto's Elite Four have second teams in the data that no script fights. (Plan 41 · EG5.)
- **The Hall of Fame** keeps the last thirty teams of every region in one list with no region, and every region hands Sinnoh's Champion Ribbon. (Plan 41 · EG2.)
- **Swarms** exist in Sinnoh and Johto only, and effort values are shown on no screen. (Plans 38 and 36.)
- **Rooms built with nothing to play**: the Musical theatre's lobby, Pokéstar Studios' gate, Nimbasa's Battle Institute and the two link-game rooms of Two Island and Mossdeep. (Plans 42 and 37.)
- **The modern Day Care** hands down no hidden ability. (Plan 36 · TR6.)
- **More was built than the sweep thought**: HeartGold's Trainer House, the Battle Royal format, the whole Pokéathlon, Johto's photographer and his album, Join Avenue's salon, Lumiose's boutique, Emerald's Mirage Island and Buena's Password.

### Found after the plans were written

The completeness critic, looking for anything a player would miss that no plan covered, found four gaps of high value, each checked in the code, and folded into the plans:

- **A catch gives no EXP** under the modern rules, where every game since X and Y gives it as a faint does (the throw ends the battle without awarding any). Plan 40 · GE5, beside the critical captures.
- **Unova has no Town Map**: no `townmap.json`, so its Town Map shows nothing, Fly has no Unova tab, no region can fly to Unova, and an Unova listing's AREA page draws no map. Plan 38 · EX10, first of that plan.
- **Items the rules run that nothing gives**: 103 of the 104 Mega Stones (the Tower of Mastery's Lucarionite is the one given), the 15 stones of our own Megas, the species Z-Crystals, Unova's DNA Splicers and Reveal Glass, most of the Drives (the Shock Drive is given), and other form items. Plan 40 · GE11 widens plan 36 · TR3's test of sources to every item the rules run, places what the finished regions owe, and names what each later region's plan owes.
- **Moves no Pokémon can learn**: species learn the newest level-up moves, but their machines are Platinum's TMs and Sword and Shield's base TRs and their tutors Platinum's, so about 27 later moves (Tera Blast among them) are taught by nothing, and Hydrapple can't be had. Plan 36 · TR9.

And smaller ones, placed the same way: Unova's Day Care takes no Pokémon (36 · TR6); Unova's shaking grass, dust clouds, rippling water and bridge shadows are imported and never met (38 · EX11, on plan 10 · F12's rustle); chain fishing and the Repel prompt (38 · EX3); fossils can't be revived in Unova or in HeartGold's Kanto (40 · GE3); Johto's Berry Pots (39 · CR5); the later regions' Champion ribbons (41 · EG2); Johto's lucky number draw on the radio (42 · PS3); Mom's savings in Johto (43 · LF7).

## Considered and left out

- **Battles in real time** (Legends: Z-A). Plan 23 builds Lumiose's era on the turn-based battle. A real-time battle is a second battle engine (each action's rules on a clock, cooldowns for turns, the battle's face drawn anew), the largest single feature of the sweep, and the user's question below.
- **What needs the server**: playing together in the field (the Union Circle), the Underground with friends, the GTS and online Wonder Trade. Plan 07 holds or can hold them; no session deploys a server.
- **Spin-offs whose play doesn't fit a turn-based journey**: the team arena (Unite), the fighting game (Pokkén), the brawlers (Rumble), the trading card game, the drawing program (Art Academy), Conquest's kingdoms and Sleep's research by the night; plan 44 gives each its reason. Pokémon Masters' trainers paired with their Pokémon are plan 37 · FC3's tree of partners and plan 13 · V20's friend as partner already; Magikarp Jump's loop of raising one Pokémon is the Pokéathlon's and plan 36's drills.
- **Judged low value or held elsewhere**, each with the reason its plan gives: the Royal Unova's cruise (the crossings are a fade with no vessel drawn); Omega Ruby's Battle Resort (Hoenn follows Emerald and is finished; its streak house is 37 · FC2's in Kalos); a stand-in for the Dream World (its hidden abilities come from the modern Day Care, the DexNav and the Ability Patch, 36 · TR6 and TR3); the Rotom Rally (Hoenn's timed Cycling Road run is built; Galar's own is plan 33's); PokéPark's attractions and the Wi-Fi Plaza (41 · EG3's plaza is the gathering place of ours; the Plaza's games need other players); Pokémon Stadium's minigames (the Pokéathlon's ten events are built); Pokémon Quest's expeditions (35 · BN6's cave job); the Time Capsule (built: plan 08 · P20 to P22); Coach Trainers (plan 12's tips and plan 13's medals); the Delta Episode, a second story per region (41's cases and its gathering are this game's episodes; a region's own would be its plan's); the Indigo Disk's Item Printer and Synchro Machine (plan 34 · PD11 and plan 07); X and Y's part-time jobs at the hotel (Poké Jobs and the plaza's stalls pay); the style meter (plan 43's card and look).
- **Small things a region's plan can take if wanted**: Lumiose's taxis (a ride between a city's stops, plan 23's Lumiose), a daily sale in the Marts (a row of the daily events), QR codes (nothing to scan), the Pokéwalker's real-world steps (hardware; plan 35's stroll counter counts the game's own), the Memory Link (it needs an earlier game's save).
- Each plan also names what of its own category it leaves out, and why.

## Questions for the user

1. **Battles in real time for Lumiose's era.** *Recommended:* keep plan 23's turn-based Z-A; Lumiose's era keeps its places, Wild Zones, Rogue Megas and Royale. The alternative is a plan of its own for a real-time battle mode beside the turn-based core, written before plan 23 · Z22.
2. **The order.** *Recommended:* first what is built and unreachable or missing from a finished region, which needs nothing new: Unova's Town Map (38 · EX10), the tools and items with no source (36 · TR3, 40 · GE11), the moves nobody can learn (36 · TR9), the species checks and trades (40 · GE1 to GE3), the catch's EXP and the charms (40 · GE5) and the rematches' parity (41 · EG5); then the hub and the circuit (41 · EG1 to EG4), bonding (35 · BN1 to BN5) and the field's outbreaks (38 · EX1 to EX3); then the rest of 36 to 40; and last the pastimes, the player's life and the side modes (42 to 44), each of which the user may drop whole. The alternative is any order the user prefers; the plans' gates say what must come first.
3. **The plans' own decisions.** Each plan lists its choices with a recommendation; as everywhere in the roadmap, a session goes ahead on the recommended answer unless the user says otherwise, and every new story, festival, gift or design of a species is shown to the user as an outline before its session builds it.

## Where they run

The plans place their sessions in 00-overview's lanes, and [00 · Overview](../00-overview.md) has them under "Plans 35 to 44". In short: lane 1 (story) takes the cases, the epilogue, the rematches and the species checks; lane 2 (map and rooms) the hub, the hideaways and the arranged Safari; lane 3 (rules) the facilities, the charms, powers and hidden abilities; lane 5 (field and people) bonding, the outbreaks and the salon; lane 6 (comfort, tools and guards) the training halls, crafting, the gift box, the trade board, the card and the photo book. Most first sessions need nothing that isn't built.
