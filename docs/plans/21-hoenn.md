# Plan 21 · Hoenn

Written 2026-10-06, before any session.

**Goal**: Hoenn as the third region of the chain, read from Emerald through plan 18's GBA reader: its map from Littleroot Town to Ever Grande and the sea floor under its eastern routes, its story with both teams, the three titans and Wallace, its eight badges and its trainers, Dive as a way of travelling, Secret Bases, the Contest Hall of the Generation 3 kind, the Battle Frontier island whose seven facilities already have their rules elsewhere, and a song for every role of Emerald's music table, all in our own art, sound and words. The player comes in by ferry from Johto and leaves by ship for Sinnoh.

## Where we are

- **The chain**: `Data/RegionDatabase.cs` has Hoenn as a `Region` with no `Start`, so `IsBuilt` is false, and its two `RegionLink`s (`Johto → Hoenn`, `Hoenn → Sinnoh`) are `Transport.Undecided`; `CheckTravel` answers `TravelCheck.DestinationNotBuilt`. `Transport` is `Undecided` or `Boat`. Plan 18 · W4 proposes the table every region confirms: Johto to Hoenn by ferry from Olivine to Slateport, Hoenn to Sinnoh by ship from Slateport to Canalave, and `RegionLink.Vessel`; W5 the journey on screen (`common.Crossing`); W1 the region record (`Data/regions/<id>.json`: the professor, starters, badges, dex, heroes), W2 badges per region, W3 the regional Pokédexes from PokeAPI (`PokemonSpecies.DexNumbers`; "hoenn" is Emerald's 202), W4 what crosses (a fresh journey: the party into the boxes, a starter from the new professor).
- **The reader**: plan 18 · W6 and W7 read pret/pokeemerald's `data/maps/<Map>/map.json`, `layouts.json`, `map.bin` and `metatile_attributes.bin` into the world files Sinnoh's maps are made of (`Data/WorldFiles.cs`: `WorldChunkFile`, `WorldMatrixFile`, `WorldAreaFile` with its `Land` and `Water` tables, `AreaObject`, `AreaWarp`, `AreaSign`, `AreaTrigger`), give every `MB_*` behaviour a `TileBehavior` and the ones Platinum lacks (underwater, the secret bases, the rails) a value above `0xE5` with a row in `TileBehaviors.InGame`, stitch connections into matrices with a per-tile `WorldChunkFile.Areas`, write `dive` connections as warps, and build rooms from layouts (`MapSetting.Indoors`). Plan 19 · K1 and K2 build the first region on it (Kanto's `Cover.Kanto` rows, the interior table, `FindBuildings` by the doors' destinations); this plan repeats their shape for Hoenn and adds what Hoenn alone has.
- **The field**: `TravelMode` is `OnFoot`, `Surfing` or `Cycling`; `FieldMoves` is Surf, Waterfall, RockClimb and Flash (`FieldMovement.MovesOf`); `FieldMovement.Step` ends in `StepKind` Blocked, Walk, Hop, Land, Climb or Jump (a Bicycle's ramp); `FieldMovement.CanStartSurf` and `Player.StartSurf` begin a surf, `SurfMount` draws the one swimmer. Plan 02 · S2 puts the field moves in the party menu (`PartyScreen.ActionsFor`, `common.Use<Move>`) and checks each where it is used (`Overworld/FieldMoveRules.cs`: `FieldMove`, Platinum's fifteen, neither Dive nor Secret Power among them; `Check` on a `FieldSpot`; `BadgeFor`, Platinum's badges, which the obstacles' common scripts ask again with `if badge`); Fly goes to Platinum's twenty spots (`UI/FlyScreen.cs`, `Data/SpawnLocations.cs`) on the map of Sinnoh. Nothing dives: `TileBehavior` is Platinum's (`Sea`, `River`, `Waterfall`, `Bridge`, `ShopShelf` at `0xE5`), `MapSetting` is `Outdoors` or `Cave`, `ArtLook.FieldRig(hour, map)` gives a cave `CaveFieldRig` and everything else the sky's, and `BattleArena` has `Water` but nothing under it. `FieldWeather` already has `Sandstorm` and `Ash`, `Weathers.Of` reads Platinum's `"Sandstorm"` and `"SlowAshfall"`, `WeatherFx` draws both and `AmbienceBed.Sandstorm` sounds: Route 111's desert and Route 113's ash need no new weather.
- **Secret Power** is a battle move (`BattleCore.Unique.cs`, by `BattleConditions.Terrain`); the field knows nothing of it, and no tile is a hollow to open.
- **Condition and contests**: `Pokemon.Beauty` is the one condition a Pokémon keeps (`SavedPokemonData.Beauty`); plan 06 · R14 brings the five conditions, sheen and Poffins, R17 Platinum's Super Contests (Visual, Dance, Acting), Ball Capsules and ribbons; `moves.json` carries no contest data. Hoenn's contest is a different game on the same condition.
- **The Frontier**: plan 06 · R18 builds the Battle Tower, Factory, Hall, Castle and Arcade with Battle Points and records; plan 14 · B8 to B10 build Emerald's Dome, Palace, Arena, Pike and Pyramid as an annex of Sinnoh's Frontier (its decision 2), with their rules and rooms. Hoenn's island needs only its map and its desks.
- **Species**: every species of Hoenn is hand-built (plan 03's Hoenn batches, 2026-10-06: `Graphics/PokemonModels.Hoenn1.cs`, Treecko to Delcatty, `Hoenn2.cs`, Sableye to Armaldo, `Hoenn3.cs`, Castform to Deoxys), with their forms (Castform's weathers and Deoxys's formes in `PokemonModels.Forms.cs`, the Megas and the Primal Kyogre and Groudon in `PokemonModels.Megas.cs`), and `CheckShapes` already gives Castform its shape in battle (plan 06 · R7).
- **Music**: `Data/music/` has `common`, `kanto` and `sinnoh`; `MusicDirector.Resolve(role, region, exists)` looks in the region's folder first; `MusicRole` has one villain family (`EyeGalactic`, `BattleGalactic`, `BattleGalacticBoss`, `VictoryGalactic`, `VictoryGalacticBoss`); `Data/audio/sound-map.json` gives each trainer class its eye theme by short name (`SoundMap`). Emerald gives its two teams two eye themes and one battle theme.
- **Trainers**: Platinum's table is plan 06 · R9's `Data/trainers.json` (927 trainers, written by `tools/DataImporter` from the decompilation's `res/trainers/data` and read by `TrainerDatabase`: the AI flags, the items, the double flag, the team built as the original builds it, the prize money of the class's formula). `TrainerDatabase.Fill` gives a placed trainer its own when its overlay names the same team or none, and plan 08 · P7 (to come) leaves the overlays only the lines. The table holds Sinnoh's alone: a region's trainers are added by a reader writing the same record shape (`TrainerRecord`, `TrainerPokemonRecord` in `Data/TrainerDatabase.cs`), into a file per region in its world folder as plan 18's decision 8 recommends.
- **The roadmap's order**: the README builds the chain in generation order, Sinnoh first, and plan 19 (Kanto) and 20 (Johto) stand before this one; nothing in Sinnoh waits for anything here. The five systems Emerald alone has (Dive, Secret Bases, the Generation 3 contest, the two bikes, the PokéNav) are built only behind Hoenn's maps, so Platinum's play doesn't move.

## Design

### The source: Emerald

pret/pokeemerald at a pinned commit, the fourth repository of `Sources.cs` (plan 18 · W6 adds the pin and its folder list). Emerald over Ruby and Sapphire because it has both teams, Rayquaza's part, Juan, Wallace as Champion, Steven at Meteor Falls, the Battle Frontier and the Battle Tents. The assets policy holds as plan 01 · M1 set it: the reader keeps layouts and numbers (metatiles, behaviours, elevations, where people and warps stand, who a trainer is and what they carry, a map's music and weather by name, the encounter tables, the flags' and variables' names) and never a tile's graphics, a palette, a song or a line of text; the checkout stays in the git-ignored cache.

### Hoenn's world folder

`Data/world/hoenn/` with the layout of `Data/world/sinnoh/`: `world.json` with `"region": "Hoenn"`, the generated `matrices/`, `chunks/`, `areas/` and `habitats.json`, hand-written `overlays/<key>.json`. The reader stitches Emerald's connected maps into one matrix, `Hoenn` (measured in tiles of the whole region, as `Sinnoh` is), one per cave floor and room, and **one for the sea floor**: Emerald's underwater maps (`Underwater_Route124` to `Underwater_Route129`, `Underwater_Route105`, `Underwater_Route134`, `Underwater_SeafloorCavern`, `Underwater_SootopolisCity`, `Underwater_MarineCave`) reach their routes by `dive` and `emerge` connections and no map of the land, so they are stitched into `HoennUnderwater` at the offsets of the routes they lie under, and a tile of the sea floor has the coordinates of the tile of sea above it. Its entry says `"setting": "Underwater"`, a third `MapSetting`. Area keys are Emerald's map names in lower case (`littleroot_town`, `route_101`, `granite_cave_1f`, `underwater_route_124`), which collide with none of Sinnoh's or Kanto's.

`Cover.Hoenn` holds the cover rows by tileset and metatile range (plan 18's mechanism; Hoenn's two dozen tilesets): lawn, tall grass, the red earth of Route 111 and its sand, Route 113's ash, the sea, Fortree's canopy, Lavaridge's hot springs, Sootopolis's white stone, Pacifidlog's logs, the sea floor's sand and rock. Buildings are metatiles, so there is no `WorldModels` catalogue: `MapStructures.FindBuildings` finds each block, its kind from the door's destination map or an overlay row, and `BuildingArt.StyleOf` dresses it by town. Hoenn's towns need ways of building of their own, each a row of the style guide's "Buildings" section **before** `Architecture` grows and `BuildingArt.Styles.cs` paints it: Littleroot's and Oldale's cottages, Petalburg, Rustboro's stone offices, Dewford's and Slateport's seaside boards (`Harbour` and `Seaside` may serve), Mauville's yellow stucco, Verdanturf, Fallarbor's ash-roofed huts, Lavaridge's bathhouses, Fortree's tree houses (`BuildingModels` builds a house on a trunk, and Emerald's `MB_FORTREE_BRIDGE` becomes a deck `DeckAt` reads, over the forest floor), Lilycove, Mossdeep, Sootopolis's crater terraces, Pacifidlog's houses on logs (decks over `Sea`), Ever Grande's flowered stone. Rooms are imported layouts (plan 18 · W7) with an interior table for Hoenn's tilesets, so no room is hand-made. Heights are the elevation nibble, as plan 18 · W6 plates them.

### The region's record

Hoenn's `Data/regions/hoenn.json` (plan 18 · W1): Professor Birch, with our own welcome on Emerald's beats; Treecko, Torchic and Mudkip, the rival taking the one that beats the player's (`StoryState.RivalStarterFor`); the badges Stone, Knuckle, Dynamo, Heat, Balance, Feather, Mind and Rain; the dex "hoenn" (202, from plan 18 · W3's `DexNumbers`), the National Pokédex opened by the Hall of Fame alone (Birch's lab after the credits: a `NationalUnlockRule` of W3's); the music folder `hoenn`; the start in the moving van, then the player's room in Littleroot Town. The two heroes are Brendan and May: the one the player isn't is **the rival**, named by the look, so written lines say `{rival}` and the introduction asks no rival's name, as Emerald asks none; Wally is a person like any other. Emerald's heal locations are the record's `Spawns` (plan 18 · W1 and W7), so Fly, Teleport and a whiteout in Hoenn land in Hoenn, as plan 19 has Kanto's (plan 02 · S2 keeps the place to come back to, `VAR_SPAWN_LOCATION`; plan 06 · R10 sends the whiteout there). The gates tie the field moves to Hoenn's badges (the record's `Gates`, which plan 02 · S2's check, `FieldMoveRules.BadgeFor`, reads once plan 18 · W2 has made it the region's): Cut to Stone, Flash to Knuckle, Rock Smash to Dynamo, Strength to Heat, Surf to Balance, Fly to Feather, Dive to Mind, Waterfall to Rain.

Hoenn is fought by the rules the game began under (`Ruleset`, plan 06 · R1), Platinum's by default, on Emerald's teams and levels; Emerald's own Generation 3 rules are not a third preset, which H2 records in `docs/mechanics/rulings.md` beside plan 19's ruling. `GameEngine.TerrainAt` reads the tile and then `MapArea.BattleBackground`, which the reader fills from the map's `battle_scene`.

### Dive and the sea floor

- **Rules** (`Overworld/FieldMovement.cs`, GPU-free): `TravelMode.Diving` and `FieldMoves.Dive` (the next free flag after plan 20's `Whirlpool`), set by `MovesOf` when a party Pokémon knows Dive. Plan 18's table gives the tiles: `DeepWater` (Emerald's `MB_DEEP_WATER`, the dark water one dives into; surfable), `Seaweed` (`MB_SEAWEED`, the sea floor's grass, with encounters), `NoSurfacing` and `SeaweedNoSurfacing` (`MB_NO_SURFACING`, `MB_SEAWEED_NO_SURFACING`). `FieldMovement.CanDive(map, x, y, walker)` says yes on `DeepWater` while surfing with the move and the Mind Badge; `CanSurface(map, x, y)` says yes under any tile but the two that forbid it. A step under the sea is a surfing step at surfing's pace, with no running, no bike and no ledges; `TileBehaviors.HasEncounters` counts `Seaweed`.
- **Where it goes**: a dive is from any `DeepWater` tile to the same tile of `HoennUnderwater`, and back, so `WorldAreaFile` gains `Dive` (the key of the area below) and `Emerge` (the one above), which the reader fills from the same `dive` and `emerge` connections plan 18 · W6 writes as warps, leaving its warp list for doors. `GameEngine.TryDive`, beside `TryStartSurf`, starts a `TransitionKind.Fade` with the bubbles' sound and lands the player on the other map at the same coordinates in `Diving` or `Surfing`; `Player.StartDive` and `Surface` set the mode. Dive joins plan 02 · S2's party menu as a `FieldMove` of its own (`PartyScreen.ActionsFor`, a case of `FieldMoveRules.Check`, `common.UseDive`) and asks the same rule.
- **Wild Pokémon** of the sea floor are the area's `Water` table, which the reader fills from Emerald's underwater `water_mons`: `Map.RollWildEncounter(x, y, steps, water: true)` on a `Seaweed` tile, through `WildEncounterRules` unchanged. Battles under the sea are on `BattleTerrain.Water` with a stage of their own, `BattleArena.Underwater`, built by `BattleArenas` and lit by `ArtLook.ArenaRig` (the style guide's "Arenas (G8)" first).
- **The look** (the style guide's "Water" gains "The sea floor" before the code): `ArtLook.UnderwaterFieldRig`, one rig whatever the hour as the cave's is (a blue-green light from above, fog close and blue, the post's saturation down); the sea floor is sand and rock cover with kelp (`NatureArt.Kelp`, swaying like tall grass through `MeshBuilder.Sway`, in `MeshPass.Ground`); caustics are one more haze layer of `WeatherFx.Haze`'s kind drawn after the composite, timed by `FieldLife.Now`; bubbles rise from the swimmer (`FieldLife.Bubbles`, cells of `LifeArt`). The camera stays `FieldCamera.Default`. The surface is never seen from the steep camera, so nothing is drawn for it. One layer and the kelp are weighed with `profile` before and after, under the 8 ms budget on High.
- **Sound**: `AmbienceBed.Underwater`, a muffled bed in `Ambience.Entries` with its row in `docs/sound-effects.md` ("Ambience") and `SoundTests`, chosen by `FieldAmbience.Around` on an underwater map; `hoenn/underwater.mml` for Emerald's `MUS_UNDERWATER` role, named by the underwater areas' overlays.

### Secret Bases

- **Finding one**: Secret Power, a `FieldMove` of its own in plan 02 · S2's party menu, used facing a tile whose behaviour is one of Emerald's `MB_SECRET_BASE_SPOT_*` (plan 18's table gives each kind a value: a cave wall, a tree, a shrub) runs `common.SecretBaseSpot`: `if knows "Secret Power"`, the question, then a new command `openbase`, which makes the hollow the player's. Emerald's base layouts (one per spot kind and place, about two dozen) are rooms the reader writes as `Indoors` matrices, so the base is a map like any room, reached by a warp the engine points at the spot.
- **The base** is `Models/SecretBase.cs` (GPU-free): the spot (area key and tile), the layout, the decorations placed (`Placed`: a decoration's name, x and y). The save keeps one base, `SaveData.SecretBase`, and a store of decorations, `SaveData.Decorations` (names and counts; they are not bag items, as Emerald keeps them in the PC): moving to another spot empties the old one, Emerald's rule. The catalogue is `Data/decorations.json`, our own file transcribed from Emerald's decoration table (name, size in tiles, kind, what it blocks, price, where it is sold: numbers and names, with every picture ours, painted by `PropModels` by kind).
- **Placing**: `DecorationScreen` (`UI/`, on the kit: a grid cursor over the room, the store's list, place and take back, in methods without input) is shared with plan 06 · R16's Underground base: whichever lands first builds it, taking a catalogue and a room, and the other uses it. In the room a decoration is a `Prop` of a new `PropType.Decoration` with its `Model` the catalogue's name; `Map.Props` is rebuilt when the base is entered and the scene with it. Other people's bases come only by Emerald's record mixing, which has no place here (decision 4).

### The Contest Hall

Emerald's contest is one hall, Lilycove's, with four ranks (Normal, Super, Hyper, Master), and a round of five appeals: the entrants are first ranked by the condition of the contest's type, then each turn plays a move whose contest effect gives hearts, jams the others, chains a combo, or stirs the crowd. No Visual round and no Dance round. What is shared with plan 06 · R17's Super Contests: the five conditions and sheen (R14), ribbons (R17's), `MoveData.Contest` (a move's contest type, effect, appeal and jam, read by the importer from the pinned decompilation's own tables: one field, whichever session is first), the results board and the entrants' row on the kit. What is Hoenn's own: `Models/Contest/AppealRound.cs` (GPU-free: four entrants, their order, each turn's effect, the crowd, the score, every roll from the round's own `Random`), the ranks and what each asks (the ribbon of the rank below), the opponents from Emerald's contest tables (names, species, condition: numbers), Pokéblocks (`Models/Pokeblocks.cs`: the blender with up to four berries, flavours from the berries' data R14 reads for Poffins, raising the same conditions as Poffins do, and feeding `Pokemon.Beauty` for Feebas), and the hall's rooms. `ContestScreen` draws the round (`ModernUi.Contest.cs`; the style guide's "Menu screens (G10)" first). Winning Master rank with a high score hangs a painting in the Lilycove Museum, a flag and a line.

### The story

Chapters on Emerald's `data/maps/<Map>/scripts.inc` and `data/scripts/*.inc` for what happens and in what order, keeping its flags' and variables' names (`FLAG_BADGE01_GET`, the hide flags of each map's objects, `VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE`) with a `HOENN_` prefix where a name meets Platinum's or Kanto's (plan 19's `KANTO_` rule, and its test that compares the lists), every line in our own words (`docs/scripts.md`; the README's "Writing a scene"). Scripts go in `Data/scripts/<area key>.txt`; what every Hoenn game starts with joins `common.NewGame`; each chapter adds a story walk with `HeadlessScriptHost` (battles won, the flags, items, badges and party checked at the end), and one that changes what a save in play must know raises `StoryState.CurrentVersion` with its step in `StoryMigration`.

The two teams are two sets of people with the same `Script`s where Emerald's grunts share one; Maxie, Archie and their admins are the named cast. The titans are scripted wild battles that cannot be caught and flee, until the post-game: plan 06 · R9's roamer already runs on its first chance (`BattleKind.Roamer`), and what this plan adds is the script's word for such a battle (`wildbattle` starts a plain one, or one that can't be fled, today) and the rule that no ball holds a titan. The field scenes that show them (Groudon waking under Mt. Chimney, Kyogre's submarine, the two in Sootopolis's harbour, Rayquaza's descent) use plan 10 · F1's field sprites where it has landed, and until then the camera, the weather and the text. Tate and Liza are one trainer of two people with the double flag, as Route 204's twins are (`doubleBattle`), and the Space Center a multi battle beside Steven, R9's `battle a and b with c`. Latias or Latios roams after the television at home, Emerald's choice between red and blue as a question; the Regis come by the Sealed Chamber's riddles, written as our own.

### Tests and shots

`HoennTests` is to Hoenn what `SouthWestTests` and `WorldTests` are to Sinnoh and plan 19's `KantoTests` to Kanto: the index says what is built, every chunk in view has its file, every overlay entry points at something, every warp lands on open ground and leads back, people stand where they can be spoken to, wild Pokémon are the area's, and `WorldWalk.From` Hoenn's start reaches every open area through warps, doors, item balls and dive spots (`WorldWalk` learns that a `DeepWater` tile reaches the tile below). The harness gets a `hoenn` mode, not part of `all` (its people would move every battle of the modes after it): it starts a new game in Hoenn, shoots each open town, route, cave, room and stretch of sea floor by day and after dark (`h01_littleroot_day`, …), walks from Littleroot Town to Rustboro City at running pace timing every frame and printing what streaming cost, and shows the chapters' scenes (`hs01_the_van`, …) through the game's own `TryInteract`, `Steering` and the question box's buttons. Two runs draw the same pictures: whatever is new takes its chance from `Dice` and its time from `FrameClock`, a contest round rolls on its own seed, and a change is judged by `diff` of a `hoenn` run against a `hoenn` run.

## Risks

- **Order.** Plan 18 · W6 and W7 and plan 19 · K1 and K2 must land first: the reader, the cover mechanism, the interior table and `FindBuildings`' kinds are theirs, and repeating them here would fork the importer. H1 waits; nothing of Sinnoh's waits for H1.
- **The sea floor's offsets.** A dive lands on the same coordinates, so the reader must place each underwater map exactly under its route; an offset wrong by a tile puts the player in rock. `HoennTests` holds every `DeepWater` tile of an open area over an open tile of the floor and every floor tile's emerge under water.
- **What plan 06 · R9 left**: R9 is done, and Tate and Liza's double, the Space Center's multi battle, a Pokémon that flees and the Safari battle are in. Still to come: the titans' battle as a script's word with no catch (this plan's own, in H7) and the Safari Zone's steps (the Great Marsh's place, plan 01 and R16). H6 can be written before that place lands with the beat marked, as plan 02's chapters do for their mechanics, but not finished.
- **Two sessions on one field.** `MoveData.Contest` and `DecorationScreen` are shared with plan 06 · R17 and R16: whichever is first builds it, and the second reads it. A session that starts without checking the other's status writes it twice.
- **Frame cost under the sea and in Fortree**: one haze layer over 4K, kelp as sway geometry, and a forest with houses in it; H5 and H6 run `profile` before and after and keep the preset's budget.
- **Platinum's defaults.** Nothing here runs on a Sinnoh map: `Diving` is never entered without a `DeepWater` tile, the new behaviour values sit above Platinum's, the rules are the game's `Ruleset`, and `all` diffs empty against the run before each session.
- **The named cast's looks** are plan 11's to design; until then Hoenn's cast wears stand-ins, which the shots will show.
