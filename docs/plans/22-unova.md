# Plan 22 · Unova

Written 2026-10-06, before any session.

**Goal**: Unova as the fifth region of the chain, the first with no decompilation: its map from Black 2 and White 2 by the source plan 18 decides, its story from Aspertia City to the Pokémon League and the old roads of the post-game in our own words, its four seasons (a month each of the real calendar, changing the field's colours, the winter's drifts, the wild Pokémon and Deerling's coat, in Unova alone), its own Pokédex order, the triple and rotation battles plan 14 builds, the Battle Subway, the Pokémon World Tournament in its own hall at Driftveil, the Hidden Grottoes, and a song for every role of its music table, in our own art, sound and words. The player comes in by ship from Sinnoh and leaves by plane for Kalos.

## Where we are

- **The chain**: `Data/RegionDatabase.cs` has Unova as a `Region` with no `Start`, so `IsBuilt` is false; its two `RegionLink`s (`Sinnoh → Unova`, `Unova → Kalos`) are `Transport.Undecided`, and `Transport` is `Undecided` or `Boat`. Plan 18 · W4 proposes the rows every region confirms (Sinnoh to Unova by ship from Canalave to Castelia; Unova to Kalos by plane from Mistralton, with `Transport.Plane` and `RegionLink.Vessel`), W5 the journey on screen (`common.Crossing`), W1 the region record (`Data/regions/<id>.json`: the professor, the starters, the badges, the dex, the heroes, `WorldModels.Catalogue(region)`), W2 the badges per region, W3 every regional Pokédex from PokeAPI's `pokedexes.csv` into `PokemonSpecies.DexNumbers`, W4 what crosses (a fresh journey: the party into the boxes, a starter from the new professor). Plan 18's decision 7 keeps the player's own figure in every region.
- **The source**: `tools/DataImporter/Sources.cs` pins three public commits (`DecompCommit`, `PokeApiCommit`, `ShowdownCommit`) and plan 18 · W6 adds two GBA ones. No decompilation of a Generation 5 game exists. Plan 18's decision 5 chooses where its layouts come from: (a) a reader of the user's own dump with its hash pinned and nothing of it in the repository, (b) hand-authoring with plan 16 · T9's editor, (c) generated layouts in the region's shape. The DS reader is `tools/MapImporter`: `DecompMaps` (its `Folders`, the headers, `AreaEvents`), `LandData` (a 32×32 chunk's four sections: behaviours, props, the model's names and the lie of its polygons through `TerrainModel`, the BDHC heights), `Matrix`, `Cover.Rules` (a tile's look from its texture's name) and `WorldWriter`, which writes `WorldChunkFile`, `WorldMatrixFile`, `WorldAreaFile` and `habitats.json` (`Data/WorldFiles.cs`). Generation 5's map containers are cousins of Platinum's; the same classes read what matches and new ones what doesn't.
- **Seasons** exist nowhere. `GameClock` (`Core/GameClock.cs`) is `TimeOfDay`, `Fixed`, `Hour` from `DateTime.Now` and `ForHour`'s table, and the date, `Today`, which `FixedDate` fixes (the harness sets the first of June 2026); `GameSettings.TimeOfDay` and `OptionRow.TimeOfDay` fix it; `ArtLook.FieldRig(hour, map)` gives the light, `ArtLook.Weathered` changes it for the weather, `ArtLook.SnowGrade` grades a snow arena. What is white in winter is the import's: `MapArea.Snowbound` (`Data/World.cs` sets it where snow cover outnumbers green), `TerrainCover.Snow` and `Ice`, the snow behaviours (`TileBehavior.ShallowSnow` to `DeepestSnow`, which `TileBehaviors.KeepsFootprints` reads), `TreeModels.Pine(…, snow:)`; a lawn's tones are `PixelGround`'s own `Grass` and `GrassLight`, a tree's green is a tint `TreeModels.Pine` and `Round` mix over grey leaf sheets (`NatureArt.Leaves`, painted in `NatureArt.TreeTones`), the tall grass and the tufts have `NatureArt`'s own shades, and `SceneTextures.Leaves`, `Needles`, `TallGrass`, `LawnTuft` and `Flowers` are one shared sheet each, made in `SceneTextures.Warm`. Plan 01 · M7's weather calendar (`calendar.json`, Platinum's `sYearlyWeather`, read by `GameClock.Today`) is Platinum's weather by the day of the year, not a season.
- **Deerling and Sawsbuck** are in `species.json` with their coats as forms of `FormKind.Look` (`Deerling-Summer`, `Deerling-Autumn`, `Deerling-Winter`, the spring coat the species itself; Sawsbuck the same) and each coat evolves into its own (`fromForm`, `targetForm`). `Pokemon.ChangeForm` changes a form, `SavedPokemonData.Form` keeps it; nothing changes it with a calendar. Unova's 156 species are hand-built (plan 03's Unova batches, its decision 3: `PokemonModels.Unova1.cs` to `Unova4.cs`) and so are the coats: `Deerling-Summer` to `Sawsbuck-Winter` are in `PokemonModels.Forms`, made in `PokemonModels.Regional.cs` by `DeerlingBuild` and `SawsbuckBuild` (`PokemonModels.Unova3.cs`, on a private `Season` of their own), so a coat that changes changes the model and the sprite with it.
- **Wild Pokémon**: `WorldAreaFile.Land` has twelve slots met 20, 20, 10, 10, 10, 10, 5, 5, 4, 4, 1 and 1 times in a hundred and `Water` five met 60, 30, 5, 4 and 1, the same weights Black and White use; `HabitatArea` lists an area's by `Morning`, `Day`, `Night` and `Surf`; `Map.RollWildEncounter(x, y, steps, water, thick, lead)` rolls through `WildEncounterRules`. Plan 02 · S2 brought the rods (`Overworld/Fishing.cs`, the area files' `OldRod`, `GoodRod` and `SuperRod` tables, `MapArea.RodEncounters`); plan 06 · R13 owes the slots the time of day swaps in and swarms.
- **Formats**: `BattleFormat` is `Single` or `Double`. Plan 14 · B6 and B7 build triples and rotations (`BattleFormat.Triple`, `Rotation`, `TrainerRecord.Format`), B11 and B12 the World Tournament (`Models/Tournament.cs`, `Data/tournaments.json` with a section per region, held in Jubilife's Global Terminal for Sinnoh), plan 06 · R18 the Battle Tower's streaks and Battle Points.
- **Rules**: Unova is fought by the rules the game began under (`Ruleset`, plan 06 · R1). Plan 06 · R24 to R27 owe the moves and abilities of Generation 5; until they land, a move of Unova's that the engine can't run is `Partial` or `None` in `docs/mechanics/coverage.md`. `PokemonSpecies.HiddenAbility` is in the data and given to nobody (plan 03 · D1), as Platinum has it.
- **Music**: `Data/music/` has `common`, `kanto` and `sinnoh`; `MusicDirector.Resolve(role, region, exists)` looks in the region's folder first; `Data/audio/sound-map.json` gives each trainer class its eye theme (plan 21 lets a class's row name a file of the region's folder).
- **The roadmap's order**: the README builds the chain in generation order, so plans 19, 20 and 21 stand before this one and nothing in Sinnoh waits for anything here. Everything Unova alone has (seasons, the grottoes, the Subway's lines, the hall at Driftveil) is built only behind Unova's maps, so Platinum's play doesn't move.

## Design

### The source

Black 2 and White 2 over Black and White (decision 1): the larger map (Aspertia, Virbank, Lentimas, Humilau and the sea routes, with the old roads of Black and White as the post-game), the Pokémon World Tournament, the Hidden Grottoes, the Subway's full set of lines, the 301 species of its Pokédex. The plan is written for plan 18's decision 5 taken as (a), and says what (b) and (c) change.

**(a) The dump reader**, `tools/MapImporter/Dump/`, beside `DecompMaps` and plan 18's `Gba/`, sharing `WorldWriter` and `TerrainModel` with them. It reads one ROM dump the user owns, found by `--dump <path>` (never under the repository; `.gitignore` already ignores the importer's `.cache`), whose SHA-256 is pinned as `Sources.DumpHash`: a run on a file with another hash refuses, so two runs read the same bytes as surely as a pinned commit. What it reads, by the community's documentation of the Generation 5 file system (Project Pokémon's pages and the tables SDSME-type editors are built on; the reader's README lists each container and what is kept, as Platinum's does): the map files (a 32×32 table of two bytes a tile, the behaviour and the movement permission, into `WorldChunkFile.Behaviours` and `Solid` through a new `Gen5Behaviours.cs`, a table onto `TileBehavior` that takes Platinum's value where the meaning is Platinum's and plan 18 · W6's GBA value (`0xED` to `0xFE`) where it is the GBA's, and gives what neither has (dark grass among them) a value of its own, each a row of `TileBehaviors.InGame` and of a new section of `docs/tile-behaviours.md` (`TileBehavior` is a `byte` with few values left free, so if they run out U1 widens it and the chunk file's rows of two hex digits a tile, Sinnoh's files reading as they do, and records it in `docs/data-files.md`); the buildings a map places, by model number and box, into `ChunkProp`; the terrain model's texture names and the lie of its polygons into `Cover` through `Gen5Cover.cs`, a table of Unova's texture names in `Cover.Rules`' order; the height section into `HeightPlate`s), the matrices that lay maps in a grid, the zone headers (name, music by role, weather, camera, the map's battle background, the flags for the Bicycle, running and Fly), the events (people with their looks, movement and sight, warps, triggers with their variables, signs, the item balls' items and the hidden items), the trainer tables (class, name, party: species, level, item, moves where the original chose them), and the encounter tables, which in Generation 5 hold a table a season where a place changes with it, each with grass, dark grass, shaking grass, water, rippling water, fishing and rippling fishing (Generation 5 has one rod, so fishing fills the area file's `SuperRod`). What it never keeps: a texture (the four seasonal texture sets least of all), a model's vertices beyond finding which tiles a polygon covers, a sound, a line of text. The command is `--game black2 --dump <path>` into `out/unova/` (the mosaic by cover and by behaviour, a picture per area, `report.md` with the unknown textures and behaviours counted), and `--data --region unova` writes `Data/world/unova/` by its `world.json`. The world files are checked in as Sinnoh's are (the open areas and the chunks in view of them), so the game builds from the repository alone and the dump is needed only to run the reader; CI (plan 16 · T4) never runs it. The layouts the reader finds hard are cross-checked against PokeAPI's pinned checkout, which the data importer already has: `encounters.csv`, `encounter_slots.csv`, `location_areas.csv` and `encounter_condition_values.csv` (whose `season-spring` to `season-winter` are the seasons' tables) for the versions `black-2` and `white-2`, numbers and names.

**(b) By hand** changes U1 and U2 into the first of a session per chapter: each chapter draws its areas with plan 16 · T9's editor as `MapFile` grids from the community's maps of the region and the user's memory, the reader's sessions are not written, the seasons' layouts are two drawings of each place that changes, and the encounter tables come from PokeAPI as above. **(c) Generated** changes them into a `UnovaGraph` (towns, routes, their lengths and what each holds) and a generator of layouts in the region's shape, which would be ours and not the place; the rest of the plan stands. Under every source the rule holds: layouts and numbers, never a tile's graphics.

### Unova's world folder

`Data/world/unova/` with the layout of `Data/world/sinnoh/`: `world.json` with `"region": "Unova"`, the generated `matrices/`, `chunks/`, `areas/` and `habitats.json`, hand-written `overlays/<key>.json`. The reader stitches the overworld into one matrix, `Unova` (positions in tiles of the whole region, as `Sinnoh` is), one per cave floor, tower storey, bridge of its own and room (Generation 5's rooms are map files too, so they are matrices with plan 18 · W7's `MapSetting.Indoors` and need no hand-made files). Area keys are the dump's zone names in lower case (`aspertia_city`, `route_19`, `castelia_sewers`, `giant_chasm`), which collide with none of the other regions'.

Buildings are models placed on the map, as Sinnoh's are, so `WorldMapBuilder.PlaceModels` lays them out from a catalogue of Unova's own, `Data/WorldModels.Unova.cs` behind plan 18 · W1's `WorldModels.Catalogue(region)`, every model a plain stand-in (`StandInUntil`) until the session that builds its town. Unova's towns need ways of building of their own, each a row of the style guide's "Buildings" section **before** `Architecture` grows and `BuildingArt.Styles.cs` paints it: Aspertia's hillside houses, Floccesy's cottages and the ranch, Virbank's docks and the complex's chimneys, Castelia's towers (`BuildingModels.Stages`, the tallest stages the game has), Nimbasa's lights, Driftveil's warehouses and the market, Mistralton's hangars, Lentimas's dry stone, Undella's seaside villas, Lacunosa's walled houses, Opelucid in the version's look (decision 4), Humilau's houses on the water (decks over `Sea`, as plan 21 builds Pacifidlog), and in the post-game Nuvema, Accumula, Striaton, Nacrene's warehouses and Icirrus. The great bridges (Skyarrow, the Driftveil Drawbridge, the Tubeline, Village, Marvelous) are decks `DeckAt` reads with the camera the zone header names (`FieldCamera`); the Marine Tube is a room whose walls are the sea.

### Seasons, in Unova alone

Black and White's rule: a month each of the real calendar, round and round (January spring, February summer, March autumn, April winter, May spring), so a season is `(month − 1) mod 4`. Seasons over Sinnoh as well were weighed and refused: Platinum has no seasons, and a season over Sinnoh would move Platinum's defaults. So a season exists only where the region's record says so (`Region.HasSeasons`, Unova true and every other region false), and `GameEngine.SeasonHere` is `null` on any other map. Everything seasonal asks `SeasonHere`, never the clock, which is what keeps Sinnoh's shots what they are.

- **The clock** (`Core/GameClock.cs`): `Season { Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter }`, `GameClock.Season` from `GameClock.Today`'s month by the rule above (so `GameClock.FixedDate` fixes it too), `GameClock.FixedSeason` set from a new `GameSettings.Season` through `ApplySettings` (an `OptionRow.Season` beside `TimeOfDay`, "Follow the calendar" by default, the Options line of the style guide's "Menu screens (G10)" first). The harness fixes the season as it fixes the time of day (`engine.Settings`, then `ApplySettings`), always: its fixed date (the first of June) would otherwise give every mode summer, the same each run but not the season the mode means. A season changes as a game is loaded or as the day turns while a game is open (`GameClock.Season` read once a frame); the change is told on the field ("Spring has come to Unova." through `Toast`), and the maps are rebuilt behind the next fade.
- **The look** (the style guide gains "Seasons" under "Overworld" before the code): `Graphics/Seasons.cs` (GPU-free) is `SeasonPalette.For(season)`: the lawn's two tones in place of `PixelGround`'s `Grass` and `GrassLight`, the trees' tints in place of the greens `TreeModels.Pine` and `Round` mix (the leaf sheets are grey, so the tint alone turns a tree), the tall grass's and the tufts' shades, the flowers' set, and in winter snow lying on every tile of `TerrainCover.Grass` and on every tree, so `PixelGround.Bake` and `TreeModels` take the palette with the map and `SceneTextures` keeps a sheet per season of the tall grass, the tufts and the flowers (`TallGrass(season)`, `tall_grass_autumn`), made in `Warm`. A chunk's bake is seeded by its tiles' places and the season, so `WorldTests.AChunksGroundIsTheSameHoweverMuchIsBakedWithIt` holds in each; `MapScene.Prepare(map, chunk)` carries the season it baked for, and `WorldRenderer` drops its scenes when `SeasonHere` moves. A winter's snow is a look: the tile's behaviour stays the dump's, so footprints and the pace follow `KeepsFootprints` as ever, unless the winter layout below gives the tile a snow behaviour. `ArtLook.Seasoned(rig, season)` grades the light as `Weathered` grades it for the weather (autumn warmer, winter paler, within the style guide's numbers for snow well below white), applied by `WorldRenderer` after `Weathered`. The sign on arrival gains a second line in Unova ("Route 19 · Winter"; `LocationSign.Show(name, note)`; the style guide's "Field menus and notices").
- **Layouts that change**: where a place has a layout a season (Icirrus City's marsh frozen into `Ice` in winter, the drifts of Twist Mountain and Route 7 that open a way nobody walks in summer), the reader writes the other seasons' chunks beside the first and `WorldMatrixFile.Seasons` names them (chunk ids per season where they differ; absent, one chunk serves all four, so Sinnoh's files don't change). `WorldMapBuilder.Build(world, entry, season)` picks them, and `MapDatabase` rebuilds the world's maps at a season's turn behind the fade (`MapDatabase.RebuildWorld(folder)`); `Map.ApplyPresence` and the save's position go on as they are, since positions are the region's tiles.
- **Wild Pokémon**: `WorldAreaFile.Seasons` holds, where the dump's tables differ, a `Land` and `Water` table per season; `MapArea.TablesFor(season)` gives the one in force and `Map.RollWildEncounter` asks it, before plan 06 · R13's time-of-day slots swap into whichever is chosen. Dark grass rolls a wild pair, fought as the wild double battle plan 06 · R9's core already plays (`BattleSetup.WildPokemon` with two), which in Sinnoh the field starts only while a partner walks beside the player (Cheryl in plan 02 · S6); shaking grass, dust clouds and rippling water are an `AreaSpecial` table each, rolled on a step by `Dice` and shown with plan 10 · F12's rustle. `habitats.json` lists a species under every season it is met in (`HabitatWays` gains the four), and the Pokédex's AREA page says which.
- **Deerling and Sawsbuck** (`Models/Seasons.cs`, GPU-free): `SeasonalForms.FormFor(species, season)` (spring the species itself, the other three its coats). A Deerling or Sawsbuck in the party takes the season's coat as a game is loaded and as the day turns, one in the boxes as it is withdrawn, through `Pokemon.ChangeForm`; outside Unova (`SeasonHere` null) a coat stays what it is, as the later games keep it, and an evolution carries the coat over (`fromForm` and `targetForm`, in the data today). The menu sprite follows `ModelName`, so nothing else changes.

### The region's record

Unova's `Data/regions/unova.json` (plan 18 · W1): Professor Juniper, with our own welcome on Black 2's beats; Snivy, Tepig and Oshawott, handed over by Bianca at Aspertia's outlook rather than by the professor (a new field of the record, `StarterGiver`, names a person of the first area's overlay; the briefcase script, `common.Briefcase`, which since plan 02 · S4 adds the Pokémon chosen to the team, serves); the badges Basic, Toxic, Insect, Bolt, Quake, Jet, Legend and Wave; the dex `updated-unova` (301, from plan 18 · W3's `DexNumbers`; `original-unova`'s 156 under Black and White), the National Pokédex opened by the Hall of Fame alone (a `NationalUnlockRule` of W3's); the new `HasSeasons` true; the music folder `unova`; the start in the player's room at Aspertia City. The record's `Heroes` are Nate and Rosa, and neither appears: the player keeps their own figure (plan 18's decision 7) and Black 2 and White 2 give the other hero no part (decision 5); Hugh is the rival and a person like any other, Bianca the professor's assistant by name and not by `{assistant}`, so written lines name them. The gates are none: from Generation 5 a field move asks no badge, so the record's `Gates` is empty and plan 02 · S2's `FieldMoveRules.BadgeFor` asks nothing in Unova once plan 18 · W2 has made it read the region's. The whiteout, Fly and Teleport go to the record's `Spawns` (the Pokémon Centers and the player's house, plan 18 · W1) as plans 19 and 21 have them.

Unova is fought by the game's `Ruleset`, Platinum's by default, on Black 2's teams and levels; Generation 5's own rules are not a third preset, which U2 records in `docs/mechanics/rulings.md` beside plans 19 and 21's rulings. Flags and variables have no original names here, since the dump has numbers: ours are `FLAG_UNOVA_<place>_<what>` and `VAR_UNOVA_…`, and the reader writes an object's hide flag as `FLAG_UNOVA_0x<number>` so a hand-named flag and a numbered one never meet; `StoryTests`' rule that every name is something that exists holds as it is.

### Tests and shots

`UnovaTests` is to Unova what plan 21's `HoennTests` is to Hoenn: the index says what is built, every chunk in view has its file, every overlay entry points at something, every warp lands on open ground and leads back, people stand where they can be spoken to, wild Pokémon are the area's in every season, and `WorldWalk.From` Aspertia reaches every open area through warps, doors, item balls and the seasons' drifts (a winter walk and a summer walk, each its own). `SeasonTests` (GPU-free) holds the month rule, the fixed override, `SeasonHere` null on every Sinnoh and Kanto map, the forms, the tables, and a chunk baked the same under each season twice. The harness gets an `unova` mode, not part of `all` (its people would move every battle of the modes after it), which starts a new game in Unova with the season fixed to spring, shoots each open town, route, cave, bridge and room by day and after dark (`u01_aspertia_day`, …), walks from Aspertia to Virbank at running pace timing every frame, and shows the chapters' scenes (`us01_the_outlook`, …) through `TryInteract`, `Steering` and the question box's buttons; and a `seasons` mode (not part of `all`) that shoots Route 19, Floccesy Town and Icirrus City in each of the four (`season_<name>_<area>`), with a Deerling's sprite on the party screen in each. Unova's towns join `profile` as plan 04 · G11 asks of every new scene, and the winter's snow over a whole route is weighed there under the 8 ms budget on High. Two runs draw the same pictures: the season and the time are fixed, the grottoes and the special grass roll on `Dice`, and a change is judged by `diff` of an `unova` run against an `unova` run.

## Risks

- **The reader's unknowns.** The Generation 5 containers are documented by the community, not by a decompilation: a field read wrong shows as a wrong tile, not an error. U1's mosaics by behaviour and cover, the report's unknown counts (Sinnoh's 99.5% the bar) and PokeAPI's encounter tables as a second witness are the net; the user judges the pictures, as plan 01 · M4's decision 8 has it.
- **A private file.** Reproducibility of the import rests on `DumpHash`; of the game on the world files checked in. A session that opens an area needs the user's dump at hand; nothing else does, and CI never does.
- **Generation 5's moves and abilities** are plan 06 · R24 to R27's. Until then Unova's trainers use moves marked `Partial` or `None`, which plan 06 · R9's AI, asking a move by Platinum's battle effect, takes for a plain hit (`MoveEffectId.Hit`) and neither side can use in full; each chapter lists the moves its leaders need and the coverage floor rises as they land.
- **Seasons rebuild the world.** A season's turn rebuilds Unova's maps and drops every baked chunk; behind a fade that is a load, and U3 times it. A change in the middle of a walk is told and waits for the next fade, never rebuilt under the player's feet. Sinnoh pays nothing: no `Seasons` list, no season, the same bake.
- **Two runs, one picture.** A harness mode that forgot to fix the season would draw its fixed date's (the first of June: summer), the same every run but not the season it means; the harness sets it beside the time of day in its setup, for every mode, and `SeasonTests` reads the harness's own setting.
- **What plan 14 owes**: the triples and rotations of the routes and of the Tournament's events, the Tournament's hall. U6 to U8 are written with the beat marked, as plan 02's chapters mark their mechanics, and U11 finishes them.
- **The behaviour byte.** `TileBehavior` is a `byte`, plan 18 · W6 gives the GBA's values the top of it, and Generation 5 has meanings neither game has: U1 counts them before writing any, and widening the enum and the chunk file's rows is its own step with `WorldTests` baking Sinnoh's chunks unchanged.
- **Order against the roadmap.** Plans 18 (W1 to W5), 19, 20 and 21 stand before this one; U1 waits for plan 18's decision 5 and nothing of Sinnoh waits for U1.
- **The version's exclusives** leave the other version's Opelucid, tower and Pokémon out; trading (plan 07) and plan 03 · D12's zone are where the missing species come from, as between the two cartridges.

## Decisions for the user

1. **Which game.** *Recommended:* Black 2 and White 2 (the larger map, the Tournament, the grottoes, the full Subway, the 301-species Pokédex, the old roads as the post-game). The alternative is Black and White, with N's story first-hand and the smaller map, which the reader can take just as well and this plan would reorder.
2. **The source.** Taken in plan 18 (decision 5). *Recommended:* there and here, (a), the user's own dump read with its hash pinned. Under (b) or (c) U1 and U2 change as "The source" says and the rest stands.
3. **The seasons' clock.** *Recommended:* Black and White's rule, a month each of the real calendar, with the option row to fix one. The alternative is a season a week, so a player meets all four in a month, which is ours and not the game's.
4. **The version's exclusives.** *Recommended:* the dump's version only (its Opelucid, its tower or treehollow, its dragon and Pokémon); the other version's species come by trade or D12's zone. The alternative builds the other version's places by hand with plan 16 · T9's editor.
5. **Nate and Rosa.** *Recommended:* neither appears, since the player keeps their own figure (plan 18's decision 7) and Black 2 and White 2 give the hero not chosen no part. The alternative puts both in the Tournament's bracket or a Subway car as trainers of their own.
6. **The old roads.** *Recommended:* built in U10 as the post-game, since the dump has them whole. The alternative leaves Nuvema to Icirrus closed and ends Unova at the League.
7. **Pokéstar Studios, the Musical and the Funfest missions.** *Recommended:* Join Avenue built, Pokéstar and the Musical left as doors until asked for (each a small game of its own, on the kit), the Funfest out as online. The alternative builds Pokéstar's scripted battles as a thirteenth session.
8. **Seasonal music.** *Recommended:* none; an area's theme and its night arrangement serve all four seasons. The alternative gives the routes a winter arrangement each, four times plan 05 · A6's work for the routes.
