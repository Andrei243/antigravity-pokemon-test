# Plan 18 · The other regions: the framework, the crossings and the importers

Written 2026-10-06, before any session.

**Goal**: what every region after Sinnoh needs before its own plan (19 · Kanto, 20 · Johto, 21 · Hoenn, 22 · Unova, 23 · the later regions) can start: the engine's Sinnoh-only parts made to belong to a region (the professor and the introduction, the starters, the badges, the regional Pokédex and its habitats, the Trainer Card, the model catalogue, the fallback map), the journey between regions with a rule for what comes along and for the level curve, every regional Pokédex's numbers from PokeAPI, a reader of the GBA decompilations' map format that writes the same world files Sinnoh's maps are made of, and the decision on where the layouts of Generation 5 and later come from. Nothing here opens a region: a region's plan does, on this framework.

## Where we are

- **The chain** is `Data/RegionDatabase.cs`: nine `Region`s in generation order (`Id`, `Name`, `Generation`, `Start`, `Arrival`, `Maps`, `StoryCompleteFlag`, `IsBuilt`, `ArrivalSpot`), eight `RegionLink`s (`From`, `To`, `Transport`, `DepartureMap`), `Transport { Undecided, Boat }`, `CheckTravel` (`TravelCheck.StoryUnfinished`, `DestinationNotBuilt`, `Ready`) and `AttendantLines`, two stand-in lines per outcome. Kanto is the hand-made `PalletTown` and `PalletPlayerHouse`; `StoryState.CompleteRegion` and `IsRegionComplete` read the `<Id>HallOfFame` flag. `GameEngine.TravelTo` is a fade and `player.SetPosition` at the `ArrivalSpot`; a script reaches it through `ScriptScreen.Travel` (`FieldHost` in `GameEngine.Scripts.cs`; `common.Attendant` is `travel` then `sayown`). `RegionTests` holds the order, the links, that every map belongs to one region and that warps never cross one. The README's "Regions" decides the chain and defers each later link to the region that builds it; plan 01 · M12's Town Map, plan 03 · D10's Pokédex, plan 02 · S1's eight badges, S2's field-move gates and fly spots, and plan 04 · G10's introduction are all Sinnoh's.
- **Sinnoh by name**, in code a second region can't use: `Habitats.Sinnoh` reads `world/sinnoh/habitats.json` and nothing else; `PokedexMode { Sinnoh, National }`, `PokemonSpecies.SinnohNumber` (set by `Importer.cs` from the decompilation's `sinnoh_pokedex.json`), `Pokedex.Entries(PokedexMode)`, `Pokedex.Modes`, `CanUnlockNational` (which asks `IsRegionComplete("Sinnoh")`), `PokedexScreen.Mode`; `Badge { Coal … Beacon }`, `StoryState.BadgeMask` with `SetBadges` masking to `0xFF`, `BadgeCount`, `ModernUi.BadgeNames` and the card's "BADGES n / 8" (`ModernUi.Records.cs`, `TrainerCardInfo.Badges` one `int`, `TitleScreen.CountBadges`); the parser's `givebadge` and `if badge` through `r.Enum<Badge>`; `FieldMoveRules.BadgeFor` (plan 02 · S2), the badge each field move asks for, by the same enum (Forest for Cut, Fen for Surf, Relic for Defog); `Data/SpawnLocations.cs` (S2), the original's twenty fly spots and the Pokémon Centers Teleport goes back to (`VAR_SPAWN_LOCATION`), all tiles of the map of Sinnoh, which Fly (`UI/FlyScreen.cs`) and Teleport warp to by name, as Dig and an Escape Rope note their way out (`exitSpot`) only on leaving it; `IntroScreen.Professor = "Prof. Rowan"`, `ShownSpecies = "Buneary"` and five arrays of his lines, the figure drawn as `CharacterStyle.For("ROWAN")`; `StarterSelectScreen.Starters` and `StoryState.Starters`, both Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup; `InitializeNewGame`'s free Turtwig (plan 02 · S4 removes it); `MapDatabase.StartMap = "Sinnoh"`, which `MapDatabase.Get` returns for any name it doesn't know; `WorldModels.Of(name)`, Sinnoh's catalogue of 170 models; `PlayerIdentity.CharacterFor`, which draws `Player` and `Assistant` as PLAYER or DAWN.
- **The save** keeps `Badges` as one `int`, `NationalPokedex` as one `bool` and `Diplomas` as names (`"Sinnoh"`, `"National"`); `StorySnapshot.Badges` is the same `int`; `SaveData.Place()` moves old saves by `WorldVersion`, `StoryMigration` by `StoryVersion` (`StoryState.CurrentVersion` is 1).
- **What belongs to a region already**, the pattern to follow: `World.LoadAll` reads every `Data/world/<folder>/` that has a `world.json` (`WorldIndexFile.Region`) and `MapDatabase.Initialize` builds every world's maps; `AudioManager.Region` is set from `RegionDatabase.RegionOfMap` and `MusicDirector.Resolve(role, region, exists)` looks in `music/<region>/` before `common/`; `EvolutionContext.Region` comes from the same lookup.
- **The importer** reads one game. `tools/MapImporter`'s `DecompMaps.Folders` are Platinum's `res/field/…` paths; `LandData` reads a 32×32 chunk's four sections, `Matrix` the grids, `WorldWriter` writes `WorldChunkFile` (behaviours in hex, `Solid`, `Cover`, `Heights` as plates, `Props`), `WorldMatrixFile`, `WorldAreaFile` (`Warps`, `Objects`, `Signs`, `Triggers`, `Land`, `Water`) and `habitats.json`; `Cover.Rules` sorts tiles by the names of the original's textures. `Sources.cs` pins three commits (`DecompCommit`, `PokeApiCommit`, `ShowdownCommit`); `MapImportTests` builds chunk files byte by byte. In the game `WorldMapBuilder.Build(world, entry)` makes a map of a matrix, `Map.AreaAt` knows one area per chunk, and `MapStructures.BuildingsOf` takes `Map.PlacedBuildings` where the import placed models and flood-fills wall, roof and door tiles (`FindBuildings`) where it didn't.
- **PokeAPI's `pokedexes.csv` and `pokemon_dex_numbers.csv`** are in the pinned checkout's `data/v2/csv/`, which `PokeApi` loads by name; nothing reads them.
- **The level curve** on arriving is named nowhere. Plan 13 · V3's level cap and `ScaleTrainers` are offered to this plan, plan 06 · R10 owes obedience and plan 07 · O5 says it follows "the badges of the region you are in"; plan 13 · V11's New Game+ carries the Pokédex and the boxes into a region played again.
- **Generation 5 and later** have no decompilation. The chain reaches Unova to Paldea in `RegionDatabase`, and nothing says where a map of theirs would come from.

## Design

**A region is a record, and its content a file.** `Region` grows what the engine reads by name today: `Folder` (`sinnoh`: the world and music folders), `Professor` (the name, the look `CharacterStyle.For` draws, the species shown, the introduction's lines), `Starters` (three), `Badges` (names, none to eight), `Gates` (the badge each field move asks for, plan 02 · S2's check), `Spawns` (where Fly lands and Teleport goes back to: S2's spawn locations, on the region's own map), `Dex` (the regional Pokédex it plays by, a PokeAPI identifier: `extended-sinnoh`), `NationalUnlock` (which rule opens the National Pokédex), `Heroes` (the boy and the girl: PLAYER and DAWN) and `Models` (the catalogue its world's props are looked up in). The chain stays code in `RegionDatabase` (the order, the links, the maps); the content comes from `Data/regions/<id>.json` (`RegionFile` in a new `Data/RegionFiles.cs`, loaded once by `RegionDatabase`), so a region's plan adds a file. Sinnoh's is written from today's constants and `IntroScreen`'s lines word for word, so the introduction plays as it does; Kanto's names Oak, Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle and Kanto's eight badges, with Rowan's look standing in for Oak's until plan 11 · C5 and plan 19 give him one. Whoever needs the region asks for the one the player stands in: `GameEngine.Here` (`RegionOfMap(currentMap.Name)`), handed to the screens (`IntroScreen`, `StarterSelectScreen`, `TrainerCardInfo`, `PokedexScreen.Open`), never a static. A script's region is that of its place (`Map.ScriptFileAt`), so `givebadge coal` and `if badge forest` keep their words: the runner resolves the name in the region's list and `ScriptLibrary.Problems` names a badge no region has.

**Badges per region.** `StoryState` keeps a mask per region (`badges: Dictionary<string, int>`; `GiveBadge(region, index)`, `HasBadge`, `BadgeCount(region)`); the `Badge` enum goes, `FieldMoveRules.BadgeFor` asks the region's `Gates` (Sinnoh's written from today's switch, so every gate asks what it asks now), and `BADGE_COUNT`, plan 13 · V3's cap and plan 06 · R10's obedience read the region the player is in. `SaveData.RegionBadges` replaces `Badges`; `StoryMigration` version 2 gives the old `int` to the region of the save's `Place()`. `TrainerCardInfo` carries the region's names and masks; the card shows the region's row and a page per region the player has entered (decision 1).

**One Pokédex, a listing per region.** Seen and caught stay by national number, shared: a species seen in Sinnoh is seen in Kanto's listing if Kanto lists it. `PokedexMode` becomes a dex id (`Pokedex.Entries(string dex)`, `"national"` among them); `Pokedex.Dexes` is the regional dex of every region the player has entered, then National once unlocked; `Diplomas` keeps its names, so the save needs no change there. `CanUnlockNational(story, region)` plays the region's `NationalUnlock`: Sinnoh's (the Hall of Fame and every species of its dex seen, today's rule) and the kinds the other games use (sixty caught, the Hall of Fame alone), each a value of a new `NationalUnlockRule` with a number in the file. `Habitats.Of(region)` reads `world/<folder>/habitats.json`; the AREA page draws the map of the dex's region, and the National listing the region the player stands in, as Platinum shows Sinnoh for everything.

**The crossing.** `RegionLink` gains `Vessel` (our own word for the attendant: ship, ferry, plane) and `Transport` gains `Plane`. The proposal, each link confirmed by its region's plan when it names its departure map:

| Link | Transport | Leaves from | Lands at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kanto to Johto | ship | Vermilion's harbour (today's `PalletTown` pier stands in) | Olivine |
| Johto to Hoenn | ferry | Olivine | Slateport |
| Hoenn to Sinnoh | ship | Slateport | Canalave |
| Sinnoh to Unova | ship | Canalave | Castelia |
| Unova to Kalos | plane | Mistralton | plan 23's |
| Kalos to Alola, Alola to Galar, Galar to Paldea | plane | plan 23's | plan 23's |

The journey itself is a script, `common.Crossing`: the attendant's question, the fade, a dark screen with the vessel's own sound (a horn and an engine from `SoundDesign`, in `SoundBank` and `docs/sound-effects.md`) and one line, then the arrival with the new region's name sign and its music, as `TravelTo` does today. A region that wants a deck to walk on (HeartGold's ship) builds it as a map of its own and points the link's `Scene` at it. `RegionTests` requires a departure map with an attendant for every built region whose next region is built.

**What comes along: a fresh journey** (decision 2, recommended (a)). Every Pokémon remembers the region it joined the player in (`Pokemon.Region`, saved; plan 07 · O2's met data sits beside it; a Pokémon received by trade joins in the region it arrived in). At the crossing the party is emptied into the boxes and the player lands with none; the new professor gives a starter as the region's story does. Until the region's Hall of Fame, `PCScreen` shows the earlier regions' Pokémon greyed, with the reason, and `Crossing.CanTake(pokemon, region, story)` (`Models/Crossing.cs`, GPU-free) refuses to withdraw them; afterwards everything goes anywhere, and obedience (plan 06 · R10) by the region's badges is the series' own guard. Money, the bag, the Pokédex, the card and the records cross; a region's key items stay in the bag and do nothing elsewhere, because their scripts are the region's; the Bicycle, the rods and an Escape Rope, which plan 02 · S2 made work by the item's own use (`BagScreen.UsedInField`), work wherever the place allows them. None of it touches a game played in one region: a game begun with `--region Sinnoh` never crosses, and Platinum's defaults don't move. The alternatives are (b), the team carried and every region's trainers and wild levels raised by plan 13 · V3's `ScaleTrainers` and cap, which the cap of 100 ends after Hoenn, and (c), a flat 50 for every later region, which the first general ruling of `docs/mechanics/rulings.md` puts behind the `Ruleset` chosen at the start, never behind a region.

**The GBA reader** (`tools/MapImporter/Gba/`, beside `DecompMaps`, sharing nothing but `WorldWriter`'s files). It reads pinned commits of pret/pokeemerald (Hoenn) and pret/pokefirered (Kanto), the fourth and fifth repositories of `Sources.cs`, listed in the importer's README's table: `data/maps/map_groups.json` and each `data/maps/<Map>/map.json` (its layout, music, weather, map type, region map section, the cycling, running and escape flags, `connections` with their offsets, the four event lists), `data/layouts/layouts.json` with each layout's `map.bin` (sixteen bits a block: ten of metatile id, two of collision, four of elevation), each tileset's `metatile_attributes.bin` (the `MB_*` behaviour in its low bits; two bytes a metatile in Emerald, four in FireRed), `include/constants/metatile_behaviors.h` and `metatile_labels.h`, `src/data/wild_encounters.json`, the trainers and their parties (`src/data/trainers.h` and `trainer_parties.h`, or the `trainers.party` text newer commits keep: the pin decides), the heal locations, the region map's section names, the item balls' `finditem` lines and the hidden items' entries. Never `tiles.png`, a palette or a tileset's graphics: the folder list can't fetch them, and no vertex, texture or palette reaches the repository.

- **Behaviours**: `GbaBehaviours.cs` maps every `MB_*` onto a `TileBehavior` (`MB_TALL_GRASS` to `TallGrass`, `MB_LONG_GRASS` to `VeryTallGrass`, `MB_POND_WATER` to `River`, `MB_OCEAN_WATER` to `Sea`, `MB_WATERFALL`, `MB_ICE`, `MB_JUMP_EAST` to `LedgeEast`, the doors to `Door`, the arrow warps to the `Exit` values, `MB_PC` to `Computer`, `MB_COUNTER`, the bookshelves) and gives what Platinum has no value for (underwater, the secret bases, the rails, a jump to the north, the Cycling Road's pull) a value of its own above `0xE5` with a row in `TileBehaviors.InGame`. Collision bits set is the blocked flag. `docs/tile-behaviours.md` gains a section the importer writes, "The GBA's behaviours": each `MB_*`, the value it becomes, and its count on each game's maps.
- **Heights**: a flat plate per run of tiles of one elevation, 1.4 tiles a level above elevation 3, so a straight step over a level's edge is 1.4 (over `FieldMovement.StepLimit`: a cliff for the walker and a face for `Relief`), and a tile of elevation 0 (the GBA's "any level": stairs, a ledge's foot, a cave mouth) gets a sloping plate whose halfway point is 0.7 from either side, under `Relief.WeldLimit`, so it walks and draws as a slope; elevation 15 (a bridge, on both levels) is a deck plate over the ground's, as Sinnoh's `DeckAt` reads.
- **Cover**: `GbaCover.cs` in `Cover.cs`'s order: the behaviour where it shows, then the metatile's label where `metatile_labels.h` names it, then a table of metatile ranges per tileset written by hand in the region's session, judged from the importer's behaviour picture and the user's memory of the place (plan 01 · M4's decision 8: no picture of the original is kept); what nothing names is `Unknown`, listed per tileset with counts in the report, Sinnoh's 99.5% the bar. A roof, a wall or a door metatile is `Building` cover, which `WorldMapBuilder.Look` already makes a solid wall.
- **Connections** become matrices: from the region's first town at the origin, every map a `connection` reaches is placed at its offset; a set no connection reaches (the Sevii Islands, Hoenn's underwater) is a matrix of its own, joined by warps, and `dive` connections are written as warps; the grid is padded to 32 and cut into chunks. A map isn't chunk-aligned, so `WorldChunkFile` gains an optional `Areas` list (one character a tile, indexing the matrix's areas) that `WorldMapBuilder` reads into a per-tile grid behind `Map.AreaAt`; Sinnoh's chunks go on without it. Two connections that disagree are reported and the first placed wins.
- **Events**: `object_events` to `AreaObject` (the graphics id as `Looks`, the movement type, the range, trainer type and sight, the hiding flag, the script label; an item ball's item from its script's `finditem`), `warp_events` to `AreaWarp`, `coord_events` to `AreaTrigger` (its `var` and `var_value`), `bg_events` to `AreaSign` (a sign's script; a hidden item's item, count and flag). `wild_encounters.json`'s land and water tables have Platinum's own twelve and five slots with the same weights, so they fit `WorldAreaFile.Land` and `Water` as they are; the three rods go to the area file's `OldRod`, `GoodRod` and `SuperRod`, which plan 02 · S2's fishing reads (the GBA's rods have two, three and five slots at weights of their own, which the area file gains a place for), Rock Smash to a `RockSmash` list for plan 06 · R13, and both to `habitats.json`. The trainers and their parties go to the region's trainer file in the record shape of plan 06 · R9's `Data/trainers.json` (`TrainerRecord` and `TrainerPokemonRecord` in `Data/TrainerDatabase.cs`; decision 8), with what that game works out for each Pokémon (its personality, its IVs), and `TrainerDatabase.Fill` gives a placed trainer its team as it gives Sinnoh's; an overlay's `trainer` block carries the lines alone, as plan 08 · P7 leaves Sinnoh's. The heal locations are the region's `Spawns`.
- **Buildings and rooms**: a GBA building is tiles, so `MapStructures.FindBuildings`'s flood fill finds it, its kind from the door's destination map (`_PokemonCenter_1F`, `_Mart`, `_Gym`), its storeys from the footprint's depth, its style from the area (`Architecture` grows a region's towns in that region's plan). Every interior is a layout of its own: the reader writes each as a matrix with a new `MapSetting.Indoors`, and `WorldMapBuilder` builds a room from it with `IsIndoors` set and the furniture its behaviours name (`Computer`, `Counter`, the bookshelves, `Television`) from `PropModels`, so a GBA region's rooms need no hand-made files where Sinnoh's wait for plan 01 · M11.
- **The command**: `--game emerald|firered` reads that pin into `out/<region>/` (a mosaic by cover and by behaviour, every map's picture, `report.md`), and with `--data` writes `Data/world/<folder>/` by its `world.json`, as Sinnoh's does. Johto is plan 20's question (decision 6): pret/pokeheartgold keeps the DS layout `DecompMaps` already reads.

**Generation 5 and later** (decision 5). No public decompilation exists, and three ways remain: (a) a reader of the user's own dump of Black 2 (and of the 3DS games for Kalos and Alola), written against the community's documentation of the DS and 3DS containers, the dump never in the repository, its hash pinned in `Sources.cs` so a run against another says so, and only the world files checked in, as Sinnoh's are; (b) hand-authoring with plan 16 · T9's editor, from memory, which plan 01's decision 1 keeps for fixes alone; (c) a route-and-city graph with layouts generated in the region's shape, which would be ours and not the place. The rule holds in each: layouts and numbers, never a tile's graphics. Galar and Paldea have no tile grid at all, which is plan 23's own question. Nothing of this is a session here; plan 22 starts from the decision.

**Where the code goes.** New: `Data/RegionFiles.cs` and `Data/regions/<id>.json`, `Models/Crossing.cs`, `tools/MapImporter/Gba/` (`GbaMaps.cs`, `GbaBehaviours.cs`, `GbaCover.cs`, `GbaWorld.cs`). Changed: `Data/RegionDatabase.cs`, `Story/StoryState.cs` and `StoryMigration.cs`, `Story/ScriptParser.cs` and `ScriptRunner.cs` (the badge words), `Overworld/FieldMoveRules.cs` (the gates), `Data/SpawnLocations.cs`, `Data/TrainerDatabase.cs` (every world's trainer file), `Core/SaveManager.cs`, `Models/Pokedex.cs`, `Models/Pokemon.cs`, `Data/Habitats.cs`, `Data/WorldFiles.cs` (`Areas`, `RockSmash`, the rods' weights), `Data/World.cs` (`WorldMapBuilder`'s per-tile areas and rooms), `Overworld/TileBehavior.cs` (the new values), `UI/IntroScreen.cs`, `StarterSelectScreen.cs`, `TrainerCardScreen.cs`, `PokedexScreen.cs`, `PCScreen.cs` with their `ModernUi` files, `tools/DataImporter/Sources.cs` and `Importer.cs`, `tools/MapImporter/Program.cs`, `Data/scripts/common.txt`. Untouched: everything that fights a battle or draws the field.

**GPU-free and drawn.** `RegionFile`, `Region`, `Crossing`, the Pokédex's dexes, the badge masks, the migration and the whole reader take no window: tests drive them. What draws: the card's pages, the Pokédex's dex row, the greyed box rows and the crossing's dark screen, each on the kit with harness shots, and two runs still draw the same pictures (nothing here rolls `Dice` or reads a clock). No pass is added: nothing here costs the frame.

- **The badge change crosses plan 02's chapters**, which write `givebadge` lines as they go: the words don't change, only the enum behind them, and `StoryMigration` carries a save over. Do W2 before S5 hands out the Coal Badge, or the migration moves one more bit.

## Decisions for the user

1. **The Trainer Card.** *Recommended:* the region's badges on the front and a page per region entered. The alternative is every region's row on one card, forty slots most of which are empty for most of the game.
2. **What crosses with the player.** *Recommended:* (a), a fresh journey: the party into the boxes, the earlier regions' Pokémon resting until the new Hall of Fame, money, bag, Pokédex, card and badges along. The alternatives: (b) the team carried and plan 13 · V3's scaling and cap over every region, which ends at level 100 after Hoenn; (c) a flat 50 for later regions, only ever behind the modern `Ruleset`.
3. **One Pokédex or one per region.** *Recommended:* one, with a listing per region and the National one; seen and caught are the player's. The alternative is a Pokédex handed over anew in each region with its own seen and caught, as the games have it, which loses the National count across the chain.
4. **`MapDatabase.Get`'s fallback.** *Recommended:* throw for an unknown name once a second world folder exists, with `TryGet` for whoever may hold a stale name; `SaveData.Place()` keeps moving the old saves. The alternative keeps the map of Sinnoh as the answer to everything, which hides a wrong warp until someone walks through it.
5. **Where Generation 5 and later come from.** *Recommended:* (a), a reader of the user's own dump with its hash pinned, nothing of it in the repository but the world files; (b) by hand with plan 16 · T9's editor; (c) generated layouts in the region's shape. Taken here, used by plan 22.
6. **Johto's source.** *Recommended:* pret/pokeheartgold through the DS reader `DecompMaps` is, with a pin and the differences plan 20 finds. The alternative is pret/pokecrystal through a third reader for the Game Boy's format, and Gold and Silver's Johto rather than HeartGold's.
7. **The player across regions.** *Recommended:* the same figure everywhere (the look chosen at the start; plan 11 · C9's outfits let the player dress for a region), with the region's two heroes drawn only for the assistant's and the rival's roles. The alternative draws the player as each region's own hero, which changes who the player is at every crossing.
8. **Where another region's trainers live.** *Recommended:* a file per region in its world folder (`Data/world/kanto/trainers.json`), in the record shape of plan 06 · R9's `Data/trainers.json` (`TrainerRecord`, `TrainerPokemonRecord`), written with `--data` by the map importer's reader of that game; Platinum's stays where R9 put it, `TrainerDatabase` reads it and every world folder's, and a placed trainer is looked up in its own region's file, so FireRed's Brock and HeartGold's, under one id, never meet. The alternative is one `Data/trainers.json` for every game, with a region field on each record and ids made distinct where two games share one.
