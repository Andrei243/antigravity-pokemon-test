# Plan 18 · The other regions: the framework, the crossings and the importers

Written 2026-10-06, before any session.

**Goal**: what every region after Sinnoh needs before its own plan (19 · Kanto, 20 · Johto, 21 · Hoenn, 22 · Unova, 23 · the later regions) can start: the engine's Sinnoh-only parts made to belong to a region (the professor and the introduction, the starters, the badges, the regional Pokédex and its habitats, the Trainer Card, the model catalogue, the fallback map), the journey between regions with a rule for what comes along and for the level curve, every regional Pokédex's numbers from PokeAPI, a reader of the GBA decompilations' map format that writes the same world files Sinnoh's maps are made of, and the decision on where the layouts of Generation 5 and later come from. Nothing here opens a region: a region's plan does, on this framework.

## Where we are

- **The chain** is `Data/RegionDatabase.cs`: nine `Region`s in generation order (`Id`, `Name`, `Generation`, `Start`, `Arrival`, `Maps`, `StoryCompleteFlag`, `IsBuilt`, `ArrivalSpot`), eight `RegionLink`s (`From`, `To`, `Transport`, `DepartureMap`), `Transport { Undecided, Boat }`, `CheckTravel` (`TravelCheck.StoryUnfinished`, `DestinationNotBuilt`, `Ready`) and `AttendantLines`, two stand-in lines per outcome. Kanto is the hand-made `PalletTown` and `PalletPlayerHouse`; `StoryState.CompleteRegion` and `IsRegionComplete` read the `<Id>HallOfFame` flag. `GameEngine.TravelTo` is a fade and `player.SetPosition` at the `ArrivalSpot`; a script reaches it through `ScriptScreen.Travel` (`FieldHost` in `GameEngine.Scripts.cs`; `common.Attendant` is `travel` then `sayown`). `RegionTests` holds the order, the links, that every map belongs to one region and that warps never cross one. The README's "Regions" decides the chain and defers each later link to the region that builds it; plan 01 · M12's Town Map, plan 03 · D10's Pokédex, plan 02 · S1's eight badges, S2's field-move gates and fly spots, and plan 04 · G10's introduction are all Sinnoh's.
- **Sinnoh by name**, in code a second region can't use: `Habitats.Sinnoh` reads `world/sinnoh/habitats.json` and nothing else; `PokedexMode { Sinnoh, National }`, `PokemonSpecies.SinnohNumber` (set by `Importer.cs` from the decompilation's `sinnoh_pokedex.json`), `Pokedex.Entries(PokedexMode)`, `Pokedex.Modes`, `CanUnlockNational` (which asks `IsRegionComplete("Sinnoh")`), `PokedexScreen.Mode`; `IntroScreen.Professor = "Prof. Rowan"`, `ShownSpecies = "Buneary"` and six arrays of his lines, the figure drawn as the `ROWAN` character (`CharacterStudio.Render`); the rival's name, which plan 02 · S4 asks in the introduction (`IntroPhase.AskRival`, the `AboutRival` lines), offering `PlayerIdentity.DefaultRivalName = "Barry"` and keeping one name for the whole game (`PlayerIdentity.RivalName`, `SaveData.RivalName`, `{rival}` in lines); `MapDatabase.StartMap = "Sinnoh"`, which `MapDatabase.Get` returns for any name it doesn't know; `WorldModels.Of(name)`, Sinnoh's catalogue of 170 models; `PlayerIdentity.CharacterFor`, which draws `Player` and `Assistant` as PLAYER or DAWN.
- **The save** keeps `Badges` as one `int`, `NationalPokedex` as one `bool` and `Diplomas` as names (`"Sinnoh"`, `"National"`); `StorySnapshot.Badges` is the same `int`; `SaveData.Place()` moves old saves by `WorldVersion`, `StoryMigration` by `StoryVersion` (`StoryState.CurrentVersion` is 2 since plan 02 · S4's `StoryMigration.PastTheOpening`).
- **What belongs to a region already**, the pattern to follow: `World.LoadAll` reads every `Data/world/<folder>/` that has a `world.json` (`WorldIndexFile.Region`) and `MapDatabase.Initialize` builds every world's maps; `AudioManager.Region` is set from `RegionDatabase.RegionOfMap` and `MusicDirector.Resolve(role, region, exists)` looks in `music/<region>/` before `common/`; `EvolutionContext.Region` comes from the same lookup.
- **The importer** reads one game. `tools/MapImporter`'s `DecompMaps.Folders` are Platinum's `res/field/…` paths; `LandData` reads a 32×32 chunk's four sections, `Matrix` the grids, `WorldWriter` writes `WorldChunkFile` (behaviours in hex, `Solid`, `Cover`, `Heights` as plates, `Props`), `WorldMatrixFile`, `WorldAreaFile` (`Warps`, `Objects`, `Signs`, `Triggers`, `Land`, `Water`) and `habitats.json`; `Cover.Rules` sorts tiles by the names of the original's textures. `Sources.cs` pins three commits (`DecompCommit`, `PokeApiCommit`, `ShowdownCommit`); `MapImportTests` builds chunk files byte by byte. In the game `WorldMapBuilder.Build(world, entry)` makes a map of a matrix, `Map.AreaAt` knows one area per chunk, and `MapStructures.BuildingsOf` takes `Map.PlacedBuildings` where the import placed models and flood-fills wall, roof and door tiles (`FindBuildings`) where it didn't.
- **PokeAPI's `pokedexes.csv` and `pokemon_dex_numbers.csv`** are in the pinned checkout's `data/v2/csv/`, which `PokeApi` loads by name; nothing reads them.
- **The level curve** on arriving is named nowhere. Plan 13 · V3's level cap and `ScaleTrainers` are offered to this plan, plan 06 · R10 owes obedience and plan 07 · O5 says it follows "the badges of the region you are in"; plan 07 · O6 has Flat 50 only as a rule of an online room; plan 13 · V11's New Game+ carries the Pokédex and the boxes into a region played again.
- **Generation 5 and later** have no decompilation. The chain reaches Unova to Paldea in `RegionDatabase`, and nothing says where a map of theirs would come from.
- **Where a game begins, and a voyage.** (Written before W10, which changed the first sentence: since 2026-10-17 `RegionDatabase.First` is Sinnoh and a plain NEW GAME begins there, and `--region Kanto` starts the stand-in.) A plain NEW GAME began in `RegionDatabase.First`, the stand-in Kanto, whose attendant can never let anyone leave, since Kanto has no story to finish; Sinnoh is reached only through `--region Sinnoh` (`Program.RegionArgument` sets `GameEngine.NewGameRegion`, which the harness sets for every run). The title asks one question after NEW GAME, which rules (`TitlePhase.ChooseRules`, drawn by `ModernUi.Prompt`; plan 06 · R1), and `StartNewGame(name, look, rules, rival)` takes the answers. Since plan 02 · S4 Sinnoh's `Start` is the player's room (`PlayerHouse2F`, whose `OnEnter` begins the opening) and Sinnoh has no `Arrival`, so its `ArrivalSpot` is that room too. Since plan 01 · M7 Canalave City is open: Sailor Eldritch's `Ferry` (`Data/scripts/canalave_city.txt`) asks and then `warp`s the player to Iron Island, the island's sailor sails them back to the pier (`warp "Sinnoh" 44 750 left`), and `WorldWalk` takes a person's `warp`s as a way through (`WarpsOf`). An overlay can add people of our own (`npcs`, as the lakefront's waiting rival is). A script can ask among up to six answers (`choose`, the `ChoiceBox`). Nothing leads back along the chain: `LinkFrom` gives one way on, and `RegionTests` holds each region's link to the next and the last region's to nothing.

## Design

**A region is a record, and its content a file.** `Region` grows what the engine reads by name today: `Folder` (`sinnoh`: the world and music folders), `Professor` (the name, the look `CharacterStyle.For` draws, the species shown, the introduction's lines), `Starters` (three), `Badges` (names, none to eight), `Gates` (the badge each field move asks for, plan 02 · S2's check), `Spawns` (where Fly lands and Teleport goes back to: S2's spawn locations, on the region's own map), `Dex` (the regional Pokédex it plays by, a PokeAPI identifier: `extended-sinnoh`), `NationalUnlock` (which rule opens the National Pokédex), `Heroes` (the boy and the girl: PLAYER and DAWN), `Rival` (the name the introduction offers for the rival plan 02 · S4 asks about: Sinnoh's Barry, today's `PlayerIdentity.DefaultRivalName`; none where the region's rival is its other hero), `HasStory` (a new game there begins with its story's opening: Sinnoh's since S4, in the player's room with nothing; a region without one, Kanto's stand-in, begins with its first starter and a few items, as `InitializeNewGame` gives a Turtwig today) and `Models` (the catalogue its world's props are looked up in). The chain stays code in `RegionDatabase` (the order, the links, the maps); the content comes from `Data/regions/<id>.json` (`RegionFile` in a new `Data/RegionFiles.cs`, loaded once by `RegionDatabase`), so a region's plan adds a file. Sinnoh's is written from today's constants and `IntroScreen`'s lines word for word, so the introduction plays as it does; Kanto's names Oak, Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle and Kanto's eight badges, with lines of our own on Rowan's beats and Rowan's look standing in for Oak's until plan 11 · C6 and plan 19 give him one. Whoever needs the region asks for the one the player stands in: a new `GameEngine.Here` (`RegionOfMap(currentMap.Name)`), handed to the screens (`StarterSelectScreen`, `TrainerCardInfo`, `PokedexScreen.Open`), never a static. `IntroScreen` runs before any map is loaded, so it is handed the region the new game starts in (`NewGameRegion`, the region chosen at NEW GAME once W10 is there, or `RegionDatabase.First`). The rival belongs to his region: the save keeps a name per region (a new `SaveData.RivalNames`, W4; today's `RivalName` read as the save's region's), `{rival}` names the rival of the region the player is in, and a region reached by a crossing asks for its rival's name in its own opening (a new script command, `namerival`, W4). A script's region is that of its place (`Map.ScriptFileAt`), so `givebadge coal` and `if badge forest` keep their words: the runner resolves the name in the region's list and `ScriptLibrary.Problems` names a badge no region has.

**Badges per region.** `StoryState` keeps a mask per region (`badges: Dictionary<string, int>`; `GiveBadge(region, index)`, `HasBadge`, `BadgeCount(region)`); the `Badge` enum goes, `FieldMoveRules.BadgeFor` asks the region's `Gates` (Sinnoh's written from today's switch, so every gate asks what it asks now), and `BADGE_COUNT`, plan 13 · V3's cap and plan 06 · R10's obedience read the region the player is in. `SaveData.RegionBadges` replaces `Badges`; a `StoryMigration` step at the next `StoryState.CurrentVersion` (plan 02's chapters and plan 08 · P7 add steps too) gives the old `int` to the region of the save's `Place()`. `TrainerCardInfo` carries the region's names and masks; the card shows the region's row and a page per region the player has entered (decision 1).

**One Pokédex, a listing per region.** Seen and caught stay by national number, shared: a species seen in Sinnoh is seen in Kanto's listing if Kanto lists it. `PokedexMode` becomes a dex id (`Pokedex.Entries(string dex)`, `"national"` among them); `Pokedex.Dexes` is the regional dex of every region the player has entered, then National once unlocked; `Diplomas` keeps its names, so the save needs no change there. `CanUnlockNational(story, region)` plays the region's `NationalUnlock`: Sinnoh's (the Hall of Fame and every species of its dex seen, today's rule) and the kinds the other games use (sixty caught, the Hall of Fame alone), each a value of a new `NationalUnlockRule` with a number in the file. `Habitats.Of(region)` reads `world/<folder>/habitats.json`; the AREA page draws the map of the dex's region, and the National listing the region the player stands in, as Platinum shows Sinnoh for everything.

**The crossing.** `RegionLink` gains `Vessel` (our own word for the attendant: ship, ferry, plane) and `Transport` gains `Plane`. The proposal, each link confirmed by its region's plan when it names its departure map:

| Link | Transport | Leaves from | Lands at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kanto to Johto | ship | Vermilion's harbour (today's `PalletTown` pier stands in) | Olivine |
| Johto to Hoenn | ferry | Olivine | Slateport |
| Hoenn to Sinnoh | ship | Slateport | Canalave |
| Sinnoh to Unova | ship | Canalave | Castelia |
| Unova to Kalos | plane | Mistralton | plan 23's |
| Kalos to Alola, Alola to Galar, Galar to Paldea | plane | plan 23's | plan 23's |

The journey itself is a script, `common.Crossing`: the attendant's question, the fade, a dark screen with the vessel's own sound (a horn and an engine from `SoundDesign`, in `SoundBank` and `docs/sound-effects.md`) and one line, then the arrival with the new region's name sign and its music, as `TravelTo` does today. It has the shape of plan 01 · M7's ferry, a person's script that asks and then warps (Sailor Eldritch's `Ferry` to Iron Island), with `TravelTo` for the warp. A first arrival lands on the region's `ArrivalSpot`. A region whose story begins at home leaves `Arrival` unset, so the crossing lands at its `Start` and its opening plays there, as Sinnoh's does in the player's room since plan 02 · S4 (`PlayerHouse2F`'s `OnEnter`), and the new professor's starter comes as the region's story gives it; the table's port is where the vessel docks, and W8 makes it the region's `Harbour`, where every return lands. A region that wants a deck to walk on (HeartGold's ship) builds it as a map of its own and points the link's new `Scene` at it. `RegionTests` requires a departure map with an attendant for every built region whose next region is built (today `TestEveryBuiltRegionWithAWayOnHasAnAttendantAtItsDeparture` checks only a link that names one).

**What comes along: a fresh journey** (decision 2, recommended (a)). Every Pokémon remembers the region it joined the player in (`Pokemon.Region`, saved; plan 07 · O2's met data sits beside it; a Pokémon received by trade joins in the region it arrived in). At the crossing the party is emptied into the boxes and the player lands with none; the new professor gives a starter as the region's story does. Until the region's Hall of Fame, `PCScreen` shows the earlier regions' Pokémon greyed, with the reason, and `Crossing.CanTake(pokemon, region, story)` (`Models/Crossing.cs`, GPU-free) refuses to withdraw them; afterwards everything goes anywhere, and obedience (plan 06 · R10) by the region's badges is the series' own guard. Money, the bag, the Pokédex, the card and the records cross; a region's key items stay in the bag and do nothing elsewhere, because their scripts are the region's; the Bicycle, the rods and an Escape Rope, which plan 02 · S2 made work by the item's own use (`BagScreen.UsedInField`), work wherever the place allows them. None of it touches a game that stays in one region (one begun in Sinnoh, whose way on leads to no built region yet, or one chosen as a region alone at NEW GAME, W10), and Platinum's defaults don't move. The alternatives are (b), the team carried and every region's trainers and wild levels raised by plan 13 · V3's `ScaleTrainers` and cap, which the cap of 100 ends after Hoenn, and (c), a flat 50 for every later region (plan 07 · O6's room rule made a story rule), which could only ever be a choice at NEW GAME (plan 13 · V1's `RunRules`), never a rule of a region: the `Ruleset` of the first general ruling in `docs/mechanics/rulings.md` is Platinum's rules against the modern ones, not levels.

**Harbours both ways** (W8, W9; decision 9). With one way on and none back, a Champion who sails on leaves a region out of reach for good: its rematches, its post-game (plan 19 · K9's Sevii Islands), its habitats for the Pokédex, its Frontier. HeartGold and SoulSilver run the S.S. Aqua and the Magnet Train both ways after the League; here every region's departure becomes a harbour that does the same. A harbour opens once its own region's Hall of Fame is set (decision 9 (a)), and then sails on (the chain's way, or W10's `Next`) and back to every other region the player has entered (a story flag per region, `<Id>Entered` beside `<Id>HallOfFame`, set by `StartNewGame` and `TravelTo`): a new `RegionDatabase.LinksFrom(region, story)` lists them, the way on first. Nobody leaves a region before its Hall of Fame, so every way back lands in a finished region. `Crossing.Depart` sorts the party by the destination: whoever `Crossing.CanTake` allows there stays, the rest go into the boxes, which is W4's fresh journey on a first arrival (nobody stays) and the whole team on a way back. A return lands on the region's new `Harbour`, the `MapSpot` beside its attendant; the voyage is the harbour's own vessel (its region's link's `Transport` and `Vessel`) with W5's dark screen. A harbour asks where with the field's `ChoiceBox` (style guide, "Questions in the field": six answers at most, so a harbour with more than five destinations asks in two steps, the way on first and OTHER REGIONS for the rest), and `common.Crossing` sails there. A place with a voyage inside its region too (Eldritch's boat on the pier Sinnoh's harbour shares, plan 21's Lilycove with the Frontier's ferry) asks its own question first in the place's script, as `Ferry` does, and calls `common.Attendant` for the regions; a voyage inside a region stays a warp, as plan 20 · J15's S.S. Aqua does. Sinnoh's harbour is Canalave's pier: an attendant of our own in `overlays/canalave_city.json`'s `npcs`, shown by `SinnohHallOfFame`, so Platinum's Canalave is unchanged until the post-game. Fly crosses too (W9), between two regions entered and finished, to W1's `Spawns`. A game of one region alone (W10) has no harbour at all.

**Where a journey begins** (W10; decisions 10 and 11, answered 2026-10-12). Every NEW GAME begins in Sinnoh, the region the project is named for, and nothing asks about it: no title phase, no argument of `StartNewGame`, no field of the save. `RegionDatabase` orders the chain with Sinnoh first and the others in generation order after it (Sinnoh, Kanto, Johto, Hoenn, Unova, Kalos, Alola, Galar, Paldea), each linked to the next and the last to nothing; `RegionDatabase.First` is therefore Sinnoh and `GameEngine.NewGameRegion` (from `--region`, the harness and the tests) is the only way to begin elsewhere. A game that finishes Sinnoh sails on to Kanto, whose stand-in is built, and so on down the chain; nothing leads back by harbour (W8 is left out) and Fly is the way back (W9). The questions the first draft of this session carried, which regions to offer, the whole journey or one region alone, `RegionStart`, `SaveData.StartRegion` and `OneRegion`, `TravelCheck.NotThisJourney` and the chain going round (decision 11), went with the user's answer: there is no one-region game and no ring from Paldea to Kanto.

**The GBA reader** (`tools/MapImporter/Gba/`, beside `DecompMaps`, sharing nothing but `WorldWriter`'s files). It reads pinned commits of pret/pokeemerald (Hoenn) and pret/pokefirered (Kanto), the fourth and fifth repositories of `Sources.cs`, each file added to the importer's README's table of what is read ("What is read, and what is not"): `data/maps/map_groups.json` and each `data/maps/<Map>/map.json` (its layout, music, weather, map type, region map section, the cycling, running and escape flags, `connections` with their offsets, the four event lists), `data/layouts/layouts.json` with each layout's `map.bin` (sixteen bits a block: ten of metatile id, two of collision, four of elevation), each tileset's `metatile_attributes.bin` (the `MB_*` behaviour in its low bits; two bytes a metatile in Emerald, four in FireRed), `include/constants/metatile_behaviors.h` and `metatile_labels.h`, `src/data/wild_encounters.json`, the trainers and their parties (`src/data/trainers.h` and `trainer_parties.h`, or the `trainers.party` text newer commits keep: the pin decides), the heal locations, the region map's section names, the item balls' `finditem` lines and the hidden items' entries. Never `tiles.png`, a palette or a tileset's graphics: the folder list can't fetch them, and no vertex, texture or palette reaches the repository.

- **Behaviours**: `GbaBehaviours.cs` maps every `MB_*` onto a `TileBehavior` (`MB_TALL_GRASS` to `TallGrass`, `MB_LONG_GRASS` to `VeryTallGrass`, `MB_POND_WATER` to `River`, `MB_OCEAN_WATER` to `Sea`, `MB_WATERFALL`, `MB_ICE`, `MB_JUMP_EAST` to `LedgeEast`, the doors to `Door`, the arrow warps to the `Exit` values, `MB_PC` to `Computer`, `MB_COUNTER`, the bookshelves) and gives what Platinum has no value for (underwater, the secret bases, the rails, the Cycling Road's pull) a value of its own among the ones the original leaves unused at the top of its byte, `0xED` to `0xFE` (`0xEA` to `0xEC` are Platinum's own shelves, which `TileBehavior` doesn't name yet), with a row in `TileBehaviors.InGame`; `MB_JUMP_NORTH` becomes Platinum's own jump north, `0x3A`, named then. Collision bits set is the blocked flag. `docs/tile-behaviours.md` gains a section the importer writes, "The GBA's behaviours": each `MB_*`, the value it becomes, and its count on each game's maps.
- **Heights**: a flat plate per run of tiles of one elevation, 1.4 tiles a level above elevation 3, so a straight step over a level's edge is 1.4 (over `FieldMovement.StepLimit`: a cliff for the walker and a face for `Relief`), and a tile of elevation 0 (the GBA's "any level": stairs, a ledge's foot, a cave mouth) gets a sloping plate whose halfway point is 0.7 from either side, under `Relief.WeldLimit`, so it walks and draws as a slope; elevation 1, the water's at every level, takes the plate of the nearest land, so a shore is a level step for surfing out and landing; elevation 15 (a bridge, on both levels) is a deck plate over the ground's, as Sinnoh's `DeckAt` reads.
- **Cover**: `GbaCover.cs` in `Cover.cs`'s order: the behaviour where it shows, then the metatile's label where `metatile_labels.h` names it, then a table of metatile ranges per tileset written by hand in the region's session, judged from the importer's behaviour picture and the user's memory of the place (plan 01 · M4's decision 8: no picture of the original is kept); what nothing names is `Unknown`, listed per tileset with counts in the report, Sinnoh's 99.5% the bar. A roof, a wall or a door metatile is `Building` cover, which `WorldMapBuilder.Look` already makes a solid wall.
- **Connections** become matrices: from the region's first town at the origin, every map a `connection` reaches is placed at its offset; a set no connection reaches (the Sevii Islands, Hoenn's underwater) is a matrix of its own, joined by warps, and `dive` connections are written as warps; the grid is padded to 32 and cut into chunks. A map isn't chunk-aligned, so `WorldChunkFile` gains an optional `Areas` list (one character a tile, indexing the matrix's areas) that `WorldMapBuilder` reads into a per-tile grid behind `Map.AreaAt`; Sinnoh's chunks go on without it. Two connections that disagree are reported and the first placed wins.
- **Events**: `object_events` to `AreaObject` (the graphics id as `Looks`, the movement type, the range, trainer type and sight, the hiding flag, the script label; an item ball's item from its script's `finditem`), `warp_events` to `AreaWarp`, `coord_events` to `AreaTrigger` (its `var` and `var_value`), `bg_events` to `AreaSign` (a sign's script; a hidden item's item, count and flag). `wild_encounters.json`'s land and water tables have Platinum's own twelve and five slots with the same weights, so they fit `WorldAreaFile.Land` and `Water` as they are; the three rods go to the area file's `OldRod`, `GoodRod` and `SuperRod`, which plan 02 · S2's fishing reads (the GBA's Old Rod has two slots and its Good Rod three, at weights of their own, which the area file gains a place for; its Super Rod's five are Platinum's), Rock Smash to a new `RockSmash` list (Platinum's rocks hide no Pokémon, so plan 06 · R13 has no roll for it: the region plan that opens the first such rock adds the roll), and both to `habitats.json`. The trainers and their parties go to the region's trainer file in the record shape of plan 06 · R9's `Data/trainers.json` (`TrainerRecord` and `TrainerPokemonRecord` in `Data/TrainerDatabase.cs`; decision 8), with what that game works out for each Pokémon (its personality, its IVs), and `TrainerDatabase.Fill` gives a placed trainer its team as it gives Sinnoh's; an overlay's `trainer` block carries the lines alone, as plan 08 · P7 leaves Sinnoh's. The heal locations are the region's `Spawns`.
- **Buildings and rooms**: a GBA building is tiles, so `MapStructures.FindBuildings`'s flood fill finds it, its kind from the door's destination map (`_PokemonCenter_1F`, `_Mart`, `_Gym`), its storeys from the footprint's depth, its style from the area (`Architecture` grows a region's towns in that region's plan). Every interior is a layout of its own: the reader writes each as a matrix with a new `MapSetting.Indoors`, and `WorldMapBuilder` builds a room from it with `IsIndoors` set and the furniture its behaviours name (`Computer`, `Counter`, the bookshelves, `Television`) from `PropModels`, so a GBA region's rooms need no hand-made files where Sinnoh's wait for plan 01 · M11.
- **The command**: `--game emerald|firered` reads that pin into `out/<region>/` (a mosaic by cover and by behaviour, every map's picture, `report.md`), and with `--data` writes `Data/world/<folder>/` by its `world.json`, as Sinnoh's does. Johto is plan 20's question (decision 6): pret/pokeheartgold keeps the DS layout `DecompMaps` already reads.

**Generation 5 and later** (decision 5). No public decompilation exists, and three ways remain: (a) a reader of the user's own dump of Black 2 (and of the 3DS games for Kalos and Alola), written against the community's documentation of the DS and 3DS containers, the dump never in the repository, its hash pinned in `Sources.cs` so a run against another says so, and only the world files checked in, as Sinnoh's are; (b) hand-authoring with plan 16 · T9's editor, from memory, which plan 01's decision 1 keeps for fixes alone; (c) a route-and-city graph with layouts generated in the region's shape, which would be ours and not the place. The rule holds in each: layouts and numbers, never a tile's graphics. Galar and Paldea have no tile grid at all, which is plan 23's own question. ## Decisions for the user

1. **The Trainer Card.** *Recommended:* the region's badges on the front and a page per region entered. The alternative is every region's row on one card, forty slots most of which are empty for most of the game.
2. **What crosses with the player.** *Recommended:* (a), a fresh journey: the party into the boxes, the earlier regions' Pokémon resting until the new Hall of Fame, money, bag, Pokédex, card and badges along. The alternatives: (b) the team carried and plan 13 · V3's scaling and cap over every region, which ends at level 100 after Hoenn; (c) a flat 50 for later regions, only ever as a choice at NEW GAME (plan 13 · V1).
3. **One Pokédex or one per region.** *Recommended:* one, with a listing per region and the National one; seen and caught are the player's. The alternative is a Pokédex handed over anew in each region with its own seen and caught, as the games have it, which loses the National count across the chain.
4. **`MapDatabase.Get`'s fallback.** *Recommended:* throw for an unknown name once a second world folder exists, with `TryGet` for whoever may hold a stale name; `SaveData.Place()` keeps moving the old saves. The alternative keeps the map of Sinnoh as the answer to everything, which hides a wrong warp until someone walks through it.

7. **The player across regions.** *Recommended:* the same figure everywhere (the look chosen at the start; plan 11 · C9's outfits let the player dress for a region), with the region's two heroes drawn only for the assistant's and the rival's roles. 9. **Two-way travel** (W8, W9).
