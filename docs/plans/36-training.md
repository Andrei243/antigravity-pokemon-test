# Plan 36 · Training: effort, the tools and powers

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: making a team the way the later games let a player make one, in every region of the journey. A training hall of ours stands in each region, with small games that train a Pokémon's effort in the stat chosen (X and Y's Super Training as a mechanic) and a screen that shows effort at all, which nothing does today. The later games' training tools (mints, Bottle Caps for peak training, the Ability Capsule and Patch, Exp. Candies, Tera Shards and a keeper who changes a Tera Type) are sold, won and found in every region instead of nowhere. Hidden abilities are handed down by the Day Care as the modern rules hand them down, beside the sources the game has. And powers: timed boosts to EXP, effort, prize money, prices, catching, encounters and hatching (O-Powers and Roto Powers as a mechanic), learned in the halls, paid from a charge that fills as the player walks, with a free one each day. The rules of the tools are built (plan 06 · R28b); this plan gives them places, people and screens, and owns the powers system that plan 39's meals and others will grant through.

## Design

- **The other regions** (TR4): each hall behind a door of its overlay's `locked`, chosen after the house has been looked at from outside; where a region has none, the hall's four people stand in a corner of its department store (Celadon, Goldenrod, Lilycove, Shopping Mall Nine). Kalos and Alola take theirs with plan 23 if their cities come after TR4; Galar and Paldea with plans 33 and 34, as a grid room behind a building of theirs. Hisui, Sinnoh's past, has none.
- **What opens when** (decision 1): the drills and the coach's reset from the first visit to a hall; the tools, peak training, the teacher and the keeper once any region's Hall of Fame is entered. Sinnoh's story keeps its pace, and every later region has them on arrival.
- **The script command `train`** (an `Op` at the enum's end, both hosts, `ScriptTests.EveryCommand`, `docs/scripts.md`), on the Pokémon the last `choosepokemon` chose: `open` (RESULT 1 once a Hall of Fame is entered), `drill` (the drill screen; RESULT the grade), `reset <stat>|all`, `peak` (TR3), `tera` (TR5) and `prize` (what the records have earned). The halls' people run common scripts (`common.TrainingCoach`, `TrainingClerk`, `TrainingTeacher`, `TeraKeeper`), so a region's hall is a room, a door and four people.

### Effort drills

- **Three small games of ours, each training two stats** (`Models/TrainingDrills.cs`, GPU-free, on the Pokéathlon's shape: thirty frames a second, `Step(AthlonInput)`, `Advance(dt, input)` in whole frames only, a pure function of its seed and the presses, an `Autopilot` for tests and the harness), each half a minute:
  - *the throw* (Attack, or Sp. Atk as a shot of energy): a target slides along the back wall and shrinks as the drill goes; the aim moves left and right and a press lets a shot fly, which lands a moment later; the middle scores three, the ring two, the board one;
  - *the guard* (Defense, or Sp. Def against pellets of energy): pellets come down three lanes; the guard moves into a lane and a press raises it as a pellet arrives; on time scores two, late one, and a miss stuns for a moment;
  - *the dash* (Speed as a sprint of bursts, HP as a long run with stamina): presses in rhythm keep the pace, a held press jumps a hurdle, a stumble costs pace or stamina.
- **Grades and effort**: bronze, silver and gold by score bands of ours, set so the autopilot earns silver and gold wants care (the Pokéathlon's medals' way). `EffortDrills.Award(pokemon, stat, grade, rules)` puts 8, 16 or 24 points into the stat (decision 3): plain points, with no Pokérus, Power item or Macho Brace, cut by the rules' limits, and the stats reckoned at once (battle effort waits for a level, as Platinum's does; a drill's shows on the screen that gave it, a ruling). `WhyNot` refuses an Egg, a stat at its limit and a full 510. Drills are free and as often as wanted, and the Pokémon's own stats change nothing in them, so any Pokémon trains anything.
- **The screen** (`UI/TrainingScreen.cs`, `GameState.Training`, logic in methods with no input: `Open(pokemon)`, `Move`, `Confirm`, `Cancel`, `Advance(dt)`, `Play` for tools; drawn by `ModernUi.Training.cs` from a new section of the style guide, "Training", after "The Pokéathlon", written first): the stat, the drill's briefing, a count of three, the drill in 2D with the Pokémon as its field sprite, the grade, and the six bars before and after.
- **Records and prizes**: `TrainingRecords` (each drill and stat's best, the golds counted, the prizes given) saved as `SaveData.Training`, null with none, so no save version is raised. The prizes are a table of ours, `Data/training.json`'s `drillPrizes`: a stat's first gold gives its vitamin; once the tools are open every tenth gold a Bottle Cap and every fiftieth a Gold Bottle Cap; with a Tera Orb in the bag every fifth a shard of the Pokémon's own first type (TR5).
- **The coach's reset** takes one stat's effort or all six to nought, free (X and Y's Reset Bag as a service); the berries that lower effort and the Fresh Start Mochi stay as they are.

### Powers

- **The rule** (`Models/Powers.cs`, GPU-free): `PowerKind` (`Exp`, `Effort`, `Money`, `Bargain`, `Catch`, `Encounter`, `Stealth`, `Hatch`; a new kind is a value at the end, a row of `Data/powers.json` and its one reader), a level of one to three, a length in seconds of play, and `PowerState` (the levels learned, the charge, what is in force, the day the last free one was drawn), saved as `SaveData.Powers`, null with none. `Use(kind, level)` pays the level from the charge. `Grant(kind, level, seconds)` is the one door every other source goes through (the free one, a script, plan 39's meals). One of each kind is in force at once, a new one replacing the old, and Encounter and Stealth put each other out. Lengths count down from the play time summed from `dt`, never `WallClock`, so the harness repeats and a paused game loses nothing. `WhyNot` gives the reason one can't be used: nothing learned, too little charge, the run's NO POWERS.
- **The numbers** are ours, a table in `Data/powers.json` and the rulings, starting from the old games' (EXP ×1.2, ×1.5, ×2; prize money ×1.5, ×2, ×3; prices 90%, 75%, 50%): ten minutes of play at every level; a charge of ten points that fills a point every 200 steps (`GameEngine.OnStep`); a power costs its level.
- **One reader each**:
  - `Exp` in `BattleCore.Give`, after affection's factor and before the crossed region's, and `Effort` doubling each foe's yield there, through `CoreSetup.Powers` (a `PowerSet` of plain levels); `Money` in `BattleCore.PrizeMoney`; `Catch` on the species' rate in `CatchCalculator.Shakes`, through `BattleConditions.Powers`. All four are fixed as the battle begins, last to its end and are kept by `BattleRecord` and the Vs. Recorder's file (`BattleRecordFile`), so a replay gives the same log. The run's cap still holds: `LevelCaps.Allowed` comes after every factor.
  - `Encounter` and `Stealth` add to `EncounterSteps.Meets`' share beside the radio's, through `EncounterMoment`; `Hatch` adds cycles in `Breeding.CyclesPerCycle`; `Bargain` is read by `ShopScreen.PriceOf`, set as the shop opens as Easy's prices are.
- **In hand**: the Power Watch, a key item of ours (`Overrides/items.json`, an id from the same free block, a field use of its own, registrable on the item button), given by the first teacher, opens the panel (`UI/PowersScreen.cs`, `GameState.Powers`, `ModernUi.Powers.cs`): the kinds learned with their levels, the charge, today's free power, and what is in force with its minutes. Chips of ours stand over the field (a kind's icon and its minutes) and a toast says when one wears off (the style guide's "Menu screens" and "Field menus and notices" first). A key item rather than a START entry, since the start menu is already crowded in Johto and Hoenn with the Pokégear and the PokéNav.
- **Sources** (decision 5): each hall's teacher teaches the kinds of its region (Sinnoh's `Exp`, `Money`, `Encounter` and `Stealth`; Kanto's `Catch`; Johto's `Hatch`; Hoenn's `Bargain`; Unova's `Effort`; `powers.json`'s `teachers`) at a level by the Halls of Fame entered (one with one, two with two, three with four: ours), through `power learn`, a second `Op`. Once a game day the panel offers a free power among those learned, at level one or two, drawn from the day's number (`SpecialEncounters.DailyNumber`) through `Core/SplitMix.cs`, never `Dice`, so no other draw moves. Anything else grants through `Grant`; a script with `power grant <kind> <level> <minutes>`.
- **The run** (plan 13): a NO POWERS restriction (`RunRules.NoPowers`, a `RunRow` with its words, a case in `RunPanel.Change`), off unless chosen and refused at the panel alone.

## Risks

- **Free effort and cheap tools change the balance.** Drills are free and unlimited, but held to 252 and 510; the tools, peak training and powers wait for a Hall of Fame (decision 1); the EXP power comes before the run's cap and a crossed region's ceiling, which still hold; a run can bar powers. The numbers are tables a session can turn down.
- **A battle is a turn ahead.** Powers that change a battle are fixed as it begins and kept by its record and file, so a replay and the Vs. Recorder agree, and one that wears off mid-battle lasts to its end. A new field of `CoreSetup` or `BattleConditions` is a field of the file too (CLAUDE.md's rule).
- **Two clocks.** Powers count play time from `dt`; Poké Jobs keep the wall clock (plan 15). The two never meet, and the harness fixes both.
- **Draws that move.** Today's power is drawn from the day's number without `Dice`; the modern Day Care's draws come after Platinum's, so a Platinum Egg is unchanged, but Eggs under the modern rules change and the `daycare` mode's shots after an Egg move (accept them by `diff`).
- **A door may hide a poor house.** A house the original never used may be drawn badly from outside; TR4 looks at each before it opens it and falls back to the department store. Sinnoh's hall is new to Platinum's world, but behind a door the original left unused, changing none of its places or story.
- **Powers move odds that Platinum's rules keep exact.** Plan 10's second check of F8 kept timed boosts out for that reason (plan 06 · decision 5 and ruling 5). They are read only where a game hands a battle or a step its `PowerSet`, which is every game played by the modern rules and never a battle given Platinum's, so `battles-platinum.txt`, the oracles and every test that makes its own battle see no power; the Power Watch and the teachers appear only in a game by the modern rules.
- **Several plans want powers.** Plan 39's meals, plan 41's plaza, plan 43's phone and plan 38's places all grant or show them; one door (`Grant`) and a kind at the enum's end with its one reader keep them from building second timers. Plan 10 · F8's `MealPowers` is replaced by this, should F8 ever run.
- **Items owed elsewhere.** The obtainability test names the plan that owes each item it excuses, and that plan's session empties the line.

## Decisions for the user

1. **What the halls open before a Hall of Fame.** *Recommended:* the drills and the reset from the first visit, so Sinnoh's hall is a place to train from the tenth chapter, and the tools, peak training, the teacher and the keeper once any region's Hall of Fame is entered. The alternatives are the whole hall after the Hall of Fame only, or all of it from the start, as X and Y gave Super Training.
2. **The later games' tools before their own regions.** *Recommended:* every hall carries every tool from the first Hall of Fame on, since the carried team needs them in every region and the regions that brought them (plans 33 and 34) are far off; those plans still place the originals' own sources. The alternative keeps each tool to the region of its games, so none can be had until plans 23, 33 and 34.
3. **How much effort a drill gives.** *Recommended:* 8, 16 and 24 points for bronze, silver and gold, a stat full in about eleven golds (five or six minutes). The alternative is X and Y's 4, 8 and 12, twice as many drills.
4. **Where the halls stand.** *Recommended:* a hall of ours in each region behind a door the original left unused (Sinnoh's in Sunyshore City, door 7), its people in the department store where no door is free. The alternative is one hall only, in Sunyshore City, reached by Fly from every region.
5. **Powers' gate and price.** *Recommended:* learned from the halls' teachers after a Hall of Fame, each use paid from a charge that fills by walking, ten minutes of play each, and a free one a day. The alternatives are powers from the start of Sinnoh, as X and Y gave theirs, or no charge and each power once a day.
6. **The Tera keeper.** *Recommended:* in every hall once the bag holds a Tera Orb, at fifty shards of the type, with Paldea's own placed by plan 34. The alternative is a keeper in Paldea only.

7. **How the later machines are numbered.** *Recommended:* machines of ours in a new block of ids (9800 and up; ours run to 9571 today, plan 35 takes the 9600s and TR5 and TR8 the 9700s), named by their moves' order in the games they come from (`TM93` and on for Scarlet and Violet's), so the TMs pocket reads on from Platinum's 92. The alternative gives them no numbers and lists them by their move's name only, which breaks the bag's TM01 style of ordering. *Taken by TR9 (2026-11-07) at its recommended answer*: 212 machines, TM93 to TM304, ids 10000 to 10211 (`MoveTeaching.LaterFrom` 10000, `LaterTo` 10399): two hundred and more don't fit in the hundred of 9800, and plan 39 · CR1's Crafting Kit had taken 9900 by the time TR9 reached main.
