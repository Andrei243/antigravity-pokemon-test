# Plan 20 · Johto

Written 2026-10-06, before any session.

**Goal**: Johto from HeartGold and SoulSilver, read from pret/pokeheartgold with the same importer and into the same world files as Platinum's Sinnoh: its map opened stretch by stretch with its people, trainers, wild Pokémon and music, its story from New Bark Town to the Indigo Plateau in our own words on HGSS's beats, the Pokégear with its phone, map card and radio, Apricorns and Kurt's seven balls, Whirlpool and Headbutt, the Safari Zone, and the venues plan 15 wrote the Bug-Catching Contest and the Pokéathlon for, placed where they belong. Johto is the second region of the chain (README, "Regions"): a Kanto champion arrives with an empty party and Elm's starter, and `JohtoHallOfFame` opens the way to Hoenn. Nothing of it touches a game played in Sinnoh, so Platinum's defaults don't move.

## Design

**The importer reads a second DS game.** The paths and shapes that differ become a `DsGame` record in `tools/MapImporter/DsGame.cs` with two instances, `Platinum` (today's `DecompMaps.Folders` and readers) and `HeartGold` (the folders above, pinned in `Sources.cs` as `HeartGoldRepo`, `HeartGoldCommit` and `HeartGoldFolders`, the archive fetched whole since a sparse checkout can't take half a file); `DecompMaps` takes one. New readers beside it: `Narc.cs` (the archive's allocation table and its members, nothing else), `Matrix.ParseBinary` (`src/map_matrix.c`'s layout), `MapTable.cs` for `mapname.bin` with `maps.h`'s names as keys (`new_bark_town`, `route_29`, as `WorldWriter.KeyOf` makes Sinnoh's), `AreaEvents.ParseHgss` for the events' JSON, `HgssEncounters.cs` for the one encounter file, `HgssTrainers.cs` for `trainers.json` (class, AI flags, items, species, levels, moves and whether two fight at once, into plan 06 · R9's `TrainerRecord` shape; never the `messages`), `Phonebook.cs` for `pmtel_book.json`. `LandData.Parse` is shared once J1 confirms the member format, as `TerrainModel.Read` is. `Cover` keeps two rule lists, Platinum's and HGSS's, chosen by the game. The command is plan 18 · W6's: `--game heartgold` reads the pin into `out/johto/` (the mosaics, every area's picture, `report.md` with the unknown textures and the models the catalogue doesn't know) and `--data` writes `Data/world/johto/` by its `world.json`. What never leaves the cache is what never left it for Sinnoh: no vertex, texture, palette, sound or line of text; of a model its name and its box, of a chunk's terrain the names of its textures and where their polygons lie. Kanto's headers (`regionNo` 1) are read and drawn in the pictures but written into Johto's data only for the keys `world.json` names, so Johto's files hold Johto: Routes 26 and 27, Victory Road and the Indigo Plateau are opened by key whatever bit their headers carry.

**Johto in the game.** `Data/world/johto/world.json` says `"region": "Johto"`, lists `Johto` (the overworld matrix) and each place that is a matrix of its own (Sprout Tower's floors, Union Cave, Ilex Forest, the Burned Tower, the Bell Tower, the Whirl Islands, Mt. Mortar, the Ice Path, the Dragon's Den, Victory Road, with `"setting": "Cave"` where rock stands up), and its open areas; HGSS's rooms are matrices too, so they come through the import as plan 18 · W7 builds a GBA room (`MapSetting.Indoors`, the furniture by model name), and Johto needs no hand-made room files where Sinnoh's wait for plan 01 · M11. `RegionDatabase` gains Johto's `Start` and `Arrival` (New Bark Town; decision 1) and its `Maps`; `Data/regions/johto.json` (W1's record) names Elm, Chikorita, Cyndaquil and Totodile, the Zephyr, Hive, Plain, Fog, Storm, Mineral, Glacier and Rising Badges with HGSS's gates, Johto's fly spots, the `updated-johto` dex, Ethan and Lyra as the heroes and the Johto catalogue. `WorldModels` gains `Data/WorldModels.Johto.cs`: HGSS's model names with what each is here (Sprout Tower, the Bell Tower, the Lighthouse, the Radio Tower, the Magnet Train station, the Ruins, the Dance Theater, the Day Care, the Game Corner, the Goldenrod department store), and `Architecture` Johto's towns (New Bark's cottages, Violet's and Ecruteak's old timber under tiled roofs, Goldenrod's city blocks, Olivine's harbour fronts, Azalea's and Mahogany's wood, Cianwood's stone, Blackthorn's dragon-town), each a row of `BuildingArt.StyleOf` and the style guide's "Buildings" before the code. Overlays carry our music, doors, people and lines as Sinnoh's do; a trainer's mind, items and team come from `HgssTrainers` into Johto's own trainer file, `Data/world/johto/trainers.json`, in the record shape of plan 06 · R9's `Data/trainers.json` (plan 18's decision 8), which `TrainerDatabase.Fill` gives a placed trainer as it gives Sinnoh's, so an overlay's `trainer` block carries the lines alone (plan 08 · P7). Music is `Data/music/johto/` on plan 05 · A6's pattern, the role of each area read from its header's `dayMusicId` and `nightMusicId` through `sndseq.h`'s names, and `AudioManager.Region` is "Johto" wherever `RegionOfMap` says so.

**The time of day's wild Pokémon.** HGSS gives every slot three species. `AreaEncounter` gains `Day` and `Night` (left out where equal to `Species`, so Sinnoh's files don't change), `MapArea.WildEncounters` keeps the slots, and `WildEncounterRules.Slot` picks the species of the hour (`Habitats.GrassAt(GameClock.Now)`'s mapping), which is the part of plan 06 · R13 both regions need and Sinnoh's day and night pairs can take as R13 writes them. The `hoenn` and `sinnoh` sound slots are kept on the area (`Sounds`) for the radio; the rods go to the area file's `OldRod`, `GoodRod` and `SuperRod`, which plan 02 · S2's fishing reads already, `rock_smash` beside `Water` for R13 to read, and both to `habitats.json` as Sinnoh's do.

**The Pokégear** is an entry of the start menu, shown once `FLAG_GOT_POKEGEAR` is set and only in Johto (HGSS's flags keep their names: `FLAG_GOT_RADIO_CARD`, `FLAG_GOT_EXPN_CARD`); in any other region it is nothing, as plan 18 · W4 keeps a region's key items doing nothing elsewhere, so a Sinnoh game never sees it. Its rules are `Models/Pokegear.cs`, GPU-free, saved as `SaveData.Pokegear`: the cards held, the contacts registered, the call waiting, the station tuned.
- **The phone**: contacts from `Data/world/johto/phone.json`, which the importer writes from the phone book as numbers (the caller's class, trainer, place, gift, rematch weekday and time of day) and whose lines are ours in `Data/scripts/phone.txt`, a script per caller run with the field under it as a talk is (`Op.Call` from a `call` command a trainer's own script uses to register, and `Pokegear.Place(contact)` from the screen). A trainer whose weekday and hour have come offers a rematch on the call and waits on their map with their next team (`PhoneContact.Rematches`, the trainer's later entries); `Trainer` gains `RematchStage`, which plan 06 · R12's Vs. Seeker can count up for Sinnoh's trainers the same way, the phone being Johto's door to the one rule. Incoming calls are a roll of `Dice` on a step in `GameEngine.OnStep`, only with no script running and the field quiet, and never on a map whose header forbids them. The weekday is plan 06 · R14's calendar (`GameClock` gains it there; until then every weekday is today, a row in `docs/mechanics/rulings.md`).
- **The map card** is a `ModernUi.RegionMap` drawn from `WorldHabitatsFile.Map` (one cell a chunk) with the areas placed by their headers' `worldMapX` and `worldMapY`, the player's chunk marked and a cursor that names the area; the same component is what plan 01 · M12's Town Map can draw Sinnoh with, and what plan 02 · S2's Fly map (`UI/FlyScreen.cs`, Sinnoh's today) can draw Johto with once Johto's towns are its region's `Spawns` (plan 18 · W1).
- **The radio** is a `RadioDial` (GPU-free: a frequency from 0 to 100, stations at HGSS's positions, received only where the header's `radioSignal` is set, the Unown station in the Ruins of Alph alone, Team Rocket's and Mahogany's during the chapters that have them). The Pokémon Music Channel plays our own songs over the field's theme (`Data/music/johto/radio_march.mml` and `radio_lullaby.mml`, through `AudioManager.PlayMusic(songId)`; `GameEngine.EnterArea` leaves the area's theme alone while the radio is on and brings it back when it is turned off). The March raises and the Lullaby lowers the encounter rate (`WildEncounterRules.Rate` gains the radio, with the decompilation's own numbers, read from its wild-encounter code when written), and the Hoenn and Sinnoh Sounds put the area's `Sounds` species into the slots the original gives them on their weekdays, so they wait for R14 too. The talk stations (Oak's Pokémon Talk about a species the Pokédex has seen, Buena's Password, the Places and People, the Serial Radio Drama) are lines of our own in `Data/world/johto/radio.json`, picked with `Dice`, shown in a strip under the dial; GB Sounds is decision 8.

**Apricorns and Kurt** (`Models/Apricorns.cs`, GPU-free). The 24 Johto trees are props (`PropType.ApricornTree`, placed by HGSS's tree model through the catalogue; the style guide's "Props" first) whose colours stand in `Data/world/johto/apricorns.json`, written by hand from the decompilation's table. A tree fruits once a day (`SaveData.ApricornTrees`: the calendar day each was last picked; plan 15 · E3's `WallClock` gives the date until R14's calendar does). What is picked goes into the Apricorn Box, a key item of `Overrides/items.json` with a screen of its own (`UI/ApricornBoxScreen.cs` on `ModernUi.Lists`: seven colours and their counts, `SaveData.Apricorns`), opened from the bag the way plan 02 · S2 opens the Bicycle and the rods: a `fieldUse` of its own in `Overrides/items.json`, listed by `BagScreen.UsedInField` and handed to the game by `TakeFieldUse`, which opens the box's screen; plan 06 · R11 takes the other key items the same way. Kurt is a script in `azalea_town.txt` with a new `apricorns` command (`Op.Apricorns`, `ScriptScreen.Apricorns`: the box opened to choose a colour and how many); the balls are ready the next calendar day, one order at a time (`VAR_KURT_ORDER`, `VAR_KURT_DAY`, our own). Aprijuice is plan 15's own on this box.

**The seven balls** are entries of `Overrides/items.json` (pocket `PokeBalls`, `CatchPokemon`, `PokeBall`), each a line of `BallTenths` by HGSS's numbers from the decompilation's catch code (under `src/battle/`), read at write time and held by `FormulaTests`: the Fast Ball 40 tenths for a base Speed of 100 or more; the Level Ball 80, 40 or 20 by the thrower's level against the target's; the Lure Ball 30 when fishing; the Moon Ball 40 for a species with a Moon Stone evolution (`EvolutionData`'s item); the Love Ball 80 for the same species of the other gender; the Friend Ball 10, with friendship set to 200 on the catch; the Heavy Ball an addition to the species' rate by weight, which `CatchCalculator.Shakes` applies before `Formulas.CatchRate` through a new `Formulas.BallBonus`. Two of them read the thrower's Pokémon, so `BallTenths` gains a `user`, and the Lure Ball reads a new `BattleConditions.Fishing`, set for a battle a rod's bite began (plan 02 · S2's `FishingAttempt`, which `GameEngine.UpdateFishing` turns into a wild battle). Their looks are seven rows of `BattleBall.LookOf` with marks of their own (the style guide's "Poké Ball (G8)" first). Plan 06 · R28 keeps the Sport and Park Balls and the later games' rest.

**Whirlpool and Headbutt.** HGSS's behaviours are mapped by name onto `TileBehavior` as plan 18 · W6 maps the GBA's, and the two Platinum has no value for get values above `0xE5` with rows in `TileBehaviors.InGame`: a whirlpool is a water tile, drawn as a ring on the water (the style guide's "Water"), that blocks a surfer until the party knows Whirlpool (`FieldMoves.Whirlpool`, `FieldMovement.Step` giving `Obstacle.Whirlpool`), and is crossed the way plan 02 · S2 climbs a waterfall: facing it runs a common script that asks, and the party menu offers it (`FieldMove.Whirlpool` beside S2's fifteen, with its check in `FieldMoveRules` and its gate in Johto's `Gates`); a headbutt tree is a tree the player can face and shake (`common.Headbutt`), with the area's headbutt table from the decompilation where the pin keeps one as data, else written by hand as numbers.

**The story** is plan 02's shape: a chapter writes `Data/scripts/<area key>.txt` on HGSS's beats in our own words, gives the overlay's people their scripts and the triggers theirs, sets what hides whom in `common.NewGame` (and in `common.Crossing`'s arrival for a game that comes from Kanto), and plays every way through it with `HeadlessScriptHost`. Flags keep HGSS's names. Silver is named by the player at the policeman's question with `NameEntry`, and `{rival}` fills from the region the script plays in (plan 02 · S4's field becomes one per region in the save), which plan 19's Blue needs too. Ho-Oh and Lugia both come, in HeartGold's order (decision 4); the beasts set free at the Burned Tower roam through `Models/Roamers.cs` (GPU-free, saved: a roamer's area, moved as the player crosses areas, its HP and status kept between meetings; plan 06 · R13's roamers over Sinnoh and plan 02 · S13's Mesprit take it, or give it if they come first), and are fought as plan 06 · R9's `BattleKind.Roamer`, which runs unless held. Battles are Platinum's rules or the modern ones as the game chose at its start, never a third set.

**The venues.** The Safari Zone west of Cianwood is imported as its own matrices and played with plan 06 · R9's Safari battle (`BattleKind.Safari`: Safari Balls, bait and mud) and HGSS's own stay, which has no limit of steps or time and ends when the balls run out or the player walks out, on the opening arrangement of its areas and the decompilation's base tables, the rearranging and the thirty objects left out (decision 5). The National Park is plan 15 · E5's gate and park on the imported area with HGSS's contest table as its venue, the Pokéathlon Dome north of it plan 15 · E6's building, and Route 40's Frontier Access a second door to plan 06 · R18's five facilities with one set of records (decision 7).

**GPU-free and drawn.** `Narc`, the readers, `Pokegear`, `RadioDial`, `Apricorns`, `Roamers`, the balls' arithmetic, the behaviours and `Crossing`'s use take no window; tests drive them with a seed and a fixed clock. What draws: the Pokégear's three cards, the Apricorn Box, the seven balls, the whirlpool, the Apricorn tree, Johto's towns, each with harness shots; and two runs draw the same pictures, because a call, a talk line and a tree's fruit come from `Dice` and the fixed clocks, never from `new Random()` or the system's date. Johto's chunks are Sinnoh's size, so the frame's budget holds as `world` already shows; the `johto` mode times its own walks.

## Risks

- **The archive's members** may not be Platinum's land data to the byte: a different header, or props of another size. J1 reads the loader beside it (`src/terrain_attributes.c`, `src/map_matrix.c`) before writing, and `LandData` takes a record of what differs rather than a second parser.
- **The behaviours are HGSS's**, not Platinum's numbers: a table by name, with whirlpools and headbutt trees given values above `0xE5`, and `docs/tile-behaviours.md` gains an HGSS section the importer writes; a value no name knows is reported, never guessed.
- **Text in the data.** `trainers.json` carries the original's lines and the scripts are text: `HgssTrainers` never reads `messages`, the importer writes no string of either into `Data/world/johto`, and `DataFileTests` holds that no overlay or script of Johto contains a line of the pinned `trainers.json`.
- **The calendar isn't here.** The phone's weekdays, the Sounds, the contest's days and Kurt's "tomorrow" all read a day: `WallClock` gives the date for the trees and Kurt from J6, every weekday is today until plan 06 · R14, and the rulings say so.
- **Two Kantos**: plan 19's from FireRed and LeafGreen, and HGSS's in J15. Only J15 opens the second, as Johto's own maps; their area keys collide in `Data/scripts/`, which J15 solves with a region folder; the Pokédex, badges and the chain stay as plan 18 has them.
- **Order against the roadmap.** Sinnoh's chapters are the roadmap's, and Johto runs ahead of plan 19's Kanto. J1 is cheap and proves the archive; J2 onward waits until plan 18 · W1 to W4 are in, or Johto would be built on Sinnoh's names.
- **Scope**: fifteen sessions, the chapters the longest. Each ends playable: J2 walks, J4 plays to a badge, J12 to the Hall of Fame; J13 to J15 are value past the end.

## Decisions for the user

1. **Where the player lands.** *Recommended:* New Bark Town, in the player's new house, the ship's last leg told in a line of `common.Crossing`, so HGSS's opening (Mom, the Pokégear, Elm, Route 29 at level 2) plays as written. The alternative lands at Olivine as plan 18's table proposes and bends the level curve, since Olivine's routes are a third of the way through.
2. **HGSS's Kanto.** *Recommended:* J15, optional and after plan 19: a second Kanto that is Johto's post-game, as HGSS has it three years on. The alternatives are to leave it out for good, or one Kanto map with the story deciding what is where, which puts HGSS's changes onto plan 19's FireRed layout, a different map.
3. **Where `JohtoHallOfFame` falls.** *Recommended:* at Lance, so Hoenn opens where every other region's does and J15 stays optional. The alternative waits for Red.
4. **HeartGold or SoulSilver.** *Recommended:* one Johto with both: HeartGold's order of the two birds, both versions' wild Pokémon in the tables (the pinned data is HeartGold's; SoulSilver's exclusives are added by hand as numbers and stand in for HeartGold's on half the rolls of their slot, from `Dice`), every species obtainable as plan 03 · D12 wants. The alternative is a choice at NEW GAME in plan 13 · V1's list.
5. **The Safari Zone.** *Recommended:* the opening arrangement and the base tables, played by plan 06 · R9's rules. The alternative is HGSS's rearranging of areas and placing of objects, with the tables that change by object and by day, as a later session on `SaveData`.
6. **Apricorns in the bag.** *Recommended:* the Apricorn Box as HGSS has it, one key item with a screen, which plan 15's juice needs. The alternative is seven ordinary items of the Items pocket, as Gold and Silver had them, and no box.
7. **The Battle Frontier.** *Recommended:* one Frontier with two doors and one set of records, since HGSS's and Platinum's are the same five facilities and Brains. The alternative builds Johto's as its own with records of its own.
8. **GB Sounds.** *Recommended:* an 8-bit arrangement of each Johto song as an MML section like `lowhp=`, switched on by the key item, written only where the user wants it. The alternative keeps the item a collectable.
