# Plan 25 · YD · Dynamax with punch: the proposal

Drafted 2026-10-26 as a proposal for plan 25 (battle presentation). **Nothing is built and nothing is approved**: the user answers the questions at the end, and the sessions below are written to the defaults until then. It adds no rule: like Y14 to Y16 it is the screen's alone, so the golden battles must not move (where a session would need an event of the log, it says so and asks).

**Goal**: a Dynamax, a Gigantamax and a Mega Dynamax of ours that land like the biggest thing in the game. The change into a giant is a scene of its own, with the camera, the sky, the sound and the cry swelling with it; a Max Move is a finisher, with a camera that follows it, a moment held at the impact and a target that is thrown by it; Max Guard and the three turns are seen and heard counting down to a fall; the Champion fights with the Mega Max Band open, sound and end like a boss fight. All of it inside the rules this plan has kept since Y1: a pure function of a cue's age and seed, under the quad caps, inside the 8 ms frame at 4K, nothing on the battle's generator, and nothing with the Battle Scene off but what lasts.

## Where we are (read from the code on 2026-10-26)

**The rules** (plan 06 · R22, R20c; `Battle/Sim/BattleCore.Dynamax.cs`): a flag on the move's choice (`BattleChoice.Dynamax`), three turns (`DynamaxRunsDown`, counted at the turn's end with **no event**: the screen learns the new count only when a menu opens, `BattleAnimator.SettleDynamax`), HP raised by the Dynamax Level, Max Guard (`MaxGuardName`, +4), the Max Moves' and G-Max Moves' field effects (`MaxMoveSelf`, the four G-Max side conditions, Steelsurge). Mega Dynamax is a Mega that Dynamaxes with the Mega Max Band (`WhyNotMegaDynamax`, `MegaDynamaxFormOf`, `<Mega>-Mmax`); **it has no event of its own** (R20c's question 12): its line carries R22's `Dynamaxed` with a form of `FormKind.MegaDynamax`. The log has three events: `Dynamaxed(Place, Form, Gigantamax, MaxHp, Hp)`, `DynamaxEnded(Place, Form, MaxHp, Hp)` and `MaxMoveUsed(Place, MaxMove, From)` (`BattleLog.cs` 362 to 376). A blow stopped by Max Guard is a plain line (`battle.protectedItself`), no event.

**Where a player meets it today**: nowhere in a story. No script gives the Dynamax Band or the Mega Max Band (plan 02 · S12's beat and plan 23's Sonia are unbuilt), so no player's Pokémon Dynamaxes in a game yet; the seven `_megamax` teams of `our-trainers.json` carry both bands (Cynthia's Garchomp, the Kanto Champion's Pidgeot in three teams, one to each starter, Lance's Dragonite, Steven's Metagross, Iris's Aggron) and no other team carries either, fought only once the player holds the Mega Max Band (`BattleCore.MegaMaxTeamOf`). The dens (`CoreSetup.MegaMaxDen`, rules built) wait for plan 23 · Z10, whose `BattleFormat.Raid` is four places against one. So today a tool or a test hands the bands over, and **the first player to see any of this will see it in a Champion's rematch**: that fight is the one to make land.

## Design

**Nothing moves the rules.** Every beat below starts from an event the log already carries (`Dynamaxed`, `DynamaxEnded`, `MaxMoveUsed` on a `MoveShown`, `MegaEvolved`, `Fainted`) or from what `SettleDynamax` already settles at a menu. The two places the face lacks a signal (the turn's tick, a blow on Max Guard) are read from what it has (a menu's settle; a move cue that did not land on a place whose Max Guard is up), and question 5 asks whether to add events instead.

**Time is bent, never added to the battle.** A hit-stop is a remap of a cue's *shown* age (`EffectCue.ShownAge`: the real age until the impact, held for the stop, then caught up so the cue still ends at its own length), which `MoveFx`, `BodyMotions.Of`, `StageLights.Of` and `BattleCamera` read in place of `Age`. The animator's clock, the engine's `After` and `HitDelay`, and the bar's drain are untouched, so nothing a line waits for moves, and a cue's ages are still a pure function of the time since it began. Only the set pieces that grow longer (question 2) change when lines are read, as `DynamaxTime` did in Y15.

**The camera gets a kind of shot for the later mechanics.** `ShotKind.Giant` (at the enum's end): a low shot from the ground before the platform, looking up the body (0.6 × the height above the feet, a field of view widened toward `WidestFov` 40° as the body grows, the foe's within `FoeShotLimit`), held while a `Dynamax` or `DynamaxEnd` cue plays and eased back to the overview by its end. A `Mega`, `UltraBurst` and `Tera` cue can take it too (question 4). `ShotKind.MaxMove`: the user from below for the pulse, a **whip** (an ease of 0.08 s, not a cut, sideways across the stage) to the target, a punch-in at the impact as a critical's but held through the stop, then a **pull-back** wide enough to hold the set piece to 1.4 s, back to the overview by `MaxMoveTime`, never the 0.95 s of an ordinary move. Both keep G8's rules: no move across the player's platform or within 3 units of it, a foe's shots in front of `FoeShotLimit`.

**The sky turns while a giant stands.** A `StageLight` for the giant's presence (a red-violet wash of the stage, its lights and the trainers, never the Pokémon, as Y16's washes are) eased in through the growth and held while any `CombatantView.Dynamaxed` stands, plus one layer of `ArenaFx`: red cloud banks low on the sky dome, turning, under a cap of their own (`ArenaFx` has no constant for one yet; the session writes it and a test counts it). The Max Moves' own washes lay over it as Y16's do (`StageLights.Of`, the stronger winning).

**The style guide first.** Each session writes its paragraph in "Dynamax, Max Moves and Terastallization (plan 25 · Y15)" and its camera rows in "Camera (G8)" before its code, as every session of this plan has.

## Performance and accessibility

- **Quads**: every new piece is under `MaxQuads` (124) with what it lays over: the growth about 50 → 66 with the shockwave; the heaviest Max Move about 62 → 86 with the shatter; the wreath's 12 a Pokémon unchanged (it thins, never thickens); the sky's cloud layer under the cap YD1 writes for it, counted in the test. A G-Max signature at most 124 by itself.
- **The frame**: no pass is added. The camera shots, the hit-stop, the body motions and the wash are uniforms and matrices; the shockwave and shards are quads of the effects' own atlas; the boss box is the interface's. YD0 writes the floors; each session after it runs `ab.sh` against its own start and stays within noise or says why.
- **Determinism**: each look a pure function of the cue's age (shown or real), its seed and the battle's clock; the camera of the animator's state; no `Dice`, no `Random`, no wall clock. `diff` of `later` and `demo` before and after each session shows only the shots of what it changed.
- **The outcome**: no event, line or roll is added (unless question 5 says otherwise, which would move `battles-modern.txt` only where a Dynamax plays, and be accepted in its own session).
- **The Battle Scene off** (plan 08 · P1): no shot, stop, shockwave, shatter, motion, fall or trainer's call; the sounds and the cry play, the forms change at once, and what is state stays (the pill and its tick, the boss box, the music's layer, the giant's sky, the wreath).
- **Reduce flashes and motion** (plan 12 · Q9, unbuilt): this plan keeps the existing caps (flash 0.35, shake 0.5) and puts the camera's whip, punch-in and pull-back and the hit-stop behind one number, `BattleCamera.Motion` (1 today), which Q9's `ReduceMotion` sets to 0 (the ordinary shots, no stop); Q9's `ReduceFlashes` caps `FxList.Flash` at a quarter as its plan says. If Q9 lands first, YD1 reads its rows.

## Risks

- **Taste.** Every beat is our own and long beats tire on the tenth Dynamax of a Battle Tower streak (plan 06 · decision 4 lets the Frontier use the bands). Question 3 asks for a shorter second time.
- **Pacing.** The trainer's call adds about a second to each Dynamax, and the fall half a second to each end. A Dynamax is at most twice a battle per side; the Champion's is once.
- **The camera.** Two new kinds of shot against G8's rules; every Max Move and growth from both sides in singles and doubles is tested at 0.05 s steps for the player's platform.
- **The harness's clock.** A lengthened `DynamaxTime` or `DynamaxEndTime` moves `later`'s shots after its first Dynamax (as Y15 did); the new mode keeps the rest.
- **Sound unheard.** As Y14 and Y15: nobody here can listen. The MP3s go to the user before YD5 and YD7 are called done.
- **Nothing to play it in.** Until plan 02 · S12 or plan 23 hands the bands over, only the harness and a tool see any of this (question 9).
