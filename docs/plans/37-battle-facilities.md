# Plan 37 · The later games' battle facilities and ladders

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: the post-game battle places of the later games that the Battle Frontier, its annex, the Battle Subway and the World Tournament don't cover, each built once as a GPU-free mechanic with hosts, words and a building of our own, and stood in the region whose games had it (or, where no region did, beside the annex until plan 41's hub stands). A pool of battle sets for every species comes first, because the Frontier's own sets stop at species 493 and every place here draws its trainers from the pool. Then: a streak house of single, double, triple and rotation rooms in Kalos; a tree in Alola whose hard rooms bring the game's own famous trainers and lend them as partners once beaten; a house of titles on Kalos's Route 7, where the player's own team climbs a ladder of titles against the day's guests for EXP and money; Lumiose's restaurants, menus of three battles scored by their turns; FireRed's Trainer Tower behind Seven Island's locked door; cups of fixed rules with rentals, a round of every region's Leaders at level 100 (the World Tournament seats them at 50) and a sparring room of one type; and Nimbasa's Battle Institute with its test, beside a rated ladder with monthly seasons, offline. An agency of lent Pokémon and an apprentice are optional. Triple and rotation battles, built by plan 14 and met today only at the World Tournament, find their places in the journey here. Nothing changes how a battle is fought: every room uses the formats and the Frontier's rules as they stand.

## Design

### Content of our own

The mechanics are the games'; everything a player meets is ours. Each place is named in plain words (the streak house, the partners' tree, the house of titles, the restaurants, the cups' stadium, the sparring room, the ladder), and the games' names appear only in this plan, to say where an idea came from. Hosts, staff, trainers, their names, looks and lines, the halls, the manor and the music are our own: none of the Battle Maison's sisters, the Battle Tree's legends, the Battle Chateau's noble ranks, Lumiose's restaurants or their chefs is recreated. Where the game already has a character (the regions' Leaders, Elite Four, Champions and rivals), the tree and the house of titles use them as they stand, with their own teams, looks and voices, and give them no new story. Rules that are numbers (a room's team size, a cup's level band, a tower floor's teams) are the games' numbers. The Trainer Tower and the Battle Institute are FireRed's and Black 2's own places, built as plans 19 and 22 built their regions: layouts and numbers imported, every word ours.

### The later sets (FC1)

- `tools/DataImporter`'s new `FacilitySets.cs`, beside `MasterTrainers.cs`, writes `Data/facility-sets.json` (never by hand; `docs/data-files.md`): for every species of the games' 1,025 that Showdown's random-battle files name, a set for each of its roles (the newest generation's file first), in `FrontierSetRecord`'s shape with an `Ability` and a `Tier`. The four moves are drawn on `BattleRandom` seeded by the national number and the role's place, from the role's moves that `Legality` lets the species learn and the engine runs in full, as the masters' are. The item, nature and effort follow a rule of ours from the moves and the species' stats (physical, special or mixed; fast or bulky; a support set when two of its moves are status moves). The tier is the species' random-battle level (Showdown's own measure of strength, lower the stronger) cut into four bands, or its base stat total where no file has one. Read from Showdown: the moves, the abilities, the role's name and the level, nothing else. A set the user wants otherwise is written in `tools/DataImporter/Overrides/facility-sets.json`.
- `Data/FacilitySetDatabase.cs` reads it through `GameDataFiles`; `FrontierTrainers.Build` takes a set's ability where it names one. `Models/Facilities/FacilityTrainers.cs` (a new folder, GPU-free) draws a team: so many sets of the tiers a room allows, no two of one species or holding the same item (`FrontierTrainers.SelectSets`' rule), IVs rising with the room, personalities for natures (`FrontierTrainers.PersonalityFor`), and a class and a name of ours from `Data/facility-trainers.json` (hand-written, as `subway.json`'s riders), at the level asked, every draw on the caller's sixteen-bit draw.

### The frame of the later places (FC2)

- `Models/Facilities/FacilityRecord.cs`: each place's ways (a room, a format) with the streak, the best and the latest, whether its hard room is open, and a challenge put down as plain data (the sets, personalities, the places entered and its generator's state, as `TowerPause` keeps the Tower's), kept in a new `FrontierRecords.Facilities` by place and way, null until the first is entered, so no save version is raised.
- `Models/Facilities/StreakChallenge.cs`: `BattleVenueChallenge`'s shape with a place's rules as values: how many are entered, the format (through `FrontierConditions.Format`), the round's length (one battle at a time for the house and the tree, five for the institute's test, eight for a cup), the tiers by the streak, the host's battles and the points per win. The team goes in as copies at the place's level, whole every battle (`FrontierRules.TeamFor`). Every draw is on a generator of the challenge's own whose state is kept with the record, so a challenge put down and taken up meets the same trainer (the Tower's rule).
- **Hosts**: a place's host stands in the way at the 20th battle of a normal room, and beating them opens the hard room, whose host comes at its 50th battle (X and Y's numbers). The hosts' teams are ours in `Data/our-trainers.json`, their classes rows of `Data/audio/sound-map.json`, their looks rows of `characters.json`. A hard room's host beaten for the first time leaves a trophy on the place's board, a story variable of its own as the Subway's print is, counting toward none of the Frontier's.
- **Battle Points** go into the Frontier's one purse (decision 6), as the Subway's do; each place has a counter trading them for a stock of `Data/facility-prizes.json` (hand-written, `FrontierPrizeRecord`s: the later games' battle items), and plan 36 decides whether its training tools join a stock. Each place's board is `FrontierBoard.ForFacility(place, records, story)` on the records screen, as `ForSubway` is.
- **The later mechanics**: a place's battles let both sides use what the bag or the trainer holds (a Key Stone, a Z-Ring) as any battle does, under `RunTerms.NoLaterMechanics` as everywhere; FC2 makes sure `StartFrontierBattle` hands the bag's key items to a place's battle and leaves the Frontier's own five as they are.
- **One script command for all of them**: `facility <place> <word>` (`Op.Facility` at the enum's end; `Story/ScriptRunner.Facilities.cs`; both hosts' `Facility`; `GameFlow`; the Driver; `ScriptTests.EveryCommand`; `StopsAHurry`; `docs/scripts.md`), with the frame's words (`enough`, `choose`, `check`, `begin`, `active`, `opponent`, `battle`, `won`, `lost`, `finish won|lost`, `points`, `streak`, `best`, `board`, `pause`, `resume`, `trophy`, `end`). Each later place adds its words to the same command, never a command of its own. `Core/GameEngine.Facilities.cs` is where they meet the game, and `docs/mechanics/rulings.md` gains a section, "The later games' battle places", for every number of ours.

### The streak house (FC2)

Four rooms: single (three entered), double (four), triple (six) and rotation (four), the original's counts. Trainers of FC1's sets come at the tiers the streak has reached, a battle at a time, and after any battle the player goes on, rests (the challenge put down and saved) or gives up; points come per win, rising with the streak (a table of ours). Four hosts of ours, one to a room, each with a normal and a hard team that Mega Evolves. The hall is a lobby and a battle room of ours (map files in Kalos's list in `RegionDatabase`), behind one of Lumiose's locked doors with `openedBy` `KalosHallOfFame` in the city's overlay. Until plan 23 · Z4 sets that flag in play, tests and the harness set it.

### The partners' tree (FC3)

Three rooms: single (three), double (four) and multi (two, beside a partner), each with a hard room opened at its 20th battle. `Data/tree-cast.json` (hand-written) lists the game's own famous trainers: a trainer id of the tables, the look, and the region whose Hall of Fame lets them in (the regions' Champions, Elite Four, rivals and Leaders the game has). One of them stands in the way now and then in a hard room, with three of their own team at 50 (`Tournament.ThreeOf`); one beaten in the tree may be asked to partner the multi rooms, and stands in the tree's salon as the Battle Salon's partners stand, bringing two of their team that keep clear of the player's species and items (`BattleSubway.MakeFriend`'s rule). FRIEND AS PARTNER (plan 13 · V20) plays the partner where it is on. The tree's host is ours; the 50th battle of a hard room is a Champion of the cast drawn from the regions finished, never a fixed pair. Z-Moves and Mega Evolution as the bag and the trainers hold them.

### The house of titles (FC4)

A manor of ours on Kalos's Route 7 (drawn into the route with `tools/MapDraw`, found at its door), open from the first visit. The player's own team fights at its own levels and is paid EXP and money as after any trainer's battle (decision 4). Each day the house draws its guests (the Battleground's way: `random` into variables, each guest once a day by a `FLAG_DAILY_` flag of ours). Members of ours bring three of FC1's sets at a level set by the title over the highest of the player's team (`Models/Facilities/TitlesHouse.cs`, a table of ours), made at met levels and left alone by the battle's relevelling, as the `_beyond` teams are. The regions' Leaders come on days of the week, the Elite Four and Champions at the top two titles, each region's once its Hall of Fame is set, with their rematch teams met as `RematchLevels` meets them. A win scores points by the guest; eight titles of our own (none of the original's ranks) come at thresholds of ours, kept as story variables, and the title stands on Kalos's page of the Trainer Card (`CardPage.Region`).

### Lumiose's restaurants (FC5)

Three restaurants of ours behind three of Lumiose's locked doors (warp 22 first), each a menu of three courses fought in one format (doubles, triples, rotations) against its staff: trainers of `Data/world/kalos/trainers.json` with their `format`, at Kalos's levels a step above the streets round them, relevelled and grown as Kalos's trainers are. Each menu is played once a day (`FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_<n>`) and its dish (an item) is given by the courses' `TURNS` against a table of ours: the fewer turns, the better the dish. A fourth table charges money for its menu and pays in rarer items. The menus are scripts and data; plan 39 · CR7's waiters (the Seven Stars Restaurant, Celadon City's restaurant, Olivine City's café, two stalls) stand in other places and give meals with powers, which these dishes are not. The staff's lines never name a species, since a crossed region grows its trainers' teams (`GrownNamesTests`).

### The cups, the Leaders' round and the sparring room (FC7)

- `Data/cups.json` (hand-written): each cup a rules record (levels from and to, a total of levels, the species let in by evolution, height, weight or type, the clauses, three picked of six, singles or doubles), with the Stadium games' five cups' numbers and names and prizes of ours. `Models/Facilities/Cups.cs`: `WhyNot(team, cup)` beside `LinkSetup.Check`'s clauses; eight battles in a row; the foe's six shown as plan 28 · I2's `TeamPreview` gives them and three picked; copies at the cup's level (`LevelScaling`); rentals of FC1's sets that meet the cup (`FactoryScreen.OpenToRent`; the World Tournament's `worldRental` events already lend three of a Leader's or Champion's team, which these do not repeat).

- **The sparring room**: a type chosen, three of it entered, an endless streak of trainers whose three are of that type, at 50, with a record per type. ### The institute's test and the ladder (FC8)

- **The test**, at Nimbasa's Battle Institute's receptionist (Black 2's object, our words): five battles at 50, single or double, against trainers of FC1's sets rising by the battle; each scored by a formula of ours (won, turns, HP left, the foe's tier) to a total out of 9,999 and a rank of ours; the best of each format kept, and a board of made-up names of ours with the player's among them.
- **The ladder**, at a second counter of ours in the same room: a rating of ours (an Elo with a fixed factor) that a win raises and a loss lowers, opponents drawn about the rating with FC1's sets at its band's tiers, a season to each calendar month of `GameClock.Today` (which the harness fixes), each season's rules a `LinkRoom` (Flat 50 single, double, or a season that bans a list), bands named by us, a prize in Battle Points by the best band reached, and the rating carried into the next season by half (decision 8). `Models/Facilities/Ladder.cs` and a new `UI/LadderScreen.cs` (the season, the rating, the band, the last results, the test's sheet). These rules are what plan 33 · GL7's Galar tower may take.

### Optional: the agency and the apprentice (FC9, FC10)
