# Plan 35 · Bonding and the boxes' home

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: the team's life outside battle, and a home for the Pokémon in the boxes. Plan 10's camp grows into a close-up where one Pokémon is petted by hand, fed treats of our own, brushed clean of what a battle left on it and cured, with faces that answer the hand. The follower gains a mood, and words for where it is and how it has been treated. A pasture of our own, reached from every PC, lets a box of thirty walk about to be visited, fed, cared for and taken along, and Poké Jobs grow into the pasture's fields, with a nursery that hatches Eggs and a cave of finds, each Pokémon seen at its work. If the user wants them, a stroll counter that a boxed Pokémon rides in while the player walks, and two play rounds. These are Pokémon-Amie, Pokémon Refresh, Yellow's partner, the later games' pastures, Poké Pelago and the Pokéwalker as mechanics; everything seen, said and heard is ours.

## Design

**One book of care.** Everything the plan adds to a Pokémon's day is one GPU-free record kept by its own id: the soil a battle left on it, how full it is today, the days its pat and its grooming last counted, its mood, and whether a cure is due. The book is the save's (`SaveData.Care`), not the Pokémon's, so `Pokemon`, `CopyStateFrom`, the battle's mirror, a trade and the Home box carry nothing of it, and a Pokémon that leaves the save is pruned from it. No battle rule reads soil or mood (decision 3), so the golden battles hold; what changes in play is friendship, by numbers of ours capped by the day.

**Content of our own.** The plan takes the mechanics of Pokémon-Amie, Pokémon Refresh, Yellow's partner, the later games' pastures, Poké Pelago and the Pokéwalker, and nothing else of them. The close-up's tools, the treats and their names, the meadow and its keeper, the confectioners and bakers, the stroll counter's courses and small games, and every line, picture and sound are ours: no game's minigame is remade, no Puff, Bean or Malasada is named, no isle is laid out and no course is copied. Where the game already has someone (the Pokétch Company's president), the plan gives them a line as they stand.

### The care book (BN1)
- `Models/Care.cs`: `CareBook` (records by `Pokemon.Id`; `Of`, `Prune`, `ToSaved`, `Restore`) and `CareRecord`. A day is the calendar day `GameClock.Today` gives, counted as `ApricornBox.DayOf` counts it.
- **Soil after a battle**: each Pokémon of the player's that the core sent out (`BattleCore.SentOut`, the original's participant mask, which Burmy's cloak already reads with the battle's ground in `FormRules.CloakBurmy`; the core holds copies of the team, which keep their `Pokemon.Id`, so the book finds the game's own Pokémon by it) takes the mark of where it fought: dust on `BattleTerrain.Sand` or in a sandstorm, mud in the Great Marsh or a puddle, leaves on `Grass`, wet on `Water` or in rain, snow on `Snow` or `Ice` or in hail or snow, nothing on a plain, in a building or on a bridge (a table of ours). Two marks at most, the newest kept.
- **Meals**: five pips of fullness a day; a berry fills one, a treat its grade (BN3); a full Pokémon refuses; each meal raises friendship (`FriendshipEvent.Fed` for a berry, a treat's own numbers).
- **Pats, grooming and cures**: a pat counts for friendship once a day (`Petted`), a mark cleaned once a day (a new `FriendshipEvent.Groomed`, ours, appended); a status is cured once for each battle the Pokémon fought since its last cure, which is `CampCare`'s gift moved from the whole team onto each Pokémon.
- **Mood**: from −100 to 100, read as five faces (glum, low, calm, cheerful, elated); lifted and lowered by a win, a loss, a faint, a level, an evolution, a hatching, a heal (the script's `heal`, which already tells the host through `IScriptHost.Milestone`), a pat, a meal by its taste, a grooming, a cure, poison's bite in the field and a mark left on through a walk cycle; settling toward calm by a walk cycle for the team and by a day for the boxes. The numbers are ours, in the rulings.
- `CampVisit` and `CampCare` become views over the book, so F6's screen keeps working before BN2.
