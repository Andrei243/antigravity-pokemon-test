# Plan 41 · The whole journey: a hub, the Champions' circuit and episodes of our own

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: what ties the regions together once a player has won several of them. A small region of our own lies between them, reached from every harbour and by Fly. In it stand: a covered plaza whose stalls the player sets out and ranks up, gathering services each region keeps to itself today and guests from every region finished; one hall where every region's Hall of Fame stands; the Champions' circuit, every finished region's League fought again in its own rooms at one raised level, ending in a final of all the Champions at the hub; a board of missions set in the regions, drawn by the week, some of them against the clock; an office where Looker takes a case in each region, played as clues and deductions; and, last, an epilogue in which the leaders of the villain teams the game already has gather in a fortress of ours. Beside the hub, the Gym Leaders of every finished region battle again where their region gives them no rematch today. The mechanics are the later games' (the Festival Plaza, Funfest Missions, the Looker Bureau, Detective Pikachu's cases, Team Rainbow Rocket); the places, people, stories, words, art and music are ours.

## Design

**Content of our own.** The plan takes mechanics, never content. The hub and its name, its people, the plaza's stalls and keepers, the guests' lines, the office's name, every case's story, clue and culprit, the coalition of the epilogue, its fortress and the one who gathers it, the missions' words and every song are ours, drawn and written for this game. The Festival Plaza's facilities, the Funfest's missions, the Looker Bureau's episodes, Detective Pikachu's partner and Team Rainbow Rocket are not recreated, and their names appear only to say where an idea came from. Where the game already has a character the plan uses them as they stand in it: Looker (his look, his Croagunk and his manner as plan 02 wrote them), the Leaders, the Elite Four and the Champions (their looks, teams and bands), and the villain teams' leaders and admins, with teams of ours where they need new ones. No Pokémon talks. Every story (each case, the epilogue's outline) is shown to the user as an outline before its session builds it, as plan 03 · D14's designs are.

**A region of our own, off the chain** (EG1; decisions 1 to 3). The hub is a `Region` with the id `Hub`, a file (`Data/regions/hub.json`) and a world folder drawn with `tools/MapDraw/hub/`, standing outside the chain's order: `Region.Side` (the file's `side`, a flag as plan 23 · Z17 has it, with the link's `Kind` telling hub from branch; whichever of EG1, Z17 and Z21 lands first writes the seam in Z17's shape: `Region.Side` a flag of the region file, `RegionLink.Kind { Next, Side }` and `SideLinksFrom`). It has no story, Pokédex, badges, levels or cap of its own. (Jubilife's Global Terminal, which the sweep proposed, is a building of Sinnoh's map and can't stand between the regions.) It opens with the second Hall of Fame (a new `RegionDatabase.HubOpen(story)`), and from then on every finished region's harbour offers it after the regions entered (a side link of every region, `SideLinksFrom`, joined to `LinksFrom`'s answers on the harbour's own vessel, landing on the hub's arrival the first time and on its harbour after, `LandingOf`), and the hub's own harbour sails to every region entered with no Hall of Fame asked of it. Fly lists it last once entered (`Crossing.FlyRegions`; its `townmap.json` and spawns). Whatever walks the chain steps round it: `RegionDatabase.With` joins a pack's region after the last chain region, not after the hub; `RegionTests` hold the chain without it and the hub's attendant with it; `--region` refuses a side region; the Trainer Card gives it no page (`TrainerCardInfo.RegionPages`). **Its rules are borrowed**: a new `GameEngine.RulesHere` is the furthest finished region (`RematchLevels.Furthest`) while the player stands in a side region with none of its own, and `Here` everywhere else, and the readers above ask it (the battle's badges, the Marts, the field helpers, the levels and the cap, a save's summary), so a traded Pokémon obeys at the hub as where the player last won. A script there names a region's badges outright (`if badge thunder of kanto`). The town is small: a harbour hall with the attendant and a guide who crosses a Champion to the doors of the regions' battle facilities (`BattleFrontier`, `HoennBattleFrontier`, `GearStation`, and plan 37 · FC7's cups hall once it stands) by `travel to`, with a guide at each to cross back, as Route 40's does; a Pokémon Center on the common plan with plan 07's counter upstairs (`common.GlobalLink`); the plaza's hall, the hall of Champions, the circuit's arena and Looker's office; the board; an islet beyond, shut until EG9. It has an `Architecture` of its own (the style guide's "Buildings" row first), songs of ours on the sound its file names (plan 31's fuller one once MU1 has landed), an empty `habitats.json` and no wild Pokémon.
