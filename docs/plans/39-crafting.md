# Plan 39 · Materials, crafting and cooking

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: things the player makes along the journey through every region. Wild Pokémon leave materials behind after a battle, and spots in the field give them once a day; a kit turns them into balls, medicine and field tools wherever the player stands; a TM maker on every Pokémon Center's PC makes again a TM the player has had, since a TM is used up in every game; two different berries growing side by side cross into one of fifteen rare berries, fourteen of which nothing else in the game gives; a pot at the camp cooks dishes of our own whose meal heals the team and gives one of plan 36's timed powers, and waiters in the restaurants the regions already have serve meals the same way. It opens with Sinnoh's Hall of Fame, in a workshop of ours behind one of Sunyshore City's unused doors, and goes with the team into every region after it. It takes plan 10 · F8's cooking over (decision 1), so that Galar's curry and Paldea's sandwiches (plans 33 and 34) cook on its pot.

## Design

**Opened by Sinnoh's Hall of Fame, kept in every region** (decision 2). A workshop of ours stands behind Sunyshore City's door 8: a room of ours drawn with `tools/RoomEditor` (`SunyshoreWorkshop`), its door an overlay `doors` entry `openedBy` `FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED`. Its craftswoman gives the kit, a key item of ours through `Overrides/items.json` whose `fieldUse` opens `UI/CraftingScreen.cs` on a new `GameState.Crafting` from the bag's USE and the item button, as the Apricorn Box opens. Before the kit nothing of the plan is seen: no Pokémon leaves anything, the field's spots are hidden by the kit's flag (`FLAG_GOT_CRAFTING_KIT`, ours), the PC asks nothing new, no waiter serves. So Sinnoh's story is Platinum's, no options row is needed (the kit is the switch), and every mode of the harness that doesn't give the kit keeps its pictures.

**Content of our own.** Only the mechanics are taken: a Pokémon leaves something behind (Scarlet and Violet), materials gathered and made into balls and medicine at a kit (Legends: Arceus), a machine makes a TM from materials (Scarlet and Violet), two berries side by side cross (X and Y), a meal gives a power for a time (Sword and Shield's curry, Scarlet and Violet's sandwiches). Everything the player meets is ours: the materials and their names, the recipes, the dishes and their names, the crossing table, the TM maker's costs, the workshop, its people, the waiters, their stalls and every line. Nothing of Scarlet's materials by species, Legends: Arceus's recipes, Sword and Shield's curry list, Scarlet's sandwich recipes or meal powers, or X and Y's mutation pairs is copied. What the game already holds is used as it stands: the things made are items of the data (Platinum's balls and medicine, Hisui's balls since plan 06 · R28), the crossed berries are the data's own, and the Seven Stars Restaurant, Celadon City's restaurant and Olivine City's café are the originals' rooms, with a waiter of ours added.

### Materials

- **The pouch** (decision 3): counts in the kit, 999 of each at most, as the Apricorn Box keeps its counts. `Models/Materials.cs` (GPU-free: `MaterialData`, `MaterialTable` over `Data/materials.json`, `MaterialPouch` with `Add`, `Take`, `Count`, `ToSave` and `Load`) has three kinds: a Pokémon's (a common and a rare material for each of the eighteen types, named for what a Pokémon of the type might shed), the field's (about ten: stone, ore, wood, resin, fibre, herbs, shells, salt, frost and the like) and the pantry's (about ten goods bought for cooking, CR6). Each has a worth in money, which the recipes are held to.
- **What a wild Pokémon leaves** (`MaterialDrops.For(foe, rng)`): one felled or caught in a battle of the field leaves one to three of its type's common material (its second type's half the time) and now and then its type's rare one, more of both at a high level; our numbers, in a new section of the rulings, "Materials, crafting and cooking". Never a trainer's Pokémon, nor anything in the Frontier, the Safari Game, Pal Park's show, the Bug-Catching Contest or the catching lesson; each foe felled of a horde or an SOS chain leaves its own. It is decided in `EndBattle` on `fieldRandom`, only while the kit is in the bag (a battle without it draws nothing more), and told in one line of the field's toast once the field is back, never when nothing was left.
- **What the field gives** (CR2): gathering spots, things of the map beside the Apricorn trees (`NPC.GatherSpotType` in five kinds: rock, wood, plant, shore, snow), laid by an overlay's new `gather` list, shown once the kit is given and bearing once a calendar day as a tree does (picked by key and day, `Models/Gathering.cs`), each kind's finds a weighted table of `materials.json`. A tree cut and a rock smashed with the kit in the bag leave wood or stone one time in three (`common.CutTree`, `common.Rock`). While the game is shut, plan 35 · BN6's cave job brings stone and ore into the pouch once the kit is held (rows of its finds and a `JobResult.Materials`, written by whichever of CR2 and BN6 comes second, never the two in one wave); this plan adds no job kind of its own.

### Making things

- **The kit's MAKE tab** (CR3): the recipes of `Data/recipes.json`'s `kit` section, each the materials and items it takes and what it gives: the Poké, Great and Ultra Balls, Hisui's five throwing balls (their first source), a little medicine, Repels, Escape Ropes, Honey and the four mulches (which feed CR5's berries). `Models/Crafting.cs` (`CraftRecipe`, `CraftRecipes`, `Crafting.WhyNot`, `Make`) takes and gives exactly, refuses a full bag and makes nothing for less than its Mart price in its materials' worth. A made thing is no find: the randomiser leaves it as its recipe names it, and the code says so (CLAUDE.md's rule for a place that gives an item). The workshop's apprentice trades three of a type's common material for one of its rare.
- **The TM maker** (CR4, decision 4): a tab of the same screen, opened from a PC (`common.PC`'s modern branch offers it once the kit is in the bag, through `craft tm`). It lists every TM and TR the player has ever had in the bag (`Inventory.MachinesHad`, noted as a machine is added and saved as `SaveData.MachinesHad`; an older save knows those in its bag) and makes one at a cost by its move: that type's common material, its rare one for a strong move, and money by the move's power (`recipes.json`'s `machines` tiers). A made TM teaches as a found one does (`MoveTeaching.Learn`) and is used up the same way; an HM is never made. It makes the machines the data has (Platinum's 92 TMs and the 100 TRs, and the later TMs 93 to 304 plan 36 · TR9 added): a TM for every move would be a new table of machines and every species' `TmMoves`, which no session of this plan owns.

### Crossing berries

`Overworld/BerryCrossing.cs` (CR5, GPU-free): when a berry is planted on a soil that touches another growing a different berry (the host hands the soils on the eight tiles round), and the pair is one of `recipes.json`'s `crosses`, both patches note the cross (`BerryPatch.Cross`, saved inside `SaveData.Berries`; null in an older save). Each harvest of a noted patch rolls once on the field's chance for one of its fruit to be the cross. `MinutesPass` stays plain arithmetic and a patch with no cross draws nothing, so `BerryPatchesTests` and the `berries` mode's shots stand. The table is ours, by flavour: two berries whose strongest flavour is the one a stat's natures like cross into that stat's pinch berry (Liechi, Ganlon, Salac, Petaya, Apicot), and a pinch berry crossed with others gives the rarer ten (Lansat, Starf, Enigma, Micle, Custap, Jaboca, Rowap, Roseli, Kee, Maranga); every one is reachable from berries the world gives. It works on every soil that touches another (decision 5), and the crosses found are a page of the kit (CROSSES).

### Cooking and meals

- **The pot takes plan 10 · F8 over** (CR6, decision 1). `Models/Kitchen.cs` (GPU-free) is the pot at thirty frames a second, as `PoffinPot` and `AthlonEvent` are: a function of time and three buttons, its timed presses through `TimingAssists`. Its ingredients come from the pouch's pantry and the bag's berries, Honey and mushrooms; the dish is matched by `recipes.json`'s `kitchen` section and graded in four steps. The team eats: HP back by the grade, conditions cured as the camp cures them, each Pokémon's `Fed` of the visit counted. The dish starts one power of plan 36's table, its strength and length by the grade. Plan 36 · TR7's `PowerKind` lists `Exp`, `Effort`, `Money`, `Bargain`, `Catch`, `Encounter`, `Stealth` and `Hatch`; a dish that wants a type's encounters or more materials left behind needs two kinds it lacks, so CR6 adds `TypeEncounter` and `Materials` at the end of that enum, each a row of `Data/powers.json` in plan 36's shape with its one reader (the encounter rules read the first, `MaterialDrops` the second), never a second system; a second meal follows plan 36's rule for two powers of a kind.
- **Where it is cooked**: a phase of the camp's screen (`CampScreen.Cook`, drawn by `ModernUi.Camp.cs`), the start menu offering CAMP while the FOLLOWER row is on or the kit is in the bag (`GameEngine.HasCamp`). The pantry is sold into the pouch (`ShopScreen.OpenForPouch`, beside `OpenForPoints`) at the workshop's counter and by every waiter of CR7. Galar's curry (plan 33 · GL2) and Paldea's sandwiches (plan 34 · PD7 and its decision 4) are sections their plans add to `recipes.json`, cooked on this pot.
- **Restaurants and stalls** (CR7): a waiter of ours in the Seven Stars Restaurant at Valor Lakefront (Sinnoh), Celadon City's restaurant (Kanto) and Olivine City's café (Johto), and a stall of ours in Slateport City's market (Hoenn) and on Castelia City's avenue (Unova), each serving three dishes of ours from `recipes.json`'s `stands` rows (the dish, its price, its power), eaten there and then through `craft meal "<dish>"` after the script's own `choose` and `takemoney`, and shown from the workshop's opening by its flag. Kalos's and Alola's are plan 23's to place by the same row and word, Galar's and Paldea's plans 33's and 34's.
