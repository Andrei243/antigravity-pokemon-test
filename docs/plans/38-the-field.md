# Plan 38 · The field: outbreaks, hideaways and the sky

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: what the field offers in every region beyond its tables, for a journey that carries one team through every region and has a post-game in each. A place's grass is crowded by an outbreak of one species for a day, told on the Town Map beside a visitor of another region that a scan calls up for an hour; the wild Pokémon seen in the grass may be bright, a run of catches of one species pays, and hidden items glint; Johto's trees drop Pokémon to Headbutt and Route 119 hides Hoenn's Feebas; rifts open for an hour with Pokémon and finds of their own; hideaways open under Sinnoh's Underground, their Pokémon drawn by statues in the secret base; the team's flier soars over a region to places that rise for one day; and Johto's Safari Zone is arranged with HeartGold's objects, with a reserve of ours in Unova as an optional last. Two sessions finish Unova's own field first: its Town Map, which it has never had (EX10), and Black 2's shaking grass, dust clouds, rippling water and bridge shadows, which are imported and never met (EX11). Each is a GPU-free rule tested with a seed and a fixed clock, opened where it belongs (most by the region's Hall of Fame) and shot in a harness mode of its own; none of it moves a draw the game makes today.

## Design

**Nothing new is drawn from the dice.** What a day or an hour holds (its outbreaks, the hour's rift, the places of the day, a visitor's place) is worked out from the day's number, the hour and the region by `Core/SplitMix.cs`, never drawn from `Dice`; a bonus rolls (`RerollForShiny`) only where a step of it is reached; whether a Pokémon is bright is read from its personality. With nothing in force `MakeWild` draws what it draws today, so `battles-*.txt`, `levels.txt` and every harness mode before this plan's stay as they are.

**Opened where it belongs.** The day's events (outbreaks, the visitor, rifts) and soaring begin in a region with its Hall of Fame (`Region.StoryCompleteFlag`, as hordes and our species do; decision 1); the hideaways with Sinnoh's National Pokédex; the streak and bright Pokémon with the WILD POKéMON row at VISIBLE (decision 4); the glint with a row of its own (decision 5); Headbutt, Route 119's Feebas and the Safari's objects where their games have them. The rules take a region and its places, so Kalos and Alola join as their Halls of Fame are built, a region's rows of ours (visitors, rifts, places of the day) are added with them, and Galar's and Paldea's open maps take the same rules for their encounter zones (plan 32). Every rule writes its numbers and their source in a new section of `docs/mechanics/rulings.md`, "The field beyond the tables"; a save keeps each new field null when empty, so no version is raised.

**Content of our own.** The mechanics are the later games' (Scarlet's and Legends: Arceus's outbreaks, Sun and Moon's scan, Sword's bright Pokémon, Let's Go's streak, Brilliant Diamond's hideaways and statues, Omega Ruby's soaring and its places found by day, Legends: Arceus's rifts); the content is ours. Every place, person, name, word, table, picture and sound the plan adds is our own: the hideaways' habitats and pieces, the statues' look, the places of the day, the visitors' and the rifts' tables, the view from the sky and its beacons, the reserve in Unova. No hideaway, mirage spot, distortion, host or mascot of a game is made again, and the games' names appear here only to say where an idea came from. Where a finished region's own game has the rule (Emerald's television outbreak and Route 119's Feebas, HeartGold's Headbutt trees and Safari objects, Black 2's swarms if its data holds them), the numbers are read from the pinned decompilation as that region's were (species, levels, places, tiles), never a line or a picture, and every word is ours.

### Outbreaks (EX1)

- **A day's outbreaks** (`Overworld/Outbreaks.cs`, new): each region has two a day (the rulings' number) at places with a land table of twelve slots (never a Safari Zone, the contest's park, a Gym or a place a story holds), each one species of that place's own table that isn't its commonest, a legendary, a mythical or a slot of ours, and never the place of the day's swarm (a swarm, Platinum's and HeartGold's, stays as it is: it fills slots 0 and 1 for a day in one place and has no crowd, count or bonus rolls, and an outbreak is a second, richer thing beside it, not a replacement). `EncounterMoment.Outbreak` lays it in slots 0 to 5 (eighty in a hundred) after the swarm and before the second slot's and ours, so those keep theirs; seen in the grass, up to ten stand in the place (a new `FieldPokemon.MostInOutbreak`).
- **The count and its pay**: every one of the species beaten or caught in its place is counted (`SpecialEncounters.OutbreakMet`, by place, cleared by a new day); one more shiny roll from thirty met and two from sixty (Scarlet's steps; decision 3), given in `MakeWild` to that species in that place alone.
- **The finished regions' own**: Hoenn's is Emerald's television outbreak (its species, place, level, moves, share and days, read from the pinned pokeemerald into Hoenn's `encounters.json`, new), beside the outbreaks as Sinnoh's and Johto's swarms are; Unova's swarms are Black 2's if the pinned pokebw2 holds them, as R13's `Swarms` on Unova's places, and the rulings say so if it doesn't.

### The day's report and the visitor (EX2)

- **The report** (`Overworld/FieldReport.cs`, new): `FieldReport.Of(region, story, encounters, today, hour)` is what the region has today (its outbreaks with their counts, the visitor and its minutes, the hour's rift once EX5 is built). The Town Map turns to it on the item button in `TownMapMode.Item` and `Card` (the Pokégear's map): a ring with the species' menu icon on each cell, a list in the place's panel (decision 2). The map as Platinum draws it stays the first page, and a swarm and a roamer are still unmarked.
- **The visitor** (`Overworld/Visitors.cs`, new; `Data/visitors.json`, ours): for each region, seven rows by the day of the week, each a species of another region's Pokédex at a place of this one; a scan from the report once a day, after the region's Hall of Fame and with the National Pokédex, calls the day's row for an hour of the game's own time, counted from `dt` while the field or a battle is on screen, as the Bug-Catching Contest counts its minutes (`SpecialEncounters.Visitor`). `EncounterMoment.Visitor` lays it in its place's grass by the rulings' share, never on an outbreak's place.

### Seen in the grass: bright Pokémon, streaks and glints (EX3)

- **Bright Pokémon** (`Overworld/BrightPokemon.cs`, new): a wild Pokémon in sight may be bright, by bands of how many of its species were beaten or caught (`Research.RecordOf`, Sword's bands; decision 3), read from its personality; a bright one has one to three perfect IVs by the count and the first of its species' Egg moves it doesn't know in place of its last, shimmers round its card in the grass and once as it comes out in battle (`CueKind.Bright` beside `CueKind.Shiny`, raised by the face on `Entered`, never a log event; `Pokemon.Bright`, not saved). The count's shiny rolls (Sword's steps) go to every one of the species met in sight.
- **The catch streak** (`Overworld/CatchStreak.cs`, new; `SaveData.CatchStreak`): catches in a row of one species met in sight, broken by a catch of another or the chained species fleeing; Let's Go's steps (eleven, twenty-one, thirty-one) give perfect IVs and shiny rolls as `DexNavRules.Bonus` sets them, and the count stands in the field's corner where the Safari Game's does.
- **Glints** (`Overworld/Glints.cs`, new): an unfound hidden item within three tiles glints every two seconds of the field's clock (a cell of `LifeArt`), behind a new `OptionRow.ItemGlints` (`GameSettings.ItemGlints`, a `RulesDefault`, before Help). ### Rifts (EX5)

- **A name only**: these rifts are tears in the field that last an hour. They are not the rift after the last region that leads to Hisui and Lumiose (plan 23 · Z17, Z21), nor the rifts of Spear Pillar (`PropType.Rift`, `RigKind.Rift`); the one thing shared is the picture `SceneEffect.Rift`, which this plan stands on a tile. Code and words keep them apart (`Overworld/Rifts.cs`, `ui.json`'s `rifts.*`).

- **When and where** (`Overworld/Rifts.cs`, new): each hour of the clock a region may have one rift (a share of the hours, the rulings' number), at a place with ground and a centre tile, both worked out from the day's number, the hour and the region; open for that hour, after the region's Hall of Fame.
- **What is in one**: within twelve tiles of its centre the grass and the rods meet the rift's table (`Data/rifts.json`, ours: for each region, species of other regions by the ground and the region's own rarest at its post-game levels, with one shiny roll more), and three finds lie on its ground (things of the map with EX3's glint, picked up as an item ball is, gone for the day by a daily flag of ours) from a list of items (stones, shards, PP Ups and the like).
- **How it looks** (`Core/GameEngine.Rifts.cs`, new): a toast as it opens while the player is outdoors in the region, `SceneEffect.Rift` standing on its centre, a violet wash over the ground of its reach eased by distance (laid like a climate), motes rising, a sound of ours, its mark on the report.

### Soaring and the places of the day (EX7)

7. **The hideaways.** *Recommended:* twelve, laid by rule from pieces of ours, Generations 1 to 4 by type, opened with the National Pokédex. The alternatives draw them by hand with the room editor, or give them every generation's species by type.
8. **Who carries the player in the sky.** *Recommended:* the team's flier, the one whose Fly it is or the helper the field helpers lend. The alternative is a mount of ours, given once in each region after its Hall of Fame by a person of ours.
