# Plan 40 · Gifts, events, trades and the completed Pokédex

Written 2026-11-07, before any session, at the user's request: one of ten plans (35 to 44) from [the study of what else to add](studies/what-else-to-add-study.md).

**Goal**: the rewards that come from outside a battle, for a journey through every region with a carried team. A gift box that hands out gifts of our own by words, by dates and by milestones, through a courier in the Pokémon Centers (the later games' Mystery Gift as a mechanic, offline). Festivals of our own on the game's calendar. The regions' own in-game trades, which their plans left waiting, and a board of trades with made-up trainers that changes by the day, with blind trades beside it (Wonder Trade as a mechanic, offline). Every built region's Pokédex, and the whole National Pokédex, held by a test to ways of getting each species with nobody else, and what those tests owe placed. Diplomas for every Pokédex and a Pokédex of the species of our own. The later games' charms for a full Pokédex, with the critical captures the Catching Charm needs and the EXP a catch has given since Generation 6. Legendaries that come back after their region's Hall of Fame unless caught. And, optionally, records mixed between the player's own saves. Nothing here needs a server or reaches beyond the machine.

## Design

**Content of our own.** The mechanics are the games' (a gift box and its courier, a questionnaire that opens it, gifts by words and dates, blind trades and a board of requests, charms for a full Pokédex, critical captures, legendaries that return after the League), and the content in them is ours: the gifts, their senders, their cards and the words that call them up; the festivals' names, dates, decorations, stalls and lines; the made-up trainers of the board and the blind trades (names, looks and numbers from lists of our own); the couriers', designers' and desks' lines; and the event Pokémon, which are species of the data with moves, balls and ribbons the data allows, never a copy of a distribution. The regions' own in-game trades are those regions' content, read from their decompilations as numbers and names, as Platinum's four were, with lines of our own. The designers and couriers are the people the imported rooms already hold, playing their part in our words. No character, mascot or event of a specific game is recreated.

- **What is placed** (GE3), each as plan 03 · D12a placed Diamond's and Pearl's species in Sinnoh: the other version's species in that version's own places, through the overlays' `national` slots once the region's National condition holds (LeafGreen's in Kanto, Ruby's and Sapphire's in Hoenn, White 2's in Unova, SoulSilver's where Johto's rows lack one), read from PokeAPI's encounters of that version in the importer's cache; a Linking Cord from a person of ours in each region; FireRed's Game Corner Pokémon prizes, which plan 19 · K5 left (`celadon_city_game_corner_prize_room.txt`). ### The regions' own trades, the board and blind trades (GE2, GE8)

- **The trades as data.** `tools/DataImporter` reads each pinned decompilation's in-game trade table (FireRed's, Emerald's, HeartGold's, and Black 2's `res/field/trades/`) as numbers and names (species wanted and given, nickname, first trainer's name, number and look, IVs, personality, item, contest values) and writes `Data/trades.json`, which `NpcTrades.All` reads in place of its table in code; Platinum's four come from `res/npc_trades` the same way, and our three are rows of `tools/DataImporter/Overrides/trades.json`. A trade with a person keeps R12's rules (the given one's level, no trade evolution).
- **The board** (`Models/TradeBoard.cs`, GPU-free): three requests a day for each region, drawn from the day's number and the region by `SplitMix` (never `Dice`, so a day draws the same board however often it is looked at, as Buena's password and Pal Park's six are). Each is a `BoardRequest`: a made-up trainer (name, look and number from `Data/trade-board.json`'s lists of our own), a species wanted (one the player has seen, of the region's Pokédex, never legendary, mythical or ours) and its least level, and a Pokémon offered (a species of a region entered, never legendary or mythical, ours only once `OursSlots.Open`, a regional form only of a region entered), with an item and, one time in four, the language of a trainer from abroad (so the Masuda method's foreign parent can be had with nobody else). It arrives at the given one's level.
- **Blind trades** (`TradeBoard.Blind`): give any Pokémon that isn't an Egg or the last able to fight, and receive the day's nth draw: a species of the regions entered, first stages most often, at the given one's level, from a made-up trainer; five a day (decision 8).
- **The way through.** `trade board <n>` and `trade blind`, new words of R12's `trade` in both hosts; the Pokémon chosen from the team or the boxes; the received one through `ReceiveTradedPokemon` (its trade evolution plays, as the later games' blind trades do); a run under way refused as the Home box refuses (`HomeMoves.RunHolds`); the randomiser asked as `givepokemon` asks. Today's trades are `SaveData.TradeBoard` (null when none).
- **Where.** Sinnoh's GTS receptionist (`GlobalTerminal1F.Gts`, her Linking Cord kept) and one desk in each other built region: the link receptionists of Celadon's and Lilycove's Centers' upper floors, a desk of ours in Goldenrod's Pokémon Center (HeartGold's Global Terminal, warp 16 of `goldenrod.json`, is a shut building whose sign says so, and stays shut), a desk of ours in Castelia's. The Global Terminal's backdrop lady gives a backdrop for a first board trade. `UI/TradeBoardScreen.cs` (`GameState.TradeBoard`) shows the day's three cards, the blind trade's row with how many are left, and the trainer's card after a trade.

- **Critical captures** (`Ruleset.CriticalCaptures`, the modern rules'; decision 5): in `CatchCalculator.Shakes`, which takes a `BattleConditions` and the battle's generator, one more roll of a `RollKind` of its own (appended, so `BattleRandom`'s other kinds keep their draws), a chance from the catch rate the ball and the foe make and from the National Pokédex's caught count as the later games read them, doubled by the Catching Charm; a critical capture is one shake check, `BallThrown` carries `Critical` at its end, and the face plays one shake and a sound of its own. The rest of the later catch arithmetic stays as R28 left it.
- **EXP for a catch** (`Ruleset.ExpForACatch`, the modern rules' only; the critic's item 1): since X and Y a caught wild Pokémon gives the team EXP as a faint does. `BattleCore.ThrowBall` (`Battle/Sim/BattleCore.cs`) emits `Caught` and calls `End(BattleResult.EnemyCaught)` at its three ends and never calls `AwardExp`, which only a faint reaches (`if (!down.IsPlayerSide) AwardExp(down)`). The catch awards it for the caught foe after the `Caught` event and before `End`, so its EXP, level and move lines play as after a faint and before the battle's end; `BattleCore.Give` still applies the crossed region's half, the run's cap and Easy's. A `Ruleset` row of the modern rules alone, so `battles-platinum.txt` holds; the Frontier's battles give no EXP and are unmoved; a `BattleRecord` keeps the rule through its rules preset.

### Legendaries met again (GE6)

Every legendary or mythical met by a script comes back when its region's Hall of Fame is entered again, unless caught, as Platinum's and the later games' do: each meeting's script sets a caught flag of its own on `RESULT` 3 (`FLAG_<REGION>_CAUGHT_<SPECIES>`, beside the fought flag it sets today), and each region's Hall of Fame script gains Sinnoh's lines (`if not flag … clearflag …`), its knocked-out roamers set loose again (`Roamers.Defeated`). Decision 7 keeps them in their own places.

### The gift box (GE7)

### Festivals (GE9)

## Risks

- **Rewards that shorten the journey**: blind trades and the board give species freely. Five blind trades a day, the date's own draw, species of regions already entered, no legendaries, and the charms only after the work.
- **A legendary met twice**: a wrong flag would let a caught legendary come back. The caught flag is set by its own meeting, an older save is marked by the version step, and the test plays every meeting caught and beaten.
- **The date in the pictures**: a festival or a dated gift on the harness's day would move every mode's shots. None covers 1 June; the `gifts` mode moves `FixedDate` and puts it back.
- **Critical captures move catches**: under the modern rules every catch draws one more roll, so the harness's catches (`demo`'s ball) may change. `battles-modern.txt` should hold still, since the trainer AI throws no ball, and the session checks that it does; it runs the modes that catch before and after and says what moved.
- **Many places at once**: couriers, trades, designers and festivals touch overlays in five regions. Each region's files are hot by the overview's rules, and the content check runs on each.
- **Four decompilations' tables**: their trade tables are read as numbers and names only, from the pins in `tools/DataImporter/Sources.cs`, never their text; a version's difference is FireRed's, Black 2's or HeartGold's as the region's import already chose.
- **Words nobody finds**: a gift by words with no hint is a gift nobody gets; the test holds each pair to a line that says it.
- **One lobby, two people**: GE7's questionnaire man and courier and plan 43 · LF10's film desk both put a person in Jubilife TV's lobby (`JubilifeTV1F`, which also holds the Lottery Corner); whichever session lands second takes another tile and says so.
- **Other saves**: the gift box, the board and the couriers read nothing but the save in play; record mixing reads the other slots read-only, writes only the save in play, and passes a damaged slot over.

## Decisions for the user

1. **Where gifts are handed over.** *Recommended:* a courier in every Pokémon Center (Johto's Marts) of every built region, the imported couriers taking the part and Sinnoh's and Unova's Centers gaining one, as the games did. 3. **Which completion earns which charm.** *Recommended:* the first regional Pokédex seen whole earns the Oval Charm, the second the Exp. Charm, the third the Catching Charm and the fourth the Mark Charm, in the order the player earns them, and the National Pokédex caught the Shiny Charm, any region's designer handing over what is due.
