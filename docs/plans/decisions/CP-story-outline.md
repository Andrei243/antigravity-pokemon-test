Written 2026-11-12 from the user's own beats of that day (the climax, the two professors, the villain team implied, the level-`???` fight, the professors staying Pokémon) and plan 46's fixed facts (the Tales, the winter masks, the Sound type, Team Zenith, gyms in any order, the sun that must not stand still). **Everything here is a proposal except the beats marked "the user's"**; names are working names, to be replaced by invented ones with Romanian roots (decision 4) and reviewed by the user (decision 7).

## The user's beats (fixed)

1. **Two professors**, one woman and one man. Doina Lupescu (*lup*, the wolf) studies the tales and the songs that carry them; Ilie Sorescu (*soare*, the sun; Sfântul Ilie is the thunder saint of the folk tales) studies the sky and the sun-tales. Both are proposals; the user left the name of the man to us.
2. **Team Zenith** is implied as the one using **Furtunaur** for its ends. The professors arrive only because each had a hunch that the player would need help against the team.
3. **The first fight with Furtunaur is lost**: its level shows as `???`, it beats the whole team.
4. The professors arrive, **heal the team and help**; in binding Furtunaur they are **transformed**: Ilie into **Solzmeu**, Doina into **Lunacolac**. They weaken Furtunaur so the player can battle it and **catch it**.

6. **The professors stay Pokémon** (no restoration, no extraction), for the game to have a darker side.

## The shape of the story

Carpathia has eight gyms in any order, so the story is keyed to **how many badges the player holds** (as Paldea's is) and to **the tales found**, never to a fixed route. Four acts, three of them movable:

| Act | Opens at | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Prologue | the start | the starter, the two professors' lessons, the first tale, the rival |
| I · The first signs | 1 badge | the sky behaves strangely; Team Zenith's first work |
| II · The stilling | 4 badges | a day that does not end; the stronghold; the leader slips away |
| III · The ridge | 8 badges and the Champion's door | the climax |
| After | the Hall of Fame | the roamers, the sanctuary, the empty labs |

## Cast (working names)

- **The player**, a young Trainer from the plains who hears the tales told at home.
- **Professor Doina Lupescu**, whose lab is in an old walled town of the plateau. Warm, dry, sings under her breath; keeps a book of tales she calls "the only true map of the country". She gives the **Book of Tales** and teaches the first tale.
- **Professor Ilie Sorescu**, whose observatory is on the first pass. Gruff and funny, loves the sun and says it is the only thing that never lies; collects the sun-tales. He gives the **first mask pattern** and the first lesson on the sky.
- **The rival**: **Radu**, a shepherd's son who wants to be the region's best singer and Trainer, or his sister **Ilinca** by the player's look (as Hoenn's rival is the hero the player isn't). He sings badly and knows it; his arc is that he learns to listen. In the post-game he becomes the apprentice who keeps a lab open.
- **Team Zenith** (*Echipa Zenit*): wants the sun at its highest for ever. Its leader, **Aurelian Zenovie**, is an astronomer from the capital who lost someone to a long winter; he does not think he is a villain. Grunts are the "Stillers", dressed in gold and white. His four executives each tend one stage of the plan: the Stilling machine, the binding chains, the stolen tales and the false dawn.
- **The two apprentices**, one of each professor: **Mara** (Doina's) and **Costel** (Ilie's). They take over the labs afterwards.
- **The elders** of the Tale sites: the people who give each tale, so that tales are found, never bought.

## The eight places and their trials (types and trials suggested, in any order)

Each place has a Tale site beside it, and **a tale found there**. Trials are tasks in the open world, then the leader.

| Place | Type | Trial | The tale found there |
|---|---|---|---|
| the coast and its old casino | Water | a boat course through the harbour | the sea-tale |
| the delta's floating villages | Flying | gliding from reed-tower to reed-tower | the Ielele's night dance |
| the high pass | Rock | a stone-balancing climb on the hairpin | Baba Dochia's cold |
| the ice cave | Ice | a slide through a frozen salt-mine's lake | the cold that returns |
| the wooden north | Grass | a *căluș* dance with the masked dancers | the Draco's banner (first part) |

The **Zmeu's** and the **Vârcolac's** tales are the two the professors will tell at the climax, which is why they are found at the plateau and the castle, but any order works: the climax asks only that the player holds them.

## Act by act

**Prologue.** The player's village keeps its midsummer night, the Sânziene. The professors' lessons are in two different places: Doina teaches that a tale is a thing you can *tell* in battle, Ilie that the sun is the clock the country keeps. Each says something that will matter: Doina, *a tale told long enough gets a body*; Ilie, *the sun does not stand still, and that is its only mercy*. The rival is introduced losing a singing contest.

**Act I · 1 badge.** The sky behaves: a noon that lasts an hour too long over a village, a night that comes late. Stillers appear at first as a rumour (strangers in gold buying clocks). The player and the rival meet one in a market; the Stiller leaves a brochure of a new "Day Without End". Doina and Ilie each telephone the player in the same week, each with a hunch that something is wrong, without knowing the other did.

**Act II · 4 badges.** One day does not end: the sun hangs at noon over the whole plateau for a day and a night, the shepherds' flocks stop in the road, the festivals' lanterns cannot be lit. The Stillers' machine, in the old castle on the crag (which doubles as the Ghost gym's place on a different floor), is found and broken in an assault at the castle's top. **Zenovie is not there**: he has already gone with his chained book to the ridge, leaving a note that says only *"It must be done before the winter."* The professors are not in the scene; each is told by the player's phone call to wait.

**Act III · the ridge.** On the high ridge, in a storm, the hairpin road climbs to a glacial lake and a tunnel. Furtunaur is there, bound by braided chains of lightning, its book chained to its back, roaring, thunder walking down the tunnel. The Stillers' equipment is on the ridge: a brass frame of rings and mirrors, a locked cabinet of tales. Zenovie is gone (the storm, the lake, a lantern left burning; the game does not say). The team is implied as the cause: the player faces what they unleashed.

1. **The first fight.** Furtunaur's level shows as `???`. It attacks, and the whole team falls. This is a scripted loss: no white-out, the screen goes white and the sound drops.
2. **The professors arrive.** Doina and Ilie, each on a hunch, each by a different road, arrive within a few seconds of each other. They heal the team. They say little: *"I had a feeling."* *"So did I."* The rival may be on the ridge too, to watch.
3. **The binding.** They do not know how to unbind Furtunaur, only how to *hold* it: they tell the two old tales, the sun-tale and the moon-tale, and the Book of Tales answers. The storm quiets as a weather. But a tale told that hard takes its teller: the lightning turns on them. **Ilie becomes Solzmeu; Doina becomes Lunacolac.** There is no speech: the player sees it happen.
4. **The second fight.** Furtunaur is weakened, bound between the two new legendaries' light and night. It is battled with its real level shown and its HP low, and **caught**.
5. **The roamers.** Solzmeu and Lunacolac, once the storm clears, turn and leave without a look: they have no one to follow. They begin to roam.

**After the ridge.** The Champion is met before the ridge and, as Cynthia is in Sinnoh, helps the player at different moments of the story (Act I/II scenes, the stronghold assault, the ridge); the Hall of Fame and the League come after. Then:
- **The empty labs.** Doina's lab and Ilie's observatory stand open; their apprentices Mara and Costel keep them, and each lab keeps a table of unfinished notes the player can read, including the last page each professor wrote about the other.
- **The roamers.** Solzmeu and Lunacolac roam the region, hard to catch, appearing at the Tale sites the professors showed the player. Their first appearance quotes something one of the professors always said. **When they are caught, their Pokédex entries and cries carry something of them, never explained outright.**
- **The Dacian sanctuary** of stone circles in the forest: Stindrac the war banner (the mythical) and the post-game Tale of the banner.
- **The rival** keeps the observatory open and sings a verse from the professors' favourite doină at the sanctuary, once; that is all.
- **Team Zenith's remnants** are not a story: a few Stillers can be met, no longer in gold, running the market clocks and saying nothing.
