# Plan 46 · Carpathia's story, chapter by chapter

Written 2026-11-12, the same day as [the outline](CP-story-outline.md) and the user's answers to it, at the user's request ("the story plan should go on"). It turns the approved framework into chapters, scene by scene, so that CP18 to CP27 each know what they build. **Everything here is a proposal** except what the outline marks as the user's and the answers of 2026-11-12; names are invented with Romanian roots (decision 4), and every Romanian word I am not sure of is in the review table at the end (decision 7). Nothing is taken from any game: the beats are ours, the lines are outlines to be written in the sessions.

Read with: [plan 46](../46-carpathia.md), [the outline](CP-story-outline.md), [the starters and legendaries](CP-proposals-starters-legendaries.md), [the Pokédex briefs](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) (files 1 to 5), [the study](../studies/romania-region-study.md).

## What this settles

The sources were written over three days and disagree in places. The order of authority is: **the user's answers of 2026-11-12** (the outline's last two sections) win over **the outline's proposals**, which win over **the proposals file's "Story" lines** and **plan 46's first sketch** (its CP25 bullet, "the Zmeu and the Vârcolac" as the villain's chapter). What follows corrects each:

1. **The ridge opens at 7 badges**, not 8 (the outline's act table says 8; the answers say 7). The League asks all eight. So a player may fight the climax with one gym left, and win the eighth after it.
2. **The twelve tales are plan 46's twelve.** The outline's table had the cold tale twice (the high pass and the ice cave) and a "sea-tale" that is not among them. Here each of the twelve is found once (section 5).
3. **The Solomonar's tale is Furtunaur's**, found in its chained book on the ridge after the catch, as the proposals call it "the region's last tale". The outline had it at the opera; the opera gets the Miorița.
4. **Solzmeu and Lunacolac are not found at the stronghold** (the proposals' Story lines): they do not exist until the ridge, where the professors become them. The stronghold holds the Stilling machine.
5. **Who keeps the labs.** The outline gives the labs to Mara and Costel and also says the rival "becomes the apprentice who keeps a lab open". Settled here as: **Mara keeps Doina's lab; Costel keeps Ilie's observatory, and the rival comes to help him** (he keeps its door and its lamp; Costel keeps the instruments). Open question 7 asks the user to confirm.
6. **The prologue is a *șezătoare***, the village's evening of tales, held whatever the calendar says, where the outline had the Sânziene night: the festivals keep their own dates (plan 40), and a new game may begin in any month. The Sânziene stay as the first tale taught.
7. **The Draco's tale** is found whole at the Dacian sanctuary in the post-game, not in two parts (the outline's "first part" in the wooden north is dropped: the wooden north gives Muma Pădurii's, the Grass starter's tale).
8. **The region file's one professor is Doina.** She speaks the new-game introduction and gives the starters; Ilie is the story's second professor, met in the prologue at his observatory.

## 1 · The chapters

### How the story is keyed

Eight gyms in any order, so nothing waits for a gym by name: a scene waits for **how many badges the player holds** (`if badges >= N`) and, at the ridge, for **two tales held** (the Zmeu's and the Vârcolac's). Each scene stands **in its own place** and is open from its count on; the region's phone sends the player there when a count is reached (the professors and the apprentices call, as Johto's contacts do). A scene left undone stays open; the next act does not wait for it unless this document says so.

| Act | Opens at | Where | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prologue | a new game, or the crossing from Paldea | the home village, the plateau town, the first pass | the *șezătoare*, the starters, the Book of Tales, the first tale, the rival |
| I · The first signs | 1, 2 and 3 badges | the plateau town's market, the observatory, the stone faces, the painted monastery, the coast's casino | strangers buying clocks, a stolen wall, a night that comes late |
| II · The stilling | 4 and 5 badges (6 for the capital's scene) | the plateau (the endless noon), the castle's keep, the salt mine, the capital | a day that does not end, the stronghold's assault, the chains |
| III · The ridge | 7 badges, the plateau's and the castle's among them | the hairpin road, the glacial lake, the tunnel, the crest | the climax |
| The League | 8 badges | the capital's palace | the Elite Four and the Champion |
| After | the Hall of Fame | everywhere | the empty labs, the roamers, the sanctuary, the rival's verse |

**Levels** are Carpathia's own (1 to 70, plan 46), the mapped figure in brackets as plan 46's band gives it (story about 262 to 286, Champion about 286, post-game to about 296; CP18 writes the region's `levels` and these figures move with it). The first three gyms are fixed at **15, 22 and 29**; the fourth to the eighth are scaled to the badges held, as Paldea's are: **35, 41, 47, 52, 57** for the fourth to the eighth won, whichever gym it is.

**The ridge's rule.** The crest above the glacial lake opens at 7 badges **if the plateau's and the castle's are among them**, because the binding needs the Zmeu's tale (the plateau's) and the Vârcolac's (the castle's) in the player's Book. With seven badges and one of those two missing, the ranger at the lake says the storm is too high, and Ilie's notes (or Doina's phone call) say the old tales of the sun and the moon are the only rope for it. So the gym left for after the ridge is one of the other six. **The ridge also waits for Act II's two scenes on the way**: the stronghold (II-2) and the chains (II-3) must be done, or the crest would come with the Stilling machine still running and the executives' "second" battles fought first. A player with seven badges who skipped either is told so at the lake (Costel calls about the noon that will not end, or the ranger has seen chains carried up from the salt mine "that stopped halfway"). II-4 (the capital) is not needed.

**What "the ridge opens" means on the map.** The hairpin road is the region's way over the mountains and is walked from the start of Act I (the high pass's gym town and the first pass's observatory are on it, below the lake). What is shut until Act III is **the last stretch above the glacial lake**: the tunnel through the top and the crest beyond it, held by the storm (hail in the tunnel's mouth, a ranger's barrier, the lightning walking down the road at night). After the climax the crest is ordinary ground with its view and a quiet lake.

### Prologue · The evening of tales

**Opens**: a new game begun in Carpathia, or a Champion crossing from Paldea.

**P1 · The home village, an evening.** The player's home is a village of the southern plain, on the river's terrace. Tonight is the village's *șezătoare*, the winter-evening gathering where the women spin and the old people tell tales (held whatever the calendar says: it is the village's evening for the professor's visit). The player wakes, comes down; Mom (or Dad: the player's parent, by the region's file) says the professor from the plateau has come to hear the old people's tales and has brought something in a painted chest.
- *Who*: the player's parent, the village's elder (Bunica Floarea, working name), the neighbours, the rival.
- *Said, in outline*: the elder tells a short tale of the midsummer fairies (the Sânziene) while the spindles turn; the rival, a shepherd's son from the hill farm, sings a verse badly and the room laughs kindly; he says he will be the best singer *and* the best Trainer in the country.
- *Features*: a grid room (the house), an open village (plan 32), `scene`, `act`, `mood`, `pose sit`, the voice of the elder.

**P2 · The painted chest.** Professor **Doina Lupescu** opens a painted dowry chest (*lada de zestre*) with the three starters on its lid's three painted panels: Lynkit, Salamint, Pelicub. She asks the player to choose; the rival takes the one strong against the player's (Lynkit against Pelicub, Salamint against Lynkit, Pelicub against Salamint). Doina's line to remember: *a tale told long enough gets a body*.
- *Gets*: the starter.
- *Features*: the briefcase's screen re-skinned as the chest (`StarterSelectScreen` with the region's look), `{starter}` words, `namerival` (Radu or Ilinca by the player's look, open question 8 asks whether the player may rename them).

**P3 · The first battle.** Outside under the walnut tree, the rival's battle at once (`battle rival as … first canlose`). Doina watches, then asks the player to come to her lab in the plateau town tomorrow.

**P4 · The pony.** In the morning the rival's family at the hill farm lends the player a Hucul pony's saddle and the pony (Hutulmare's line is the region's mount; plan 34 · PD2's `MovementGrants`): *the pony* is the first traversal grant. The rival's father teaches the saddle in one line; the shepherd dogs (Ciobipup, Mioritar) round up the sheep in the yard.
- *Gets*: the pony (plains and passes: the trot and the sprint).
- *Features*: `MovementGrants` on the capsule, a grant by script.

**P5 · Doina's lab in the walled plateau town.** The first town of the plateau (painted houses whose roofs have eyes, a clock tower over the gate). Doina gives the **Pokédex** and the **Book of Tales**, and teaches the **Sânziene's tale** (midsummer: healing doubled), the region's first, telling the player how a tale is told in battle. Her apprentice **Mara** shows how to catch (the catching lesson: `catchinglesson "Barzlet" by "Mara"`).
- *Gets*: the Pokédex, the Book of Tales, the Sânziene's tale, five Poké Balls.
- *Features*: `tale learn "sanziene"` (CP12), the Book's screen (CP12), `catchinglesson … by`.

**P6 · Ilie's observatory on the first pass.** Up the lower hairpins, a white dome over the plateau. Professor **Ilie Sorescu**, gruff and funny, shows the player the sun through the telescope's filter, says the sun is the clock the whole country keeps, and gives the **first mask pattern** (the bear) and a handful of felt and fur for the crafting kit (plan 39; if the bag has no kit, his apprentice **Costel** hands a spare). His line to remember: *the sun does not stand still, and that is its only mercy*. He mentions, in passing, an old colleague from the capital who "loves the sun too much" (Aurelian Zenovie, unnamed here).
- *Gets*: the bear mask's pattern, mask materials.
- *Features*: plan 39's crafting kit, `craft learn "mask_bear"` (CP13), a grid room (the dome) with a telescope set piece.

**P7 · The country opens.** Back at the observatory's door, Costel unfolds the map: eight places with a leader each, "in any order you like, my professor says the sun doesn't mind". From here the region is open by pony.

**A Champion crossing from Paldea** lands on the coast's harbour (`Arrival`). Doina is on the quay collecting a crate of old books off the same ship; she knows who they are from the papers, asks if they have heard tales told, and brings them by pony to the village's *șezătoare*, where P1 to P7 play with the lines that know a Champion (`if CROSSED_IN`). They choose a starter as anyone does. `namerival` is asked at P2.

### Act I · The first signs (1 to 3 badges)

**I-1 · Strangers buying clocks** (1 badge; the plateau town's market). In the market square under the clock tower, a man in a white coat with a gold sunburst on the chest is buying every clock the clockmaker has and paying double. He is a **Stiller** of **Team Zenith**. He gives the player and the rival a brochure for "a Day Without End". He battles the rival; the player battles a second Stiller.
- **The Champion's first meeting.** A woman with a long braid and a *bucium* across her back, who has been listening at the clockmaker's door, asks the Stiller one question ("Who sold you the hands?": every clock the Stillers buy has its hands taken off) and he leaves. She introduces herself only as **Ruxandra**, a player of the *bucium* who walks the country's old sites; she says nothing of being the Champion (the player learns it at the League, as Sinnoh's player learns Cynthia's title). She tells, in three lines, her own tale (section 2) and says she will be at the stone faces.
- *Features*: open-world people (plan 32 · OW6), `battle self`, a tag battle not yet.

**I-2 · The sun's hero** (1 badge; the observatory). Ilie, back from the market rumour, teaches **Făt-Frumos' tale** (youth without age: no stat may fall), the tale of the hero who rides the sun's horse. If the player's starter is Salamint's line, he notes that its line is the hero's smith.
- *Gets*: Făt-Frumos' tale.
- **After the ridge** this scene can't play (Ilie is gone): Costel gives the tale from Ilie's notes instead, with a line of his own (`if flag FLAG_CARPATHIA_RIDGE_DONE`).

**I-3 · The glide** (2 badges; the plateau of stone faces, at the cliff's edge). Ruxandra waits by the great stone face looking down at the plateau. She shows the player the old fliers' way off the heights (the zburător's, she calls it) and gives the **glide** (a cloak with a stiffened felt wing, or the player's Flying Pokémon's: CP21 decides how it looks). She leaves for the coast.
- *Gets*: the glide (plan 34 · PD2's glide on the capsule).

**I-4 · The rival on the pony road** (2 badges; wherever the player first rides the road between two provinces after the second badge). The rival's second battle. He has learned the Sânziene's tale and tells it in battle (a trainer's tale, CP11).

**I-5 · The blank wall** (2 badges; the painted monastery in the wooden north). The monastery's outer wall is painted with the tales, scene by scene. One scene has gone blank overnight. The nuns' painter (a Carpathian Smeargle with an old monk) says strangers came with a cabinet. Executive **Teodor Uitescu** is in the cloister, lifting the paint off a second scene into a glass-fronted cabinet: Team Zenith is taking the tales so that no one can tell them against the endless day. Battle; he leaves with the cabinet but the second scene, half lifted, settles back on the wall.
- *Gets*: the monastery's thanks (a Fresco item for crafting, CP13), nothing else: the tale painted there is the Grass leader's to tell.
- *Features*: `effect sparkles` as the paint settles back, the wall's scenes as a prop whose look changes by flag (`FLAG_CARPATHIA_WALL_LIFTED`), `battle self as "zenith_teodor_1"`.

**I-6 · The night that comes late** (3 badges; the coast's old casino on the shore). Night does not come to the port: the casino's dome has been fitted with mirrors that throw the sunset back over the town for hours. Executive **Sabina Oglindaru** stands on the dome's gallery. A tag battle with the rival against Sabina and a Stiller (`battle sabina and stiller with rival`), then she breaks the mirror circuit herself and leaves by boat, saying the false dawn was only "a rehearsal". Night falls on the port all at once; the casino's sprite (the Spiriduș) is seen in a window.
- *Features*: the stage's light (plan 25 · `StageLight`) for the over-bright evening, `effect burst` as the mirrors go dark, an open map's hour held for the scene (engine need 4).

**I-7 · Two calls** (3 badges, on the next step outdoors after both I-5 and I-6 or after any two of I-1 to I-6). Doina calls: she has a feeling about this team, the tales are being taken for a reason. A minute later Ilie calls: he has a feeling about the sun, the clocks have no hands. Neither mentions the other. (The outline's beat: each with a hunch, not knowing the other did.) Like every scene with the professors, it closes for good once the ridge is done; II-1's calls close with it.
- *Features*: the region's phone (engine need 7), `wait` between the two.

### Act II · The stilling (4 to 6 badges)

**II-1 · Noon that does not end** (4 badges; the next time the player is on the plateau). The sun stops at noon over the plateau. A shepherd's flock stands still in the road; the plateau town's festival lanterns cannot be lit because evening never comes; the clock tower's hands have been taken. **What it changes**: the plateau's areas only (not the coast, the delta, the north, the capital), **both the look and the hour**: the hour reads noon there, so wild tables and evolutions that ask for night cannot be had on the plateau while it lasts; they can anywhere else. It lasts until II-2 is won, and II-2 is open at once.
- Doina and Ilie each call the player within the hour; each offers to come. The player is given the choice "Wait, I'll go" (the only answer that goes on): each agrees to wait. (The professors are not in Act II.)
- *Features*: a story-held hour over named areas (engine need 3), the stage's light of the open sky at noon held, the rival's call.

**II-2 · The stronghold** (4 badges; the castle on the crag). The castle is two buildings on one crag: **the lower halls and chapel are the Ghost gym** (open from the start, any order) and **the keep above is Team Zenith's**, shut "for restoration" by a company of theirs until Act II. Now the keep's gate is open and the Stillers hold it.
1. At the gate, **Ruxandra** is already there, says the player shouldn't go up alone, and battles beside the player: a tag battle against two Stillers (`battle a and b with ruxandra`).
2. In the courtyard the **rival** stays to hold the stair against the Stillers coming up from the chapel side ("Go! I'll make them listen to me sing!").
3. Up the keep's stair, three floors of Stillers (open-world trainers inside a grid room: the keep is a grid room behind its door, plan 32 · decision 6).
4. At the top, under the open roof, the **Stilling machine**: a brass orrery of rings and mirrors catching the sun and holding it. Executive **Grigore Pendulescu**, the team's clockmaker, battles beside it.
5. Beaten, he tries to stop the machine himself and can't; the player's starter breaks its last ring (a scripted move, `anim`/`effect burst`). The sun races to evening across the plateau: the held hour is released and eased to the clock's own over a few seconds while the camera looks out over the plateau (engine need 3).
6. **Zenovie is not there.** On his desk, a note in a careful hand: *"It must be done before the winter."* A chain's broken link lies beside it. Ruxandra reads the note and says nothing.
- *Gets*: the plateau's evening back; the Stillers' Key (opens the salt mine's foundry door, II-3).
- *Features*: `battle … with`, the grid keep, `letterbox`, a held effect, `camera look` over the plateau, the hour released.

**II-3 · The chains** (5 badges; the salt mine, its great hall with the lake, the wheel and the boat). Below the salt lake, a foundry where Stillers are forging braided chains of something that crackles. Executive **Zamfira Lănțoiu** commands it. She battles the player on the lake's boat landing; beaten, she has the chains carried out by the mine's old lift and goes with them. She drops her ledger: a column of numbers, a drawing of three heads, and *"two tales to hold it: the sun's and the moon's"*. The player can't read more.
- *Features*: the cave at full size (plan 32 carves), the boat on the salt lake (a set piece, or the delta's boat grant), `battle self as "zenith_zamfira_1"`.

**II-4 · The second sun** (6 badges; the capital, at the arch on the boulevard). At dusk a second "sun" rises inside the capital's arch: Sabina's false dawn, mirrors on the arch catching the city's lights. The city's people stand in the boulevard staring. Sabina's second battle (alone), then her mirrors go dark. She says the real dawn will be "on the ridge, and it will not end", and that she never wanted the winter gone, only the dark. She is not seen again in the story.
- *Features*: the capital's open map, the stage's light, `effect burst`.

**Not before the ridge**: Teodor's second battle and Zamfira's second are on the ridge's road (III-1, III-2). Grigore is beaten once. The rival's last battle before the ridge is III-0.

### Act III · The ridge (7 badges, the plateau's and the castle's among them)

This is the user's climax. Each step below is one scene (`scene`), and the whole act is one evening into one night.

**III-0 · The rival at the foot** (the high pass's town, by the road up). The rival is waiting with the pony: he battles the player (his strongest team before the League, and he tells the Miorița's tale if he has it, the Sânziene's if not), then says he is coming up to watch, "someone should be there to remember it".

**III-1 · The lower bends** (the hairpin road above the high pass, at the first wide bend). Executive **Teodor Uitescu**, without his cabinet, sits on the parapet. He battles a second time, half-heartedly; beaten, he says the cabinet went up hours ago, "with him", and that he only took the tales because "a tale told against you is a weapon, and we didn't want a war". He walks down the road and is not seen again.

**III-2 · The glacial lake and the tunnel's mouth.** The lake below the crest, a storm over the tunnel. Executive **Zamfira Lănțoiu** and a line of Stillers hold the tunnel's mouth. Ruxandra comes up the road behind the player. Zamfira's second battle (alone, the strongest Zenith team, level 54). Beaten, Zamfira says the chains are on it now and "nobody can take them off, not even him", and that the Stillers are leaving. **The Champion's reason not to go on**: the Stillers who are leaving are leaving *down* the road in panic, the storm is bringing rocks onto the bends, and the rival and the pony are in the road; Ruxandra stays at the tunnel's mouth to hold the road and get everyone down, and sends the player through: *"It is your Book they need up there, not my horn. Go."* The rival stays with her.

**III-3 · The crest.** Through the tunnel onto the crest in a storm (hail, lightning, the whole sky dark at sunset). **Furtunaur**, three heads, bound by braided chains of lightning to iron stakes in the rock, its book chained to its back, roars; thunder walks down the tunnel behind the player. No Zenith person is there. **What is there**: the brass frame of rings and mirrors (a second machine, half built), the locked glass cabinet of tales, and **a lantern left burning** at the edge of the crest above the lake, a length of chain beside it running over the edge. Nothing more is said: this is all the game ever shows of Zenovie (the user's answer 1). The player walks to the bound dragon; it sees them.

**III-4 · The first fight.** Furtunaur's level shows as **`???`**. Its first move knocks out the lead; every move knocks out a Pokémon; nothing the player does moves its HP bar. When the last Pokémon falls **the screen goes white and the sound drops** (a held white `effect burst`, the music stopped). No white-out: the game goes on in the same place.
- *What the battle needs*: a wild battle the player cannot win, run from, catch from or white out of: the level hidden, the foe taking no damage, a loss that ends in the script (`canlose` on a `wildbattle`) and that a Nuzlocke run does not bury (open question 4). Built since 2026-11-12 (`docs/mechanics/rulings.md`, "Staged battles"): `wildbattle "Furtunaur" … hidelevel canlose`; a staged battle buries no Nuzlocke dead.

**III-5 · The arrival.** Out of the white: the storm, the player on one knee. Footsteps from two sides of the crest, a few seconds apart: **Ilie** up the old shepherds' path from the east, **Doina** up the screes from the west. Each kneels and heals the team (the healing `canlose` gives is shown as theirs: `anim heal`-like light from a lantern and a flask). They look at each other.
- Ilie: *"I had a feeling."*
- Doina: *"So did I."*
- Nothing else is said of how they came. (Nobody called them: the user's beat is that each came on a hunch.)

**III-6 · The binding.** They do not know how to unbind Furtunaur, only how to hold it. Doina holds out her hand for the player's **Book of Tales**; it opens by itself to two pages. Ilie reads **the Zmeu's tale** (the sun cannot set) and Doina **the Vârcolac's** (the eclipse), together, over the storm (the tales' own layers of the battle music, their skies over the crest: gold on Ilie's side, deep blue on Doina's). The storm quiets into a weather; the chains brighten; Furtunaur sinks to the rock.
- Then the lightning turns on them. **There is no speech**: the player sees it happen. Letterbox, slow motion; a burst of gold where Ilie stands, a burst of night-blue where Doina stands; when the light falls, **Solzmeu** stands where Ilie stood, the sun-disc behind its shoulders, and **Lunacolac** where Doina stood, the crescent in its jaws. Doina's scarf is caught on Lunacolac's mane; Ilie's spectacles lie on the rock.
- *Features*: `letterbox`, `slowmo`, `effect burst` ×2 and `effect beam`, `camera path`, the two tales' skies (CP12), `hide doina`/`hide ilie` and their flags, `show solzmeu`/`show lunacolac` (open-map people drawn as Pokémon, plan 32 · OW6/OW9's figures).

**III-7 · The second fight.** Furtunaur, bound between the two new legendaries' light and night (Solzmeu and Lunacolac stand on either side of the field as part of the stage, people of the map drawn as Pokémon, never in the battle), is battled with **its real level shown, 56**, and its HP at a third. It can't be run from.
- **If it faints**: it rises again in its chains at a third of its HP and the battle is fought again at once (the script loops; a line says the chains will not let it fall).
- **If the balls run out**: the Stillers' cabinet beside the frame holds a box of Ultra Balls (an item the script gives before the second fight to any bag with fewer than five).
- **The catch.** On a catch, Furtunaur's chains break into light.
- **Runs**: under a run that bars catching, the second battle is won by a knock-out and Furtunaur, unchained, lies down on the crest, and is met again there after the Hall of Fame; under a Monotype run it goes to the box as any catch does (open question 4).
- *Features*: `wildbattle "Furtunaur" 56 nofleeing hp 33` (a starting-HP option is new, engine need 1), the loop, `RESULT` 3.

**III-8 · The roamers.** The storm clears into a cold clear night with the moon up and the sun's last red behind the ridge. Solzmeu turns to face the dawn's side; Lunacolac to the moon's. They do not look at the player. Then the **first creepy moment** (section 6, V1): Solzmeu's growl has a man's words inside it, Ilie's *"the sun does not stand still"*, and Lunacolac answers with four notes of the tune Doina hummed in her lab. They leave: Solzmeu off the crest's east edge in a gust of heat, Lunacolac down the western screes. They begin to roam (section 6).

**III-9 · What is left.** Furtunaur's chained book has fallen open: the player learns **the Solomonar's tale** (the storm: a weather the teller owns). The cabinet of tales opens with the chains: its stolen scenes drift out as light toward the north (the monastery's wall is whole again when visited). Ruxandra and the rival come through the tunnel; the rival sees the scarf and the spectacles and says nothing; Ruxandra picks up the spectacles and gives them to the player. Her one line: *"Bring these to the observatory. Then come to the capital when you have all eight."*
- *Gets*: the Solomonar's tale, Ilie's spectacles (a key item, given to Costel in III-10), Doina's scarf if the player picks it up (a key item, given to Mara).

**III-10 · The labs** (the next visit to each). At the observatory Costel has set two cups out, one for Ilie; he takes the spectacles and puts them on the telescope's eyepiece. At Doina's lab Mara has the kettle on; she takes the scarf and hangs it on the lab's door. Each says only that the professor "will be back when the tales say so". (The darker side the user asked for: nobody says the professors are gone.)

### The League (8 badges)

**L-1 · The palace.** The capital's palace, too big for its square, is the Pokémon League. Its guard counts eight badges.
**L-2 · The Elite Four**, each in a room of the palace (the rooms are CP26's; their types proposed here): **Dark** (the night watchman of the old town), **Fighting** (a *căluș* master), **Fairy** (a keeper of the Sânziene's meadows), **Ground** (the keeper of the mud volcanoes; a Dragon room was looked at, but the batch's only Dragon line is Stimalaur's, the Champion's ace). Each tells a tale in battle (the AI's, CP11). Each of the four has one line about the professors if the ridge is done (*"Lupescu taught me that tale"*).
**L-3 · The Champion.** **Ruxandra Zorescu** is in the throne room playing the *bucium*. She says the dawn always comes, and that someone had to bring the player that far without being the one to finish it. Her battle (section 2), her tale told (the Draco's), the Hall of Fame.
**L-4 · The Hall of Fame and the credits.** The Hall of Fame's scene, then the credits song (the region's Lyria song). The credits end on the observatory at dawn, two cups on the table.
**L-5 · Home.** The player wakes at home; the parent says the village's *șezătoare* is tonight again, and that the old people have a new tale: "two beasts on the ridge, one of the sun and one of the moon".
