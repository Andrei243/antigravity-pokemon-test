# Plan 46 · The Carpathian Pokédex, 1b: more of the plateau and the first routes (Nos. 31 to 52)

Written 2026-11-12, when the user asked for a Pokédex of about 240 to 250 species. The second file of the plateau's briefs; [the first](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) says how they are read and used. The numbers are given by a script that numbers the whole Pokédex in file order (the `b` files after their area, the Fates, legendaries and mythicals last).

### 31. Pisicut: a village kitten
- **Romanian**: Pisicuț
- **Typing**: Normal
- **The Kitten Pokémon**, No. 10031. 0.3 m, 2.4 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Motanor at level 30.
- **Look**: a small round kitten with soft grey tabby stripes, curled up as if asleep on a warm stove, its paws tucked under its chest. A red woollen thread is tied round its neck in a little bow, and its pink nose and sleepy half-shut green eyes give it a cosy, contented look.

### 32. Motanor: the old tomcat
- **Romanian**: Motanor
- **Typing**: Normal / Dark
- **The Tomcat Pokémon**, No. 10032. 0.9 m, 9.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.

A long striped tail hangs down like a sash, and its sly yellow eyes glint half-lidded as it sits upright on a wooden fence post at night.
