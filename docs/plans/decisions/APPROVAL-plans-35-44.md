# Outline sheet of plans 35 to 44: names and content of ours, for the user's word

Written 2026-11-07 (docs only; `docs/plans/00-overview.md`, "Waiting on the user", item 5). Every name, story, gift, festival, design and partner below is **invented for this game**: nothing is taken from an existing game beyond the genre or mechanic the plan names. Characters the game already has (Looker, the regions' Leaders, Elite Four, Champions and the villain teams' leaders and admins) appear as they stand.

**How to answer.** Each question has options `a`, `b`, `c`; **★ marks the recommended default**. Answer by number and letter ("1a, 2b, 3★..."). **A question left unanswered is the default** (★), so you may answer only what you want changed. `x` means "none of these: I will say my own", and then write the name or the line. Where a question lists numbered rows (gifts, species), answer "keep all" or name the rows to drop.

**What waits for this sheet** (the ◇ sessions of `00-overview.md`): plan 41 · EG1 (the hub's names), EG7 to EG9 (cases and gathering); 37 · FC3 (partners); 40 · GE7 and GE9 (gifts, festivals); 42 · PS2 and PS4 (shows, films); 43 · LF9 (folk); 44 · SD3 (the syndicate). Nothing else waits.

Questions are numbered 1 to 66 in nine parts (A to I).

## B. The cases of the agency (plan 41 · EG7, EG8)

Each case: Looker gives it, you find four to six clues in the region's post-game places, you deduce twice (a wrong deduction costs a line and a retry), then you battle the culprit (a person of ours, at the region's post-game level) and are paid. Pick one per region. A culprit's Pokémon are chosen in the session.

**7. Sinnoh's case (the first; EG7).**
a ★ **The Villa Larder.** Food stores vanish from the Resort Area villa's pantry before every party. Clues: a crate label with the wrong date, bite marks of a young Gible, a boat log with a gap. Culprit: ferry captain **Ferren Dray**, who skims supplies and blames the Pokémon.
b **The Lakes' Lamps.** The keepers of three lakeside lamps find them taken in a night. Clues: oil on a Route 213 gate, a ledger page, a footprint of one boot with a heel nail. Culprit: collector **Wilhelmina Vane**, who wants the set.
c **The Borrowed Voice.** Trainers get calls on their Pokétch in the voice of someone else. Clues: a radio hobbyist's receipt at Veilstone, an echo on the call, a borrowed tape. Culprit: radio hobbyist **Tobias Marl**, who is only joking and has gone too far.

**8. Kanto's case.**
a ★ **The Ferry's Extra Passenger.** A seat on the Sevii ferry is always paid for and always empty. Clues: a ticket stub, a duffel left at Two Island, a fisherman's remark. Culprit: dockhand **Dov Harlan**, who sells the seat twice.
b **The Quiet Gym.** A Gym's trophies go missing one at a time. Culprit: its own caretaker **Edda Pruitt**, polishing them at home for an exhibit nobody asked for.
c **Night Market at the Cave.** A stall appears near Cerulean Cave only on moonless nights. Culprit: peddler **Alaric Sween** with a tray of fakes.

**9. Johto's case.**
a ★ **The Silent Bell.** A temple bell has stopped ringing and no one saw anyone near it. Clues: a rope cut clean, a sleeping Pokémon that never wakes at noon, a note in a monk's hand. Culprit: a novice, **Brother Anselm Voss**, who wanted one quiet day to think.
b **The Alph Etchings.** Chalk marks appear at the ruins overnight, copying the wall. Culprit: schoolteacher **Mirelle Dunn**, teaching her pupils in secret.
c **The Berry Thief of Route 43.** A fence of berries is picked bare on rainy days. Culprit: a boy's Meganium that nobody knew was his; the human is **Cedric Alloway**, ashamed to say it.

**10. Hoenn's case.**
a ★ **The Salt Run.** Crates marked "salt" arrive at Dewford, light as air. Clues: a customs seal with an extra stamp, a sailor's song, a tide table. Culprit: skipper **Ottoline Marsh**, smuggling lamp parts, not salt.
b **The Dim Lighthouse.** A beacon flickers off exactly when a certain boat passes. Culprit: keeper **Rafe Ellery**, hiding his daughter's late return.
c **The Dewford Ledger.** A fisherman's account book has two hands. Culprit: his apprentice **Pearl Haddon**.

**11. Unova's case.**
a ★ **The Double-Booked Hall.** Two tournaments are scheduled for one afternoon and both organisers say they asked first. Clues: a stamped schedule, an eraser smudge, a janitor's key log. Culprit: the hall's clerk **Isidore Calloway**, who sold the slot twice.
b **The Join Avenue Till.** One shop's takings come up short every Saturday. Culprit: a cheerful regular, **Marigold Teague**, putting coins in the wrong till by habit.
c **The Quiet Rails.** A train in the Gear Station runs ten minutes slow only on Tuesdays. Culprit: station master **Barnaby Lowe**, running an errand.
