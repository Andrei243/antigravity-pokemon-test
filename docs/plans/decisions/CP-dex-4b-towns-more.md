# Plan 46 · The Carpathian Pokédex, 4b: more of the towns, the crafts and the folk (Nos. 207 to 235)

Written 2026-11-12, when the user asked for a Pokédex of about 240 to 250 species. The second file of the towns' and folk's briefs; [the first](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) says how they are read and used. The numbers are given by a script that numbers the whole Pokédex in file order (the `b` files after their area, the Fates, legendaries and mythicals last).

### 207. Ulcel: a clay jug
- **Romanian**: Ulcel
- **Typing**: Ground
- **The Clay Jug Pokémon**, No. 10207. 0.4 m, 6.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Ulcior at level 30.
- **Look**: a small round clay jug with a narrow neck and one curved handle, glazed cream and painted with brown and green swirls and a little rooster on its belly. Two bright dot eyes sit under the lip of its mouth, and it waddles on stubby clay feet.
