# Plan 46 · The Carpathian Pokédex, 3: the river, the delta, the sea and the caves (Nos. 115 to 146)

The third file of picture briefs; [the first](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) says how they are read and used.

### 115. Morunel: a sturgeon fry
- **Romanian**: Morunel
- **Typing**: Water
- **The Fry Pokémon**, No. 10115. 0.4 m, 2.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Belugrand at level 40.
- **Look**: a small grey sturgeon fry with a long pointed snout, four little whiskers under it and rows of tiny bony plates along its sides. Shark-like tail fin; inquisitive eyes.

### 116. Belugrand: the great river sturgeon
- **Romanian**: Morunar
- **Typing**: Water / Rock
- **The Ancient Fish Pokémon**, No. 10116. 4.2 m, 900.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a colossal, ancient beluga sturgeon of the Danube, slate-grey above and pale below, with rows of rugged stone-like bony shields along its back and sides. Its wide mouth has long barbels, and a crown of shield plates sits on its head. It looks prehistoric and calm, with tiny old eyes.

### 117. Somnel: a catfish
- **Romanian**: Somnel
- **Typing**: Water
- **The Drowsy Pokémon**, No. 10117. 0.5 m, 6.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Somnoros at level 33.
- **Look**: a small, smooth, dark olive catfish with a huge wide mouth, two very long whiskers and two short ones, and sleepy half-closed eyes (*somn* means both catfish and sleep). A little bubble floats from its mouth like a snore.
