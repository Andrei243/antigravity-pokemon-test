## The three Fates

The *ursitoare* are the three who come on the third night after a child's birth to spin, measure and cut the thread of its life. A trio of minor legendaries.

### 236. Ursivia: the spinner
- **Romanian**: Ursivia
- **Typing**: Psychic / Fairy
- **The Spinner Pokémon**, No. 10236. 1.5 m, 30.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a tall, slender, ethereal creature in a white robe of spun wool, her long face serene and young. She holds a distaff and a spindle, and a single shining golden thread spins out from it, floating around her in loops. Soft dawn-pink light.

### 237. Ursifira: the measurer
- **Romanian**: Ursifira
- **Typing**: Psychic / Grass
- **The Measurer Pokémon**, No. 10237. 1.5 m, 32.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a tall ethereal creature in a robe of green and gold woven with leaves, her face calm and mature. She holds a long golden thread stretched between her hands, measuring it, and knots in the thread glow like small stars. Warm midday light.

### 238. Ursimora: the cutter
- **Romanian**: Ursimora
- **Typing**: Psychic / Ghost
- **The Cutter Pokémon**, No. 10238. 1.5 m, 28.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a tall ethereal creature in a robe of deep violet and grey with silver embroidery, her face old and gentle, eyes half closed. She holds a small pair of silver shears and the end of a golden thread, and her robe fades into evening mist. Calm, not menacing.

## The legendaries

### 239. Solzmeu: the sun-stealer
- **Romanian**: Solzmeu
- **Typing**: Fire / Dragon
- **The Sun Thief Pokémon**, No. 10239. 3.4 m, 310.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: the *zmeu* of the folk tales: a man-shaped dragon, tall and long-limbed, standing upright, scaled in bronze and burnished gold, with a proud horned head and a flowing mane of flame. A great blazing sun-disc is caught behind its shoulders like a halo it stole, and its broad wings fold around it like a royal cloak. Powerful, regal, a little arrogant.

### 240. Lunacolac: the moon-eater
- **Romanian**: Lunacolac
- **Typing**: Dark / Sound
- **The Eclipse Pokémon**, No. 10240. 3.0 m, 280.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: the *vârcolac* that swallows the moon: a vast wolf with night-blue fur flecked with stars, a thick mane like the night sky, and a silver crescent glowing in its open jaws as if a bite of the moon were still in them. Its howl is visible as rings of pale silver light spreading through the air. Its eyes are two small full moons.

### 241. Furtunaur: the storm-dragon
- **Romanian**: Furtunaur
- **Typing**: Dragon / Electric
- **The Storm Rider Pokémon**, No. 10241. 7.0 m, 450.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: the *balaur* the *solomonari* ride through the storms: a long, serpentine dragon with three heads on long necks, storm-grey scales with lighter cloud-white bellies, and wings like torn storm clouds. Lightning crackles along its spines, and on its back it wears a rider's harness of braided lightning with an old wizard's book chained to it. Hailstones swirl around it.

### 242. Stindrac: the war banner
- **Romanian**: Stindrac
- **Typing**: Sound / Dragon
- **The War Banner Pokémon**, No. 10242. 4.0 m, 60.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the ancient Dacian *draco* banner come alive: a bronze wolf's head with an open, howling mouth, and behind it a long serpent body of brightly coloured woven cloth (crimson, gold and green) that ripples like a windsock. The wind blowing through its mouth makes it howl; visible sound rings stream from its jaws. It flies, coiling through the air.

## The mythicals of the story

Ielele, Mumapadura and Maiastra were proposed as optional on 2026-11-09. Since 2026-11-12, when the user asked for more mythicals that move the story, they and the four after them each have a part in it ([the story's chapters](CP-story-chapters.md), section 9). All seven still wait for the user's approval of their designs.

### 243. Ielele: the night fairies
- **Romanian**: Ielele
- **Typing**: Fairy / Sound
- **The Night Dance Pokémon**, No. 10243. 1.6 m, 9.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the *Iele*, three beautiful, dangerous fairies of the night meadows, as one creature: three slender translucent figures in flowing white veils, joined at the hands, spinning in a circle above the grass in moonlight. Their hair streams like mist, and a ring of trampled glowing grass lies beneath them. Enchanting and slightly eerie.

### 244. Mumapadura: the forest mother
- **Romanian**: Mumapădure
- **Typing**: Grass / Ghost
- **The Forest Mother Pokémon**, No. 10244. 2.4 m, 200.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: *Muma Pădurii*, the old mother of the forest: a tall, hunched figure part tree and part old woman, her body gnarled bark, her long hair hanging moss and roots, her face a wise and fearsome old visage with eyes like two glowing hollows in a trunk. She leans on a crooked branch, and mushrooms and owls nest in her shoulders.

### 245. Maiastra: the wonder bird
- **Romanian**: Măiastra
- **Typing**: Fire / Sound
- **The Wonder Bird Pokémon**, No. 10245. 1.8 m, 20.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the *Pasărea Măiastră* of the fairy tales, the wonder bird whose song heals and enchants: a slender golden bird with an upright, smooth, polished body that shines like burnished bronze, a long neck with its beak raised in song, and a long tail of flame-gold feathers. Its song appears as golden rings of light.

## The three brothers of the night (mythical, new 2026-11-12)

In the tales three brothers carry the night across the sky between them: *Murgilă* brings the dusk, *Miezilă* holds the midnight, *Zorilă* opens the dawn. Team Zenith's plan for an endless day is a plan to stop them, and the Champion's family name is the youngest's. Each is met alone, at its own hour.

### 246. Murgila: the brother of dusk
- **Romanian**: Murgilă
- **Typing**: Dark / Fire
- **The Dusk Pokémon**, No. 10246. 2.0 m, 140.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the elder brother who brings the evening: a tall, long-legged dark-bay horse-like creature with a man's patient face in its long muzzle, its coat the colour of a sky after sunset, deep red and violet, with the last red light glowing along its back like embers. Its mane and tail are trails of dusk cloud with the first stars caught in them, and it walks with its head low, as if pulling the dark behind it on a rope.

### 247. Miezila: the brother of midnight
- **Romanian**: Miezilă
- **Typing**: Dark / Psychic
- **The Midnight Pokémon**, No. 10247. 2.2 m, 160.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the middle brother who holds the midnight: a massive, silent night-black creature like a great black horse or ox, its coat full of stars like the deep sky, a slim silver crescent resting between its curved horns. Its eyes are two closed lids with a faint silver line under them; it never seems to open them. Everything around it is hushed: a ring of dark, still air surrounds it.

### 248. Zorila: the brother of dawn
- **Romanian**: Zorilă
- **Typing**: Fire / Fairy
- **The Dawn Pokémon**, No. 10248. 1.7 m, 70.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the youngest brother who opens the dawn: a light, quick, deer-like creature with a pale rose-gold coat that brightens from grey at its hooves to gold at its back, long slender legs, and a mane of streaming morning light in pink and gold. A small golden morning star hangs between its antler-like branches of light, and dew drops fall from its hooves where it steps.

## The magic horse (mythical, new 2026-11-12)

### 249. Nazdravan: the horse that eats embers
- **Romanian**: Năzdrăvanul
- **Typing**: Fire / Flying
- **The Wonder Steed Pokémon**, No. 10249. 2.1 m, 300.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none. Mythical.
- **Look**: the *cal năzdrăvan* of the fairy tales, the hero's magic horse that ate glowing embers and grew wings: a lean, powerful horse with a coat of burnished bronze and smouldering red, two great wings of flame-tipped feathers folded at its shoulders, and a mane and tail of fire. Its hooves leave sparks, its eyes are wise and old, and a little smoke rises from its nostrils as if it had just eaten coals. In the story it is first seen hiding as an old stabled horse, wings folded tight under a ragged horse blanket and its mane banked low to smouldering embers; draw it as it really is.
