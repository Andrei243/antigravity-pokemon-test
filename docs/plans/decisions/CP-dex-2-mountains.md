# Plan 46 · The Carpathian Pokédex, 2: the forests and the mountains (Nos. 31 to 64)

The second file of picture briefs; [the first](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) says how they are read and used.

### 31. Urslet: a bear cub
- **Romanian**: Ursuleț
- **Typing**: Normal
- **The Cub Pokémon**, No. 10031. 0.6 m, 18.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Ursabrun at level 32.
- **Look**: a round brown bear cub with a pale honey-coloured muzzle, small round ears and a pale crescent of fur on its chest. It hugs a broken honeycomb to its belly and has honey on its nose.

### 32. Ursabrun: the Carpathian brown bear
- **Romanian**: Ursabrun
- **Typing**: Normal / Ground
- **The Mountain Bear Pokémon**, No. 10032. 2.3 m, 320.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a massive brown bear with a hump of muscle over its shoulders, darker fur on its legs and a grizzled silver mane down its back. Its claws are long and stone-grey, made for digging out roots, and moss and pine needles cling to its coat. A pale honey crescent on its chest; deep-set calm eyes.

### 33. Lupling: a wolf pup
