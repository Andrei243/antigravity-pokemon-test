# Plan 46 · The Carpathian Pokédex, 4: the folk and the towns (Nos. 174 to 206)

The fourth file of picture briefs; [the first](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) says how they are read and used. Since 2026-11-12 the Fates, the legendaries and the mythicals are [the last file](CP-dex-6-legends-and-mythicals.md), at the end of the Pokédex.

## The folk of the tales

### 174. Strigoias: a restless spirit
- **Romanian**: Strigoiaș
- **Typing**: Ghost
- **The Restless Pokémon**, No. 10174. 0.5 m, 0.8 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Strigoion at level 38, at night.
- **Look**: a small, pale, mischievous ghost wrapped in a rough linen sheet like a shroud, only its round glowing eyes and a cheeky grin showing. A sprig of garlic flower is tied round it, which it seems annoyed by. Its lower edge is tattered and floats.

### 175. Strigoion: the strigoi
- **Romanian**: Strigoion
- **Typing**: Ghost / Dark
- **The Undying Pokémon**, No. 10175. 1.8 m, 25.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a tall, gaunt spirit of the folk tales who will not rest: a ragged dark cloak of village homespun, a pale long face with glowing red-violet eyes, long thin fingers and a shock of white hair. Its lower body is a whirl of grave mist and fallen leaves. Spooky and folk-like rather than a vampire: no fangs, no bat wings.

### 176. Sanzienel: a midsummer fairy bud
- **Romanian**: Sânzienel
- **Typing**: Fairy
- **The Midsummer Bud Pokémon**, No. 10176. 0.3 m, 0.4 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Sanziana on a summer night with high friendship (plan 46 · CP3 settles the method).
- **Look**: a tiny fairy made of a bunch of yellow lady's bedstraw flowers (*sânziene*), a little round face in the middle of the golden blossoms, gauzy wings like dandelion seed and a glow like a firefly.

### 177. Sanziana: the midsummer fairy
- **Romanian**: Sânziana
- **Typing**: Fairy / Grass
- **The Midsummer Pokémon**, No. 10177. 1.4 m, 6.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a graceful fairy of Midsummer Night, slim and floating, in a flowing white folk dress embroidered with golden flowers, wearing a crown woven from yellow bedstraw blossoms. Long golden hair trails into strands of flowers, and tiny golden lights dance around her as she spins. She is a creature, not a human: leaf-shaped ears, petal hands.

### 178. Zburel: a little comet
- **Romanian**: Zburel

- **Evolution**: into Zburator at level 36, at night.

### 179. Zburator: the night flyer
- **Romanian**: Zburător
- **Typing**: Fire / Flying
- **The Night Flyer Pokémon**, No. 10179. 1.6 m, 12.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: the *zburător* of the folk tales, a spirit that flies as a fiery comet by night: a sleek, swift bird-like creature with a body of glowing coal, wings of long ribbon-like flames and a long comet's tail trailing sparks across the night. Its head has a crest of white-hot flame and a handsome, proud face.

### 180. Stimel: a lake serpent hatchling

- **Evolution**: into Stimara at level 30.

### 181. Stimara: a lake serpent
- **Romanian**: Știmara
- **Typing**: Dragon / Water
- **The Glacial Lake Pokémon**, No. 10181. 2.4 m, 60.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: into Stimalaur at level 55.
- **Look**: a long, slender lake serpent of the mountain lakes, deep blue with silvery scales on its belly, a mane of flowing fins down its back like water weed, and small horn-buds. Four short legs with webbed claws. Mist rises from its scales.

### 182. Stimalaur: the spirit of the waters
- **Romanian**: Știmalaur
- **Typing**: Dragon / Water
- **The Water Spirit Pokémon**, No. 10182. 6.5 m, 340.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: the *știma apelor*, the guardian spirit of the glacial lakes, as a great, powerful dragon: a long coiling body of deep teal and glacier blue, long flowing fins like a veil of water, a crown of pale ice-blue horns and a long beard of mist. Its eyes are like still lake water. It rises from water in a spiral, mighty and graceful.

### 185. Oupictat: a painted egg

### 186. Frescuta: a fresco sprite

### 187. Frescanta: the painted wall's spirit

### 188. Dulgherel: a carpenter beetle

### 189. Poartaur: the carved gate
- **Romanian**: Poartaur
- **Typing**: Grass / Rock
- **The Carved Gate Pokémon**, No. 10189. 2.5 m, 400.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a living monumental wooden gate of Maramureș: two massive oak pillars as legs and a beam across as shoulders, under a small shingled roof for a head. Every surface is carved with the folk motifs of the region: the rope twist, the sun, the tree of life. Its face is a carved sun in the middle of the top beam, and ivy grows on its pillars.

### 198. Coloanfinit: the endless column

### 205. Dochita: the cold's little old woman

### 206. Babadochia: the old woman of March
- **Romanian**: Babadochia
- **Typing**: Ice / Fairy
- **The Nine Coats Pokémon**, No. 10206. 1.5 m, 40.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: Baba Dochia of the legend, who climbed the mountain with her flock and shed her nine coats one by one as the March sun warmed her: a tall, hunched creature wrapped in nine layered sheepskin coats of white and grey, the outermost slipping off her shoulders, snow and icicles in her fleece and a long white braid. She leans on a shepherd's staff; small snowdrops grow where she stands. Wise, stern but kind face.
