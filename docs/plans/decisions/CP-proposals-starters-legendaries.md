## The legendaries

### Solzmeu, the sun-stealer (box legendary; was the Zmeu)

- **Name**: **Solzmeu** / Solzmeu (*sol*, the sun, and *zmeu*). Alternatives: Aurozmeu (*aur*, gold), Zmeusoare (*soare*, sun).
- **Type**: Fire / Dragon. **Total**: 680 (Attack 130, Sp. Atk 120, Speed 110).
- **Look**: a man-shaped dragon of the tales, tall and long-limbed, scaled in bronze and gold, a sun-disc caught behind its shoulders like a halo it stole; wings folded like a cloak.
- **Story**: the villain team wants it to hold the sun in the sky for ever. Found at the stronghold castle at the story's height.
- **Ability**: *Noonday* (on entry the sun stays and can't be replaced by another weather while it is on the field).
- **Tale**: its own, the Zmeu's tale (the sun cannot set); its Tale Form holds the sun before it, Fire / Dragon stronger.
- **Signature move**: *Sun Seizure* (Fire, special, 120, sets the harsh sun for five turns, which the foe's weather can't end).

### Lunacolac, the moon-eater (box legendary, the balance; was the Vârcolac)

- **Name**: **Lunacolac** / Lunacolac (*luna*, the moon, and *vârcolac*). Alternatives: Vârcoluna, Eclipcolac.
- **Type**: Dark / **Sound**. **Total**: 680 (Attack 125, Speed 125, Sp. Def 110).
- **Look**: a vast wolf of night-blue fur, its jaws ringed with a crescent's silver as if a bite of the moon were still in them; its howl is visible as rings in the air.
- **Story**: met as the player stands against the endless day; it eats the stolen sun's light to bring the night back.
- **Ability**: *Eclipse* (on entry the field darkens for five turns: every Pokémon's accuracy falls, Dark and Ghost moves are stronger).
- **Tale**: the Vârcolac's tale (the eclipse).
- **Signature move**: *Moon Howl* (Sound, special, 110, hits all foes, removes the weather).

### Furtunaur, the Solomonar's storm-dragon (box legendary, the third; was Balaurion)

- **Name**: **Furtunaur** / Furtunaur (*furtună*, storm, and *balaur*, the many-headed dragon the *solomonari* ride). Alternatives: Solomondra, Grindinaur (*grindină*, hail).
- **Type**: Dragon / Electric (alternative: Dragon / Ice, for the hail the solomonari bring; the user picks). **Total**: 680 (Sp. Atk 130, Speed 120, HP 100).
- **Look**: a long serpentine dragon with three heads, storm-grey scales, a rider's harness of braided lightning on its back, and the solomonar's book chained to it.
- **Story**: the keeper of the weather, met high on the ridge in a storm after the villains are stopped; it carries the region's last tale.
- **Ability**: *Three Heads* (its first move each turn strikes three times at 40% power).
- **Tale**: the Solomonar's tale (the storm: a weather the teller owns).
- **Signature move**: *Hailstorm Rider* (Electric, special, 100, sets the teller's chosen weather).

### Stindrac, the war banner (mythical; was the Draco)

- **Name**: **Stindrac** / Stindrac (*stindard*, banner, and *drac*, dragon; the Dacian draco banner: a wolf's head on a serpent's cloth body that howled when the wind went through it).
- **Type**: **Sound** / Dragon. **Total**: 600.
- **Look**: a bronze wolf's head with an open mouth and a long body of coloured cloth that ripples like a windsock; it flies on the wind and howls as it moves.
- **Story**: the post-game, at the Dacian sanctuary of stone circles in the forest.
- **Ability**: *War Cry* (its team's attacks rise each turn it is on the field, up to three).
- **Tale**: the Draco's tale (the war banner).
- **Signature move**: *Banner Howl* (Sound, special, 100, raises its team's Attack and Sp. Atk).

### The mythicals of the story (2026-11-12, to approve)

The user asked on 2026-11-12 for more mythicals that move the story. The two above and Măiastra (in the Pokédex's briefs since 2026-11-09) are now in the story, and four more are new. What each does is [the chapters' section 9](CP-story-chapters.md); their picture briefs are [the Pokédex's last file](CP-dex-6-legends-and-mythicals.md). Answer each with **yes**, **no** or a change; each can be dropped alone without touching the user's fixed beats.

- **Murgila** / Murgilă: Dark / Fire, the brother of dusk, a dark-bay horse with a patient face who pulls the evening behind him; caged in the Stilling machine (the endless noon), freed at the stronghold (600).
- **Miezila** / Miezilă: Dark / Psychic, the brother of midnight, a vast night-black beast with a crescent between its horns and eyes always shut; kept from his hour by the false dawns, seen under the capital's arch (600).
- **Zorila** / Zorilă: Fire / Fairy, the brother of dawn, a rose-gold deer of morning light; steps out of the first light after the ridge and looks at the Champion, whose family name is his (600). The three brothers are Ruxandra's tale; the folklore source is marked **Unsure** in the chapters' review table, so it is the first thing to confirm.
- **Nazdravan** / Năzdrăvanul: Fire / Flying, the magic horse of the tales that eats embers; Ilie's "old horse" behind the observatory, gone after the ridge (600).
- **Ielele**: they take the rival's singing voice and give it back when he learns to listen.
- **Muma Pădurii** (Mumapadura in the Pokédex): her roots hold the monastery's tales against Team Zenith.
- **Măiastra** (Maiastra): answers the rival's first verse at the sanctuary; her healing song cannot bring the professors back.

## The people (decision 17)

- **The professor**: Professor **Doina Lupescu**, who studies the tales and the songs that carry them, her lab in an old walled town of the plateau.
- **The rival**: **Radu**, a shepherd's son from the hills who wants to be the region's best singer and trainer at once, or **Ilinca**, his sister, by the player's look (as Hoenn's rival is the hero the player isn't).
- **The villain team**: **Team Zenith** (*Echipa Zenit*): they want the sun at its highest, for ever. Their leader is an astronomer who lost someone to a long winter.
