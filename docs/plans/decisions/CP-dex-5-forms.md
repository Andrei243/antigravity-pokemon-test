# Plan 46 · The Carpathian Pokédex, 5: Carpathian forms of existing species (Nos. 140 to 148)

The fifth file of picture briefs; [the first](CP-dex-1-plateau.md) says how they are read and used. A Carpathian form is numbered 11000 + its species' National Pokédex number (plan 46). Each must keep the species' own body and silhouette and change only what the brief says; the generator is told the species' name so the picture stays recognisably that Pokémon's form. Plan 46 asked for about fifteen; these nine are the ones with a clear reason to exist in Carpathia, and more can follow.

### 140. Carpathian Sableye: from the salt mines
- **Romanian**: Sableye din Carpatia
- **Typing**: Ghost / Rock
- **The Salt Gem Pokémon**, No. 11302. 0.5 m, 14.0 kg.
- **Evolution**: none.
- **Look**: a regional form of the Pokémon Sableye, living in the salt mines: its body pale and crusted with white and pink rock salt instead of purple, its gem eyes and chest gem made of clear amber-coloured salt crystal, small salt cubes on its shoulders. Same small crouching goblin-like body and sharp claws as Sableye.
