# Where the later items are found

Plan 06 · R28b: the items of the games after Platinum (ids from 1000 in `items.json`, 442 of them) all run since R28 (`docs/mechanics/coverage.md`, "The later games' items"), but none is placed anywhere in Sinnoh: Platinum's own world has only Platinum's items. This is where the games themselves give each kind, for the chapters of the later regions to place them (plan 03 · D12 for what Sinnoh may hold of them, plans 19 to 23 for the regions). Hand-written; the games' places are named as their own guides and maps name them, in our own words, and nothing here is read by the game.

A chapter that places an item ticks nothing here: it writes the item into its area's files and the place into this table's last column.
