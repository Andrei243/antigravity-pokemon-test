# D14 Johto: reference drawings

**References only. These are not game art and are not shipped.** Nothing in the game reads this folder: the engine's project copies `Assets/` and `Data/` to the build output, never `docs/`, and `.gitignore` leaves the folder alone.

One SVG per species of ours in plan 03 · D14's second batch (Johto, Nos. 2162 to 2245), drawn from its design in [`docs/plans/D14-proposals-johto.md`](../../../plans/D14-proposals-johto.md): the look, the typing, the colours it names and the size. They are for the 3D sculpting sessions to look at beside the written look, in the clean style of a creature's official picture: one creature, a three-quarter view facing left, a dark outline (`#1b1b2b`), flat colours with one shade tone and a highlight, light from the upper left, a plain pale ground. Our own designs only, drawn from the document alone.

Open [`index.html`](index.html) for the contact sheet (no external resources): the twelve of the shortlist first, then the other twenty-two, each captioned with its number, typing, what it evolves from and its height and weight.

## What a sculptor can take from them, and what not

- **Take**: the silhouette and its main masses, which parts there are and where they join, the colours and where each goes, and the one new feature each design names (the black-tipped tail, the glowing spots, the carried web, the pearl, the flange spikes, the leafy crowns, the glowing disc, the crystals, the shoulder horns, the two shoulder heads, the drill…).
- **Don't take**: exact proportions or the back of the body (one view only, simple shapes, hand-written SVG), nor sizes between species: every drawing fills the same frame, so go by the heights in the captions. A female's difference is mentioned in the design and not drawn.
- The three beasts (Raitenko, Enkaiou, Suiseiren) share one body, as do Zenitheon and Eclipseon, and Cerberhound and Siegephant are built on the same four-legged base; their differences are drawn on top.

Files are named `<No>-<Name>.svg`, 512 by 512.
