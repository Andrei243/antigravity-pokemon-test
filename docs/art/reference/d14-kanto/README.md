# D14 Kanto: reference drawings

Reference pictures of the 46 species of our own in plan 03 · D14's first batch (Nos. 2020 to 2146, [D14-proposals-kanto.md](../../../plans/D14-proposals-kanto.md)), for the sessions that sculpt their 3D models. **They are references only: not part of the game, not read by any tool, not shipped.** Nothing in the engine's project, the data importer or the harness copies `docs/art/reference/`.

- One SVG per species, `<No>-<Name>.svg`, viewBox 512×512: one creature on white, outline `#1b1b2b` of about 5 units, flat colours with one shade and one highlight.
- `index.html` puts them all on one page (inline, no external resources), each with its number, typing, the species it evolves from, and a mark on the twelve of the shortlist.
- Each is drawn by hand from its entry's written brief (the "Look", typing, colours, size) and nothing else: our own designs, no official artwork traced or imitated. The Eeveelution relatives (2134 to 2136) share one base drawing, and so do the three birds (2144 to 2146).

What a sculptor can take from them: the silhouette and its proportions, where the new feature sits and how big it is, the colour blocking and which parts take which colour. What not: these are simple vector drawings, many in a plain side or three-quarter view with stiff limbs; anatomy, poses, the back and the exact shapes of faces are the sculpt's to decide, and where a drawing and the written brief differ, the brief wins.
