# Hoenn past 100: reference drawings

Plan 03 · D14, batch 3. One SVG for each of the 51 Hoenn species of our own designed in
[`docs/plans/D14-proposals-hoenn.md`](../../../plans/D14-proposals-hoenn.md) (Nos. 2262 to 2381),
named `<No>-<Name>.svg`, and `index.html`, a contact sheet with every drawing inline (name,
number, typing, what it evolves from; the shortlist's twelve first, with a red edge).

**These are references for the sessions that sculpt the 3D models, nothing else.** They are not
game assets: the engine's project copies only `PokemonPlatinumEngine/Data` to its output, nothing
under `docs/` is built, copied or read by the game, and `.gitignore` does not ignore this folder.

## How they were made

- Each is drawn from its written look, typing, colours, size and weight in the proposal document,
  which is the whole source: no official artwork was traced, copied or imitated.
- One creature, centred, mostly a three-quarter view facing left (Ravagoose faces right, towards
  its rival Scimitail), on white with a pale ground shadow; no text in the drawing.
- viewBox `0 0 512 512`, outline `#1b1b2b` about 5 units, flat colours with one shade tone and one
  highlight per part, a soft halo where the document says a part glows.
- The pairs and sets share one drawing varied by colour and detail: Anodion/Cathodion,
  Photurion/Lampyria, Selenolith/Heliolith, Gulpereel/Sirenyss, the three Regis, Aurelias/Aurelios.

## What a sculptor can take from them, and what not

They are simple hand-placed shapes (ellipses, polygons, a few curves), so they read like
children's-book cut-outs, not like finished official art. Use them for:

- the silhouette and the pose's idea, the proportions between the parts, and above all **where
  each new feature of the design sits** (the crest, the third cone, the chimneys, the bells…);
- the palette: the base, shade and highlight of every part, and which parts glow.

Don't use them for anatomy, the exact shape of a limb or a face, or the back view (none is drawn).
A few are weaker than the rest: Anomalorex's curled limbs and Carillone's bells are crowded, the
eon pair's wings are flat, and Prismaria's and Nimbusong's heads are small against their bodies.
