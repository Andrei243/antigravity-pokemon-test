# R20b Megas: reference drawings

**References only. These are not game art and are not shipped.** Nothing in the game reads this folder: the engine's project copies `Assets/` and `Data/` to the build output, never `docs/`, and `.gitignore` leaves the folder alone.

One SVG per Mega of our own in plan 06 · R20b, the fifteen starters' final forms with no Mega (Mega Typhlosion to Mega Quaquaval), drawn from each entry's **Look** in [`docs/plans/R20b-mega-proposals.md`](../../../plans/R20b-mega-proposals.md), with its typing in mind. The proposal still awaits the user's answers (plan 06's R20b entry records none yet), so every entry as written is drawn; a design the user changes is drawn again.

They are for the 3D sculpting session to look at beside the written look, in the clean style of a creature's official picture: one creature, a three-quarter view facing left, a dark outline (`#1b1b2b`), flat colours with one shade tone and a highlight, light from the upper left, a plain pale ground. They are our own drawings of the briefs, not copies of any official picture of the species.

Open [`index.html`](index.html) for the contact sheet (no external resources), each captioned with its typing, the species and number it Mega Evolves from and its stone.

## What a sculptor can take from them, and what not

- **Take**: the new silhouette and where the Mega's new pieces sit on the body (the crater, the snowcapped ridge and grove, the flame mane and wrist rings, the crown and cape with epaulettes, the leaf hood, the kabuto and drawn blades, the cowl and vine bow, the belt plate, the floating spheres, the drum on the back, the fireball, the scope, the flower bomb, the crown of fire and guitar-head tail, the crescent headdress and bead streamers), and their colours.
- **Don't take**: the base species' body as such: it is drawn simply and is no substitute for our own sculpt of the species, which the Mega is built from. One view only, so nothing of the back.
- The nine standing Megas share one two-legged base and the four on all fours one four-legged base; Serperior and Primarina are drawn on their own.

Files are named `<Species>-Mega.svg`, 512 by 512.
