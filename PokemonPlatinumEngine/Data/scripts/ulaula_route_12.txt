# Route 12, Ula'ula Island (plan 23, the wave after the loose ends). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beat as it
# is publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The rest of the islands: Hapu, the guardians' ruins, the last
# routes and the shut doors"): Hapu of Poni meets the player at the east end of the road and registers her Mudsdale in
# the Ride Pager (Overworld/RidePager.cs: a flag alone). VAR_ALOLA_HAPU is her count: 0 before this road, 1 after it,
# 2 once she has been heard out before the Ruins of Abundance (haina_desert.txt).
#
# The people: hapu stands by the road until her scene is played (FLAG_ALOLA_HAPU_ROUTE_12 hides her).


script HapuMudsdale
  scene "Hapu on Route 12"
  face hapu player
  speaker "Hapu"
  say "Hey. You're the island challenger, aren't you? I'm Hapu. I'm from Poni Island, way out west."
  say "I've been walking the islands to see how the people live. This ridge is hard going on foot, though."
  say "Here. My Mudsdale knows the way over any rock. Put her in your Ride Pager and she'll come when you call."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_MUDSDALE
  text "{player} registered Mudsdale in the Ride Pager! Mudsdale can gallop over rough ground."
  say "I'll be off. There's a shrine past the desert I want to see. Maybe I'll see you there."
  hide hapu
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_HAPU_ROUTE_12
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_HAPU 1
  endscene
  end


script HapuTalk
  speaker "Hapu"
  say "You look like you could use a strong pair of legs under you. Hold on a moment."
  call HapuMudsdale
  end
