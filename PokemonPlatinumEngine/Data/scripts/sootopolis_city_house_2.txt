# A house in Sootopolis City (plan 21 · H8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/SootopolisCity_House2/scripts.inc). An old woman asks
# about Mt. Pyre's orbs.


script OldWoman
  face self player
  speaker "Old Woman"
  ask "Mt. Pyre... Two orbs lie side by side at its summit. Did you know?"
  if no goto Didnt
  say "Yes, two orbs side by side... Seeing them together is somehow soothing."
  end
label Didnt
  say "No? Perhaps you ought to go and see them for yourself."
  end
