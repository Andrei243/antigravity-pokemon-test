# Dunsparce Tunnel (plan 19 · K9, parts a and c), on FireRed's beats (data/maps/ThreeIsland_DunsparceTunnel/
# scripts.inc). The prospector has been digging for gold; once the player has the National Pokédex he hands over a
# Nugget he found (FLAG_GOT_NUGGET_FROM_DUNSPARCE_TUNNEL) and his dig has broken through to the port's east steps,
# which FireRed shows by laying another layout of the map (LAYOUT_..._DUG_OUT) in its ON_TRANSITION: here that is
# FLAG_THREE_ISLAND_TUNNEL_DUG_OUT, which world.json swaps the chunk on and the two overlays open the east way on.


# FireRed's ON_TRANSITION: the dug-out tunnel with the National Pokédex, and the prospector moved back to the wall
# without it (the overlay's second prospector, hidden once the flag is set)
script OnEnter
  if nationaldex setflag FLAG_THREE_ISLAND_TUNNEL_DUG_OUT
  end

script Prospector
  face self player
  speaker "Prospector"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_NUGGET_FROM_DUNSPARCE_TUNNEL goto Given
  if nationaldex goto Nugget
  say "I'm digging for gold under this island. Every day, a little deeper. One day I'll strike it rich!"
  end
label Nugget
  say "Look here, I struck gold at last! Well, a little bit. I've more than enough now, so you have this one."
  setflag FLAG_GOT_NUGGET_FROM_DUNSPARCE_TUNNEL
  give "Nugget"
  end
label Given
  say "My pick came out the other side this morning! Walk it through and you'll come up by the harbour steps."
  end
