# Dunsparce Tunnel (plan 19 · K9, part a), on FireRed's beats (data/maps/ThreeIsland_DunsparceTunnel/scripts.inc).
# The prospector has been digging for gold; once the player has the National Pokédex he hands over a Nugget he found
# (FLAG_GOT_NUGGET_FROM_DUNSPARCE_TUNNEL). FireRed's tunnel dug through to the east is part b's.


script Prospector
  face prospector player
  speaker "Prospector"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_NUGGET_FROM_DUNSPARCE_TUNNEL goto Given
  if nationaldex goto Nugget
  say "I'm digging for gold under this island. Every day, a little deeper. One day I'll strike it rich!"
  end
label Nugget
  say "Look here, I struck gold at last! Well, a little bit. I've more than enough now, so you have this one."
  setflag FLAG_GOT_NUGGET_FROM_DUNSPARCE_TUNNEL
  give "Nugget"
  end
label Given
  say "One day this tunnel will reach all the way through the island. Then I'll find the motherlode!"
  end
