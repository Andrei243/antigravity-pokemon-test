# Sandgem Town (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_sandgem_town.s).
#
# The professor's assistant meets the player at the edge of town and takes them to the lab, out of whose door the
# rival comes running; coming back out with a Pokédex, the professor gives a TM and the assistant shows the
# player round. VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE: 1 taken to the lab, 2 shown round.


# The west edge of town (the original's trigger 2).
script MeetAssistant
  music "common/assistant"
  face sandgem_counterpart player
  emote sandgem_counterpart exclaim
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Oh! You're {player}, right? Professor Rowan is waiting for you in his lab. Come on, it's right here!"
  fade out
  place player 168 843 up
  place sandgem_counterpart 167 843 up
  fade in
  sound "door_open"
  show rival
  sound "bump"
  walk player down 1
  emote player exclaim
  music "common/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Whoa, {player}! Slow as ever. ...You know, the professor isn't scary at all. He's just kind of strange!"
  say "Anyway, I'm off. Later!"
  walk rival down 1 right 7 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_RIVAL
  music "common/assistant"
  face sandgem_counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Was that your friend? He's in a real hurry, isn't he? ...Well, in we go!"
  hide sandgem_counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE 1
  warp "RowanLab" 5 8 up
  end


# Out of the lab with the Pokédex (the original's OnFrame_ExitLab).
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE != 1 end
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 0 end
  sound "door_open"
  show prof_rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "{player}! One more thing."
  face player prof_rowan
  say "Take this with you."
  give "TM27"
  say "A Technical Machine teaches a Pokémon a move. Each one can be used only once, so think before you use it."
  say "That one holds Return. The more your Pokémon cares for you, the stronger the move becomes."
  hide prof_rowan
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_PROF_ROWAN
  sound "door_close"
  music "common/assistant"
  place sandgem_counterpart 168 845 up
  walk sandgem_counterpart up 1
  face player sandgem_counterpart
  face sandgem_counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "The professor had TMs? I never knew! ...Oh, let me show you round Sandgem!"
  camera pan 177 843 0.8
  say "That's the Pokémon Center, with the red roof. They'll heal your Pokémon there whenever you like, and it's free."
  camera pan 187 843 0.8
  say "And that's the Poké Mart, with the blue roof. You can buy Poké Balls and Potions there."
  camera release 0.6
  say "Don't forget to tell your family you're helping the professor! Heal your Pokémon at the Center first, though."
  say "See you later, {player}!"
  walk sandgem_counterpart down 1 left 3
  hide sandgem_counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE 2
  end


# The assistant, spoken to (plan 02 · S14; the original's CounterpartTalk_Counterpart, script 10300). The Hall of
# Fame brings her back to stand before the lab, and what she says goes by how far the Pokédex has come: with every
# Pokémon of Sinnoh seen (CheckLocalDexCompleted: all 210 of the Sinnoh Pokédex) she wants it shown to the professor,
# which is where the post-game begins (plan 02 · S15). Until then she has heard the news from him.
#
# Left for plan 02 · S15, which writes the scenes they lead to: the elder of Celestic Town's message (asked before
# the congratulations while FLAG_UNLOCKED_DIALGA_PALKIA_SPEAR_PILLAR is unset) and the word of Dialga or Palkia at
# Spear Pillar after it, and with the National Pokédex her sister, Bebe and the Poké Radar's lesson. The original
# also asks whether today is the player's birthday, which this game doesn't keep.
script Counterpart
  if not flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Tour
  speaker "{assistant}"
  if nationaldex goto National
  if var SEEN >= 210 goto SeenAll
  say "{player}! The professor told me everything! Congratulations, Champion!"
  end
label SeenAll
  say "Hi, {player}! May I have a look at your Pokédex?"
  say "... ... ..."
  say "Oh! You've seen every kind of Pokémon there is in Sinnoh! Come on, let's show it to Professor Rowan!"
  end
label National
  say "So that's the National Pokédex! There's a whole world of Pokémon to put in it now, {player}."
  end
label Tour
  sayown
  end
