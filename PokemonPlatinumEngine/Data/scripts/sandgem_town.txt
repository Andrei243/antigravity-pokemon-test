# Sandgem Town (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_sandgem_town.s).
#
# The professor's assistant meets the player at the edge of town and takes them to the lab, out of whose door the
# rival comes running; coming back out with a Pokédex, the professor gives a TM and the assistant shows the
# player round. VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE: 1 taken to the lab, 2 shown round.


# The west edge of town (the original's trigger 2).
script MeetAssistant
  music "common/assistant"
  face sandgem_counterpart player
  emote sandgem_counterpart exclaim
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Oh! You're {player}, right? Professor Rowan is waiting for you in his lab. Come on, it's right here!"
  fade out
  place player 168 843 up
  place sandgem_counterpart 167 843 up
  fade in
  sound "door_open"
  show rival
  sound "bump"
  walk player down 1
  emote player exclaim
  music "common/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Whoa, {player}! Slow as ever. ...You know, the professor isn't scary at all. He's just kind of strange!"
  say "Anyway, I'm off. Later!"
  walk rival down 1 right 7 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_RIVAL
  music "common/assistant"
  face sandgem_counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Was that your friend? He's in a real hurry, isn't he? ...Well, in we go!"
  hide sandgem_counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE 1
  warp "RowanLab" 5 8 up
  end


# Out of the lab with the Pokédex (the original's OnFrame_ExitLab).
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE != 1 end
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 0 end
  sound "door_open"
  show prof_rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "{player}! One more thing."
  face player prof_rowan
  say "Take this with you."
  give "TM27"
  say "A Technical Machine teaches a Pokémon a move. Each one can be used only once, so think before you use it."
  say "That one holds Return. The more your Pokémon cares for you, the stronger the move becomes."
  hide prof_rowan
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_PROF_ROWAN
  sound "door_close"
  music "common/assistant"
  place sandgem_counterpart 168 845 up
  walk sandgem_counterpart up 1
  face player sandgem_counterpart
  face sandgem_counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "The professor had TMs? I never knew! ...Oh, let me show you round Sandgem!"
  camera pan 177 843 0.8
  say "That's the Pokémon Center, with the red roof. They'll heal your Pokémon there whenever you like, and it's free."
  camera pan 187 843 0.8
  say "And that's the Poké Mart, with the blue roof. You can buy Poké Balls and Potions there."
  camera release 0.6
  say "Don't forget to tell your family you're helping the professor! Heal your Pokémon at the Center first, though."
  say "See you later, {player}!"
  walk sandgem_counterpart down 1 left 3
  hide sandgem_counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE 2
  end


# The assistant, spoken to (plan 02 · S14; the original's CounterpartTalk_Counterpart, script 10300). The Hall of
# Fame brings her back to stand before the lab, and what she says goes by how far the Pokédex has come: with every
# Pokémon of Sinnoh seen (CheckLocalDexCompleted: all 210 of the Sinnoh Pokédex) she wants it shown to the professor,
# which is where the post-game begins (plan 02 · S15). Until then she has heard the news from him.
#
# With the National Pokédex (plan 02 · S15, part a) she has more to say, in the original's order: has the player
# heard her sister's news of swarms (until FLAG_TALKED_TO_COUNTERPART_SISTER_WITH_NATIONAL_DEX), have they met Bebe
# (until her Eevee is given), then the Poké Radar's lesson, for which she goes off up Route 202 and waits there
# (FLAG_STARTED_COUNTERPART_POKE_RADAR_TUTORIAL, VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE 2; route_202.txt), and after it one of three
# things about the Radar and the Pokédex.
#
# Until every Pokémon of Sinnoh has been seen she passes on what she has heard (plan 02 · S15, part c), in the
# original's order: the elder of Celestic Town has something to tell the player, until the elder has told it
# (FLAG_UNLOCKED_DIALGA_PALKIA_SPEAR_PILLAR, CelesticTownNorthHouse.txt); after that the professor's word that Dialga or
# Palkia may show itself at Spear Pillar, unless one of the two has been battled there since the Hall of Fame was last
# entered (VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE or VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE 1), when she has her congratulations.
#
# Her words for a National Pokédex filled to the last species wait for a way to fill it (plan 03 · D12a). The original
# also asks whether today is the player's birthday, which it reads from the console's own profile: this game keeps
# none, and the question is left out for good.
script Counterpart
  if not flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Tour
  face self player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  if nationaldex goto National
  if var SEEN >= 210 goto SeenAll
  # CounterpartTalk_ElderHasSomethingToTell, and CounterpartTalk_CheckDialgaOrPalkiaAtSpearPillar
  if not flag FLAG_UNLOCKED_DIALGA_PALKIA_SPEAR_PILLAR goto Elder
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE == 1 goto Champion
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE == 1 goto Champion
  say "Oh, {player}! The professor told me something. He thinks Dialga or Palkia might show itself at Spear Pillar again!"
  end
label Elder
  say "Hi, {player}! You know the elder of Celestic Town, don't you? She asked me to tell you she has something important to say to you."
  end
label Champion
  say "{player}! The professor told me everything! Congratulations, Champion!"
  end
label SeenAll
  say "Hi, {player}! May I have a look at your Pokédex?"
  say "... ... ..."
  say "Oh! You've seen every kind of Pokémon there is in Sinnoh! Come on, let's show it to Professor Rowan!"
  end
label National
  if not flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_COUNTERPART_SISTER_WITH_NATIONAL_DEX goto Sister
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_EEVEE goto Bebe
  if not flag FLAG_STARTED_COUNTERPART_POKE_RADAR_TUTORIAL goto Lesson
  random 3
  if result == 0 goto HowsTheRadar
  if result == 1 goto Sparkles
  say "I'm helping the professor with the National Pokédex too, {player}. I'll never catch up with you, but I'm not giving up!"
  end
label HowsTheRadar
  say "How are you getting on with the Poké Radar, {player}? The Pokémon it finds aren't ones you'd meet just walking about."
  end
label Sparkles
  say "Did you know? Once in a long while a patch of grass doesn't shake for the Poké Radar: it sparkles. I wonder what's in one of those..."
  end
label Sister
  if girl goto SisterLucas
  say "So that's the National Pokédex! Oh, {player}, have you had a chat with my little sister lately? She's found out something you'll want to hear."
  end
label SisterLucas
  say "So that's the National Pokédex! Say, {player}, have you seen my little sister lately? She's picked up some news you can use."
  end
label Bebe
  say "{player}, have you met Bebe, in Hearthome City? She looks after the Pokémon Boxes. She's been asking after people filling in the National Pokédex!"
  end
label Lesson
  # CounterpartTalk_StartPokeRadarTutorial: she goes off east, to the road north
  setflag FLAG_STARTED_COUNTERPART_POKE_RADAR_TUTORIAL
  say "The professor gave you a Poké Radar, didn't he? I'll show you how to use it! Come and find me on Route 202, in the tall grass."
  if facing left walk self up 1
  move self right 10
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  waitmoves
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE 2
  end
label Tour
  sayown
  end
