# The Humilau Gym (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/humilau_city_gym.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Black 2's pool of lily pads is not walked here: the walkway from the door, the cross-walk with two ace trainers on it and the
# dock where Marlon (0) waits once he is back from his swim (VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 2). His battle (Black 2's TRAINER_LEADER_MARLON;
# _2 is the Challenge Mode's, plan 13's) gives the Wave Badge, the region's eighth (`givebadge wave`), and TM55
# (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM55), which our data's table, Platinum's, fills with its own move; then he goes out ahead (3).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  end


script GymGuide
  face 7 player
  speaker "Guide"
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_HUMILAU goto Water
  if badge wave goto Won
  if var VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU < 2 goto Away
  say "Most days you'd hop the lily pads and slide over the water. Today the pads are tied up, so the walkway takes you to the dock."
  say "Water-type Pokémon don't like Grass and Electric moves. Remember that!"
  end
label Water
  say "This Gym may feel like a resort, but the Gym Leader's no pushover! Here's a little something. Stay sharp!"
  give "Fresh Water"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_HUMILAU
  end
label Away
  say "If you're looking for the Gym Leader, he went swimming off into the sea, shouting about the waves! Try looking for him."
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Marlon! Now you've got every Badge in Unova. That's something to shout about!"
  end


script Marlon
  face 0 player
  speaker marlon "Marlon"
  if badge wave goto Won
  scene "Marlon's battle"
  say "Sup! Here already, huh? You look strong! Shoots! Let's do this!"
  battle 0 as "unova_leader_marlon"
  say "You don't just look strong, you're strong for real! I got swept right away, yo."
  say "Oh yeah. I was so surprised I almost forgot! I've gotta give you this."
  givebadge wave
  text "{player} received the Wave Badge from Marlon!"
  say "That's the Wave Badge, Unova's newest Gym Badge! Pretty sweet, right? Now you've got all eight, so any Pokémon will listen to you."
  say "Oh yeah, I've got a TM for you too."
  give "TM55"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM55
  say "Use it when the waves are with you, 'K? Shoots! I'm off, then. Hope it comes in handy!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 0
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MARLON_GYM
  if var VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU < 3 setvar VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 3
  fade in
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM55 goto Tm
  say "Waves can be rough or calm, but it's still the same sea. There's lots of ways to look at the same thing, yo."
  end
label Tm
  say "Whoa, you forgot your TM! Here."
  give "TM55"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM55
  end
