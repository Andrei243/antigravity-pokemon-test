# Route 5 (plan 22 · U6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats
# (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/route_5.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_5 is the route's count (Black 2's 0x4139): 0 until Bianca has caught up, 1 afterwards.
#
# As the player first comes in from Nimbasa's gate, Bianca (bianca, ours) runs out of it after them (our trigger on
# 372, 437 to 439, at 0): she gives HM02 and tells of the Hidden Grottoes, then hurries back to Nimbasa. Black 2's
# grotto she takes the player to is plan 22 · U11's.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script BiancasFly
  scene "Bianca on Route 5"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_ROUTE_5
  show bianca
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "{player}! Heeey! Wait for me!"
  face player right
  face bianca player
  say "Phew, I caught you! Good timing, too. I've been wanting to give you this!"
  give "HM02"
  say "Ta-da! It's Fly! Teach it to a Pokémon and it can carry you to any town you've visited, like a Pokémon Center you know."
  ask "Oh, and {player}, do you know about Hidden Grottoes?"
  if yes goto Knows
  say "Then let me tell you! Sometimes there's a little hollow among the trees where Pokémon like to hide. That's a Hidden Grotto!"
  goto Leaves
label Knows
  say "You do? Great! Keep your eyes open, then. You might find one just around the corner!"
label Leaves
  say "Oops, I said I'd help the professor this afternoon! See you, {player}! Good luck in Driftveil!"
  walk bianca right 1
  hide bianca
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_ROUTE_5
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_5 1
  endscene
  end
