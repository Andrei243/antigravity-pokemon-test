# Lysandre's laboratory under his café in Lumiose City (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as
# they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): past
# two of Team Flare's grunts, Lysandre waits among his machines, tells of the ultimate weapon and battles; beaten, he goes
# to Team Flare's base under Geosenge Town to wake it. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 27 becomes 28 here,
# which opens the hidden way down in Geosenge's ring of stones (geosenge_town.txt's HiddenDoor). His team is Kalos's
# trainers.json's kalos_lysandre_1: a Gyarados that Mega Evolves with his own Key Stone.
#
# The people: the two grunts (trainers of the room) and lysandre (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_LYSANDRE_LABS once he leaves).


script Lysandre
  face lysandre player
  speaker "Lysandre"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY != 27 end
  scene "Lysandre's laboratory"
  say "So you came. I hoped you would. You have strength, {player}, and strength should see what I have built."
  say "Three thousand years ago a king of Kalos built a weapon to end a war. It ended far more than that."
  say "I mean to use it again. Not for war: to sweep the world clean, and leave it to the few who deserve it."
  say "You disagree? Then show me your answer the only way it matters."
  battle self as "kalos_lysandre_1"
  say "Your answer is strong. It changes nothing. The weapon lies under Geosenge Town, and it is already waking."
  say "Come and watch the world become beautiful, if you like."
  hide lysandre
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_LYSANDRE_LABS
  defeat "kalos_flare_grunt_labs_1"
  defeat "kalos_flare_grunt_labs_2"
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 28
  objective kalosFlareHQ
  endscene
  end


# Plan 23 · Z4b, Looker's fifth case (LumioseCity_LookerBureau.txt, VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 5): once Malva has told of him
# (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS), Xerosic of what was Team Flare has set up among Lysandre's machines, with two scientists and
# Essentia, who is Emma in a suit he controls. The scientists battle first; Essentia then battles three times without
# rest, until her Espurr's cry reaches her through the suit; and Xerosic gives in. That ends the case and begins
# Looker's farewell (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_FAREWELL, VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 6). The people: xerosic, essentia,
# scientist_clothilde and scientist_ernest (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS until FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS_DONE).

script ScientistClothilde
  face scientist_clothilde player
  speaker "Clothilde"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LABS_CLOTHILDE goto Beaten
  say "Visitors? Dr. Xerosic is not to be disturbed. His work is nearly finished!"
  battle self as "kalos_scientist_clothilde"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LABS_CLOTHILDE
  say "Disturbed anyway. He won't be pleased."
  end
label Beaten
  say "The suit was only ever a test. Nobody was supposed to get hurt. That's what he told us."
  end

script ScientistErnest
  face scientist_ernest player
  speaker "Ernest"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LABS_ERNEST goto Beaten
  say "Team Flare is finished, but science goes on! Out of the way!"
  battle self as "kalos_scientist_ernest"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LABS_ERNEST
  say "Science goes on... somewhere else, I suppose."
  end
label Beaten
  say "I'm going back to teaching. Children never control anybody with a suit."
  end

script Essentia
  face essentia player
  speaker "Essentia"
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_LABS_CLOTHILDE goto Waiting
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_LABS_ERNEST goto Waiting
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_ESSENTIA_FREED goto Freed
  scene "Essentia"
  say "Intruder. Remove intruder."
  speaker none
  text "{player} called out to the figure in the suit by Emma's name. It did not answer to it."
  speaker "Essentia"
  say "Remove... intruder."
  battle self as "kalos_essentia_1"
  heal
  say "Order repeats. Remove intruder."
  battle self as "kalos_essentia_2"
  heal
  say "Remove... {player}... Remove..."
  battle self as "kalos_essentia_3"
  heal
  speaker none
  cry "Espurr"
  text "A small grey Pokémon darted in past the machines and cried out at the figure in the suit: Mimi!"
  speaker "Essentia"
  say "Mimi...? Mimi! {player}? Where am I? The suit... I can't take it off!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_ESSENTIA_FREED
  speaker "Xerosic"
  say "Remarkable. A Pokémon's cry, stronger than my signal. I did not account for affection."
  endscene
  end
label Waiting
  say "..."
  speaker none
  text "The figure in the dark suit stands perfectly still, as if waiting for an order."
  end
label Freed
  say "Make him take it off, {player}. Please."
  end

script Xerosic
  face xerosic player
  speaker "Xerosic"
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_ESSENTIA_FREED goto Watching
  scene "Xerosic gives in"
  say "You beat my scientists, and then my best experiment, three times. And a little Espurr finished the job."
  say "Lysandre wanted to remake the world. I only wanted to see what a person could become, with the right suit."
  say "Very well. The signal is off. The suit is hers now, with nobody's orders in it but her own."
  speaker "Essentia"
  say "...It still fits. Maybe I'll keep it. A bureau needs a secret agent, right?"
  speaker "Xerosic"
  say "I suppose your detective will want to arrest me. Tell him I will be waiting at the café, with a very black coffee."
  hide xerosic
  hide essentia
  hide scientist_clothilde
  hide scientist_ernest
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS_DONE
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_FAREWELL
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_EMMA_AWAY
  setvar VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 6
  endscene
  end
label Watching
  say "My scientists will deal with you. And if not them, Essentia will. Do watch, it is quite something."
  end
