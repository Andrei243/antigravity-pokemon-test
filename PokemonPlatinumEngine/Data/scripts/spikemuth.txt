# Spikemuth (plan 33 · GL5, its second session), the old town behind its walls at Circhester Bay's west end, on the open
# world's engine: its places are Data/world3d/galar/places/spikemuth.json. Spikemuth has no stadium: its gym is the street,
# Team Yell's two on it, and Piers on the stage at its end, who never Dynamaxes. The lines are our own, on Sword and
# Shield's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Circhester, Route 9, Spikemuth and
# Hammerlocke's stadium"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is postwick.txt's count: 34 through the gate, 35 the Dark Badge.


# Piers on the stage: the seventh badge, and his word about his sister
script Piers
  face piers player
  speaker "Piers"
  if badge dark goto Won
  scene "Piers"
  say "So you got past the shutter. And past Team Yell. My sister must think you're worth it."
  say "I'm Piers. This town's Leader, for what that's worth. No stadium, no Power Spot, no giants. Just a stage and a battle."
  say "Let's make some noise, then."
  battle piers as "galar_piers_1"
  say "...Alright. That was a good gig."
  say "The Dark Badge. Take it out of here and show everyone what Spikemuth thinks of you."
  givebadge dark
  text "{player} received the Dark Badge from Piers!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_SPIKEMUTH_GYM
  defeat "galar_yell_grunt_bryn"
  defeat "galar_yell_grunt_eira"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_MARNIE_SPIKEMUTH
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 35
  say "Seven. Raihan's in Hammerlocke, back the way you came. Tell him I said he won't get an easy one."
  objective galarRaihan
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Look after Marnie on the road, eh? She won't ask you to."
  end
