# Mt. Coronet, the northern hall of its first floor (plan 02 · S10, S12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_mt_coronet_1f_north_room_1.s).
#
# Two Team Galactic grunts stand side by side across the passage to the stairs down, which lead under the mountain
# to Route 216 and the north. They talk between themselves about the fog below, and go only when Commander Mars is
# beaten at Lake Verity (FLAG_HIDE_MT_CORONET_1F_NORTH_ROOM_1_GRUNTS_M, lake_verity.txt).
#
# Once the button in their headquarters' control room has been pressed (plan 02 · S11) a third grunt stands in the
# hall's south, who has found that nobody reaches the summit from this side
# (FLAG_HIDE_MT_CORONET_1F_NORTH_ROOM_1_GRUNT_F, set again at Sendoff Spring, plan 02 · S12).


script Grunts
  face grunt_m_1 down
  speaker "Galactic Grunt"
  say "The fog down those stairs is thicker than soup. I couldn't see my own boots."
  face grunt_m_2 up
  say "Don't look at me. None of my Pokémon can use Defog. So here we stand, until somebody tells us otherwise."
  face grunt_m_1 left
  face grunt_m_2 left
  say "And nobody gets past us in the meantime. That means you!"
  end


# The grunt who came the wrong way round (the original's MtCoronet1FNorthRoom1_GruntF).
script GruntF
  speaker "Galactic Grunt"
  say "Everyone was told to gather at the top of Mt. Coronet. So I came in from Route 211, like a fool."
  say "There's no way up to the peak from this side! I'll have to go all the way round to the other entrance..."
  end
