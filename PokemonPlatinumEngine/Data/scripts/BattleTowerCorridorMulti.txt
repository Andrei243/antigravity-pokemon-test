# The corridor of the Battle Tower's Multi Battle Room (plan 06 · R18a). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# scripts_battle_tower_corridor_multi.s and frontier_scripts_battle_tower_corridor_multi.s are the original.

# The player comes out of the west lift and the partner chosen in the Battle Salon out of the east one; they meet
# before the middle doors and go in side by side. Anyone else finds an empty corridor and the lifts back down.


script OnEnter
  tower active
  if result == 0 end
  tower partnered
  if result < 0 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_BATTLE_TOWER_MULTI_PARTNER
  show partner
  place partner 14 3 down
  move partner down 1 left 5 up 1
  walk player down 1 right 5 up 1
  waitmoves
  sound "door_slide"
  hide partner
  warp "BattleTowerMultiBattleRoom" 7 9 up
  end
