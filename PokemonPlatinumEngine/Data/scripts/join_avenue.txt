# Three of the avenue's shopfronts are open, each a shopkeeper of ours behind a counter in one of the street's bays:
# the market (marts.json's join_avenue_market), the flower shop's mulches (join_avenue_flowers) and the café's drinks
# (join_avenue_cafe). Black 2's shops are opened by the avenue's visitors, ranked up and run by the player as its
# manager; that is plan 22 · U11's (the rulings, "Unova's second chapter, part c"). The counters' ends read as the
# shops' signs.


script Market
  speaker "Shopkeeper"
  say "Welcome to the avenue's market! Balls, medicine, everything a Trainer needs for the road ahead."
  shop "join_avenue_market"
  say "Thanks for shopping on Join Avenue! Tell your friends!"
  end


script Flowers
  speaker "Florist"
  say "Hello there! Mulch for your berries, and sweet honey for the Pokémon who love it. Have a look!"
  shop "join_avenue_flowers"
  say "Grow something lovely! Come back and tell me how it turned out."
  end


script Cafe
  speaker "Waitress"
  say "Welcome to the café! Something cold to drink before you head into Nimbasa?"
  shop "join_avenue_cafe"
  say "Enjoy! And mind the crowds on the way into the city."
  end
