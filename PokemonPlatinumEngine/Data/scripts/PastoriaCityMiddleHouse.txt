# The boy wants to see a male and a female Combee at once, on the team (team combee: the original's
# CheckPartyCombeeGenderCount), and gives the Macho Brace for the sight, once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_MIDDLE_HOUSE_MACHO_BRACE). The first time he only asks
# (FLAG_TALKED_TO_PASTORIA_CITY_MIDDLE_HOUSE_BUG_CATCHER).


script Combee
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_MIDDLE_HOUSE_MACHO_BRACE goto Good
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_PASTORIA_CITY_MIDDLE_HOUSE_BUG_CATCHER goto Look
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_PASTORIA_CITY_MIDDLE_HOUSE_BUG_CATCHER
  say "Hey, have you caught a Combee of each kind, a boy and a girl?" "If you have, I want to see the two of them together." "Show me and I'll give you a Macho Brace. Deal?"
  end
label Look
  team combee
  if result == 0 goto None
  if result == 1 goto One
  say "Wow! A girl Combee AND a boy Combee! I've gone all giddy!" "Here's your reward, like I promised. Go on, take it!"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_MIDDLE_HOUSE_MACHO_BRACE
  give "Macho Brace"
label Good
  say "You're good! You'd make a great Bug Maniac!"
  end
label None
  say "Aww, you haven't got even one Combee?" "They're so cute, the way the smell of Honey brings them buzzing along." "Show me a boy and a girl together, OK? I'll give you a Macho Brace if you do."
  end
label One
  say "That's it! A Combee! It's sooo cute!" "It'd be even better with one of the other kind beside it, though. The girls are rare, you know, so everybody wants one." "Show me a boy and a girl together and the Macho Brace is yours."
  end
