# The gate between Route 218 and Canalave City (plan 01 · M11, part d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_route_218_gate_to_canalave_city.s).
#
# The professor's assistant, who is {assistant}'s father, waits in the middle of the room for the player to come
# through (the original's trigger, a column five tiles deep at x 7, while VAR_ROUTE_218_GATE_TO_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE
# is 0). He upgrades the Pokédex so that it tells males from females and one form from another (the original's
# TurnOnPokedexFormDetection: FLAG_ENABLED_POKEDEX_FORM_DETECTION here, set and read by nothing yet, plan 03), and
# leaves by the east door (FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_218_GATE_TO_CANALAVE_CITY_SCIENTIST_M).


# Route218GateToCanalaveCity_CoordEvent_ScientistM
script Upgrade
  emote scientist_m exclaim
  if var PLAYER_Y == 5 walk scientist_m right 1 up 2
  if var PLAYER_Y == 6 walk scientist_m right 1 up 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 walk scientist_m right 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 8 walk scientist_m right 1 down 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 9 walk scientist_m right 1 down 2
  face scientist_m right
  face player left
  call Words
  if var PLAYER_Y == 5 walk scientist_m down 2 right 4
  if var PLAYER_Y == 6 walk scientist_m down 1 right 4
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 walk scientist_m up 1 right 3 down 1 right 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 8 walk scientist_m up 1 right 4
  if var PLAYER_Y == 9 walk scientist_m up 2 right 4
  call Leaves
  end


# Spoken to where he stands, by someone who came in the other way
script Assistant
  call Words
  call Leaves
  end


script Leaves
  sound "stairs"
  hide scientist_m
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_218_GATE_TO_CANALAVE_CITY_SCIENTIST_M
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_218_GATE_TO_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 1
  return


script Words
  speaker "Assistant"
  say "{player}! It's been a long time!" "Ah, that look. You're wondering who on earth I am, aren't you?" "I work for Professor Rowan. And I'm {assistant}'s father, as it happens."
  say "The professor asked me to upgrade your Pokédex. I had a hunch you'd come through this gate sooner or later, so here I've waited." "Might I borrow your Pokédex a moment, {player}?"
  say "Now, where does the software go... In here, was it?" "Ka-chunk. Ka-chunk!"
  sound "pc_on"
  setflag FLAG_ENABLED_POKEDEX_FORM_DETECTION
  say "There, all done! Your Pokédex is upgraded." "I'd better say what's new. It can now show you how the males and the females of a Pokémon differ."
  say "Well, I'll let you get on with your work for the professor." "One more thing, if I may: keep an eye on Team Galactic. The professor is worried about what they're up to."
  return
