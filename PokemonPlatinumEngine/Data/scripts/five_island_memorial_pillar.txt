# The Memorial Pillar on Five Island (plan 19 · K9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/FiveIsland_MemorialPillar/scripts.inc).
#
# A man keeps watch by a stone raised for his Onix. Lemonade left at the stone was his Onix's favourite; once the
# player leaves one he thanks them with TM42 (FLAG_GOT_TM42_AT_MEMORIAL_PILLAR, FireRed's). FireRed's second flag, for
# a bag with no room, has no use here: a bag always has room.


script MemorialMan
  face memorial_man player
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TM42_AT_MEMORIAL_PILLAR goto Thanked
  say "This stone stands for my Onix. We were together for a long, long time."
  say "It loved Lemonade more than anything. I come here every day to keep it company."
  end
label Thanked
  say "Thank you for the Lemonade. I'm sure my Onix was glad of it."
  end


script Memorial
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TM42_AT_MEMORIAL_PILLAR goto Placed
  text "Words are cut into the stone: 'For Onix, my dearest friend.'"
  if not item "Lemonade" end
  ask "Leave a can of Lemonade at the stone?"
  if no end
  take "Lemonade"
  text "{player} set a can of Lemonade at the foot of the stone."
  face memorial_man player
  speaker "Man"
  say "Oh... You left Lemonade for my Onix? That was its favourite. How kind of you."
  say "Please, take this. I'd like you to have it."
  give "TM42"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM42_AT_MEMORIAL_PILLAR
  say "Facade hits all the harder when a Pokémon is burned, poisoned or paralysed. My Onix never gave up, either."
  end
label Placed
  text "A can of Lemonade sits at the foot of the stone."
  end
