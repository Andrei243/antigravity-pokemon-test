# Nimbasa City's amusement park (plan 22 · U6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# The Ferris wheel's attendant (wheel, ours, before the wheel's queue lane) gives a ride round the wheel: Black 2 rides
# it beside a Trainer of the day, after a battle; ours is the ride itself, a scene of the fade and lines over the dark,
# with the city by day or its lights after dark, and the player back on the ground where they stood.


script FerrisWheel
  face wheel player
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Ferris wheel! One turn takes in the whole of Nimbasa City, the stadiums and the sea beyond."
  ask "Would you like a ride?"
  if no goto Later
  say "Step into the car, please. Enjoy the view!"
  scene "The Ferris wheel"
  fade out 0.8
  if time night goto Night
  text "The car climbs slowly over the amusement park. The stadiums, the Gear Station and the river spread out below..."
  text "Far off to the west, a long bridge reaches across the water toward a city of warehouses."
  goto Down
label Night
  text "The car climbs slowly into the dark. Below, the whole of Nimbasa glitters like a box of jewels..."
  text "Far off to the west, the lights of a long bridge stretch across the black water."
label Down
  text "The car sways gently as it comes back down to the ground."
  fade in 0.8
  endscene
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome back! The view is different every time. Ride again whenever you like!"
  end
label Later
  say "No hurry! The wheel turns all day and all night."
  end
