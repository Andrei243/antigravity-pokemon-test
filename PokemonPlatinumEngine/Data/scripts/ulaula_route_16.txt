# Route 16, Ula'ula Island (plan 23 · Z7; plan 23, the rest of the islands). The lines are our own. Dexio and Sina, the
# Kalos professor's two assistants, stand by their caravan here as Sun and Moon have them, give the Zygarde Cube once,
# and put the cells it holds together into a Zygarde: its 10% Forme once ten are gathered, its 50% Forme at fifty, and
# at a hundred one whose cells cling together, with Power Construct (rulings, "The Battle Tree, the power plant,
# Zygarde's cells and the rest of the islands"). The cells are things of the map about Alola (common.ZygardeCell).


script Dexio
  face self player
  speaker "Dexio"
  if item "Zygarde Cube" goto Assemble
  say "Alola! We came all the way from Kalos, following something green that slipped through the islands' soil."
  speaker "Sina"
  say "Tiny Zygarde Cells, scattered all over Alola! On their own they're shy, but this Cube gathers them right up."
  say "We'd love for you to collect them. The more you find, the more of Zygarde we can put back together!"
  give "Zygarde Cube"
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "Bring the Cube back whenever you've found ten or more. We'll be here, keeping the caravan warm."
  end
label Assemble
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_10_MADE goto Ten
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_50_MADE goto Fifty
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_100_MADE goto Hundred
  say "Every cell of Alola, gathered and put together. Professor Sycamore won't believe us!"
  end
label Ten
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELLS < 10 goto Short
  ask "Your Cube holds {var:VAR_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELLS} cells. Shall we put ten of them together into a Zygarde?"
  if no goto Later
  if not party < 6 goto Full
  givepokemon "Zygarde-10" 50
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received Zygarde!"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_10_MADE
  speaker "Sina"
  say "Ten cells make a swift little Zygarde. Find fifty, and we can make a bigger one!"
  end
label Fifty
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELLS < 50 goto Short
  ask "Your Cube holds {var:VAR_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELLS} cells. Shall we put fifty of them together into a Zygarde?"
  if no goto Later
  if not party < 6 goto Full
  givepokemon "Zygarde" 50
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received Zygarde!"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_50_MADE
  speaker "Sina"
  say "Fifty cells! And yet... the Cube feels like it wants more. Every last one of them, maybe."
  end
label Hundred
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELLS < 100 goto Short
  ask "All one hundred! Shall we put them together into a Zygarde whose cells never let go of one another?"
  if no goto Later
  if not party < 6 goto Full
  givepokemon "Zygarde-50-Power-Construct" 60
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received Zygarde!"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_100_MADE
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "When it's pushed to its limit in battle, its scattered cells will rush back to it. You'll see its true shape!"
  end
label Short
  say "The Cube holds {var:VAR_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELLS} cells for now. Keep searching! They hide in the grass, by the sea, even in caves."
  end
label Full
  say "Your team is full! Make some room, and we'll put the Zygarde together for you."
  end
label Later
  say "No hurry. The cells aren't going anywhere. Well, they wriggle a bit."
  end


script Sina
  face self player
  speaker "Sina"
  if item "Zygarde Cube" goto Hint
  say "Have you talked to Dexio? He's the one holding the box. Well, he thinks he is. I packed it!"
  end
label Hint
  say "Some cells only come out in the quiet places: a cemetery, a beach no one visits, the top of a mountain."
  end
