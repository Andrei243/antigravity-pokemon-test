# The Champion's room (plan 20 · J12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's
# beats (scr_seq_T10R0601).
#
# As the challenger comes up to the dais (the area's first trigger, on VAR_UNK_40C7 0), Lance comes down to meet them:
# his words and his battle (a battle lost ends it there, and the hall's arrival puts the League back); then Mary of
# the radio runs in with Professor Oak for the new Champion (FLAG_LANCES_ROOM_VISITORS, ours), and Lance leads the
# player through the door behind him (FLAG_HIDE_LANCES_ROOM_EXIT, ours) to the Hall of Fame. His rematch teams are
# plan 20 · J15's.


script Lance
  scene "Lance"
  if var PLAYER_X == 5 walk player right 1
  if var PLAYER_X == 7 walk player left 1
  face player up
  speaker "Lance"
  say "I've been waiting for you, {player}."
  walk t10r0601_wataru down 3
  say "I knew that one day you would stand here. Ever since the Lake of Rage, I've wanted to see how far you would go."
  say "We fought side by side once. Today I stand in your way, as the League's Champion should."
  say "My dragons have never been beaten in this room. Show me everything you have!"
  battle t10r0601_wataru as "johto_champion_lance"
  speaker "Lance"
  say "...It's over. I've lost."
  say "You and your Pokémon have a bond I've rarely seen. You trusted each other, and that trust won through."
  say "As the Champion, I'm proud to say it: you are the new Champion of the Pokémon League!"
  setvar VAR_UNK_40C7 1
  setflag FLAG_LANCES_ROOM_VISITORS
  show t10r0601_kurumi
  show t10r0601_ookido
  place t10r0601_kurumi 5 24 up
  place t10r0601_ookido 7 24 up
  speaker "Mary"
  say "Oh, wait! Wait for us, please!"
  walk t10r0601_kurumi up 13 fast
  walk t10r0601_ookido up 13
  face player down
  speaker "Mary"
  say "Is it over already? We've missed the whole thing! I'm Mary, from Pokémon Talk on the radio. I came to interview the new Champion!"
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "Calm yourself, Mary. {player}, you've won. I heard it from the doorway. Congratulations, Champion!"
  say "When I handed you that Pokédex, I never dreamt you'd be standing here so soon. Your Pokémon trust you. That's the strength of a true trainer."
  face player up
  speaker "Lance"
  say "{player}, come with me. There is one room left, where the League keeps the names of its Champions."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LANCES_ROOM_EXIT
  sound "door_open"
  # (to the doorway's foot: the door's own tile, 6, 2, is the way through, which he goes by as the player does)
  walk t10r0601_wataru up 6
  hide t10r0601_wataru
  sound "door_open"
  warp "IndigoLeague_HallOfFame" 6 21 up
  endscene
  end


# Lance, in the moment between his battle and his going (nobody speaks to him otherwise: the trigger stops the
# challenger first)
script Champion
  speaker "Lance"
  say "Come, {player}. The Hall of Fame is through here."
  end
