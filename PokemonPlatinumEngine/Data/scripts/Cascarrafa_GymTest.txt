# Kofu's hall, the end of his gym test (plan 34 · PD6): Kofu sends a challenger west to Porto Marinada's auction for the
# morning's best crate (FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_ERRAND), the crate is won there (porto_marinada.txt), and he battles once it
# is brought back; a gym trainer stands by the hall. Our own lines, on Scarlet and Violet's beat of the errand.


# Kofu's battle for the Water Badge. He Terastallizes his last into a Water type. Plan 34 · decision 3 at its
# recommendation: from the fourth gym on, a leader's team is scaled to the badges the challenger holds, its best at the
# cap of that many badges (paldea.json's "caps"), so in the academy's order (three held) Kofu's team is the original's
# and taken first it is no harder than Katy's (Data/world/paldea/trainers.json: paldea_kofu, paldea_kofu_b0 to _b7)
# Plan 34 · PD9: after Paldea's Hall of Fame the leader asks for a rematch once a day (FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_KOFU_REMATCH;
# docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "After Paldea's Hall of Fame"), with the team of paldea_kofu_rematch
script Leader
  face kofu player
  speaker "Kofu"
  if badge water goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_TEST_DONE goto Battle
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_ERRAND goto Waiting
  say "A challenger! Wonderful, wonderful. I'm Kofu, Cascarrafa's gym leader, and today I'm cooking for the whole gym!"
  say "Only I'm short one thing. Go west over the hills to Porto Marinada, and win me the morning's sea urchins at the auction."
  say "Bring me that crate and we'll battle while the pot boils. Off you go, quick as a current!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_ERRAND
  end
label Waiting
  say "The auction's at Porto Marinada, west over the hills and down to the sea. Bid smart, and bring me those urchins!"
  end
label Battle
  scene "Kofu"
  say "You got them! And still cold, too. That's the spirit I like to see in a challenger. Fantastic!"
  say "Now then. My Water types move like the tide: they come in slow, and then they come in all at once!"
  if badges >= 7 goto Held7
  if badges >= 6 goto Held6
  if badges >= 5 goto Held5
  if badges >= 4 goto Held4
  if badges >= 3 goto Held3
  if badges >= 2 goto Held2
  if badges >= 1 goto Held1
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b0"
  goto Beaten
label Held1
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b1"
  goto Beaten
label Held2
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b2"
  goto Beaten
label Held3
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu"
  goto Beaten
label Held4
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b4"
  goto Beaten
label Held5
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b5"
  goto Beaten
label Held6
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b6"
  goto Beaten
label Held7
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_b7"
label Beaten
  say "Swept away! What a feast of a battle. You've earned it, challenger: the Water Badge!"
  givebadge water
  text "{player} received the Water Badge from Kofu!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_CASCARRAFA_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_marisa"
  say "Stay for supper next time. There's always more in the pot than I meant to make!"
  endscene
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "A challenger who isn't a student? Ah, no, no. Enrol at the academy in Mesagoza first, then come back hungry!"
  end
label Won
  if not flag PaldeaHallOfFame goto Chat
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_KOFU_REMATCH goto Chat
  ask "The Champion! My team's been training on a diet of the finest catch. Fancy a second helping?"
  if no goto Chat
  say "Ha! Fresh from the sea, and full of fight!"
  battle kofu as "paldea_kofu_rematch"
  say "Delectable! Come back tomorrow. I'll have a new recipe for you."
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_KOFU_REMATCH
  end
label Chat
  say "Back again? The urchins went into the best stew I've made all year. Thanks to you!"
  end
