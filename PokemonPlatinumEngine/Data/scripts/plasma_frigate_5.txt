# Kyurem's chamber in the Plasma Frigate (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/plasma_frigate_5.s, read for the order alone).
#
# As the player comes up from the hold (our trigger, 11 to 13, 18, VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 7) Zinzolin (1) shows them Kyurem (0), the
# source of the ice fired on Opelucid City; Hugh (hugh_bridge) runs in, and the battle is a tag battle beside him (Black 2's
# TRAINER_RIVAL_28, _29 and _30 by the starter against TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_GRUNT_49 and TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_ZINZOLIN). Hugh asks after
# his sister's Purrloin and Zinzolin won't answer; Kyurem has recovered, and the Shadow Triad (2, 3) put both Trainers ashore on
# Route 21 (8), where the Frigate's flight is unova_route_21.txt's.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  end


script Kyurem
  scene "Zinzolin and Kyurem"
  place player 12 18 up
  face 1 player
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "You are an impressive Trainer to have come this far. Since you have gone to such trouble, let me show you something."
  speaker none
  text "Under the great cannon, something huge stirred in the cold..."
  cry "Kyurem"
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "The legendary Pokémon of ice! Its name is Kyurem. The missiles of ice we fired into Opelucid City were made with Kyurem's power and Team Plasma's science."
  say "Well. You could become a threat to Team Plasma, so we shall be rid of you here."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_BRIDGE
  show hugh_bridge
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Not with me around, you won't! {player}, thanks for switching off the barrier. That was a big help!"
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "Hmph! Then we shall be rid of you both!"
  if starter "Snivy" goto WithEmboar
  if starter "Tepig" goto WithSamurott
  partner hugh_bridge "unova_rival_30"
  goto Fight
label WithEmboar
  partner hugh_bridge "unova_rival_28"
  goto Fight
label WithSamurott
  partner hugh_bridge "unova_rival_29"
label Fight
  battle 4 and 1 with partner
  partner off
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "I'm not losing to Team Plasma! ...What is this place, anyway?"
  say "That Pokémon... It's freezing just to look at. Was it the one that attacked Opelucid City?"
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "Hmph. You are cleverer than I thought. With that much sense, why sneak into our ship at all?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Isn't it obvious? I'll do whatever it takes to get my sister's Pokémon back! Were you the one who stole a Purrloin in Aspertia City?"
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "If it was only a Purrloin, someone took it and is using it now. Why can't you see that? There are other Purrloin."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "That's the only Purrloin in the world that my grandpa caught for my little sister!"
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "One person's feelings. To you they matter a great deal. To everyone else, they are a trifle."
  say "Ah. Kyurem has recovered completely. We shall put the DNA Splicers to good use. Shadow Triad, I leave these two to you."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_BRIDGE
  show 2
  show 3
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Don't mess with me! You're the one who lost!"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "..."
  fade out 0.6
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_BRIDGE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_BRIDGE
  speaker none
  text "There is a rush of wind, and the cold deck is gone from under your feet..."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 8
  endscene
  warp "Unova" 798 242 left
  fade in
  end


script Zinzolin
  face 1 player
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "Children have no business in this chamber."
  end


script Grunt
  face 4 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Don't get any closer to Kyurem!"
  end
