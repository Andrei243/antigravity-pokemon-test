# Four Island (plan 19 · K9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/FourIsland/scripts.inc).
#
# The first time the player comes ashore (VAR_MAP_SCENE_FOUR_ISLAND 0) the rival is by the harbour, back from the Day
# Care with an Egg of his own; he says so and leaves (1). FireRed walks him up to the player and off; here he speaks
# where he stands and a dark screen takes him away. Six Island's Pokémon Center sets the variable too, for a player
# who has met him there first (six_island_pokemon_center_1f.txt).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_FOUR_ISLAND == 0 goto Rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FOUR_ISLAND_RIVAL
  end
label Rival
  scene "The rival on Four Island"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_FOUR_ISLAND_RIVAL
  show four_island_rival
  music "kanto/rival"
  emote four_island_rival exclaim
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey, {player}! You came all the way out here too? Took you long enough."
  say "The old couple on this island run a Day Care. I left two of mine there and they found an Egg. Can you believe it?"
  say "I'm going to hatch it and raise it into the toughest Pokémon around. Smell you later!"
  fade out 0.4
  hide four_island_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FOUR_ISLAND_RIVAL
  music area
  fade in 0.4
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_FOUR_ISLAND 1
  endscene
  end
