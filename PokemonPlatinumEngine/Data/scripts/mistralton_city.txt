# Mistralton City (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats
# (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/mistralton_city.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON is the city's count (Black 2's 0x40c2, 0x40c1 and 0x40cb together): 0 as the player first comes up
# from Chargestone Cave, 1 once Professor Juniper and Skyla have had their say and gone, 2 once the Jet Badge is won,
# 3 once Juniper is back from the Celestial Tower and waits in the Cargo Service's hall with Skyla, 4 once the flight to
# Lentimas Town has been taken (mistralton_city_3.txt).
#
# As the player comes up the road from the cave our trigger across it (100 to 104, 301, at 0) has Professor Juniper (4)
# there to meet them, with the Pokédex's thanks in a Master Ball, and Skyla (3) burst in asking for the lift over Twist
# Mountain the professor promised: Juniper wants to see the Celestial Tower first and goes, and Skyla asks which the
# player will do before she goes back to her Gym. The kids' treasure (0) is a ball at the runway's edge that a boy
# in the greenhouse names once the Jet Badge is won (mistralton_city_2.txt); the kids (1, 2) and their Ducklett (8)
# come as it is picked up and give TM40 (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM40).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script JuniperAndSkyla
  scene "Professor Juniper and Skyla"
  place player 103 298 left
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_MISTRALTON
  place 4 101 298 right
  show 4
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Hi there, {player}! It's lovely to be able to talk to you in person at last. I'm Professor Juniper!"
  say "You took the Pokédex and came all the way out here with your partners. Let me see how you are getting on."
  say "Marvellous! You've met so many Pokémon already. Thank you for your hard work. This is a token of my gratitude."
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_MASTER_BALL goto AfterBall
  give "Master Ball"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_MASTER_BALL
  say "A Master Ball is the most powerful Poké Ball there is. It catches any Pokémon without fail."
  say "Journeys are about meeting Pokémon, so never let one slip by. Keep the Master Ball for the one you really can't let go."
label AfterBall
  say "It still amazes me how much the Pokémon of this region have changed in two years. My research will never end. That's what makes it fun!"
  speaker "???"
  say "Professor Juniperrrrr!"
  place 3 91 298 right
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SKYLA_MISTRALTON
  show 3
  walk 3 right 7 fast
  face 4 3
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Why, if it isn't Skyla!"
  say "{player}, this is Skyla. She's Mistralton City's Gym Leader."
  speaker "Skyla"
  say "Why are you so surprised, Professor? You asked for a lift in my plane to cross Twist Mountain, since you can't reach Opelucid City on foot!"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Ha ha, you're quite right! But I have a quick favour to ask first. I'd like to have a look at the Celestial Tower. Would you mind waiting until I'm through?"
  speaker "Skyla"
  say "Honestly! Okay, but don't take all day!"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "See you, {player}! Be sure to get along with all kinds of Pokémon!"
  walk 4 left 8
  hide 4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_MISTRALTON
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_TOWER
  face 3 player
  speaker "Skyla"
  say "Honestly! I can never tell whether she's laid back or just doesn't listen to anything outside her own head."
  say "So you're {player}? I'm Skyla, Mistralton's Gym Leader, and a pilot besides. Nice to meet you!"
  say "What's next? Will you come to my Gym and challenge me? Or will you follow the professor to the Celestial Tower and train a little?"
  say "As long as I get to fly her over Twist Mountain in the end, I don't mind which!"
  walk 3 left 6 fast
  hide 3
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SKYLA_MISTRALTON
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SKYLA_GYM
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON 1
  endscene
  end


script SkylaOutside
  face 3 player
  speaker "Skyla"
  say "Are you coming to my Gym? Or to the Celestial Tower? Either is fine by me!"
  end


script JuniperOutside
  face 4 player
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Isn't it a marvellous place for research? So much wind! So much sky!"
  end


# The ball at the runway's edge, which the boy in the greenhouse names once the Jet Badge is won
script RunwayTreasure
  scene "The kids' treasure"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_RUNWAY_KIDS
  place 1 72 328 left
  place 2 72 330 left
  place 8 73 329 left
  show 1
  show 2
  show 8
  speaker "Youngster"
  say "Oh! You found our treasure!"
  speaker "Girl"
  say "Found it!"
  speaker "Youngster"
  say "Just kidding! Our Ducklett already knows Aerial Ace, so we'll give you this TM instead!"
  give "TM40"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM40
  setflag own
  say "Aerial Ace never misses! We hope it helps you win!"
  cry "Ducklett"
  speaker "Girl"
  say "See you! Bye-bye!"
  hide 1
  hide 2
  hide 8
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_RUNWAY_KIDS
  endscene
  end
