# The Pokémon League's hall on the Indigo Plateau (plan 20 · J10, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own. The guard before the Elite Four's door stands in the doorway itself, as HeartGold's does; spoken to
# with Johto's eight Badges he steps out of it, as Sinnoh's guard does (PokemonLeagueNorthPokemonCenter.txt), and
# FLAG_INDIGO_PLATEAU_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY, a flag of ours, keeps him aside. The League's battles are its rooms'
# (plan 20 · J12); the rival who waits here after the Hall of Fame (HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_INDIGO_PLATEAU_RIVAL) is
# the post-game's, plan 20 · J15.


# Coming in again finds the guard where he stepped to; and whoever comes in faces the Elite Four from the first again
# (plan 20 · J12): the four rooms' doors shut and their masters unbeaten (FLAG_HIDE_<WHO>S_ROOM_EXIT, which each room
# sets as its master is beaten), Lance on his dais with nobody come in (VAR_UNK_40C7 0, FLAG_LANCES_ROOM_VISITORS and
# FLAG_HIDE_LANCES_ROOM_EXIT cleared), as HeartGold puts the League back whenever it is left. Nobody comes back out
# through a room's door here: a battle lost or the Hall of Fame takes the player elsewhere.
script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_WILLS_ROOM_EXIT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_KOGAS_ROOM_EXIT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_BRUNOS_ROOM_EXIT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_KARENS_ROOM_EXIT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LANCES_ROOM_EXIT
  clearflag FLAG_LANCES_ROOM_VISITORS
  setvar VAR_UNK_40C7 0
  if flag FLAG_INDIGO_PLATEAU_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY place t10r0101_counterm 12 8 down
  end


# He looks the Badges over and steps out of the doorway, to the side away from the player
script DoorGuard
  speaker "Guard"
  if flag FLAG_INDIGO_PLATEAU_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY goto Moved
  say "Beyond this door the Elite Four are waiting. Only a Trainer with all eight of Johto's Gym Badges goes through."
  if badges < 8 goto Short
  say "Let me see... Eight Badges, every one of them Johto's. You may go in."
  if facing left goto FromTheEast
  walk self right 1
  if facing right goto FromTheWest
  face self down
  goto Away
label FromTheWest
  face self left
  goto Away
label FromTheEast
  walk self left 1
  face self right
label Away
  setflag FLAG_INDIGO_PLATEAU_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY
  say "Once you start, you face all four and then the Champion. There is no coming back out until it is over."
  end
label Short
  say "Come back when you have all eight. The Elite Four won't see anyone with fewer."
  end
label Moved
  say "Rest your Pokémon well. Nobody will heal them for you between the rooms."
  end
