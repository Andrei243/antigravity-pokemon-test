# Agatha's room, the third of Kanto's Elite Four (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PokemonLeague_AgathasRoom/scripts.inc). As Lorelei's room
# (pokemon_league_loreleis_room.txt): spoken to, she battles; beaten, her door opens (FLAG_DEFEATED_AGATHA).


script Agatha
  face agatha player
  speaker "Agatha"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_AGATHA goto Beaten
  scene "Agatha"
  say "I am Agatha of the Elite Four. Oak's taken a liking to you, has he? That old fool."
  say "Pokémon are for battling! I'll show you what a real trainer can do, child."
  battle self as "kanto_elite_four_agatha"
  speaker "Agatha"
  say "Oh ho! You're something special, child."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_AGATHA
  sound "door_open"
  say "I see now why Oak thinks so highly of you. Go on, then. You're nearly at the top."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "You win! Now be off with you. There's one more of us waiting."
  end
