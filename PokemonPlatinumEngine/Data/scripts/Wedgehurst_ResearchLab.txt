  end

  end


# After the Hall of Fame (plan 33 · GL7, part b; plan 06 · R20c's question 7): Sonia back at her lab with the Wishing
# Stars and the Key Stone's light, setting a Key Stone into a Dynamax Band. The player keeps both and is given a third
# item, the Mega Max Band (FLAG_GOT_MEGA_MAX_BAND), which lets a Mega-Evolved Pokémon Dynamax; and she points to the
# den by the Lake of Outrage. Without a Key Stone (a game begun in Galar) she says where one comes from. Our own lines.
script Sonia
  face sonia_lab player
  speaker "Sonia"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_MEGA_MAX_BAND goto Given
  say "Champion! I've been reading about the Wishing Stars again, and about a stone from Kalos whose light does something very like theirs."
  if not item "Key Stone" goto NoStone
  if not item "Dynamax Band" goto NoStone
  scene "Sonia's band"
  say "Is that a Key Stone? And your Dynamax Band! Hold still, I want to try something I've only ever drawn on paper."
  text "Sonia sets a sliver of Wishing Star beside a Key Stone of her own into a new band, and the two lights run together."
  say "There! A Mega Max Band. With this, a Pokémon that's Mega Evolved can still Dynamax. Keep your old band and stone, too."
  give "Mega Max Band"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_MEGA_MAX_BAND
  say "Oh, and the Watt traders say a den by the Lake of Outrage has started glowing black. Something Mega-sized, I'd bet."
  endscene
  end
label NoStone
  say "If you ever find a Key Stone, from Kalos I'd guess, bring it here with your Dynamax Band. I've a band I'm itching to build."
  end
label Given
  say "How's the Mega Max Band? I want every reading you can bring me from that den by the Lake of Outrage!"
  end
