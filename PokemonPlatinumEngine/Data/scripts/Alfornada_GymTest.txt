# Tulip's studio, the end of her gym test (plan 34 · PD6, its second session). The test is her photographs: the
# photographer calls three moods, and the challenger strikes the pose that fits each. Each shot taken right is kept
# (VAR_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_PHOTOS 1 and 2) and the next is called at once; a pose that doesn't fit ends the turn, and that
# same mood is called again next time. All three and the test is passed (FLAG_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_TEST_DONE). A gym trainer
# stands in the studio, and Tulip at its far end battles a challenger who has passed. Our own lines, moods and poses.


# The photographer's session: three moods, each matched by one of three poses (never the last, which the cancel button
# picks)
script Photos
  speaker "Photographer"
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_TEST_DONE goto Done
  if var VAR_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_PHOTOS >= 2 goto Resolve
  if var VAR_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_PHOTOS >= 1 goto Calm
  say "Ah, the challenger! Tulip wants three shots of you before she'll battle. I call the mood, you give me the pose. Ready?"
  say "First: joy! Show me pure, sunny joy!"
  choose "Strike a pose!" "A wide smile, arms flung open" "A thoughtful frown" "Back to the camera"
  if result != 0 goto Blurred
  text "Click! The flash went off."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_PHOTOS 1
label Calm
  say "Lovely! Next: calm. The stillness of a lake at dawn."
  choose "Strike a pose!" "Jumping in the air" "Eyes half closed, hands folded" "Pointing at the camera"
  if result != 1 goto Blurred
  text "Click! The flash went off."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_PHOTOS 2
label Resolve
  say "Gorgeous. Last one: resolve. You're about to face a gym leader. Show me."
  choose "Strike a pose!" "A big yawn" "Chin up, fist clenched, looking straight ahead" "A nervous wave"
  if result != 1 goto Blurred
  text "Click! The flash went off, and the photographer lowered the camera with a satisfied sigh."
  say "Perfect, perfect, perfect. Tulip will love these. You've passed the test! She's waiting at the back."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_TEST_DONE
  end
label Blurred
  text "Click! The photographer looked at the picture and winced."
  say "Hmm. That's not quite the mood, darling. Come back to me and we'll take that shot again."
  end
label Done
  say "Your photos are drying on the wall. Go on, Tulip's waiting."
  end

# Tulip's battle for the Psychic Badge. She Terastallizes her last into a Psychic type. Plan 34 · decision 3 at its
# recommendation, as the leaders' before her: her team is scaled to the badges the challenger holds, its best at the cap
# of that many badges, so in the academy's order (six held) it is the original's (Data/world/paldea/trainers.json:
# paldea_tulip, paldea_tulip_b0 to _b7)
script Leader
  face tulip player
  speaker "Tulip"
  if badge psychic goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_ALFORNADA_TEST_DONE goto NotYet
  scene "Tulip"
  say "I've seen your photographs. Lovely work. I'm Tulip, Alfornada's gym leader, and I make people look their best."
  say "My Psychic types are beautiful, and beauty can be very, very strong. Shall we?"
  if badges >= 7 goto Held7
  if badges >= 6 goto Held6
  if badges >= 5 goto Held5
  if badges >= 4 goto Held4
  if badges >= 3 goto Held3
  if badges >= 2 goto Held2
  if badges >= 1 goto Held1
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b0"
  goto Beaten
label Held1
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b1"
  goto Beaten
label Held2
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b2"
  goto Beaten
label Held3
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b3"
  goto Beaten
label Held4
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b4"
  goto Beaten
label Held5
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b5"
  goto Beaten
label Held6
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip"
  goto Beaten
label Held7
  battle tulip as "paldea_tulip_b7"
label Beaten
  say "Oh, that was exquisite. You've earned this. The Psychic Badge, and a little of my admiration too."
  givebadge psychic
  text "{player} received the Psychic Badge from Tulip!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_ALFORNADA_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_bruno"
  say "Do come back when you have a new team. I'd love to photograph them."
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "Not until I've seen your photographs, darling. My photographer is by the door."
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "You're not enrolled at the academy, are you? Then I'm afraid I can't battle you. Rules, darling."
  end
label Won
  say "Back again? You still have that glow. Must be the badge."
  end
