# Lugia's cave at the bottom of the Whirl Islands (plan 20 · J11, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D40R0107).
#
# Lugia is there for whoever has the Silver Wing (FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_SILVER_WING, bell_tower_roof.txt) and is battled where it
# rests, at level 45, with no running. Caught, it is gone for good (FLAG_JOHTO_CAUGHT_LUGIA, ours); beaten, it dives away
# (HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_WHIRL_ISLAND_LUGIA) and is back with the next entry into Johto's Hall of Fame, whose scene clears
# the flag unless it was caught.


# D40R0107_000: Lugia
script Lugia
  cry "Lugia"
  speaker none
  say "The Silver Wing glows, and the great bird of the sea opens its eyes!"
  wildbattle "Lugia" 45 nofleeing
  if var RESULT == 3 goto Caught
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_WHIRL_ISLAND_LUGIA
  hide self
  say "Lugia slipped beneath the dark water and was gone..."
  end
label Caught
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_CAUGHT_LUGIA
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_WHIRL_ISLAND_LUGIA
  hide self
  end
