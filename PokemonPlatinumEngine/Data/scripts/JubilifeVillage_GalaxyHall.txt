# Galaxy Hall, the Galaxy Team's headquarters in Jubilife Village (plan 23 · Z17). The lines are our own, on Legends:
# Arceus's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hisui's first steps"). VAR_HISUI_STORY is
# prelude_beach.txt's count.
#
# The people: kamado at his desk and the medic behind her counter, always; laventon (FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_LAVENTON_HALL,
# cleared as he goes to report), cyllene and partner (FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_CYLLENE_HALL, FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_PARTNER_HALL,
# cleared as they leave the Wallflower). Legends: Arceus puts the Commander's office upstairs; this hall is one room.


# Called to the Commander: the stranger questioned, a place offered on the Survey Corps after an entry trial, the
# Pokédex from the professor and a bed for the night (ours: Legends: Arceus holds the trial first and gives the quarters
# after; here the night comes first, and the trial is plan 23 · Z18's)
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_HISUI_STORY != 5 end
  scene "The Commander"
  walk player up 5
  speaker "Commander Kamado"
  say "So. You are the one who fell out of a crack in the sky."
  say "I am Kamado, commander of the Galaxy Team. This land of Hisui is harsh, and its people fear the Pokémon that roam it."
  speaker "Prof. Laventon"
  say "Commander, this young one brought my three back without a single Poké Ball thrown in anger. A rare gift!"
  speaker "Commander Kamado"
  say "A gift is no proof. The Galaxy Team has no place for idle hands, nor for strangers who can't pull their weight."
  say "Here is my offer. Join the Survey Corps, and help the professor fill his Pokédex. But first you will pass an entry trial in the Obsidian Fieldlands."
  say "Until then you may sleep under our roof. There are quarters free just east of this hall."
  speaker "Prof. Laventon"
  say "And you'll want this, {player}. The Pokédex! Every Pokémon you meet will be written down in it."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Welcome aboard, almost! Any Pokémon you can't carry, leave at the pastures on Canala Avenue. The keeper looks after them."
  speaker "Captain Cyllene"
  say "Rest tonight. The trial will not wait for you in the morning."
  setvar VAR_HISUI_STORY 6
  objective hisuiQuarters
  endscene
  end


# The people of the hall, after their scene
script Kamado
  speaker "Commander Kamado"
  if var VAR_HISUI_STORY < 6 goto Waiting
  say "The Obsidian Fieldlands are where you will prove yourself. Do not disappoint the professor."
  end
label Waiting
  say "The professor tells me a stranger is waiting at the Wallflower. I will see them when they are brought to me."
  end

script Laventon
  speaker "Prof. Laventon"
  say "A complete Pokédex of Hisui, the first ever made! With your help, {player}, I believe we can do it."
  end

script Cyllene
  speaker "Captain Cyllene"
  say "The Survey Corps walks the wilds and writes down what it finds. It is not a life for the faint-hearted."
  end

script Partner
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "I joined the Survey Corps last spring. Once you pass your trial, we'll be out in the Fieldlands together!"
  end
