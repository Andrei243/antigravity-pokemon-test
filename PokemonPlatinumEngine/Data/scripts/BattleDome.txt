# The Battle Dome's hall (plan 14 · B8). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the beats of
# Emerald's Battle Dome: the bracket shown before each round, the opponent's card, two of the three sent in, the other
# matches of the round fought while the player waits, and the bracket again at the end.
#
# A whole tournament is this one arrival script, which loops round by round (Models/Frontier/BattleDome.cs; `bracket`).
# The tournament lives between the desk and the hall as the host's Dome, so a game closed in the middle of one finds no
# tournament here: the script does nothing, and the next one begun at the desk ends the streak (DomeRecord.InProgress).


script OnEnter
  bracket active
  if result == 0 end
  clearflag FLAG_BATTLE_DOME_SHOW_OPPONENT
  hide opponent
  place player 8 10 up
label Round
  bracket round
  speaker "Announcer"
  if result == 3 goto TheFinal
  say "Round {number} of the Battle Dome's tournament! Let's look at the bracket."
  goto Bracket
label TheFinal
  say "The final! Only two Trainers are left standing. Let's look at the bracket."
label Bracket
  bracket show
  bracket opponent
  place opponent 8 3 down
  setflag FLAG_BATTLE_DOME_SHOW_OPPONENT
  show opponent
  walk opponent down 2
  text "{foe} steps into the ring!"
  bracket pick
  bracket battle
  if result == 0 goto Lost
  call Leaves
  bracket won
  if result == 1 goto Champion
  speaker "Announcer"
  say "Victory! The other matches of the round are over too. On to the next!"
  goto Round
label Champion
  speaker "Announcer"
  say "And there it is! {player} is the champion of the Battle Dome!"
  bracket show
  bracket finish won
  bracket points
  text "{player} received {number} BP!"
  fanfare item
  goto Home
label Lost
  call Leaves
  bracket lost
  bracket finish lost
  speaker "Announcer"
  say "Defeated! {player}'s tournament ends here. A fine effort all the same!"
label Home
  bracket end
  fade out 0.6
  warp "BattleFrontierAnnex" 7 5 down
  fade in
  end


# The opponent goes off the stage again
script Leaves
  clearflag FLAG_BATTLE_DOME_SHOW_OPPONENT
  hide opponent
  return
