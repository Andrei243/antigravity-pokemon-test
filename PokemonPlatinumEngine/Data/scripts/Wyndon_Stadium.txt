# Wyndon Stadium's lobby (plan 33 · GL6): where the Champion Cup is fought. The doors behind the desk lead to the pitch
# (Wyndon_StadiumPitch.txt). Coming back out after the Semifinals, the sky over Wyndon turns red: the Darkest Day, and
# Hop's word to go to the Slumbering Weald's altar. VAR_GALAR_STORY 39 to 40 (postwick.txt's count). Our own lines.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 39 goto DarkestDay
  end
label DarkestDay
  scene "The Darkest Day"
  text "The lobby's lights flicker and die. Through the glass doors, the sky over Wyndon has turned the colour of embers."
  speaker "Hop"
  say "{player}! Did you see the sky? Everyone's saying the power's gone wild all over Galar, and nobody can find Leon!"
  say "It's just like the tapestries in the vault. The Darkest Day, and the two heroes who stopped it with a sword and a shield."
  say "The stories all end at the old altar in the Slumbering Weald. If there's anything left of the heroes, it's there. Let's go!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DARKEST_DAY
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_ALTAR
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 40
  objective galarAltar
  text "Hop dragged {player} to the station, and the last train south ran them all the way home to Postwick."
  warpto "Galar" weald_entry
  endscene
  end


script Receptionist
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 46 goto Champion
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 43 goto Finals
  say "Welcome to Wyndon Stadium and the Champion Cup! The Semifinals are on the pitch, through the doors behind me."
  end
label Finals
  say "The Finals are under way! Your opponents are waiting on the pitch. Good luck, challenger!"
  end
label Champion
  say "Galar's new Champion! It's an honour. The pitch is yours whenever you like."
  end


script CupGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 46 goto Champion
  say "Everyone left in the Cup has eight badges, so there's no type to lean on now. Bring a team that covers everything!"
  end
label Champion
  say "I've guided challengers through eight stadiums for years. You're the first I've seen go all the way."
  end
