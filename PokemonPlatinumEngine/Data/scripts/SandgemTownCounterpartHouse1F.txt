# The grandfather speaks of the professor, and of how many Pokémon there are once the National Pokédex is the
# player's (plan 02 · S15, part a). The little sister cheers the player on, and tells a Champion with the National
# Pokédex of the day's swarm (the original asks for both): common.SwarmNews (plan 06 · R13), which begins the swarms
# the first time and sets FLAG_TALKED_TO_COUNTERPART_SISTER_WITH_NATIONAL_DEX, which her big sister or brother in the
# street asks about.


script Grandfather
  if nationaldex goto National
  say "So that Rowan is back, is he? A remarkable man. He was studying Pokémon before I had a grey hair on my head." "My son and my grandchild both help him with his research these days."
  end
label National
  say "My word! Every Pokémon of Sinnoh in your Pokédex? Rowan must be beside himself. Think what it means for his research!" "But mark this, young Trainer. There are three hundred kinds of Pokémon in this world... no, four hundred..." "No, no, no! Far, far more than that!"
  end


script LittleSister
  if not nationaldex goto Cheers
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Swarm
label Cheers
  say "Who are you?" "... Oh! You're {player}? You're doing that Pokédex thing for the professor!"
  if girl goto Brother
  say "Wow! Just like my big sister! Go for it!"
  end
label Brother
  say "Wow! Just like my big brother! Go for it!"
  end
label Swarm
  say "Oh, hi, {player}! Wait till you hear what was on TV! I want you to do even better than {assistant}, so listen!"
  call SwarmNews
  end
