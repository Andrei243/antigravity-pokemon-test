# Route 10's northern half as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part n). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_R10).
#
# Zapdos comes to the Power Plant's door once the generator turns again (shown by FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED: the
# plant's story, route_10_power_plant_repaired.txt) and waits until it has been fought (FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_ZAPDOS,
# ours), as Articuno does in the Seafoam Islands: beaten or caught it is gone for good, run from it flies off
# (HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_10_ZAPDOS) and is back on the next visit.
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat for a bird of lightning (style guide, "Legendary scenes"), as FireRed's Zapdos in its
# plant: the bars, the camera to it, two bursts of lightning a moment apart with the picture shaken by each, the cry and
# a line; the scene ends before the battle, so the hold never skips it.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_ZAPDOS clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_10_ZAPDOS
  end

# R10_003: Zapdos
script Zapdos
  speaker none
  scene "Zapdos at the Power Plant's door"
  music stop
  face player self
  letterbox on 0.4
  # Met from below, it is behind the player's own head from the field's camera: a step back, facing it, shows it
  if facing up walk player back down 1
  camera pan 1419 184 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect burst 1419 184
  camera shake 0.4
  # A burst lit again on its own tile takes the place of the one still playing there: the second strike waits for
  # the first's 0.6 s to end
  wait 0.6
  effect burst 1419 184
  camera shake 0.4
  wait 0.6
  cry "Zapdos"
  emote player exclaim
  text "Sparks crackled through the air as Zapdos spread its jagged wings!"
  if facing up walk player up 1
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Zapdos" 50
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_ZAPDOS
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_10_ZAPDOS
  hide self
  end
label Fled
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_10_ZAPDOS
  hide self
  text "Zapdos flew off over the river!"
  end
