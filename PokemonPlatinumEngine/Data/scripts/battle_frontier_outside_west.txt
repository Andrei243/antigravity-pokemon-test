# The Battle Frontier island's west half (plan 21 · H12, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/BattleFrontier_OutsideWest/scripts.inc).
#
# The ferry's attendant on the quay sails the player back to Lilycove City's harbour, where the ferry from the island
# moors (lilycove_city_harbor.txt, FerryAttendant). Emerald's ferry also runs to Slateport; this one runs to Lilycove
# alone, as decision 2 of plan 21 has every voyage out of Lilycove.


script FerryAttendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Battle Frontier! The ferry back to the mainland leaves whenever you're ready."
  ask "Shall I take you back to Lilycove City?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Very good. Please come aboard."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed back to Lilycove City..."
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Stay
  say "Enjoy the Frontier! I'll be here when you want to sail home."
  end


# The halls' doors (plan 21 · H12, part m; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The Battle Frontier island's halls"). This game
# has one Battle Frontier, Sinnoh's, with one set of records, and its facilities and the annex's venues are Sinnoh's
# rooms; a warp never crosses regions, so each hall's door stays a `locked` warp, and its attendant, read at the door,
# crosses a holder of the Frontier Pass to the hall's lobby there (`travel to`, GameEngine.CrossTo) as Scott does. The
# hall is kept (VAR_HOENN_FRONTIER_HALL) so that the Hoenn guide on Sinnoh's grounds sails back to this door.

script DomeDoor
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_FRONTIER_PASS goto NoPass
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Battle Dome! Its tournaments are held in the annex of the Battle Frontier in Sinnoh."
  ask "Shall we take you there? Your team comes along, and your records stay as they are."
  if no goto Later
  setvar VAR_HOENN_FRONTIER_HALL 2
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} crossed with the attendant to the Battle Frontier in Sinnoh..."
  travel to "BattleFrontierAnnex" 7 9 up
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you're ready. I'll be at this door."
  end
label NoPass
  text "The door is shut. A sign says that only holders of a Frontier Pass may enter."
  end

# Omega Ruby's and Alpha Sapphire's Audinite (plan 40 · GE11, decision 11: those games give it on their post-game's island of
# battles, the Battle Resort, which the Battle Frontier stands for here): the gentleman by the Dome's road gives it, once;
# afterwards he says his line (the overlay's), sayown
script Audinite
  if flag FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_AUDINITE goto Own
  face self player
  say "A Trainer who made it to this island deserves a keepsake. My Audino and I won this stone in a tournament long ago."
  give "Audinite"
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_AUDINITE
  say "An Audino holding the Audinite can Mega Evolve, and its healing grows kinder still. Treasure it, young one."
  end
label Own
  sayown
  end
