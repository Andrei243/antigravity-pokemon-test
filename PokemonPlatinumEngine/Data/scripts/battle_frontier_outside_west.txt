# The Battle Frontier island's west half (plan 21 · H12, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/BattleFrontier_OutsideWest/scripts.inc).
#
# The ferry's attendant on the quay sails the player back to Lilycove City's harbour, where the ferry from the island
# moors (lilycove_city_harbor.txt, FerryAttendant). Emerald's ferry also runs to Slateport; this one runs to Lilycove
# alone, as decision 2 of plan 21 has every voyage out of Lilycove.


script FerryAttendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Battle Frontier! The ferry back to the mainland leaves whenever you're ready."
  ask "Shall I take you back to Lilycove City?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Very good. Please come aboard."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed back to Lilycove City..."
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Stay
  say "Enjoy the Frontier! I'll be here when you want to sail home."
  end
