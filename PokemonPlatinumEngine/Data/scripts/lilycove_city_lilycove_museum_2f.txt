# The Lilycove Museum's gallery (plan 21 · H11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LilycoveCity_LilycoveMuseum_2F/scripts.inc): five frames, one for each quality of the
# Contest Hall, empty until the painter of the Contest Lobby hangs there the winner of a Master Rank contest of 800
# points or more, and the curator who gives a Glass Ornament once all five hang. Emerald draws a painting by changing
# the wall's two metatiles; ours is looked at, in a screen of its own (`appeals view`).


# A frame, read from before it: the painting (RESULT 1) or the card beneath an empty one
script CoolPainting
  appeals view cool
  if result == 1 end
  text "An empty frame. A card beneath it reads: Coolness."
  end

script BeautyPainting

  if result == 1 end

# The curator of the gallery (Emerald's LilycoveCity_LilycoveMuseum_2F_EventScript_Curator): thanks for each painting,
# and for all five a Glass Ornament for the player's secret base, once
script Curator
  face self player
  speaker "Curator"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_GLASS_ORNAMENT goto Honoured
  appeals paintings
  if result == 5 goto All
  if result == 0 goto None
  say "Thank you! A new painting has been added to the gallery. The visitors do love to see the Pokémon that won at the Contest Hall."
  say "There is room for more. Each of the five frames is waiting for a winner of its own."
  end
label None
  say "I wish to fill these five frames with the paintings of Pokémon that win Master Rank contests at the Contest Hall."
  say "The painter in the Contest Lobby makes one when a Pokémon wins with a really outstanding performance. If you have such a Pokémon, do enter it!"
  end
label All
  say "Oh! It's you! You have filled every frame in my gallery with a winner."
  say "Since your Pokémon were hung here, more guests than ever come to see them. The museum owes you a great deal."
  say "Please, take this as a token of my gratitude."
  openbase give "Glass Ornament"
  if result == 0 goto NoRoom
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_GLASS_ORNAMENT
  fanfare item
  text "{player} received the {thing}!"
  say "It's a decoration for your secret base. May it shine there as your Pokémon shine on stage!"
  end
label NoRoom
  say "Oh, your PC has no room for it. Make some, and come back. I will keep it for you."
  end
label Honoured
  say "It's an honour to have you visit again. Your Pokémon have made this gallery what it is."
  end
