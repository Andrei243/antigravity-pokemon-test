# Saffron City's Fighting Dojo (Johto's leftovers). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats.
#
# The Gym Leaders who rang the player on their day (Models.PhoneRematch, phone.txt) wait here until fought, each by
# the flag that keeps their rematch waiting (FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_<ID>, saffron_fighting_dojo.json's shownBy). Each
# battles as their second team (Johto's trainers.json, the rows of rematches.json) and, beaten, clears the flag and
# is gone, until their next call asks again. A Leader is battled as a team (`battle self as`), never as a trainer of
# the map.


script Falkner
  face t11r0101_gsleader1 player
  speaker "Falkner"
  say "My birds have trained in every wind. Let's see if they can catch you now!"
  battle self as "johto_leader_falkner_falkner_2"
  say "Grounded again... but my birds will soar higher next time."
  clearflag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_FALKNER
  end
