# Snowpoint City (plan 02 · S11, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_snowpoint_city.s).
#
# The town's part in the story is its Gym (SnowpointGym.txt, plan 01 · M9: Candice and the Icicle Badge, which lets
# Rock Climb take the player up Acuity Lakefront's rock face) and Maylene, warming up in its Pokémon Center
# (SnowpointPokemonCenter.txt). Snowpoint Temple stands shut at the top of town: its guard stands before the east
# half of its door, and whoever steps up to the west half (the original's trigger 0, one tile at 366, 198, while
# VAR_SNOWPOINT_CITY_STATE is 0) is turned back.
#
# After the Hall of Fame (plan 02 · S15, part a) the sailor on the quay takes a Champion to the Battle Zone: the
# original asks FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED and nothing else, and lands the player on the Fight Area's pier at 623, 434, facing
# east (TakeShipFromSnowpoint). The first time, Cynthia comes down to the quay to see the player off
# (FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_CITY_CYNTHIA, set from the start by common.ChapterEight; FLAG_SAILED_TO_BATTLE_ZONE once she
# has). Candice, who comes up the street to let a Champion with the National Pokédex into the temple
# (FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_CITY_CANDICE), comes with the temple: plan 02 · S15, part b.


# The temple's guard turns the player back (the original's coordinate event, script 3).
script TempleGuard
  face temple_guard left
  speaker "Temple Guard"
  say "Only those the temple has chosen may set foot in Snowpoint Temple."
  say "I'm sorry, but I must ask you to leave."
  face temple_guard down
  walk player down 1
  end


# The sailor of the ship to the Battle Zone (the original's script 12, SnowpointCity_SailorSSSpiral)
script Ship
  face self player
  if not flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto NotYet
  ask "Ahoy, Champion! This ship is bound for the Battle Zone. Shall we cast off?"
  if no goto Stay
  if not flag FLAG_SAILED_TO_BATTLE_ZONE call CynthiaSeesOff
  speaker self
  say "All aboard, then! Next stop, the Fight Area!"
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 623 434 right
  end
label NotYet
  sayown
  end
label Stay
  say "Suit yourself. But mind: there's no surfing across that sea. The only way to the Battle Zone is by this ship."
  end


# SnowpointCity_Cynthia: she comes down from the town to the head of the gangway, behind the player (a tile short of
# the original's place, which is the tile at the player's back, where the player's own head would hide her)
script CynthiaSeesOff
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_CITY_CYNTHIA
  place cynthia 366 241 left
  show cynthia
  walk cynthia left 4 down 2 left 6 down 1
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "{player}! I'm so glad I waited. I had a feeling you would come this way."
  emote player exclaim
  face player up
  walk cynthia right 1
  wait 0.25
  say "Do you know, I think it is a fine thing that our world keeps changing."
  say "New places, new Pokémon, new people to meet. However far you go, there is always more of it."
  walk cynthia left 1
  face cynthia down
  say "So go and see the far-off places, {player}. Meet everything they have to show you."
  say "And when you come back, tell me all about it. Safe travels, Champion."
  walk cynthia up 1 right 3 up 2 right 7
  hide cynthia
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_CITY_CYNTHIA
  face player down
  setflag FLAG_SAILED_TO_BATTLE_ZONE
  return
