# Route 15, Ula'ula Island (plan 23, the wave after Ultra Space). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beat as it is
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Poni's last places..."): Grimsley, a gambler from far away, waits on the
# beach and registers Sharpedo in the Ride Pager (RidePager.SharpedoFlag), once. Sharpedo is called under a surfer, goes at
# a bicycle's top speed and rams the rock that shuts the islet off this beach (common.SeaRock).


script Grimsley
  face grimsley player
  speaker "Grimsley"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_SHARPEDO goto Done
  say "Life is a gamble, and the sea is the biggest gamble of all. I lost my way out here, and won a fine Sharpedo instead."
  say "It is wasted on a man who only sits on the sand. You take it. Call it with your Ride Pager whenever you are on the water."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_SHARPEDO
  text "Sharpedo was registered in the Ride Pager!"
  say "It can ram rocks a Lapras would never dare. There is one off this very beach, if you fancy your luck."
  end
label Done
  say "The tide comes in, the tide goes out. Win or lose, a gambler waits. And so do I."
  end
