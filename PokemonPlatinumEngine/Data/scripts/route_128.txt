# Route 128 (plan 21 · H8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/Route128/scripts.inc).
#
# The Seafloor Cavern's last scene (seafloor_cavern_room_9.txt) sends the player up onto the islet in the middle of
# the route with Archie and Maxie beside them and VAR_ROUTE128_STATE at 1, and the route's own arrival plays the rest:
# Archie looks round at the storm he has made, Maxie tells him what it means and the two leave to stop it; Steven flies
# in, sees the downpour spreading from over Sootopolis and flies on there (VAR_ROUTE128_STATE 2). The triathletes Isaiah
# and Katelyn swap numbers for the PokéNav once beaten.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_TEN call HoennChapterTen
  if var VAR_ROUTE128_STATE != 1 end
  scene "Kyogre's storm over Route 128"
  wait 0.6
  face route128_archie left
  wait 0.4
  face route128_archie right
  wait 0.4
  face route128_archie down
  speaker "Archie"
  say "What... what has happened? What is this wretched sight?"
  say "Did I... Have I made some terrible mistake?"
  walk route128_archie back up 1
  say "I only... I only wanted..."
  walk route128_maxie right 1
  face route128_archie up
  speaker "Maxie"
  say "Do you see it now, Archie? Do you see what your dream has turned into?"
  say "We have no time. Something must be done before this is past anyone's power to stop!"
  face route128_maxie down
  face player up
  say "{player}, say nothing. I know I have no right to judge Archie."
  say "And I doubt that people like us can do anything against power like this."
  say "But we can't stand here and watch. Putting an end to it is our task, Archie's and mine."
  face route128_maxie left
  face player left
  wait 0.4
  say "Those ancient Pokémon... Their power is beyond anything we imagined. They have thrown all of nature off its balance..."
  # (the islet runs west to the reef's edge: Archie goes first, Maxie after him)
  walk route128_archie left 3
  walk route128_maxie left 3
  hide route128_maxie
  hide route128_archie
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_128_MAXIE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_128_ARCHIE
  wait 0.4
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_128_STEVEN
  show route128_steven
  cry "Skarmory"
  wait 0.6
  face player right
  speaker "Steven"
  say "{player}! What is going on here?"
  face player up
  say "This is terrible... First a heat wave, and now this downpour."
  say "If it doesn't stop, it will drown all of Hoenn. No, the whole world."
  say "These storm clouds are spreading from over Sootopolis City. What on earth is happening there?"
  say "There's no sense standing here wondering. Sootopolis may have the answer."
  face route128_steven down
  face player right
  say "{player}... I don't know what you mean to do, but don't do anything reckless."
  say "Right. I'm going to Sootopolis."
  cry "Skarmory"
  hide route128_steven
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_128_STEVEN
  setvar VAR_ROUTE128_STATE 2
  endscene
  end


# Isaiah, the triathlete who laps the reef
script Isaiah
  if not defeated self goto Fight
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_HOENN_ISAIAH goto Known
label Ask
  speaker "Isaiah"
  say "You swim like you battle: fast and hard. Let's keep pushing each other!"
  ask "Register Isaiah in the PokéNav's Match Call?"
  if no goto Refused
  addcontact hoenn_isaiah
  say "Great! I'll call you from the reef."
  end
label Refused
  say "Fair enough. Another hundred laps for me, then."
  end
label Known
  trainerline after
  end
label Fight
  trainerline before
  battle self
  goto Ask


# Katelyn, the triathlete who races the sea
script Katelyn
  if not defeated self goto Fight
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_HOENN_KATELYN goto Known
label Ask
  speaker "Katelyn"
  say "You beat me fair and square. I want a rematch once I've trained some more!"
  ask "Register Katelyn in the PokéNav's Match Call?"
  if no goto Refused
  addcontact hoenn_katelyn
  say "I'll call you the moment I'm faster!"
  end
label Refused
  say "Then I'll just have to find you out here again."
  end
label Known
  trainerline after
  end
label Fight
  trainerline before
  battle self
  goto Ask
