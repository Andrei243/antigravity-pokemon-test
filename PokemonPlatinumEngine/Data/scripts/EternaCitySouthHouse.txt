# The south house of Eterna City (plan 01 · M11, part a, built it; its guest plan 02 · S15, part a), on the original's
# plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_eterna_city_south_house.s).
#
# Professor Oak stays here once he has met the player in Pal Park's lobby (FLAG_HIDE_ETERNA_CITY_SOUTH_HOUSE_PROF_OAK,
# set from the start by common.ChapterThree and cleared there). The first time he is spoken to he gives the Upgrade
# an acquaintance sent him (FLAG_RECEIVED_ETERNA_CITY_SOUTH_HOUSE_UPGRADE) and tells of three bird Pokémon of his own
# region sighted in Sinnoh: Moltres, Zapdos and Articuno are set roaming (roamer start, plan 06 · R13;
# FLAG_ACTIVATED_ROAMING_LEGENDARY_BIRDS). From then on he sets loose again whichever the Hall of Fame has brought
# back after it was knocked out (VAR_ROAMING_<BIRD>_STATE 3 made 0), and has a word for whoever has caught all three
# (FLAG_CAUGHT_ROAMING_LEGENDARY_BIRDS).
#
# Left out: his rating of the National Pokédex (PokedexRatings_CheckOak), whose steps are counted against the 493
# Pokémon of the original's and wait for plan 03 to say what this game's National Pokédex asks for.


script Oak
  face self player
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ETERNA_CITY_SOUTH_HOUSE_UPGRADE goto Letter
label Birds
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_ROAMING_LEGENDARY_BIRDS goto Pokedex
  # EternaCitySouthHouse_CheckCaughtAllRoamingLegendaryBirds: 1 is a roamer caught (Roamers.Captured)
  if var VAR_ROAMING_MOLTRES_STATE != 1 goto NotAll
  if var VAR_ROAMING_ZAPDOS_STATE != 1 goto NotAll
  if var VAR_ROAMING_ARTICUNO_STATE != 1 goto NotAll
  setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_ROAMING_LEGENDARY_BIRDS
  say "What? You have caught Moltres, Zapdos and Articuno? All three?!"
  say "In all my years in Kanto I never managed so much as one. {player}, you are a marvel. May I look at them some time?"
  end
label NotAll
  if flag FLAG_ACTIVATED_ROAMING_LEGENDARY_BIRDS goto Again
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ETERNA_CITY_SOUTH_HOUSE_UPGRADE goto Pokedex
  say "{player}! Good of you to call. And how is that Pokédex of yours coming along?"
  emote self exclaim
  say "Oh, before I forget! An acquaintance sent me something, and I can think of nobody better to have it."
  give "Upgrade"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ETERNA_CITY_SOUTH_HOUSE_UPGRADE
  say "There is a Pokémon that evolves when it is traded holding that Upgrade. I shall leave you to find out which."
  # EternaCitySouthHouse_ActivateRoamingLegendaryBirds
  roamer start "Moltres"
  roamer start "Zapdos"
  roamer start "Articuno"
  setflag FLAG_ACTIVATED_ROAMING_LEGENDARY_BIRDS
  say "Now, here is something that will interest you. Three bird Pokémon of legend from my own Kanto have been sighted here in Sinnoh: Moltres, Zapdos and Articuno!"
  say "They never stay anywhere for long. Keep your eyes open on the routes, {player}, and your Pokédex ready."
  end
label Again
  # EternaCitySouthHouse_TryReactivateRoamingLegendaryBirds: 3 is one the Hall of Fame has brought back (Roamers.Reset)
  if var VAR_ROAMING_MOLTRES_STATE == 3 call MoltresAgain
  if var VAR_ROAMING_ZAPDOS_STATE == 3 call ZapdosAgain
  if var VAR_ROAMING_ARTICUNO_STATE == 3 call ArticunoAgain
  say "The legendary birds are rare in the extreme, {player}. To come across even one of them is a stroke of luck."
  say "Should one get away from you, don't lose heart. They are still out there, somewhere in Sinnoh."
  end
label Pokedex
  say "A Pokédex is never truly finished, {player}. That is the pleasure of it. Go on meeting Pokémon, and come and tell me of them."
  end
# Plan 29 · LS11a, ours in place of the original's event: once he has met the player here, Oak writes the Oak's
# Letter that asks them to meet him at the white rock on Route 224 (FLAG_RECEIVED_OAKS_LETTER). The original's
# Route224_OnTransition shows him and Marley there for whoever carries the letter; here giving it does that
# (VAR_SHAYMIN_EVENT_STATE 1, and VAR_ROUTE_224_STATE 1 for Marley's walk up to the rock: route_224.txt)
label Letter
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_OAKS_LETTER goto Birds
  if not nationaldex goto Birds
  say "Ah, {player}! There is something I've been meaning to ask of you."
  say "At the very end of Route 224 stands a great white rock. They say it is where people once gave thanks."
  say "I should like to see it for myself. Here: a letter with the way written down. Meet me there, won't you?"
  give "Oak's Letter"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_OAKS_LETTER
  setvar VAR_SHAYMIN_EVENT_STATE 1
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_224_STATE 1
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_224_PROF_OAK
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_224_MARLEY
  say "Route 224 lies beyond Victory Road. I'll set off at once. Don't keep an old man waiting too long!"
  end

script MoltresAgain
  setvar VAR_ROAMING_MOLTRES_STATE 0
  roamer start "Moltres"
  return

script ZapdosAgain
  setvar VAR_ROAMING_ZAPDOS_STATE 0
  roamer start "Zapdos"
  return

script ArticunoAgain
  setvar VAR_ROAMING_ARTICUNO_STATE 0
  roamer start "Articuno"
  return
