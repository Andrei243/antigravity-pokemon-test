# Hau'oli City's Trainers' School (plan 23, Alola's last door on Melemele: Z5's "Left" named it). Sun and Moon keep a
# school in the city where four pupils and their teacher battle a newcomer; ours is a classroom of our own with our own
# people and lines (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hau'oli City's Trainers' School"). Each pupil battles once (their
# FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_<NAME>); once all four are beaten the teacher battles too and gives three Quick Balls
# (FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_TEACHER).


script Keahi
  face self player
  speaker "Keahi"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_KEAHI goto After
  say "Today's lesson is type matchups. I learn better by battling, so you're my homework!"
  battle self as "alola_youngster_keahi"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_KEAHI
  speaker "Keahi"
  say "I got the matchups right and still lost. Maybe the lesson is about speed too."
  end
label After
  say "Some Pokémon only come out by day and others only by night. They take turns with the island!"
  end


script Pualani
  face self player
  speaker "Pualani"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_PUALANI goto After
  say "My partner can tell how strong you are by your aura. It's buzzing like mad. Let's see if it's right!"
  battle self as "alola_lass_pualani"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_PUALANI
  speaker "Pualani"
  say "It was right. It's always right. I should have listened to it."
  end
label After
  say "A Pokémon's aura shows how it feels. Bug Pokémon gather wherever people are smiling."
  end


script Aukai
  face self player
  speaker "Aukai"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_AUKAI goto After
  say "I sit by the window so I can watch the seabirds. Today I'll watch you lose instead!"
  battle self as "alola_school_kid_aukai"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_AUKAI
  speaker "Aukai"
  say "Hmm. The seabirds saw that too, didn't they."
  end
label After
  say "Bug Pokémon like to chew through the berry fields' fences on Route 2. Farmers don't like them much."
  end


script Kanoe
  face self player
  speaker "Kanoe"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_KANOE goto After
  say "I've read every book on that shelf twice. Now I want to test what I've learned on a real Trainer!"
  battle self as "alola_school_kid_kanoe"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_KANOE
  speaker "Kanoe"
  say "Books don't tell you how fast a real battle moves. I'll write that down."
  end
label After
  say "Some of Alola's Pokémon look nothing like their cousins elsewhere. The islands changed them, the book says."
  end


script Teacher
  face self player
  speaker "Iwalani"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_TEACHER goto After
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_KEAHI goto Class
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_PUALANI goto Class
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_AUKAI goto Class
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_KANOE goto Class
  say "You've battled every one of my pupils. That's a better lesson than any I could write on the board."
  say "Then let the teacher sit the test too. Show me what the island challenge looks like up close!"
  battle self as "alola_teacher_iwalani"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SCHOOL_TEACHER
  speaker "Iwalani"
  say "Top marks. Take these for the road: a Quick Ball works best thrown the moment a battle starts."
  give "Quick Ball" 3
  end
label Class
  say "Welcome to the Trainers' School! My pupils are itching to battle someone new."
  say "Battle all four of them, and then come and see me for the final exam."
  end
label After
  say "Come back whenever you like. My pupils still talk about the day you visited."
  end
