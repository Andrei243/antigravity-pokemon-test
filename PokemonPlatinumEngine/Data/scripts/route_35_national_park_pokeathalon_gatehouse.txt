# The gate house between Route 35, the National Park and the Pokéathlon Dome (plan 15 · E5): its officer keeps the
# Bug-Catching Contest's counter, and coming in from the park while a contest is under way asks whether the player is
# done.

script Officer
  setvar VAR_BUG_CONTEST_GATE 0
  call BugContestDesk
  end

script OnEnter
  bugcontest on
  if result == 0 end
  setvar VAR_BUG_CONTEST_GATE 0
  call BugContestReturn
  end
