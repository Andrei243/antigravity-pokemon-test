# The Ruins of Alph's southeast chamber (plan 20 · J5). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beat: the stone panel in the floor, its picture of an Aerodactyl broken into sixteen pieces, to be put back
# together. What solving it opens (the floor giving way to the rooms below, the Unown) is a later chapter's.


# The panel (HeartGold's background event at 6, 6)
script Panel
  if flag FLAG_ALPH_AERODACTYL_PANEL_SOLVED goto Whole
  text "A stone panel is set in the floor. It shows an Aerodactyl, broken into sixteen pieces that seem to slide."
  ask "Try to put the picture back together?"
  if no goto Left
  puzzle aerodactyl
  if result == 0 goto Left
  setflag FLAG_ALPH_AERODACTYL_PANEL_SOLVED
  sound "boulder"
  text "The last piece slid home with a deep click. Somewhere under the Ruins, something rumbled."
  end
label Left
  text "The pieces lie where they were."
  end
label Whole
  text "The panel shows an Aerodactyl, whole again. The stone around it is warm to the touch."
  end
