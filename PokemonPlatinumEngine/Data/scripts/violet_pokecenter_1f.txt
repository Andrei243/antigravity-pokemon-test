# Violet City's Pokémon Center (plan 20 · J4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T22PC0101).
#
# Once the Zephyr Badge is won, Elm's aide waits here with the Mystery Egg: it is an Egg after all, and Elm wants the
# player to raise it. In HeartGold the Egg hatches into Togepi; here it is Togepi's Egg from the start (`giveegg`).


script Aide
  face self player
  speaker "Aide"
  say "{player}! Over here. The professor sent me with the Egg from Mr. Pokémon."
  say "He's sure it really is a Pokémon Egg. And he thinks it hatches faster in the company of other Pokémon."
  say "He'd like you to take it with you on your journey. Will you look after it?"
  if party >= 6 goto Full
  giveegg "Togepi"
  text "{player} received the Egg!"
  say "Keep it on your team and walk with it. The professor would love to hear what hatches!"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TOGEPI_EGG
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VIOLET_POKECENTER_AIDE
  hide self
  end
label Full
  say "Oh, but your team is full! Make room for it, and come back to me."
  end
