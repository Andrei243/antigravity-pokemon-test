# Driftveil Market (plan 22 · U6, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# Three of the market hall's people sell, each from their own stall (marts.json's driftveil_market_herbs, _goods and
# _sweets, our own stock where Black 2's market changes its wares with the ships that come in).


script Herbs
  face self player
  speaker "Seller"
  say "Welcome to the market! Herbs, bitter and good for you, the way our grandmothers brewed them."
  shop "driftveil_market_herbs"
  say "Bitter, but they work wonders. Come again!"
  end


script Goods
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  say "Ahoy! I've brought these across the sea, and I'll let them go for a fair price."
  shop "driftveil_market_goods"
  say "Fair winds to you, Trainer!"
  end


script Sweets
  face self player
  speaker "Nursery Aide"
  say "Sweets for the market's children, and for any Pokémon with a taste for them!"
  shop "driftveil_market_sweets"
  say "Don't let them eat it all at once!"
  end
