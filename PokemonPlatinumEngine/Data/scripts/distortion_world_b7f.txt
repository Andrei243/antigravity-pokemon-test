# The Distortion World, B7F (plan 02 · S12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_distortion_world_b7f.s). distortion_world_1f.txt says what VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS
# counts.
#
# The first time the slab brings the player down (progress 7) Cynthia is at its foot, says Giratina is close, and
# goes on up the path to where Cyrus waits (progress 8). The row before the two (the original's event) starts their
# argument: he comes down to her, she steps aside, and he says what he means by justice (progress 9). Spoken to, he
# battles (galactic_boss_cyrus_distortion_world); a battle lost leaves him standing there. Beaten (progress 10), he
# walks out past the player, Cynthia heals the team, and the two go up to the last stretch before Giratina's room,
# which lies north of where she stops.
#
# Slabs do not move yet (plan 01 · M8), so Cynthia does not ride down with the player: she is met at the foot.


# Where the two stand, by how far the story has come.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS == 8 goto Waiting
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS == 7 goto First
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS == 9 goto Talking
  hide cyrus
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS < 7 hide cynthia
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS >= 10 place cynthia 15 37 up
  end
label Waiting
  place cyrus 11 38 down
  place cynthia 11 42 up
  end
label Talking
  place cynthia 10 42 right
  place cyrus 11 42 left
  end
label First
  # DistortionWorldB7F_OnFrame_FirstEntry
  place cyrus 11 38 down
  place cynthia 10 52 up
  wait 0.8
  face player up
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "This is the bottom of it. Giratina is up ahead. And I don't think it is alone."
  walk cynthia right 1
  walk cynthia up 3 fast
  walk cynthia up 7
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 8
  end


# The original's DistortionWorldB7F_CoordEvent_CynthiaCyrus, from each of its three tiles.
script CynthiaAndCyrus
  scene "The argument"
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 9
  place cyrus 11 38 down
  place cynthia 11 42 up
  face player up
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Cyrus. So you were here ahead of us."
  move cynthia left 1
  walk cyrus down 4
  face cyrus left
  face cynthia right
  say "Why? Why unmake a world to change it? If it is a world without spirit you want, you could have gone away by yourself and felt nothing, and troubled no one."
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "And leave it as it is? Spirit is the flaw. It makes people weak and cruel by turns, and it is in all of them."
  say "I will not hide from an imperfect world. I will end it and make it right. That is not cruelty. That is my justice."
  endscene
  end


# Cyrus (the original's DistortionWorldB7F_Cyrus), spoken to from the south or the east.
script Cyrus
  scene "Cyrus's last battle"
  face cyrus player
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "You again, at the end of everything. Very well. I will not lose to you a third time. The shadow Pokémon is mine to bring down, and you are in my way!"
  battle cyrus as "galactic_boss_cyrus_distortion_world"
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 10
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "...Beaten. Here, of all places."
  say "And now you will go to that Pokémon and calm it, and this crooked world and the other will both go on as they are. You would undo everything I have done."
  if var PLAYER_X != 11 goto Aside
  walk player right 1
  face player left
label Aside
  face cynthia down
  walk self down 7
  face player down
  walk self down 3 fast
  hide cyrus
  # Cynthia comes over to the player
  walk cynthia right 1
  face cynthia player
  face player cynthia
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Don't believe a word of it. A world with nothing in it to hurt would have nothing in it to love, either."
  say "Hold still, {player}. Your Pokémon have earned this."
  fanfare heal
  heal
  speaker none
  say "Cynthia tended {player}'s team. Every Pokémon is back at full health!"
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Now. Let's go and meet Giratina."
  move cynthia up 6 right 4 down 1
  wait 0.9
  if var PLAYER_Y == 43 goto FromSouth
  walk player left 1 up 6 right 4
  goto There
label FromSouth
  walk player left 1 up 7 right 4
label There
  waitmoves
  face cynthia up
  face player down
  say "It is just ahead of you, and it is furious: at Cyrus, at what was done to time and space, perhaps at all of us."
  say "It has to be shown that not everyone means it harm. Catch it or beat it, whichever you can. Either will quiet it. I'll be right behind you."
  endscene
  end


# Cynthia (the original's DistortionWorldB7F_Cynthia).
script Cynthia
  face cynthia player
  speaker "Cynthia"
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS >= 10 goto After
  say "A world put right by emptying it? That is no justice. It is only a man who was hurt, and never learned what to do with it."
  end
label After
  say "You and your Pokémon are bound to each other. I watched it just now. Take that with you to Giratina. It is the one thing Cyrus never had."
  end
