# The entrance hall's floor has two sand pits (the original's triggers 0 and 1, Black 2's MapChangeQuicksand): whoever
# steps into one sinks through to the floor below, the west pit to 10, 10 of RelicCastle_B1F and the east to 15, 11.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script SandPitWest
  call Sinking
  warp "RelicCastle_B1F" 10 10 down
  fade in 0.5
  end


script SandPitEast
  call Sinking
  warp "RelicCastle_B1F" 15 11 down
  fade in 0.5
  end


script Sinking
  sound "use_sand_tomb"
  speaker none
  text "The sand gives way underfoot...!"
  fade out 0.5
  return
