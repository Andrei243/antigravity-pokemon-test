# The Pastoria Gym (plan 01 · M9 1b), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_pastoria_city_gym.s).
#
# A pool whose water stands at one of three heights, and floats that ride on it: stepping on a button moves the water
# (waterbutton, the original's PressPastoriaGymButton): blue raises it to the decks, green brings it to the walkways
# half way down, orange lets it out. Each colour's buttons are the room's step triggers, which run while that colour's
# own variable is 0, so the colour pressed last does nothing until another one is. The green is pressed as the
# player comes in (OnEnter, and the puzzle's own Arrive). Crasher Wake gives the Fen Badge and TM55, counts his
# trainers as beaten, and sets what Pastoria City reads next (VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE, the grunt by the Croagunk
# statue gone and its scene blocked) for plan 02 · S8.


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x01 0
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 1
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x03 0
  end


# The buttons: blue raises the water to the decks
script BlueButton
  waterbutton blue
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x01 1
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 0
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x03 0
  end

# Green brings it to the walkways in the pool
script GreenButton
  waterbutton green
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x01 0
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 1
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x03 0
  end

# Orange lets it all out
script OrangeButton
  waterbutton orange
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x01 0
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 0
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x03 1
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge fen goto Won
  say "Hey there, champ in the making! Welcome to the Pastoria Gym!"
  say "The Leader, Wake... sorry, CRASHER Wake, raises Water types. Grass and Electric moves will make a real splash."
  say "Fire or Ground against all that water? Bold! But that's your call."
  say "Step on a button and the water moves: blue fills the pool, green brings it halfway, orange lets it out. Ride the floats!"
  end
label Won
  say "{player}! You beat Wake... I mean Crasher Wake! Didn't he look like he was having the time of his life?"
  say "Winning's great, but don't forget to have fun. That's what he'd tell you too."
  end


script GymStatue
  speaker none
  if badge fen goto Won
  say "PASTORIA CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Crasher Wake. Certified Trainers: {rival}"
  end
label Won
  say "PASTORIA CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Crasher Wake. Certified Trainers: {rival}, {player}"
  end


script Wake
  speaker "Crasher Wake"
  if badge fen goto Won
  say "Hah! A challenger! Feast your eyes on the pride of the Pastoria Gym: me, Crasher Wake!"
  say "My Pokémon were raised in roaring white water. They'll soak up whatever you throw and drag you under!"
  say "Let's make some waves!"
  battle self as "leader_wake"
  say "Wahahah! Swept off my feet by the undertow! What a battle! You've earned this, no question."
  givebadge fen
  text "{player} received the Fen Badge from Crasher Wake!"
  defeat "fisherman_erick"
  defeat "sailor_damian"
  defeat "fisherman_walter"
  defeat "sailor_samson"
  defeat "tuber_jacky"
  defeat "tuber_caitlyn"
  setvar VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 3
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PASTORIA_CITY_GRUNT_M
  setflag FLAG_BLOCK_PASTORIA_CITY_CROAGUNK_EVENT
  say "With the Fen Badge, your Pokémon can use Surf outside of battle. Go ride the waves of Sinnoh!"
  say "And here's something extra, for a team that hits as hard as yours."
label Tm
  give "TM55"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_WAKE_TM55
  say "TM55 is Brine. It hits twice as hard when the target has less than half its HP left. Finish them with a splash!"
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_WAKE_TM55 goto Tm
  say "Win or lose, the best battles are the ones you finish grinning. That's how I fight. How about you?"
  end
