# The S.S. Aqua's deck (plan 20 · J15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_P01R0301).
#
# The crewman at the head of the gangway makes the crossing: from Olivine to Vermilion City, or back. Where the ship
# lies is FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_AT_VERMILION (a flag of ours), which the gangway's two ways go by (overlays/ss_aqua_1f.json).
# HeartGold's voyage is a night aboard, the lost granddaughter found and a sleep in the cabin; here the crossing is
# told over the dark, as the region's own crossings are (the rulings' "HeartGold's Kanto, part b").


script Crewman
  face p01r0301_seaman_2_2 player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_AT_VERMILION goto ToOlivine
  ask "Welcome aboard the S.S. Aqua! We're bound for Vermilion City in Kanto. Shall we cast off?"
  if no goto Wait
  say "Anchors aweigh! Make yourself at home. We'll be there before you know it."
  fade out 0.8
  text "The S.S. Aqua sped east across the sea, skimming the waves..."
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_AT_VERMILION
  fade in 0.8
  say "We've docked at Vermilion City, Kanto! Mind your step on the gangway."
  end
label ToOlivine
  ask "Welcome aboard the S.S. Aqua! We're bound for Olivine City in Johto. Shall we cast off?"
  if no goto Wait
  say "Anchors aweigh! Make yourself at home. We'll be there before you know it."
  fade out 0.8
  text "The S.S. Aqua sped west across the sea, skimming the waves..."
  clearflag FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_AT_VERMILION
  fade in 0.8
  say "We've docked at Olivine City, Johto! Mind your step on the gangway."
  end
label Wait
  say "No rush. The gangway's behind you, if you'd rather stay ashore a while longer."
  end
