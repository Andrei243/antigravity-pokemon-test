# The Pokétch Company (plan 06 · R14b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_poketch_co_1f.s): the president hands out a new app for the first Gym Badge, the third,
# the fifth and the seventh, one at a time and in that order, and after the last talks of the Poké Radar's ideas.


script President
  speaker "President"
  say "Ah, the young Trainer from the campaign! I am the president of the Pokétch Company. How is that Pokétch treating you?"
  if badges < 1 goto OneBadge
  if not poketchapp MemoPad goto MemoPad
  if badges < 3 goto ThreeBadges
  if not poketchapp MarkingMap goto MarkingMap
  if badges < 5 goto FiveBadges
  if not poketchapp LinkSearcher goto LinkSearcher
  if badges < 7 goto SevenBadges
  if not poketchapp MoveTester goto MoveTester
  if item "Poké Radar" goto Radar
  say "People all over Sinnoh dream up apps of their own. Keep your eyes open, and your Pokétch will keep growing!"
  end
label MemoPad
  say "And you've a Gym Badge! I've just finished a new app. Here, let me put it on for you."
  poketchapp MemoPad
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Memo Pad was added to the Pokétch!"
  say "That's the Memo Pad. Jot down anything you like, and rub it out when you're done!"
  end
label MarkingMap
  say "Three Gym Badges! Then you'll want this one. I've only just finished it."
  poketchapp MarkingMap
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Marking Map was added to the Pokétch!"
  say "The Marking Map shows Sinnoh with marks you can put wherever you like. Some Pokémon that roam about show up on it too!"
  end
label LinkSearcher
  say "Five Gym Badges! You've earned another of my apps."
  poketchapp LinkSearcher
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Link Searcher was added to the Pokétch!"
  say "The Link Searcher tells you how many people near you are playing over a link. Handy for finding friends!"
  end
label MoveTester
  say "Seven Gym Badges! Splendid! Here is my newest app."
  poketchapp MoveTester
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Move Tester was added to the Pokétch!"
  say "With the Move Tester you can check how well a move of one type does against Pokémon of others."
  end
label OneBadge
  say "Our engineers are always dreaming up new apps. Win a Gym Badge, and I'll have one ready for you!"
  end
label ThreeBadges
  say "I'm hard at work on another app. Come back when you have three Gym Badges!"
  end
label FiveBadges
  say "My next app is nearly done. Come back with five Gym Badges and it's yours!"
  end
label SevenBadges
  say "I've one more app in the works. Win seven Gym Badges, and I'll give it to you!"
  end
label Radar
  say "A Poké Radar! Hmm... perhaps I should ask people what apps they'd like next. What a thought!"
  end
