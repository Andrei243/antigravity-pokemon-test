# The Radio Tower's observation deck (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D23R0106).
#
# Archer waits at the north rail while the takeover is at its height (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 4). Beaten, he breaks
# Team Rocket up as Giovanni did before him and is gone (FLAG_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_ACTIVE cleared), and the Director, set
# free from the warehouse, comes up in the lift: he gives the player the Rainbow Wing (FLAG_UNK_093), found at the top
# of the tower that stood here before the Radio Tower, and tells what his friend in Cianwood City says of it. The
# takeover is over (5; FLAG_BEAT_RADIO_TOWER_ROCKETS), the Director goes back to his desk
# (FLAG_HIDE_RADIO_TOWER_5F_OFFICE_DIRECTOR cleared), and HeartGold also moves on what waits in Mahogany Town and
# Blackthorn City: the souvenir shop's saleswoman (FLAG_HIDE_MAHOGANY_SHOP_SALESWOMAN, VAR_UNK_407A 1) and the guard
# before the Blackthorn Gym, who stands aside (FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_GYM_GUARD_INFRONT set, _ASIDE cleared). The
# Director who comes up the lift is a person of our own: HeartGold keeps him beside the deck, where this room has
# no floor (FLAG_HIDE_RADIO_TOWER_DECK_DIRECTOR, a flag of ours that the arrival sets).


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_RADIO_TOWER_LIFT_FLOOR 1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_RADIO_TOWER_DECK_DIRECTOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 4 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  end


# D23R0106_001
script Archer
  face d23r0106_rkanbum player
  speaker "Archer"
  scene "Archer on the observation deck"
  say "So, you've made it all the way up here. You must be quite a Trainer."
  say "We took this tower to tell the world Team Rocket is back. When he hears it, our leader Giovanni will come home from his training."
  say "Team Rocket will be great again, and I won't let a child stand in our way!"
  battle d23r0106_rkanbum as "johto_executive_archer_archer" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Archer"
  say "How can this be? Everything we dreamed of, gone... I wasn't equal to it after all."
  say "As Giovanni did before me, I'm breaking up Team Rocket, here and now. Farewell."
  fade out 0.4
  hide d23r0106_rkanbum
  sound "stairs"
  clearflag FLAG_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_ACTIVE
  setflag FLAG_BEAT_RADIO_TOWER_ROCKETS
  fade in
  if var PLAYER_X == 7 walk player down 1 right 1
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 walk player down 1 left 1
  face player down
  show deck_director
  place deck_director 8 13 up
  walk deck_director left 3 up 8 right 3
  face deck_director player
  speaker "Director"
  say "You've beaten them already? Thank you! You've saved every Pokémon in the country. Here, you more than deserve this."
  give "Rainbow Wing"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_093
  say "A tower stood on this very spot long ago. When it came down to make way for the Radio Tower, that feather was found at its top."
  say "A friend of mine in Cianwood City tells me that whoever holds a Rainbow Wing may climb the Bell Tower in Ecruteak City."
  say "Well, I must get back to my office."
  walk deck_director left 3
  face deck_director right
  say "Oh, I almost forgot! My friend says the legendary Pokémon won't come just for the wing. There's something else you need..."
  say "Now what was it? I can't for the life of me remember."
  walk deck_director right 3
  face deck_director player
  say "Waiting for something more, were you? That was a feint! Now I really must go. Goodbye!"
  walk deck_director left 3 down 8 right 3 up 1
  hide deck_director
  setvar VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 5
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_RADIO_TOWER_5F_OFFICE_DIRECTOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_RADIO_TOWER_5F_PETREL_REVEALED
  setvar VAR_UNK_407A 1
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_MAHOGANY_SHOP_SALESWOMAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_GYM_GUARD_INFRONT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_GYM_GUARD_ASIDE
  endscene
  end
label Lost
  speaker "Archer"
  say "Know your place, child. Team Rocket's return can't be stopped."
  endscene
  end
