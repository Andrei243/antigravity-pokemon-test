# The second basement of the passage under Goldenrod City (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D37R0104).
#
# Team Rocket's grunts and two burglars hold the floor while the takeover is at its height (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER
# 4; FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1 hides them otherwise). Its corridors are cut by ten shutters, each a row of things of
# the map shown while its flag of ours says it is shut (FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_<n>_SHUT; HeartGold keeps them in
# temporary variables). Three switches in the middle room each turn a set of them over, as HeartGold's do: the blue
# one shutters 2, 3, 4, 5 and 7, the green one 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 and 9, the red one 1, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 8. Coming in puts
# them back as HeartGold's arrival does, 2 and 9 shut and the rest open, so green, red and blue in turn open the way to
# the warehouse. The purple shutter before the warehouse's corner opens from its own switch on the far side, for good
# (FLAG_OPENED_GOLDENROD_PURPLE_GATE). The red-haired boy waits for the player at the top of the floor's east room
# (VAR_UNK_40A0): HeartGold keeps him beyond the floor until then, here he comes down the stairs from the passage.


# D37R0104_006
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 4 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GOLDENROD_B2F_RIVAL
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_0_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_1_SHUT
  setflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_2_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_3_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_4_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_5_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_6_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_7_SHUT
  clearflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_8_SHUT
  setflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_9_SHUT
  end


# D37R0104_000: the blue switch
script Blue
  sound "gear"
  call Gate2
  call Gate3
  call Gate4
  call Gate5
  call Gate7
  end


# D37R0104_001: the green switch
script Green
  sound "gear"
  call Gate0
  call Gate2
  call Gate4
  call Gate6
  call Gate8
  call Gate9
  end


# D37R0104_002: the red switch
script Red
  sound "gear"
  call Gate1
  call Gate3
  call Gate5
  call Gate6
  call Gate7
  call Gate8
  end


# D37R0104_003: the purple switch
script Purple
  if flag FLAG_OPENED_GOLDENROD_PURPLE_GATE end
  sound "gear"
  setflag FLAG_OPENED_GOLDENROD_PURPLE_GATE
  end


# Each shutter turned over: shut if it was open, open if it was shut
script Gate0
  if flag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_0_SHUT goto Open0
  setflag FLAG_GOLDENROD_B2F_GATE_0_SHUT
  return

  return

script Gate
  speaker none

  end


# D37R0104_004: the red-haired boy comes down the stairs after the player
script Rival
  scene "The red-haired boy under Goldenrod"
  music "johto/rival"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_GOLDENROD_B2F_RIVAL
  show goldenrod_b2f_rival
  place goldenrod_b2f_rival 27 4 left
  emote player exclaim
  walk goldenrod_b2f_rival left 2 down 1 left 4
  walk player right 1
  face goldenrod_b2f_rival player
  face player goldenrod_b2f_rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Not so fast. What I told you before was a lie. I thought if I stuck to you, you'd lead me to that Lance."
  say "He never turned up. Never mind. I meant to beat you after him, but you'll do first. I always settle my debts!"
  if starter "Chikorita" goto VsTyphlosion
  if starter "Cyndaquil" goto VsFeraligatr
  battle goldenrod_b2f_rival as "johto_rival_silver_17"
  goto Won
label VsTyphlosion
  battle goldenrod_b2f_rival as "johto_rival_silver_18"
  goto Won
label VsFeraligatr
  battle goldenrod_b2f_rival as "johto_rival_silver_12"
label Won
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...I don't get it. Was that Lance right? That I don't treat my Pokémon properly? Love... trust... Is that what I'm missing?"
  say "Is that why I keep losing? I don't understand. But I'm not finished. Not now, not like this. I'll never give up!"
  setvar VAR_UNK_40A0 1
  walk goldenrod_b2f_rival right 4 up 1 right 2
  hide goldenrod_b2f_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GOLDENROD_B2F_RIVAL
  music area
  endscene
  end
