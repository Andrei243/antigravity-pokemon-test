# The Marine Tube's gate north of Undella Town (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own. Until Opelucid City's chapter has ended (VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID 7, Cheren's word that the Frigate made for Humilau City)
# a worker turns the player back from the tube; once it has, the way is open for good (VAR_UNOVA_TUBE 1).


script TubeClosed
  if var VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID == 7 goto Open
  speaker "Worker"
  say "Sorry! We're polishing the Marine Tube's glass today. Come back another time, would you?"
  walk player left 1
  end
label Open
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_TUBE 1
  end
