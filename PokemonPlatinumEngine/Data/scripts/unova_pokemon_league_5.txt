# Shauntal's room of the Elite Four (plan 22 · U9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league_5.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_SHAUNTAL is 0 until Shauntal is beaten in this challenge, 1 after; the plaza's arrival puts it back to 0, as a new
# challenge begins there. Shauntal battles with Black 2's first team (unova_elite_four_shauntal) and, once Unova's Hall of Fame has been
# entered, with the team of the second round (unova_elite_four_shauntal_2). Once beaten, the light in the corner (Pad) carries the
# player back down to the League's hall, beside the arm to this room.


script Shauntal
  face 0 player
  speaker "Shauntal"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_SHAUNTAL == 1 goto Beaten
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Again
  say "'A lone Trainer, a dragon at her side, crossing the world in search of the truth...' Forgive me, that's a line from the novel I'm writing."
  say "You must be the challenger. I'm Shauntal of the Elite Four. My Ghost-type Pokémon and I will be your opponents."
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_shauntal"
  goto Won
label Again
  say "Back again? Good! Every return is a new chapter. Let's write this one together!"
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_shauntal_2"
label Won
  speaker "Shauntal"
  say "Every Pokémon on the field fought on, hurting and tired. Thank you, all of you. What a chapter that was."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_SHAUNTAL 1
  speaker none
  text "A light began to glow in the corner of the room."
  end
label Beaten
  say "Battles hurt, for us and for our Pokémon. Remember that, and you'll never forget the bonds you share."
  say "That's what I try to put into my stories."
  end


# The light in the corner, once Shauntal is beaten: back down to the League's hall
script Pad
  speaker none
  text "The light carried {player} back down to the hall."
  warp "UnovaPokemonLeague_Hall" 24 39 right
  end
