# Professor Oak's aide hands out an Everstone for twenty kinds caught (FireRed's FLAG_GOT_EVERSTONE_FROM_OAKS_AIDE).


script Aide
  speaker "Aide"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_EVERSTONE_FROM_OAKS_AIDE goto Given
  if not flag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX goto NoDex
  ask "Hi! I'm one of Professor Oak's aides. Shall I see how many kinds of Pokémon you've caught?"
  if no goto Declined
  if var CAUGHT < 20 goto NotYet
  say "{var:CAUGHT} kinds! Splendid. The Professor wants you to have this."
  give "Everstone"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_EVERSTONE_FROM_OAKS_AIDE
label Given
  say "A Pokémon holding an Everstone won't evolve. Handy, if you like one just as it is."
  end
label NoDex
  say "I'm studying the Pokémon of Rock Tunnel for Professor Oak."
  end
label Declined
  say "All right. Come and see me whenever you like."
  end
label NotYet
  say "Only {var:CAUGHT} so far. Come back once you've caught twenty kinds, and I'll have something for you."
  end
