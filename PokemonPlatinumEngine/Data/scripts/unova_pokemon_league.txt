# The Pokémon League's plaza (plan 22 · U9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GUARD is 0 until the veteran by the steps (1) has been told the player is ready, 1 after. As the player comes to
# the foot of the steps (Black 2's own trigger across them, 31 to 33, 48) he asks: yes and he lets them by, no and they step back.


# Anyone on the plaza is between challenges (the League's hall has no way back out, and a battle lost inside wakes the player in
# the Pokémon Center here): the Elite Four and the Champion wait to be beaten afresh
script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpF
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_SHAUNTAL 0
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GRIMSLEY 0
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_MARSHAL 0
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_CAITLIN 0
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_IRIS 0
  end


script Guard
  face 1 player
  speaker "Veteran"
  say "The Pokémon League is where Trainers seek strength and show it. To show it, you need only defeat the Elite Four and then the Champion."
  say "Challenge the Elite Four in any order you like. Beat all four, and the Champion's way opens."
  say "But hear me: once you go in, there's no turning back. You battle until you have beaten them all, or been beaten yourself."
  ask "Are you ready to go in?"
  if no goto NotYet
  say "Then go, and good luck!"
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GUARD 1
  end
label NotYet
  say "Don't skimp on getting ready. The Pokémon Center is just there. Heal your Pokémon and come back."
  walk player down 1
  end


# The veteran by the steps, spoken to once he has let the player by
script GuardTalk
  face 1 player
  speaker "Veteran"
  say "Once you go in, you battle until you have beaten them all, or been beaten yourself."
  end


# The veteran by the Pokémon Center
script Prepare
  face 0 player
  speaker "Veteran"
  say "The Pokémon League is the greatest challenge a Trainer can take on!"
  say "Get ready here first. Lose even once inside, and you start all over again."
  end
