# A house in Mauville City (plan 21 · H3, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MauvilleCity_House2/scripts.inc): the woman here would give her husband's Coin Case for a
# Harbor Mail, which Slateport's Mart sells, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_COIN_CASE).


script Woman
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_COIN_CASE goto Given
  if item "Harbor Mail" goto Trade
  say "My husband spends every afternoon at the Game Corner. I wish I had some pretty Harbor Mail to write to my friends."
  end
label Trade
  say "Oh! Is that Harbor Mail? I've wanted some for ages."
  ask "Would you trade it for my husband's Coin Case?"
  if no goto Refused
  take "Harbor Mail"
  give "Coin Case"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_COIN_CASE
label Given
  say "That Coin Case kept my husband at the Game Corner all day. Now he'll have to find something else to do!"
  end
label Refused
  say "Oh, never mind. It is lovely mail, after all."
  end
