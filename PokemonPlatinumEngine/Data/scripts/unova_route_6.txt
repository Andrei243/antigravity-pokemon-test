# Route 6 (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/route_6.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 is the route's count (Black 2's 0x40c9 and 0x40ca): 0 until the Plasma Frigate has sailed, 1 while
# Cheren waits on the path before the Season Research Lab (the arrival sets it), 2 once he has taken the player in
# (unova_route_6_2.txt tells the cold snap and gives Surf), 3 once Cobalion has run across the west bridge.
#
# Once VAR_UNOVA_PWT is 3 Cheren (8, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_ROUTE_6) stands on the path; our trigger across it (138, 371
# to 375, at 1), or the player speaking to him, has him ask the player to come with him: they walk to the lab's door and
# in. On the bridge in the west (our trigger on 102 to 104, 365, at 2) Cobalion shows itself across the water; Rood (10) and
# an old man (9) come up beside the road to wonder at it and stay to talk.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  if var VAR_UNOVA_PWT < 3 end
  if var VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 != 0 end
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 1
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_ROUTE_6
  end


script CherenCalls
  call CherenTakesYouIn
  end


script CherenOnTheRoad
  face 8 player
  speaker "Cheren"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 == 1 goto Calls
  say "I'm going over the lab's records a while longer. Take care out there, {player}."
  end
label Calls
  call CherenTakesYouIn
  end


# Cheren asks the player to come with him to the lab, then they go in together
script CherenTakesYouIn
  scene "Cheren calls the player to the lab"
  place player 138 373 left
  face 8 player
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "{player}, there you are. I hoped you would come this way."
  say "Come with me for a moment, would you? There's something I want to talk over, and the lab is the quietest place for miles."
  walk 8 up 3
  walk player left 3 up 2
  walk 8 up 1
  hide 8
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_ROUTE_6
  walk player up 1
  endscene
  warp "Route6_SeasonLab" 5 8 up
  return


# On the bridge in the west, once the lab has been visited (our trigger on 102 to 104, 365, at 2)
script CobalionPasses
  scene "Cobalion on Route 6"
  camera pan 98 365 1
  place cobalion 98 365 right
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COBALION_ROUTE_6
  show cobalion
  wait 0.6
  cry "Cobalion"
  wait 1.2
  hide cobalion
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COBALION_ROUTE_6
  camera release 1
  place 10 102 363 down
  place 9 103 363 down
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROOD_ROUTE_6
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_OLDMAN_ROUTE_6
  show 10
  show 9
  face player up
  speaker "Rood"
  say "That Pokémon...?"
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "That's the legendary Pokémon Cobalion! I told you the tale the other day, remember?"
  speaker "Rood"
  say "Cobalion! What a noble presence it has."
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "They say Cobalion, Virizion and Terrakion stood against people to protect Pokémon from a war that the people had started."
  say "When people fight, there is no peace for Pokémon either. The three learned how far the deeds of humans can reach into the world of Pokémon."
  say "But why would it show itself to people at all? Is it on watch over Unova for the Pokémon's sake, after what Team Plasma did two years ago? Or does it feel a new trouble coming?"
  speaker "Rood"
  say "Unforgivable... So Ghetsis's ambition and malice are still spreading, and still bending the lives they touch."
  say "Did Cobalion show itself to you by chance, or to ask for your help? I cannot know. Unlike Lord N, I was never able to understand the minds of Pokémon."
  say "But if you can win Cobalion's trust, it will be a great strength to you on your journey."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 3
  endscene
  end


script OldManAboutCobalion
  face 9 player
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "People and Pokémon alike would do well to trust one another. That's all the three legends ever wanted, I think."
  end


script RoodAboutCobalion
  face 10 player
  speaker "Rood"
  say "If Cobalion wishes to find you, it will. Be patient and keep your Pokémon close."
  end
