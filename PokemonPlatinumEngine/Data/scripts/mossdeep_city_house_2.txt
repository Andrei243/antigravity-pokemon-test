# A house of Mossdeep City (plan 21 · H8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MossdeepCity_House2/scripts.inc).
#
# The other half of the Fortree boy's errand (fortree_city_house_4.txt): his Wingull is here once he has sent it off
# (FLAG_HIDE_MOSSDEEP_CITY_HOUSE_2_WINGULL cleared there). Spoken to, it cries, the letter delivered
# (FLAG_WINGULL_DELIVERED_MAIL), and flies out of the door and home (FLAG_HIDE_FORTREE_CITY_HOUSE_4_WINGULL cleared),
# where the boy now gives the Mental Herb.


script Wingull
  face self player
  cry "Wingull"
  speaker "Wingull"
  say "Wingull: Pihyoh!"
  setflag FLAG_WINGULL_DELIVERED_MAIL
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_FORTREE_CITY_HOUSE_4_WINGULL
  if facing left goto West
  walk self right 1 down 2 left 1 down 1 fast
  goto Gone
label West
  walk self down 3 fast
label Gone
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MOSSDEEP_CITY_HOUSE_2_WINGULL
  end
