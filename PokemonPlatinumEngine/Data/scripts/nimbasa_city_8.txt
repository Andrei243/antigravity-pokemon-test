# The Small Court's lobby in Nimbasa City (plan 42 · PS5; rulings, "Sports days"). Black 2's room, our words.
# As at the Big Stadium: a match is on in the twilight hours, and between matches the receptionist takes visitors
# down onto the court of the day's sport (SportsDays.On: tennis on Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday, basketball
# on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday).

script OnTransition
  if time twilight goto On
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON
  end
label On
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON
  end

script Receptionist
  face self player
  if time twilight goto GameOn
  if weekday tuesday goto Basketball
  if weekday thursday goto Basketball
  if weekday saturday goto Basketball
  say "Welcome to the Small Court! Today is tennis day."
  ask "The court is open to visitors between matches. Would you like to go down?"
  if no goto Bye
  say "Enjoy the court! The players don't mind a battle between rallies."
  warp "NimbasaCity_TennisCourt" 6 24 up
  end
label Basketball
  say "Welcome to the Small Court! Today is basketball day."
  ask "The court is open to visitors between matches. Would you like to go down?"
  if no goto Bye
  say "Enjoy the court! The players don't mind a battle between drills."
  warp "NimbasaCity_BasketballCourt" 6 24 up
  end
label GameOn
  say "A match is on right now, so the courts are closed to visitors. Do go up to the stands and cheer!"
  end
label Bye
  say "Come back any time. The courts are open between matches!"
  end
