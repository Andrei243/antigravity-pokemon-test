# Plan 44 · SD4: Orsolya Keld, Lockwork's head, a harbour clockmaker (the user's answer 40a), and the machine stopped
script Head
  face self player
  speaker self
  if not flag FLAG_LOCKWORK_UNOVA_ADMIN_BEATEN goto Busy
  say "Listen. Do you hear it ticking? Every lock in five regions keeps time with this machine."
  say "I made clocks for the harbour once. A clock never leaves, never tires, never chooses another."
  say "So I made hearts like clocks. Perfectly loyal partners. Never afraid of losing anyone again."
  say "My own Ampharos was the first. It hasn't left my side in years. Let me show you what loyalty is."
  battle self as "lockwork_head"
  speaker self
  say "It stopped. It actually stopped. And my Ampharos is looking at you as if you were the clockmaker."
  say "...Perhaps a heart was never meant to keep time."
  setflag FLAG_LOCKWORK_MACHINE_STOPPED
  fade out 0.5
  hide self
  fade in 0.5
  end
label Busy
  say "Juno is still on the floor below. Nobody comes up these stairs without her say."
  end
