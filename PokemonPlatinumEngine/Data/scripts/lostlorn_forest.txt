# The Lostlorn Forest (plan 22 · U6, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black
# 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/lostlorn_forest.s, read for the order alone).
#
# A traveller (7, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LOSTLORN_TRAVELLER, Black 2's 0x03c7) stands in the clearing's north-west corner. He
# asks after Zoroark, gives a TM whatever the answer and tells the forest's rumour; then he is gone, and a Zoroark
# (zoroark, ours, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LOSTLORN_ZOROARK) cries where he stood and bounds off into the trees. Black 2's TM95
# (Snarl) is no TM of our data's: the traveller gives TM46, Thief, Dark as Snarl is (the rulings).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script Traveller
  scene "The traveller in the Lostlorn Forest"
  place player 8 12 up
  face self player
  speaker "Traveller"
  say "Well, hello there, Trainer. A Pokédex too, I see. I'm a traveller. I wander the world and talk with whoever I meet."
  ask "Tell me, do you know of a Pokémon called Zoroark?"
  if no goto DontKnow
  say "Then you know it can fool the eye. Good. Only be sure it never fools you."
  goto Gift
label DontKnow
  say "No? Then mind yourself. The Pokémon you've never heard of is the easiest one to be fooled by."
label Gift
  say "Whether you trust what you see or doubt it all is your own choice. I've enjoyed our talk. Here, a keepsake."
  give "TM46"
  say "They say a Zoroark came to this forest two years ago and dressed the grass and the trees in its illusions."
  say "Only a rumour, of course. But there's always a grain of truth in a rumour..."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LOSTLORN_TRAVELLER
  hide self
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LOSTLORN_ZOROARK
  show zoroark
  cry "Zoroark"
  speaker none
  text "Grrrowf!"
  walk zoroark left 3 fast
  hide zoroark
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LOSTLORN_ZOROARK
  text "The traveller was a Zoroark all along! It vanished into the trees."
  endscene
  end
