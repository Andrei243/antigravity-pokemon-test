# Poni Meadow, Poni Island (plan 23, the wave after Ultra Space). The lines are our own. The dancer explains the meadow's
# purple nectar and hands one over, once (FLAG_ALOLA_PONI_MEADOW_NECTAR): an Oricorio that sips it takes the Sensu Style,
# as Ula'ula Meadow's dancer gives the Red Nectar.


script Dancer
  face dancer player
  speaker "Dancer"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_PONI_MEADOW_NECTAR goto Done
  say "The Oricorio of Poni dance a slow, graceful dance. They learn it from the purple flowers, and so did I!"
  say "Take this nectar. Give it to an Oricorio from any other island and it will dance like a ghost on a summer night."
  give "Purple Nectar"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_PONI_MEADOW_NECTAR
  end
label Done
  say "Four islands, four flowers, four dances. I have seen them all, and Poni's is the one that makes me cry."
  end
