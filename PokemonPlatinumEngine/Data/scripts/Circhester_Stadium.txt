# Circhester Stadium's lobby (plan 33 · GL5, its second session): the sixth gym of the challenge, Gordie's in a Sword
# game and Melony's in a Shield one (VAR_GALAR_VERSION). The mission is through the back (Circhester_StadiumMission.txt),
# and the pitch beyond it (Circhester_StadiumPitch.txt). Our own lines.


script Receptionist
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if badge rock goto Won
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  say "Welcome to Circhester Stadium! Gordie's gym mission is through the doors behind me. Watch the floor!"
  say "A gym trainer stands at each of the course's gates. Pass each one's test, then Gordie awaits on the pitch."
  end
label Shield
  say "Welcome to Circhester Stadium! Melony's gym mission is through the doors behind me. Wrap up warm!"
  say "A gym trainer stands at each of the course's gates. Pass each one's test, then Melony awaits on the pitch."
  end
label Won
  say "Congratulations on your sixth badge! Route 9 runs west of town, along the bay."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge rock goto Won
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  say "Gordie's Rock types are as tough as they come. Water, grass and fighting moves will crack them!"
  end
label Shield
  say "Melony's Ice types run cold. Fire, fighting, rock and steel moves will melt them!"
  end
label Won
  say "Six badges! The bay west of town is too deep to wade. The mechanic on its shore can help."
  end
