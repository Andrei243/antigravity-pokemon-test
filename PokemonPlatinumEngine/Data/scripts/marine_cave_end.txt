# The Marine Cave's inner room (plan 21 · H12, part e). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MarineCave_End/scripts.inc).
#
# Kyogre waits in the pool at the room's heart until it is beaten or caught (FLAG_DEFEATED_KYOGRE). The room's arrival shows
# it and readies the step before the pool (the area's trigger 0, VAR_TEMP_1 1), as Emerald's OnTransition does; that step
# meets it at level 70. Run from, it dives away and is back the next time the room is come to. Beaten or caught, the rain
# over the route the cave opened from stops and its way in closes (common.HoennAbnormalWeatherEnd), and the Weather
# Institute's next report can only be of Terra Cave.


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_KYOGRE goto Gone
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_MARINE_CAVE_KYOGRE
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 1
  end
label Gone
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MARINE_CAVE_KYOGRE
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 0
  end


script Kyogre
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_KYOGRE end
  scene "Kyogre in the Marine Cave"
  face player up
  wait 0.4
  cry "Kyogre"
  camera shake 0.6
  wait 0.8
  speaker none
  text "Kyogre rose from the depths of the pool!"
  endscene
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 0
  wildbattle "Kyogre" 70
  hide marine_cave_kyogre
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MARINE_CAVE_KYOGRE
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_KYOGRE
  call HoennAbnormalWeatherEnd
  end
label Fled
  text "Kyogre dived down into the depths of the pool..."
  end


# Kyogre looked at across the water before the step that wakes it
script Waiting
  speaker none
  text "A huge blue Pokémon floats in the pool, perfectly still."
  end
