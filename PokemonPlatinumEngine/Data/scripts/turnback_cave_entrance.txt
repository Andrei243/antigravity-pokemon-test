# Turnback Cave's entrance (plan 01 · M8). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_turnback_cave_entrance.s).
#
# Every door of the entrance and of the rooms further in is aimed as the player comes in (`turnback`, the
# original's InitTurnbackCave): back the way one came is the entrance again, and the other three doors lead on.
# The entrance starts the count over.

script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_TURNBACK_CAVE
  setvar VAR_TURNBACK_CAVE_PILLARS_SEEN 0
  setvar VAR_TURNBACK_CAVE_ROOMS_VISITED 0
  turnback
  end

# The words cut into the rock by the way in
script Inscription
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_GIRATINA goto Offering
  say "Words are cut deep into the rock: \"Walk past three pillars, and the master of the other side will be waiting.\""
  end
label Offering
  say "Words are cut deep into the rock: \"Walk past three pillars, and leave something for the place where the worlds touch.\""
  end
