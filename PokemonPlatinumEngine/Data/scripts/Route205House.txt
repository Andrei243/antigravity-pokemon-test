# The woman of the house lets a tired traveller rest: the team is healed behind a fade, as often as asked.


script Rest
  ask "You look worn out... Won't you rest here a while?"
  if no goto Proud
  fade out
  fanfare heal
  heal
  fade in
  say "There. You and your Pokémon look as fresh as the morning!"
  end
label Proud
  say "Being young is no reason to run yourself into the ground, you know."
  end
