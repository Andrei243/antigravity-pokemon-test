# Sidney's room, the first of Hoenn's Elite Four (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/EverGrandeCity_SidneysRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# Coming in, Scott is missed for good (FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_EVERGRANDE, as Emerald's ON_TRANSITION sets it). Spoken to,
# Sidney battles (a battle lost ends it there, and the game wakes the player in the League's hall, which puts the four
# rooms back); beaten, he opens the door behind him (FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_SIDNEY, which the room's door is opened by).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_EVERGRANDE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_EVER_GRANDE_POKEMON_CENTER_1F_SCOTT
  end


script Sidney
  face sidney player
  speaker "Sidney"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_SIDNEY goto Beaten
  scene "Sidney"
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  say "Well, look at you! I'm Sidney of the Elite Four. You came all this way, so I like you already."
  say "But liking you won't stop me. I play dirty, and I play to win. Can you keep up with that?"
  say "No more talking. Let's have a battle we'll both remember!"
  battle self as "hoenn_sidney"
  speaker "Sidney"
  say "Ha! That's it, I'm beaten. And I don't even mind. That was a battle and a half!"
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_SIDNEY
  sound "door_open"
  say "Go on through. The rest of the Elite Four won't go easy on you. Show them what you showed me!"
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "Don't look back now. The next room is waiting for you. Go and win it!"
  end
