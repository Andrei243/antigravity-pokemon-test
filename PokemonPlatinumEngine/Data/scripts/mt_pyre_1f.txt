# Mt. Pyre's ground floor (plan 21 · H7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MtPyre_1F/scripts.inc): the old woman by the stairs gives a Cleanse Tag once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_CLEANSE_TAG).


script CleanseTagWoman
  face self player
  speaker "Old Woman"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CLEANSE_TAG goto Given
  say "Ghost Pokémon wander these halls, child. Some of them would rather you weren't here at all."
  say "Here, take this. It will keep the wilder ones away from you."
  give "Cleanse Tag"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CLEANSE_TAG
  end
label Given
  say "Let one of your Pokémon hold that Cleanse Tag at the head of your team, and wild Pokémon will keep their distance."
  end
