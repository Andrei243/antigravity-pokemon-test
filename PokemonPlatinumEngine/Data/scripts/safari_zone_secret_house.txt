# The Safari Zone's secret house (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/SafariZone_SecretHouse/scripts.inc): the zone's exploration prize, HM03, Surf, for
# whoever finds the house (FLAG_GOT_HM03).


script Attendant
  speaker "Attendant"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM03 goto Surf
  scene "The secret house"
  say "Congratulations! You've found the Safari Zone's secret house, and won our grand prize!"
  give "HM03"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_HM03
  endscene
label Surf
  say "HM03 is Surf. A Pokémon that knows it can carry you across water, once you have the Soul Badge from Fuchsia's Gym."
  end
