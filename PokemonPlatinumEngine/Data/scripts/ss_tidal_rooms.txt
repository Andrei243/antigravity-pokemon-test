# The S.S. Tidal's cabins (plan 21 · H12, part g). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/SSTidalRooms/scripts.inc).
#
# The bed of the player's cabin: a rest heals the team (Emerald's Common_EventScript_OutOfCenterPartyHeal) and passes the
# voyage, so the ship comes in (VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 1, bound for Lilycove, becomes 3; 2, bound for Slateport, becomes 4;
# ss_tidal_corridor.txt has the values). Ours: Emerald's ship is halfway after one rest out of Lilycove and in after a
# second, and comes in by itself after enough steps; here one rest brings it in, and nothing else does.


script Bed
  speaker none
  ask "A soft bed. Lie down for a rest?"
  if no end
  fade out 0.8
  heal
  text "{player} took a rest. The ship's engines hummed below..."
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 1 goto Lilycove
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 2 goto Slateport
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Lilycove
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 3
  fade in 0.8
  text "Attention, passengers. We have arrived at Lilycove City."
  end
label Slateport
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 4
  fade in 0.8
  text "Attention, passengers. We have arrived at Slateport City."
  end
