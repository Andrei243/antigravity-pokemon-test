# The old lady's rest stop on Route 111 (plan 21 · H4, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route111_OldLadysRestStop/scripts.inc): she heals the team behind a fade, and
# asks again whether another rest is wanted.


script OldLady
  speaker "Old Lady"
  ask "Oh my, your Pokémon look worn out. Why not have a rest here?"
  if no goto Shy
  say "That's it. Take all the time you need."
  call Rest
  ask "Still feeling tired? Another rest would do you good."
  if no goto Shy
  say "Of course. Rest as long as you like."
  call Rest
  say "There now, everyone's bright-eyed again. Off you go, and take care!"
  end
label Shy
  say "Don't be shy, dear. My door is always open."
  end


script Rest
  fade out
  fanfare heal
  heal
  fade in
  return
