# Jubilife Village, Hisui (plan 23 · Z17). The lines are our own, on Legends: Arceus's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hisui's first steps"): no script of the original is read. VAR_HISUI_STORY is
# prelude_beach.txt's count.
#
# The people: laventon at the south gate (FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_LAVENTON_VILLAGE, cleared as he leaves the beach).


# Up from Prelude Beach into the village for the first time: the professor goes to report, and sends the player to wait
script Welcome
  scene "The professor at the south gate"
  face laventon player
  face player up
  speaker "Prof. Laventon"
  say "Here we are: Jubilife Village, home of the Galaxy Team. When the Team first came ashore there was nothing here but grass and wind."
  say "I must tell the Commander about you before anyone else does. A stranger who fell out of the sky will take some explaining!"
  say "Would you wait for me at the Wallflower? It's on Canala Avenue, down the west side of the village. I shan't be long!"
  walk laventon up 3
  hide laventon
  setflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_LAVENTON_VILLAGE
  clearflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_LAVENTON_HALL
  setvar VAR_HISUI_STORY 4
  objective hisuiWallflower
  endscene
  end


# The professor, should he be spoken to while he stands there (he leaves in the same scene)
script Laventon
  speaker "Prof. Laventon"
  say "The Wallflower is on Canala Avenue, to the west. Wait for me there!"
  end


# The front gate's guards: the Obsidian Fieldlands lie beyond, for members of the Galaxy Team alone until the entry
# trial (plan 23 · Z18)
script FrontGate
  speaker "Gate Guard"
  if var VAR_HISUI_STORY >= 7 goto Trial
  say "Beyond this gate lie the Obsidian Fieldlands. Only members of the Galaxy Team may pass, and you're not one of us. Not yet."
  end
label Trial
  say "Your entry trial is out in the Fieldlands, is it? The captain will send for you when everything's ready. Rest up till then."
  end


# The pastures' keeper: the Pokémon the player can't carry are kept here (the boxes)
script Pastures
  speaker "Pasture Keeper"
  if party < 1 goto Empty
  say "These are the pastures. Leave any Pokémon you can't carry with me, and I'll see that they eat well and sleep warm."
  pc
  end
label Empty
  say "These are the pastures, where the Galaxy Team keeps its Pokémon. Come back once you have some to leave with me."
  end
