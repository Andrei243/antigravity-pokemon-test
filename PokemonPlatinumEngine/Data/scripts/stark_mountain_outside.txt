# Stark Mountain's foot and mouth (plan 01 · M10, part b; its story plan 02 · S15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_stark_mountain_outside.s).
#
# The way to the mouth runs round the pool of lava: east of it, along its south side, up its west side and back along
# the top. Two grunts of Team Galactic stand at the far corner, on that way. Whoever steps onto the tile where the
# road reaches the pool (the original's trigger at 752, 235, while VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_STATE is 0) sees them
# across the lava: they walk to the mouth, wonder aloud whether old Charon can ever stand in for their boss, and go in
# (FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_GRUNTS, state 1).
#
# Once Charon has been taken in the mountain's heart (stark_mountain_room_3.txt, which sets the state to 2, shows
# Looker and Buck here and puts the player down before the mouth), the three part as the place is come to: Looker has
# the Magma Stone and what moving it is said to wake, Buck takes it back in to where it belongs and comes out again
# with an invitation to his place in the Survival Area, and Looker says goodbye to Sinnoh (state 3). Buck's place is
# the Battleground (plan 02 · S15, part c), and it is there, once he has been spoken to, that Heatran is let wake.
#
# Stood in for: Looker's own tune (plan 05 · A6) and Buck's look (plan 11 · C6: a youngster stands for him). Buck's
# way off is the road round the pool as far as its south-west corner, where the original walks him the whole way
# round out of sight. Looker stands east of the player (752, 233, the overlay's "at") where the original stands him
# to the north, and Buck comes back out to his own tile, a step further than the original's.


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_STARK_MOUNTAIN_EXTERIOR
  # StarkMountainOutside_OnFrame_LookerBuck
  if var VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_STATE == 2 call Parting
  end


# StarkMountainOutside_CoordEvent_Grunts: seen from across the lava
script GruntsGoIn
  scene "The grunts at Stark Mountain"
  camera pan 747 233 0.8
  move grunt_m_1 right 4
  wait 0.25
  move grunt_m_2 up 1 right 3
  waitmoves
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "This is it. Stark Mountain! What we're after is right down in the middle of it. Come on. Let's get it over with."
  move grunt_m_1 down 2 right 4 up 1
  wait 0.25
  move grunt_m_2 right 1 down 2 right 3 up 1
  waitmoves
  face grunt_m_2 right
  say "Sure, but... that old man. Can he really take over? He's no Master Cyrus, is he?"
  face grunt_m_1 left
  wait 0.4
  face grunt_m_1 up
  say "You're telling me. Old Charon may call himself a Commander, but nobody takes Master Cyrus's place."
  say "...I've been thinking this might be a good moment to be done with Team Galactic altogether."
  move grunt_m_1 up 2
  wait 0.25
  move grunt_m_2 right 1 up 2
  waitmoves
  sound "stairs"
  hide grunt_m_1
  hide grunt_m_2
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_GRUNTS
  setvar VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_STATE 1
  camera release 0.8
  endscene
  end


# StarkMountainOutside_OnFrame_LookerBuck: the player stands before the mouth (751, 233), Buck to the west and Looker
# to the east (the original stands him to the north, where the player's own head would hide him from the field's camera)
script Parting
  scene "The parting"
  face player right
  face looker left
  face buck right
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Haaah! The air of the outside! It is delicious, no?"
  say "...Hkk! Kaff! Gah! I take it back. It is nothing but ash!"
  wait 0.5
  say "Now then, the two of you. Look. This is what they came for: the Magma Stone."
  say "The old stories say that to move it is to wake the Pokémon Heatran, and that when Heatran wakes, Stark Mountain erupts."
  say "...And I, I have moved it. It is here, in my coat. Hmm. What is a man to do?"
  face player left
  speaker "Buck"
  say "Easy, mister! Hand it over. I know just where it goes. It'll be back in its place before the mountain's even noticed!"
  walk buck up 2
  sound "stairs"
  hide buck
  face looker up
  wait 0.4
  face looker left
  face player right
  speaker "Looker"
  say "That boy has the energy of ten, yes?"
  say "{player}, I owe you the reason I am here. After we parted on Mt. Coronet I went through their hideout from the roof to the cellar, and what I found there was Charon's plan."
  say "It led me to this mountain, and to a very long day spent as a rock. And now that he is taken, my work in Sinnoh is done."
  wait 0.5
  show buck
  sound "stairs"
  walk buck down 2
  face buck right
  face player left
  speaker "Buck"
  say "Done! The Magma Stone's back where it belongs. Heatran can sleep on. Probably!"
  say "And {player}! You're something else as a Trainer. Come by my place some time, won't you? It's in the Survival Area. See you there!"
  walk buck down 1 left 5 up 2 left 3
  walk buck down 12 fast
  hide buck
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_BUCK
  face player right
  face looker left
  speaker "Looker"
  say "And I, too, must be on my way."
  say "Where I come from they say that a good meeting is paid for at the parting. For years I took it for a pretty phrase. Today I find it is simply true."
  say "Farewell, {player}, my friend. Wherever I am sent next, I shall not forget Sinnoh. Or you."
  fade out 0.6
  hide looker
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_LOOKER
  fade in 0.6
  setvar VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_STATE 3
  endscene
  return


# Whoever gets a word with the grunts before they move (nobody can: the trigger lies on the only way round to them)
script Grunt
  face self player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Hey! No sightseers! This mountain is Team Galactic's business today."
  end
