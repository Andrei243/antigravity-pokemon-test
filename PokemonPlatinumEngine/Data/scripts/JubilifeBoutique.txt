# Jubilife City's boutique (plan 11 · C10). docs/scripts.md describes the language. Platinum has no boutique: the
# shop, its clerk and its lines are our own, in the southwest house the original fills with townsfolk alone.


# The clerk behind the counter: the boutique's stock, tried on and bought on the wardrobe screen.
script Clerk
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome to the Jubilife Boutique! Hats, tops, bottoms, shoes and bags, all in the latest Sinnoh styles."
  say "Try on anything you like. Whatever you buy is yours to keep, and you can change into it at home whenever you wish."
  wardrobe "jubilife"
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Thank you for stopping by! Do come again."
  end


# The salon's chair beside the counter (plan 43 · LF3): the cut, the hair's colour, the eyes and the skin, each paid at the chair.
script Stylist
  speaker "Stylist"
  say "Welcome to the chair! A new cut, a new colour, new eyes or a new tone: sit down and we'll find your look."
  wardrobe salon
  speaker "Stylist"
  say "There! Every mirror in Jubilife will want a second look."
  end

# The dye house's counter (plan 43 · LF3): a garment owned, a dye, the fee and the dye's berry.
script Dyer
  speaker "Dyer"
  say "A garment of yours, a berry and a little money, and I'll dye it any colour on my shelf."
  wardrobe dye "jubilife"
  speaker "Dyer"
  say "Hang it to dry in the sun and it'll keep its colour for good."
  end


# The photo studio's corner (plan 43 · LF5): a backdrop, a Pokémon beside you, Seals for stickers and a frame; the first visit gives a frame.
script Photographer
  speaker "Photographer"
  if flag FLAG_PHOTO_STUDIO_FRAME_GIVEN goto Welcome
  say "Oh, a new face! I keep a little studio in this corner: a backdrop, a Pokémon beside you, a few Seals for stickers."
  say "The first sitting is on the house. Take this frame, a gilt one. It looks lovely on everything."
  setflag FLAG_PHOTO_STUDIO_FRAME_GIVEN
  goto Sit
label Welcome
  say "Back for another picture? Step onto the stage whenever you're ready."
label Sit
  photo studio
  speaker "Photographer"
  say "Every print goes into your photo book. Come back any time!"
  end
