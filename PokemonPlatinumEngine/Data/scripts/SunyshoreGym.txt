# The Sunyshore Gym's first room (plan 01 · M9, part 2c), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_sunyshore_city_gym_room_1.s).
#
# Three rooms of gears over the dark, whose arms are the walkways (the game's own rule, SunyshoreGears). A button on a
# gear's hub turns every gear of the room at once, each its own way: here a quarter turn on. Coming in from the city the
# gears are in their first state; coming back from the second room, in the state that leads back (the engine lays them
# out as the player comes in, as the original's InitPersistedMapFeaturesForSunyshoreGym does).


# SunyshoreGymRoom1_Init: the buttons' variable is nought, so every step onto one presses it
script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x00 0
  end


# SunyshoreGymRoom1_Button: on the hubs of the gears at either end
script Button
  gearbutton normal
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge beacon goto Won
  say "Hey there! How's it going, future Champ? Let me take a peek at your Badges."
  say "One, two, three... Whoa! Seven already! Beat this Gym and the Pokémon League is next!"
  say "Which means this is the last time I get to give you any advice. So listen up, one last time!"
  say "The Gym Leader here is a master of Electric-type Pokémon!"
  say "That's all I've got. The rest is up to you!"
  end
label Won
  say "Oh! {player}! Did you beat the Gym Leader? Well, of course you did!"
  say "But you know what? I'm not done being impressed yet. You and your Pokémon have so much more in you!"
  say "Still, I'm proud of you. Eight Sinnoh Badges! Way to go, kiddo!"
  end


# The original names the certified trainers only once the Badge is won: the player, and the rival as well once he has won
# it too (VAR_RIVAL_BEAT_SUNYSHORE_GYM), which his scene in Sunyshore City sets after the player's win. That scene is plan
# 02 · S13's, and its branch of the statue comes with it.
script GymStatue
  speaker none
  if badge beacon goto Won
  say "SUNYSHORE CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Volkner"
  end
label Won
  say "SUNYSHORE CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Volkner. Certified Trainers: {player}"
  end
