# The Hotel Richissime by Rouge Plaza (plan 23 · Z4b; its door opens with the Hall of Fame). The lines are our own, on X
# and Y's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos after its Hall of Fame"): in Looker's
# first case one of his tickets hides in the potted plant by the stairs; in his fifth (VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 5) Malva of the
# Elite Four waits in the lobby, battles, and tells who Essentia is and who controls her, which opens the way down under
# Lysandre's old café to his laboratory (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS, LumioseCity_LysandreLabs.txt).
#
# The people: a receptionist, a guest, the designer (Designer, below), malva (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_MALVA until
# FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_MALVA_DONE).


# Looker's first case: one of his tickets, in the pot where the rich wait for their keys
script Ticket
  if var VAR_KALOS_LOOKER != 1 goto Plant
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKET_HOTEL goto Plant
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKET_HOTEL
  addvar VAR_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKETS 1
  fanfare item
  text "A card is tucked among the leaves, with an eye drawn on it in ink." "{player} found one of Looker's tickets!"
  end
label Plant
  text "A palm in a gilded pot. Even the plants here look expensive."
  end


# Looker's fifth case: Malva in the lobby
script Malva
  face malva player
  speaker "Malva"
  scene "Malva at the hotel"
  say "So you're the Champion who keeps poking about in alleys. Anatole says you battle well. Let me see for myself."
  battle self as "kalos_malva_looker"
  say "Hmph. Fine. You've earned an answer."
  say "The thief in the dark suit calls herself Essentia. She's your little friend Emma, in a suit that isn't hers."
  say "A scientist of what's left of Team Flare made it. Xerosic. The suit takes orders from him, and so does whoever wears it."
  say "I wanted no part of his tinkering. He's set up in Lysandre's old laboratory, under the café. The waiter knows the way."
  say "Go and get your friend back, Champion. I'd go myself, but I have a reputation to keep."
  hide malva
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_MALVA_DONE
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS
  endscene
  end


# Plan 40 · GE4 and GE5, for Kalos (plan 23, after Kalos finished): Kalos's designer, of ours, in the hotel's lobby.
# Like the other regions' designers he hands over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned (charm due, charm give;
# Models/Charms.cs) and reads the diplomas the Pokédex has handed out (diploma, DIPLOMAS). With nothing to say of
# either he says his own line (the map's).
script Designer
  face self player
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  if diploma Ours goto OursDiploma
  if diploma Kalos goto OwnDiploma
  if var DIPLOMAS >= 3 goto ManyDiplomas
  if var DIPLOMAS > 0 goto AnyDiploma
  sayown
  end
label Charms
  say "Pardon me, but is that Pokédex full? Every last page! Allow me to shake your hand."
  say "We who draw the Pokémon keep a few charms for those who see them all. Here, this is yours."
label Give
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Give
  say "Fill in another Pokédex, in some other region, and come and find me. There may be another charm in it."
  end
label OursDiploma
  say "That diploma is for no region's list at all. It's for the Pokémon added beyond them!" "You saw every one? I could use you to check my sketches."
  end
label OwnDiploma
  say "All three of Kalos's Pokédexes, every page seen! The Central, the Coast and the Mountains." "Lumiose's painters would weep. I nearly am."
  end
label ManyDiplomas
  say "Several diplomas on one Pokédex? You must have seen more Pokémon than I have drawn." "Does your bag have room for anything but paper?"
  end
label AnyDiploma
  say "A diploma on your Pokédex! A whole region's list is a great many pages." "Kalos has three lists of its own. Fill them in and show me."
  end
