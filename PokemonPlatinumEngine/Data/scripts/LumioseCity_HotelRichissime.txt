# The Hotel Richissime by Rouge Plaza (plan 23 · Z4b; its door opens with the Hall of Fame). The lines are our own, on X
# and Y's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos after its Hall of Fame"): in Looker's
# first case one of his tickets hides in the potted plant by the stairs; in his fifth (VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 5) Malva of the
# Elite Four waits in the lobby, battles, and tells who Essentia is and who controls her, which opens the way down under
# Lysandre's old café to his laboratory (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS, LumioseCity_LysandreLabs.txt).
#
# The people: a receptionist, a guest, malva (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_MALVA until FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_MALVA_DONE).


# Looker's first case: one of his tickets, in the pot where the rich wait for their keys
script Ticket
  if var VAR_KALOS_LOOKER != 1 goto Plant
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKET_HOTEL goto Plant
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKET_HOTEL
  fanfare item
  text "A card is tucked among the leaves, with an eye drawn on it in ink." "{player} found one of Looker's tickets!"
  end
label Plant
  text "A palm in a gilded pot. Even the plants here look expensive."
  end


# Looker's fifth case: Malva in the lobby
script Malva
  face malva player
  speaker "Malva"
  scene "Malva at the hotel"
  say "So you're the Champion who keeps poking about in alleys. Anatole says you battle well. Let me see for myself."
  battle self as "kalos_malva_looker"
  say "Hmph. Fine. You've earned an answer."
  say "The thief in the dark suit calls herself Essentia. She's your little friend Emma, in a suit that isn't hers."
  say "A scientist of what's left of Team Flare made it. Xerosic. The suit takes orders from him, and so does whoever wears it."
  say "I wanted no part of his tinkering. He's set up in Lysandre's old laboratory, under the café. The waiter knows the way."
  say "Go and get your friend back, Champion. I'd go myself, but I have a reputation to keep."
  hide malva
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_MALVA_DONE
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS
  endscene
  end
