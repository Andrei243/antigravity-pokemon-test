# The old woman lets whoever is on the way up to Stark Mountain rest: the team is healed behind a fade, as often as
# asked. The original asks again at once after a rest, and again after that for as long as the answer is yes; here a
# rest is one talk, and she says to rest again whenever one likes.


script Rest
  say "Oh, dear, are your Pokémon worn out? Then you must rest. Yes, what a good idea!"
  ask "Will you rest here a while?"
  if no goto Sure
  say "That's right, that's right. You've earned a good rest."
  fade out
  fanfare heal
  heal
  fade in
  say "Oh, but you still look tired to me. You'll be wanting another rest, won't you?" "Rest again whenever you like. Yes, what a good idea!"
  end
label Sure
  say "Is that so? You're quite sure, now?" "It's you I'm thinking of. You wouldn't be putting me out at all!"
  end
