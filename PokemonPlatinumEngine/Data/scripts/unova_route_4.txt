# Route 4 (plan 22 · U5, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats
# (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/route_4.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA is Castelia's count (castelia_city_10.txt has it whole).
#
# Just north of the gate a row of Crustle, taken by everyone for boulders, blocks the road (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CRUSTLE_
# ROUTE_4). Once Colress has said he would wait here (11), he stops the player before them (our trigger on 427 to 432,
# 584), wakes them with his machine, battles the player (Black 2's TRAINER_COLRESS), hands over a Protein and goes on
# north (12). The worker (16) talks of the boulders before and of the Crustle after.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script ColressWaits
  scene "Colress on Route 4"
  face 15 player
  speaker "Colress"
  say "I've been waiting for you! What's the matter? Curious about what's behind me?"
  say "Those aren't rocks at all. They're Crustle, carrying their boulders on their backs. Now, observe."
  say "This machine of mine was made to draw out a Pokémon's energy. Let's see what it does for them!"
  cry "Crustle"
  hide 12
  hide 13
  hide 14
  hide 17
  hide 18
  hide 19
  hide 20
  hide 21
  hide 22
  hide 23
  hide 24
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CRUSTLE_ROUTE_4
  say "Those Crustle... Were they lying here all along, too worn out to move, boulders and all?"
  say "Team Plasma once said that to bring out the potential of Pokémon, people must let them go free. I disagree."
  say "Science is what will draw out their power. That is what I believe, and why I study."
  say "Come to think of it, I never asked your name. ...{player}. I'll remember it."
  say "Now, let me see whether you're a Trainer who can draw out a Pokémon's hidden strength!"
  battle 15 as "unova_colress"
  say "I see! Like the Gym Leaders, or the Elite Four and the Champion of the Pokémon League, you bring out your Pokémon's strength by battling at their side."
  say "Please, take this. Consider it my thanks for the data!"
  give "Protein"
  say "How frustrating! If only we could talk with Pokémon, drawing out their power might be simple. But that's hardly science, is it?"
  say "Until we meet again, {player}!"
  walk 15 up 3 fast
  hide 15
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_ROUTE_4
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 12
  endscene
  end


script Crustle
  text "A row of boulders blocks the road. Strength won't shift them... Are they breathing?"
  end


script Worker
  face 16 player
  speaker "Worker"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA >= 12 goto After
  say "These boulders just turned up all in a line one morning. Strength won't shift them an inch!"
  end
label After
  say "Crustle, all along! If you want to see more of them, they live out in the desert to the north."
  end
