# The Ruins of Alph (plan 41 · EG8). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own. The ruins' own people
# speak through common.txt; this file holds the Open Door Agency's case in Johto.


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the Alph Etchings (plan 41 · EG8)
# The researcher shows the chalk copies labelled like a blackboard, the camper has seen lanterns walk the ruins in a line
# after dark, and a schoolchild tells of a teacher with chalk on her sleeves and a quiz on the Unown. Once Looker has the
# deductions the schoolteacher, Mirelle Dunn, waits in the ruins (FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_MIRELLE_WAITS).

script CaseJohtoResearcher
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_TAKEN goto Case
  say "Every morning, chalk all over the paving. Copies of the carvings! We scrub, and the next morning there are more."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLUE_CHALK goto Again
  say "You're helping Looker? Then look closely at the chalk. Every copy has its letter written under it. A, B, C, all in order."
  say "In a neat, round hand, like a blackboard. Vandals don't label their work."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLUE_CHALK
  text "{player} noted the chalk copies, labelled letter by letter."
  end
label Again
  say "Every copy labelled, in order. Whoever it is, they want somebody to learn the letters."
  end
label Thanks
  say "The class comes by day now, and they clean up after themselves. Some of them read the carvings better than I do!"
  end

script CaseJohtoCamper
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_TAKEN goto Case
  say "I camp out here for the quiet. The ruins at night are something else. Spooky, but nice."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLUE_LANTERNS goto Again
  say "Night visitors? I've seen them! Little lanterns, walking through the ruins in a line. Slowly, like a parade."
  say "And a grown-up voice saying, quietly now, keep together. I thought they were ghosts at first. Ghosts don't say that."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLUE_LANTERNS
  text "{player} noted the lanterns walking the ruins after dark."
  end
label Again
  say "Lanterns in a line, and somebody keeping everyone together. Not very ghostly."
  end
label Thanks
  say "No more lanterns at night. I almost miss them."
  end

script CaseJohtoPupil
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_TAKEN goto Case
  say "We came on a school trip! I know all the Unown letters. Well, nearly all of them."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLUE_DUST goto Again
  say "How do I know the letters? Miss Dunn teaches us. She always has chalk dust all over her sleeves in the morning."
  say "And every morning she gives us a quiz on the Unown. She says we'll be the best readers of the ruins in Johto!"
  setflag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CLUE_DUST
  text "{player} noted the teacher's chalky sleeves and her Unown quiz."
  end
label Again
  say "Chalk on her sleeves every single morning. Miss Dunn says it's from the blackboard."
  end
label Thanks
  say "We get to come to the ruins in the daytime now! Miss Dunn says it's much better for our eyes."
  end

script CaseJohtoMirelle
  face self player
  speaker "Mirelle"
  if flag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CULPRIT end
  scene "MirelleFaced"
  say "Oh dear. You've worked it out, haven't you? Yes, the chalk is mine. My pupils and I come here at night."
  say "The ruins teach better than any book. But if you mean to stop us, you'll have to pass my test first!"
  battle self as "case_johto_mirelle"
  say "...Top marks. Very well, I'll go and see the researchers myself, and ask properly. Tell Looker, would you?"
  setflag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_CULPRIT
  clearflag FLAG_CASE_JOHTO_MIRELLE_WAITS
  hide self
  endscene
  end
