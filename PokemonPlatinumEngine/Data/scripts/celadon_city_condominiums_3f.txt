# Celadon Mansion's third floor in Kanto (plan 19 · K5, part b): FireRed's team of game makers at work. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# The designer is one of those who hand over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned (plan 40 · GE5; charm due, charm
# give; Models/Charms.cs): any regional Pokédex seen whole, whichever region's, and the National one caught whole. With
# nothing due he talks of his maps (his own lines, the overlay's).


script Designer
  face self player
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  if diploma Ours goto OursDiploma
  if diploma Kanto goto OwnDiploma
  if var DIPLOMAS >= 3 goto ManyDiplomas
  if var DIPLOMAS > 0 goto AnyDiploma
  sayown
  end
label Charms
  say "Hold on, is that your Pokédex? Every page filled in! That's the best thing anyone has shown me all year."
  say "We keep a few charms here for the players who get that far. Go on, take it: it's yours."
label Give
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Give
  say "Fill in another Pokédex and drop by again. I'll find something more for you."
  end
# Plan 40 · GE4, ours: he reads the diplomas the Pokédex has handed out (diploma, DIPLOMAS). They sit after the lines
# above so that the lines' numbers in the translation files stay where they were.
label OursDiploma
  say "Is that the diploma for the Pokémon that no region lists? Nobody on this floor has ever got one!" "We keep drawing new ones faster than we can number them, and you saw every single one."
  end
label OwnDiploma
  say "A diploma for the whole Kanto Pokédex! We worked on that map for years, and you've seen everything we put in it." "Well, nearly everything. Not the stuff we left in the cupboard."
  end
label ManyDiplomas
  say "Diplomas from several Pokédexes? You've seen more Pokémon than our whole team has drawn!" "Come to think of it, that's not a high bar. But it's a long way to walk, so well done."
  end
label AnyDiploma
  say "Hey, that's a diploma on your Pokédex! Somebody did the hard part." "Every region lists different Pokémon. Go and fill in another, and come and show us."
  end
