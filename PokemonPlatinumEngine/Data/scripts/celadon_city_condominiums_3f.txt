# Celadon Mansion's third floor in Kanto (plan 19 · K5, part b): FireRed's team of game makers at work. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# The designer is one of those who hand over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned (plan 40 · GE5; charm due, charm
# give; Models/Charms.cs): any regional Pokédex seen whole, whichever region's, and the National one caught whole. With
# nothing due he talks of his maps (his own lines, the overlay's).


script Designer
  face self player
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  sayown
  end
label Charms
  say "Hold on, is that your Pokédex? Every page filled in! That's the best thing anyone has shown me all year."
  say "We keep a few charms here for the players who get that far. Go on, take it: it's yours."
label Give
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Give
  say "Fill in another Pokédex and drop by again. I'll find something more for you."
  end
