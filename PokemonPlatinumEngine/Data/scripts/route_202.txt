# Route 202 (plan 02 · S4, and the first beat of S5). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_route_202.s).
#
# The assistant waits at the south end of the road. Until the player has been home and been given the rival's
# parcel, they send the player back to tell their family; with it, they show how a Pokémon is caught and give the
# player Poké Balls. VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE: 1 shown.
#
# After the Hall of Fame (plan 02 · S15, part a): once the professor has given the Poké Radar, the assistant offers in
# Sandgem Town to show how it is used (sandgem_town.Counterpart makes the state 2) and waits for the player in the
# grass further up the road, at the original's 186, 817. Spoken to there she shows a patch of grass shaking, tells of
# the chain, and goes back to stand before the lab (state 3).


# Route202_OnTransition: while her lesson waits she stands in the grass (the original's ClearFlag is its Sandgem
# script's; here she comes onto the road as the player does, already in her place)
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE != 2 end
  place counterpart 186 817 down
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_202_COUNTERPART
  end


# The assistant, spoken to (the original's script 7): the Poké Radar's lesson, or the road's first scene again
script Counterpart
  if var VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE == 2 goto Radar
  call CatchingLesson
  end
label Radar
  face self player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "{player}! You came! Right, the Poké Radar. Let me show you how it works."
  camera pan 189 818 0.8
  face self right
  face player right
  wait 0.25
  say "You use it standing in tall grass, like that patch over there, and then you watch. Here and there the grass will shake, and a Pokémon is hiding in every patch that does."
  say "Walk straight into one, without stepping out of the grass, and it jumps out at you."
  camera release 0.6
  face self player
  face player self
  say "And here's the strange part. Catch it, or make it faint, and the grass shakes again. Go into another shaking patch and the very same kind of Pokémon is there!"
  say "Keep that going and you have a chain. The longer the chain, the likelier it is that a patch will sparkle instead of shaking... I'll leave you to find out what's in those."
  say "One more thing: the Radar's battery runs flat when you use it. Fifty steps on foot charges it up again. Good luck, {player}!"
  # Route202_CounterpartLeavePokeRadar: round the player and back down the road to Sandgem Town
  if facing up walk self left 1
  walk self down 3 left 10
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_202_COUNTERPART
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE 3
  end


# The road north out of Sandgem Town (the original's trigger 6).
script CatchingLesson
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_PARCEL goto Lesson
  face counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_ROUTE_202_COUNTERPART goto Again
  say "{player}! Have you told your family you're helping the professor? Go home and tell them first. Your mom will worry otherwise!"
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_ROUTE_202_COUNTERPART
  walk player right 1
  end
label Again
  say "Go home and tell your family first!"
  walk player right 1
  end
label Lesson
  music "common/assistant"
  face counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Oh, {player}! Are you off to Jubilife City? ...Do you know how to catch a Pokémon? I'll show you. Watch closely!"
  catchinglesson "Bidoof" 2
  say "That's how it's done! Weaken it first, then throw a Poké Ball. Here, take some of these."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "Good luck with the Pokédex, {player}! I'll see you around!"
  walk counterpart left 3 fast
  hide counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_202_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_202_STATE 1
  end
