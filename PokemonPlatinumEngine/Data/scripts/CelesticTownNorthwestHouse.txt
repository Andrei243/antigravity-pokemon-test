# Celestic Town's shop, the north-west house (plan 01 · M11, part c), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_celestic_town_northwest_house.s).
#
# The town has no Poké Mart: the old couple behind this counter are its two clerks, she with the common counter by
# the badges and he with the town's own (marts.json, "celestic"), and both are the common Clerk. The man in glasses
# gives a pair away by the hour, each once: Choice Specs in the morning (4 to 10), Black Glasses by day and at
# twilight (10 to 20), Wise Glasses at night (20 to 4), as the original's GetHour has them.


script Glasses
  say "Put on a pair of glasses and the whole world looks different." "Take them off again and it looks different once more. Makes you think, doesn't it?"
  if time morning goto Specs
  if time day goto Black
  if time twilight goto Black
  # CelesticTownNorthwestHouse_TryGiveWiseGlasses
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_WISE_GLASSES goto WiseWord
  say "So, how about changing the way the world looks to your Pokémon?"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_WISE_GLASSES
  give "Wise Glasses"
  say "Night is for thinking over what the day has taught you. Wise Glasses are the pair for that."
label WiseWord
  say "Wise Glasses put a little more power into a Pokémon's special moves."
  end
# CelesticTownNorthwestHouse_TryGiveChoiceSpecs
label Specs
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_CHOICE_SPECS goto SpecsWord
  say "So, how about changing the way the world looks to your Pokémon?"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_CHOICE_SPECS
  give "Choice Specs"
  say "Morning is where a new day starts. Put on the Choice Specs and choose well."
label SpecsWord
  say "A Pokémon in Choice Specs can use only the one move it picks." "Its Sp. Atk goes up to make up for it, though."
  end
# CelesticTownNorthwestHouse_TryGiveBlackGlasses
label Black
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_BLACKGLASSES goto BlackWord
  say "So, how about changing the way the world looks to your Pokémon?"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_BLACKGLASSES
  give "Black Glasses"
  say "The sun is at its brightest about now. Just the hour for a pair of Black Glasses."
label BlackWord
  say "Black Glasses make Dark-type moves hit harder."
  end
