# Lurking Steel (plan 34 · PD4, its second session): the third Titan of the Path of Legends, Orthworm, met as it bursts
# out of the sand in the cutting east off the East Province's third area, beaten and chased to its den on the ledge at the
# cutting's end, beaten again there once the Herba Mystica has made it bigger, and the herbs it left shared with the
# mount, which can leap again. A stand-in until plan 34 · PD6's second session moved it to the cutting east off the East
# Province's third area, north of Levincia (places/lurking_steel.json). VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS (stony_cliff.txt's header has the whole count) goes on:
#   5  the Titan Bombirdier beaten: the Titan Orthworm lurks in the cutting
#   6  the Titan Orthworm beaten in the cutting and fled to its den (FLAG_PALDEA_ORTHWORM_FLED)
#   7  the Titan Orthworm beaten at its den (FLAG_PALDEA_ORTHWORM_BEATEN), its herbs shared: the mount leaps high
#      (FLAG_PALDEA_MOUNT_HIGH_JUMP); the next Titan's version is set for the basin north of Cortondo
#      (FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_TUSK with Koraidon, FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_TREADS with Miraidon)
# The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats.


# The Titan out of the sand: the first battle, and its flight to the ledge
script Orthworm
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS == 5 goto Titan
  end
label Titan
  scene "The Lurking Steel Titan"
  faceto titan_orthworm player
  cry "Orthworm"
  text "The sand heaves. A length of steel as thick as a tree trunk rears up out of the ground, its feelers glowing red!"
  wildbattle "Orthworm" 29 titan defense
  text "The Titan Orthworm grinds its segments, burrows into the sand and bursts out again at the cutting's far end!"
  walkto titan_orthworm lurking_steel_ramp_foot fast
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_ORTHWORM_FLED
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 6
  endscene
  end


# Up on the ledge: the Titan at its Herba Mystica, the second battle, and Arven come up the ramp to share the herbs
script OrthwormDen
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS == 6 goto Den
  end
label Den
  scene "The Lurking Steel Titan's Herba Mystica"
  faceto player titan_orthworm_den
  text "The Titan Orthworm is coiled round a clump of glowing herbs, swallowing them stalks and all."
  text "Its steel plates ring like a bell and its feelers blaze. It uncoils toward {player}!"
  wildbattle "Orthworm" 29 titan all
  text "The Titan Orthworm is beaten. It shrinks to an ordinary Orthworm and tunnels away under the sand."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_ORTHWORM_BEATEN
  placeat arven_lurking_steel lurking_steel_ramp_foot
  walkto arven_lurking_steel lurking_steel_herbs fast
  faceto player arven_lurking_steel
  speaker "Arven"
  say "Three! I could feel that one through my boots the whole way here. These herbs are bitter. Perfect."
  faceto arven_lurking_steel player
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT == 2 goto Miraidon
  text "Koraidon ate the sandwich, crouched low, and sprang straight up higher than Arven's head."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_TUSK
  goto Strength
label Miraidon
  text "Miraidon ate the sandwich, sank on its legs, and shot straight up higher than Arven's head."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_TREADS
label Strength
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_MOUNT_HIGH_JUMP
  text "Some of its old strength has come back. It can leap high again! (Riding, the jump button now clears ledges twice as tall.)"
  say "The next Titan's in the dusty basin north of Cortondo. People say the ground shakes when it runs."
  say "That's a long way west. Good thing your ride's feeling better."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 7
  endscene
  end
