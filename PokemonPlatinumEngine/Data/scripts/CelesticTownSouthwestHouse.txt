# The black belt has no use for a watch and gives the Pokétch's Analog Watch app away, once: the app itself is what
# says it was given. His Happiny has its cry.


script Watch
  if poketchapp AnalogWatch goto Have
  say "I must bear down and train. To someone who trains, the hour means nothing." "So what would I want with a watch? Here, you have it!"
  fanfare keyitem
  poketchapp AnalogWatch
  text "{player} added the Analog Watch app to the Pokétch!"
label Have
  say "Hands or numbers: which sort of watch do you like best?" "Myself, I tell the time by an hourglass."
  end
