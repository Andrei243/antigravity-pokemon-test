# The Battle Pike's hall of three doors (plan 14 · B10). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# the beats of Emerald's Battle Pike (BattleFrontier_BattlePikeThreePathRoom): before every room the attendant senses what
# waits behind one of the three doors, and the player chooses one.
#
# The round lives between the desk and the rooms as the host's Pike (Models/Frontier/BattlePike.cs; `pike`), and is not
# saved: a game closed in the middle of one finds no round here, and the next begun at the desk ends the streak. The door
# south leads back to the annex's lobby; a round walked out of that way is given up.


script OnEnter
  pike active
  if result == 0 end
  place player 6 9 up
  pike hint
  if result == 4 goto Queen
  pike hintdoor
  speaker "Attendant"
  if result == 1 goto Middle
  if result == 2 goto Right
  say "Room {number} of fourteen. Something stirs behind the door on the left..."
  goto Sense
label Middle
  say "Room {number} of fourteen. Something stirs behind the middle door..."
  goto Sense
label Right
  say "Room {number} of fourteen. Something stirs behind the door on the right..."
label Sense
  pike hint
  if result == 0 goto Nostalgia
  if result == 1 goto Whispering
  if result == 2 goto Creatures
  say "I can feel people waiting in there. Someone eager, I think."
  end
label Nostalgia
  say "It feels nostalgic, somehow. Like something from long ago."
  end
label Whispering
  say "I can hear whispering. Quiet voices, just behind the wall."
  end
label Creatures
  say "I sense Pokémon in there. Strong ones, and restless."
  end
label Queen
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Room {number}... Oh! The same presence fills every door at once."
  say "The Pike Queen is waiting. Whichever door you take, you'll meet her."
  end


# The three doors in the north wall: the room behind the one walked into is drawn, and the player goes through
script LeftDoor
  pike active
  if result == 0 end
  pike door 0
  call Through
  end

script MiddleDoor
  pike active
  if result == 0 end
  pike door 1
  call Through
  end

script RightDoor
  pike active
  if result == 0 end
  pike door 2
  call Through
  end

script Through
  fade out 0.4
  warp "BattlePikeRoom" 6 9 up
  fade in
  return
