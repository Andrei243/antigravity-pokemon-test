# Route 125 (plan 21 · H8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/Route125/scripts.inc). Ernest, the sailor, swaps numbers for the PokéNav once beaten.


script Ernest
  if not defeated self goto Fight
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_HOENN_ERNEST goto Known
label Ask
  speaker "Ernest"
  say "That was a fine battle, as rough and fair as the open sea. Let's swap numbers, shall we?"
  ask "Register Ernest in the PokéNav's Match Call?"
  if no goto Refused
  addcontact hoenn_ernest
  say "Aye. I'll ring you when the sea has a story to tell."
  end
label Refused
  say "No? Then may the wind be at your back."
  end
label Known
  trainerline after
  end
label Fight
  trainerline before
  battle self
  goto Ask
