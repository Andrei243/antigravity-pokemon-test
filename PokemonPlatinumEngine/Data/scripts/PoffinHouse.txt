# The Poffin House in Hearthome City (plan 06 · R14c), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_poffin_house.s, scripts_poffin_common.s).
#
# The cook by the near table asks whether the player wants to cook: with a Poffin Case (the Pokémon Fan Club's
# chairman gives it) and a Berry in the bag, the bag opens on its Berries and each one chosen is stirred into a Poffin
# (UI/PoffinCookingScreen.cs, Models/PoffinPot.cs), until the player stops. Cooking in a group needs wireless play,
# which this game hasn't yet (plan 07), so only cooking alone is offered. The reporter of the TV's Poffin corner waits
# for plan 08's programmes.


# The cook (the original's common script 9400, PoffinCommon_PokefanF)
script Cook
  speaker "Cook"
  say "Hello there! This is where Berries are cooked into Poffins."
label Menu
  choose "Would you like to do some cooking?" "COOK" "INFO" "EXIT"
  if result == 0 goto Cook
  if result == 1 goto Info
  goto Bye
label Info
  say "Choose a Berry from your Bag and drop it into the pot, then get stirring!"
  say "An arrow tells you which way to stir. Mind your speed: stir too slowly and the batter burns..."
  say "...stir too quickly and it slops over the side. Do it well, and out comes a tasty Poffin!"
  say "Cooks can share a pot, up to four of them, and the more there are, the more Poffins each takes home."
  goto Menu
label Cook
  if not item "Poffin Case" goto NoCase
  poffin check
  if result == 1 goto NoBerry
  if result == 2 goto Full
  wait 0.5
  poffin cook
  goto Bye
label NoCase
  say "Oh, I'm sorry, dear. You'll need a Poffin Case to keep the Poffins you cook."
  end
label NoBerry
  say "Oh dear, you haven't a single Berry to cook!"
  goto Bye
label Full
  say "Your Poffin Case is full to the brim!"
  goto Bye
label Bye
  say "Come back whenever you feel like cooking!"
  end


# By the window, the original's idol: how to stir through the three stages
script Idol
  say "Hi there! Let's get cooking!"
  say "Want my secret for Poffins your Pokémon will love? At the start, while it's runny, stir gently so nothing spills."
  say "Once it starts to thicken, stir hard, in big circles!"
  say "And at the very end, make a wish for something yummy and whisk away like mad!"
  end


# At the long table: rare Berries, and more cooks round the pot
script OldMan
  say "Got some Berries with you? The rarer the Berry, the tastier the Poffin."
  say "And the more cooks round a pot, the better it turns out."
  end


script OldWoman
  say "A good meal puts a spring in anyone's step, people and Pokémon alike."
  say "We're not so very different, are we?"
  end


# The girl at the near table: cooking with friends (the original's Wi-Fi Club)
script SchoolKid
  say "When my friends come round we all stir the same pot. Everybody gets more Poffins that way!"
  end
