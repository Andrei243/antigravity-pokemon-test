# Ultra Plant (plan 23 · Z8), a world of Ultra Space. The lines are our own, on Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ultra Space and the Battle Royal Dome"): no picture, map, line or
# script of the games is read. It is come to through the Ultra Wormhole over the Altar's dais (poni_altar.txt) and left
# by the one it is come in by (common.UltraWayHome). Its Ultra Beast, Xurkitree, waits at 32,16: met as a wild Pokémon
# after a staged beat, at level 60, and back each time the world is come to until it is caught
# (FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_XURKITREE); FLAG_ALOLA_MET_XURKITREE says it has been met, and the first meeting
# counts in VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA_MET, which the Lightseekers count to seven at the Altar.


script OnEnter
  effect rift 32 55 hold
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_XURKITREE end
  text "A hall of metal, humming. Lamps burn along its corridors, though nobody tends them."
  end


script Beast
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "Xurkitree"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 32 16 1.0
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect beam 32 16 wait
  cry "Xurkitree"
  text "The cables over the floor stood up, and they weren't cables. A tangle of light and wire came crackling down the hall!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Xurkitree" 60
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_XURKITREE
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_XURKITREE addvar VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA_MET 1
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_XURKITREE
  hide self
  end
