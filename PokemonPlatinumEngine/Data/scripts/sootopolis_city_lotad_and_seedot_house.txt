# A house in Sootopolis City (plan 21 · H8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/SootopolisCity_LotadAndSeedotHouse/scripts.inc).
#
# Two brothers, each sure his favourite is the bigger: the elder measures any Seedot he is shown, the younger any
# Lotad, each against a record of his own (Emerald's VAR_SEEDOT_SIZE_RECORD and VAR_LOTAD_SIZE_RECORD; nothing until one
# has been measured, so the first one shown is the biggest yet). A bigger one than the record earns an Elixir and
# becomes the record (Models/SizeContest.cs; sizes in centimetres, docs/mechanics/rulings.md). The bag always has room.


script SeedotBrother
  face self player
  speaker "Hiker"
  say "Do you know Seedot? You hardly ever see one in Sootopolis. I love big Seedot. The bigger the better!"
  say "My little brother says Lotad are bigger. Silly! Seedot are bigger, everyone knows that."
  if not has "Seedot" goto None
  say "Huh? You have a Seedot with you? P-please, show me!"
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto None
  team size "Seedot" VAR_SEEDOT_SIZE_RECORD
  if result == 3 goto Other
  if result != 2 goto Smaller
  say "{size}! Wow, that's a big one! It might even beat the Lotad my brother saw!"
  say "Thanks for showing me. Here's my thanks!"
  give "Elixir"
  team size keep VAR_SEEDOT_SIZE_RECORD
  say "The biggest Seedot ever! Bigger than any Lotad!"
  end
label Smaller
  say "{size}, is it? Hmm... I've seen a bigger Seedot than that."
  end
label Other
  say "Well, this is something... But it isn't a Seedot!"
  end
label None
  say "No big Seedot? That's a shame. If you find a big one, come and show me."
  end


script LotadBrother
  face self player
  speaker "Fisherman"
  say "Do you know Lotad? They're rare in Sootopolis. I love big Lotad, I mean really love them!"
  say "My big brother says Seedot are bigger. Wrong! Everybody knows Lotad are way bigger."
  if not has "Lotad" goto None
  say "Huh? Have you got a Lotad? P-please show me!"
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto None
  team size "Lotad" VAR_LOTAD_SIZE_RECORD
  if result == 3 goto Other
  if result != 2 goto Smaller
  say "{size}! Wow, that is big! It might be bigger than the huge Seedot my brother saw!"
  say "Thanks for showing me! Here's my thanks!"
  give "Elixir"
  team size keep VAR_LOTAD_SIZE_RECORD
  say "The biggest Lotad ever! Bigger than any Seedot!"
  end
label Smaller
  say "{size}? Hmm... I've seen a bigger Lotad than this one."
  end
label Other
  say "Well, isn't that something! But it's not a Lotad!"
  end
label None
  say "No big Lotad? How disappointing... If you get a big one, come and show me!"
  end
