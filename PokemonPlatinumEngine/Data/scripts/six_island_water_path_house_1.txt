# A house by the Water Path on Six Island (plan 19 · K9, parts b and c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/SixIsland_WaterPath_House1/scripts.inc): a woman who measures any
# Heracross of the team she is shown and keeps the biggest (Models/SizeContest.cs, which is the original's own
# arithmetic; her record is VAR_HERACROSS_SIZE_RECORD, nothing until she has measured one). A Heracross bigger than
# her record earns a Nest Ball, every time, and becomes the record; the first one shown always does, as in FireRed,
# whose record begins at nothing. Sizes are told in centimetres here (docs/mechanics/rulings.md). The original gives
# nothing and keeps nothing when the bag is full; the bag here always has room.


script Beauty
  face self player
  speaker "Beauty"
  if not has "Heracross" goto Longing
  say "Oh! Oh my! Is that a Heracross with you?" "I simply must know how big it is. May I measure it? Please?"
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Denied
  team size "Heracross" VAR_HERACROSS_SIZE_RECORD
  if result == 3 goto Other
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_NEST_BALL_FROM_WATER_PATH_HOUSE_1 goto Biggest
  if result == 0 goto Smaller
  if result == 1 goto Same
  goto Biggest
# SixIsland_WaterPath_House1_EventScript_ShownBigHeracross
label Biggest
  say "{size}! A horn to horn length of {size}!" "That is the finest Heracross I have ever been shown." "Here, you must take this. A splendid Pokémon deserves a splendid ball."
  give "Nest Ball"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_NEST_BALL_FROM_WATER_PATH_HOUSE_1
  team size keep VAR_HERACROSS_SIZE_RECORD
  say "I shall write it on my chart at once. Do come back if you find a bigger one!"
  end
# SixIsland_WaterPath_House1_EventScript_LoveItNeedItHeracross
label Longing
  say "Heracross are the most marvellous Pokémon! The bigger the horn, the better." "Bring one to my door and I'll measure it for you, on my word."
  end
label Same
  say "{size}... and my chart says {record}." "The very same, to the millimetre! How uncanny."
  end
label Smaller
  say "This one comes to {size}. A fine Heracross, truly." "But the chart says {record}, and the chart does not lie."
  end
# SixIsland_WaterPath_House1_EventScript_ThisWontDo
label Other
  say "This won't do at all. I measure Heracross and nothing else!"
  end
label Denied
  say "No? Oh, how disappointing. I had my tape ready and all."
  end


# The chart on the wall (SixIsland_WaterPath_House1_EventScript_SizeRecord)
script Chart
  team record "Heracross" VAR_HERACROSS_SIZE_RECORD
  if result == 0 goto Blank
  say "A chart for writing down Heracross sizes. The biggest so far: {record}."
  end
label Blank
  say "A chart for writing down Heracross sizes. Nothing is written on it yet."
  end
