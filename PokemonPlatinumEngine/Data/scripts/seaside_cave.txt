# The Seaside Cave (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/seaside_cave.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Rocky the black belt (12) and his three Roggenrola guard the way in from Undella Bay: beaten, they go off to train
# (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROCKY). The Crustle (9) that lies like a great boulder before the way out to Route 21's east beach wakes only
# to the Colress Machine (FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE, Colress's gift on Route 22): it battles at level 42 and scuttles off, caught
# or not, and the machine breaks (FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE_BROKE).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  end


script Rocky
  face 12 player
  speaker black_belt "Rocky"
  say "Hm! If you want past here, you'll have to beat me and my Roggenrola. We're as sturdy as rock!"
  say "But my Roggenrola and I only battle strong Trainers. Can you break our rock-hard will?"
  battle 12
  say "Hm! Your strength is the real thing! My Roggenrola and I must become sturdier still. We're off to train. Farewell!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 12
  hide 8
  hide 10
  hide 11
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROCKY
  fade in
  end


script Crustle
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE goto Machine
  text "A great boulder blocks the way. It doesn't look as if any Pokémon could move it..."
  end
label Machine
  ask "It's a great boulder... Use the Colress Machine on it?"
  if no end
  scene "The Crustle in the Seaside Cave"
  text "{player} switched on the Colress Machine! It hums, and the boulder begins to shake..."
  text "It wasn't a boulder at all! A Crustle was sleeping in its rock!"
  cry "Crustle"
  wildbattle "Crustle" 42
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CRUSTLE
  hide self
  speaker none
  text "The Colress Machine gave a pop and a puff of smoke. It's broken..."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE_BROKE
  endscene
  end
