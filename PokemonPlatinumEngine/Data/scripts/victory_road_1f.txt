# Victory Road's first floor (plan 01 · M10, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on the original's beats (scripts_victory_road_1f.s).
#
# A collector stands in the way through to the cave's back rooms and Route 224 beyond them. The original's arrival
# script takes him away once the Hall of Fame has been entered (FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED) and the National Pokédex is in
# hand; here the Hall of Fame alone moves him, until the professor's gift of the National Pokédex is something a
# script can ask about (plan 02 · S15 adds that question to this line).

script OnEnter
  # VictoryRoad_OnTransition (the flag is the original's; Fly doesn't come here)
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_VICTORY_ROAD
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_COLLECTOR
  end


# The collector (the original's script 2). His second line is for a Champion whom he still keeps out, which in the
# original is one without the National Pokédex: here nobody hears it until this floor's arrival asks for that too
# (plan 02 · S15), since the Hall of Fame alone takes him away.
script Collector
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Champion
  say "There's nothing down this way for you, not yet. Your road runs up and out, to the Pokémon League!"
  say "Come back when you've a Champion's name to your own. Then we'll see about the caves behind me."
  end
label Champion
  say "A Champion! Then you'll want what lies behind me: Pokémon by the dozen that Sinnoh has never seen."
  say "But a Pokédex that stops at Sinnoh would be wasted on them. Have the professor see to yours first, and I'll stand aside."
  end
