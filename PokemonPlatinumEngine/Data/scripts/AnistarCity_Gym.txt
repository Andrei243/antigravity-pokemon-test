# Anistar City's Gym (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): Olympia, a seer, battles for the
# Psychic Badge, Kalos's seventh (`givebadge psychic`), once the Fairy Badge is won (VAR_KALOS_STORY 26); then every screen in Kalos shows
# Lysandre, who says he will end the world as it is, and Olympia sends the player to his café in Lumiose City.
# VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 26 becomes 27 here; Lysandre leaves his café's table for the
# laboratory under it (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_LYSANDRE_CAFE, LumioseCity_LysandreCafe.txt). Her team is Kalos's trainers.json's
# kalos_olympia_1.


script Olympia
  face olympia player
  speaker "Olympia"
  if badge psychic goto Won
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 26 goto Later
  scene "Olympia"
  say "The stars said you would come today, at this very hour. I am Olympia."
  say "They did not say who would win. Fate is a thread. Let us see which way it pulls."
  battle self as "kalos_olympia_1"
  say "Ah. The thread pulled your way. It so often does, for those who pull back."
  say "The Psychic Badge is yours."
  givebadge psychic
  text "{player} received the Psychic Badge from Olympia!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_ANISTAR_GYM
  defeat "kalos_psychic_lucile"
  defeat "kalos_psychic_aurele"
  give "TM29"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_OLYMPIA
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY != 26 goto After
  say "Wait. Something dark is coming. Look at the screen..."
  speaker "Lysandre"
  say "People of Kalos. I am Lysandre. This world grows ugly, crowded with those who take and never give."
  say "Team Flare will make it beautiful again. The ultimate weapon will wake, and only the chosen will remain."
  speaker "Olympia"
  say "His café in Lumiose City. That is where the thread leads. Go, {player}, and quickly."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_LYSANDRE_CAFE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 27
  objective kalosLabs
label After
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "The stars say you are not yet ready to face me. Visit Laverre's Gym first, and its tree."
  end
label Won
  say "The stars over Kalos are troubled. Follow the thread, {player}."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge psychic goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Olympia's Psychic types fear bugs, ghosts and the dark. Choose well!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Olympia! I bet she saw that coming."
  end
