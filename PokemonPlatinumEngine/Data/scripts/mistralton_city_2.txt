# The boy (3) tells of the kids' treasure once the Jet Badge is won: he clears FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_RUNWAY_TREASURE and the
# ball lies at the runway's edge (mistralton_city.txt) until it is picked up (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM40).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script GreenhouseBoy
  face 3 player
  speaker "Boy"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM40 goto Done
  if not badge jet goto Wait
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_RUNWAY_TREASURE goto Told
  say "Whoa! That's a Jet Badge! You beat Skyla! Okay, I'll tell you something cool: we left our treasure at the edge of the runway!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_RUNWAY_TREASURE
  say "It's a TM, and it's TM40, Aerial Ace! We hope it's the key to your next win!"
  end
label Wait
  say "If you have a Badge from Mistralton, I'll tell you something cool!"
  end
label Told
  say "Our treasure's at the edge of the runway! Go and get it!"
  end
label Done
  say "Aerial Ace always hits its target! I hope it comes in handy!"
  end
