# The boy at the table gives a Great Ball, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_OREBURGH_CITY_EAST_HOUSE_2F_GREAT_BALL), and goes
# straight on to what he thinks about Poké Balls, as the original does.


script GreatBall
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_OREBURGH_CITY_EAST_HOUSE_2F_GREAT_BALL goto Thinking
  say "Wow... Your Pokémon's eyes are positively sparkling! That makes me so happy. Here, I want you to have this!"
  give "Great Ball"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_OREBURGH_CITY_EAST_HOUSE_2F_GREAT_BALL
label Thinking
  say "Pokémon spend so much of their time inside their Poké Balls, don't they?" "I keep wondering which kind of ball each one would like best."
  end
