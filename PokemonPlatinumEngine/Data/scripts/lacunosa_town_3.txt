# The storyteller's house in Lacunosa Town (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/lacunosa_town.s and lacunosa_town_3.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_LACUNOSA is the town's count (lacunosa_town.txt has it whole): 1 as the player is brought in by Professor Juniper and
# Bianca, 2 once the tale has been told and they have gone. The old woman (0) tells it again to anyone who asks.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LACUNOSA != 1 end
  scene "The old tale of Lacunosa Town"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_STORYTELLER
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_STORYTELLER
  place 1 5 5 right
  place 2 7 5 left
  show 1
  show 2
  walk player up 3
  face 0 player
  speaker "Storyteller"
  say "You must be the ones who came to hear the old tale of Lacunosa Town."
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "That's right. Please, tell us."
  speaker "Storyteller"
  say "All right, my dears. Please sit and listen."
  call UnovaLacunosaTale
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Wasn't that an interesting folktale? We can't say what the Pokémon was, but the power in the story is remarkable!"
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "I know... A power that can freeze everything around it. It could rival the legendary dragons!"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Yes, Bianca. It's rather like the dragons I told you of in Lentimas Town, who scorched Unova long ago."
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Professor, do you think the Pokémon from the old story and the legendary dragons could be connected?"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "The meteorite."
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "The meteorite?"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "The dragons were woken from stones, the Light Stone and the Dark Stone. Suppose the meteorite in the tale and those stones were one and the same..."
  say "Remember that elements from the same age were found in the Dragonspiral Tower, where the dragons slept, and the pieces start to fit."
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "If your theory's right, it must be a really strong Pokémon. Why would it come out only at night, though? Maybe it doesn't like sunlight?"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "We'd have to look into it more deeply before I could say anything about that."
  say "Now that I think of it, the name Lacunosa may come from lacunosus clouds, clouds that look like a net or a fence. I wonder whether the name is related."
  say "Oh, sorry, I've rambled on, haven't I? {player}, could you ask Drayden about this, if you get the chance?"
  say "I'm going to do a little fieldwork. Bianca, you'll help me!"
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Sure thing!"
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "Oh, one more thing. Opelucid City's mayor, Drayden, wrestles with his Pokémon to make them tough. Don't be surprised!"
  walk 1 down 3 right 1
  hide 1
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_STORYTELLER
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Professor Juniper, wait up!"
  walk 2 down 3 left 1
  hide 2
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_STORYTELLER
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_LACUNOSA
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LACUNOSA 2
  endscene
  end


script TheOldTale
  face 0 player
  speaker "Storyteller"
  ask "Do you want to hear that old tale of Lacunosa Town again? It always takes me a little time to tell."
  if no goto NotNow
  call UnovaLacunosaTale
  end
label NotNow
  say "Oh, fine, fine. Come back when you have time."
  end
