# The Battle Frontier's grounds (plan 01 · M10, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_battle_frontier.s).
#
# The Battle Tower's doors are open (plan 06 · R18a), the Battle Factory's (R18b) and the Battle Hall's and the
# Battle Castle's (R18c); the Battle Arcade's are shut until its inside is built (R18d). The Exchange Service
# Corner's two booths trade Battle Points for prizes; the third, the scratch-off cards',
# waits for its cards. The reporter whom the original's arrival script moves about the grounds waits for the
# television programmes (plan 08).

# The two booths that trade Battle Points for prizes (the original's scripts 23 and 25: PokeMartFrontier): the west
# one vitamins and things to hold, the east one Technical Machines. One prize to a visit: the question is asked and
# the talk ends, where the original's counter stays open.
script ExchangeItems
  face self player
  say "Welcome to the Exchange Service Corner, where Battle Points are traded for prizes!"
  frontier prize items
  call ExchangeTrade
  end

script ExchangeMachines
  face self player
  say "Welcome to the Exchange Service Corner! This booth trades Battle Points for Technical Machines."
  frontier prize machines
  call ExchangeTrade
  end

# What follows a booth's question: the prize chosen is traded for, if the points are there.
script ExchangeTrade
  if result == 0 goto Bye
  frontier buy
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  text "{player} traded {price} BP for the {item}."
  say "Thank you! We hope to see you again."
  end
label Short
  say "I'm sorry. You don't have the Battle Points for that."
  end
label Bye
  say "We hope to see you again."
  end


# The way into the Battle Factory (the original's script 37, BattleFrontier_CoordEvent_EnterBattleFactory): whoever
# steps under its porch is walked on in to the head of the lobby's stairs.
script EnterBattleFactory
  walk player right 2 fast
  sound "door_slide"
  warp "BattleFactory" 1 7 right
  end


# The ways into the Battle Hall and the Battle Castle (the original's scripts 34 and 35), which stand on the west of
# the grounds: whoever steps under either porch is walked on in to the mat inside its lobby's door (plan 06 · R18c).
script EnterBattleHall
  walk player left 2 fast
  sound "door_slide"
  warp "BattleHall" 25 9 left
  end

script EnterBattleCastle
  walk player left 1 fast
  sound "door_slide"
  warp "BattleCastle" 21 9 left
  end


# The third booth, which sells scratch-off cards for a Battle Point each (the original's common script 10490).
script ScratchCards
  face self player
  say "Scratch-off cards! One Battle Point a card, and every card a chance at a prize."
  say "...Or there will be, as soon as the cards come in. Try me again another day."
  end


# The statues either side of the way in from the gate (the original's scripts 31 and 32).
script StatueWest
  text "A statue of a trainer holding a Poké Ball high, with a Pokémon just caught inside it."
  end

script StatueEast
  text "A plate on the statue's foot gives it a name: Proud of the Catch."
  end


# The model in the middle of the plaza (the original's script 33).
script ScaleModel
  text "A model of the whole Battle Frontier, small enough to walk round: the tower, the four halls and the plaza between them."
  end
