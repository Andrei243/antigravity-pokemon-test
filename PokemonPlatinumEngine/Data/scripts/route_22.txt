# Route 22 (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats
# (data/maps/Route22/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE22, as FireRed counts it: 1 once Oak has given out the Pokédex, when the rival waits on the road
# west for whoever comes (the area's triggers 0 to 2); 2 that battle fought; 3 and 4 the second, before the League's
# gate, which is plan 19 · K7's (triggers 3 to 5). The first may be lost and the game goes on.


script EarlyRival
  scene "The rival on Route 22"
  music "kanto/rival"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_22_RIVAL
  show route22_rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey! {player}! Over here!"
  face player left
  if var PLAYER_Y == 194 goto Across
  if var PLAYER_Y == 195 goto OneDown
  walk route22_rival down 2 right 7
  goto Met
label OneDown
  walk route22_rival down 1 right 7
  goto Met
label Across
  walk route22_rival right 7
label Met
  face route22_rival player
  say "Heading for the Pokémon League, are you? Forget it. You need Badges to get in, and you've got none."
  say "The guard won't even let you through the gate. Still, how about a battle while you're here?"
  if starter "Bulbasaur" goto VsCharmander
  if starter "Charmander" goto VsSquirtle
  battle route22_rival as "kanto_rival_route22_early_bulbasaur" canlose
  goto After
label VsCharmander
  battle route22_rival as "kanto_rival_route22_early_charmander" canlose
  goto After
label VsSquirtle
  battle route22_rival as "kanto_rival_route22_early_squirtle" canlose
label After
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto Lost
  say "Huh, not bad. My Pokémon just needed a warm-up, that's all."
  goto Leave
label Lost
  say "Ha! Told you. You're nowhere near ready for the League."
label Leave
  heal
  say "I'm off to Pewter City to get myself a Badge. Try to keep up, {player}!"
  music "kanto/rival"
  walk route22_rival left 8 fast
  hide route22_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_22_RIVAL
  music area
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE22 2
  endscene
  end


# The second wait (plan 19 · K7), once Giovanni is beaten and the eight Badges won (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE22 3, which the
# Viridian City Gym sets): the rival comes along the road from the west as before, and battles with the team he means to
# take to the League. This battle can't be lost and the game go on (FireRed's trainerbattle_no_intro). Afterwards he
# heads for the gate ahead of the player (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE22 4).
script LateRival
  scene "The rival before the League's gate"
  music "kanto/rival"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_22_RIVAL
  show route22_rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Hold it right there!"
  face player left
  if var PLAYER_Y == 194 goto Across
  if var PLAYER_Y == 195 goto OneDown
  walk route22_rival down 2 right 7
  goto Met
label OneDown
  walk route22_rival down 1 right 7
  goto Met
label Across
  walk route22_rival right 7
label Met
  face route22_rival player
  say "So you've got all eight Badges too. I knew you'd turn up sooner or later."
  say "I've been training non-stop. My team's the strongest it's ever been, and I'm taking it all the way to the top."
  say "Before I go, I'm going to show you the difference between us. Ready?"
  if starter "Bulbasaur" goto VsCharmander
  if starter "Charmander" goto VsSquirtle
  battle route22_rival as "kanto_rival_route22_late_bulbasaur"
  goto After
label VsCharmander
  battle route22_rival as "kanto_rival_route22_late_charmander"
  goto After
label VsSquirtle
  battle route22_rival as "kanto_rival_route22_late_squirtle"
label After
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "What? I lost? ...Fine. That just means I'll have to win where it really counts."
  say "The Pokémon League is past Victory Road, beyond the gate. The trainers there are the best in the land."
  say "I'll be waiting for you at the top, {player}. Don't keep me waiting too long!"
  music "kanto/rival"
  walk route22_rival left 7 fast
  hide route22_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_22_RIVAL
  music area
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE22 4
  endscene
  end
