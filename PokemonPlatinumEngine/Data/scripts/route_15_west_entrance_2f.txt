# The upper floor of Route 15's gate (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route15_WestEntrance_2F/scripts.inc).
#
# Professor Oak's aide hands out an Exp. Share for fifty kinds caught (FireRed's FLAG_GOT_EXP_SHARE_FROM_OAKS_AIDE),
# and the left pair of binoculars catches sight of a great bird far out over the sea, which the Pokédex then counts
# as seen (FireRed's showmonpic and SetSeenMon: Articuno, whose home is the Seafoam Islands, part b's).


script Aide
  speaker "Aide"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_EXP_SHARE_FROM_OAKS_AIDE goto Given
  if not flag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX goto NoDex
  ask "Hello! I'm one of Professor Oak's aides. Shall I see how many kinds of Pokémon you've caught?"
  if no goto Declined
  if var CAUGHT < 50 goto NotYet
  say "{var:CAUGHT} kinds! That's remarkable. The Professor asked me to give you this."
  give "Exp. Share"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_EXP_SHARE_FROM_OAKS_AIDE
label Given
  say "A Pokémon holding an Exp. Share gains experience from every battle, even if it never steps out."
  end
label NoDex
  say "I'm watching the sea for Professor Oak. You'd be amazed at what flies over it."
  end
label Declined
  say "Very well. Drop by whenever you like."
  end
label NotYet
  say "{var:CAUGHT} kinds so far. Come back once you've caught fifty, and I'll have something special for you."
  end


script LeftBinoculars
  speaker none
  text "Looking through the binoculars... A great blue bird is flying far out over the sea!"
  seen "Articuno"
  end
