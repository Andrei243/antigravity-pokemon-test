# Flint's room, the third of the Elite Four's (plan 01 · M9, part 2d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_pokemon_league_flint_room.s).
#
# As Aaron's room: the door shuts behind the challenger, and the door on to Lucian's passage opens once Flint is
# beaten. He stamps on the spot between the two halves of his greeting, and has other words for a challenger who has
# entered the Hall of Fame before (FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED, which the Hall of Fame's room sets). He is drawn as a
# youngster until plan 11 makes his look. Once Charon has been arrested at Stark Mountain he brings his rematch team
# (plan 02 · S15, part b).


# PokemonLeagueFlintRoom_OnFrame_EnterRoom, by the door's own flag (see Aaron's room)
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_FLINT_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR end
  walk player up 2
  sound "lift_stop"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_FLINT_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  show entrance_door
  end


script Flint
  speaker "Flint"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_FLINT goto Beaten
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Again
  say "So you're the one. Volkner told me a Trainer had lit a fire under him again. The first in years! That was you, wasn't it?"
  say "I owe you for that. He's my oldest friend, and I couldn't do it myself."
  # PokemonLeagueFlintRoom_Movement_WalkFastInPlace: he runs on the spot, faster and faster (here a start and a beat)
  emote self exclaim 0.8
  wait 0.4
  say "But that's that, and this is this! I'm Flint of the Elite Four, and my Fire Pokémon have been burning for a battle like this one."
  say "Come at me with everything you have!"
  goto Fight
label Again
  say "You again! Good! A Champion who keeps coming back to battle is my kind of Champion."
  say "My fire hasn't gone down one bit since last time. Let's see about yours!"
label Fight
  # PokemonLeagueFlintRoom_StartFlintRematchBattle: the stronger team, once Charon is caught at Stark Mountain
  if flag FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN goto Rematch
  battle self as "elite_four_flint"
  goto Fought
label Rematch
  battle self as "elite_four_flint_rematch"
label Fought
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_FLINT
  sound "lift_stop"
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_FLINT_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  hide exit_door
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto WonAgain
  say "...Whew. Burned right down to the ashes. And it feels great!"
  say "I can see what Volkner saw in you. Go on! One more of us, and then the Champion."
  end
label WonAgain
  say "Burned out again! You only get hotter every time, don't you?"
  say "Go on through. I'll be stoking the fire for the next time."
  end
label Beaten
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto BeatenAgain
  say "The next room is Lucian's. He's quiet, and he's the strongest of the four of us. Don't let the books fool you."
  end
label BeatenAgain
  say "Go on, Champion. Lucian's waiting, and he hates being kept from his reading."
  end
