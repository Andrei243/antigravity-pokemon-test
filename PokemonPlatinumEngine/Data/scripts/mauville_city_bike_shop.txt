# Rydel's Cycles in Mauville City (plan 21 · H3, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/MauvilleCity_BikeShop/scripts.inc).
#
# Rydel asks whether the player came from far away: yes, and he gives the bicycle chosen (FLAG_RECEIVED_BIKE); no, and
# he lets it be (FLAG_DECLINED_BIKE) until asked again. Afterwards he swaps one bicycle for the other whenever asked.
# The Mach Bike is fast and climbs muddy slopes; the Acro Bike hops along rails and up bumpy slopes
# (Overworld/FieldMovement.cs, BikeKind).


script Rydel
  face self player
  speaker "Rydel"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_BIKE goto Swap
  if flag FLAG_DECLINED_BIKE goto Ask
  say "Welcome to Rydel's Cycles! I'm Rydel, and I love bicycles more than anything."
label Ask
  ask "Say, you look like a traveller. Did you come all the way here from somewhere far off?"
  if no goto NotFar
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_BIKE
  say "Then you'll need a bike to get around Hoenn! I'll give you one, free. Which kind would you like?"
  say "The Mach Bike is built for speed, fast enough to ride up a muddy slope. The Acro Bike can hop and balance on rails."
  choose "Which bike will you take?" "Mach Bike" "Acro Bike"
  if var RESULT == 1 goto Acro
  say "The Mach Bike! Hold on tight, it really flies."
  give "Mach Bike"
  goto Later
label Acro
  say "The Acro Bike! You'll be doing tricks in no time."
  give "Acro Bike"
label Later
  say "If you want to try the other kind, just come back. I'll swap them for you any time!"
  end
label NotFar
  setflag FLAG_DECLINED_BIKE
  say "Oh, you're local? Then you probably don't need a bike from me. Come back if you change your mind!"
  end
label Swap
  ask "Want to swap your bike for the other kind?"
  if no goto Keep
  if item "Acro Bike" goto ToMach
  if item "Mach Bike" goto ToAcro
  say "Hm? You're not carrying your bike. Is it in your PC? Bring it along and I'll swap it."
  end
label ToMach
  say "Right, the Acro Bike for the Mach Bike. Here you go!"
  take "Acro Bike"
  give "Mach Bike"
  say "Come back whenever you feel like swapping again."
  end
label ToAcro
  say "Right, the Mach Bike for the Acro Bike. Here you go!"
  take "Mach Bike"
  give "Acro Bike"
  say "Come back whenever you feel like swapping again."
  end
label Keep
  say "Glad you like the one you've got! Ride safely."
  end


# The Mach Bike's handbook: a page at a time
script MachHandbook
  choose "Mach Bike Handbook: which page?" "How to ride" "Muddy slopes" "Close"
  if var RESULT == 0 goto Ride
  if var RESULT == 1 goto Slopes
  end
label Ride
  text "\"The Mach Bike is the fastest bike there is. Point it where you want to go and hold on!\""
  end
label Slopes
  text "\"Only a Mach Bike at full speed can ride up a muddy slope. Anything slower can't get up it.\""
  end


# The Acro Bike's handbook: a page at a time
script AcroHandbook
  choose "Acro Bike Handbook: which page?" "Rails" "Bumpy slopes" "Close"
  if var RESULT == 0 goto Rails
  if var RESULT == 1 goto Bumps
  end
label Rails
  text "\"The Acro Bike can hop onto a narrow rail and ride along it. It can't turn while it's up there!\""
  end
label Bumps
  text "\"A bumpy slope is too rough for any other bike, but the Acro Bike can hop its way up.\""
  end
