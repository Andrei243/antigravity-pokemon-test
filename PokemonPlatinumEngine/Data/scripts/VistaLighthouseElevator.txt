# The Vista Lighthouse's lift (plan 02 · S13), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language
# (scripts_vista_lighthouse_elevator.s).
#
# The lighthouse's door in Sunyshore City and the car's open door in the gallery at the top both lead in here, and the
# car carries whoever comes in to the other end at once (the original's OnFrame): two steps in, a turn to the door, the
# ride, two steps out. The original knows which way to go from the floor its rider came from (GetFloorsAbove, on the
# way in it remembers); here the two ends each say so as they are come to (VAR_VISTA_LIGHTHOUSE_FLOOR, a variable of
# our own: 0 set by Sunyshore City's arrival script, 1 by the gallery's), so the car goes to the other one. The door
# in its front wall leads out to the city, for whoever is ever left standing in it.
#
# The ride is the lift's own set piece (plan 09 · L12; the original's PlayElevatorAnimation with its four rounds of
# the car's lights): the indicator's arrow runs the way the car goes, the car hums and shudders, and a chime says
# it has arrived.


# VistaLighthouseElevator_OnFrame_Init
script OnEnter
  walk player up 2
  face player down
  if var VAR_VISTA_LIGHTHOUSE_FLOOR == 1 goto Down
  # VistaLighthouseElevator_ElevatorUp, and its ElevatorAnimation
  anim elevator up 4
  walk player down 2
  sound "stairs"
  warp "VistaLighthouse" 6 10 down
  end
# VistaLighthouseElevator_ElevatorDown
label Down
  anim elevator down 4
  walk player down 2
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 886 791 down
  end
