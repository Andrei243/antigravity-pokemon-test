# VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_19, the route's count as Black 2 keeps it: 0 Bianca waiting past the gate; 1 her lesson given; 2 Alder
# met, gone ahead to Floccesy Town; 3 his training given at home (floccesy_town_2.txt). The people: 5 Bianca
# (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_ROUTE_19), 1 Alder (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ALDER_ROUTE_19).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


# Past the gate (the route's trigger 0, on 0): Bianca shows how a Pokémon is caught, catching one with her own
# Munna, and goes back up the hill. Mom is home again and Hugh's sister with her.
script BiancasLesson
  scene "Bianca's lesson"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_ROUTE_19
  show 5
  face 5 player
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Heeey! Over here!"
  say "This kind of reminds me of the day I set out on Route 1..."
  say "OK, here's how it works. The Pokédex fills in by itself whenever you meet a Pokémon."
  say "And when you catch one, it records even more about it! So, watch closely. I'll catch one now."
  catchinglesson "Patrat" 2 by "Bianca" "Munna" 7
  say "Phew! I did it! OK, the important part: weaken a wild Pokémon first, then throw a Poké Ball."
  say "If it's healthy it'll just break out. A tired Pokémon is much easier to catch!"
  say "You're taking that Town Map down to your friend, right? Floccesy Town is straight down this road."
  say "Bye for now! Meet lots of Pokémon, and catch lots of them, OK?"
  walk 5 up 1
  hide 5
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_ROUTE_19
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MOM_ASPERTIA
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGHS_SISTER
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MOM_HOME
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_19 1
  endscene
  end


# The bottom of the route (trigger 1, on 1): Alder, once Champion of Unova (the overlay stands him on the sand by the
# path, since Black 2's tile for him is shut in by rock here), stops the player, looks the new partner
# over and goes ahead to Floccesy Town to give a lesson.
script AlderMeets
  scene "Alder on Route 19"
  speaker "???"
  say "You there! Young Trainer!"
  walk 1 left 2
  face 1 player
  face player 1
  speaker "Alder"
  say "The name's Alder! I go where the wind takes me, and I tell everyone I meet how good it is to walk with Pokémon."
  say "And who might you be? {player}, from Aspertia City! Ha! Up the hill, eh?"
  say "Your {starter} is a fine-looking Pokémon. But you're not quite a seasoned Trainer yet, are you?"
  say "Right! I'll give you a little training. Follow me to Floccesy Town!"
  walk 1 right 6
  hide 1
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ALDER_ROUTE_19
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_19 2
  endscene
  end
