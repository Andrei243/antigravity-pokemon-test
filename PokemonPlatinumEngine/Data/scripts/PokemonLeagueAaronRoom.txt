# Aaron's room, the first of the Elite Four's (plan 01 · M9, part 2d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_pokemon_league_aaron_room.s).
#
# The challenger walks two steps in and the door shuts behind them (the door is a thing of the map, brought on by its
# flag); the door on to Bertha's passage stands shut until Aaron is beaten. Both doors and FLAG_DEFEATED_AARON are put
# back by the Pokémon Center's own script each time the player comes in there, as the original's are. He is drawn as a
# youngster until plan 11 makes his look. Once Charon has been arrested at Stark Mountain
# (FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN, plan 02 · S15, part b) he brings his rematch team, as the original's does.


# PokemonLeagueAaronRoom_OnFrame_EnterRoom. The original runs it while a local variable is nought, which a save made
# in the room would lose; the door's own flag says the same and is kept.
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_AARON_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR end
  walk player up 2
  sound "lift_stop"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_AARON_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  show entrance_door
  end


script Aaron
  speaker "Aaron"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_AARON goto Beaten
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  say "Hello there, and welcome to the Pokémon League! I'm Aaron, the first of the Elite Four."
  say "Let me ask you something. Do you know why I battle with Bug Pokémon? Because nothing is more beautiful, that's why. The shine of a shell, the hum of wings... They're perfect."
  say "I came to this room to become perfect too, alongside them. So come on! Let me measure myself against you!"
  # PokemonLeagueAaronRoom_StartAaronRematchBattle: the stronger team, once Charon is caught at Stark Mountain
  if flag FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN goto Rematch
  battle self as "elite_four_aaron"
  goto Fought
label Rematch
  battle self as "elite_four_aaron_rematch"
label Fought
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_AARON
  sound "lift_stop"
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_AARON_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  hide exit_door
  say "I lost, with the toughest and most beautiful Bug Pokémon there are... We aren't perfect yet, it seems."
  say "That was a wonderful battle, though. The door is open. Go on to the next room!"
  end
label Beaten
  say "Battling goes deeper than I knew. I'll have to think it all through again with my Pokémon."
  say "Three of us are still ahead of you. Go on!"
  end
