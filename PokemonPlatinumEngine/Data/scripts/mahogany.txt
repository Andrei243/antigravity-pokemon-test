# Mahogany Town (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# With the takeover begun by the last of the Storm, Mineral and Glacier Badges (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 1, which
# mahogany_gym_leader_room.txt sets when the Glacier Badge is the last), Professor Elm rings as the player comes out
# into the town (common.JohtoTakeoverCall), as HeartGold rings the Pokégear on the next map.
#
# A game that cleared the Team Rocket Hideout before Lance gave HM10, Whirlpool (plan 20 · J11) finds it in the bag
# without a word the next time it comes to the town, so no save is left without the HM; nothing else changes.


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HM_WHIRLPOOL goto Takeover
  if not flag FLAG_ROCKET_HIDEOUT_CLEARED goto Takeover
  additem "HM10"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HM_WHIRLPOOL
label Takeover
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 1 call JohtoTakeoverCall
  end


# T28_000: the man who sells Rage Candy Bars by the street's east end (Johto's leftovers; HeartGold's seller, our
# words). He stands on its north row, so the way east to Route 44 stays open, and sells one bar at a time for 300
script RageCandySeller
  face t28_gsmiddleman1 player
  speaker "Man"
  say "Mahogany's famous Rage Candy Bars! Every tourist takes one home. Crunchy, sweet, and good for what ails your Pokémon!"
  choose "One Rage Candy Bar for $300?" "Yes, please" "No, thanks"
  if result == 0 goto Buy
  say "Your loss! They're the talk of Johto, you know."
  end
label Buy
  takemoney 300
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  give "Rage Candy Bar"
  say "Thanks! Come back for another any time."
  end
label Short
  say "Hm? You're a little short. Come back when you've got $300."
  end
