# Rad Rickshaw's Cycle Shop in Eterna City (plan 02 · S6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_cycle_shop.s).
#
# The manager is away until the player frees him from Team Galactic's building (FLAG_HIDE_CYCLE_SHOP_POKEFAN_M); back
# behind his counter, he gives the newest Bicycle (FLAG_RECEIVED_BICYCLE), and from then until the player also has an
# Explorer Kit, Eterna's ways out are watched (VAR_ETERNA_CITY_BLOCK_EXITS_STATE 1, scripts/eterna_city.txt). His
# Clefairy waits for Pokémon that stand in the field (plan 10).


# The manager (the original's script 1).
script Manager
  speaker "Manager"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_BICYCLE goto Manual
  say "You're the one who rescued me! I want to say thank you properly, so here."
  say "It's the very newest Bicycle we have. No, no, I won't hear a word against it. Take it!"
  give "Bicycle"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_BICYCLE
  setvar VAR_ETERNA_CITY_BLOCK_EXITS_STATE 1
  speaker "Manager"
label Manual
  say "Since it's the newest model, let me go through the manual with you."
  say "\"Change gear with the run button. The lower gear isn't so quick, but it's easy to steer.\""
  say "\"The higher gear is hard to steer and hard to stop, but oh, is it fast!\""
  end


# The boy minding the shop (the original's script 3).
script ShopBoy
  speaker "Shop Boy"
  if flag FLAG_TEAM_GALACTIC_LEFT_ETERNA_BUILDING goto Back
  say "The manager went over to Team Galactic's building, and he still isn't back. What can be keeping him?"
  end
label Back
  say "What did Team Galactic even want with other people's Pokémon?"
  end
