# Captain Stern's shipyard (plan 21 · H3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/SlateportCity_SternsShipyard_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Dock, the foreman, is puzzling over the submarine's plans. Shown the Devon Goods, he sends the player on to the
# Oceanic Museum, where the captain has gone (FLAG_DOCK_REJECTED_DEVON_GOODS); and Team Aqua, who were queuing at the
# museum's door, go inside (FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_TEAM_AQUA). Once the goods are delivered he waits for a veteran
# sailor's advice, and Mr. Briney comes to give it once Mossdeep's Mind Badge is won (plan 21 · H8: the Gym's script
# clears FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_STERNS_SHIPYARD_MR_BRINEY, which HoennChapterNine sets, as Emerald's new game does).


script Dock
  speaker "Dock"
  if badge mind goto Briney
  if flag FLAG_DELIVERED_DEVON_GOODS goto Veteran
  if flag FLAG_DOCK_REJECTED_DEVON_GOODS goto FindStern
  say "Hmm... If we put the engine here, the hull bends there... I can't make heads or tails of these plans."
  face self player
  say "Oh, sorry! I'm Dock, Captain Stern's foreman. Parts from Devon? For the captain?"
  say "He's not here. He went over to the Oceanic Museum. Could you take them to him there?"
  setflag FLAG_DOCK_REJECTED_DEVON_GOODS
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_TEAM_AQUA
  end
label FindStern
  face self player
  say "The captain's at the Oceanic Museum, just up the road. Please get those parts to him!"
  end
label Veteran
  face self player
  say "Thanks to those parts, the submarine's coming along. But I could use the advice of an old sea hand."
  end
label Briney
  face self player
  say "Hi! Mr. Briney has joined us to lend a hand. With an old sea hand's know-how, the work's going twice as fast!"
  end


# Mr. Briney, come to help once the Mind Badge is won
script Briney
  speaker "Mr. Briney"
  say "Hm? Ah, it's you! I've decided to help Dock here. A ship needs an old salt's touch, after all!"
  end
