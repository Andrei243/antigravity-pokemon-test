# Lorelei's room, the first of Kanto's Elite Four (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PokemonLeague_LoreleisRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# Spoken to, Lorelei battles (a battle lost ends it there, and the game wakes the player as every battle lost does);
# beaten, she opens the door behind her (FLAG_DEFEATED_LORELEI, which the room's door is opened by). The hall's Pokémon
# Center puts her back (indigo_plateau_pokemon_center_1f.txt). Her rematch team (FireRed's, once the Sevii Islands
# are linked) is played since plan 19 · K9, part b, once FLAG_SYS_CAN_LINK_WITH_RS is set.


script Lorelei
  face lorelei player
  speaker "Lorelei"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_LORELEI goto Beaten
  scene "Lorelei"
  say "Welcome to the Pokémon League. I am Lorelei of the Elite Four."
  say "No one can match me where Ice-type Pokémon are concerned. Not even a trainer who has come this far."
  say "Your Pokémon will be frozen where they stand. Shall we begin?"
  if flag FLAG_SYS_CAN_LINK_WITH_RS goto Stronger
  battle self as "kanto_elite_four_lorelei"
  goto Won
label Stronger
  battle self as "kanto_elite_four_lorelei_2"
label Won
  speaker "Lorelei"
  say "You melted my ice... How remarkable."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_LORELEI
  sound "door_open"
  say "Go on, then. The next room is open. But the three who wait beyond are stronger than I am."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "You're better than I thought. Go on ahead. You've only just had a taste of what the League can do."
  end
