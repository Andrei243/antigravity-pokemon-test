# Team Galactic's headquarters, the second floor (plan 02 · S11, part b), rebuilt to the original's plan.
# docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_galactic_hq_2f.s).
#
# The members' floor: a room of machines, a television room, a nap room whose two beds anybody may rest in, and a
# kitchen where a report on the statue of Eterna City lies on the table. The floor is cut into seven parts, joined
# by warp panels; two of its panels lead to the hall where Cyrus speaks (GalacticHQHall.txt).


# A bed of the nap room (the original's script 1), from beside it or from its foot: a rest heals the team.
script Bed
  speaker none
  ask "It's a bed, neatly made. Lie down for a rest?"
  if no goto Leave
  say "{player} was asleep almost at once..."
  fade out 0.6
  fanfare heal
  heal
  wait 0.4
  fade in
  say "{player} and the Pokémon woke up fully rested!"
label Leave
  end


# The report on the kitchen table (script 6): two pages, each read only if the player goes on.
script Report
  speaker none
  ask "A report lies on the table: \"The Pokémon Statue of Eterna City\". Read it?"
  if no goto PutBack
  say "From the first plate, taken off the statue: \"...DIALGA is made, who gives out time... Joy and sorrow come round by turns, and so do the hours. One river of time carries everyone. Such is DIALGA's gift.\""
  ask "A note in the margin: the words that had worn away were read with a device of the boss's own making. Keep reading?"
  if no goto PutBack
  say "From the second plate, taken off the statue: \"...PALKIA is born, who lays worlds side by side... Neither quite living nor quite not, all things float through space, and come to rest in one same world. Such is PALKIA's gift.\""
  say "The same note in the margin: read with a device of the boss's own making."
label PutBack
  say "{player} put the report back on the table."
  end

# The rooms' signs, the creed, and the notes on the sink and the fridge, read with no name on the box.
script Notice
  speaker none
  sayown
  end
