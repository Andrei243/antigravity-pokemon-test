# Faraway Island's shore (plan 21 · H12, part g). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/FarawayIsland_Entrance/scripts.inc).
#
# The S.S. Tidal's sailor, who brought the player on the Old Sea Map's pass, waits on the shore to sail them back to
# Lilycove's harbour (Emerald's FarawayIsland_Entrance_EventScript_Sailor). The ship itself is not drawn.


script Sailor
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  say "This must be the island on that old map. I've never seen grass grow so tall. I'll stay with the ship."
  ask "Shall we sail back to Lilycove?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Aye. Hold on tight."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed back to Lilycove City..."
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Stay
  say "As you like. I'll be here when you're ready."
  end
