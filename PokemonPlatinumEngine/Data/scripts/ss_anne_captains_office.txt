# The S.S. Anne's captain's cabin (plan 19 · K4, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/SSAnne_CaptainsOffice/scripts.inc).
#
# The captain is too seasick to stand. The player rubs his back, and he gives HM01 Cut (FLAG_GOT_HM01) and the ship
# makes ready to sail: VAR_MAP_SCENE_VERMILION_CITY 1, which ss_anne_exterior.txt reads as the player comes off her.


script Captain
  speaker "Captain"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM01 goto Better
  scene "The seasick captain"
  say "Ooh... I feel terrible... Seasick, of all things. A captain, seasick..."
  text "{player} rubbed the captain's back.\nRub-rub... Rub-rub..."
  fanfare heal
  wait 0.8
  face ss_anne_captain player
  say "Whew! Thank you, I feel much better now. You came to see my Pokémon, didn't you?"
  say "To thank you, I'll teach you a secret technique. A sailor's best friend for clearing a way through the trees!"
  give "HM01"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_HM01
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_VERMILION_CITY 1
  say "That's Cut. Teach it to a Pokémon, and with the right Badge it can cut down small trees outside of battle."
  say "Now, if you'll excuse me, the S.S. Anne must make ready to sail. Safe travels!"
  endscene
  end
label Better
  face ss_anne_captain player
  say "The S.S. Anne will be setting sail soon. You'd better get ashore before we cast off!"
  end
