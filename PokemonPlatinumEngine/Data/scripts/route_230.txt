# Route 230 (plan 29 · LS11a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# A Pokémon Ranger of ours on the shore by the Fight Area, in place of the original's Manaphy Egg, which came from
# another game: she found an Egg drifting in the sea and wants it raised by a trainer who has read of the Prince of the
# Sea. She is there for a Champion who has read Mr. Backlot's book on Manaphy in the Pokémon Mansion
# (FLAG_SKIP_POKEMON_MANSION_OFFICE_BOOK_SEEN_MANAPHY_CHECK, PokemonMansionOffice.txt) until the Egg is taken
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_230_MANAPHY_EGG); her own flag, FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_230_RANGER, is kept by the route's arrival.
#
# Not a battle, so the beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes") has no cry: the bars, the camera on her and the Egg in her
# hands, the sea's sparkles and its waver on the water before her, a line, and the Egg handed over once the scene is
# over. A Manaphy bred with a Ditto gives a Phione's Egg, as Platinum's does (Models/Breeding.cs).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_230_RANGER
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_230_MANAPHY_EGG end
  if not flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED end
  if not flag FLAG_SKIP_POKEMON_MANSION_OFFICE_BOOK_SEEN_MANAPHY_CHECK end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_230_RANGER
  end


script Ranger
  face self player
  speaker sea_ranger "Pokémon Ranger"
  say "Oh! Hello. I'm a Pokémon Ranger. I found this Egg drifting in the sea this morning, all on its own."
  say "It's no Egg I've ever seen. The old books say the Prince of the Sea leaves its Eggs to the currents..."
  ask "You've read of it too, haven't you? Would you raise it for me?"
  if no goto NotNow
  if party >= 6 goto Full
  scene "The Egg from the sea"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera follow ranger 0.8
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 683 431
  effect ripple 683 431
  speaker none
  text "The Egg is cool to the touch, and something inside it seems to sway with the waves."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  fanfare pokemon
  giveegg "Manaphy"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_230_MANAPHY_EGG
  text "{player} received the Egg from the Pokémon Ranger!"
  speaker sea_ranger "Pokémon Ranger"
  say "Thank you! Keep it with you, out by the sea if you can. I'd better get back to my patrol."
  fade out
  hide ranger
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_230_RANGER
  fade in
  end
label NotNow
  say "No? I'll stay here a while. The sea seems to want it to hatch somewhere near."
  end
label Full
  say "Oh, your team is full. Leave one in a PC Box and come back, and the Egg will be waiting."
  end
