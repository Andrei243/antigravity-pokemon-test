# Ryme's hall, the end of her gym test (plan 34 · PD6, its second session). The test is a rap battle, as Scarlet and
# Violet's beat has it: the MC raps three lines and the challenger answers each with the line that rhymes. Each line
# answered right is kept (VAR_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_RAP 1 and 2) and the next follows at once; a line missed ends the
# turn, and the MC gives that same line again next time. Three right and the test is passed
# (FLAG_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_TEST_DONE). A gym trainer stands in the hall, and Ryme at the stage's far end battles a
# challenger who has passed. Our own lines and our own verses.


# The MC's rap battle: three lines, each answered by one of three, the rhyme the right one (never the last answer, which
# the cancel button picks)
script RapBattle
  speaker "MC"
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_TEST_DONE goto Done
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_RAP >= 2 goto LineThree
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_RAP >= 1 goto LineTwo
  say "Yo! You want Ryme? You go through me first. Three lines, you answer each one with a line that rhymes. Ready? Hit it!"
  say "Came up the mountain where the cold wind blows..."
  choose "Answer the MC!" "Got my team behind me, and the whole crowd knows!" "I forgot my scarf at home." "Is there a café here?"
  if result != 0 goto Missed
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_RAP 1
  say "Ha! Not bad! That's one."
label LineTwo
  say "Line two! Here in Montenevera, the snow don't quit..."
  choose "Answer the MC!" "Can we turn the heating up?" "So I came to battle, and I'm hot as it!" "I like snow, actually."
  if result != 1 goto Missed
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_RAP 2
  say "Ooh, the crowd felt that! That's two."
label LineThree
  say "Last one! Ryme's on the stage, and the lights are bright..."
  choose "Answer the MC!" "Could someone dim them a bit?" "So I step up now, and I'm ready to fight!" "Is that the time already?"
  if result != 1 goto Missed
  text "The crowd roared. The MC threw both arms in the air."
  say "That's three for three! You passed Ryme's test! Get up on that stage, the boss is waiting!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_TEST_DONE
  end
label Missed
  text "The beat stumbled. Somewhere in the crowd, somebody winced."
  say "Ohhh, you lost the rhyme! No shame. Talk to me again and I'll give you that line one more time."
  end
label Done
  say "You already got the crowd. Now go get the boss!"
  end

# Ryme's battle for the Ghost Badge. She Terastallizes her last into a Ghost type. Plan 34 · decision 3 at its
# recommendation, as Kofu's and Larry's are (Cascarrafa_GymTest.txt): her team is scaled to the badges the challenger
# holds, its best at the cap of that many badges, so in the academy's order (five held) it is the original's
# (Data/world/paldea/trainers.json: paldea_ryme, paldea_ryme_b0 to _b7)
script Leader
  face ryme player
  speaker "Ryme"
  if badge ghost goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_MONTENEVERA_TEST_DONE goto NotYet
  scene "Ryme"
  say "So you're the one who got my crowd going. I'm Ryme. Montenevera's gym leader, and the loudest thing on this mountain."
  say "My Ghost types keep the beat even after the music stops. Let's see if you can keep up!"
  if badges >= 7 goto Held7
  if badges >= 6 goto Held6
  if badges >= 5 goto Held5
  if badges >= 4 goto Held4
  if badges >= 3 goto Held3
  if badges >= 2 goto Held2
  if badges >= 1 goto Held1
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b0"
  goto Beaten
label Held1
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b1"
  goto Beaten
label Held2
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b2"
  goto Beaten
label Held3
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b3"
  goto Beaten
label Held4
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b4"
  goto Beaten
label Held5
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme"
  goto Beaten
label Held6
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b6"
  goto Beaten
label Held7
  battle ryme as "paldea_ryme_b7"
label Beaten
  say "That's a mic drop if I ever heard one. The Ghost Badge is yours. Wear it loud."
  givebadge ghost
  text "{player} received the Ghost Badge from Ryme!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_MONTENEVERA_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_tas"
  say "Come back for a show some night. I'll save you a spot by the stage."
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "Get past my MC first. Rap battle, three lines. The crowd'll tell you when you've got it."
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "Not enrolled at the academy? Then this stage isn't yours yet. Come back with a student card."
  end
label Won
  say "Back for an encore? The badge is yours already. The crowd still remembers you, though."
  end
