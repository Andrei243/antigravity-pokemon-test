# The Ruins of Alph's northeast chamber (plan 20 · J5). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beat: the stone panel in the floor, its picture of a Kabuto broken into sixteen pieces, to be put back
# together. Solving it opens the floor under the player, who lands in the underground hall with the Unown (plan 20 · J5's leftovers).


# The panel (HeartGold's background event at 6, 6)
script Panel
  if flag FLAG_ALPH_KABUTO_PANEL_SOLVED goto Whole
  text "A stone panel is set in the floor. It shows a Kabuto, broken into sixteen pieces that seem to slide."
  ask "Try to put the picture back together?"
  if no goto Left
  puzzle kabuto
  if result == 0 goto Left
  setflag FLAG_ALPH_KABUTO_PANEL_SOLVED
  # The hall's door on the grounds opens for good, and the player drops into the hall below (HeartGold drops them into
  # a hidden room on the way: the rulings say why this game doesn't)
  setflag FLAG_ALPH_HALL_OPEN
  sound "boulder"
  text "The last piece slid home with a deep click. The floor shuddered, and then it was gone from under your feet!"
  warp "RuinsOfAlph_UndergroundHall" 26 4
  text "You landed in a great hall under the Ruins. Its walls are carved with rows of strange shapes."
  end
label Left
  text "The pieces lie where they were."
  end
label Whole
  text "The panel shows a Kabuto, whole again. The stone around it is warm to the touch."
  end
