# Rose Tower's lobby (plan 33 · GL6), Macro Cosmos's headquarters in Wyndon: on the Darkest Day the chairman's secretary
# Oleana stands beside the lift up to the Energy Plant (off the tile its car puts its riders down on), and the lift runs once she is beaten (its warp is there from
# FLAG_GALAR_OLEANA_BEATEN). VAR_GALAR_STORY 41 to 42 (postwick.txt's count). Our own lines.


script Oleana
  face oleana player
  speaker "Oleana"
  if flag FLAG_GALAR_OLEANA_BEATEN goto Done
  scene "Oleana"
  say "The chairman is busy saving Galar's future, and he does not wish to be disturbed. Least of all by children."
  say "Leon went up and has not come down. Whatever you think you can do up there, you will not be doing it."
  battle oleana as "galar_oleana_1"
  say "...Then go. Perhaps the chairman will see sense from you, since he would not hear it from me."
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_OLEANA_BEATEN
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 42
  objective galarEnergyPlant
  text "The lift's doors slide open behind her."
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "The lift is open. Go, before I change my mind."
  end


# After the Hall of Fame (plan 33 · GL7): Leon has turned Rose's tower into the Battle Tower and runs it, and battles the
# Champion once a day in its lobby on a team past the Cup's (galar_leon_rematch). His first defeat there sets
# FLAG_GALAR_LEON_TOWER_WON, which the objectives read. Galar's ranked ladder (plan 33 · decision 5) is owed
script LeonTower
  face leon_tower player
  speaker "Leon"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_GALAR_LEON_TOWER goto Rested
  say "Champion! Rose's tower is the Battle Tower now, and I'm the one who runs it."
  ask "But the one Trainer I want to battle is you. A rematch, here and now?"
  if no goto Later
  battle leon_tower as "galar_leon_rematch" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_GALAR_LEON_TOWER
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_LEON_TOWER_WON
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Beaten twice! I'll take that as a promise you'll keep coming back. Same time tomorrow, Champion?"
  end
label Lost
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Ha! The tower's master keeps his crown today. Rest up and come back. I'll be right here."
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you're ready. The tower never closes."
  end
label Rested
  say "That was a champion time! Come back tomorrow and we'll do it all over again."
  end


# The Battle Tower's ladder (plan 33 · GL7, part b; plan 33 · decision 5; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Galar's Battle
# Tower ladder"): after the Hall of Fame the lobby's receptionist runs Galar's ranked ladder (Models/Frontier/GalarTower.cs).
# Three Pokémon of different species holding different items go in as copies at Level 50 or under, whole before every
# battle, nothing from the Bag. Ten ranks: three wins take a Trainer up a rank, a loss ends the run and from the fifth
# rank on costs a win; at the Master Rank the third win is Leon's battle. Each win earns Battle Points, handed over as the
# run ends. The ladder is the story's own variables, so a game saved mid-run goes on at its battle. Our own lines.
script TowerDesk
  face tower_receptionist player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if not flag GalarHallOfFame goto Before
  galartower active
  if result == 1 goto GoOn
  galartower rank
  say "Welcome to the Battle Tower! You stand at Rank {number} of ten. Three wins take you up a rank."
  choose "What would you like to do?" "BATTLE" "INFO" "EXIT"
  if result == 1 goto Info
  if result == 2 goto Bye
  galartower enough
  if result == 0 goto NotEnough
  say "Please choose the three Pokémon who will battle for you."
  galartower begin
  galartower choose
  if result == 0 goto Changed
  galartower check
  if result == 1 goto SameKind
  if result == 2 goto SameItem
  say "Very good! Your opponent is waiting. Good luck!"
  goto Run
label GoOn
  say "Welcome back! Your run is waiting for you."
  goto Run
label Info
  say "Three Pokémon battle for you, all of different species and holding different items, at Level 50 or under. Nothing from the Bag may be used."
  say "Three wins take you up a rank. A loss ends your run, and from Rank 5 on it costs you a win as well."
  say "At the Master Rank, the tenth, Leon himself waits for whoever wins twice there."
  galartower beaten
  say "Times you have beaten Leon here: {number}."
  end
label NotEnough
  say "You need three Pokémon of different species, holding different items, to battle here. {banned} may not take part."
  end
label Changed
  galartower finish
  say "Come back when you're ready to battle."
  end
label SameKind
  galartower finish
  say "Two Pokémon of the same species can't battle together here. Please choose again."
  end
label SameItem
  galartower finish
  say "Two Pokémon holding the same item can't battle together here. Please choose again."
  end
label Bye
  say "We hope to see you on the ladder soon!"
  end
label Before
  sayown
  end
label Run
  call Ladder
  end


script Ladder
  scene "A run up the Battle Tower"
  fade out 0.6
  music stop
label Next
  galartower leon
  if result == 1 goto Leon
  galartower rank
  text "Rank {number} of the Battle Tower."
  galartower opponent
  text "{foe} steps onto the court to battle!"
  goto Fight
label Leon
  galartower opponent
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Master Rank! Then it's my turn. Three on three, Champion, and this time I'm not holding anything back!"
  speaker "Receptionist"
label Fight
  galartower battle
  if result == 0 goto Lost
  galartower won
  if result == 2 goto LeonBeaten
  if result == 1 goto RankUp
  ask "A win! Will you battle on?"
  if no goto Down
  goto Next
label RankUp
  text "Rank up! {player} is now at Rank {number}!"
  ask "Will you battle on?"
  if no goto Down
  goto Next
label LeonBeaten
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Beaten at the top of my own tower! That's what a real Champion looks like. Come climb back up to me any time."
  speaker "Receptionist"
  goto Down
label Lost
  galartower lost
  text "The run ends here."
label Down
  galartower finish
  endscene
  fade in
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if result == 0 goto NoPoints
  say "Welcome back! For your run you've earned {number} Battle Points."
  return
label NoPoints
  say "Welcome back! Please battle again soon."
  return
