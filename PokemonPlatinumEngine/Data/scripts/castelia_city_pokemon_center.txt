# Castelia City's Pokémon Center (plan 22 · U5, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# Plan 40 · GE3, ours: the link's receptionist hands out a Linking Cord whenever the bag holds none, as Sinnoh's GTS
# receptionist does (GlobalTerminal1F.Gts), so that Unova's Pokémon that evolve by a trade (Karrablast and Shelmet with
# each other among them) can evolve with nobody else while the link is closed.


script LinkingCord
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "I'm sorry, the link to other players is closed for now."
  if item "Linking Cord" goto Holding
  say "But a Pokémon that grows only through a trade needn't wait for it. A Linking Cord does the same for it. Here!"
  give "Linking Cord"
  say "Use it on the Pokémon as you would a stone. Come back for another once yours is used up."
  goto TradeBoardOffer
label Holding
  say "You still have a Linking Cord. Use it on a Pokémon that grows through a trade, then come back for another."
  goto TradeBoardOffer


# Plan 40 · GE4, ours: Unova's designer, who stands in Castelia City's Pokémon Center where Black 2 keeps its own
# elsewhere. Like the other regions' designers he hands over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned (charm due, charm
# give; Models/Charms.cs) and reads the diplomas the Pokédex has handed out (diploma, DIPLOMAS). With nothing to say
# of either he says his own line (the overlay's).
# Plan 40 · GE8: the trade board kept at the same desk (common.TradeDesk)
label TradeBoardOffer
  ask "There's also the trade board, where Trainers far and wide post the trades they'd like. Would you care to see it?"
  if no goto TradeBoardLater
  call TradeDesk
label TradeBoardLater
  say "Very well. The board changes every day, so do look in again!"
  end


script Designer
  face self player
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  if diploma Ours goto OursDiploma
  if diploma Unova goto OwnDiploma
  if var DIPLOMAS >= 3 goto ManyDiplomas
  if var DIPLOMAS > 0 goto AnyDiploma
  sayown
  end
label Charms
  say "Excuse me, is that a full Pokédex? Every page! I have to shake your hand."
  say "Those of us who draw these Pokémon keep a few charms for the ones who see them all. Please, take this."
label Give
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Give
  say "Fill in another Pokédex, somewhere else, and look me up. I may have another one for you."
  end
label OursDiploma
  say "That diploma isn't for any region's list at all. It's for the Pokémon we added ourselves!" "You saw every one of them? I should hire you as a proofreader."
  end
label OwnDiploma
  say "A diploma for the whole Unova Pokédex! Castelia's towers are tall, but that's taller." "Every page seen. I draw for a living and I haven't seen them all myself."
  end
label ManyDiplomas
  say "Several diplomas on one Pokédex? You must have more pages seen than I have sketches." "Do you ever stay in one place long enough to unpack?"
  end
label AnyDiploma
  say "That's a diploma on your Pokédex! Congratulations! A whole region's list is a lot of pages." "There are Pokédexes in other regions too. Fill in another and come and show me."
  end
