# Rustboro City's Gym (plan 21 · H2, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/RustboroCity_Gym/scripts.inc).
#
# Roxanne battles for the Stone Badge (FLAG_BADGE01_GET is the badge itself, `givebadge stone`) and gives TM39, Rock
# Tomb (FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_ROCK_TOMB). Her Badge counts the Gym's trainers beaten (Emerald's SetGymTrainers), adds one to
# Petalburg's count of Badges (VAR_PETALBURG_GYM_STATE: 2 + four Badges opens Norman's rooms, plan 21 · H4), and sets
# VAR_RUSTBORO_CITY_STATE 1, which brings on the theft of the Devon Goods outside (rustboro_city.txt).


script Roxanne
  face roxanne player
  speaker "Roxanne"
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_ROXANNE goto Rematch
  if badge stone goto Won
  scene "Roxanne"
  say "Hello! I'm Roxanne, the Leader of the Rustboro Gym. I teach at the Trainers' School, too."
  say "I learned everything I know about battling from books and from my Rock-type Pokémon."
  say "Let's see if your lessons were learned in the field. Show me what you can do!"
  battle self as "hoenn_roxanne_1"
  say "So I lost... I see now that there's still so much I have to learn."
  say "You've earned this. Please accept the Stone Badge, the official proof of your victory here."
  givebadge stone
  text "{player} received the Stone Badge from Roxanne!"
  say "With the Stone Badge, your Pokémon can use Cut outside of battle, to clear small trees from your path."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_RUSTBORO_GYM
  setvar VAR_RUSTBORO_CITY_STATE 1
  addvar VAR_PETALBURG_GYM_STATE 1
  defeat "hoenn_josh"
  defeat "hoenn_tommy"
  defeat "hoenn_marc"
label Tm
  say "And please take this TM as well. It's one I use myself."
  give "TM39"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_ROCK_TOMB
  say "TM39 holds Rock Tomb. It hurls boulders at the foe and slows it down, too."
  say "There are other Gyms waiting for you. I hope you'll keep studying as you travel. I know I will!"
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_ROCK_TOMB goto Tm
  say "Battles teach lessons that no textbook can. I'm grateful for ours, {player}."
  # Spoken to with her Badge, she gives her number for the PokéNav (plan 21 · H12, part g; ours)
  if flag FLAG_ENABLE_ROXANNE_MATCH_CALL end
  if not flag FLAG_SYS_POKENAV_GET end
  setflag FLAG_ENABLE_ROXANNE_MATCH_CALL
  say "If you'd like a rematch one day, I'll leave you my number. Do call me."
  addcontact roxanne
  text "Registered Roxanne in the PokéNav."
  end
# The rematch a Match Call asked for (plan 21 · H12, part g; Emerald's RustboroCity_Gym_EventScript_RoxanneRematch, a
# double battle): each time one team stronger, Emerald's TRAINER_ROXANNE_2 to _5, the last again once all are beaten
label Rematch
  if party < 2 goto NeedTwo
  say "You came! I've prepared a new lesson for us both. Two Pokémon each, this time. Shall we begin?"
  if not defeated "hoenn_roxanne_2" goto Second
  if not defeated "hoenn_roxanne_3" goto Third
  if not defeated "hoenn_roxanne_4" goto Fourth
  battle self as "hoenn_roxanne_5"
  goto Rematched
label Second
  battle self as "hoenn_roxanne_2"
  goto Rematched
label Third
  battle self as "hoenn_roxanne_3"
  goto Rematched
label Fourth
  battle self as "hoenn_roxanne_4"
label Rematched
  speaker "Roxanne"
  clearflag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_ROXANNE
  say "I lost again... but every battle with you is a page I'll study. Please call again."
  end
label NeedTwo
  say "A rematch is two Pokémon at a time. Please come back with at least two on your team."
  end


# The guide by the door: advice before the Badge, praise after
script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_RUSTBORO_GYM goto Won
  say "Hey there, rookie! Roxanne uses Rock-type Pokémon. They're tough, but they can't take Water or Grass moves."
  say "Rock moves hit Fire and Flying types hard, so think twice before you send those out!"
  end
label Won
  say "Whoa, you beat Roxanne! You're a natural. The next Gym is a long way off, so take care out there!"
  end
