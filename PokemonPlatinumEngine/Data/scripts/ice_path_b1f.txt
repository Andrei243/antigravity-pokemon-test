# The Ice Path, B1F (plan 20 · J11, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; on HeartGold's beats
# (scr_seq_D39R0102, and the field's own boulder that falls through a hole).
#
# Four holes in the floor. Whoever walks onto one falls through to the floor below (triggers 0 to 3, HeartGold's
# D39R0102_000 to _003, which drop the player onto the tile of B2F each names and set VAR_UNK_40AB 1 for the landing,
# ice_path_b2f.txt). A boulder pushed with Strength onto a hole (a pit of the overlay's) drops through too: it is gone
# from here for good by its own flag (FLAG_HIDE_ICE_PATH_BOULDER_<N>_INIT, the object's), and stands on the ice below
# from then on (FLAG_HIDE_ICE_PATH_BOULDER_<N>_FALLEN cleared), where it stops whoever slides into it. Which boulder
# falls through which hole doesn't matter: the hole decides where it lands, as in HeartGold, whose holes each have the
# fallen boulder of their own number under them. The holes stay open, and are still fallen through.


# D39R0102_000 to _003: the player falls through a hole
script Hole1
  setvar VAR_UNK_40AB 1
  walk player onspot down 2
  call Fall
  warp "IcePath_B2F" 12 12 down
  end

script Hole2
  setvar VAR_UNK_40AB 1
  walk player onspot down 2
  call Fall
  warp "IcePath_B2F" 10 21 down
  end

script Hole3
  setvar VAR_UNK_40AB 1
  walk player onspot down 2
  call Fall
  warp "IcePath_B2F" 20 10 down
  end

script Hole4
  setvar VAR_UNK_40AB 1
  walk player onspot down 2
  call Fall
  warp "IcePath_B2F" 21 23 down
  end

script Fall
  sound "ledge"
  return


# The field's own: a boulder pushed onto a hole drops through it to the ice below
script Boulder1
  hide self
  setflag own
  sound "boulder"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ICE_PATH_BOULDER_1_FALLEN
  setflag FLAG_ICE_PATH_HOLE_1_FILLED
  end
