# The old church in Driftveil City, home of the former Team Plasma (plan 22 · U6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/driftveil_city_6.s,
# read for the order alone).
#
# Once Rood has tested the player at the door (driftveil_city.txt, VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL 2) the house's arrival plays his
# tale: how Team Plasma broke in two, Ghetsis's new Team Plasma, and why the old members live here under Clay's eye.
# Hugh (12) listens, says his piece and leaves; the worker at the Gym's door goes with the tale's end (at 3).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  if var VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL != 2 goto Done
  scene "Rood's tale"
  face 0 player
  speaker "Rood"
  say "Welcome to our home. Everyone here once wore Team Plasma's uniform. Now we look after Pokémon who have nowhere else to go."
  say "Two years ago, Team Plasma meant to part people from their Pokémon. Lord N believed that was right for Pokémon."
  say "But it was Ghetsis who led us, and he only wanted to rule Unova. When Lord N lost his way, Ghetsis's plan fell apart."
  say "Afterwards some of us came to see how wrong we had been. We stayed together, to make up for what we did."
  say "The others went back to Ghetsis. That is the Team Plasma you have met: the same uniforms, and none of Lord N's dream."
  say "Clay, the Gym Leader, gave us this house. He keeps an eye on us, but he never once turned us away."
  face 12 0
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "So you're sorry now. That's fine for you. Saying sorry doesn't give anyone back what Team Plasma took."
  say "Five years ago they stole my little sister's Purrloin. I'm going to find it, and I'm going to stop them. All of them!"
  speaker "Rood"
  say "I understand. If we can help you find it, we will. I promise you that."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...{player}, I'm going on ahead. See you at the Gym!"
  walk 12 down 4 fast
  hide 12
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_PLASMA_HOUSE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_DRIFTVEIL_GYM_WORKER
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL 3
  speaker "Rood"
  say "The Gym Leader has finished his work, I believe. Go and challenge him. Our door is always open to you."
  endscene
label Done
  end


script Rood
  face 0 player
  speaker "Rood"
  say "We cannot undo what we did. We can only do better now, one Pokémon at a time."
  end


script FormerMember
  face 8 player
  speaker "Former Member"
  say "Thank you again for the bridge. Rood says we all need a friend to stand with us now and then."
  end


script Hugh
  face 12 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Former members or not, it's Team Plasma. I'll hear him out. For now."
  end
