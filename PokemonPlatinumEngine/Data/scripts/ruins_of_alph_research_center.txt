# Plan 40 · GE3, ours: HeartGold's Helix Fossil and SoulSilver's Dome Fossil are found smashing the rocks round the Ruins.
# Here a researcher who digs there hands over the one, and on a later visit the other (FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HELIX_FOSSIL and
# FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_DOME_FOSSIL, ours), for Pewter City's museum to bring back to life (pewter_museum_of_science.txt).


script Digger
  speaker "Scientist"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_DOME_FOSSIL goto Done
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HELIX_FOSSIL goto Dome
  say "The carvings on the walls look like letters. There are dozens of shapes, and no two quite alike."
  say "When I'm not reading them, I break up the rocks round the Ruins. Fossils turn up inside, now and then. Take this one!"
  give "Helix Fossil"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HELIX_FOSSIL
  say "I hear the museum in Pewter City has learned how to bring them back to life. Imagine it!"
  end
label Dome
  say "Back again? I broke open another rock this morning, and look what was inside. You have it."
  give "Dome Fossil"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_DOME_FOSSIL
  say "Two kinds of shell from the old sea, so close to the Ruins. The people who built them must have seen these alive."
  end
label Done
  say "The carvings on the walls look like letters. There are dozens of shapes, and no two quite alike."
  end
