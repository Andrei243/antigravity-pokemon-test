#
# Two clerks sell over the west counter (marts.json's veilstone_2f_up and veilstone_2f_mid: the battle items and the
# vitamins), and the third gives away the Pokétch's Counter app. The attendant recommends something: to a regular
# customer one of four at random in the original (CheckIsDepartmentStoreRegular, a count of purchases this game
# doesn't keep), to anyone else the first of them, which is what she does here (docs/mechanics/rulings.md).


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_VEILSTONE_STORE_LIFT_FLOOR 3
  call VeilstoneStoreElevator.Stands
  end


# VeilstoneStore2F_Attendant
script Attendant
  choose "Hello. May I help you with anything?" "WHAT'S GOOD?" "NOTHING, THANKS"
  if result == 0 goto Protein
  say "Please enjoy your visit."
  end
label Protein
  say "Might I interest you in a Protein? It is a drink that raises Attack."
  end


# VeilstoneStore2F_PoketchVendor
script Poketch
  if poketchapp Counter goto Given
  ask "We're giving away a Pokétch app, quite free, as a promotion. The Counter app, over the counter! Would you like it?"
  if no goto Declined
  if not poketch goto NoWatch
  say "There you are. Go forth and count!"
  fanfare keyitem
  poketchapp Counter
  text "{player} added the Counter app to the Pokétch!"
  end
label Given
  say "I do hope that Counter app is proving useful."
  end
label Declined
  say "Not interested? I'll be here if you change your mind."
  end
label NoWatch
  say "Oh! But you have no Pokétch to put it on. Come back when you have one!"
  end
