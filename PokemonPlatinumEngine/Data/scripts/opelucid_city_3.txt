# Drayden's house, upstairs (plan 22 · U8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own. The woman (1) gives
# a Ring Target once to whoever will try to work it out, as Black 2's does (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_RING_TARGET).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpC
  end


script RingTarget
  face 1 player
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_RING_TARGET goto Got
  say "I have an item I don't know how to use. Would you give it a try and see if you can make it work?"
  say "You may be able to master it. Here it is!"
  give "Ring Target"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_RING_TARGET
  say "If a Pokémon holds a Ring Target, it can be hit even by a move that would usually have no effect on it."
  end
label Got
  say "If a Pokémon holds a Ring Target, it can be hit even by a move that would usually have no effect on it. For example, a Normal-type move can hit a Ghost-type Pokémon."
  end
