# player comes in by GameEngine.FurnishGallery), each read on its tile: the Hall of Fame's record opens on that region's
# teams, its first team kept however many came after. EARLIER TEAMS keeps those that entered before a team's region was
# kept, and the circuit's plinth the teams that won the Laurel Circuit's final. The registrar at the east desk begins a
# circuit (Models/ChampionsCircuit.cs); Ismene Quill keeps the records.

script Keeper
  face self player
  speaker "Ismene"
  if flag FLAG_GATHERING_DONE goto Encore
  say "Welcome to the Laurel Gallery. I keep its records: every team every region has crowned, and the first of each above all."
  say "Read a plinth to see its teams. I've read every one of them myself, more times than I care to count."
  end
label Encore
  say "Ah, my star! Tickets for next season are selling nicely. The leaders don't know about the matinees yet."
  end


# The Laurel Circuit's desk (plan 41 · EG4): every finished League fought again in its own rooms at one level, each
# stamped by its Hall of Fame, and then a final of the Champions in the Laurel Court across the avenue
script CircuitDesk
  face self player
  speaker "Registrar"
  circuit running
  if result == 1 goto Running
  circuit count
  if result > 0 goto Again
  say "This is the Laurel Circuit's desk. A circuit takes you back to every League you've won, to fight its Elite Four and Champion again in their own rooms."
  goto Offer
label Again
  say "Back for another circuit? Every one you win raises the next by five levels."
label Offer
  circuit level
  say "This circuit's teams would all meet you at level {result}. Each League you win again stamps your card when you enter its Hall of Fame."
  say "With every League stamped, the final opens in the Laurel Court: all the Champions you've beaten, one after another."
  ask "Will you begin a circuit?"
  if no goto Later
  circuit begin
  say "Then your circuit has begun. Good luck out there, Champion."
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you're ready. The desk will be here."
  end
label Running
  circuit ready
  if result == 1 goto Ready
  circuit stamps
  say "Your circuit is under way. Stamps so far: {result}. Win each League again and enter its Hall of Fame to stamp it."
  end
label Ready
  say "Every League is stamped! The final waits for you in the Laurel Court, across the avenue."
  end
