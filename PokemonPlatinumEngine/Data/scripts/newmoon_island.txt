# Newmoon Island (plan 01 · M10, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_newmoon_island.s).
#
# Arriving sets what the original's does: the island counts as come to, and it shows on the maps from then on (the
# original's EnableHiddenLocation: VAR_HIDDEN_LOCATION_NEW_MOON_ISLAND holds its number for the island, 0x0229).
#
# The sailor on the pier sails back to Canalave City. The way here is the Harbor Inn's dream (plan 29 · LS11a:
# CanalaveHarborInn.txt), which sets the player down on the pier with the sailor away
# (FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_SAILOR); he is back once the island's wood has been come to
# (FLAG_ENTERED_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST, ours, set by newmoon_island_forest.txt), so that whoever walks back out of it,
# Darkrai met or not, can sail home. Afterwards Canalave City's sailor sails here too (canalave_city.txt's Ferry).

script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_NEWMOON_ISLAND
  setvar VAR_HIDDEN_LOCATION_NEW_MOON_ISLAND 553
  if flag FLAG_ENTERED_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST clearflag FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_SAILOR
  end


script Ferry
  face self player
  ask "Not a place to linger, this. Shall we sail back to Canalave City?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Aye, aye! Anchors aweigh!"
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 44 750 left
  end
label Stay
  say "All right, then. Tell me when you want to go. I'll not be wandering off."
  end
