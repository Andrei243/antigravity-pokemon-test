# The Glitter Lighthouse's lift (plan 20 · J9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D27R0108).
#
# The lift joins the ground floor and the light room at the top, and carries whoever comes in to the other end at once
# (HeartGold's car does the same, from the floor it remembers: GetDynamicWarpFloorNo). Here the two ends each say so
# as they are come to (VAR_OLIVINE_LIGHTHOUSE_LIFT_FLOOR, a variable of our own: 0 set by the ground floor's arrival
# script, 1 by the light room's), as the Vista Lighthouse's car does in Sinnoh: two steps in, the ride, out at the
# other end. Its doors are open once Jasmine has stepped aside from the one at the top (FLAG_UNK_1D8).


script OnEnter
  walk player up 2
  face player down
  if var VAR_OLIVINE_LIGHTHOUSE_LIFT_FLOOR == 1 goto Down
  anim elevator up 4
  text "The lift has reached the top of the lighthouse."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "OlivineLighthouse_LightRoom" 8 5 down
  end
label Down
  anim elevator down 4
  text "The lift has reached the ground floor."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "OlivineLighthouse_1F" 8 5 down
  end
