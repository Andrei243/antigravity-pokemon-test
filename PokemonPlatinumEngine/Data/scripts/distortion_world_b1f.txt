# The Distortion World, B1F (plan 02 · S12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_distortion_world_b1f.s). distortion_world_1f.txt says what VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS
# counts.
#
# The first time the player comes down the slab (progress 2) Cynthia is at its foot, wonders whether Giratina will
# show itself, and goes on ahead across the gaps (progress 3). Further on, before the wall that is walked sideways, a
# step (the original's event, progress 3) brings Mesprit's cry and Mesprit itself, which drifts up the wall and is
# gone: the way on (progress 4).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS != 2 hide cynthia
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS != 2 end
  # DistortionWorldB1F_OnFrame_FirstEntry
  face player up
  wait 0.8
  face cynthia down
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "There is no sign of it. Giratina can see us, I'm sure of that. Will it let us see it?"
  say "Mind the gaps between the stones. A good run carries you over. Watch how I go."
  move cynthia left 1 down 2
  wait 0.8
  face player left
  waitmoves
  face cynthia left
  wait 0.4
  walk cynthia left 6 fast
  walk cynthia down 3 fast
  walk cynthia left 3 fast
  hide cynthia
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 3
  end


# The original's DistortionWorldB1F_CoordEvent_Mesprit.
script Mesprit
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 4
  cry "Mesprit"
  speaker none
  say "Kyauuun!"
  place mesprit 12 22 up
  show mesprit
  face player left
  wait 1.0
  walk mesprit up 2
  walk mesprit up 9 fast
  hide mesprit
  face player up
  end
