# The Radio Tower's fourth floor (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D23R0104).
#
# The floor's arrival shows Team Rocket's people (FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1) at the takeover's height
# (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 4), with Mary of Pokémon Talk, her Meowth and DJ Ben herded into a corner by the grunt
# who guards them, and the staff (_2) once it is over (5), when Mary gives the player Bright Powder
# (FLAG_GOT_BRIGHTPOWDER_FROM_MARY). Proton holds the stairs on the floor's east side.


# D23R0104_000 and _008
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_2
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 5 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_2
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER != 4 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  place d23r0104_kurumi 18 12 right
  place d23r0104_tsure_poke_static_meowth 19 12 down
  place d23r0104_gsman2 20 12 down
  end


# D23R0104_004
script Mary
  face d23r0104_kurumi player
  speaker "Mary"
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 4 goto Held
  if flag FLAG_GOT_BRIGHTPOWDER_FROM_MARY goto Thanked
  say "There's my hero! Please, I'd like you to have this, as a thank-you."
  give "Bright Powder"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_BRIGHTPOWDER_FROM_MARY
  say "Give it to a Pokémon to hold. Its glitter dazzles the other side, so their moves miss more often."
  end
label Held
  say "Why is this happening to us? Meowth, do something!"
  end
label Thanked
  say "Listen out for me on Professor Oak's Pokémon Talk!"
  end


# D23R0104_005
script Meowth
  cry "Meowth"
  text "Meowth: Meowth..."
  end


# D23R0104_006: DJ Ben
script Ben
  face d23r0104_gsman2 player
  speaker "Ben"
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 4 goto Held
  say "Do you listen to our music? Pokémon March, the Lullaby... I pick every tune myself."
  end
label Held
  say "Do you know what Team Rocket is missing? Music. Anyone who loved music could never do a thing like this."
  end


# D23R0104_007: the grunt who keeps Mary and Ben in their corner
script Guard
  if defeated "johto_team_rocket_f_grunt_4" goto Beaten
  face d23r0104_rocketw player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Proton's leading this mission, you know. Isn't he just the coolest?"
  say "Who are you, anyway? If you're getting in Proton's way... I mean Team Rocket's way, then I won't have it!"
  battle d23r0104_rocketw as "johto_team_rocket_f_grunt_4"
label Beaten
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Team Rocket and Proton are all I need. Pokémon? Who cares about them?"
  end
