# The Olivine Gym (plan 20 · J9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats
# (scr_seq_T26GYM0101).
#
# Until Amphy is cured (VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE below 2, olivine_lighthouse_light_room.txt) Jasmine is at the
# lighthouse, and so are the two who come to watch her battle: the room's own arrival keeps the three off the floor
# (FLAG_HIDE_JASMINE_IN_GYM and its two, which HeartGold's new game sets and the light room's scene clears). Jasmine
# battles, gives the Mineral Badge (`givebadge mineral`) and TM23, Iron Tail (FLAG_GOT_TM23_FROM_JASMINE). The Gym
# has no trainers of its own. Whichever of Chuck, Jasmine and Pryce gives the last of the Storm, Mineral and Glacier
# Badges starts Team Rocket's takeover (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 1, plan 20 · J11, part b).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE >= 2 end
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_JASMINE_IN_GYM
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLIVINE_GYM_GENTLEMAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLIVINE_GYM_GIRL
  end


# T26GYM0101_000
script Jasmine
  face t26gym0101_gsleader6 player
  speaker "Jasmine"
  if badge mineral goto Won
  scene "Jasmine"
  say "Thank you again for Amphy. Now let me show you the other side of me: a Gym Leader's."
  say "My Pokémon are of steel. They're harder than anything you've faced. Are you ready?"
  battle self as "johto_leader_jasmine_jasmine"
  say "Your Pokémon cut through steel... You're stronger than I imagined. The Mineral Badge is yours."
  givebadge mineral
  text "{player} received the Mineral Badge from Jasmine!"
  if not badge storm goto Said
  if not badge glacier goto Said
  setvar VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 1
label Said
  say "Wear it proudly. Steel only grows harder under the hammer, and so do the Trainers who keep going."
  endscene
label Tm
  say "And please take this. A move I'm very fond of."
  give "TM23"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM23_FROM_JASMINE
  say "TM23 is Iron Tail. It strikes with a tail as hard as steel, and may lower the foe's Defense."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_TM23_FROM_JASMINE goto Tm
  say "Amphy shines every night now. Whenever I see its light out at sea, I think of you."
  end


# T26GYM0101_005
script GymGuy
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge mineral goto Won
  if var VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE >= 2 goto Back
  say "Hey there, challenger! Sorry, the Leader's out. Jasmine's up at the lighthouse, looking after a sick Pokémon."
  say "She won't battle anyone until it's well again. That's just how kind she is."
  end
label Back
  say "Jasmine's back! And she's not the gentle girl you'd think in battle. Her Steel types barely feel a scratch."
  say "Fire, Fighting and Ground moves are your best bet. Good luck, champ!"
  end
label Won
  say "You broke through her steel! I knew there was something about you."
  end


# T26GYM0101_003
script Gentleman
  speaker "Gentleman"
  if badge mineral goto Won
  say "Jasmine is back, and so are we. She battles beautifully. I wouldn't miss it for the world."
  end
label Won
  say "What a battle! Jasmine looked happy, even in defeat. You must have reminded her of something."
  end

  end
label Won

  end
