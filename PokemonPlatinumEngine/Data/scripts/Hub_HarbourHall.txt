# The hub's harbour hall (plan 41 · EG1). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# The harbourmaster at the west desk is the hub's attendant (common.Attendant): its harbour sails to every region
# entered, asking no Hall of Fame (RegionDatabase.LinksFrom). The guide at the east desk crosses a Champion to the doors
# of the regions' battle facilities by `travel to` (GameEngine.CrossTo), as Route 40's guide does: Sinnoh's Battle
# Frontier, Hoenn's Battle Frontier island and Unova's Gear Station, each once that region's Hall of Fame is entered. A
# guide of ours waits at each to cross back (common.HubGuide). Plan 37 · FC7's cups hall joins the list once it stands.


script FacilityGuide
  face self player
  say "I guide Champions to the great battle facilities of the regions, and back here whenever they like."
  choose "Where would you like to go?" "SINNOH'S FRONTIER" "HOENN'S FRONTIER" "THE GEAR STATION" "NOWHERE"
  if result == 3 goto Stay
  if result == 1 goto Hoenn
  if result == 2 goto Unova
  if not flag SinnohHallOfFame goto NotYet
  say "Sinnoh's Battle Frontier it is. Follow me, and keep close."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} followed the guide to Sinnoh's Battle Frontier..."
  travel to "BattleFrontier" 54 51 right
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Hoenn
  if not flag HoennHallOfFame goto NotYet
  say "Hoenn's Battle Frontier island it is. Follow me, and keep close."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} followed the guide to Hoenn's Battle Frontier..."
  travel to "HoennBattleFrontier" 20 67 right
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Unova
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame goto NotYet
  say "Unova's Gear Station it is. Follow me, and keep close."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} followed the guide to Unova's Gear Station..."
  travel to "GearStation" 3 11 left
  fade in 0.8
  end
label NotYet
  say "I'm sorry, I can only take Champions of that region. Come back once you've entered its Hall of Fame."
  end
label Stay
  say "Take your time. I'll be at this desk whenever you'd like to go."
  end
