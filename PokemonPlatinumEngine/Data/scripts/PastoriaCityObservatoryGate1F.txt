# The Great Marsh's observatory in Pastoria City, ground floor (plan 01 · M11, part b), on the original's plan.
# docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_pastoria_city_observatory_gate_1f.s).
#
# The Safari Game is started at the counter: whoever walks up the room toward the door at its back steps onto the
# original's coordinate event before the attendant (trigger 0), is asked, pays the fee and is let into the marsh
# with thirty Safari Balls and five hundred steps (safari start; Models/SafariGame.cs). Turned down, or short of
# the fee, they are sent a step back. The original also turns away whoever has no room left for a Pokémon in the
# team or the boxes; here a catch with no room is the battle's own to refuse.
#
# Coming back through the marsh's door with a game still on, the player is asked whether to leave: yes ends the
# game, no leads back in. A game that ended in the marsh (its last step, its last ball: common.SafariTimeUp and
# SafariOutOfBalls, which set VAR_SAFARI_GAME_STATE to 2) brings the player here too. Either way the cowgirl by the
# wall comes up with the Pokétch's Matchup Checker for whoever caught five Pokémon or more in that one game, once
# (SAFARI_CAUGHT; FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_OBSERVATORY_GATE_1F_MATCHUPCHECKER).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SAFARI_GAME_STATE == 2 goto Over
  if not safari end
  speaker "Attendant"
  ask "You still have time left. Are you sure you want to leave the Great Marsh?"
  if no goto Back
  say "Very well. I'll take the Safari Balls you have left." "Thank you for playing. We hope to see you again!"
  safari end
  goto Out
label Back
  say "Then do enjoy the rest of your Safari Game!"
  warp "GreatMarsh" 68 116 up
  end
label Over
  setvar VAR_SAFARI_GAME_STATE 0
label Out
  walk player down 4
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_OBSERVATORY_GATE_1F_MATCHUPCHECKER end
  if var SAFARI_CAUGHT < 5 end
  walk cowgirl right 4
  face cowgirl up
  face player down
  speaker "Cowgirl"
  say "Well now, how many Pokémon did you catch in that Safari Game? Five at least?" "Ayup! So you did! Isn't that something!" "Here you are, then: the Matchup Checker, for your Pokétch."
  fanfare keyitem
  poketchapp MatchupChecker
  text "{player} added the Matchup Checker app to the Pokétch!"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_OBSERVATORY_GATE_1F_MATCHUPCHECKER
  walk cowgirl left 4
  face cowgirl right
  end


# The original's coordinate event before the counter (script 2)
script Counter
  face player left
  if var PLAYER_X >= 5 call StepWest
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Great Marsh, the home of Pastoria's Safari Game!"
  ask "All you can catch for just $500! Would you like to play a Safari Game?"
  if no goto Away
  say "That will be $500, please."
  takemoney 500
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  safari start
  say "Here are your Safari Balls."
  text "{player} received 30 Safari Balls."
  say "We'll call you when your game is over. Until then, enjoy yourself! Off you go into the wild!"
  walk player up 3 right 1
  face player up
  warp "GreatMarsh" 68 116 up
  end
label Short
  say "I'm sorry. You don't have enough money."
label Away
  walk player down 1
  end

script StepWest
  walk player left 1
  face player left
  return


script Explain
  ask "Welcome to the Great Marsh! Is this your first Safari Game?"
  if no goto Enjoy
  say "You go into the Great Marsh with 30 Safari Balls, and they are the only balls you may throw in there." "The game is over when your last ball is thrown, or when you have walked 500 steps." "Do enjoy the Great Marsh, just as nature left it!"
  end
label Enjoy
  say "I see. Then enjoy your Safari Game!"
  end


script Cowgirl
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_PASTORIA_CITY_OBSERVATORY_GATE_1F_MATCHUPCHECKER goto Have
  say "Howdy. Playing the Safari Game, are you?" "How would you like to play a little game of mine at the same time?" "There's only the one rule, and it's a simple one: catch five Pokémon or more in a single Safari Game." "Manage that, and the Matchup Checker for your Pokétch is yours."
  end
label Have
  say "The Matchup Checker shows how well two of your Pokémon get along." "It can't tell how you and I would get along as friends, mind."
  end
