# The S.S. Tidal's corridor (plan 21 · H12, part g). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/SSTidalCorridor/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE, ours (Emerald's has a halfway state each way): 1 bound for Lilycove, out of Slateport; 2 bound for
# Slateport, out of Lilycove; 3 in at Lilycove; 4 in at Slateport. The desks of both harbours set 1 or 2 as they let the
# player aboard; a rest in the cabin's bed brings the ship in (ss_tidal_rooms.txt). The sailor by the gangway lets the
# player off once she is in, onto the quay of the harbour she has come to (Emerald's ExitLilycove and ExitSlateport, 8, 11
# in both rooms), and the voyage is over (0).


script ExitSailor
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 3 goto Lilycove
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 4 goto Slateport
  say "We're still at sea. Your cabin's down the corridor, if you'd like a rest while we sail."
  end
label Lilycove
  say "We've arrived at Lilycove City. Mind the gangway on your way down!"
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 0
  fade out 0.8
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Slateport
  say "We've arrived at Slateport City. Mind the gangway on your way down!"
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 0
  fade out 0.8
  warp "SlateportCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
