# The S.S. Tidal's corridor (plan 21 · H12, part g). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/SSTidalCorridor/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE, ours: 1 bound for Lilycove, out of Slateport; 2 bound for Slateport, out of Lilycove; 3 in at
# Lilycove; 4 in at Slateport; 5 and 6 halfway to Lilycove and to Slateport (plan 21 · H12, part k: Emerald's
# SS_TIDAL_HALFWAY_LILYCOVE and _SLATEPORT). The desks of both harbours set 1 or 2 as they let the player aboard, and
# the voyage's steps from nought (VAR_CRUISE_STEP_COUNT); a rest in the cabin's bed takes the ship halfway, a second
# brings her in (ss_tidal_rooms.txt), and so does the 205th step taken aboard (Emerald's SS_TIDAL_MAX_STEPS:
# GameEngine.CruiseStep starts ReachedStepCount). The sailor by the gangway lets the player off once she is in, onto the
# quay of the harbour she has come to (Emerald's ExitLilycove and ExitSlateport, 8, 11 in both rooms), and the voyage
# is over (0).


script ExitSailor
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 3 goto Lilycove
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 4 goto Slateport
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 5 goto Halfway
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 6 goto Halfway
  say "We're still at sea. Your cabin's down the corridor, if you'd like a rest while we sail."
  end
label Halfway
  say "We're about halfway there. It won't be long now, but your cabin's free if you'd like another rest."
  end
label Lilycove
  say "We've arrived at Lilycove City. Mind the gangway on your way down!"
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 0
  fade out 0.8
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Slateport
  say "We've arrived at Slateport City. Mind the gangway on your way down!"
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 0
  fade out 0.8
  warp "SlateportCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end


# The voyage's last step aboard (GameEngine.CruiseStep): the ship comes in
script ReachedStepCount
  speaker none
  setvar VAR_CRUISE_STEP_COUNT 0
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 2 goto Slateport
  if var VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE == 6 goto Slateport
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 3
  text "Attention, passengers. We have arrived at Lilycove City."
  end
label Slateport
  setvar VAR_SS_TIDAL_STATE 4
  text "Attention, passengers. We have arrived at Slateport City."
  end
