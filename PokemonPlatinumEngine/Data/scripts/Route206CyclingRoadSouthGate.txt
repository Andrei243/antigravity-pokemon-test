# The gate at the foot of the Cycling Road (plan 01 · M11, part d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_route_206_cycling_road_south_gate.s).
#
# The Cycling Road lies north of the room. The row of the lane just past the gate keepers (the original's trigger at
# y 6, while VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 is 0) turns back anyone on foot; a rider is waved through and can't get off the
# Bicycle from there to the road (FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE). The row before it (y 8, while the variable is 1) lets a
# rider coming back off again, and whoever comes in from the road is riding and stays so until that row.
#
# The Battle Girl gives the Flag once, an Accessory (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_SOUTH_GATE_ACCESSORY_FLAG).


# Route206CyclingRoadSouthGate_OnFrame_TryForceBiking
script OnEnter
  if var PLAYER_Y <= 3 setflag FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE
  end


# Route206CyclingRoadSouthGate_CoordEvent_OnlyCyclists
script OnlyBicycles
  if var CYCLING == 1 goto Riding
  emote cashier_m_west exclaim
  speaker "Gate Keeper"
  say "Hold on! The Cycling Road is open to cyclists only. You'll need to be on a Bicycle."
  walk player down 1
  end
label Riding
  setflag FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 1
  end


# Route206CyclingRoadSouthGate_CoordEvent_ClearFlagForceBikingInGate
script OffTheRoad
  clearflag FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 0
  end


# Route206CyclingRoadSouthGate_BattleGirl
script BattleGirl
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_SOUTH_GATE_ACCESSORY_FLAG goto Given
  say "What's a Bicycle without one of these?" "A Flag! Everybody ought to have one. It's an Accessory for your Pokémon, too."
  giveaccessory "Flag"
  if result == 0 goto Full
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_SOUTH_GATE_ACCESSORY_FLAG
  end
label Full
  say "Oh, your Fashion Case has no room for it. Come back when it does!"
  end
label Given
  say "Go on through Mt. Coronet from here and you come to Hearthome City, where the Pokémon Contests are held." "Dress your Pokémon up in your Accessories and give one a try!"
  end
