# The Water Labyrinth on Five Island (plan 19 · K9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/FiveIsland_WaterLabyrinth/scripts.inc).
#
# An old gentleman gives an Egg to a trainer whose lead Pokémon loves them as much as a Pokémon can (LEAD_FRIENDSHIP
# 255, FireRed's GetLeadMonFriendship 6), once (FLAG_GOT_TOGEPI_EGG). With a full team he asks the player to come
# back, as FireRed's FLAG_NO_ROOM_FOR_TOGEPI_EGG does; here nothing need be remembered for that.


script EggGentleman
  face gentleman player
  speaker "Gentleman"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TOGEPI_EGG goto Given
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP < 255 goto NotYet
  say "My word, how fond your Pokémon is of you! I can see it in its eyes. You're just the trainer I've been waiting for."
  say "I have a Pokémon Egg I'd like raised by someone like you. Would you take it?"
  if party >= 6 goto Full
  fanfare pokemon
  giveegg "Togepi"
  speaker none
  text "{player} received an Egg from the gentleman!"
  speaker "Gentleman"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TOGEPI_EGG
  say "Take good care of it. Love it as your Pokémon loves you."
  end
label Full
  say "Ah, but you have no room for it on your team. Come back once you've made some."
  end
label NotYet
  say "A trainer and a Pokémon who trust each other completely are a fine thing to see. Treat yours well, won't you?"
  end
label Given
  say "How is the Egg? Whatever hatches from it, I'm sure it will grow up happy with you."
  end
