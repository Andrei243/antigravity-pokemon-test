# The Champion's room (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/EverGrandeCity_ChampionsRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# The whole room is one scene, played as the challenger comes in: up the room to Wallace, the Champion, his words and his
# battle (hoenn_wallace). Beaten, he opens the door behind him (FLAG_HOENN_DEFEATED_WALLACE, a flag of ours: Emerald
# swaps the door's tiles). Then the rival runs in (May for a boy, Brendan for a girl), has a word of advice and finds
# it is too late for it; Professor Birch comes in after, and says what the Pokédex has come to; and Wallace leads the
# player through the door to the Hall of Fame, the rival and the professor waiting behind. A battle lost ends it there,
# as every battle lost does, and the League's hall puts the four rooms back. A save from before this stretch is given its
# hiding here (common.HoennChapterTwelve).


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_TWELVE call HoennChapterTwelve
  if flag FLAG_HOENN_DEFEATED_WALLACE end
  scene "The Champion"
  walk player up 3
  wait 0.6
  walk player up 2
  music "common/eye_champion"
  face champions_room_wallace player
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "Welcome, {player}. I am Wallace, the Champion of the Hoenn region."
  say "Do you remember the day you won the Rain Badge from Juan? Something in the way you battled stayed with me."
  say "I wondered then whether you would make it all the way here. And now here you stand."
  say "Then let me show you what a Champion is. You and your Pokémon will see a battle like no other!"
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_WALLACE goto WallaceAgain
  battle champions_room_wallace as "hoenn_wallace"
  addcontact wallace
  text "Registered Wallace in the PokéNav."
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "I, the Champion, have been beaten... You and your Pokémon were simply magnificent."
label WallaceBeaten
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_DEFEATED_WALLACE
  sound "door_open"
  say "You fought with everything you had, and your Pokémon fought for you. That is a true Champion's battle."
  # The rival runs in from the hall behind
  sound "door_open"
  music "hoenn/rival"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CHAMPIONS_ROOM_RIVAL
  place champions_room_rival 6 11 up
  show champions_room_rival
  walk champions_room_rival up 4 left 1 up 1
  face champions_room_rival right
  face player left
  speaker "{rival}"
  if girl goto BrendanAdvice
  say "{player}! I came as fast as I could. Listen, about the Champion, I've got some advice for you..."
  emote champions_room_rival exclaim
  face champions_room_rival up
  wait 0.3
  face champions_room_rival right
  say "Wait... Is it already over? You beat the Champion? Oh, {player}, that's amazing!"
  goto Professor
label BrendanAdvice
  say "{player}! I ran all the way here. Look, before you take on the Champion, you need to know..."
  emote champions_room_rival exclaim
  face champions_room_rival up
  wait 0.3
  face champions_room_rival right
  say "Hang on. It's over already? You won? Seriously? ...Yeah. Of course you did."
label Professor
  # The professor comes in after
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CHAMPIONS_ROOM_BIRCH
  place champions_room_birch 6 11 up
  show champions_room_birch
  walk champions_room_birch up 4 right 1 up 1
  face champions_room_birch left
  face player right
  speaker "Prof. Birch"
  say "Phew! There you are, {player}! I came to see how my Pokédex was getting along, and look what I've walked into."
  say "You've filled it with so many Pokémon on your journey. It's done more for my research than I ever hoped."
  say "And you've become the Champion as well! Congratulations, {player}! I couldn't be prouder."
  face champions_room_wallace up
  wait 0.3
  face champions_room_wallace player
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "{player}, there is somewhere I must take you. Come with me, please."
  walk champions_room_wallace up 2
  face champions_room_wallace down
  walk player up 2
  face champions_room_birch up
  walk champions_room_rival right 1 up 1
  face player down
  say "I'm sorry, but only the new Champion may come through this door. Would the two of you wait out here?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Of course! Go on, {player}. We'll be right here when you come back!"
  face player up
  sound "door_open"
  hide champions_room_wallace
  walk player up 1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PETALBURG_GYM_GREETER
  warp "EverGrandeCity_HallOfFame" 7 15 up
  endscene
  end
label WallaceAgain
  battle champions_room_wallace as "hoenn_wallace_rematch"
  clearflag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_WALLACE
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "Magnificent, once again! You and your Pokémon have only grown more splendid since we last danced."
  goto WallaceBeaten
  end


# Wallace, the rival and the professor, where nobody stands but in the scene
script Wallace
  face self player
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "You are the Champion now, {player}. Carry it well."
  end


script Rival
  face self player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "We'll wait here, {player}. Go on!"
  end


script Birch
  face self player
  speaker "Prof. Birch"
  say "Go on, {player}. We'll be right here."
  end
