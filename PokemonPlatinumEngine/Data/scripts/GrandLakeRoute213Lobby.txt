# The receptionist has no room free, and lets a traveller rest instead: the team is healed behind a fade, as often
# as asked. Looker questions the guests here after the Team Galactic grunt has run past along the beach (plan 02 ·
# S8: route_213.txt clears FLAG_HIDE_GRAND_LAKE_ROUTE_213_LOBBY_LOOKER, valor_lakefront.txt sets it again once the
# grunt is cornered): he sends the player to search outside, and the second time is found in the middle of the
# receptionist's boasting (FLAG_TALKED_TO_GRAND_LAKE_ROUTE_213_LOBBY_LOOKER).


script Rest
  say "Good day. You would like a room for the night? Ah, but you have no reservation." "I'm afraid every room is taken. You are welcome to rest here a while, though."
  fade out
  fanfare heal
  heal
  fade in
  say "Your Pokémon are quite recovered. We hope to see you again!"
  end


script Looker
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_GRAND_LAKE_ROUTE_213_LOBBY_LOOKER goto Again
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_GRAND_LAKE_ROUTE_213_LOBBY_LOOKER
  say "Ah, my friend, it is you! Yes, I am at this hotel now." "I question the guests, one by one. Has anybody seen a suspicious character?" "I ask for your help. Search outside, if you will." "Our man, he may have slipped past already without a soul noticing."
  face self right
  end
label Again
  face self right
  say "Is that so? The Champion of the Hoenn region has stayed in this hotel?" "But that is of no importance! A suspicious character, I said! Your boasting, I do not need!"
  face self player
  say "Inside the hotel, I will search. You, I ask to search outside." "Our man, he may have slipped past already without a soul noticing."
  face self right
  end
