# The Sunyshore Gym's third room (plan 01 · M9, part 2c), rebuilt to the original's plan. The lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_sunyshore_city_gym_room_3.s).
#
# Three rows of gears: the upper two high up by Volkner's platforms, the lowest down by the door, and four gears on edge
# whose bars join the sides. The buttons on the upper gears turn them all a quarter on, those on the lower a half. Volkner
# gives the Beacon Badge (Waterfall outside battle) and TM57, and his win lets the rival come running into Sunyshore
# City (VAR_SUNYSHORE_CITY_STATE, for plan 02 · S13). He is drawn as a youngster until plan 11 · C6 makes his look.


# SunyshoreGymRoom3_Init
script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x00 0
  end


# SunyshoreGymRoom3_TopButtons
script TopButtons
  gearbutton normal
  end


# SunyshoreGymRoom3_BottomButtons
script BottomButtons
  gearbutton double
  end


script Volkner
  speaker "Volkner"
  if badge beacon goto Won
  say "...So. A challenger."
  say "Every so often a Trainer makes it all the way up here. And every time, the battle bores me. I barely have to try."
  say "...Sigh. I'm Volkner, the Leader of this Gym. People call me the strongest Gym Leader in Sinnoh, but..."
  say "Whatever. Let's just get this over with."
  say "Though I'd like it if you were the Trainer who reminds me what makes battling fun!"
  battle self as "leader_volkner"
  say "...Heh. Heheh. Hahaha!"
  say "That was the most fun I've had battling in I don't know how long!"
  say "And knowing you and your team will keep climbing higher... that's got me excited all over again."
  say "This makes eight Gym Badges for you. You've more than earned it!"
  givebadge beacon
  text "{player} received the Beacon Badge from Volkner!"
  defeat "ace_trainer_zachery"
  defeat "ace_trainer_destiny"
  defeat "guitarist_jerry"
  defeat "guitarist_preston"
  defeat "guitarist_lonnie"
  defeat "poke_kid_meghan"
  defeat "school_kid_forrest"
  defeat "school_kid_tiera"
  setvar VAR_SUNYSHORE_CITY_STATE 2
  say "The Beacon Badge lets your Pokémon use Waterfall even outside of battle. You'll need it to reach the Pokémon League."
  say "And with all eight Badges, every Pokémon you have will obey you, whatever its level."
  say "Here. Take this as well."
label Tm
  give "TM57"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_VOLKNER_TM57
  say "TM57 is Charge Beam. If it hits, it may raise your Pokémon's Special Attack. Pretty electrifying, right?"
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_VOLKNER_TM57 goto Tm
  say "Your next stop is the Pokémon League."
  say "Cross the sea, then fight your way up through Victory Road."
  say "Show the League how well you and your team know each other. Even the Elite Four will have to work to keep you back!"
  end
