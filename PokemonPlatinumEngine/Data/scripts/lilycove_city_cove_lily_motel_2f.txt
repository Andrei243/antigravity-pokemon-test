# The Cove Lily Motel's upper floor (plan 21 · H7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LilycoveCity_CoveLilyMotel_2F/scripts.inc): Scott is taking a nap in one of the
# rooms, and the first time he is woken he counts it as a meeting (FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_LILYCOVE, VAR_SCOTT_STATE).
#
# The game designer who comes to stay after Hoenn's Hall of Fame hands over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned
# (plan 40 · GE5; charm due, charm give; Models/Charms.cs), where Emerald's gives a diploma: any regional Pokédex seen
# whole, whichever region's, and the National one caught whole. With nothing due he says his own line (the overlay's).


script Scott
  face self player
  speaker "Scott"
  if flag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_LILYCOVE goto Met
  say "Mm... Oh! {player}! You caught me napping. I've been out watching trainers all day."
  say "I'd much rather be watching a battle than sleeping, mind you. Have you been to the Contest Hall yet?"
  addvar VAR_SCOTT_STATE 1
  setflag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_LILYCOVE
  end
label Met
  say "Lilycove's full of strong trainers. You'll have no shortage of battles here, {player}."
  end

script Designer
  face self player
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  if diploma Ours goto OursDiploma
  if diploma Hoenn goto OwnDiploma
  if var DIPLOMAS >= 3 goto ManyDiplomas
  if var DIPLOMAS > 0 goto AnyDiploma
  sayown
  end
label Charms
  say "That's a Pokédex? Let me look... Every single page filled in! You really did it!"
  say "I said I'd have something for you, and I do. This charm is for players who see it through."
label Give
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Give
  say "Fill in another Pokédex, wherever you go next, and come and show me. I may have something more."
  end
# Plan 40 · GE4, ours: she reads the diplomas the Pokédex has handed out (diploma, DIPLOMAS). They sit after the lines
# above so that the lines' numbers in the translation files stay where they were.
label OursDiploma
  say "That's the diploma for the Pokémon that no region lists! I've only ever drawn those in the margins." "You saw every one of them. I'm going to need a bigger sketchbook."
  end
label OwnDiploma
  say "A diploma for all of Hoenn's Pokémon, from the sea floor to the sky! That's a whole region seen!" "I'd say it was the sea air that did it, but I know it was you."
  end
label ManyDiplomas
  say "Diplomas from several Pokédexes? You've seen more of the world than I've drawn!" "Next time I get stuck on a design, I'm going to ask you what you remember."
  end
label AnyDiploma
  say "Is that a diploma on your Pokédex? Wonderful! Somebody has been busy." "Every region has a list of its own. Fill in another, and let me see it."
  end
