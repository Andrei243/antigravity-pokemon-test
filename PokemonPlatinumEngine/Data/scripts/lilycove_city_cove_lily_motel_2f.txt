# The Cove Lily Motel's upper floor (plan 21 · H7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LilycoveCity_CoveLilyMotel_2F/scripts.inc): Scott is taking a nap in one of the
# rooms, and the first time he is woken he counts it as a meeting (FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_LILYCOVE, VAR_SCOTT_STATE).


script Scott
  face self player
  speaker "Scott"
  if flag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_LILYCOVE goto Met
  say "Mm... Oh! {player}! You caught me napping. I've been out watching trainers all day."
  say "I'd much rather be watching a battle than sleeping, mind you. Have you been to the Contest Hall yet?"
  addvar VAR_SCOTT_STATE 1
  setflag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_LILYCOVE
  end
label Met
  say "Lilycove's full of strong trainers. You'll have no shortage of battles here, {player}."
  end
