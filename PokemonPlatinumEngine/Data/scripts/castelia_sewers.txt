# The Castelia Sewers (plan 22 · U5, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black
# 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/castelia_sewers.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA is the city's count (castelia_city_10.txt has it whole).
#
# Hugh (0) waits inside the way down and comes along (7): he heals the team and walks behind the player, his team the
# one strong against the player's starter (Black 2's TRAINER_RIVAL_13, _14 and _15). In the north passage two grunts
# of Team Plasma (1, 2) are battled side by side, Hugh beside the player (Black 2's multi battle, 342 and 356); they
# run, Burgh (20) comes along the passage from the west, Hugh hands over HM04 and goes after the rest of Team Plasma,
# a stranger in a white coat (19, Colress, who names himself on the plaza later) praises the battle and slips away,
# and Burgh heads back to his Gym (8). Black 2's dropped-item girl (21) is left out.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


# Inside the way down from Thumb Pier (our trigger on 83, 20 and 21, at 6)
script HughJoins
  scene "Hugh in the sewers"
  face 0 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Team Plasma's down here somewhere, I just know it. Taking them on alone would be too much, even for me."
  say "So we'll do it together! First, let me patch up your team."
  heal
  text "{rival} healed your Pokémon."
  say "OK! Let's find Team Plasma!"
  if starter "Snivy" goto WithPignite
  if starter "Tepig" goto WithDewott
  partner 0 "unova_rival_15"
  goto Along
label WithPignite
  partner 0 "unova_rival_13"
  goto Along
label WithDewott
  partner 0 "unova_rival_14"
label Along
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 7
  endscene
  end


script Hugh
  face 0 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Team Plasma's down here. Let's go!"
  end


# The passage north, where two of Team Plasma are talking (our trigger on 50 to 52, 9, at 7)
script PlasmaInTheSewers
  scene "Team Plasma in the sewers"
  place player 52 8 up
  place 0 50 8 up
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey!"
  face 1 down
  face 2 down
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Huh? What do you want?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Team Plasma! You rotten crooks!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Crooks? How rude! We're freeing Pokémon. Don't get in the way of our noble work!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Freeing them? Don't make me laugh! You steal Pokémon, and then you use them like tools!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Shut it! Now hand over your Pokémon!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Let's do this, {player}!"
  battle 1 and 2 with partner
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Ugh, they're tough! Whatever. We've got the Pokémon we came for. Let's get out of here!"
  walk 1 down 1 left 9 fast
  hide 1
  walk 2 left 2 down 1 left 9 fast
  hide 2
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SEWER_GRUNTS
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Tch! They ran off like a bunch of Patrat! Come on, there might be more of them further in!"
  speaker "???"
  say "I don't think there's any need for that, you two."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SEWERS_BURGH
  place 20 41 7 right
  show 20
  walk 20 right 8
  face player left
  face 0 left
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Are you... Burgh, the Gym Leader?"
  speaker "Burgh"
  say "One and the same! I'm Burgh. And there's nobody suspicious further in, I've just looked."
  say "Team Plasma worries me too, but let's get out of here, shall we? This sewer is full of Bug Pokémon... Actually, that part I like."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Huh... Didn't you and the other Gym Leaders fight Team Plasma two years ago?"
  face 0 player
  say "Thanks for helping out, {player}. Here, take this!"
  partner off
  give "HM04"
  say "I'll find the rest of those Team Plasma punks. I'll never forgive Pokémon thieves. Never!"
  walk 0 down 2 right 6 fast
  hide 0
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_SEWERS
  face player left
  speaker "Burgh"
  say "He's gone..."
  speaker "???"
  say "You there!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SEWERS_COLRESS
  place 19 51 4 down
  show 19
  walk 19 down 1
  face player up
  face 20 19
  say "That was a remarkable display of battling. The way you drew out your Pokémon's power against Team Plasma..."
  say "Simply fascinating. I'll be watching with interest!"
  walk 19 up 1 fast
  hide 19
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SEWERS_COLRESS
  face 20 player
  speaker "Burgh"
  say "Who... was that?"
  say "Well, never mind. What will you do now? You could stay down here and train your Pokémon..."
  say "Or you could come and challenge me at my Gym! I'll be waiting, my muse and I!"
  walk 20 left 8 fast
  hide 20
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SEWERS_BURGH
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BURGH_GYM
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 8
  endscene
  end
