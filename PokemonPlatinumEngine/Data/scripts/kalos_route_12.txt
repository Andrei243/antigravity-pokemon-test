# The stable on the Blossom Road (plan 42 · PS8; rulings, "The riding race"): its master runs a track beside the road. The
# player races their own Pokémon of a riding size against three of the stable's riders on their own mounts; first place
# pays Battle Points. Nothing in Kalos's games was ever raced on, so the race and its words are ours.
script RaceStable
  speaker "Stable Master"
  ride raceruns "blossom"
  if result > 0 goto Again
  say "Welcome to the Blossom Road stable! Rhyhorn, Tauros and Rapidash are bred for speed here, and we race them round the track."
  say "Any Pokémon big enough to carry a rider can run it. Hold the run button to push, press the button to jump the rocks, and lean into every turn."
  goto Ask
label Again
  ride racebest "blossom"
  say "Back on the track? Your best here is {number} points."
label Ask
  ask "Care to race your own Pokémon against my three riders?"
  if no goto Later
  ride race "blossom"
  if result == -1 goto NoMount
  if result == 0 goto Later
  if result == 1 goto Won
  if result == 2 goto Second
  if result == 3 goto Third
  say "A good run! The track is hard on newcomers. Come back when you have found its rhythm."
  end
label Third
  say "Third place, and in front of the dust of the last of us. Take the stable's thanks for the ride."
  text "{player} received {number} Battle Point(s)!"
  end
label Second
  say "Second place! You were close. The stable pays a little for a place like that."
  text "{player} received {number} Battle Point(s)!"
  end
label Won
  say "First home! Not one of my riders has beaten that. Here is the stable's prize."
  text "{player} received {number} Battle Point(s)!"
  end
label NoMount
  say "None of your Pokémon is big enough to carry a rider, or they are not fit to run. Come back with a sturdier one!"
  end
label Later
  say "No hurry. The track isn't going anywhere. Come back whenever you like!"
  end
