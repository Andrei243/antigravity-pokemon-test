# Castelia City's street north from the plaza to the gate for Route 4 (plan 22 · U5, part c). docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own.
#
# Plan 40 · GE3, our words on Black 2's beat (Black 2 gives an Eevee in Castelia City at level 10): once the Insect Badge
# is won, the Trainer here gives the player one of her family's Eevee, once (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_EEVEE, a flag of ours).


script EeveeGift
  speaker "Ace Trainer"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_EEVEE goto Given
  if not badge insect goto Plain
  say "You beat Burgh? Then you're just the Trainer I was waiting for!"
  say "My family's Eevee had more little ones than our flat can hold. One of them keeps staring at the gate to Route 4."
  ask "Would you take it with you on your journey?"
  if no goto Later
  if not party < 6 goto Full
  givepokemon "Eevee" 10
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_EEVEE
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received Eevee!"
  say "Eevee can grow into so many different Pokémon. I can't wait to hear which one it chooses!"
  end
label Later
  say "Oh... Well, it'll be right here if you change your mind."
  end
label Full
  say "Your team looks full. Make some room, then come back for it!"
  end
label Given
  say "How's Eevee doing? Has it chosen what it wants to be yet?"
  end
label Plain
  say "Beyond Route 4 lies Nimbasa City. I hear its Gym Leader is a model!"
  end
