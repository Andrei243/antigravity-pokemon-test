# Cinnabar Island (plan 19 · K6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/CinnabarIsland/scripts.inc).
#
# The Gym's door is locked until the Secret Key has been picked up in the Pokémon Mansion's basement: its ball's flag
# (FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_MANSION_B1F_SECRET_KEY) is what FireRed's arrival asks, and the step before the door turns the
# player back while VAR_TEMP_1 is 0. Bill, who waits here once Blaine is beaten to take the player to One Island, and
# the ferry are plan 19 · K7's: until then they stay out of sight (a save from before this chapter never came here).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_CINNABAR_ISLAND == 0 setflag FLAG_HIDE_CINNABAR_BILL
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CINNABAR_SEAGALLOP
  if flag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_MANSION_B1F_SECRET_KEY setvar VAR_TEMP_1 1
  end

script LockedGymDoor
  face player up
  speaker none
  text "The door is locked. There's a keyhole, but no key in sight."
  walk player down 1
  end

script GymSign
  speaker none
  if badge volcano goto Won
  text "Cinnabar Island Pokémon Gym\nLeader: Blaine\nRiddles and fire for every challenger."
  end
label Won
  text "Cinnabar Island Pokémon Gym\nLeader: Blaine. Winning trainers: {rival}, {player}."
  end
