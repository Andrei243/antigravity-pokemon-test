# The Giant Chasm's cave (plan 22 · U9, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/giant_chasm_2.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_CHASM is the chapter's count: 0 until Hugh is met in the cave, 1 once he has gone on to the crater, 2 once Rood's
# stand against the grunts is over, 3 once the Shadow Triad have been beaten aboard the Frigate, 4 once Ghetsis is beaten
# (giant_chasm_6.txt), when FLAG_UNOVA_CHASM_CLEARED turns the cave's way into the crater from the crater with the Frigate
# (giant_chasm_4) to the crater as it is afterwards (giant_chasm_3).
#
# A spy of Rood's in a grunt's clothes (10) waits beside the way up into the crater. Until the Plasma Frigate has flown from
# Route 21 (VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 9) he turns everyone back from it (our trigger, 23, 51, the tile before the way up); then, as the
# player comes to it, Hugh catches up, the spy tells them where the Frigate lies and goes off to warn Rood, and Hugh runs on
# ahead. Two of the new Team Plasma (7, 8) keep the way out to Route 23 until the chapter is over.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpE
  end


script HughInTheCave
  if var VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU == 9 goto Catch
  face 10 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Hold it! The crater isn't safe right now. Something huge is coming, and I'm keeping watch for it."
  walk player down 1
  end
label Catch
  scene "Hugh in the Giant Chasm"
  place player 23 51 up
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_CAVE
  show hugh_cave
  face player hugh_cave
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! You got here first? I ran the whole way from Route 21 and you still beat me."
  say "So this is the Giant Chasm. It's freezing in here... That has to be Kyurem's doing."
  face player 10
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Psst! Easy, easy. I'm not one of them. Rood of the old Team Plasma asked me to keep watch in these clothes."
  say "Their ship came down in the crater up this way. The new Team Plasma are guarding it, and Rood has gone to face them."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "The old Team Plasma, standing up to the new one? Then we'd better hurry before they get hurt!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "I'll go back and tell the others that help has come. Thank you, really!"
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_CHASM_SPY_GONE
  hide 10
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Come on, {player}! My sister's Purrloin could be on that ship!"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_CAVE
  hide hugh_cave
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROOD_CHASM
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CRATER_GRUNTS
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_CRATER
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 1
  endscene
  fade in
  end


# The spy, spoken to: before the Frigate has flown from Route 21 he keeps everyone out of the crater
script Spy
  face 10 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU == 9 goto Waiting
  say "This crater's no place to be right now. Something huge is on its way here, and I'm watching for it."
  say "...Don't tell anyone, but I'm with Rood. Go and help the people of Humilau first, all right?"
  end
label Waiting
  say "You came! Their ship is up in the crater. Go on and I'll warn the others."
  end


# The two who keep the way out to Route 23
script Guard
  face self player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Nobody goes out this way! Lord Ghetsis's orders!"
  end
