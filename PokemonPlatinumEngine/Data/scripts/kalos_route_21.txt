# Route 21, the last road (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): the neighbour waits on the road
# for one last battle before the League (VAR_KALOS_RIVAL_LEAGUE 0 until it, 1 after), and the guard at Victory Road's
# mouth lets through only a Trainer with all eight of Kalos's Badges (VAR_KALOS_VICTORY_ROAD 1 once one has come). The
# neighbour's teams are Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_rival_league_*.
#
# The people: guard, rival (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_21, set by the scene).


# Into the road from the village (the trigger across it east of the neighbour, on VAR_KALOS_RIVAL_LEAGUE 0)
script Neighbour
  scene "The neighbour before Victory Road"
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}. I thought you'd come this way. Victory Road is just ahead, and the League past it."
  say "Ever since Vaniville I've been chasing you. One more battle. Everything I've got!"
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainstChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_league_chespin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstChespin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_league_fennekin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_league_froakie" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto RivalWon
  say "That's it, then. You're the one who's going to stand in front of the Champion. Go and win."
  goto Off
label RivalWon
  say "I won? I won! Then you'd better win twice as hard up there."
  goto Off
label Off
  say "I'll be cheering for you. Loudly. Embarrassingly."
  walk rival right 3 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_21
  setvar VAR_KALOS_RIVAL_LEAGUE 1
  endscene
  end

script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Victory Road's mouth is just ahead. The guard checks your Badges."
  end


# The tile before Victory Road's mouth (the trigger on it, on VAR_KALOS_VICTORY_ROAD 0)
script Gate
  speaker "Guard"
  if badges >= 8 goto Pass
  say "Halt! Only a Trainer with all eight of Kalos's Badges may enter Victory Road."
  walk player down 1
  end
label Pass
  say "Eight Badges! Very well. Victory Road is open to you, and the Pokémon League lies beyond it."
  setvar VAR_KALOS_VICTORY_ROAD 1
  end

script Guard
  speaker "Guard"
  if badges >= 8 goto Pass
  say "I guard the way to the League. Come back with all eight Badges of Kalos."
  end
label Pass
  say "The League is beyond Victory Road. Good luck, Trainer."
  end
