# The Fight Area (plan 01 · M10, part a; its story plan 02 · S15, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_fight_area.s).
#
# The ship from Snowpoint City lands a Champion on the pier (snowpoint_city.Ship, at 623, 434, facing east), and the
# rival, who has been waiting there, takes the player to the Battle Frontier's gate, where Flint of the Elite Four and
# Volkner of Sunyshore's Gym stand with a crowd round them. The two battle the player and the rival together; once
# they are beaten they and the crowd go, Palmer comes out of the gate and bowls his son over, and Buck, who watched it
# all, asks the player to the Survival Area.
#
# VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE: 0 before the rival has been met on the pier, 1 once he has brought the player to the gate
# (he waits there, and asks again whenever he is spoken to), 2 once Flint and Volkner are beaten.
#
# Two trainers stand across the way north to Route 225 and two more at the water's edge on Route 230
# (FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_BLOCKADE): they stand aside for whoever has won that battle and has the National Pokédex,
# whichever comes second (the battle's end here, Professor Oak's visit in RowanLab.txt).


script OnEnter
  # FightArea_OnTransition: until the battle is won the rival waits before the gate
  if var VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE == 1 place rival 655 426 up
  if var VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE != 0 end
  # FightArea_OnFrame_Rival: as the player steps ashore
  if var PLAYER_X != 623 end
  if var PLAYER_Y != 434 end
  face rival left
  emote rival exclaim
  walk player right 4
  call ToTheGate
  call TeamUp
  end


# The rival, spoken to (the original's script 14): on the pier by someone who came another way, and before the gate
# for as long as the battle hasn't been won.
script Rival
  if var VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE == 0 goto Pier
  # FightArea_Rival: he turns back to the two at the gate, and the player comes round to stand at his side
  face rival up
  if facing left goto FromTheEast
  if facing down goto FromTheNorth
  if facing up goto FromTheSouth
  goto Beside
label FromTheEast
  walk player down 1 left 2 up 1
  goto Beside
label FromTheNorth
  walk player left 1 down 1
  goto Beside
label FromTheSouth
  walk player left 1 up 1
label Beside
  face player up
  speaker "{rival}"
  ask "Well, {player}? Ready now? Let's show those two what we're made of!"
  if no goto NeedsHelp
  call Battle
  end
label NeedsHelp
  call INeedYourHelp
  end
label Pier
  face rival player
  call ToTheGate
  call TeamUp
  end


# From the pier to the Frontier's gate (FightArea_OnFrame_Rival). The original walks the two of them across the town
# to the rival's own tune; the townspeople wander across that road here, so the picture closes on the pier and opens
# before the gate (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "After the Hall of Fame, part a").
script ToTheGate
  music "common/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! There you are! I've been on this pier so long I know every plank of it by name!"
  say "No time to explain. Something big is going on up at the gate. Come on, come with me!"
  fade out
  place player 654 426 up
  place rival 655 426 up
  fade in
  music area
  setvar VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE 1
  return


script TeamUp
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "See them? That's Flint of the Elite Four. And the one beside him runs the Sunyshore Gym!"
  ask "They say they'll take on anybody, two at a time. So how about it, {player}? You and me, together!"
  if no goto NeedsHelp
  call Battle
  return
label NeedsHelp
  call INeedYourHelp
  return


# FightArea_INeedYourHelp
script INeedYourHelp
  face rival left
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "What?! Come on, {player}! I can't take those two on alone, and there's nobody I'd sooner have beside me!"
  return


# FightArea_BattleVolknerFlint and FightArea_PostBattleVolknerFlint. A battle lost is a white-out, as the original's:
# the rival is back before the gate when the player comes again.
script Battle
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Yes! That's the spirit! The two of us can flatten them!"
  move rival up 1
  walk player up 1
  waitmoves
  face flint down
  speaker "Flint"
  say "Ha! So these are the two who mean to try us? Good! Burn as bright as you can. I want to see it!"
  face volkner down
  speaker "Volkner"
  say "...Show me what you can do. It has been a while since a challenger made me sit up."
  if starter "Chimchar" goto Chimchar
  if starter "Piplup" goto Piplup
  battle volkner as "leader_volkner_fight_area" and flint with "rival_fight_area_turtwig"
  goto Won
label Chimchar
  battle volkner as "leader_volkner_fight_area" and flint with "rival_fight_area_chimchar"
  goto Won
label Piplup
  battle volkner as "leader_volkner_fight_area" and flint with "rival_fight_area_piplup"
label Won
  face volkner left
  speaker "Flint"
  say "... ... ..."
  speaker "Volkner"
  say "Don't burn yourself out over it, Flint. They were simply better than us today."
  face volkner down
  say "That was a blast. I had forgotten a battle could feel like that. Thank you, both of you."
  fade out
  hide flint
  hide volkner
  hide ace_trainer_m_3
  hide ace_trainer_f_2
  hide black_belt_3
  hide battle_girl_2
  hide sailor_3
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_BATTLERS_AND_SPECTATORS
  fade in
  face rival left
  face player right
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "We did it! Did you see their faces? Right, no time to lose. I'm off to the Battle Fronti--"
  # Palmer comes out of the gate at a run
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_PALMER
  place palmer 655 423 down
  show palmer
  sound "bump"
  text "Thud!"
  move palmer down 1 fast
  walk rival down 2 fast
  face rival up
  waitmoves
  face player down
  wait 0.15
  face player up
  face palmer left
  wait 0.3
  face palmer right
  wait 0.3
  face palmer down
  speaker "Palmer"
  say "Hm? What's all this? I heard there was a battle worth seeing before my gate, and here it's over already!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Ow, ow, ow... Hey! Watch where you're... D-Dad?!"
  walk rival up 2 fast
  speaker "Palmer"
  say "Well, well! If it isn't {rival}! And you must be {player}. I've heard a great deal about you."
  say "So you two beat that pair. Then I'll look forward to your challenge. You know where to find me!"
  walk palmer up 1 fast
  sound "stairs"
  hide palmer
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_PALMER
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...That was my dad. He's the Tower Tycoon. The strongest Trainer in the whole Battle Frontier."
  say "Argh! What do I do? I came all this way to beat him, and I fell over the moment I saw him!"
  say "No. No! I know what I do. I get tougher. Way tougher! Starting right now! See you, {player}!"
  move rival down 8 fast
  face player down
  waitmoves
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_RIVAL
  # Buck, who watched from beside the Mart
  walk buck up 1 left 2
  face player right
  speaker "Buck"
  say "Whoa, that was something! You're pretty good, you know that? The name's Buck."
  say "I've a place up in the Survival Area. If you're passing that way, come and look me up!"
  move buck down 8
  wait 0.3
  face player down
  waitmoves
  hide buck
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_BUCK
  # FightArea_RemoveBlockade: the ways on are open to whoever has the National Pokédex too
  if nationaldex setflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_BLOCKADE
  setvar VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE 2
  return


# Flint and Volkner before their battle (the original's scripts 18 and 19): each turns back to the gate.
script Flint
  face self player
  speaker "Flint"
  say "You there! Think you're up to it? Find yourself a partner and we'll see how hot you burn!"
  face self down
  end

script Volkner
  face self player
  speaker "Volkner"
  say "Sorry about my friend. He means well. He dragged me out here because he thinks it will do me good."
  face self down
  end


# Buck beside the Mart, watching (the original's script 25)
script Buck
  face self player
  speaker "Buck"
  say "Who are those two? Everybody who goes up against them comes back down again. I can't take my eyes off it!"
  face self left
  end


script Ferry
  face self player
  ask "Heading back to the mainland? I can have you in Snowpoint City before the tea's gone cold."
  if no goto Stay
  say "Anchors aweigh, then! Wrap up warm: it's snowing where we're going."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 356 246 up
  end
label Stay
  say "You'd sooner fly, I expect. Suit yourself! The ship's not going anywhere without me."
  end


# The fisherman by the water north of the quay (the original's script 8) gives away the Super Rod once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD), and after that only his advice.
script Fisherman
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD goto Advice
  ask "The Super Rod! Now that is a rod. It hooks the big ones, out where the water's deep. Wouldn't you like one?"
  if no goto Refused
  say "I knew it! One angler can always tell another. Here, take this one."
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD
  say "Go and try your luck! Any water will do, but the sea round this island is the place for it."
  end
label Refused
  say "What? You don't care for fishing? ...I'll pretend I didn't hear that."
  end
label Advice
  say "Here's an angler's advice: the same water gives up different Pokémon to a different rod."
  say "So fish every pond three times over, with the Old Rod, the Good Rod and the Super Rod."
  end
