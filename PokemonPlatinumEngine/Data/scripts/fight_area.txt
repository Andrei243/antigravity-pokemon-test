# The sailor on the pier takes the player back to Snowpoint City, to the quay beside the ship's own sailor there. The
# way here is that sailor's, once the Hall of Fame has been entered (the original's SnowpointCity_SailorSSSpiral asks
# FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED and lands the player at 623, 434, facing east): plan 02 · S15 writes it, with the rival's scene
# that begins as the player steps ashore (FightArea_OnFrame_Rival) and the tag battle before the Battle Frontier's gate.

script Ferry
  face self player
  ask "Heading back to the mainland? I can have you in Snowpoint City before the tea's gone cold."
  if no goto Stay
  say "Anchors aweigh, then! Wrap up warm: it's snowing where we're going."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 356 246 up
  end
label Stay
  say "You'd sooner fly, I expect. Suit yourself! The ship's not going anywhere without me."
  end


# The fisherman by the water north of the quay (the original's script 8) gives away the Super Rod once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD), and after that only his advice.
script Fisherman
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD goto Advice
  ask "The Super Rod! Now that is a rod. It hooks the big ones, out where the water's deep. Wouldn't you like one?"
  if no goto Refused
  say "I knew it! One angler can always tell another. Here, take this one."
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD
  say "Go and try your luck! Any water will do, but the sea round this island is the place for it."
  end
label Refused
  say "What? You don't care for fishing? ...I'll pretend I didn't hear that."
  end
label Advice
  say "Here's an angler's advice: the same water gives up different Pokémon to a different rod."
  say "So fish every pond three times over, with the Old Rod, the Good Rod and the Super Rod."
  end
