# Goldenrod City's Bike Shop (plan 20 · J7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T25R0201): the owner, new in town with no customers, lends the player a Bicycle to ride
# about Johto and be seen on (FLAG_UNK_089).


script Owner
  face t25r0201_gsmiddleman1 player
  speaker "Owner"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_089 goto Lent
  say "Welcome to my Bike Shop! I only just moved here from Kanto, and not a soul has bought a bike yet."
  ask "Say, how would you like a Bicycle? Ride it around Johto and show everyone how great it is!"
  if no goto No
  ask "Really? You'll help me out? In that case, it's yours. No charge!"
  if no goto No
  give "Bicycle"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_089
  say "Ride it everywhere you go! When people ask where you got it, tell them about my shop."
  end
label Lent
  say "How's the Bicycle? Make sure everyone in Johto sees you on it!"
  end
label No
  say "Oh... Well, if you change your mind, I'll be right here."
  end
