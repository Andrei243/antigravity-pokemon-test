# Route 47 (plan 20 · J8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats
# (scr_seq_R47).
#
# Coming onto the route notes that the player has walked the cliff path (VAR_UNK_40EB 1), which the woman of the Cliff
# Edge Gate asks (cliff_edge_gate.txt). The Embedded Tower's rooms, Crasher Wake and Chuck are a later session's.


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_UNK_40EB 1
  # Chuck and Crasher Wake train by the waterfall once Johto's Champion holds Kanto's eight Badges too (Johto's
  # leftovers: HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_47_CHUCK and _CRASHER_WAKE, laid as the route is come to)
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_47_CHUCK
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_47_CRASHER_WAKE
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame end
  if badges of kanto < 8 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_47_CHUCK
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_47_CRASHER_WAKE
  end


# R47_004: Chuck, training beside the waterfall
script Chuck
  face r47_gsleader5 player
  speaker "Chuck"
  say "HWAAAH! Oh, it's you! This waterfall's spray is perfect for training. A visitor from far away told me about it."
  say "He's up there on the rocks, roaring louder than the falls! Go and say hello."
  end


# R47_003: Crasher Wake, a Gym Leader of Sinnoh, come to train under Johto's waterfalls (our words on HeartGold's beat)
script CrasherWake
  face r47_leader3 player
  speaker "Crasher Wake"
  say "Hah! Crasher Wake, Gym Leader of Pastoria City in faraway Sinnoh! I heard Johto's waterfalls were the toughest around."
  say "Chuck and I have been taking turns under the falls. My Pokémon have never been so fired up! Wahahah!"
  end


# R47_001: the hiker by the hole in the cliff
script Hiker
  speaker "Hiker"
  if flag FLAG_MET_ROUTE_47_EMBEDDED_TOWER_HIKER goto Again
  say "Hello there! See that hole in the cliff? They say a great tower once stood here, half of it buried in the rock."
  say "It reached up into the sky and down into the earth, and some say right to the bottom of the sea."
  say "It's crumbled away now. All that's left is the way in and a room full of strange patterns."
  setflag FLAG_MET_ROUTE_47_EMBEDDED_TOWER_HIKER
  end
label Again
  say "I reckon it was built to honour Pokémon bound up with the sea, the land and the sky."
  end
