# Plan 44 · SD4: Juno, Lockwork's admin of Unova's cell, in the tower's lobby; the stairs behind her go up to the head
script Admin
  face self player
  speaker self
  if defeated "lockwork_unova_admin" goto Beaten
  say "Welcome to Lockwork's head office. Every crate that left a port in five regions was signed for on this floor."
  say "You've undone four cells. You won't undo the fifth."
  battle self as "lockwork_unova_admin"
  speaker self
  say "Fine. Go up. Keld will want to see the one who's been unpicking her locks."
  setflag FLAG_LOCKWORK_UNOVA_ADMIN_BEATEN
  end
label Beaten
  say "The stairs are behind me. She's waiting by the machine."
  end


# Plan 44 · SD4: the courier, once the machine has stopped, with every locked Pokémon the player knocked out instead of
# reclaiming (HeartsRecord.Missed), six at a time, until every one is reclaimed
script Courier
  face self player
  speaker self
  heart courier
  if var RESULT == 0 goto Empty
  say "The machine's stopped, but the crates still came back. Every one you knocked out went straight into my van."
  say "Keld says I'm to hand them over. I say you'll have to take them, one ball at a time."
  battle self canlose
  speaker self
  say "Whatever's still in the van, I'll have it here next time you come."
  end
label Empty
  say "The van's empty. Every crate that came back has gone home with you. Good riddance to the lot."
  end
