# Mt. Moon as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part f). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D02R0101).
#
# Once Johto's Hall of Fame is entered the red-haired boy waits in Mt. Moon by its western mouth, training where nobody
# bothers him, as he said he would on Victory Road. Spoken to, he battles with the team that beats the player's
# starter, at the levels HeartGold gives him here, and goes; that is VAR_UNK_40FC 1, which begins the Dragon's Den's
# post-game (dragons_den.txt), as in HeartGold. The cave's own arrival keeps him off the floor (FLAG_UNK_25C,
# HeartGold's own) unless he is waiting, so a save of Johto from before this session needs no story version.


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_UNK_25C
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame end
  if var VAR_UNK_40FC != 0 end
  clearflag FLAG_UNK_25C
  end


script Rival
  face d02r0101_gsrivel player
  speaker "{rival}"
  scene "The rival in Mount Moon"
  music "johto/rival"
  say "...You. Of all the caves in Kanto, you had to walk into this one."
  say "I came here to train where nobody would find me. I've been thinking about what it means to be strong."
  say "Not winning at any cost. Something else. My Pokémon and I, together. I want to see if I've got it right."
  say "{player}! Battle me. Here, now!"
  if starter "Chikorita" goto VsTyphlosion
  if starter "Cyndaquil" goto VsFeraligatr
  battle self as "johto_rival_silver_14"
  goto Won
label VsTyphlosion
  battle self as "johto_rival_silver_15"
  goto Won
label VsFeraligatr
  battle self as "johto_rival_silver_16"
label Won
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...Hmph. So that's where I stand."
  say "I'm not angry. That's new. I'm going somewhere the dragons train. Don't follow me. Or do. I don't care."
  fade out
  hide d02r0101_gsrivel
  setflag FLAG_UNK_25C
  setvar VAR_UNK_40FC 1
  fade in
  music area
  endscene
  end
