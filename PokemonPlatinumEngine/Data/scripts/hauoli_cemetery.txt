# Hau'oli Cemetery, Melemele Island (plan 23, the rest of the islands; rulings, "The Battle Tree, the power plant,
# Zygarde's cells and the rest of the islands"). The lines and the story are our own. After dark a little girl stands by
# a grave in the cemetery's south and waits for her friend, a Misdreavus who went on ahead of her
# (FLAG_ALOLA_CEMETERY_NIGHT shows her, set by the arrival at night and cleared by day). VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL is her
# story: 0 until she is first spoken to; 1 once she has asked about her friend; 2 once the flowers by the east lamp have
# given up what her friend left there; 3 once she has gone after her friend (FLAG_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL_GONE).


script OnEnter
  if time night goto Night
  if time latenight goto Night
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_CEMETERY_NIGHT
  end
label Night
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CEMETERY_NIGHT
  end


script GhostGirl
  face self player
  speaker "Little Girl"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL == 2 goto Goodbye
  if var VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL == 1 goto Again
  say "Oh! You can see me? Most people walk right past."
  say "I'm waiting for my friend. She's a Misdreavus. She floated off ahead of me a long, long time ago."
  say "She always said she'd leave me a sign where the flowers grow brightest. But I can't find it in the dark..."
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL 1
  end
label Again
  say "The flowers that grow brightest... Maybe by one of the lamps? Could you look for me?"
  end
label Goodbye
  say "That's hers! I'd know that tag anywhere. She was here, and she waited for me all this time."
  say "Then I shouldn't keep her waiting any longer, should I? Thank you for looking. Thank you for seeing me."
  fade out 1.0
  cry "Misdreavus"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL_GONE
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL 3
  fade in
  speaker none
  text "...The little girl was gone. Only the scent of night flowers lingered by the grave."
  end


# The flowers by the east lamp: what the girl's friend left for her
script LampFlowers
  speaker none
  if var VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL == 1 goto Found
  text "Flowers grow thick around the foot of the lamp. They glow faintly in its light."
  end
label Found
  text "Something is tucked deep among the flowers..."
  give "Spell Tag"
  text "It's an old tag, worn soft at the edges. A Misdreavus's name is written on it in a child's hand."
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL 2
  end


script Mourner
  face self player
  speaker "Woman"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_CEMETERY_GIRL == 3 goto After
  say "My grandmother used to tell a story about a little girl and her Misdreavus. They were never apart, until they were."
  say "She said that on quiet nights, the girl still comes here looking for her friend."
  end
label After
  say "Last night the cemetery felt... lighter, somehow. As if somebody finally found what they were looking for."
  end


# The Pokémon the Island Scan found here today (plan 23, the rest of the islands; HeaheaCity_ResearchLab.IslandScan)
script ScanLitwick
  speaker none
  cry "Litwick"
  text "It's the Litwick the Island Scan picked up! It's ready to battle!"
  wildbattle "Litwick" 10
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_ALOLA_SCAN_LITWICK_MET
  end
