# Ultra Ruin (plan 23 · Z8), a world of Ultra Space. The lines are our own, on Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ultra Space and the Battle Royal Dome"): no picture, map, line or
# script of the games is read. It is come to through the Ultra Wormhole over the Altar's dais (poni_altar.txt) and left
# by the one it is come in by (common.UltraWayHome). Its Ultra Beast, Guzzlord, waits at 32,16: met as a wild Pokémon
# after a staged beat, at level 60, and back each time the world is come to until it is caught
# (FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_GUZZLORD); FLAG_ALOLA_MET_GUZZLORD says it has been met, and the first meeting
# counts in VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA_MET, which the Lightseekers count to seven at the Altar.


script OnEnter
  effect rift 32 55 hold
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_GUZZLORD end
  text "A city once stood here. Its streets are broken, and ash falls without end. Whatever was here has been eaten."
  end


script Beast
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "Guzzlord"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 32 16 1.0
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect mist 32 16 wait
  cry "Guzzlord"
  text "The ground shook. Something vast and hungry dragged itself out of the ruins, its mouths wide open."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Guzzlord" 60
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_GUZZLORD
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_GUZZLORD addvar VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA_MET 1
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_GUZZLORD
  hide self
  end
