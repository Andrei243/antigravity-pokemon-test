# Grimsley's room of the Elite Four (plan 22 · U9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league_6.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GRIMSLEY is 0 until Grimsley is beaten in this challenge, 1 after; the plaza's arrival puts it back to 0, as a new
# challenge begins there. Grimsley battles with Black 2's first team (unova_elite_four_grimsley) and, once Unova's Hall of Fame has been
# entered, with the team of the second round (unova_elite_four_grimsley_2). Once beaten, the light in the corner (Pad) carries the
# player back down to the League's hall, beside the arm to this room.


script Grimsley
  face 0 player
  speaker "Grimsley"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GRIMSLEY == 1 goto Beaten
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Again
  say "Win or lose, it all comes down to a roll of the dice. That's the thrill of it."
  say "I'm Grimsley of the Elite Four, and my Dark-type Pokémon play to win. Let's see where the dice land."
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_grimsley"
  goto Won
label Again
  say "Another round, then? I never turn down a game. Let's play!"
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_grimsley_2"
label Won
  speaker "Grimsley"
  say "Well, well. The dice weren't with me today. No regrets, though. I played my hand as well as I could."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GRIMSLEY 1
  speaker none
  text "A light began to glow in the corner of the room."
  end
label Beaten
  say "A loss is a loss. I'll take it with a smile, and I'll be back at the table soon enough."
  end


# The light in the corner, once Grimsley is beaten: back down to the League's hall
script Pad
  speaker none
  text "The light carried {player} back down to the hall."
  warp "UnovaPokemonLeague_Hall" 25 37 down
  end
