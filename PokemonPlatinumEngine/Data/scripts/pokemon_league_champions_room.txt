# The Champion's room (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/PokemonLeague_ChampionsRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# The whole room is one scene, played as the challenger comes in: up the passage to the rival, who beat the Elite Four
# first and is the Champion; his words and the battle (his team by the starter he took, the one strong against the
# player's: kanto_champion_first_<his>); then Oak comes in (FLAG_HIDE_OAK_IN_CHAMP_ROOM cleared), has a word for each,
# and takes the player through the door behind (FLAG_DEFEATED_CHAMP) to the Hall of Fame. A battle lost ends it there,
# as every battle lost does, and the hall's Pokémon Center puts the League back.


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_CHAMP end
  # Oak comes in only once the rival is beaten (FireRed's new game hides him; here the room does, for every save)
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OAK_IN_CHAMP_ROOM
  hide champions_room_prof_oak
  scene "The Champion"
  walk player up 9
  music "common/eye_champion"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey, {player}! I was looking forward to seeing you here."
  say "While you were busy out there, I beat the Elite Four. That makes me the new Pokémon League Champion!"
  say "I've built the perfect team. Strong against every type, ready for anything. Nobody can touch us."
  say "And now I get to prove it against you, of all people. {player}! I'm the strongest trainer in the world!"
  if starter "Bulbasaur" goto VsCharmander
  if starter "Charmander" goto VsSquirtle
  battle champions_room_rival as "kanto_champion_first_bulbasaur"
  goto Fought
label VsCharmander
  battle champions_room_rival as "kanto_champion_first_charmander"
  goto Fought
label VsSquirtle
  battle champions_room_rival as "kanto_champion_first_squirtle"
label Fought
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "NO! That can't be! You beat my best?"
  say "After all that work to get here... my reign is over already? It's not fair!"
  music "kanto/oak"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_OAK_IN_CHAMP_ROOM
  show champions_room_prof_oak
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "{player}!"
  walk champions_room_prof_oak up 5
  face player down
  say "So you've won! Congratulations! You are the new Pokémon League Champion!"
  say "You've grown so much since the day you left Pallet Town with that first Pokémon of yours. You truly came of age!"
  face champions_room_prof_oak up
  say "{rival}... I'm disappointed in you. Do you know why you lost?"
  say "You forgot to treat your Pokémon with trust and love. Without that, you will never become the best."
  face champions_room_prof_oak down
  say "{player}, come with me. There is a room ahead where your victory will be kept for all time."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_CHAMP
  sound "door_open"
  walk player right 1 up 6 left 1
  sound "stairs"
  warp "PokemonLeague_HallOfFame" 5 11 up
  endscene
  end


# The rival, once the battle is over (nobody stands here by then but in a script).
script Champion
  face champions_room_rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Go on, then. Don't keep the old man waiting."
  end
