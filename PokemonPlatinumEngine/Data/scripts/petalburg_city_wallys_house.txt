# Wally's house in Petalburg City (plan 21 · H4, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/PetalburgCity_WallysHouse/scripts.inc).
#
# Wally's father brings the player here from the Gym once Norman is beaten (VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE 4,
# petalburg_city_gym.txt) and gives HM03, Surf, as thanks for helping Wally catch his first Pokémon
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_SURF, VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE 5). Emerald walks the two of them from the Gym's door across the
# city to the house's; here the dark that closes in the Gym opens in the house.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE != 4 end
  scene "Surf from Wally's father"
  face player right
  face wallys_house_wallys_dad left
  speaker "Wally's Father"
  say "{player}, forgive me for hurrying you off like that. But I had to thank you properly."
  say "Wally's grown so much healthier since he went to Verdanturf. And it all began with the Pokémon you helped him catch."
  say "That made him happier than I've seen him in years. And it made me happy too, as his father, that he has a friend like you."
  say "It isn't much of a thank-you, but please take this."
  give "HM03"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_SURF
  say "HM03 holds Surf. A Pokémon that knows it can carry you over the water, to all sorts of places you couldn't reach before."
  setvar VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE 5
  endscene
  end


script WallysDad
  speaker "Wally's Father"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_SURF goto Surf
  if flag FLAG_THANKED_FOR_PLAYING_WITH_WALLY goto Wondering
  say "Ah, you must be {player}. Thank you for looking after our Wally earlier."
  say "He's been frail since he was a baby, so we've sent him to my relatives in Verdanturf Town. The air there is much cleaner."
  say "He's already set off, you see. I wonder how far he's got by now?"
  setflag FLAG_THANKED_FOR_PLAYING_WITH_WALLY
  end
label Wondering
  say "I wonder how our Wally is getting on."
  end
label Surf
  say "Wally will be coming home soon. I can hardly wait."
  end


script WallysMom
  speaker "Wally's Mother"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_SURF goto Secret
  say "Wally was so happy when he told us he'd caught a Pokémon. I hadn't seen him smile like that in ages."
  end
label Secret
  say "Don't tell my husband, but Wally has gone off from Verdanturf without a word to anyone."
  say "He's frail, but he's stubborn too. I'm sure he'll come home safe and sound one day."
  end
