# Shalour City's Gym (plan 23 · Z3, first part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Route 10 to Shalour City and the Key Stone, drawn by hand"): Korrina, a
# fighter on skates, battles for the Rumble Badge, Kalos's third (`givebadge rumble`), and gives a TM. The hall is one
# room with no puzzle (X and Y's rails are not built). VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: the Badge makes
# it 21, and Korrina is back at the tower (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_KORRINA_TOWER cleared). Her team is Kalos's trainers.json's
# kalos_korrina_1; her two trainers are the hall's own.


script Korrina
  face korrina player
  speaker "Korrina"
  if badge rumble goto Won
  scene "Korrina"
  say "You made it! I'm Korrina, and this is the Shalour Gym. We train on wheels here, so keep up!"
  say "Grandpa says a Trainer's heart shows in battle. Let's see what yours is made of!"
  battle self as "kalos_korrina_1"
  say "Wow! You hit hard and you never stopped moving. That's a real fighter!"
  say "This is the Rumble Badge. You've earned it, and Grandpa's Key Stone too, I bet."
  givebadge rumble
  text "{player} received the Rumble Badge from Korrina!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_SHALOUR_GYM
  defeat "kalos_black_belt_lucien"
  defeat "kalos_battle_girl_ines"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 21 goto Tm
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 21
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_KORRINA_TOWER
  objective kalosMastery
label Tm
  say "And take this. My Pokémon use it to tense every muscle before they strike, harder and harder."
  give "TM08"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_KORRINA
  say "Now go and see Grandpa at the tower. I'll skate over and meet you there!"
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_KORRINA goto Tm
  say "Grandpa's waiting for you at the tower. Go, go!"
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge rumble goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Korrina's Fighting types fear flying, psychic power and the fair folk. Choose well!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Korrina! Your fists must be made of iron."
  end
