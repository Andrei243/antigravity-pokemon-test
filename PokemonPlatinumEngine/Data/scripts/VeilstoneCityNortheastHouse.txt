# Veilstone City's north-east house (plan 01 · M11, part b), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_veilstone_city_northeast_house.s).
#
# The man of the house found a Porygon wandering near Team Galactic's building and gives it away at level 25, once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE_PORYGON; FLAG_TALKED_TO_VEILSTONE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE_POKEFAN_M once
# he has told how he came by it). Only a team with room takes it, as in the original. The nickname the original
# offers afterwards waits for the naming prompt (plan 12 · Q19).


script Porygon
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE_PORYGON goto After
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_VEILSTONE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE_POKEFAN_M goto Again
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_VEILSTONE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE_POKEFAN_M
  say "You're a Trainer, aren't you?" "You see, there's a Pokémon I'd like somebody to look after. It's called Porygon." "I found it wandering about near Team Galactic's building, and I'm no Trainer myself."
  ask "Would you take it off my hands?"
  goto Answer
label Again
  say "You're a Trainer, aren't you?" "About that Pokémon I'd like somebody to look after, the Porygon..."
  ask "Would you take it off my hands?"
label Answer
  if no goto Declined
  if party >= 6 goto Full
  say "Wonderful! Thank you! The Porygon will be glad of it too." "I suspect it ran away from those Team Galactic goons."
  givepokemon "Porygon" 25
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE_PORYGON
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received the Porygon!"
  end
label Full
  say "Thank you! ... Oh, but you've a whole team with you already." "If you want this Porygon, put one of them in a PC Box first."
  end
label Declined
  say "Is that so..." "Then I'll look after it a while longer. Do come and see me if you change your mind."
  end
label After
  say "That building of Team Galactic's bothers me so much I can hardly sleep." "It's so strange. Like a robot that might get up and walk off at any moment."
  end
