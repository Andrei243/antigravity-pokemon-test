# Route 14, the marshy trail north of Lumiose City (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): the neighbour
# waits half way up the trail and battles. VAR_KALOS_RIVAL_NORTH is 0 until that battle and 1 after. The neighbour's
# teams are Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_rival_route14_*: the starter the neighbour took at the café is the one strong
# against the player's.
#
# The people: rival (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_14, set by the scene).


# Up the trail from Lumiose (the trigger across the trail below the neighbour, on VAR_KALOS_RIVAL_NORTH 0)
script Neighbour
  scene "The neighbour on Route 14"
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! You got the power running again? Everyone in Lumiose is talking about the kid who beat Team Flare."
  say "I'm not going to fall behind you now. Let's battle, right here in the mud!"
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainstChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_route14_chespin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstChespin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_route14_fennekin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_route14_froakie" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto RivalWon
  say "Again! You're always one step ahead of me. I'll catch up, you'll see."
  goto Off
label RivalWon
  say "Got you this time! Don't let it go to your head... or mine."
  goto Off
label Off
  say "Laverre's Gym Leader uses fairies. They're prettier than they look, and tougher."
  walk rival up 3 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_14
  setvar VAR_KALOS_RIVAL_NORTH 1
  endscene
  end

script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Watch your step. Half this trail is puddle."
  end
