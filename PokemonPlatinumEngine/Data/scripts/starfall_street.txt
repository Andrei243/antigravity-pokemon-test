# Starfall Street (plan 34 · PD5): Team Star's five bases in a stand-in valley north of Mesagoza, until plan 34 · PD6
# moves each to its province. At each gate a grunt starts a raid (`starraid begin`): the player's first three Pokémon
# sent by Let's Go at the crew's Pokémon round the yard against the clock (Models/StarRaids.cs). The game starts the
# base's RaidWon or RaidLost script as the raid ends; won, the boss comes out of the gate and battles with the
# Starmobile, on the base's own stage. Cassiopeia calls as the street is first come to and after each boss.
# VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL counts:
#   0  not begun
#   1  Cassiopeia's first call heard at the street's mouth (the academy's welcome first, VAR_PALDEA_STORY 7)
#   2  to 6  one more for each boss beaten (FLAG_PALDEA_STAR_<BOSS>_BEATEN), 6 the five
# Each base: FLAG_PALDEA_STAR_<BOSS>_RAIDED once its raid is won (the boss shown), _BEATEN once the boss is.
# The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats.


# Out of the pass onto the street: the first call
script CassiopeiaCalls
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotYet
  scene "Cassiopeia's first call"
  sound "phone_ring"
  text "{player}'s phone buzzes. The caller's name is nothing but a little star."
  speaker "Cassiopeia"
  say "Hello. You don't know me, and that's fine. Call me Cassiopeia."
  say "This street is where Team Star keeps its five bases. They used to be students. Now they mostly guard gates."
  say "I want them broken up. Raid a gate, beat the boss behind it. I'll be watching. Good luck, {player}."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL 1
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  end


# Cassiopeia after a boss: a word for each, and her thanks after the fifth
script CassiopeiaAfter
  sound "phone_ring"
  speaker "Cassiopeia"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL >= 6 goto AllFive
  say "I saw that. One more crew without its boss. The others will have heard by now."
  say "Take a rest if you need it. The gates aren't going anywhere."
  return
label AllFive
  say "That's the fifth. Every base on Starfall Street has lost its boss."
  say "Thank you, {player}. Really. There's more I should tell you, but not over the phone. Not yet."
  return


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Giacomo's base: the Dark crew, the west gate

script GiacomoGate
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_STAR_GIACOMO_BEATEN goto Quiet
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_STAR_GIACOMO_RAIDED goto Out
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL < 1 goto Shoo
  faceto giacomo_grunt player
  speaker "Team Star Grunt"
  say "A raid? On us? Fine! Our Pokémon will run you right off the street."
  ask "Start the raid on the gate?"
  if no goto Later
  placeat player giacomo_gate
  text "Fell fifteen of the crew's Pokémon before the clock runs out! Send your team out with Let's Go!"
  starraid begin "giacomo"
  end
label Later
  say "Thought so. Off you go."
  end
label Shoo
  speaker "Team Star Grunt"
  say "Nothing to see here. This is a Team Star gate, so keep walking."
  end
label Out
  speaker "Team Star Grunt"
  say "The boss is out. Don't look at me, go and talk to him."
  end
label Quiet
  speaker "Team Star Grunt"
  say "No boss, no base. Guess I'll go to class tomorrow. Maybe."
  end

script GiacomoRaidWon
  scene "Giacomo comes out of his gate"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_STAR_GIACOMO_RAIDED
  text "The last of the crew's Pokémon falls back, and the gate's shutter rattles up."
  faceto player giacomo_boss
  speaker "Giacomo"
  say "So you're the one wrecking my set. I spin the tracks on this street, and the music's not over."
  say "Meet the Starmobile. Let's turn it up!"
  endscene
  call GiacomoFight
  end

script GiacomoRaidLost
  placeat player giacomo_gate
  text "Time's up! The crew rallies and the gate holds. Catch your breath and try again."
  end

script GiacomoBoss
  faceto giacomo_boss player
  speaker "Giacomo"
  say "Back for an encore? Fine by me."
  call GiacomoFight
  end

script GiacomoFight
  battle giacomo_boss as "paldea_star_giacomo"
  speaker "Giacomo"
  say "The record skipped. All right, you win. The crew's done here."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_STAR_GIACOMO_BEATEN
  addvar VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL 1
  call CassiopeiaAfter
  return
