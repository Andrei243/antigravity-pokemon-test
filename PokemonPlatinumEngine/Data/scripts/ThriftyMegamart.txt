# The old Thrifty Megamart, Tapu Village, Ula'ula Island: Captain Acerola's trial (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own,
# on Sun and Moon's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ula'ula, Poni, Aether Paradise
# and the League"): three ghosts drift about the shop's shelves and the totem Mimikyu waits at the counter. The totem
# is fought as a totem (plan 23 · Z6: its aura, a Haunter at its first call, no ball, no running; wave 91). VAR_ALOLA_STORY 44 is the way in, 45 the
# trial passed.
#
# The people: acerola, the three ghosts and the totem are shown by FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_TRIAL while the trial is under way.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY != 44 end
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_TRIAL end
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_TRIAL_DONE end
  scene "Acerola's trial begins"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_TRIAL
  speaker "Acerola"
  say "Welcome to the old Thrifty Megamart! Everything's half off. Forever."
  say "Three little ghosts live among the shelves. Find them, and be gentle, and I'll let you meet my Mimikyu."
  say "He's very shy, so don't be surprised if he's very strong."
  endscene
  end


script AcerolaInside
  speaker "Acerola"
  say "Three ghosts, then Mimikyu. Mind the aisle with the cold spot. It's not a draught."
  end


script Den1
  text "A Gastly drifted out from between the shelves!"
  wildbattle "Gastly" 23 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_DEN_1
  hide self
  end

script Den2
  text "A Misdreavus drifted out from between the shelves!"
  wildbattle "Misdreavus" 24 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_DEN_2
  hide self
  end

script Den3
  text "A Duskull drifted out from between the shelves!"
  wildbattle "Duskull" 24 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_DEN_3
  hide self
  end


script Totem
  speaker none
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_DEN_1 goto NotYet
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_DEN_2 goto NotYet
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_DEN_3 goto NotYet
  scene "The totem Mimikyu"
  text "A little cloth Pikachu peeked out from behind the counter... and the dark behind it began to move!"
  cry "Mimikyu"
  # A totem (plan 23 · Z6): its aura raises every stat, it calls a Haunter at once, and it can't be caught
  wildbattle "Mimikyu" 25 totem all ally "Haunter"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_TRIAL_DONE
  hide self
  hide acerola
  speaker "Acerola"
  say "You did it! Mimikyu likes you. I can tell, because he didn't eat you."
  say "This is the Ghostium Z. With it, a Ghost-type move becomes a Z-Move. Look after it, and look after the ghosts."
  give "Ghostium Z"
  say "Team Skull has taken over Po Town, north of the village. Their boss is bad news. Be careful, okay?"
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 45
  objective alolaPoTown
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  text "The counter's quiet. The ghosts among the shelves come first."
  end


# Meltan (plan 23, Alola finished; plan 29 · LS11's rule for Alola, docs/plans/decisions/LS11-proposals-event-legendaries.md,
# "Alola", built to its recommended answers, which the user may still change). No game of Alola has it: the games give it
# from elsewhere. Ours nibbles the old shop's bolts once Alola's Hall of Fame is entered (the room shows it by the region's
# flag), at level 60, and leaves behind it the 400 Meltan Candy that Melmetal's evolution asks for, once
# (FLAG_ALOLA_MELTAN_MET hides it, whatever the battle's end).
script Meltan
  scene "Meltan in the old Thrifty Megamart"
  music stop
  letterbox on 0.4
  # (one tile south of it, the player would hide it behind their head: they step back first)
  if var PLAYER_X != 13 goto Look
  if var PLAYER_Y != 12 goto Look
  walk player down 2
  face player up
label Look
  camera pan 13 11 1.0
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  shake self 6
  effect sparkles 13 11 1.5
  cry "Meltan"
  speaker none
  text "A little drop of liquid metal was gnawing a shelf's last bolt. Its hex nut of a head swung round to {player}!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Meltan" 60
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MELTAN_MET
  hide self
  music area
  text "Where it had fed, the shelf's floor glittered with little nuggets of metal, sweet-smelling and warm."
  give "Meltan Candy" 400
  text "They are what a Meltan sheds as it grows. Enough of them in the Bag, and a Meltan becomes something greater."
  end
