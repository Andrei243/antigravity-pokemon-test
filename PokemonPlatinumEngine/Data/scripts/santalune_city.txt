# Santalune City (plan 23 · Z1, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos drawn by hand, its first chapter and its roller skates"): the neighbour waits by
# the way in from Route 3 and battles, the friends gather on the square, and the Gym's Badge (SantaluneCity_Gym.txt)
# is the first of Kalos. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 7, Tierno's battle had, becomes 8 here, and 9
# once the Bug Badge is won.
#
# The people: rival (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_SANTALUNE, cleared on Route 3), shauna (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_SHAUNA_SANTALUNE) and
# tierno and trevor (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_FRIENDS_SANTALUNE), shown on the square by the neighbour's scene. The neighbour's
# teams are Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_rival_santalune_*: the Pokémon the neighbour took at the café is the one strong
# against the player's (Fennekin against Chespin, Froakie against Fennekin, Chespin against Froakie).


# A save from before this part of the story was written finds the people of Santalune's part of the story as a
# new game does
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_SANTALUNE_BEGUN call KalosSantalune
  end


# Into the city from Route 3 (the trigger across the way in, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 7): the neighbour wants a battle
script Neighbour
  scene "The neighbour in Santalune"
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "You made it, {player}. I've been waiting. The forest wasn't too much trouble, I hope?"
  say "Let's see how far our partners have come since the café. Ready?"
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainstChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_santalune_chespin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstChespin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_santalune_fennekin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_santalune_froakie" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto RivalWon
  say "You're quick to learn. I'll have to train harder."
  goto Gym
label RivalWon
  say "A good battle. You'll catch up soon, I'm sure of it."
label Gym
  say "The Gym here is north of the fountain. Its Leader, Viola, uses Bug types and she's very patient."
  say "Earn her Badge, and the road to Lumiose City opens up. I'll go on ahead and meet you there."
  walk rival right 5 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_SANTALUNE
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_SHAUNA_SANTALUNE
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_FRIENDS_SANTALUNE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 8
  objective kalosViola
  endscene
  end


# The friends on the square
script Shauna
  speaker "Shauna"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 9 goto Badge
  say "No Pikachu yet... but look at this fountain! Santalune's so pretty. Are you going to take on the Gym?"
  end
label Badge
  say "Your very first Badge! Ooh, let me see it! It's so shiny!"
  end

script Tierno
  speaker "Tierno"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 9 goto Badge
  say "Viola's bugs are tough, so go in with a Pokémon that can fly or burn. That's what I heard, anyway!"
  end
label Badge
  say "One Badge down! That deserves a dance. Or a pastry. Or both!"
  end

script Trevor
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "Every Pokémon here is in my Pokédex now. Well. Almost every one."
  end

script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Over here, {player}."
  end


# The road north to Route 4 and Lumiose City (plan 23 · Z2): before the Bug Badge (VAR_KALOS_STORY 8) the guard turns the
# player back; once Viola is beaten (9) the way is open
script NotYet
  text "A guard stands at the garden gate: only Trainers with a Badge may take the road to Lumiose City."
  walk player down 1
  end
