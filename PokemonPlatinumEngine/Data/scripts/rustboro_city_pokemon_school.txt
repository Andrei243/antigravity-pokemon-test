# The Pokémon Trainers' School in Rustboro City (plan 21 · H2, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/RustboroCity_PokemonSchool/scripts.inc): the teacher's Quick Claw
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_QUICK_CLAW), and Scott, who watches the class until the PokéNav is had.


script Teacher
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_QUICK_CLAW goto Explain
  say "Welcome to the Trainers' School! Our pupils learn everything from type matchups to held items."
  say "You're a trainer already, I can tell. Here, a little something for your Pokémon to hold."
  give "Quick Claw"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_QUICK_CLAW
  end
label Explain
  say "A Pokémon holding a Quick Claw will sometimes get to move first, however slow it is. Isn't that clever?"
  end


script Scott
  say "Hmm? Oh, don't mind me. I'm just watching the young trainers here. I'm always looking for talent."
  say "You look like you've got some yourself. I'll be keeping an eye on you!"
  end
