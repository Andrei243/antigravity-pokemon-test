# Mr. Pokémon's house at the north end of Route 30 (plan 20 · J4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_R30R0201).
#
# The errand's end: Mr. Pokémon hands over the Mystery Egg (FLAG_GOT_MYSTERY_EGG: a thing of the story here, no item
# in the bag, until a Johto key item of its own is wanted; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Johto's first chapter"),
# Professor Oak, visiting, gives the Pokédex and leaves, and Elm rings about the theft: the lab, the rival north of
# Cherrygrove City and the policeman are set for the way back (VAR_SCENE_ELMS_LAB 3).


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_GOT_MYSTERY_EGG end
  if var VAR_SCENE_ELMS_LAB < 1 end
  scene "The Mystery Egg"
  face r30r0201_gsgentleman player
  speaker "Mr. Pokémon"
  say "Hello, hello! You must be {player}. Elm told me you'd be coming in his place. Do come in!"
  say "This is what I wanted him to see. A friend of mine in the south found it and passed it to me."
  say "It's an Egg! Or at least, everyone I've asked says it must be. Nobody can tell me what's inside."
  say "Elm is the man for Pokémon Eggs. Would you take it back to him?"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_MYSTERY_EGG
  objective johtoBackToElm
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Mystery Egg!"
  face r30r0201_ookido player
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "So you're the one Elm trusted with his errand. I'm Oak. I study Pokémon too, over in Kanto."
  say "I happened to be passing through on the way to Goldenrod. I do a little radio show there, you see."
  say "That {starter} of yours looks very happy with you. You two will go far together."
  say "Here. I think you'll put this to better use than I could. It's a Pokédex."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex from Professor Oak!"
  say "It records every Pokémon you meet. Fill it in as you travel, and come and show me some day!"
  say "Well, I must be off. My show won't present itself."
  hide r30r0201_ookido
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MR_POKEMONS_HOUSE_OAK
  face r30r0201_gsgentleman player
  speaker "Mr. Pokémon"
  say "Your Pokémon looks worn out from the road. Let it rest a moment."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  sound "select"
  text "Brrrring! Brrrring! It's the Pokégear..."
  speaker "Prof. Elm"
  say "{player}?! Thank goodness. Something terrible has happened! Come back to the lab as soon as you can!"
  text "Click... The call has ended."
  setvar VAR_SCENE_ELMS_LAB 3
  setvar VAR_SCENE_CHERRYGROVE_CITY_OW 3
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ELMS_LAB_OFFICER
  endscene
  end


# Mr. Pokémon afterwards. Shown the Red Scale the Lake of Rage's Gyarados left behind (lake_of_rage.txt), he offers
# an Exp. Share for it, as HeartGold's does; refused, he asks again the next time (FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_EXP_SHARE, ours).
script MrPokemon
  speaker "Mr. Pokémon"
  if item "Red Scale" goto Scale
  # The orbs of the Embedded Tower (Johto's leftovers; our words on HeartGold's beat): the Red and Blue Orbs for Johto's
  # Champion who holds Kanto's eight Badges too, and the Jade Orb once Groudon and Kyogre have both been met there
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame goto NoOrbs
  if badges of kanto < 8 goto NoOrbs
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_ORBS goto Orbs
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_JADE_ORB goto NoOrbs
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_GROUDON goto NoOrbs
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_KYOGRE goto Jade
label NoOrbs
  if flag FLAG_GOT_MYSTERY_EGG goto After
  say "Hello, hello! Have a look round. Everything in here came from my travels."
  end
label After
  say "Elm will get to the bottom of that Egg, mark my words. Do come and tell me what hatches!"
  end
label Scale
  say "Hm? What's that you've got there? A scale... a red one! Oh my, I've never seen a Gyarados scale that colour!"
  ask "I simply must add it to my collection. Would you trade it for my Exp. Share?"
  if no goto Keep
  take "Red Scale"
  say "Wonderful! Here you are, as promised."
  give "Exp. Share"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_EXP_SHARE
  say "A Pokémon holding the Exp. Share earns a share of the experience from every battle, even sitting out."
  end
label Keep
  say "No? Well, I can see why you'd want to keep it. Do bring it back if you change your mind!"
  end
label Orbs
  say "Ah, the Champion of Johto and Kanto both! Then you're the one to have these. I found them on my travels in Hoenn."
  give "Red Orb"
  give "Blue Orb"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_ORBS
  say "They say a tower is buried in the cliffs past Cianwood, on Route 47. Take the orbs there and see what wakes!"
  end
label Jade
  say "You met both of the giants in the tower? Then this must be what the third orb was waiting for."
  give "Jade Orb"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_JADE_ORB
  say "The legend speaks of one who rules the sky and calms the land and the sea. Go back to the tower with that orb!"
  end
