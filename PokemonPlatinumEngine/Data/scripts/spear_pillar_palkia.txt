# Spear Pillar with Palkia's rift over its altar (plan 02 · S15, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_spear_pillar_palkia.s).
#
# As spear_pillar_dialga.txt, for whoever holds the Lustrous Orb: Palkia at level 70 behind its rift, quiet once it has
# been battled (VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE 1) until the Hall of Fame is next entered, and gone for good once caught
# (FLAG_CAUGHT_PALKIA). A game that holds both orbs is led to Dialga's pillar first. Its meeting is the same beat of plan 29
# as Dialga's (style guide, "Legendary scenes").


# SpearPillarPalkia_Rift
script Rift
  speaker none
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE >= 1 goto Quiet
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_PALKIA goto Quiet
  text "Beyond the rift lies a place where nothing is the way it ought to be..." "And in it, looking back: Palkia!"
  ask "Will you challenge Palkia?"
  if no end
  scene "Palkia's rift"
  music stop
  face player up
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 33 15 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  slowmo 0.5 1.5
  effect ripple 33 17
  wait 1
  effect burst 33 17
  cry "Palkia"
  camera shake 0.6
  emote player exclaim
  text "Palkia pushes out of the rift, and the space round it folds!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Palkia" 70
  setvar VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE 1
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_PALKIA
  end
label Quiet
  text "Space itself seems to pour out of the rift, more of it than anyone could find a way across." "Where does it lead?"
  end


# The Azure Flute played on the top step (plan 29 · LS11a): the original's trigger before the way down, at 31, 52 of
# each of the summit's three maps, while VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE is 1, which Celestic Town's elder sets as she gives
# the flute. Coming up from the mountain is stepping onto it, so the question is asked as the summit is come to. Yes is
# a stair of light up the pillar's steps (plan 29 · LS10's `stair`, held, climbing away north from the steps' foot as
# the camera goes up after it) and the climb to the Hall of Origin (hall_of_origin.txt). The three summits' files each
# hold this script.
script AzureFlute
  speaker none
  ask "The Azure Flute is humming softly in {player}'s bag. Play it?"
  if no end
  scene "The stair of light"
  music stop
  face player up
  letterbox on 0.4
  sound "use_grass_whistle"
  wait 0.8
  camera path 31 48 3 nowait
  effect stair 31 50 hold
  effect sparkles 31 50
  wait 1.2
  text "A stair of light rises from the pillar's steps, up and up into the sky!"
  fade out 0.6
  effect stop 31 50
  camera release 0.1
  letterbox off 0.1
  warp "HallOfOrigin" 31 52 up
  fade in
  endscene
  end
