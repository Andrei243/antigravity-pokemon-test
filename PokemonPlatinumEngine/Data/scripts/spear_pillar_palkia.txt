# Spear Pillar with Palkia's rift over its altar (plan 02 · S15, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_spear_pillar_palkia.s).
#
# As spear_pillar_dialga.txt, for whoever holds the Lustrous Orb: Palkia at level 70 behind its rift, quiet once it has
# been battled (VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE 1) until the Hall of Fame is next entered, and gone for good once caught
# (FLAG_CAUGHT_PALKIA). A game that holds both orbs is led to Dialga's pillar first.


# SpearPillarPalkia_Rift
script Rift
  speaker none
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE >= 1 goto Quiet
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_PALKIA goto Quiet
  text "Beyond the rift lies a place where nothing is the way it ought to be..." "And in it, looking back: Palkia!"
  ask "Will you challenge Palkia?"
  if no end
  cry "Palkia"
  camera shake 0.6
  text "Palkia: Gagyagyaah!"
  wildbattle "Palkia" 70
  setvar VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_PALKIA_STATE 1
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_PALKIA
  end
label Quiet
  text "Space itself seems to pour out of the rift, more of it than anyone could find a way across." "Where does it lead?"
  end
