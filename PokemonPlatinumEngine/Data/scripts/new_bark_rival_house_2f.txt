# The room upstairs next door in New Bark Town (plan 20 · J12, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on HeartGold's beat of the friend at home once all eight Badges are won (its rival's-room scene).
#
# From the Rising Badge (FLAG_UNK_0EA, dragons_den_shrine.txt) the friend waits in their room until Johto's Hall of Fame
# (the overlay's npcs: shown by the one, hidden by the other), and sends the player off toward the League.


script FriendAtHome
  face self player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_FRIEND_SAW_THE_EIGHTH_BADGE goto Again
  say "{player}! Mom said you'd come by. Is it true? All eight of Johto's Badges?"
  say "I knew it. Ever since you chose your first Pokémon in Professor Elm's lab, I knew you'd go all the way."
  say "The road to the League runs west from here, by Route 27 and Tohjo Falls. Go on. I'll be cheering for you!"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_FRIEND_SAW_THE_EIGHTH_BADGE
  end
label Again
  say "What are you still doing here? The League is waiting for you!"
  end
