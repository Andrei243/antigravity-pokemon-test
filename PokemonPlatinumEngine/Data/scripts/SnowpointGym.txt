# The Snowpoint Gym (plan 01 · M9, part 2b), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_snowpoint_city_gym.s).
#
# A bowl of ice in four rings, each a tile lower than the one round it, joined by icy slopes, with Candice's dais at
# the top of the far side. Ice slides the player on until something stops them or they come onto snow; going down a
# slope gathers speed, going up one loses it, and a slide with speed in it smashes the snowballs in its way (the
# game's own rule, IceSlide). The snowballs are all back each time the player comes in. The room's own script in the
# original hides Candice in Snowpoint City behind a flag nothing ever sets (FLAG_DUMMY_0x00EB), so it has no part here.


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge icicle goto Won
  say "Hey there! How's it going, future Champ?"
  say "See those giant snowballs all over the place? Get a good slide going down the ice and you'll smash straight through them!"
  say "Candice uses Ice types. Thaw them out with some red-hot Fire moves!"
  say "This Gym is all about focus. Keep your head clear and your feet moving!"
  end
label Won
  say "{player}! You beat Candice! Your focus was even sharper than hers, and your Pokémon could feel it!"
  end


script GymStatue
  speaker none
  if badge icicle goto Won
  say "SNOWPOINT CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Candice. Certified Trainers: {rival}"
  end
label Won
  say "SNOWPOINT CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Candice. Certified Trainers: {rival}, {player}"
  end


script Candice
  speaker "Candice"
  if badge icicle goto Won
  say "A challenger? For me? All right! I've been hoping somebody tough would come along!"
  say "Just so you know, I'm tough too. And it's because I know how to focus."
  say "Battling, picking out the perfect scarf, falling for someone... Whatever it is, it all comes down to focus!"
  say "I'll show you exactly what I mean. Get ready to lose!"
  battle self as "leader_candice"
  say "Wow, you're amazing! You've totally earned my respect."
  say "Your focus and your will just bowled my team right over. Oh, that's right! I'm meant to give you this!"
  givebadge icicle
  text "{player} received the Icicle Badge from Candice!"
  defeat "ace_trainer_sergio"
  defeat "ace_trainer_isaiah"
  defeat "ace_trainer_anton"
  defeat "ace_trainer_savannah"
  defeat "ace_trainer_alicia"
  defeat "ace_trainer_brenna"
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_GALACTIC_GRUNTS
  say "With the Icicle Badge, your Pokémon can use Rock Climb whenever you like, even outside of battle!"
  say "Oh, and take this too!"
label Tm
  give "TM72"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CANDICE_TM72
  say "TM72 is Avalanche. If the user gets hit before it moves, the move comes crashing back twice as hard!"
  say "Can you imagine the focus it takes to stand there and wait for that hit?"
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CANDICE_TM72 goto Tm
  say "Hey, do you think I should act more like an Ice-type Leader? You know, all cool and aloof?"
  say "I tried it once. I lasted about five seconds before I started giggling!"
  end
