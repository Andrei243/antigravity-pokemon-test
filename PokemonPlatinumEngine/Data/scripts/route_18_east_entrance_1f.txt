# As Route 16's gate (route_16_north_entrance_1f.txt), from the road's foot: the guard calls back whoever crosses the
# room westward without a Bicycle, and the steps by the doors note going onto the road (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE16 1, by
# the west door) or coming off it (0, by the east door).


script NeedBike
  if item "Bicycle" end
  speaker "Guard"
  face player up
  say "Excuse me! The Cycling Road is for Bicycles only. You'll need one to go that way."
  walk player right 1
  end


script OntoTheRoad
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE16 1
  end


script OffTheRoad
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE16 0
  end
