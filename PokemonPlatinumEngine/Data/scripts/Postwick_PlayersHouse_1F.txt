# The player's house in Postwick, downstairs (plan 33 · GL1). The lines are our own, on Sword and Shield's beats as
# they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Galar's first chapter"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is postwick.txt's
# count. A Champion who crosses in from Alola lands inside the door with the story at 0, and has the same first morning.


# Coming down on the first morning (or in through the door from Alola): Mum asks which of the two the player will
# wear at the opening ceremony, which is the version the game is (VAR_GALAR_VERSION, plan 33 · decision 2), and says
# Hop is at the door. Coming down the morning after the Weald: Poké Balls for the road, and the way to the professor
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 0 goto Morning
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 5 goto AfterTheWeald
  end
label Morning
  scene "Mum on the first morning"
  face mum player
  speaker "Mum"
  say "Morning, love! Did you sleep? You were up half the night watching the Champion's last match again."
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION != 0 goto Hop
  say "I've finished your scarf for the opening ceremony, by the way. Which crest should I stitch on it?"
  choose "Which crest for the scarf?" "The sword" "The shield"
  if result == 1 goto Shield
  setvar VAR_GALAR_VERSION 1
  say "The sword it is. Sharp and bold, just like you."
  goto Hop
label Shield
  setvar VAR_GALAR_VERSION 2
  say "The shield it is. Steady and true, just like you."
label Hop
  say "Oh, and Hop came round. He's waiting out front. Something about his brother's train?"
  setflag FLAG_HAS_RUNNING_SHOES
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 1
  objective galarHop
  endscene
  end
label AfterTheWeald
  scene "Mum after the Weald"
  face mum player
  speaker "Mum"
  say "There you are. Leon carried the pair of you home last night, fast asleep. The Weald, of all places!"
  say "He says you're both to go and see Professor Magnolia today. Here, take these. You'll want to catch a friend or two."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "Hop's gone on ahead to Wedgehurst. Mind the grass on Route 1, and come home if you're tired."
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_WEDGEHURST
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 6
  objective galarWedgehurst
  endscene
  end


# Mum: a rest whenever the team has one
script Mum
  if party > 0 goto Rest
  speaker "Mum"
  say "Hop's waiting for you outside. Go on, don't keep him standing there!"
  end
label Rest
  sayown
  heal
  fanfare heal
  call NextHint
  end
