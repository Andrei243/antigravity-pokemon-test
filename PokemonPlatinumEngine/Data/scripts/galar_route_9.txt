# Route 9 (plan 33 · GL5, its second session), Circhester Bay's two shores, on the open world's engine: its places are
# Data/world3d/galar/places/galar_route_9.json. The lines are our own, on Sword and Shield's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Circhester, Route 9, Spikemuth and Hammerlocke's stadium"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is
# postwick.txt's count: 32 the sixth badge, 33 the bike fitted for the water, 34 Marnie at Spikemuth's gate.
# FLAG_GALAR_BIKE_WATER is what lets the player onto the bay's deep water (OpenWorld/OpenGrants.cs, handed to the field every frame): a swim of
# the player's own until the Rotom Bike has an engine of its own.


# The mechanic on the east shore: fits the challenge's bike for the water once Circhester's gym is won
script BikeKit
  face bike_mechanic player
  speaker "Mechanic"
  if flag FLAG_GALAR_BIKE_WATER goto Fitted
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY < 32 goto NotYet
  scene "The bike's water kit"
  say "A challenger with six badges, and no way over the bay? Every League bike can take to the water. It only wants the kit."
  say "Hand it here a minute... a float under each wheel, a turbine on the back... There! Off you go across."
  text "{player}'s bike was fitted for the water! Circhester Bay can be crossed now."
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BIKE_WATER
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_MARNIE_ROUTE_9
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 33
  objective galarSpikemuth
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "Water kits for League bikes, fitted while you wait. But only for challengers who've beaten Circhester's gym."
  end
label Fitted
  say "The kit's holding up? Spikemuth's straight over the bay. Mind the cold if you fall in."
  end


# Spikemuth's gate on the west shore: its shutter down to everyone until Marnie, coming over the bay behind the player,
# lifts it; then open
script SpikemuthGate
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 34 end
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 33 goto Marnie
  text "The shutter over Spikemuth's gate is down and locked. Team Yell have painted it all over."
  walkto player route9_gate_back
  end
label Marnie
  scene "Marnie at the gate"
  faceto player marnie_route9
  text "The shutter over Spikemuth's gate was down and locked, and Team Yell's paint was all over it."
  speaker "Marnie"
  say "Sorry about that. Team Yell shut it so challengers can't get to my brother. They think they're helping."
  say "They're not. Piers wants to battle you as much as anyone. Hang on."
  text "Marnie knocked twice on the shutter, and it rattled up into the wall."
  speaker "Marnie"
  say "Spikemuth's Leader is up on the stage at the end of the street. Go on. I'll be there."
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_MARNIE_ROUTE_9
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_MARNIE_SPIKEMUTH
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 34
  objective galarPiers
  endscene
  end
