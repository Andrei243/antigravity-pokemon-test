# The Pokémon League's battle court (plan 34 · PD8, its first session): the Champion assessment after Rika's interview
# in the lobby. The Elite Four battle one after another on the same spot of the court (Rika, Poppy, Larry, Hassel), then
# the Top Champion Geeta, and last Nemona, who ends the assessment with the battle she has wanted since Cabo Poco; then
# the Hall of Fame, Paldea's story to here ended (PaldeaHallOfFame), home to Cabo Poco and the game saved there (the
# credits roll after the Way Home, AreaZero_ZeroLab.txt, since plan 34 · PD8's second session). Whoever is due stands at the court's far end, shown by the court's arrival. Everyone Terastallizes their last
# Pokémon. A loss keeps the challenger's place: the one who won waits for them again. Levels are the originals' (plan 34 ·
# decision 3: the League asks eight badges, so nothing is scaled). Our own lines.
#
# VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE: 1 interviewed, 2 Rika beaten, 3 Poppy, 4 Larry, 5 Hassel, 6 Geeta (a Champion), 7 Nemona and the
# Hall of Fame entered.


script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_RIKA
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_POPPY
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_LARRY
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_HASSEL
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_GEETA
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_NEMONA
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE == 1 goto RikaDue
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE == 2 goto PoppyDue
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE == 3 goto LarryDue
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE == 4 goto HasselDue
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE == 5 goto GeetaDue
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE == 6 goto NemonaDue
  end
label RikaDue
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_RIKA
  end
label PoppyDue
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_POPPY
  end
label LarryDue
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_LARRY
  end
label HasselDue
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_HASSEL
  end
label GeetaDue
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_GEETA
  end
label NemonaDue
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_NEMONA
  end


# The first of the Elite Four: Rika, Ground
script Rika
  face rika player
  speaker "Rika"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 2 goto Done
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE < 1 goto NotYet
  scene "Rika of the Elite Four"
  say "Told you I'd be right behind you. The interview was the easy part. Now let's see whether your team answers as well as you did."
  say "I'm Rika of the Elite Four, and my Pokémon keep their feet on the ground. Let's dig in!"
  battle rika as "paldea_rika"
  say "Well, well. That answer I'll be writing down in capital letters. Poppy's next, and don't let her size fool you."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 2
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_RIKA
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_POPPY
  hide rika
  show poppy
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "Interview first, then battles. My desk's in the lobby."
  end
label Done
  say "On you go. The next one's waiting."
  end


# The second: Poppy, Steel
script Poppy
  face poppy player
  speaker "Poppy"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 3 goto Done
  scene "Poppy of the Elite Four"
  say "Hi hi! I'm Poppy, and I'm one of the Elite Four too. The grown-ups always look surprised when I say that."
  say "My Pokémon are super tough, tougher than steel. Ready? You'd better be!"
  battle poppy as "paldea_poppy"
  say "Aww, you beat me fair and square. That's okay. I'll get even stronger and then I'll beat you back!"
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 3
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_POPPY
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_LARRY
  hide poppy
  show larry
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "Go go go! The next one's waiting!"
  end


# The third: Larry again, Medali's leader, here with his Flying types
script Larry
  face larry player
  speaker "Larry"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 4 goto Done
  scene "Larry of the Elite Four"
  say "You again. Yes, I keep Medali's gym. I also sit on the Elite Four. The League found out I was good at both and gave me both. Typical."
  say "Normal types are my day job. Up here I fly. Let's get this done before the overtime starts."
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_elite"
  say "...Right. Beaten twice by the same challenger. I'll file that under things I'm not surprised by. Hassel's next."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 4
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_LARRY
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_HASSEL
  hide larry
  show hassel
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "Go on. I'm off duty in five minutes."
  end


# The last of the four: Hassel, Dragon
script Hassel
  face hassel player
  speaker "Hassel"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 5 goto Done
  scene "Hassel of the Elite Four"
  say "Ah. You've come so far, and you've come in colour. I'm Hassel. At the academy I teach art; here, I am the last of the Elite Four."
  say "A battle is a canvas, and my dragons paint with a heavy brush. Show me what you've made of your journey!"
  battle hassel as "paldea_hassel"
  say "Magnificent... I may weep. A masterpiece, and not of my making. Geeta, the Top Champion, waits for you now."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 5
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_HASSEL
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_GEETA
  hide hassel
  show geeta
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "The Top Champion will see you now. Go and finish your painting."
  end


# The Top Champion, Geeta: a challenger who beats her is a Champion, and Nemona comes onto the court
script Geeta
  face geeta player
  speaker "Geeta"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 6 goto Done
  scene "Geeta the Top Champion"
  say "Welcome. I am Geeta, chair of the Pokémon League and its Top Champion. The four have told me about you, and they rarely agree."
  say "The last step of the assessment is mine. Show me the Trainer Paldea's gyms and Elite Four have shaped."
  battle geeta as "paldea_geeta"
  say "...Well. There's no arguing with that. By the League's authority, you are a Champion of Paldea. Congratulations."
  say "Only, it seems somebody has been waiting for that word longer than any of us."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 6
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_GEETA
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_NEMONA
  hide geeta
  show nemona
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "A Champion should never keep a rival waiting."
  end


# Nemona, the champion assessment's end: the battle she promised in Cabo Poco, one Champion to another; then the Hall
# of Fame and home
script Nemona
  face nemona player
  speaker "Nemona"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 7 goto Done
  scene "Nemona Champion to Champion"
  say "{player}! You did it! Geeta says you're a Champion, so that makes two of us, and you know what that means."
  say "Ever since that first battle on my terrace I've wanted this one: you and me, both at our very best, no holding back."
  say "Okay. Deep breath. Let's go!"
  if starter "Sprigatito" goto AgainstSprigatito
  if starter "Fuecoco" goto AgainstFuecoco
  battle nemona as "paldea_nemona_8c"
  goto Fought
label AgainstSprigatito
  battle nemona as "paldea_nemona_8a"
  goto Fought
label AgainstFuecoco
  battle nemona as "paldea_nemona_8b"
label Fought
  speaker "Nemona"
  say "...That was the best battle of my whole life. I mean it. I've never been so happy to lose!"
  say "Come on, rival. Let's get your team into the Hall of Fame where it belongs. Then we start all over again tomorrow!"
  endscene
  wait 0.4
  fade out 0.8
  halloffame
  setflag PaldeaHallOfFame
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 7
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_NEMONA
  halloffame show
  heal
  warp "CaboPoco_PlayersHouse_2F" 4 6 down
  save
  if result == 0 goto NotSaved
  sound "save"
  text "{player} saved the game."
  goto Roll
label NotSaved
  text "The game could not be saved."
label Roll
  end
label Done
  say "Champion to Champion, any time you like. And I mean any time!"
  end
