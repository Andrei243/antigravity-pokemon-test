# With the Insect Badge won (10), the stranger from the sewers (0, Colress, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_PLAZA) stops the
# player in the passage north, looks over the team, names himself and goes on to wait on Route 4 (11).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


# The passage north to the gate street (our trigger on 13 to 18, 3, at 10)
script Colress
  scene "Colress on the plaza"
  face 0 player
  speaker "???"
  ask "Pardon me. If it isn't too much trouble, might I have a look at your Pokémon?"
  if no goto Anyway
  say "I'm most grateful for your cooperation!"
  goto Looks
label Anyway
  say "Oh, come now. It's all in the name of science!"
label Looks
  say "Oh! How very interesting! Your {lead} carries itself with more confidence than others of its kind."
  say "And you a Trainer with only three Badges..."
  say "Fantastic! I couldn't tell you how you're doing it, but you're drawing out the power of your Pokémon!"
  speaker "Colress"
  say "Ah, forgive me. I'm a scientist, and my name is Colress. The subject of my research is a single question: how does one bring out a Pokémon's full power?"
  say "Is it the bond a Pokémon shares with its Trainer? Or is there some other way entirely? That's what I intend to find out."
  ask "I'd like to test my theory in a battle with you. Would you find that acceptable?"
  say "Either way, I'll be waiting for you on Route 4. It's just past the gate up ahead!"
  walk 0 up 1 fast
  hide 0
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_PLAZA
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_ROUTE_4
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 11
  endscene
  end
