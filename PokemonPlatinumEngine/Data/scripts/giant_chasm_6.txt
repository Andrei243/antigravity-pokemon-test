# The cavern at the heart of the Giant Chasm, north of the crater (plan 22 · U9, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/giant_chasm_6.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Ghetsis (ghetsis) waits on the cavern's floor with Kyurem (kyurem) before him. As the player comes up the corridor (our trigger,
# 14 to 17, 25; VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 3) he calls on Kyurem's ice; N (n) comes with Zekrom (zekrom), Black 2's dragon, to stop him;
# Ghetsis uses the DNA Splicers and Kyurem takes Zekrom into itself (kyurem_black). Ghetsis sends it at the player: the battle is
# a team of ours, Ghetsis's Kyurem in its black form (unova_ghetsis_kyurem in our-trainers.json), so that it can't be caught, as
# Black 2's can't. Beaten, it parts from Zekrom; Ghetsis battles the player himself (Black 2's TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_GHETSIS), loses
# his reason, and the Shadow Triad (triad_cavern_a to _c) take him away. N thanks the player and points them to the Pokémon League,
# and leaves on Zekrom; Kyurem goes back into the dark of the cavern. Hugh (hugh_cavern) and Cheren (cheren_cavern) come up
# afterwards, and Hugh says how to reach Route 23 (4, FLAG_UNOVA_CHASM_CLEARED: the Frigate has flown and the crater is clear).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpE
  end


script Ghetsis
  scene "Ghetsis and Kyurem"
  place player 15 24 up
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "Ah, the Giant Chasm. Here Kyurem's power answers most strongly. From here its ice will cover all of Unova."
  say "Kyurem, come!"
  cry "Kyurem"
  say "You again. There is a look in your eyes I find most unpleasant. It reminds me of someone. No matter."
  say "Kyurem! Freeze this child where they stand!"
  speaker none
  text "Kyurem breathed out a storm of ice! The cavern froze white..."
  text "...and from somewhere above, a black dragon fell through the cold, scattering the ice with a crack of lightning!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZEKROM_CAVERN
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_N_CAVERN
  show zekrom
  show n
  cry "Zekrom"
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "So you have come... The boy with no human heart. N!"
  face n up
  speaker "N"
  say "Zekrom told me Kyurem is suffering. I won't let anyone make a Pokémon suffer for their own ends. And I like Unova."
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "How touching. So the lessons I gave you to make you a king were not entirely wasted."
  say "And how kind of you to bring Zekrom. Its lightning is exactly what I need. These are the DNA Splicers!"
  speaker "N"
  say "No! That is an ugly equation. It won't work!"
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "Oh, but it will. Kyurem! Take Zekrom into yourself!"
  fade out 0.6
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZEKROM_CAVERN
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_KYUREM_CAVERN
  hide zekrom
  hide kyurem
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BLACK_KYUREM
  show kyurem_black
  fade in
  cry "Kyurem-Black"
  face n kyurem_black
  speaker "N"
  say "Zekrom...! Two Pokémon made into one... I never imagined there could be such an equation."
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "Fool. Last time I meant to use you to win people's hearts. This time I will simply take Unova by force."
  say "Come, Trainer. No one will save you now. Let us see whether you can stop THIS Kyurem!"
  battle ghetsis as "unova_ghetsis_kyurem"
  speaker none
  text "Kyurem shuddered... and Zekrom broke free of it!"
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BLACK_KYUREM
  hide kyurem_black
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_KYUREM_CAVERN
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZEKROM_CAVERN
  show kyurem
  show zekrom
  fade in
  cry "Zekrom"
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "No! The Kyurem I took such trouble to make, undone! Then I will deal with you by my own hand!"
  battle ghetsis as "unova_team_plasma_ghetsis"
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "This cannot be! I made Team Plasma! I am perfect! How can I lose to someone who doesn't even use Pokémon properly?"
  speaker "N"
  say "Father... It is hard to call you that, but please understand. Pokémon are not tools. People and Pokémon help each other."
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "Silence! Don't speak to me as if you were a person! No real person can talk to Pokémon!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_CAVERN
  show triad_cavern_a
  show triad_cavern_b
  show triad_cavern_c
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Lord Ghetsis has lost himself... We will take him from here."
  speaker "N"
  say "...I see. Without him, Team Plasma is..."
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Farewell."
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_CAVERN
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GHETSIS_CAVERN
  hide triad_cavern_a
  hide triad_cavern_b
  hide triad_cavern_c
  hide ghetsis
  fade in
  face n player
  speaker "N"
  say "Thank you, for everyone. Kyurem is safe. It has spent its strength, but it will come back here to rest."
  cry "Zekrom"
  say "Zekrom says thank you as well. Yes... I can talk with Pokémon."
  say "Two years ago a Trainer and Alder taught me that when different ideas meet, the world is changed by them."
  say "People with Pokémon go on to new places. Pokémon with people find their true strength. That is the world I want to see."
  say "And you? What are you and your Pokémon striving for? Go to the Pokémon League, and put it to the test!"
  fade out 0.6
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_N_CAVERN
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZEKROM_CAVERN
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_KYUREM_CAVERN
  hide n
  hide zekrom
  hide kyurem
  speaker none
  text "N climbed onto Zekrom's back, and the black dragon rose into the sky. Kyurem sank back into the dark of the cavern..."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_CAVERN
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_CAVERN
  show hugh_cavern
  show cheren_cavern
  fade in
  face player hugh_cavern
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "{player}! Team Plasma's ship has flown off out of the crater. Is it over?"
  say "I see... So it's finished. And you saved Kyurem from Team Plasma. You really are something."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "I'm going to take Liepard home to my sister. It still won't come out of its Poké Ball for me, but I'll be patient."
  say "That man told you to go to the Pokémon League? He's right. Right now you're the strongest Trainer in Unova!"
  say "Remember the cave where we met up? Follow it west and you come out on Route 23. Victory Road and the League are past it."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_RAMP
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LIEPARD_RAMP
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROOD_CHASM
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_CHASM_CLEARED
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 4
  fade out 0.3
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_CAVERN
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_CAVERN
  hide hugh_cavern
  hide cheren_cavern
  endscene
  fade in
  end

# Kyurem at rest (kyurem_rest; plan 22 · U10, part a): once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered it waits at the cavern's head, Black
# 2's level 70 (giant_chasm_6.s, its ninth script), and is gone for good once caught or beaten (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_KYUREM); run
# from, it sinks back into the dark and is there again the next time the cavern is come to.
script KyuremRests
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  face self player
  cry "Kyurem"
  speaker none
  text "Kyurem roared!\nA freezing wind howls through the cavern!"
  wildbattle "Kyurem" 70
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_KYUREM
  hide self
  text "The cavern is cold and silent..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Kyurem sank back into the dark of the cavern..."
  end
