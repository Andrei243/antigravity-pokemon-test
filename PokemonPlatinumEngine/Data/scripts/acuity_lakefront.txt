# Acuity Lakefront (plan 02 · S11, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_acuity_lakefront.s).
#
# The lake lies at the top of a rock face that nobody climbs without Rock Climb, and Rock Climb waits for the Icicle
# Badge: that is what keeps Lake Acuity shut until Snowpoint's Gym is beaten, in Platinum. The rival stands at the head
# of the face from the start. As the player first comes up to its foot (the original's trigger 0, two tiles across the
# path at 310 and 311, 244; VAR_ACUITY_LAKEFRONT_STATE 0) the camera goes up to him: he calls down that the rocks need
# Snowpoint's Badge, and runs on to the lake and Team Galactic. The original's RemoveObject sets the flag that hides
# him (FLAG_HIDE_ACUITY_LAKEFRONT_RIVAL). The road from Route 217 to Snowpoint City crosses the trigger, so nobody
# comes to the Gym without having heard him; a save that had been to Snowpoint before the scene was written finds him
# gone (Story/StoryMigration.cs, version 13).
#
# The way into the lake is two ways on the same tiles (the overlay's switches): the lake as it is at the start until
# Commander Mars is beaten at Lake Verity (FLAG_TEAM_GALACTIC_LEFT_LAKE_VERITY), and the lake Team Galactic has come
# to from then on (lake_acuity.txt).


# The original's OnTransition: with the Icicle Badge, Maylene has gone home from Snowpoint's Pokémon Center to her
# own Gym (Route 217 took her out of it, route_217.txt).
script OnEnter
  if not badge icicle end
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_CITY_POKECENTER_1F_MAYLENE
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GYM_MAYLENE
  end


# The rival at the head of the rock face (the original's coordinate event, script 4).
script RivalCallsDown
  camera pan 310 238 1.0
  wait 0.25
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey! {player}! Down there! You're only getting here now? You're so slow it hurts to watch!"
  say "Listen up! See how knobbly these rocks are? You can't climb them until you've got the Badge from Snowpoint's Gym. So go and get it!"
  say "Anyway, I'm not standing about. Team Galactic! Ready or not, here I come!"
  walk rival up 3 left 1 up 4
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ACUITY_LAKEFRONT_RIVAL
  camera release 0.8
  setvar VAR_ACUITY_LAKEFRONT_STATE 1
  end
