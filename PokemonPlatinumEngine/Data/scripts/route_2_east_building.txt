# The building on Route 2's east lane (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route2_EastBuilding/scripts.inc).
#
# Professor Oak's aide hands out Flash for ten species caught (FireRed's REQUIRED_SEEN_MONS, which counts those
# caught). FireRed's is HM05; Platinum's HM05 is Defog and its Flash a TM, TM70, which is what he gives here
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kanto's first chapter"). Its use outside battle asks for the Boulder Badge (Kanto's gates).


script Aide
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM05 goto Given
  if not flag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX goto NoDex
  ask "Hi! I'm one of Professor Oak's aides. Shall I see how many kinds of Pokémon you've caught?"
  if no goto Declined
  if var CAUGHT < 10 goto NotYet
  say "You've caught {var:CAUGHT} kinds already? Wonderful! The Professor asked me to give you this."
  give "TM70"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_HM05
label Given
  say "That's Flash. It lights up the darkest caves, as long as you hold the Boulder Badge from Pewter City."
  end
label NoDex
  sayown
  end
label Declined
  say "Oh. Well, come and see me whenever you like."
  end
label NotYet
  say "Hmm, only {var:CAUGHT} so far. Come back once you've caught ten kinds, and I'll have something for you."
  end
