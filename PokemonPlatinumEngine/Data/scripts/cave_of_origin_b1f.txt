# The Cave of Origin's lowest floor (plan 21 · H8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/CaveOfOrigin_B1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Wallace stands looking into the dark. Spoken to, he tells of the third ancient Pokémon, Rayquaza, which once calmed
# Groudon and Kyogre, and asks where it might be. Emerald's four answers come back to the question but one; here the
# right one, Sky Pillar, comes first, since a question asked again is answered as it first was by the tests that play
# every way through a script. Told Sky Pillar, he goes there at once (FLAG_WALLACE_GOES_TO_SKY_PILLAR,
# VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE 3), and waits at its door (FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_WALLACE cleared: Route 131 and Sky Pillar are
# plan 21 · H8, part c).


script Wallace
  scene "Wallace in the Cave of Origin"
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "Ah, you must be {player}. I've heard about what you've done."
  say "My name is Wallace. I was Sootopolis's Gym Leader once, but other matters called me away, and I left the Gym in my mentor Juan's hands."
  say "......"
  say "Groudon and Kyogre, the two raging out there, are ancient Pokémon from long before people."
  say "But they're not the only ones. There is a third, somewhere: an ancient Pokémon named Rayquaza."
  say "It's said that long ago it was Rayquaza that calmed the other two's fighting. But even I have no idea where it is now..."
  face self up
  emote self exclaim
  wait 0.8
  face self player
label Ask
  choose "{player}, do you know where Rayquaza might be? If you do, please tell me." "Sky Pillar" "Mt. Pyre" "The Cave of Origin" "I don't remember"
  if result == 1 goto MtPyre
  if result == 2 goto Here
  if result == 3 goto Forgot
  say "Sky Pillar? Of course... It must be Sky Pillar!"
  say "{player}, there isn't a moment to lose! We're going to Sky Pillar right away!"
  sound "stairs"
  fade out
  setflag FLAG_WALLACE_GOES_TO_SKY_PILLAR
  setvar VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE 3
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CAVE_OF_ORIGIN_B1F_WALLACE
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_WALLACE
  fade in
  endscene
  end
label MtPyre
  say "Mt. Pyre? But when I saw the old woman there, she said nothing of it."
  say "I can't believe she would hide something from me... Could you think about it a little harder?"
  goto Ask
label Here
  say "The Cave of Origin? But that's right here! I need you to do better than that."
  say "Please, think hard about where Rayquaza might be right now."
  goto Ask
label Forgot
  say "You don't remember? Hmm... That's a problem. Can't you remember somehow?"
  goto Ask
