# The player's room, where the adventure begins (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_twinleaf_town_player_house_2f.s).
#
# The game opens on the end of a television special about Professor Rowan's return to Sinnoh. At the player's first
# step the rival bursts in from the stairs: he saw it too, and they're off to ask the professor for Pokémon.
#
# The room is on the original's plan (plan 01 · M11, the last rooms): the adventure begins on 4, 6, before the
# television; the computer is in the north-west corner with its seat on 1, 5; the stairs' head is 8, 4.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_SPECIAL_PROGRAM_STATE != 0 end
  setvar VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_SPECIAL_PROGRAM_STATE 1
  wait 0.6
  speaker "TV"
  say "...and that brings us to the end of tonight's special, \"Meet Professor Rowan\"."
  say "After many years of research far away in the Kanto region, the professor has come home to Sinnoh, to his laboratory in Sandgem Town."
  say "We'll leave you with his own words: \"We share this world with Pokémon. Live alongside them, and you will come to understand them.\""
  say "This has been Jubilife TV. Good night, Sinnoh!"
  end


# The first step off the starting tile, whichever way: the original's four triggers round it, and its four ways
# through the scene, by the tile the player stepped onto (4, 5 to the north, 3, 6 to the west, 5, 6 to the east,
# 4, 7 to the south). He calls from the stairs' head, runs up to the player, is distracted by the computer, comes
# back and runs out again. The original starts him two tiles down the flight; here he appears at its head.
script RivalRushesIn
  sound "stairs"
  show rival
  music "common/rival"
  emote rival exclaim
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Did you see it? On TV, just now? The special on Professor Rowan!"
  # TwinleafTownPlayerHouse2F_Movement_RivalApproachPlayerNorth, West, East, South
  if var PLAYER_Y == 5 move rival left 1 down 1 left 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 3 move rival left 1 down 2 left 3 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 5 move rival down 2 left 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 move rival left 1 down 3 left 2 fast
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  waitmoves
  say "He's back in Sinnoh, and he studies Pokémon, so he's got to have tons of them! If we ask nicely, he's sure to give us one each!"
  emote rival exclaim
  # ..._RivalApproachPCNorth, West, East, South: to the seat before the computer
  if var PLAYER_Y == 5 move rival down 1 left 4 up 1 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 3 move rival up 1 left 3 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 5 move rival up 1 left 5 fast
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 move rival up 1 left 4 up 1 fast
  wait 0.2
  face player left
  waitmoves
  face rival up
  say "...Whoa, is that a new computer? Lucky!"
  face rival right
  say "...Huh? What was I saying?"
  # ..._RivalWalkBackToPlayerNorth, West, East, South
  if var PLAYER_Y == 5 walk rival right 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 3 walk rival right 1 down 1 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 5 walk rival right 1 down 1 right 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 walk rival right 1 down 2 right 1 fast
  face rival player
  say "Right! We're going to see Professor Rowan! I'll wait for you outside. If you're late, that's a ten-million fine!"
  # ..._RivalLeaveNorth, West, East, South: round the player and back to the stairs' head
  if var PLAYER_Y == 5 move rival down 1 right 3 up 2 right 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 3 move rival up 1 right 4 up 1 right 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 5 move rival up 1 right 2 up 1 right 2 fast
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 move rival up 1 right 3 up 2 right 2 fast
  wait 0.45
  face player right
  waitmoves
  hide rival
  sound "stairs"
  music area
  setvar VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_RIVAL_STATE 1
  end
