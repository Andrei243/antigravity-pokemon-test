# The player's room, where the adventure begins (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_twinleaf_town_player_house_2f.s).
#
# The game opens on the end of a television special about Professor Rowan's return to Sinnoh. At the player's first
# step the rival bursts in from the stairs: he saw it too, and they're off to ask the professor for Pokémon.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_SPECIAL_PROGRAM_STATE != 0 end
  setvar VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_SPECIAL_PROGRAM_STATE 1
  wait 0.6
  speaker "TV"
  say "...and that brings us to the end of tonight's special, \"Meet Professor Rowan\"."
  say "After many years of research far away in the Kanto region, the professor has come home to Sinnoh, to his laboratory in Sandgem Town."
  say "We'll leave you with his own words: \"We share this world with Pokémon. Live alongside them, and you will come to understand them.\""
  say "This has been Jubilife TV. Good night, Sinnoh!"
  end


# The first step off the starting tile, whichever way (the original's four triggers round it).
script RivalRushesIn
  sound "stairs"
  show rival
  music "common/rival"
  emote rival exclaim
  walk rival left 1 fast
  face player rival
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Did you see it? On TV, just now? The special on Professor Rowan!"
  say "He's back in Sinnoh, and he studies Pokémon, so he's got to have tons of them! If we ask nicely, he's sure to give us one each!"
  say "...Whoa, is that a new computer? Lucky! ...Huh? What was I saying?"
  say "Right! We're going to see Professor Rowan! I'll wait for you outside. If you're late, that's a ten-million fine!"
  walk rival right 1 fast
  hide rival
  sound "stairs"
  music area
  setvar VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_RIVAL_STATE 1
  end


# The wardrobe by the bed (plan 11 · C10): the clothes owned, changed for nothing.
script Wardrobe
  sayown
  wardrobe
  end
