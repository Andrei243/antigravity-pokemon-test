# Cinnabar Island's Pokémon Center (plan 19 · K6, part b; Bill, plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/CinnabarIsland_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Bill waits here only once he has asked the player to sail to One Island with him and been told to wait (FireRed's
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_CINNABAR_ISLAND 2, cinnabar_island.txt): a save from before part b never came here, so the room's
# arrival hides him for it, as the new game does. Spoken to, he asks again, and yes takes them both to sea
# (common.SailWithBill).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_CINNABAR_ISLAND < 2 setflag FLAG_HIDE_CINNABAR_POKECENTER_BILL
  end


script Bill
  face cinnabar_pokemon_center_bill player
  speaker "Bill"
  ask "Ready to set sail for One Island? Celio's waiting for us!"
  if no goto Later
  say "Then let's go! My pal's ferry is waiting down at the dock."
  hide cinnabar_pokemon_center_bill
  call SailWithBill
  end
label Later
  say "No rush. Get your team ready, and come back when you are."
  end
