# The Great Marsh's way in (plan 01 · M7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats.
#
# The observatory in Pastoria City is passed through until its rooms are built (plan 01 · M11), so the counter's
# question is asked as the player comes out into the marsh: for the fee, thirty Safari Balls and five hundred steps
# (SafariGame). Saying no, or not having the money, takes the player back out.

script OnEnter
  if safari end
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Great Marsh! This is where the Safari Game is played."
  say "For $500 you get 30 Safari Balls and 500 steps to catch whatever you can find out in the marsh."
  ask "Would you like to play?"
  if no goto Leave
  takemoney 500
  if result == 0 goto Short
  safari start
  sound "select"
  say "Here are your 30 Safari Balls. When they're gone, or your steps run out, the game is over."
  say "Throw bait to calm a Pokémon down, or mud to keep it in one place. Good luck out there!"
  end
label Short
  say "Oh dear, you don't have quite enough for the fee."
label Leave
  say "Then please come again some time!"
  warp "Sinnoh" 610 810 down
  end
