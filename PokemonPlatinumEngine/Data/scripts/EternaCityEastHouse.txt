# The young man knows what the plate torn off the Pokémon statue said, or as much of it as he remembers: two
# inscriptions, each asked for with a yes.


script Inscription
  say "Honestly! What is the world coming to? Somebody has ripped the plate clean off the Pokémon statue!" "Now nobody can read what was written on it. Who would do a thing like that?"
  ask "Actually... do you want to know what it said?"
  if no goto Shrug
  say "The first inscription went something like this." "\"... the making of DIA... the one who gives time...\"" "\"Where there is laughter there are tears, and likewise... the same time flows on... the blessing of DIA...\"" "Ouch. I've forgotten whole chunks of it."
  ask "Want to hear the other one?"
  if no goto Shrug
  say "The second went more or less like this." "\"... the birth of PAL... the maker of worlds side by side...\"" "\"Alive, and not alive... rifts in space... to come to the same world... the blessing of PAL...\"" "Some people say there was a third inscription once. I don't buy it." "A third Pokémon, beside the ones that rule time and space? Nothing could be as mighty as that."
  end
label Shrug
  say "Well, if you're not interested, don't let me keep you."
  end
