# The Item Expert tells of eight things worth giving a Pokémon to hold. The original lists the eight and EXIT in one
# window and comes back to it after each; a question here takes six answers, so they are asked for in two steps, and
# one item is one talk.


script ItemExpert
  say "I am the Item Expert! What a Pokémon can be given to hold, I know from end to end!" "Your Pokémon, now: do they hold anything? The better the thing held, the stronger its holder." "There are some I can vouch for. Care to hear?"
  choose "Which item shall I tell you about?" "LUM BERRY" "FOCUS SASH" "EXPERT BELT" "LIFE ORB" "OTHERS" "EXIT"
  if result == 0 goto Lum
  if result == 1 goto Sash
  if result == 2 goto Belt
  if result == 3 goto Orb
  if result == 5 goto Exit
  choose "And of these, which?" "CHOICE SCARF" "CHOICE BAND" "CHOICE SPECS" "LEFTOVERS" "EXIT"
  if result == 0 goto Scarf
  if result == 1 goto Band
  if result == 2 goto Specs
  if result == 3 goto Leftovers
# FightAreaSouthHouse_VarietyOfOtherItems
label Exit
  say "There are a great many more, of course." "And which is the one to hold comes down to how you mean to battle." "My last word on it: you'll never know until you try!"
  end
label Lum
  say "The Lum Berry!" "The Pokémon holding it shakes off whatever ails it on the spot: sleep, paralysis, confusion, the lot." "It is a berry, mind. Once eaten, it's gone."
  end
label Sash
  say "The Focus Sash!" "A Pokémon at full HP that holds one can't be knocked out by a single hit: it hangs on with 1 HP." "One more turn than you had any right to. Ask anyone who plans their battles what that is worth!"
  end
label Belt
  say "The Expert Belt!" "It lifts a super-effective move to 120% of its power." "Just the thing for a Pokémon that knows moves of several types."
  end
label Orb
  say "The Life Orb!" "Every move its holder uses hits at 130% of its power!" "But! Each time one lands, the holder pays a tenth of its own HP."
  end
label Scarf
  say "The Choice Scarf!" "It takes its holder's Speed to 150%!" "But take care: the Pokémon is held to the one move it picks."
  end
label Band
  say "The Choice Band!" "It takes its holder's Attack to 150%, and holds it to one move." "It does nothing for Sp. Atk, mind. Make no mistake about that."
  end
label Specs
  say "The Choice Specs!" "They take their holder's Sp. Atk to 150%, and hold it to one move." "They do nothing for Attack, mind. Make no mistake about that."
  end
label Leftovers
  say "Leftovers!" "At the end of every turn, once both sides have moved, the holder gets back a sixteenth of its HP." "In a close battle, that little can be the whole difference."
  end
