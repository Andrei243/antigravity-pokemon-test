# Littleroot Town (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/LittlerootTown/scripts.inc and InsideOfTruck/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE, as Emerald counts it: 0 riding in the van, 3 indoors with Mom, 4 told to set the clock,
# 6 the clock set, 7 told to go next door. VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE: 0 no Pokémon (the girl at the top of the road
# turns the player back), 1 the rival met (she calls for help instead), 2 off to save the professor, 3 the Pokédex had
# (Mom waits outside with the Running Shoes), 4 the shoes given. A new game in Hoenn begins on the street beside the
# moving van, and the van's ride is this place's arrival (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hoenn's first chapter").


# The van stops (Emerald's InsideOfTruck and LittlerootTown_EventScript_StepOffTruck): the ride told over the dark,
# the back door rolled up, Mom out of the house to meet the player and the two of them in at its door. Only a game
# begun here is in the van: a Champion who sails over arrives elsewhere.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE == 3 goto MomAtTheDoor
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE != 0 end
  if var PLAYER_X != 123 end
  if var PLAYER_Y != 292 end
  scene "The moving van"
  fade out 0
  text "The moving van rumbles and sways down a country road..."
  text "Boxes bump and slide in the dark. Then the engine coughs, and everything goes still."
  text "With a clatter, the back door rolls up. Sunlight pours in!"
  fade in
  emote player exclaim
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MOM_OUTSIDE
  show littleroot_mom
  walk littleroot_mom down 1
  walk player right 2
  face player up
  face littleroot_mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "{player}! We're here, sweetie. Was the ride very bumpy? You must be worn out."
  say "Welcome to Littleroot Town! This is our new home. It's quaint, but it's a nice place to live, isn't it?"
  say "And look, we didn't even have to lift a finger. The movers' Pokémon are carrying everything in for us!"
  say "Come on, let's go inside."
  fade out
  hide littleroot_mom
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MOM_OUTSIDE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_BRENDANS_HOUSE_TRUCK
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_FAT_MAN
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 3
  warp "LittlerootTown_BrendansHouse_1F" 8 7 up
  endscene
  end
# With the Pokédex had, Mom waits before the house (Emerald's SetMomStandingInFrontOfDoorPos)
label MomAtTheDoor
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MOM_OUTSIDE
  place littleroot_mom 125 291 down
  show littleroot_mom
  end


# The top of the road north with no Pokémon (the area's triggers 0 and 1, on VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE 0): the little
# girl will not let the player into the grass.
script NeedPokemon
  face littleroot_twin player
  emote littleroot_twin exclaim
  speaker "Little Girl"
  say "Wait! You can't go out there! Wild Pokémon jump out of the tall grass, and you haven't got one of your own!"
  say "If you had a Pokémon, it could keep you safe. But you don't, so stay in town!"
  walk player down 1
  end


# The same steps once the rival has been met (trigger 2, and a step of ours beside it, on 1): she has heard someone
# shouting out on the route.
script GoSaveBirch
  scene "Someone calling for help"
  face littleroot_twin player
  emote littleroot_twin exclaim
  speaker "Little Girl"
  say "Um, um, um! Did you hear that? Somebody's yelling out there on the route!"
  say "It sounds like someone's in trouble. Please, go and help them! You're bigger than me!"
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE 2
  endscene
  end


# Coming home with the Pokédex (triggers 3 to 8, on 3): Mom calls the player over and hands over the Running Shoes
# that Dad sent with the movers.
script RunningShoes
  scene "The Running Shoes"
  emote littleroot_mom exclaim
  face littleroot_mom player
  face player littleroot_mom
  speaker "Mom"
  say "{player}! Oh, there you are. Did you meet Professor Birch? I heard you've got a Pokémon now!"
  say "Your father will be so proud. He sent these with the movers, you know. He said every trainer needs them."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_RUNNING_SHOES
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} put on the Running Shoes!"
  say "Hold the run button down and off you go, faster than ever. Just watch where you're going indoors!"
  say "And come home now and then, sweetie. Your Pokémon will want a rest, too."
  hide littleroot_mom
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MOM_OUTSIDE
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE 4
  endscene
  end


# The people of the town by how far the story has come
script Twin
  if flag FLAG_ADVENTURE_STARTED goto GoodLuck
  if flag FLAG_RESCUED_BIRCH goto Saved
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE != 0 goto Save
  say "The professor goes out into the grass all the time to look at Pokémon."
  say "Mom says that's why his shoes are always muddy!"
  end
label Save
  say "Somebody's yelling for help on the route! Hurry!"
  end
label Saved
  say "You saved the professor? With a Pokémon? Wow! You're so brave!"
  end
label GoodLuck
  say "Are you going on a big journey now? Good luck out there!"
  end


script MomOutside
  say "Hold the run button to run, {player}. Dad's shoes are a perfect fit!"
  end
