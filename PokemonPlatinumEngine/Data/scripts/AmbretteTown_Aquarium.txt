# Ambrette Town's aquarium (plan 23, Kalos's water; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's Gyms, its water and the
# Mamoswine ride"). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beat as it is publicly described: a man of the aquarium hands
# the player the Old Rod. FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD is Kalos's own, so a Champion who brought Sinnoh's rod along is
# offered one all the same.


script OldRod
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD goto After
  say "Welcome to the Ambrette Aquarium! Every fish in these tide pools was caught off our own pier."
  say "You look like someone who'd enjoy that. Here, take this rod. We keep a few for visitors."
  give "Old Rod"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD
  say "Face the water and use it from your bag. When the line twitches, reel it in!"
  end
label After
  say "The sea off Ambrette is full of Luvdisc. They like to come in pairs, you know."
  end
