# The hiker (0) wants an Emolga for his Gigalith, once (NpcTrades "gigalith", FLAG_UNOVA_TRADED_GIGALITH).


script GigalithTrade
  face 0 player
  speaker "Hiker"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_TRADED_GIGALITH goto Done
  say "Trading Pokémon lets you get to know other Trainers! I've been thinking of a swap myself."
  ask "Kid! Have you caught an Emolga? If you have, will you trade it for my Gigalith?"
  if no goto Maybe
  say "Which Pokémon will you swap?"
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Maybe
  trade gigalith
  if result == 0 goto Wrong
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_TRADED_GIGALITH
  say "Oh! What a sweet Pokémon! Please be as kind to Gigalith as I shall be to your Emolga."
  end
label Wrong
  say "Hey, come on now! It's an Emolga I want to swap for..."
  end
label Maybe
  say "I see. Well, come and talk to me again if you change your mind!"
  end
label Done
  say "My Emolga is the dearest thing. Thanks again, kid!"
  end
