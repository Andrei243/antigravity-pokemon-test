# The house beside the Apricorn tree on Route 30 (plan 20 · J6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own. The man gives the Apricorn Box, in which Apricorns are kept (Models/Apricorns.cs).


script ApricornMan
  speaker "Man"
  if item "Apricorn Box" goto Have
  say "See the tree outside with the round fruit? Those are Apricorns."
  say "You'll want something to keep them in. Here, take my old Apricorn Box."
  give "Apricorn Box"
  say "In Azalea Town there's an old man named Kurt who can turn Apricorns into Poké Balls. Every colour makes a ball of its own."
  end
label Have
  say "Those Apricorns grow all over Johto. Kurt in Azalea Town will turn them into Poké Balls, you know."
  end
