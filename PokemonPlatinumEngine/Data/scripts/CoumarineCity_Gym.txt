# Coumarine City's Gym (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): Ramos, an old gardener, battles
# for the Plant Badge, Kalos's fourth (`givebadge plant`), once the player has come from Shalour with the Rumble Badge
# (before it, he is busy with his plants and sends them on). VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 22, the
# Tower of Mastery's scene over, becomes 23 here, and the objective points north along Route 13 to the Power Plant. His
# team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_ramos_1; his two trainers are the hall's own.
#
# Since Kalos's Gyms (plan 23) the hall is a vine tree in three tiers (the map's puzzle, CoumarinePoles): poles are climbed
# up and slid down between the tiers, and the one up to Ramos's platform hangs short until a switch of vine lets it down.


script Ramos
  face ramos player
  speaker "Ramos"
  if badge plant goto Won
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 22 goto Later
  scene "Ramos"
  say "Hoo! A young sprout come to visit an old gardener. I'm Ramos, and these plants are my pride."
  say "A plant grows slow, but it don't give way. Let's see if you've got roots as deep as theirs!"
  battle self as "kalos_ramos_1"
  say "Well, I'll be. You flattened my whole garden, and I'm grinning like a fool."
  say "Here's the Plant Badge. You've grown a fair bit, sprout."
  givebadge plant
  text "{player} received the Plant Badge from Ramos!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_COUMARINE_GYM
  defeat "kalos_gardener_flora"
  defeat "kalos_hiker_sylvain"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY != 22 goto Tm
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 23
  objective kalosPowerPlant
label Tm
  say "And this. My Pokémon send their roots deep and drink the strength right out of a foe."
  give "TM86"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_RAMOS
  say "They say Lumiose's lights went out this morning. Something's amiss at the Power Plant up the badlands road."
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "Hoo, a visitor! I'm Ramos. I'm tending my seedlings just now, sprout."
  say "Come back once you've seen the tower up in Shalour. Then we'll have ourselves a proper battle."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_RAMOS goto Tm
  say "Go on up Route 13, sprout. Lumiose won't light itself."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge plant goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Ramos's Grass types wilt before fire, ice, poison and wings. Choose well!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Ramos! The old man hasn't laughed like that in years."
  end
