# Motostoke Stadium's pitch (plan 33 · GL4): the opening ceremony of the gym challenge. The chairman's welcome, the
# gym Leaders, Leon, and the challenge declared open. VAR_GALAR_STORY 16 to 17 (postwick.txt's count). Our own lines.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY != 16 end
  scene "The opening ceremony"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_AT_THE_CEREMONY
  fade in
  text "The roar of the crowd filled the stadium as the challengers walked out onto the pitch."
  speaker "Chairman Rose"
  say "Welcome, one and all, to the opening ceremony of this year's gym challenge!"
  say "Eight gyms. Eight Leaders. Eight badges between each of you and the Champion Cup, and a chance to face Leon himself."
  say "Our Leaders have come to greet you. First, from Turffield: Milo, the gentle giant of the Grass type!"
  speaker "Milo"
  say "Hello there! The first badge is mine to give. Come to Turffield and we'll see how you get on with my Wooloo."
  speaker "Chairman Rose"
  say "And now, the man who needs no introduction. Your Champion, Leon!"
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Challengers! Every one of you could stand where I stand. Give it everything you've got. Have a champion time!"
  speaker "Chairman Rose"
  say "I declare the gym challenge open!"
  face hop_pitch player
  speaker "Hop"
  say "That was amazing! I'm off to Turffield right now. Route 3 is through Motostoke's west gate, past the Galar Mine. See you there!"
  face bede_pitch player
  speaker "Bede"
  say "Turffield? How quaint. I'll have the badge before you've found your way out of the mine."
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_AT_THE_CEREMONY
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BEDE_IN_MINE
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 17
  objective galarTurffield
  endscene
  fade out
  warpto "Galar" motostoke_stadium_door
  fade in
  end


# Kabu's battle for the Fire Badge (plan 33 · GL4, its second session), the gym challenge's third (`givebadge fire`),
# on the pitch through his mission. He Dynamaxes his last Pokémon. VAR_GALAR_STORY 23 to 24. Our own lines.
script Kabu
  face kabu player
  speaker "Kabu"
  if badge fire goto Won
  scene "Kabu"
  say "You rounded up every one of them. Good. I am Kabu, the Leader of Motostoke's gym."
  say "Many challengers fall here. The fire doesn't forgive carelessness. Show me that you've trained for this moment!"
  battle kabu as "galar_kabu_1"
  say "Hah! You turned the heat back on me. I haven't been burned like that in years."
  say "Take the Fire Badge. You've earned the right to carry it."
  givebadge fire
  text "{player} received the Fire Badge from Kabu!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_MOTOSTOKE_GYM
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_ash"
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_ember"
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 24
  # Leon and Hop wait at Hammerlocke's gate (plan 33 · GL5)
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_LEON_HAMMERLOCKE
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_HAMMERLOCKE
  objective galarHammerlocke
  say "Three badges. Keep that fire burning on the road ahead, and train every single day. Hammerlocke is north, past Route 5."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Train hard, every day. That's all there is to it, at my age or yours."
  end
