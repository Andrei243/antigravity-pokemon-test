# Lumiose City's Battle Institute (plan 23, Kalos's last species: one of the thirteen doors left shut). A room of ours on
# X and Y's place as it is publicly described, a hall on the north boulevard where Trainers sit a test of battles in a
# row. Ours is three single battles against its examiners (Ombeline, Eustache and Philippine, trainers of Kalos's table at 36
# to 40), fought one after another on the player's own team with nothing healed between, and paid as any trainer's
# battle. The test is taken once a day (FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_BATTLE_INSTITUTE, set as it begins, so a test failed is the
# day's test too), and its mark is the turns the three battles took together (TURNS: the fewer, the better), against
# bands of ours: nine or fewer an A and a PP Up, fifteen or fewer a B and a Protein, twenty-one or fewer a C and two
# Hyper Potions, more a D and two Super Potions. A first A sets FLAG_KALOS_BATTLE_INSTITUTE_TOP, which the receptionist
# remembers.


script Receptionist
  face self player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_BATTLE_INSTITUTE goto Taken
  say "Welcome to the Battle Institute. Here Trainers sit a test of three battles, one after another, with no rest between."
  ask "Will you sit today's test? Our three examiners are ready."
  if no goto Later
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_BATTLE_INSTITUTE
  say "Very good. Your mark goes by how quickly you finish. Good luck!"
  speaker "Ombeline"
  say "First examiner, Ombeline. Show me how you open a battle."
  battle ombeline as "kalos_institute_ombeline" canlose
  if lost goto Failed
  speaker "Eustache"
  say "Second examiner, Eustache. Now show me how you keep going."
  battle eustache as "kalos_institute_eustache" canlose
  if lost goto Failed
  speaker "Philippine"
  say "Last examiner, Philippine. Let us see how you finish."
  battle philippine as "kalos_institute_philippine" canlose
  if lost goto Failed
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "The test is done, in {var:TURNS} turns. Here is your mark."
  if var TURNS <= 9 goto MarkA
  if var TURNS <= 15 goto MarkB
  if var TURNS <= 21 goto MarkC
  say "A mark of D. You passed, and that is what counts. Come and sit it again tomorrow."
  give "Super Potion" 2
  end
label MarkA
  say "A mark of A! The examiners will be talking about this one for a week."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_BATTLE_INSTITUTE_TOP
  give "PP Up"
  end
label MarkB
  say "A mark of B. Quick and sure. A little more and it would have been an A."
  give "Protein"
  end
label MarkC
  say "A mark of C. A steady test, from start to finish."
  give "Hyper Potion" 2
  end
label Failed
  heal
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "The test is over for today. Your Pokémon have been seen to. Come back tomorrow and try again."
  end
label Later
  say "The examiners will wait. They are very patient people."
  end
label Taken
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_BATTLE_INSTITUTE_TOP goto TakenTop
  say "You have sat today's test already. The examiners need their rest too. Come back tomorrow."
  end
label TakenTop
  say "You have sat today's test already. Your A is still up on our board, by the way."
  end


# The examiners between tests
script Examiner
  face self player
  if subject "ombeline" goto Ombeline
  if subject "eustache" goto Eustache
  speaker "Philippine"
  say "A test is not about winning. It is about winning well."
  end
label Ombeline
  speaker "Ombeline"
  say "The first turn tells me most of what I need to know about a Trainer."
  end
label Eustache
  speaker "Eustache"
  say "No healing between battles. Plan for the third one before you start the first."
  end
