# The floor above Lilycove City's Pokémon Center (plan 21 · H7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own.
#
# Plan 40 · GE3, ours: the trade corner's receptionist hands out a Linking Cord whenever the bag holds none, as Sinnoh's
# GTS receptionist does (GlobalTerminal1F.Gts), so that Hoenn's Pokémon that evolve by a trade can evolve with nobody
# else while the link is closed.


script LinkingCord
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "This is the trade corner. I'm sorry, the link to other players is closed for now."
  if item "Linking Cord" goto Holding
  say "But a Pokémon that grows only through a trade needn't wait for it. A Linking Cord does the same for it. Here!"
  give "Linking Cord"
  say "Use it on the Pokémon as you would a stone. Come back for another once yours is used up."
  end
label Holding
  say "You still have a Linking Cord. Use it on a Pokémon that grows through a trade, then come back for another."
  end
