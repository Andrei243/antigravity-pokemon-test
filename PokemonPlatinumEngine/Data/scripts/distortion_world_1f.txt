# The Distortion World, its first floor (plan 02 · S12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_distortion_world_1f.s, and overlay 9's tables of who stands where).
#
# How far the player has come through the Distortion World is VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS, the original's own
# count (include/constants/distortion_world.h): 0 none, 1 entered 1F, 2 stood on its slab, 3 entered B1F, 4 saw
# Mesprit there, 5 spoke to Cynthia on B2F, 6 spoke to Cyrus on B3F, 7 the boulders done, 8 entered B7F, 9 heard
# Cynthia and Cyrus, 10 beat Cyrus, 11 and 12 Giratina's two shadows, 13 Giratina arrived, 14 Giratina met. The
# original shows each of its people by that count as a floor loads; here each floor's OnEnter does.
#
# The first time (progress 0) the scene plays as the player comes out of the rift: Cynthia follows through the portal,
# says where this is, and the two see Giratina pass in the distance before she goes on ahead (progress 1). She waits on
# the slab that leads down, where a step beside her brings her warning that it moves (the original's event, progress
# 2), until the player has ridden it. The portal behind leads back to Spear Pillar.
#
# Stood in for until plan 09 lands (L14): Giratina's shadow crossing the sky (the original's
# StartDistortionWorldGiratinaShadowEvent) is a shadow sweeping over the stone (plan 09 · L11's effect), its cry, a
# shake of the picture and the two looking round. Slabs do not
# move yet (plan 01 · M8): the one here is a way down through a fade, so Cynthia does not ride it with the player but
# is met again at its foot.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS >= 3 hide cynthia
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS != 0 end
  # DistortionWorld1F_OnFrame_FirstEntry
  hide cynthia
  walk player left 1
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  sound "warp"
  place cynthia 34 30 down
  show cynthia
  wait 0.6
  face cynthia right
  wait 0.3
  face cynthia up
  wait 0.3
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "This place..."
  face cynthia down
  wait 0.3
  say "Up is not up here, and far is not far. This must be the world on the other side of ours. I'll call it what it is: a distortion world."
  face cynthia left
  say "Giratina is somewhere in it, and Cyrus too. Come on. Let's find them."
  move cynthia down 2
  wait 0.3
  face player down
  waitmoves
  # Giratina passes in the distance
  effect shadow 35 31 2 right
  cry "Giratina-Origin"
  camera shake 0.8
  face player left
  face cynthia left
  emote player exclaim 0.8
  emote cynthia exclaim 0.8
  wait 0.9
  face player right
  face cynthia right
  wait 0.6
  say "That shadow... That was Giratina. It knows we are here."
  face player down
  face cynthia up
  say "It didn't come for us. It is watching. We have to hurry, before whatever angered it gets worse."
  walk cynthia left 3 down 3 left 2 down 1 left 6
  hide cynthia
  place cynthia 18 42 right
  show cynthia
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 1
  end


# The original's DistortionWorld1F_CoordEvent_CynthiaElevator: the step onto the slab's stone beside her.
script SlabMoves
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 2
  face cynthia player
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Careful. That slab with the star on it isn't fixed to anything. Step onto it and it moves."
  say "Down is the only way I can see from here. I'll be right behind you."
  end


# Cynthia on the slab's stone (the original's DistortionWorld1F_CynthiaElevator).
script CynthiaBySlab
  face cynthia player
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Why would only this one stone be a different colour? Nothing here is an accident. I'd say it wants to be stood on."
  end


# The portal back (the original's DistortionWorld1F_Portal).
script Portal
  speaker none
  ask "The air is torn here, and Spear Pillar shows through the tear. Go back to Spear Pillar?"
  if no end
  say "{player} stepped through, toward Spear Pillar."
  sound "warp"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "SpearPillarDistorted" 31 33 down
  end
