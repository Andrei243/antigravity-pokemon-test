# Aquacorde Town (plan 23 · Z1). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's first chapter"): the friends at the café on the waterfront, the box of three
# Pokémon a professor sent, the Pokédex, and Shauna's first battle. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count.
#
# The people: shauna, tierno and trevor at their table (there until Route 2 is drawn), rival beside them
# (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_AQUACORDE). Shauna's teams are Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_shauna_1 to _3: the Pokémon she
# takes is the one the player's is strong against (Froakie against Chespin, Chespin against Fennekin, Fennekin against
# Froakie).


# Up the main street onto the waterfront (the trigger's four tiles, one script each, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 2): the player
# is waved over to the table
script Cafe0
  walk player up 3 left 3
  call Friends
  end

script Cafe1
  walk player up 3 left 4
  call Friends
  end

script Cafe2
  walk player up 3 left 5
  call Friends
  end

script Cafe3
  walk player up 3 left 6
  call Friends
  end


script Friends
  scene "The friends at the cafe"
  face player up
  speaker "Shauna"
  say "There you are! Everybody, this is {player}, the one who just moved to Vaniville!"
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Hey there! I'm Tierno. Nice to meet you! I love dancing, and I'm going to have a Pokémon dance team one day."
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "I-I'm Trevor. Um. Hello."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "The four of us were asked to come here. And you too, {player}. Tierno, show them."
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Right! A professor in Lumiose City sent us Pokémon to start our journeys with. Three of them are for you guys!"
  say "Go on, {player}. You pick first!"
  starter
  text "{player} chose {starter}!"
  speaker "Shauna"
  say "Ooh, good pick! Then I'll take this one. We'll be the best of partners, won't we?"
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "And, um, the professor sent these as well. They're Pokédexes. It writes down every Pokémon you see."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex!"
  say "He wants us to see as many Pokémon as we can, all over Kalos. I-I'm going to see them all."
  speaker "Shauna"
  face shauna player
  say "Now we've all got Pokémon... {player}, let's have a battle! Right here, right now!"
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainstChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainstFennekin
  battle shauna as "kalos_shauna_3" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstChespin
  battle shauna as "kalos_shauna_1" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFennekin
  battle shauna as "kalos_shauna_2" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "Shauna"
  if lost goto ShaunaWon
  say "Wow, you're strong! Battling's so much fun when your Pokémon are trying their hardest!"
  goto Letter
label ShaunaWon
  say "We won? We won! You did great too, though. That was so much fun!"
label Letter
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "There's one more thing. The professor sent a letter for your family, {player}. Here."
  text "{player} received the professor's letter."
  say "Take it home and show your mum before you set off. The road north over the bridge goes on toward Santalune."
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "We'll be right here when you get back!"
  walk rival right 3 down 25 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_AQUACORDE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 3
  endscene
  end


# The friends at their table, after the scene
script Shauna
  speaker "Shauna"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 3 goto After
  say "Hi! Pull up a chair!"
  end
label After
  say "My partner and I are going to see every corner of Kalos together!"
  end

script Tierno
  speaker "Tierno"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 3 goto After
  say "Welcome to Aquacorde! The pastries here are the best."
  end
label After
  say "Did you show your mum yet? She's going to be so happy for you!"
  end

script Trevor
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 3 goto After
  say "Um... hello."
  end
label After
  say "The Pokédex fills in the moment you see a Pokémon. I-I'm going to check every page."
  end

script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Everyone's here. Come and sit down, {player}."
  end


# The trail north over the bridge, while Route 2 isn't drawn (plan 23 · Z1 stops at Aquacorde Town)
script NotYet
  text "The trail north to Santalune Forest is closed off for now."
  walk player down 1
  end
