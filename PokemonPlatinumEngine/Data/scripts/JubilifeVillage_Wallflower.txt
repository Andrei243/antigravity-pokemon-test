# The Wallflower, Jubilife Village's eating house on Canala Avenue (plan 23 · Z17). The lines are our own, on Legends:
# Arceus's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hisui's first steps"). VAR_HISUI_STORY is
# prelude_beach.txt's count.
#
# The people: beni behind the counter; cyllene and partner (the Survey Corps' young member the player isn't: Akari
# beside the boy, Rei beside the girl), hidden by FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_CYLLENE_WALLFLOWER and FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_PARTNER_WALLFLOWER
# but for the scene in which they come in.


# Sent to wait here by the professor: Beni's welcome, and the Survey Corps come for the stranger
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_HISUI_STORY != 4 end
  scene "The Wallflower"
  speaker "Beni"
  say "...I don't know your face. The professor sent you? Then sit, eat, and keep your stories to yourself."
  walk player up 2
  speaker "Captain Cyllene"
  say "There you are. The stranger who fell out of the sky, I presume."
  clearflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_CYLLENE_WALLFLOWER
  place cyllene 5 9 up
  show cyllene
  walk cyllene up 1 left 1
  clearflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_PARTNER_WALLFLOWER
  place partner 5 9 up
  show partner
  walk partner up 1 right 1
  face player down
  say "I am Cyllene, captain of the Galaxy Team's Survey Corps. Commander Kamado has heard Professor Laventon's report, and he will see you now."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hi there! I'm {rival}, with the Survey Corps too. Don't let the captain scare you. She looks stern, but she's kind, really."
  speaker "Captain Cyllene"
  say "Galaxy Hall is the large building on Floaro Main Street. Do not keep the Commander waiting."
  walk cyllene right 1 down 1
  hide cyllene
  walk partner left 1 down 1
  hide partner
  setflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_CYLLENE_WALLFLOWER
  setflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_PARTNER_WALLFLOWER
  clearflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_CYLLENE_HALL
  clearflag FLAG_HISUI_HIDE_PARTNER_HALL
  setvar VAR_HISUI_STORY 5
  objective hisuiHall
  endscene
  end


# Beni, behind the counter
script Beni
  speaker "Beni"
  if var VAR_HISUI_STORY >= 6 goto Member
  say "The Wallflower feeds whoever works hard. Galaxy Team or not, an empty belly helps no one."
  end
label Member
  say "So the Commander let you stay. Then you'll be hungry often. Come back when you are."
  end


# The captain and the young member, should they be spoken to while they stand here (they leave in the same scene)
script Cyllene
  speaker "Captain Cyllene"
  say "Galaxy Hall. Now, if you please."
  end

script Partner
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Galaxy Hall's just up the street. I'll see you there!"
  end
