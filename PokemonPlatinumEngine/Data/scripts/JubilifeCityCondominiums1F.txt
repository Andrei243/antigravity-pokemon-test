# says what it does ever after.


script QuickClaw
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_JUBILIFE_CITY_CONDOMINIUMS_1F_QUICK_CLAW goto Explain
  say "Oh, what a darling Pokémon you have there! Here, let it hold on to this for you."
  give "Quick Claw"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_JUBILIFE_CITY_CONDOMINIUMS_1F_QUICK_CLAW
  end
label Explain
  say "A Pokémon holding that Quick Claw will now and then strike first, even if it's a bit of a slowcoach." "Plenty of items only do anything in a Pokémon's hands. Keep an eye out for others it could hold." "... Listen to me. I sound like a schoolteacher."
  end
