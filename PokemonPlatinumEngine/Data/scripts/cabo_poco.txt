# Cabo Poco, Paldea's first place (plan 34 · PD1), on the open world's engine: its places are
# Data/world3d/paldea/places/cabo_poco.json, and a scene here names markers, never tiles. The lines are our own, on
# Scarlet and Violet's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Paldea's first chapter").
#
# VAR_PALDEA_STORY, the first chapter's count, kept here for every place of it:
#   0 a new game, in the bedroom                 4 the two in the grotto, one chosen; to the lighthouse
#   1 Mom and the director downstairs            5 Nemona's lesson in catching; north to Mesagoza
#   2 the three on the terrace, Nemona's battle   6 Nemona at Mesagoza's gate; up the stairs to the academy
#   3 the roar on the beach; into Inlet Grotto    7 the director's welcome and the Tera Orb
# VAR_PALDEA_VERSION is the version the game is (plan 34 · decision 2): 1 Scarlet, 2 Violet, chosen in Inlet Grotto with
# the legendary (InletGrotto.txt), which is also VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT (plan 30 · decision 14): the one choice decides both.


# Up on the president's terrace with the director's word: the three, the Pokédex, and Nemona's first battle
script TheThree
  scene "The three on the terrace"
  faceto player clavell_terrace
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  say "Ah, there you are. Nemona and I were just admiring the view. Every new student of mine starts with a partner."
  say "These three are from the academy's own nursery. Choose whichever calls to you. There is no wrong answer."
  starter
  text "{player} chose {starter}!"
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  say "A fine choice. And your phone, if you'll lend it to me... There. The academy's Pokédex is on it now."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  faceto player nemona_terrace
  speaker "Nemona"
  say "Your first Pokémon! Then you know what comes next. Battle me! Right here, right now!"
  say "Don't worry, I'll go easy on you. Probably. Maybe a little."
  if starter "Sprigatito" goto AgainstSprigatito
  if starter "Fuecoco" goto AgainstFuecoco
  battle nemona_terrace as "paldea_nemona_1c" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstSprigatito
  battle nemona_terrace as "paldea_nemona_1a" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFuecoco
  battle nemona_terrace as "paldea_nemona_1b" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "Nemona"
  if lost goto NemonaWon
  say "You beat me?! On your very first battle? Oh, this is the best day. You and I are going to be rivals, I can tell!"
  goto Roar
label NemonaWon
  say "That was so much fun! You've got a real feel for it already. Next time it'll be closer, I just know it."
label Roar
  text "A long, low cry rises from somewhere under the cliffs."
  speaker "Nemona"
  say "Did you hear that? It came from the beach below Poco Path. You'll want to steer clear of it until you're stronger."
  say "I'll go on ahead and wait for you at the lighthouse. Meet me there and I'll show you how to catch Pokémon!"
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  say "And I will see you both at the academy. Mesagoza is north, past Los Platos. Safe travels."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_CLAVELL_TERRACE
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_TERRACE
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_LIGHTHOUSE
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 2
  objective paldeaBeach
  endscene
  end
