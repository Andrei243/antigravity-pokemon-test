# The Canalave Gym (plan 01 · M9, part 2a), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_canalave_city_gym.s).
#
# A mine shaft of four floors, ten tiles apart, joined by red lifts and by platforms that run on rails between two
# ends (the game's own rule, CanalaveLifts): stepping onto one carries the player to its other end, and every one is
# back where it starts each time the player comes in. Byron waits on the top floor. His Mine Badge lets Strength be
# used outside battle; his win sends the rival and the professor to Canalave's library (plan 02 · S9).


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge mine goto Won
  say "Hey, champ in the making! Welcome to the Canalave Gym!"
  say "Byron uses Steel types. They shrug off most hits, but Fire, Fighting and Ground moves will rattle them."
  say "The Gym is a mine shaft. Ride the lifts up and down and the carts side to side. Each one only goes back and forth between two stops!"
  end
label Won
  say "{player}, you beat Byron! You came up through that shaft like a drill through rock!"
  end


script GymStatue
  speaker none
  if badge mine goto Won
  say "CANALAVE CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Byron. Certified Trainers: {rival}"
  end
label Won
  say "CANALAVE CITY POKéMON GYM. Leader: Byron. Certified Trainers: {rival}, {player}"
  end


script Byron
  speaker "Byron"
  if badge mine goto Won
  say "Ho! You found your way up my shaft! I am Byron, Leader of this Gym, and father to Roark of Oreburgh."
  say "My boy and I both dig deep, but where he loves the rock, I love the steel inside it."
  say "Steel doesn't bend and doesn't break. Let's see if you can dent it!"
  battle self as "leader_byron"
  say "Hmm! Well struck! My steel gave way before your team's resolve."
  givebadge mine
  text "{player} received the Mine Badge from Byron!"
  defeat "worker_gerardo"
  defeat "worker_jackson"
  defeat "worker_gary"
  defeat "black_belt_david"
  defeat "black_belt_ricky"
  defeat "ace_trainer_cesar"
  defeat "ace_trainer_breanna"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_RIVAL_BRIDGE
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_RIVAL_LIBRARY
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_RIVAL
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_COUNTERPART
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_ROWAN
  setvar VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 2
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_PROF_ROWAN
  say "With the Mine Badge, your Pokémon can use Strength outside of battle. Push the boulders out of your way!"
label Tm
  say "And this is for you. Steel's best answer to anything that comes at it!"
  give "TM91"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_BYRON_TM91
  say "TM91 is Flash Cannon. A beam of light off polished steel, and it may lower the foe's Special Defense."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_BYRON_TM91 goto Tm
  say "Your friend {rival} came through here too, and left in a hurry for the library."
  say "Go on, then! A Trainer who can dent steel shouldn't keep friends waiting."
  end
