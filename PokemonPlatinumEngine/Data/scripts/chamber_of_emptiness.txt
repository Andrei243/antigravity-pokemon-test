# The Chamber of Emptiness off Santalune City (plan 23, Kalos's last places). The lines are our own. X and Y give one of
# Kalos's two legendary Pokémon by the version, and this game has no versions: Xerneas wakes under Geosenge Town in the
# story (plan 23 · Z4), and once the Hall of Fame is entered its other half sleeps in this empty chamber, at level 70
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's last places"). Beaten or caught it is gone for good (FLAG_KALOS_YVELTAL_GONE);
# run from, it stays.


script Yveltal
  speaker none
  scene "Yveltal in the Chamber of Emptiness"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 16 7 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  text "Something vast is folded in on itself against the far wall, its wings drawn round it like a cloak."
  text "The air grows cold. Its wings open."
  effect shadow 16 7 2.5
  shake yveltal 3
  cry "Yveltal"
  effect ripple 16 7
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  wildbattle "Yveltal" 70
  if result == 2 goto Stays
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_YVELTAL_GONE
  hide self
  text "The chamber is empty again. Only the cold is left."
  goto Done
label Stays
  text "Its wings close round it once more, and it waits."
label Done
  endscene
  end
