# Lilycove City's harbour (plan 21 · H9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# Scott, who has watched the player since Petalburg, waits on the quay for a Champion of Hoenn (shown by HoennHallOfFame in
# the overlay). Emerald gives the Frontier Pass here as the Battle Frontier's ferry is offered; this game has one Battle
# Frontier, Sinnoh's (plan 06 · R18), so Scott's pass is a flag (FLAG_RECEIVED_FRONTIER_PASS) and the ferry is a crossing
# as Route 40's guide is Johto's (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Scott's way to the Battle Frontier"): `travel to` goes
# through GameEngine.CrossTo, the whole team along, onto the Frontier's grounds. The way back is the Hoenn guide on the
# grounds (battle_frontier.txt, HoennGuide), who sets the player down beside Scott.


script Scott
  face self player
  speaker "Scott"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_FRONTIER_PASS goto Offer
  say "{player}! The Champion of Hoenn at last, and I was here to see it. Didn't I say I'd be cheering?"
  say "I told you I was looking for trainers who could carry on what the Battle Frontier stands for. Here, take this."
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_FRONTIER_PASS
  text "{player} received Scott's Frontier Pass!"
  say "The Frontier is where the strongest Trainers of every region meet. Five halls, five Frontier Brains, and a place for you."
label Offer
  say "My ferry runs to the Battle Frontier whenever you like. Your team goes along, and the Frontier keeps one record of your streaks and Battle Points."
  ask "Shall we sail for the Battle Frontier now?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Wonderful! Hold on to your hat."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed with Scott for the Battle Frontier..."
  travel to "BattleFrontier" 47 51 up
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Stay
  say "No hurry. The Frontier will still be there when you are ready."
  end


# The ferry desk's attendant (plan 21 · H12, part a; Emerald's LilycoveCity_Harbor_EventScript_FerryAttendant). Before the
# Hall of Fame the S.S. Tidal takes no passengers. A Champion of Hoenn is given the pass for Southern Island the first
# time they speak to her (FLAG_ENABLE_SHIP_SOUTHERN_ISLAND: Emerald's Eon Ticket reaches the game only by an event, so the
# pass is ours, a flag as Scott's Frontier Pass is) and is sailed there whenever they like, to the beach at 13, 22.
script FerryAttendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  if not flag HoennHallOfFame goto Closed
  if flag FLAG_ENABLE_SHIP_SOUTHERN_ISLAND goto Offer
  say "Champion {player}! The harbour has been told to expect you, and I'm to give you this."
  setflag FLAG_ENABLE_SHIP_SOUTHERN_ISLAND
  text "{player} was given a ferry pass for Southern Island!"
  say "There's an island far to the south that no ferry calls at, with a garden in the middle of it. The pass lets my ferry sail there for you."
label Offer
  # Plan 21 · H12, part b: with Dad's S.S. Ticket the ferry also sails to the Battle Frontier island, to its quay at 19, 67
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SS_TICKET goto Choose
  ask "Shall I take you to Southern Island?"
  if no goto Stay
label Southern
  say "Wonderful! Come aboard."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed for Southern Island..."
  warp "SouthernIsland_Exterior" 13 22 up
  end
label Closed
  say "Welcome to Lilycove's harbour. The S.S. Tidal calls here, but she isn't taking passengers today. Please come back another time."
  end
label Stay
  say "Of course. Come back whenever you like."
  end
label Choose
  choose "Where would you like to sail?" "SOUTHERN ISLAND" "BATTLE FRONTIER" "NOWHERE"
  if result == 0 goto Southern
  if result == 2 goto Stay
  text "{player} showed the S.S. Ticket."
  say "The Battle Frontier it is! Please come aboard."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed for the Battle Frontier..."
  warp "HoennBattleFrontier" 19 67 up
  end
