# The gate house between Route 40 and the Battle Frontier (plan 20 · J13, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own.
#
# HeartGold's gate leads north to a Frontier of its own. This game has one Battle Frontier, Sinnoh's (plan 06 · R18),
# and the user ruled (2026-10-25) that Route 40's gate is a second door to it with one set of records. A warp never
# crosses regions, so the gate's north door stays shut and the guide HeartGold stands before it walks the player
# through: `travel to` is the crossing every region uses (GameEngine.CrossTo: the whole team along, the region's
# Pokédex, rival, music and name sign), onto the grounds where the gate from the Fight Area lets out. The way back is the
# Johto guide on the grounds (battle_frontier.txt, JohtoGuide). The road opens with Johto's Hall of Fame
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Route 40's way to the Battle Frontier").


script Guide
  face self player
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame goto Shut
  say "Through this door runs the road to the Battle Frontier, where the strongest Trainers of every region gather."
  say "Its five halls keep one record of your streaks and Battle Points, whichever way you come to them."
  ask "Shall I take you along the road to the Battle Frontier?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Splendid! Follow me, and keep close."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} followed the guide along the road to the Battle Frontier..."
  travel to "BattleFrontier" 47 51 up
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Stay
  say "Whenever you're ready. The Frontier isn't going anywhere."
  end
label Shut
  say "The road beyond leads to the Battle Frontier. Only Trainers who have entered the Hall of Fame may go."
  say "Come back once you've beaten the Pokémon League, and I'll take you there myself."
  end
