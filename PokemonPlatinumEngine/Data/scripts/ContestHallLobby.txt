# The Contest Hall's lobby in Hearthome City (plan 02 · S7), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_contest_hall_lobby.s).
#
# The first time in (VAR_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_STATE 0), Keira, whose Buneary the player caught at the city's edge, is
# talking with the player's mother, who taught her contests; she thanks the player and goes in to rehearse, and the
# mother, once a contest star herself, tells the player so and goes home. Fantina waits by the tables: spoken to, she
# twirls and goes back to her Gym, and its guide stops barring the door (FLAG_HIDE_HEARTHOME_CITY_GYM_GUIDE).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_STATE != 0 end
  face keira down
  emote keira exclaim
  speaker "Keira"
  say "Oh! It's you! The one who caught my Buneary!"
  face mom down
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Why, {player}! Fancy seeing you here!"
  walk player up 2
  face keira left
  wait 0.2
  face keira down
  speaker "Keira"
  say "Wait... {player}? Johanna, this is your child?"
  say "Your mom is the one who taught me everything about contests, {player}! She never said she had a Trainer in the family!"
  say "And thank you again for earlier. If Buneary had run off for good, I don't know what I'd have done."
  face keira mom
  face mom keira
  say "I'd better go and rehearse. See you, Johanna!"
  face player right
  walk keira right 3 up 3 right 3 up 4
  hide keira
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_KEIRA
  setvar VAR_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_STATE 1
  face mom down
  face player up
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Surprised? Before you were born, your mom was on that stage every week. I still come to cheer on the young ones."
  say "Contests are a whole other way to shine with your Pokémon. Who knows? One day it might be your turn up there."
  say "Well, I'll be getting home. Take care of yourself and your Pokémon, {player}!"
  face player left
  walk mom left 1 down 2 right 1 down 1
  sound "stairs"
  hide mom
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_MOM
  setflag FLAG_CONTEST_HALL_VISITED
  end


# The receptionists of the three booths (the original's common contest scripts): the contests themselves are not held
# in this game yet, so they welcome the player and say so.
script Receptionist
  say "Welcome to the Pokémon Contest Hall! I'm afraid the Super Contests are not being held today."
  say "We hope to see you and your Pokémon on our stage another time!"
  end


# Fantina by the tables (the original's script 11): a twirl, a word, and she goes back to her Gym.
script Fantina
  speaker "Fantina"
  wait 0.5
  face fantina left
  wait 0.15
  face fantina up
  wait 0.15
  face fantina right
  wait 0.15
  face fantina down
  wait 0.15
  face fantina player
  say "Ah, bonjour! You have the look of a Trainer about you. You want to battle Fantina, oui?"
  say "I come here to see the contests. The Pokémon, so graceful! But for you, I go back to my Gym."
  say "I wait for you there. Come, and we shall dance, my Ghost Pokémon and yours!"
  if facing right goto FromTheWest
  walk fantina left 6 down 4
  goto Gone
label FromTheWest
  walk fantina up 1 left 6 down 5
label Gone
  sound "stairs"
  hide fantina
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_FANTINA
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_HEARTHOME_CITY_GYM_GUIDE
  end


# The boy at the official counter (the original's script 9, plan 06 · R14c): the first time he is spoken to he looks
# for a Poffin Case and gives a Mild Poffin to a player who has one (60 of each flavour but 30, smoothness 40, the
# original's GivePoffin), then goes off along the counters; afterwards he gives it to a player who has come back with a
# case, and otherwise says to practise.
script RichBoy
  speaker "Boy"
  if not flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_RICH_BOY goto First
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_MILD_POFFIN goto NotYet
label Practice
  say "Same as with anything, the basics come first. It takes practice to win a contest!"
  end
label NotYet
  if not item "Poffin Case" goto Practice
  say "You're entering contests? And yes, you've got a Poffin Case."
  poffin room
  if result == 0 goto FullNow
  say "Then let me give you this simply splendid Poffin!"
  call GiveMild
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_MILD_POFFIN
  say "Feed it to the Pokémon you mean to enter. And take my advice: practise at the counter on the right first!"
  end
label FullNow
  say "Oh, no. Your Poffin Case is full."
  end
label First
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_RICH_BOY
  if not item "Poffin Case" goto NoCase
  say "You're entering contests? And yes, you've got a Poffin Case."
  poffin room
  if result == 0 goto FullFirst
  say "Then let me give you this simply splendid Poffin!"
  call GiveMild
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CONTEST_HALL_LOBBY_MILD_POFFIN
  say "Feed it to the Pokémon you mean to enter. And take my advice: practise at the counter on the right first!"
  goto WalkAway
label FullFirst
  say "Oh, no. Your Poffin Case is full. I'll give it to you another time."
  say "A word of advice, though: don't just rush into a contest. Practise at the counter on the right first!"
  goto WalkAway
label NoCase
  say "You're entering contests? But I don't see a Poffin Case..."
  say "It's hard to win without one. Still, if you're set on it, I won't stop you."
  say "You could always practise at the counter on the right."
label WalkAway
  if facing left goto Around
  walk self right 8
  face self down
  end
label Around
  walk self down 1 right 8 up 1
  face self down
  end


# GivePoffin VAR_RESULT, 60, 30, 30, 30, 30, 40: a Mild Poffin into the case, with the item fanfare
script GiveMild
  poffin give 60 30 30 30 30 40
  fanfare item
  text "{player} received a Mild Poffin!"
  text "The Mild Poffin was put away in the Poffin Case."
  return
