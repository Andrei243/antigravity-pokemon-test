# Verdanturf Town (plan 21 · H3, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/VerdanturfTown/scripts.inc). Two of the townsfolk talk about the tunnel to Rustboro, until Wanda's
# young man breaks through (FLAG_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_OPENED).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_VISITED_VERDANTURF_TOWN
  end


script Twin
  speaker "Girl"
  if flag FLAG_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_OPENED goto Open
  say "There's a man digging a tunnel all the way from Rustboro, they say. Why would anyone do that?"
  end
label Open
  say "The man who dug the tunnel did it all for love! He just wanted to see his girlfriend. How romantic!"
  end


script Boy
  speaker "Boy"
  if flag FLAG_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_OPENED goto Open
  say "Somebody keeps trying to bust through the cave over there. I can hear him hammering at night!"
  end
label Open
  say "The tunnel's open now! It's so easy to get to Rustboro. I can go and see the big city whenever I like."
  end
