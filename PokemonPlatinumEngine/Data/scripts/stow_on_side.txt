# The way into Glimwood Tangle: open to challengers with the fourth badge (the Fighting Badge's place, which a Shield
# game's Ghost Badge takes)
script GlimwoodShut
  if badge fighting end
  text "A League staffer's sign stands at the way into the wood: Glimwood Tangle is for challengers with four badges."
  walkto player glimwood_west
  end


# Before the mural with the fourth badge: Bede breaking into it for the chairman, the statue it was hiding, and the
# chairman ending his challenge
script Mural
  scene "The mural"
  faceto player bede_stow
  text "A great crash rang out from the hill. Stone dust rose over the mural, and someone was standing in it."
  speaker "Bede"
  say "You again! Stand back. The chairman wants wishing stars, and there are plenty in this old wall. I'm only helping it along."
  text "Bede's Pokémon struck the mural again. With a long groan the old stone gave way and fell..."
  text "Behind it stood two great figures cut from the rock: two heroes, and in their hands a sword and a shield."
  speaker "Sonia"
  say "Bede, what have you done? ...And what is that? A sword and a shield! It's the tale, but the tapestries never showed them like this."
  speaker "Chairman Rose"
  say "Bede. I asked you to gather wishing stars, not to bring down a town's history on its head."
  say "A challenger endorsed by me must make Galar proud. I'm afraid your gym challenge ends here."
  speaker "Bede"
  say "...Chairman? But I did exactly as you..."
  text "Bede went down the hill after the chairman without another word."
  speaker "Sonia"
  say "Two heroes, two Pokémon, a sword and a shield. I need to think. Go on to Ballonlea, through Glimwood Tangle. I'll write to you!"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_BEDE_AT_MURAL
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 29
  objective galarOpal
  endscene
  end
