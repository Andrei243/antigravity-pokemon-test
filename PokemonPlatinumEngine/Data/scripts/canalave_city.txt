# Canalave City (plan 01 · M7, plan 02 · S9 and S10). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# the original's beats (scripts_canalave_city.s).
#
# Sailor Eldritch's boat at the pier takes the player across to Iron Island and back (the island's own sailor
# sails back). In the original he also sails to Fullmoon and Newmoon Islands once the story has found reasons to
# go there (plan 02 and plan 01 · M10).
#
# Plan 02 · S9: crossing the bridge over the canal the first time (the original's trigger 0, a column of four tiles
# at x 47, VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 0), the rival comes running over it from the west, fresh from his own Gym Badge,
# and battles with the team that has the upper hand on the player's starter; then he tells the player to train on
# Iron Island and runs back over the bridge (state 1). He is shown for the scene alone: his flag still hides him, so
# a battle lost leaves the bridge clear for the next try.
#
# Plan 02 · S10, VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE from there on:
#   2  Byron's Badge is won (CanalaveGym.txt clears the rival's flags and the library's people's). As the player comes
#      out of the Gym the rival, waiting at its door, calls them to the library and runs there (the original's OnFrame):
#   3  the rival stands before the library's door (rival_library) and lets nobody in without HM04, which Riley gives
#      on Iron Island (iron_island.txt); with it he goes in, and the meeting waits on the third floor
#      (VAR_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_STATE 1, CanalaveLibrary3F.txt).
#   4  Lake Valor has blown (FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED). As the player comes out of the library the professor, the
#      assistant and the rival stand outside, a sailor brings the news, the rival runs off to Lake Acuity and the
#      professor sends the player to Lake Valor (the original's second OnFrame):
#   5  the professor and the assistant wait outside the library until the player has left town;
#   6  they have gone to Lake Verity (lake_verity_low_water.txt).
#
# The original's RemoveObject also sets the flag that hides whoever it takes away, so everyone a scene here sends
# off is hidden by their flag as well.


# The original's OnTransition (Cynthia has left Celestic Town once the player is here; the rival outside the library
# after the explosion; the professor and the assistant gone on the next visit) and its two OnFrame scenes.
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CYNTHIA
  if var VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE == 5 goto Gone
  if var VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE == 1 place rival_bridge 39 733 up
  if var VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE == 2 goto OutsideTheGym
  if var VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE == 4 goto AfterTheExplosion
  end
label Gone
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_PROF_ROWAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_COUNTERPART
  setvar VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 6
  end
label OutsideTheGym
  place rival_bridge 39 733 up
  # Out of the Gym's door he is waiting; anyone who came back some other way finds him gone to the library already
  if var PLAYER_X != 39 goto ToTheLibraryQuietly
  if var PLAYER_Y != 732 goto ToTheLibraryQuietly
  scene "The rival outside the Gym"
  music "common/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Took you long enough! So you got Byron's Badge too, huh?"
  say "Listen, the old professor's in town. He wants us at the library, and you know he hates waiting. Come on!"
  move rival_bridge down 1 left 5 up 10 fast
  wait 0.3
  face player left
  waitmoves
  music area
label ToTheLibraryQuietly
  hide rival_bridge
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_RIVAL_BRIDGE
  setvar VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 3
  setvar VAR_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_STATE 1
  endscene
  end
label AfterTheExplosion
  place rival_bridge 37 721 down
  if var PLAYER_X != 37 goto NewsQuietly
  if var PLAYER_Y != 719 goto NewsQuietly
  scene "The explosion's news"
  face prof_rowan down
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Hmm... That was no ordinary tremor. The ground doesn't shake like that by itself."
  # A sailor comes running up from the harbour with the news (two tiles nearer than the original stands him, so that
  # the text box does not cover him)
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_SAILOR
  place sailor 43 725 left
  show sailor
  walk sailor left 5 up 4 fast
  speaker "Sailor"
  say "Have you heard? It's all over the harbour! There's been a huge explosion out east, at Lake Valor!"
  say "They say the whole lake went up! I've got to tell the others!"
  walk sailor down 4 right 9 fast
  hide sailor
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_SAILOR
  face rival_bridge up
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "An explosion at a lake? I've got a bad feeling about this. A really bad feeling!"
  say "I'm going to Lake Acuity, right now! Whoever gets there last is paying the fine!"
  walk rival_bridge down 3 right 9 fast
  hide rival_bridge
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_RIVAL_BRIDGE
  wait 0.3
  face prof_rowan up
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "{rival} is as hasty as ever. But this time he is right to hurry."
  say "{player}! I need you at Lake Valor. Find out what has happened there. I am counting on you."
  face counterpart left
  wait 0.25
  speaker "{assistant}"
  if boy say "{player}, you'll be all right, won't you? The professor and I are going to Lake Verity. Be careful!"
  if girl say "I wonder what really happened out there... The professor and I will look into Lake Verity. Take care, {player}!"
label NewsQuietly
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_SAILOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_RIVAL_BRIDGE
  hide rival_bridge
  setvar VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 5
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_COUNTERPART
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_PROF_ROWAN
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_ACUITY_LOW_WATER_RIVAL
  endscene
  end


# The rival on the bridge (trigger 0).
script RivalOnTheBridge
  if var PLAYER_Y == 723 place rival_bridge 38 723 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 724 place rival_bridge 38 724 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 725 place rival_bridge 38 725 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 726 place rival_bridge 38 726 right
  scene "The rival on the bridge"
  show rival_bridge
  walk rival_bridge right 4 fast
  emote rival_bridge exclaim
  music "common/rival"
  walk rival_bridge right 4 fast
  face player left
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Whoa, close one! Hey, {player}! On your way to take on the Gym Leader past here, huh?"
  say "Somebody's got to make sure you're up to it first. And that somebody is me! With the brand-new Badge I just won!"
  if starter "Turtwig" goto VsTurtwig
  if starter "Chimchar" goto VsChimchar
  battle rival_bridge as "rival_canalave_city_piplup"
  goto After
label VsTurtwig
  battle rival_bridge as "rival_canalave_city_turtwig"
  goto After
label VsChimchar
  battle rival_bridge as "rival_canalave_city_chimchar"
label After
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Yeah, yeah. You're a bit better than me. Same as always."
  say "But get this: guess who's going to take on the Pokémon League and be Champion? Right first time. It's me!"
  say "Anyway, you should head over to Iron Island and toughen up some more. That's where I trained before the Gym!"
  walk rival_bridge left 9 fast
  hide rival_bridge
  music area
  setvar VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE 1
  endscene
  end


# The rival, outside the Gym after his battle (the original's script 3).
script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Go to Iron Island and toughen up! Seriously, that's what I did!"
  end


# The rival before the library's door (the original's script 25): nobody gets in without HM04 or a Pokémon that knows
# Strength. With either he goes in ahead, and the door is clear.
script RivalLibrary
  speaker "{rival}"
  if item "HM04" goto In
  if knows "Strength" goto In
  say "What? You haven't got the HM for Strength yet? Didn't I tell you to go and train on Iron Island?"
  say "There's a guy over there handing it out. Go and get it, then come back. The professor can wait. Not for long, though!"
  end
label In
  say "{player}! You're too slow! Everybody's waiting upstairs. Move it!"
  face rival_library up
  sound "door_open"
  walk rival_library up 1
  hide rival_library
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_CITY_RIVAL_LIBRARY
  sound "door_close"
  end


# The professor and the assistant outside the library, once the explosion's news has come (the original's scripts 6
# and 7).
script Rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "What is taking place at the lakes? Something is very wrong, and I mean to find out what."
  say "Lake Valor lies east of Pastoria City, {player}. Go, and be careful."
  end

script Assistant
  face self player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  if boy say "The professor and I are going to Lake Verity. It's right by home, so I know the way. You look after Lake Valor, {player}!"
  if girl say "The professor and I will take Lake Verity. It's not far from Sandgem Town. Lake Valor is yours, {player}!"
  end


# The townspeople (the original's scripts 8 to 13): their own lines, and what they say of the tremor once Lake Valor has
# blown.
script Collector
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  sayown
  end
label After
  say "That tremor just now was no earthquake. I've felt plenty of those, and none of them came with a bang."
  end

script Man
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  if not badge mine goto Own
  if var VAR_CANALAVE_CITY_STATE >= 5 goto Own
  say "A boy in a terrible hurry ran past me to the library just now. He kept stamping his feet by the door."
  say "I do hope whoever he's waiting for turns up soon."
  end
label Own
  sayown
  end
label After
  say "What in the world was that tremor? The water in the canal jumped right out of its bed!"
  end

script AceTrainer
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  sayown
  end
label After
  say "They're saying there was an explosion at Lake Valor. That's the other side of Sinnoh, and we felt it here!"
  end

script Lass
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  sayown
  end
label After
  say "My Psyduck always knows when a storm is coming. But it never saw that tremor coming at all."
  end

  end

  end

script OldMan
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  sayown
  end

  end


# CanalaveCity_SailorEldritch. Iron Island is where he always sails. While his son lies in his nightmare and the
# player has seen it (VAR_LUNAR_WING_EVENT_STATE 2, plan 02 · S15, part b: CanalaveCitySailorEldritchHouse.txt) he asks
# only whether they will go to Fullmoon Island for him, and with the Lunar Wing in the bag sends them to his son; from
# the day the boy has been looked at, Fullmoon Island is one of the places he offers (the original lands the player
# on its pier at 39, 277, facing west). Newmoon Island he never offers here: the original adds it once the Harbor
# Inn's dream has taken the player there, which belongs to one of its events (docs/mechanics/rulings.md).
script Ferry
  face self player
  if var VAR_LUNAR_WING_EVENT_STATE == 2 goto ForMyBoy
  say "Ahoy there! I sail folk over to Iron Island and back again, whenever the sea's kind."
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_CANALAVE_CITY_SAILOR_ELDRITCH_HOUSE_LITTLE_BOY goto Where
  ask "Fancy a trip across to Iron Island?"
  if no goto Stay
label IronIsland
  say "Right you are. All aboard, and hold on to something!"
  warp "Sinnoh" 100 502 right
  end
label Where
  choose "Where shall I take you?" "Iron Island" "Fullmoon Island" "Nowhere today"
  if result == 0 goto IronIsland
  if result == 2 goto Stay
  say "Fullmoon Island it is. Anchors aweigh!"
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 39 277 left
  end
label Stay
  say "No hurry. The boat's here whenever you want it."
  end
# CanalaveCity_AskGoingToFullmoonIsland
label ForMyBoy
  if item "Lunar Wing" goto TakeIt
  ask "...You. You'd go to Fullmoon Island, for my boy?"
  if no goto Waiting
  say "His mother and I are counting on you! You're the only one who can bring him out of that nightmare."
  say "There's a thing called the Lunar Wing on Fullmoon Island, and they say it will wake him. I went myself. I couldn't find it..."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 39 277 left
  end
label Waiting
  say "Things to see to first? I'll be here, with the boat ready."
  say "Please. We don't know who else to ask."
  end
label TakeIt
  say "That feather... Is that the Lunar Wing? Then take it to my son! Please, as quick as you can!"
  end
