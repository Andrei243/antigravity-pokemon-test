# The Pokémon World Tournament's grounds (plan 22 · U6, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pwt.s and plasma_frigate.s, read for the order
# alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_PWT is the grounds' count (Black 2's 0x40c6 from 2, the tournament's first visit being later): 0 until the
# Plasma Frigate's scene, 1 once it has played (the gangway is open), 2 once the Shadow Triad have put the player back
# ashore, 3 once Hugh and Cheren have had their say on the pier and the frigate has sailed.
#
# Black 2 plays the frigate's scene as the player comes out of the tournament's hall after its first tournament; the
# hall is not open yet, so ours plays at the first step onto the pier once the Quake Badge is won and Clay has told of
# the tournament (VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL 5; our trigger on 196 to 198, 476): Cheren and Hugh come up behind the player, a
# grunt of Team Plasma runs past them down the pier, Hugh goes after him and Cheren after Hugh, and Colress warns the
# player off (the rulings' "Unova's second chapter, part c"). The frigate's own scene is plasma_frigate.txt; the
# grounds' arrival plays what follows it.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  # the tournament's hall (plan 22 · U6, part d) is open once Clay has told of it, and a save that has seen the frigate has
  # been through the first tournament by the story's order (no story version)
  if var VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL >= 5 setflag FLAG_UNOVA_PWT_OPEN
  if var VAR_UNOVA_PWT >= 1 setflag FLAG_UNOVA_PWT_FIRST_TOURNAMENT
  if var VAR_UNOVA_PWT != 2 end
  scene "Hugh and Cheren on the pier"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Gah! The Shadow Triad... What are they? One second we were on deck, the next we're standing out here!"
  face hugh_pier down
  say "And the ship's leaving! Team Plasma, get back here! You still have my sister's Purrloin!"
  walk hugh_pier up 10 fast
  hide hugh_pier
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_PWT_PIER
  face cheren_pier player
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "The Shadow Triad... They can hold a person still and disappear in the blink of an eye. But it's Zinzolin who worries me."
  say "A ship that freezes everything it passes, and a plan to turn Unova to ice. Something big is coming, {player}."
  say "There's something I want to look into. I'm going on to Route 6. Be careful out there."
  walk cheren_pier up 10
  hide cheren_pier
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_PWT_PIER
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SAILED
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_PWT 3
  endscene
  end


# The first step onto the pier, once Clay has told of the tournament (our trigger on 196 to 198, 476, at 0)
script PlasmaAtThePier
  if var VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL < 5 end
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_PWT_FIRST_TOURNAMENT end
  scene "The Plasma Frigate at the pier"
  place player 197 476 up
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_PWT
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_PWT
  show hugh_pwt
  show cheren_pwt
  walk cheren_pwt down 2
  walk hugh_pwt down 2
  face cheren_pwt player
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "{player}! I hardly knew you. You've come a long way since our battle in Aspertia. So has {rival}."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Cheren came to see the tournament hall. It's not even open yet and there's already a crowd!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GRUNT_PWT
  show grunt_pwt
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Out of the way, out of the way!"
  walk grunt_pwt down 17 fast
  hide grunt_pwt
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GRUNT_PWT
  face hugh_pwt down
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "That uniform... Team Plasma! He's running for that ship at the end of the pier!"
  say "I'm going after him! {player}, come with me!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_PWT
  show colress_pwt
  walk colress_pwt right 1
  speaker "Colress"
  say "Wait. Children have no business running headlong into something so dangerous."
  face hugh_pwt colress_pwt
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "The Pokémon I'm looking for, my little sister's Purrloin... It might be on that ship! I'm not waiting!"
  walk hugh_pwt down 14 fast
  hide hugh_pwt
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_PWT
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "I'm going too. Someone has to keep him out of trouble!"
  walk cheren_pwt right 1
  walk cheren_pwt down 14 fast
  hide cheren_pwt
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_PWT
  face colress_pwt player
  speaker "Colress"
  say "Recklessness, not courage. Do they believe anything is possible simply because they have Pokémon with them?"
  say "...Or perhaps that is exactly the force I keep looking for. Go on, then. I would very much like to see what you do."
  walk colress_pwt left 3
  hide colress_pwt
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_PWT
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_FRIGATE
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_FRIGATE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_AT_PIER
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_PWT 1
  endscene
  end
