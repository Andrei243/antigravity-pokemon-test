# Cyllage City (plan 23 · Z2, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Central Kalos, drawn by hand: Route 5 to Cyllage City"): the neighbour waits at the foot
# of the main street and battles, and the Gym at its head (CyllageCity_Gym.txt) holds Kalos's second Badge.
# VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 16, the Snorlax woken, becomes 17 here, and 18 once the Cliff Badge is
# won. The neighbour's teams are Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_rival_cyllage_*: the starter the neighbour took at the
# café is the one strong against the player's.
#
# The people: rival (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_CYLLAGE, set by the neighbour's scene).


# Up from Route 8 onto the main street (the trigger across its foot, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 16)
script Neighbour
  scene "The neighbour in Cyllage"
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! You woke that Snorlax? I heard it snoring from the coast. I took the long way round."
  say "The Gym here is at the very top of the town. Its Leader climbs rock walls, and his Pokémon are as hard as the cliffs."
  say "Before you go up, let's see what you've learned since Santalune."
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainstChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_cyllage_chespin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstChespin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_cyllage_fennekin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFennekin
  battle rival as "kalos_rival_cyllage_froakie" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto RivalWon
  say "You're climbing faster than I am. Good. Then I know who I'm racing."
  goto Gym
label RivalWon
  say "Close. Very close. Rock-type Pokémon won't be as kind to you as I was, though."
  goto Gym
label Gym
  say "Water and Grass get the better of stone, they say. Good luck with the climb."
  walk rival down 3 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_CYLLAGE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 17
  objective kalosGrant
  endscene
  end

script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "The Gym is up the steps, right at the top."
  end
