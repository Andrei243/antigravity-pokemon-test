# Sky Pillar's island (plan 21 · H8, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/SkyPillar_Outside/scripts.inc).
#
# Wallace has come here from the Cave of Origin (VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE 3) and opened the pillar's door
# (FLAG_WALLACE_GOES_TO_SKY_PILLAR: the overlay's door). The first time the player comes up from the cave he walks over,
# the ground shakes, the titans' weather reaches even here (Emerald sets FLAG_SYS_WEATHER_CTRL again), and he goes back
# to Sootopolis to watch over the city, leaving the climb to the player (state 4; his Sootopolis self shown,
# FLAG_HIDE_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_WALLACE cleared). Emerald walks both a few steps toward the door before the second quake;
# here they stand where they are.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE != 3 end
  scene "Wallace at Sky Pillar"
  wait 0.5
  walk sky_pillar_wallace down 7 right 3
  face player sky_pillar_wallace
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "Ah, {player}! Forgive me. I was in such a hurry that I left you behind."
  say "I've opened the door of Sky Pillar. Now, let's go!"
  wait 0.5
  camera shake 0.8
  wait 0.8
  say "An earthquake! We haven't a moment to spare!"
  camera shake 1.4
  wait 1.4
  face sky_pillar_wallace left
  wait 0.3
  face sky_pillar_wallace right
  wait 0.3
  face sky_pillar_wallace player
  say "Hmm... This is getting worse."
  setflag FLAG_SYS_WEATHER_CTRL
  wait 1
  say "Even here, the weather has gone wrong. {player}, Rayquaza should be at the very top of this tower."
  say "I'm worried for Sootopolis. I must go back. Everything rests with you now. Don't let us down!"
  sound "warp"
  fade out
  setvar VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE 4
  hide sky_pillar_wallace
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_WALLACE
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_WALLACE
  fade in
  endscene
  end


# Wallace, spoken to before he comes over (he has nothing to add: Emerald gives him no script of his own here)
script Wallace
  face self player
  speaker "Wallace"
  say "Sky Pillar... Rayquaza must be at its top. Let's hurry!"
  end
