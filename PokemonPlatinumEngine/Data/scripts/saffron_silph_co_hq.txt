# Silph Co.'s lobby in HeartGold's Kanto (plan 20 · J15, part n). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T11R0701).
#
# The lift goes up to the research floor, where Silph Co. studies how Rotom gets into machines. Its guard lets nobody
# ride it until he has seen a Rotom on the visitor's team; from then on the lift's door is open for good
# (FLAG_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_OPEN, a flag of ours, the door's own `openedBy`). The lobby tells the lift which end the player
# is at (VAR_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_FLOOR, ours: 0 here, 1 upstairs), as the Glitter Lighthouse's ends do.


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_FLOOR 0
  # Steven (plan 20, part q): in the lobby for a Champion of Johto with Kanto's eight Badges too, until he has given his
  # gift (HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CITY_STEVEN; FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HOENN_STARTER is ours)
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CITY_STEVEN
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HOENN_STARTER end
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame end
  if badges of kanto < 8 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CITY_STEVEN
  end


# Steven's visit (our words on HeartGold's beat): he has come from Hoenn on Devon business, and gives the sixteen-Badge
# Champion one of Hoenn's first three Pokémon, at level 5. After it he goes on to Pewter's museum
# (pewter_museum_of_science.txt)
script Steven
  face t11r0701_daigo player
  speaker "Steven"
  say "You must be {player}. I'm Steven. I came from Hoenn on business with Silph Co., but I've been hearing about you."
  say "Sixteen Badges, Johto's and Kanto's both. I'd like to see what you could do with a Pokémon from my home."
  if var PARTY_COUNT >= 6 goto Full
  choose "Which would you like to raise?" "Treecko" "Torchic" "Mudkip"
  if result == 1 goto Torchic
  if result == 2 goto Mudkip
  givepokemon "Treecko" 5
  goto Given
label Torchic
  givepokemon "Torchic" 5
  goto Given
label Mudkip
  givepokemon "Mudkip" 5
label Given
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received a Pokémon from Steven!"
  say "Take good care of it. I'm off to Pewter City. They say its museum has a stone that fell from space."
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_HOENN_STARTER
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CITY_STEVEN
  hide self
  end
label Full
  say "But your team is full. Make room on it, then come back and talk to me."
  end


script Guard
  face t11r0701_policeman player
  speaker "Guard"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_OPEN goto Open
  if has "Rotom" goto Rotom
  say "That lift goes up to the research floor. Only staff may ride it, I'm afraid."
  say "Unless you've got a Rotom with you. The researchers up there are always glad to meet one."
  end
label Rotom
  scene "The lift to Rotom's room"
  emote t11r0701_policeman exclaim
  say "Is that a Rotom on your team? Then you'll want to see the research floor!"
  say "Our researchers built machines a Rotom can slip right into. Go on up. The lift's yours to use."
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_OPEN
  endscene
  end
label Open
  say "The research floor is just up the lift. Mind you don't let your Rotom wander off into the photocopiers."
  end


# Plan 40 · GE3, our words on HeartGold's beat: the receptionist hands a visitor one of the company's Upgrades, once
# (FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_UPGRADE, a flag of ours), as HeartGold's Silph Co. gives its Up-Grade in Saffron City.
script Receptionist
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "Welcome to Silph Co. We make Poké Balls, Potions and much else besides. Upstairs, our researchers study Rotom."
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_UPGRADE end
  say "Oh, and our engineers left a spare of their newest gadget at the desk. They said a visiting Trainer should have it."
  give "Upgrade"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_UPGRADE
  say "It's a box of data a Porygon can make use of. Let one hold it, and see what happens when it's traded."
  end
