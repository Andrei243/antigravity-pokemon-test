# The room at the end of Granite Cave (plan 21 · H3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/GraniteCave_StevensRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# Steven, the son of Devon's president, takes his father's Letter (FLAG_DELIVERED_STEVEN_LETTER), gives TM47, Steel
# Wing, swaps numbers for the PokéNav's Match Call (FLAG_REGISTERED_STEVEN_POKENAV) and leaves by the way in, for good
# (FLAG_HIDE_GRANITE_CAVE_STEVEN).


script Steven
  face self player
  speaker "Steven"
  if not item "Letter" goto Stranger
  scene "Steven's letter"
  say "My name is Steven. I'm interested in rare stones, so I travel here and there looking for them."
  say "Oh? A letter for me? From my father, it seems... Thank you for bringing it all this way."
  take "Letter"
  text "{player} handed over the Letter."
  setflag FLAG_DELIVERED_STEVEN_LETTER
  say "This is a thank-you for your trouble. It's a TM I'm rather fond of."
  give "TM47"
  say "Your Pokémon seem to trust you a great deal. You might even become the Champion one day."
  say "Let's register each other in our PokéNavs, so we can keep in touch."
  addcontact steven
  text "Registered Steven in the PokéNav."
  say "I'm afraid I have to hurry along now. I'm sure we'll meet again."
  if var PLAYER_Y < 8 goto Around
  walk granite_cave_steven up 5
  goto Gone
label Around
  walk granite_cave_steven left 1 up 4 right 1 up 1
label Gone
  hide granite_cave_steven
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GRANITE_CAVE_STEVEN
  endscene
  end
label Stranger
  say "Hello. I'm Steven. I came here looking for rare stones. Did you come for the same thing?"
  end
