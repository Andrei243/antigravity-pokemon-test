# The cave of Celestic Town's ruins (plan 02 · S9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on the original's beats (scripts_celestic_town_cave.s).
#
# The ancient painting is read from one of the two tiles before it (9, 3 or 10, 3). The first time, the elder comes
# in after the player and tells its legend, and Cyrus comes in after her: he will destroy the painting, and asks the
# player to stop him if they think him wrong. Said no, he shoves the player aside and stands before it; spoken to,
# he asks again. Beaten, he leaves, and the elder gives HM03 (Surf) and goes home to her house in town
# (CelesticTownNorthHouse.txt). Cynthia waits outside (VAR_CELESTIC_TOWN_STATE 1, celestic_town.txt).
#
# The original's RemoveObject also sets the flag that hides whoever it takes away, so Cyrus and the elder are hidden
# by their flags once they go.
#
# After the Hall of Fame (plan 02 · S15, part c): once Charon has been caught on Stark Mountain
# (FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN), the first look at the painting brings Cynthia in after the player. She tells
# how it has always been read (Dialga or Palkia at Spear Pillar, with the three of the lakes round it) and asks
# whether she may go on: her own reading (the three lights are Dialga, Palkia and Giratina, and the light at their
# heart whatever made the world), then a line cut into one of Sinnoh's old Plates and where Giratina fits, then what
# she believes as someone who studies myths. Each step is a question; a no at any of them and she begs pardon and
# goes. She is heard once (FLAG_TALKED_TO_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYNTHIA), and again after every entry into the Hall of
# Fame, whose script clears the flag (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt).
#
# What plan 23 · Z17 adds here, and Platinum has not: the way into Hisui, a side region reached from these ruins by a
# Champion (its plan's decision 3). Nothing of it is written; the painting's script is where its question would go.


# Coming in while Cyrus is still here (refused, or a battle lost): he stands before the painting and the elder beside
# it, on their own tiles, as the original lays its objects out afresh.
script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYRUS end
  place cyrus 9 3 up
  place elder 10 3 left
  end


# The painting (the original's script 1).
script Painting
  # CelesticTownCave_PaintingCynthia: after Stark Mountain, once for each entry into the Hall of Fame
  if not flag FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN goto Examine
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYNTHIA goto Examine
  call CynthiaReads
  end
label Examine
  if flag FLAG_EXAMINED_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_PAINTING goto Again
  scene "The painting"
  text "{player} looked closely at the ancient painting." "Strange shapes cover a whole stretch of the rock." "Three figures stand in a triangle... and at their heart, a sphere of light?" "What could it all mean?"
  setflag FLAG_EXAMINED_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_PAINTING
  # The elder comes in after the player and stands beside them
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_ELDER
  place elder 4 11 up
  show elder
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto ElderFromTheWest
  walk elder up 6 right 1 up 1 right 4 up 1
  face player left
  goto Legend
label ElderFromTheWest
  walk elder up 6 right 1 up 1 right 5 up 1
  face player right
label Legend
  speaker "Elder"
  say "As we tell it, the painting means this..."
  say "Once there were two beings, so mighty that the people of the time took them for gods."
  say "And there were three Pokémon whose power matched either of them. Those three held the two in balance, like the sides of a pyramid."
  say "That is Sinnoh's ancient legend, as Celestic Town still tells it today."
  speaker "???"
  say "I would like to hear more of that legend, if you'll allow me."
  face elder down
  face player down
  speaker "Elder"
  say "And who might you be...?"
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_ELDER
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYRUS
  place cyrus 4 11 up
  show cyrus
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 walk cyrus up 6 right 1 up 1 right 5
  if var PLAYER_X != 9 walk cyrus up 6 right 1 up 1 right 4
  face cyrus up
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "My name is Cyrus. I mean to put an end to pointless conflict and hostility. I am looking for the power to make an ideal world."
  say "So answer me this. The world cannot change today because those three Pokémon keep it in balance. Is that what you are telling me?"
  speaker "Elder"
  say "I couldn't tell you. What I do know is that the world is in balance, and I'm quite content with it as it is."
  say "Your question doesn't interest me."
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "...So you plead ignorance. How pointless."
  say "You fail to see that this world is incomplete. I will change it. And as a first step, I will destroy this painting you have guarded for so long."
  say "A new world deserves a new legend in its place. Am I mistaken?"
  ask "If you believe I am, then challenge me."
  if yes goto Fight
  say "...You choose to be a coward. A sensible choice. Stand aside, then, and watch me change the world."
  # He shoves the player aside and stands before the painting himself
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto ShoveWest
  face elder right
  walk cyrus right 1
  move cyrus up 1
  walk player right 1
  waitmoves
  face player left
  endscene
  end
label ShoveWest
  face elder left
  walk cyrus left 1
  move cyrus up 1
  walk player left 1
  waitmoves
  face player right
  endscene
  end
label Again
  text "{player} looked closely at the ancient painting." "Strange shapes cover a whole stretch of the rock." "Three figures stand in a triangle... and at their heart, a sphere of light..."
  end
label Fight
  call CyrusBattle
  endscene
  end


# Cynthia before the painting (the original's CelesticTownCave_PaintingCynthia). She comes in by the way the elder
# once did and stands beside the player, on the other of the two tiles before the painting.
script CynthiaReads
  scene "Cynthia before the painting"
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYNTHIA
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYNTHIA
  place cynthia 4 11 up
  show cynthia
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto PlayerToTheWest
  walk cynthia up 6 right 1 up 1 right 4 up 1
  face player left
  goto Reading
label PlayerToTheWest
  walk cynthia up 6 right 1 up 1 right 5 up 1
  face player right
label Reading
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "This painting... It has always been read the same way."
  say "The light in the middle is Dialga, or Palkia, as it appears at Spear Pillar. And the three lights round it are Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf."
  face cynthia player
  ask "May I go on?"
  if no goto CarriedAway
  face cynthia up
  wait 0.25
  face player up
  wait 0.5
  say "But then it struck me that there might be another way to read it."
  say "What if this triangle of lights is a different three? What if they are Dialga, Palkia and Giratina?"
  say "And the great light at their heart: is that something else again? Could it be whatever made this world of ours?"
  face cynthia player
  face player cynthia
  ask "May I go on a little longer?"
  if no goto CarriedAway
  say "Thank you for hearing my idea out."
  say "You know the old Plates that turn up all over Sinnoh? One of them carries a line that says, as near as I can put it: \"From the First One, two were let go: one of time, one of space.\""
  say "I think that line points the same way: to one more Pokémon, mightier even than Dialga or Palkia. Does that sound possible to you?"
  move cynthia down 1
  wait 0.25
  face player down
  waitmoves
  say "I can't say for certain where Giratina belongs in all this. But they say that in the Distortion World neither time nor space holds steady."
  say "That tells us something about the one Pokémon that lives there. It must be a match for Dialga and Palkia, who rule time and space..."
  say "...and yet its power must be the opposite of theirs, somehow."
  walk cynthia up 1
  face cynthia player
  face player cynthia
  ask "I'm sorry, this has gone on so long. May I say one last thing?"
  if no goto CarriedAway
  say "I wonder who it was that painted this, all that time ago."
  say "Dialga's Roar of Time... Palkia's Spacial Rend... To the people of those days it must truly have seemed that these Pokémon ruled time and space. The sight must have shaken them to the core."
  say "This painting is that awe and that wonder, set down on stone. It carried the memory to more people than anyone could count, until it had become a myth."
  say "That is what I believe, as someone who studies myths."
  say "...I have let myself run on, and for far too long. I'm sorry, and thank you. Let's meet again."
  goto Leave
label CarriedAway
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "I'm so sorry. I let myself get carried away..."
label Leave
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto LeaveFromTheEastTile
  move cynthia down 1 left 4 down 1 left 1 down 6
  goto Watch
label LeaveFromTheEastTile
  move cynthia down 1 left 5 down 1 left 1 down 6
label Watch
  wait 0.5
  face player left
  waitmoves
  hide cynthia
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYNTHIA
  endscene
  return


# Cyrus before the painting (the original's script 2), once the player has stood aside.
script Cyrus
  speaker "Cyrus"
  ask "Well? Am I mistaken? If you believe I am, then challenge me."
  if no goto Coward
  call CyrusBattle
  end
label Coward
  say "...You choose to be a coward. A sensible choice. Stand aside, then, and watch me change the world."
  end


# The battle, and the elder's thanks: HM03.
script CyrusBattle
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "...I know you, Trainer. We met on Mt. Coronet. Why would you defend a world as incomplete as this?"
  say "As the leader of Team Galactic, I will show you how wrong you are."
  battle cyrus as "galactic_boss_cyrus_celestic_town_ruins"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CYNTHIA
  setvar VAR_CELESTIC_TOWN_STATE 1
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "...Remarkable. But your answers have told me what I needed to know, Elder."
  say "If the beings of time and of space were brought together, nothing could stand in their way..."
  wait 0.3
  fade out
  hide cyrus
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_CYRUS
  fade in
  # The player and the elder turn to each other, from wherever the player stood to battle
  if var PLAYER_X == 8 walk player right 1
  if var PLAYER_X == 11 walk player left 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 4 walk player up 1
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto FacingEast
  face player left
  face elder right
  goto Gift
label FacingEast
  face player right
  face elder left
label Gift
  speaker "Elder"
  say "What a strange, strange man, talking such nonsense."
  say "The time and space of Sinnoh are full of the memories and feelings of countless people and Pokémon. It's a wonderful world. Why would anyone want to change it?"
  say "Oh, yes. I found something you might use. Take it. It was my granddaughter's, but she has no need of it now."
  give "HM03"
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_ELDER
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTH_HOUSE_ELDER
  say "Teach a Pokémon Surf, and it will carry you over lakes and the sea."
  say "You've been a great help to this town. Think of that as our thanks."
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 walk elder down 1 left 5 down 1 left 1 down 6
  if var PLAYER_X != 9 walk elder down 1 left 4 down 1 left 1 down 6
  face player down
  hide elder
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_CAVE_ELDER
  return


# The elder beside the painting while Cyrus stands before it (the original's script 3).
script Elder
  speaker "Elder"
  say "We don't look after that painting simply because it's old."
  say "We keep it for the memories it holds: a legacy of people and Pokémon beyond counting."
  say "A man who can't understand that... what sort of world could he possibly dream of?"
  end
