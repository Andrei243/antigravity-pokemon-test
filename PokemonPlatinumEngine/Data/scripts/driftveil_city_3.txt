# A block of flats of Driftveil City by the market (plan 22 · U6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own.
#
# Genesect's drives (plan 40 · GE11), where Black 2 hands them over: in Driftveil City, to a Trainer who shows a Genesect.
# The scientist who studies the Pokémon ships bring from far away (the overlay's person 3) keeps the Burn, Chill and Douse
# Drives, which came in on a ship of Team Plasma's when the P2 Laboratory was emptied (its researcher gives the Shock Drive,
# p2_laboratory_2.txt), and gives them once; to anyone else she says her line (the overlay's), sayown.
script Drives
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_RECEIVED_DRIVES goto Given
  if has "Genesect" goto Shows
  sayown
  end
label Shows
  speaker "Scientist"
  say "Is that a Genesect? An ancient Pokémon, rebuilt with a cannon on its back! I've only ever read about them."
  say "A crate came in on a ship last month, marked with Team Plasma's sign. Inside were three drives made for that cannon."
  say "They're no use to me without the Pokémon. Here, take them all!"
  give "Burn Drive"
  give "Chill Drive"
  give "Douse Drive"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_RECEIVED_DRIVES
  say "When Genesect holds a drive, its Techno Blast takes the drive's type: fire, ice or water. Try each in turn!"
  end
label Given
  speaker "Scientist"
  say "How is your Genesect getting on with its drives? Give it a new one before each battle and keep your foes guessing!"
  end
