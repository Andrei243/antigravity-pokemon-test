# The Season Research Lab (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black
# 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/route_6_2.s, read for the order alone).
#
# As the player comes in with Cheren (unova_route_6.txt, VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 1) he stands in the middle of the lab (0,
# FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_LAB), tells of the cold snap near Lacunosa Town and the readings elsewhere, and gives HM03, Surf, so
# that the player can go and look for themselves (VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 2). A researcher (8) hands a rare Deerling over once
# (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_DEERLING), and the forecaster (9) tells the weather by what the team can do.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  if var VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 != 1 end
  scene "Cheren in the Season Research Lab"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_LAB
  show 0
  walk 0 down 2
  face 0 player
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "Thanks for coming, {player}. I wanted to talk where it's quiet."
  say "Have you heard about the cold snap near Lacunosa Town? Nobody talks about it, but the records show the air there dropped far below freezing for a moment, and it was no more than a moment."
  say "There were readings almost as low around Castelia City and Driftveil City, too."
  say "Add Team Plasma and that ship of theirs, and I don't believe it's a coincidence. Somebody ought to look into it before things get worse. I hate the cold, though."
  say "Here, take this. It's a Hidden Machine with Surf in it. Teach it to one of your Pokémon and you can cross the water on its back."
  give "HM03"
  say "Go to the places I mentioned and see what's going on. I'll stay here and go through the lab's records. We'll compare notes."
  walk 0 up 2
  face 0 down
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_6 2
  endscene
  end


script CherenInTheLab
  face 0 player
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "Sorry for slowing you down. Strange things may be happening, so be careful out there!"
  end


script DeerlingGift
  face 8 player
  speaker "Researcher"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_DEERLING goto Given
  say "You can see a Deerling of every season in our lab! When it comes to Deerling, we're the experts."
  say "We found an unusually fine one. Would you raise it for us?"
  ask "Will you take it?"
  if no goto No
  if var PARTY_COUNT >= 6 goto Full
  givepokemon "Deerling" 10
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_DEERLING
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received Deerling!"
  say "It changes coat with the seasons, so you'll see it in a new colour every few months. Take good care of it!"
  end
label Given
  say "Deerling's coat changes with the season, but not what's underneath. Be kind to yours and it will show."
  end
label No
  say "Oh... Well, it'll be here if you change your mind."
  end
label Full
  say "Your team is full! Make some room, then come back and see me."
  end


# The forecaster tells the weather by what a Pokémon of the team knows
script Forecaster
  face 9 player
  speaker "Forecaster"
  say "Welcome! Show me a Pokémon that can change the weather, and I'll give you a Pokémon weather forecast."
  if knows "Sunny Day" goto Sun
  if knows "Rain Dance" goto Rain
  if knows "Hail" goto Hail
  if knows "Sandstorm" goto Sand
  say "Oh dear. None of your Pokémon knows a move that changes the weather. Bring me one that does, and you'll get your forecast!"
  end
label Sun
  say "Sunny Day! Then expect bright skies: Fire-type moves are stronger by half and Water-type moves weaker by half."
  say "Solar Beam needs no charging, ice can't form, and Thunder and Hurricane are harder to land. Don't forget your sunscreen!"
  end
label Rain
  say "Rain Dance! Then expect showers: Water-type moves are stronger by half and Fire-type moves weaker by half."
  say "Thunder and Hurricane won't miss, but Solar Beam is slow in the gloom. Don't forget your umbrella!"
  end
label Hail
  say "Hail! Then expect a hailstorm: every Pokémon that isn't an Ice type is hurt a little at each turn's end."
  say "Blizzard won't miss in it, and Solar Beam is slow. Don't forget your scarf!"
  end
label Sand
  say "Sandstorm! Then expect flying sand: every Pokémon that isn't Rock, Ground or Steel is hurt a little at each turn's end."
  say "Rock types find their defense raised. Don't forget your goggles!"
  end
