# VAR_BRINEY_HOUSE_STATE 0: the old sailor at home with Peeko, his Wingull, his boat idle on the beach; 1 Peeko is safe,
# and he would sail anywhere for the player. The sailing itself, to Dewford Town, is plan 21 · H3's.


script Briney
  speaker "Mr. Briney"
  if var VAR_BRINEY_HOUSE_STATE >= 1 goto Grateful
  say "Ahoy! I'm Briney. Once I sailed every day. These days I'd rather sit here with Peeko and watch the waves."
  end
label Grateful
  say "Ahoy, my friend! You saved my Peeko, and an old sailor never forgets a debt."
  say "Whenever you need to cross the water, my boat is yours. Just give me a little while to get her seaworthy!"
  end


script Peeko
  cry "Wingull"
  text "Peeko: Pihyoo!"
  end
