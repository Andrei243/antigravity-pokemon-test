# Victory Road's upper floor (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/VictoryRoad_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Wally catches the player up as they first come up the west passage (VAR_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_STATE 0, the two triggers):
# he runs in along the passage below, battles (hoenn_wally_vr_1), and waits there afterwards, where he stopped (the
# variable 1 or 2, which the floor's arrival reads to put him back, as Emerald's setobjectxyperm does), until the Hall of
# Fame. From the Hall of Fame on he waits by the way out to the League instead (FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_EXIT_WALLY
# cleared) and battles once more (hoenn_wally_vr_2). A save from before this stretch is given its hiding here
# (common.HoennChapterTwelve).


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_TWELVE call HoennChapterTwelve
  if var VAR_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_STATE == 1 place victory_road_entrance_wally 2 24 down
  if var VAR_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_STATE == 2 place victory_road_entrance_wally 3 24 down
  end


# The west tile of the passage: Wally runs ten tiles along the passage below and up to the player
script WallyTrigger1
  scene "Wally on Victory Road from the west"
  place victory_road_entrance_wally 12 25 left
  show victory_road_entrance_wally
  walk victory_road_entrance_wally left 10 up 1
  call Battle
  setvar VAR_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_STATE 1
  endscene
  end


# The east tile of the passage: nine tiles along, and up
script WallyTrigger2
  scene "Wally on Victory Road from the east"
  place victory_road_entrance_wally 12 25 left
  show victory_road_entrance_wally
  walk victory_road_entrance_wally left 9 up 1
  call Battle
  setvar VAR_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_STATE 2
  endscene
  end


# His words and his battle (a battle lost ends the scene there, and he is back below to try again)
script Battle
  face player down
  face victory_road_entrance_wally player
  speaker "Wally"
  say "{player}! I got every Badge too, just like you. I came all this way to tell you something."
  say "I'm not the boy who couldn't even catch a Pokémon any more. I've trained hard with my partners."
  say "So this time I won't lose. Not to anyone, not even to you!"
  battle victory_road_entrance_wally as "hoenn_wally_vr_1"
  speaker "Wally"
  say "I lost again... But it doesn't feel the same as the first time. I gave it everything I had."
  say "You go on ahead, {player}. I'm going to rest here a little, and then I'll follow you to the League!"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_ENTRANCE_WALLY
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_WALLY_VICTORY_ROAD
  return


# Wally by the passage after his battle, until the Hall of Fame
script EntranceWally
  face self player
  speaker "Wally"
  say "You go on ahead, {player}. I'll follow you to the League once I've rested!"
  end


# Wally by the way out, after the Hall of Fame: one more battle, then a word
script ExitWally
  face self player
  speaker "Wally"
  if defeated "hoenn_wally_vr_2" goto Beaten
  say "{player}! I heard you became the Champion. Congratulations! I knew you would."
  say "I've been training here ever since, so I could battle the Champion. Will you show me what that's like?"
  battle self as "hoenn_wally_vr_2"
  speaker "Wally"
  say "Wow... So that's the strength of a Champion. I've still got a long way to go."
label Beaten
  say "I'll keep training on Victory Road. One day I'll catch up with you, {player}. Just you wait!"
  end
