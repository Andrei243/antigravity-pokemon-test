# The Secluded Shore, Ula'ula Island (plan 23, the rest of the islands). The lines are our own. A girl looks for the
# right sand for her Sandygast; a Sandygast of the player's team shows her (FLAG_ALOLA_SHORE_SAND_DONE), and she gives
# a Stardust, as Ultra Sun's girl does.


script SandGirl
  face self player
  speaker "Girl"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_SHORE_SAND_DONE goto Done
  if has "Sandygast" goto Show
  if has "Palossand" goto Show
  say "My Sandygast wants new sand for its body, but it's so fussy! It needs sand that a Ghost-type has already chosen."
  say "If you had a Sandygast of your own, maybe it could show us which sand is best..."
  end
label Show
  say "Is that a Sandygast? Look, it's digging over by the rocks! That must be the good sand!"
  say "Thank you! Mine's already rolling in it. Please, take this. It washed up here last week."
  give "Stardust"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SHORE_SAND_DONE
  end
label Done
  say "My Sandygast has never looked so firm. It's the envy of every sandcastle on the island!"
  end
