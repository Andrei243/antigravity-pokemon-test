# The cable car's station on Route 112 (plan 21 · H4, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own. Emerald's attendant sells the ride up Mt. Chimney; the ride and the summit are plan 21 · H4's next part, so
# here she says the car is being looked over.


script Attendant
  speaker "Attendant"
  if flag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_112_TEAM_MAGMA goto Gone
  say "Welcome to the Mt. Chimney Cable Car. Some rough-looking people have been hanging about outside, haven't they?"
  say "I'm afraid the car is being looked over just now. Please come back later!"
  end
label Gone
  say "Those people in red have gone at last. But the car is still being looked over, I'm sorry to say."
  say "It'll be running again soon. Please come back later!"
  end
