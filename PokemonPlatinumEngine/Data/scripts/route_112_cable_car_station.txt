# The cable car's station on Route 112 (plan 21 · H4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route112_CableCarStation/scripts.inc, data/scripts/cable_car.inc).
#
# The attendant sends the car up Mt. Chimney once Team Magma's grunts have left the road outside (Emerald's grunts
# stand in the way of the door; ours stand beside it, so she waits for FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_112_TEAM_MAGMA). The station's
# corridor to the car is one tile wide: she steps up onto the platform before the car and the player follows her up
# the corridor, the picture goes dark over the ride, and the summit's station puts the player down
# (VAR_CABLE_CAR_STATION_STATE 1 going up, 2 coming down; each station's OnEnter plays its end of the ride).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_CABLE_CAR_STATION_STATE != 2 end
  place route112_cable_car_attendant 6 3 down
  fade in
  walk player down 3
  move route112_cable_car_attendant down 3
  waitmoves
  face player up
  setvar VAR_CABLE_CAR_STATION_STATE 0
  end


script Attendant
  speaker "Attendant"
  if not flag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_112_TEAM_MAGMA goto Shut
  ask "Welcome to the Mt. Chimney Cable Car! Shall I send you up to the summit?"
  if no goto Later
  say "Lovely! Step this way, please, and mind the gap."
  move route112_cable_car_attendant up 3
  waitmoves
  face route112_cable_car_attendant down
  walk player up 3
  setvar VAR_CABLE_CAR_STATION_STATE 1
  fade out
  music "hoenn/cable_car"
  text "The car sways gently on its wire as it climbs above the ash..."
  wait 1.5
  warp "MtChimney_CableCarStation" 6 4 down
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you're ready. The view from the top is worth the trip!"
  end
label Shut
  say "Welcome to the Mt. Chimney Cable Car. Some rough-looking people have been hanging about outside, haven't they?"
  say "I'm afraid I can't send anyone up while they're there. Please come back later!"
  end
