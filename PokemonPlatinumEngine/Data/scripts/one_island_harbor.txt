# One Island's harbour (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/OneIsland_Harbor/scripts.inc).
#
# The sailor sails a holder of the Tri-Pass back to Vermilion City or to Cinnabar Island (common.SeagallopTo…); Two and
# Three Island, which the pass is also for, are plan 19 · K9's. FireRed's ship at the pier's end lies past the room's
# floor here, and stays out of sight (FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_HARBOR_SEAGALLOP, a flag of ours).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_HARBOR_SEAGALLOP
  end


script Sailor
  face sailor player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if not item "Tri-Pass" goto NoPass
  say "Welcome aboard the Seagallop! Have your Tri-Pass ready."
  choose "Where would you like to sail?" "VERMILION CITY" "CINNABAR ISLAND" "NOT NOW"
  if result == 0 goto Vermilion
  if result == 1 goto Cinnabar
  say "Come back any time."
  end
label Vermilion
  say "All aboard for Vermilion City!"
  call SeagallopToVermilion
  end
label Cinnabar
  say "All aboard for Cinnabar Island!"
  call SeagallopToCinnabar
  end
label NoPass
  say "The Seagallop sails only for holders of a pass, I'm afraid. Celio in the Pokémon Center might be able to help."
  end
