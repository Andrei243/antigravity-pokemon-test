# Royal Avenue, Akala Island (plan 23 · Z6). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The rest of Akala: trials, totems and Olivia's grand trial").
#
# VAR_ALOLA_AKALA, the rest of Akala's count (Brooklet Hill's end is VAR_ALOLA_STORY 18, which this count waits for):
#   0  Lana's trial passed, nothing of the rest yet
#   1  Royal Avenue: Professor Kukui and Hau before the Battle Royal Dome, Hau's battle (this file)
#   2  Route 8: Team Skull at the motel and Wicke of the Aether Foundation (akala_route_8.txt)
#   3  Kiawe's trial at Wela Volcano Park and the Firium Z (wela_volcano_park.txt)
#   4  Mallow's trial in the Lush Jungle and the Grassium Z (lush_jungle.txt)
#   5  Olivia met at Konikoni City's gate (konikoni_city.txt)
#   6  The Ruins of Life with Lillie, Tapu Lele seen (akala_outskirts.txt)
#   7  Olivia's grand trial: Akala's stamp, the Rockium Z and Machamp in the Ride Pager (konikoni_city.txt)
#   8  The Hano Grand Resort: Lillie, Hau and Wicke's invitation (hano_beach.txt); FLAG_ALOLA_AKALA_DONE
#
# The people: kukui and hau before the dome, shown once Lana's trial is passed (FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_TRIAL_DONE) until
# the scene is played (FLAG_ALOLA_ROYAL_DONE). Hau's teams are alola_hau_royal_1 to _3, the one strong against the
# player's, its ace holding a crystal of its type.


script RoyalScene
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY < 18 goto NotYet
  scene "Royal Avenue"
  speaker "Hau"
  say "{player}! Over here! You passed Lana's trial too? Awesome!"
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Alola, cousin! This is the Battle Royal Dome. Four trainers in one ring, each against the rest. It gets loud, yeah!"
  say "It's not open to newcomers yet. So how about a regular battle out here instead, the two of you?"
  speaker "Hau"
  say "Yes! I've been training the whole way from Paniola. Let's go!"
  heal
  if starter "Rowlet" goto AgainstRowlet
  if starter "Litten" goto AgainstLitten
  battle hau as "alola_hau_royal_3" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstRowlet
  battle hau as "alola_hau_royal_1" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstLitten
  battle hau as "alola_hau_royal_2" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "Hau"
  if lost goto HauWon
  say "Aw, man! Your Pokémon are on fire today. I'm getting a malasada to recover."
  goto Done
label HauWon
  say "I won! I won! Okay, I'm still getting a malasada."
label Done
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Woo! That's what I like to see. Kiawe's trial is on Wela Volcano Park: Route 8 north, then Route 7's beach."
  hide kukui
  hide hau
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ROYAL_DONE
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_AKALA 1
  objective alolaMotel
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  end


script Kukui
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Isn't the dome something? One day you'll stand in that ring, cousin. I can feel it!"
  end

script Hau
  speaker "Hau"
  say "Royal Avenue's got everything! Shops, a dome, and a malasada stand somewhere. I just have to find it."
  end
