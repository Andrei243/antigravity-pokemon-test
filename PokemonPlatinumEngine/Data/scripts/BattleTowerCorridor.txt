# The corridor of the Battle Tower's Battle Rooms (plan 06 · R18a). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# scripts_battle_tower_corridor.s and frontier_scripts_battle_tower_corridor.s are the original.
#
# The attendant leads whoever comes out of the lift with a challenge under way to one of the corridor's four doors,
# drawn each time (the original's GetRandom 4), and the Battle Room behind it. Anyone else finds an empty corridor
# and the lift back down.


script OnEnter
  place attendant 2 4 up
  tower active
  if result == 0 end
  face attendant up
  tower door
  if result == 1 goto Second
  if result == 2 goto Third
  if result == 3 goto Fourth
  walk attendant right 4 up 1
  hide attendant
  walk player down 1 right 4 up 1
  goto In
label Second
  walk attendant right 8 up 1
  hide attendant
  walk player down 1 right 8 up 1
  goto In
label Third
  walk attendant right 12 up 1
  hide attendant
  walk player down 1 right 12 up 1
  goto In
label Fourth
  walk attendant right 16 up 1
  hide attendant
  walk player down 1 right 16 up 1
label In
  sound "door_slide"
  warp "BattleTowerBattleRoom" 7 10 up
  end
