# Poni Breakers and the Ruins of Hope, Poni Island (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md,
# "Ula'ula, Poni, Aether Paradise and the League"): Hapu waits on the shore once Dulse's trial is passed (VAR_ALOLA_STORY
# 50) and tells the player the way across the strait; the guard at the road to the Altar stands aside after the grand
# trial (FLAG_ALOLA_ALTAR_OPEN); and Tapu Fini, the sea's guardian, rests in the ruins after the Hall of Fame.


script HapuShore
  face hapu player
  speaker "Hapu"
  say "Hey. You're {player}, the one who's been collecting stamps. I've heard about you."
  say "I'm Hapu, kahuna of Poni. My grand trial is on Exeggutor Island, across this strait."
  say "You'll need something that swims. Lapras will carry you, if you've got it in the Ride Pager."
  say "I'll be there. Don't dawdle. I get bored fast, and when I get bored I get strong."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_HAPU_SHORE_DONE
  end


script AltarGuard
  face guard player
  speaker "Guard"
  say "The road up to the Altar of the Sunne and Moone is closed to everyone but the one who holds Poni's stamp."
  end


script TapuFini
  face self player
  speaker none
  cry "Tapu Fini"
  text "Tapu Fini's mist rolled over the ruins, and it rose from the water."
  if flag AlolaHallOfFame goto Fight
  text "It watched {player} for a moment, and then let the mist close again."
  end
label Fight
  wildbattle "Tapu Fini" 60 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_TAPU_FINI_DONE
  hide self
  end
