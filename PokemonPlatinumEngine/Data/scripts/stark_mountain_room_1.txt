# Stark Mountain's first room (plan 01 · M10, part b; its story plan 02 · S15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_stark_mountain_room_1.s).
#
# Arriving sets what the original's does: the place counts as come to, and Buck is gone from Route 227, where he has
# waited since his question (he comes in after the player in the next room).
#
# The first time anyone comes in (VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_1_STATE 0) Team Galactic is there before them: Commander
# Mars and Commander Jupiter side by side, and behind them Charon between two grunts. Mars wants to know what became
# of Cyrus, won't believe the answer, and battles; beaten, she steps back and Jupiter battles in her place. Beaten
# too, they have had enough: Mars leaves to look for Cyrus, Jupiter to do as she pleases, and they walk out past the
# player. Charon, who thinks them both fools and Cyrus a bigger one, has no interest in a child: he goes on up the
# room with his grunts to fetch the Magma Stone (FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_1_TEAM_GALACTIC, state 1).
#
# A battle lost is a white-out, and the scene plays again from its start the next time the room is come into, as the
# original's does (nothing is set until its end), so the two are stood back in their places first.
#
# Stood in for: the Commanders' words in battle (plan 11 · C12) and Team Galactic's tune (plan 05 · A6).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_STARK_MOUNTAIN_INTERIOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_227_BUCK
  if var VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_1_STATE == 0 call TeamGalactic
  end


# StarkMountainOutside_OnFrame_TeamGalactic (the original's own name for it): the player stands a step inside the way
# in (20, 28)
script TeamGalactic
  # A game saved further up the room before there was anyone here is brought down to meet them
  if var PLAYER_Y != 28 place player 20 28 up
  scene "Mars and Jupiter"
  place mars 20 26 down
  place jupiter 21 26 down
  face player up
  emote mars exclaim
  speaker "Mars"
  say "Hey! You! You're going to tell us where Master Cyrus went!"
  say "Where did the two of you get to, when you vanished off Spear Pillar? ...Another world? A world turned inside out, where Giratina lives?"
  say "Do you expect me to swallow that? Fine. If you won't talk sense, I'll shake some out of you!"
  walk mars down 1
  battle mars as "commander_mars_stark_mountain"
  # She steps back before she speaks (the original speaks first): right before the player she is behind their head
  walk mars up 1
  face mars down
  speaker "Mars"
  say "My Pokémon let me down again! How am I ever going to find out what happened to Master Cyrus now?"
  face jupiter left
  wait 0.4
  face jupiter down
  speaker "Jupiter"
  say "...Mars. You are hopeless. It embarrasses me to be called a Commander in the same breath as you."
  say "Very well. I'll take my turn. I want to know what became of Master Cyrus as much as you do."
  walk jupiter down 1
  battle jupiter as "commander_jupiter_stark_mountain"
  speaker "Jupiter"
  say "...How dare you!"
  wait 0.5
  emote mars exclaim
  face jupiter up
  speaker "Mars"
  say "Right! That does it for me! I'm going to find Master Cyrus myself. That inside-out world: that's where I'll look."
  say "I'm through with Team Galactic. Jupiter, it's all yours!"
  speaker "Jupiter"
  say "It galls me to follow Mars in anything, but... I'm leaving as well, and I'll do as I like from now on. Without Master Cyrus there is nothing in Team Galactic worth staying for."
  say "So there you are, old man. Do what you please with it."
  face mars up
  wait 0.4
  face mars down
  wait 0.4
  speaker "Mars"
  say "You heard us. From today we're two ordinary girls. Tidy up whatever's left any way you want!"
  walk player left 1
  face player right
  walk mars down 3
  sound "stairs"
  hide mars
  face jupiter right
  speaker "Jupiter"
  say "...An ordinary girl. Her? Whatever she is, she will never be that."
  say "And what am I to do with myself now? ...A journey, perhaps. They say it is the thing to do."
  walk jupiter left 1 down 2
  sound "stairs"
  hide jupiter
  face charon down
  face player up
  speaker "Charon"
  say "Haaah... So easily swayed, the pair of them, and so quick to flounce off. What did they ever see in Cyrus?"
  say "A boy who thought too much and grew up too little. All that trouble to build Team Galactic, and then he throws it away for a dream of his own."
  say "But no matter. Let the young keep their dreams. I prefer to keep my feet in the real world, and in the real world it is money that counts."
  say "You, child: I have no business with you. The ones who did all the hard work have just walked out, so I shall go and collect the Magma Stone myself. Tee hee!"
  move charon up 7
  move grunt_m_1 right 1 up 7
  wait 0.3
  move grunt_m_2 left 1 up 7
  waitmoves
  hide charon
  hide grunt_m_1
  hide grunt_m_2
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_1_TEAM_GALACTIC
  setvar VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_1_STATE 1
  endscene
  return


# Anyone of Team Galactic's spoken to (nobody is: the scene has them from the first step in)
script Galactic
  speaker none
  text "They are watching the way in, and haven't a word to spare."
  end
