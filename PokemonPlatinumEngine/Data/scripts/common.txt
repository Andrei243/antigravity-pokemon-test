# What every place shares: the scripts the field starts for what a person or a thing is.
# docs/scripts.md describes the language. The lines are our own.


# What every game starts with. It is run with no screen, for a new game and for a save from before the story
# was kept, so it may set flags and variables and do nothing else. A chapter that hides someone until their
# scene comes sets the flag that hides them here.
script NewGame
  end


# Someone with lines to say.
script Talk
  sayown
  end


# A signboard: its text, under the word "Sign".
script Sign
  speaker "Sign"
  sayown
  end


# An item lying on the ground in its ball. Its own flag is the one that hides the ball, so it is gone for good,
# and what was in it is the player's.
script ItemBall
  setflag own
  find own
  end


# An item nobody can see, found by looking at the place where it lies. Its own flag says it has been found.
script HiddenItem
  setflag own
  find own
  end


# The nurse of a Pokémon Center.
script Nurse
  say "Good day! You've come to a Pokémon Center."
  ask "Your Pokémon look as if they could do with a rest. Shall I see to them?"
  if no goto Goodbye
  say "Leave them with me. It won't take a moment."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "There we are! Every one of them is fit and well again."
label Goodbye
  say "Take care out there, and come back whenever you need us!"
  end


# Someone at home who looks after the team without being asked: what they have to say, and it is done.
script Healer
  sayown
  heal
  fanfare heal
  end


# The clerk of a Poké Mart: their greeting, the counter, a word on the way out.
script Clerk
  sayown
  shop
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end


# A PC.
script PC
  text "{player} switched the PC on."
  pc
  end


# Professor Rowan's briefcase, with the three Pokémon in it.
script Briefcase
  starter
  end


# Whoever takes travellers on to the next region. Where no way leads on from here, they only talk.
script Attendant
  travel
  if result == 0 sayown
  end


# A trainer: the challenge and the battle. Spoken to again once beaten, they say what they have left to say.
# A battle lost ends the script, as the player wakes up elsewhere.
script Trainer
  if defeated self goto Beaten
  trainerline before
  battle self
  end
label Beaten
  trainerline after
  if result == 0 sayown
  end
