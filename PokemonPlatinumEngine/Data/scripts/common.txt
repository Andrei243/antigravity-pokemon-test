# What every game starts with. It is run with no screen, for a new game and for a save from before the story
# was kept, so it may set flags and variables and do nothing else. A chapter that hides someone until their
# scene comes sets the flag that hides them here.
#
# Plan 02 · S4, the original's InitNewGame: the rival bursting into the bedroom and his mother calling by later,
# the rival coming out of his door, the professor, the assistant and the briefcase on Route 201, the people of
# Lake Verity's later scenes and the legendary in its cavern, the rival and the professor in Sandgem Town; and the
# guitarist who keeps the player in town until the rival has left home.
script NewGame
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TWINLEAF_TOWN_PLAYER_HOUSE_2F_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TWINLEAF_TOWN_PLAYER_HOUSE_1F_RIVAL_MOM
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TWINLEAF_TOWN_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_COUNTERPART
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_PROF_ROWAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_BRIEFCASE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_STARLY
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_PROF_ROWAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_COUNTERPART
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VERITY_CAVERN_MESPRIT
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_PROF_ROWAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_PROF_OAK
  setvar VAR_TWINLEAF_TOWN_GUITARIST_TRIGGER_STATE 1
  call ChapterTwo
  return

# The nurse of a Pokémon Center.
script Nurse
  say "Good day! You've come to a Pokémon Center."
  ask "Your Pokémon look as if they could do with a rest. Shall I see to them?"
  if no goto Goodbye
  say "Leave them with me. It won't take a moment."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "There we are! Every one of them is fit and well again."
  # The first time one of the team has Pokérus, she says what it is (CommonScript_NurseCheckPokerus)
  if flag FLAG_POKECENTER_IDENTIFIED_POKERUS goto Goodbye
  if not pokerus goto Goodbye
  setflag FLAG_POKECENTER_IDENTIFIED_POKERUS
  say "Oh, one more thing. Your Pokémon seem to be carrying something very small... Pokérus, we call it."
  say "Don't worry, it does them no harm. Pokémon who have it grow a little stronger from every battle, and it fades away after a few days."
  end
label Goodbye
  say "Take care out there, and come back whenever you need us!"
  end

# A trainer: the challenge and the battle. Spoken to again once beaten, they say what they have left to say.
# A battle lost ends the script, as the player wakes up elsewhere.
script Trainer
  if defeated self goto Beaten
  trainerline before
  battle self
  end
label Beaten
  # The Vs. Seeker found them wanting a rematch (plan 06 · R12): the team of the level the story has reached
  if rematch self goto Rematch
  trainerline after
  if result == 0 sayown
  end
label Rematch
  trainerline before
  battle self rematch
  end


# ---- Field moves (plan 02 · S2): what the obstacles and the water ask, after the original's
# scripts_field_moves.s, in our own words. The obstacle scripts run when the player faces one and presses the
# button; the Use scripts run when a field move is chosen from the party menu, which has checked the move and its
# badge already. An obstacle's own flag is local to its area: what is cleared is back once the player leaves. An
# obstacle asks in the game's own voice, with no name on the box.

# A thin tree in the way. Cut clears it, with the Forest Badge.
script CutTree
  speaker none
  if not knows "Cut" goto Cannot
  if not badge forest goto Cannot
  ask "This thin tree could be cut down. Use Cut?"
  if no end
  usemove "Cut"
  setflag own
  end
label Cannot
  text "A thin tree stands in the way. A sharp enough move might clear it."
  end

script UseCut
  usemove "Cut"
  setflag own
  end

# A heavy boulder. Strength, with the Mine Badge, lets every boulder here be pushed until the player leaves.
script Boulder
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_STRENGTH_ACTIVE goto Ready
  if not knows "Strength" goto Cannot
  if not badge mine goto Cannot
  ask "A heavy boulder. Use Strength to shift it?"
  if no end
  call StrengthOn
  end
label Ready
  text "With Strength in use, the boulders here can be pushed."
  end
label Cannot
  text "A heavy boulder. Only a Pokémon of great power could shift it."
  end

script UseStrength
  if flag FLAG_STRENGTH_ACTIVE goto Ready
  call StrengthOn
  end
label Ready
  text "With Strength in use, the boulders here can be pushed."
  end

script StrengthOn
  usemove "Strength"
  setflag FLAG_STRENGTH_ACTIVE
  text "{user} braces itself. The boulders here can be pushed now."
  return

# One of the Cycling Road's gate keepers, to someone who comes to the gate on foot: back they go.
script CyclistsOnly
  speaker "Gate Keeper"
  say "Hold on there! Past this gate is the Cycling Road, and it's for Bicycles only. Come back riding one!"
  if facing up walk player down
  if facing down walk player up
  if facing left walk player right
  if facing right walk player left
  end


# Coming round after whiting out (plan 06 · R10; the original's CommonScript_PokecenterBlackOutRecover and
# CommonScript_PlayerHouseBlackOutRecover): the game has put the player in front of the nurse of the Pokémon Center
# they last went into, or beside Mom at home, and this is the one who looks after them.
script BlackOutCenter
  say "Oh my, you came in looking exhausted. First, let me look after your Pokémon."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "There we are. Every one of them is fit and well again."
  say "Please be careful out there. We'll be here whenever you need us."
  end

script BlackOutHome
  face self player
  say "{player}! Look at you, you're worn out. Sit down and rest for a bit."
  fade out
  fanfare heal
  heal
  fade in
  say "There. You all look much better now. Off you go again, and look after each other out there!"
  end
