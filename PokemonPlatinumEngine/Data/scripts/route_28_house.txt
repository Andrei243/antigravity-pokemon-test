# A woman who once starred in films hides here from her fans with her Fearow. She asks the player to keep her secret
# and gives TM47 once (FLAG_GOT_TM47_ON_ROUTE_28, ours).


script Celebrity
  speaker "Celebrity"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TM47_ON_ROUTE_28 goto Given
  say "Oh! You found me... Please don't tell anyone I'm hiding out here. My fans never give me a moment's peace."
  say "If you keep my secret, I'll give you this. My Fearow and I used it in all of our films."
  give "TM47"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM47_ON_ROUTE_28
  say "TM47 is Steel Wing. It hits hard, and it can make the user's Defense rise too!"
  end
label Given
  say "It's so quiet out here. Nobody comes this far west but the toughest trainers."
  end
