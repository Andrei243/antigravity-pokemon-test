# The Cerulean City Gym (plan 19 · K4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/CeruleanCity_Gym/scripts.inc).
#
# Misty battles for the Cascade Badge (FLAG_BADGE02_GET is the badge itself, `givebadge cascade`, which opens Cut in
# the field) and gives TM03, Water Pulse. Her Badge counts her two trainers beaten (FireRed's set_gym_trainers 2).


script Misty
  face misty player
  speaker "Misty"
  if badge cascade goto Won
  scene "Misty"
  say "Hi, you're a new face! Trainers who want to turn pro need a policy for raising their Pokémon."
  say "Mine? It's all-out offence with Water types! Let's see if you can keep your head above water!"
  battle self as "kanto_leader_misty"
  say "Wow, you're too much! All right, you can have the Cascade Badge to show you beat me."
  givebadge cascade
  text "{player} received the Cascade Badge from Misty!"
  say "With the Cascade Badge, your Pokémon can use Cut outside of battle, to clear away small trees."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_MISTY
  defeat "kanto_picnicker_diana"
  defeat "kanto_swimmer_male_luis"
label Tm
  say "And take this too. It's one of my favourite moves."
  give "TM03"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM03_FROM_MISTY
  say "TM03 is Water Pulse. It hits with a wave of sound and water, and it can leave the foe confused."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_TM03_FROM_MISTY goto Tm
  say "Water Pulse can throw the foe off balance. Use it well, and remember who taught you!"
  end


# The guide by the door: advice before the Badge, praise after.
script GymGuy
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge cascade goto Won
  say "Yo, champ in the making! Misty's Pokémon are Water types, and they hit hard."
  say "Electric and Grass moves are what you want here. Zap them or root them out!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Misty! What a battle. We make a pretty good team, you and me!"
  end
