# The gallery at the top of the Vista Lighthouse in Sunyshore City (plan 02 · S13), on the original's plan.
# docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_vista_lighthouse.s).
#
# Volkner, the city's Gym Leader, stands before the binoculars looking out to sea: he has rebuilt his Gym down to the
# last gear, no challenger has been worth his while for a long time, and he is thinking of leaving it to challenge the
# Pokémon League himself. Spoken to, he sizes the player up, says he will throw everything he has at them, and goes
# back to his Gym by the lift (FLAG_VOLKNER_RETURNED_TO_GYM, FLAG_HIDE_VISTA_LIGHTHOUSE_VOLKNER, which the original's
# RemoveObject sets). That is what moves Flint from before the Gym's door (sunyshore_city.txt).
#
# The binoculars are read from the tile Volkner stands on, so nobody looks through them until he has gone.
#
# Stood in for still: the original's binoculars take the picture to the Pokémon League across the sea, seen through
# their lenses (UseVistaLighthouseBinoculars: the map itself is changed, with a camera of its own); here the view is
# told over a black screen. Plan 09 · L4's camera kit doesn't give it: its path and its lens move the camera over the
# map the player stands on, and the gallery is a room, seen whole, while the view is of another map with nobody in it
# (as the title's fly-over is). That wants a command of its own, which is still owed. The lift's door doesn't open and
# shut (the car stands open in its shaft).


# Our own, beside the original: the lift's car asks which way its rider came (VistaLighthouseElevator.txt)
script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_VISTA_LIGHTHOUSE_FLOOR 1
  end


# VistaLighthouse_Volkner
script Volkner
  scene "Volkner at the lighthouse"
  face self player
  speaker "Volkner"
  say "... ..."
  say "...So you're the next one. The latest Trainer come to take on Sunyshore's Gym."
  say "All right. I've made up my mind. If you turn out to be weak, I'm leaving, to challenge the Pokémon League myself."
  say "The Gym is rebuilt, down to the last gear. Nothing keeps me here any more. What I want is a battle that makes my heart race again."
  say "They call me the strongest Gym Leader in Sinnoh. So everything I have, I'm throwing at you."
  wait 0.4
  if facing left goto Round
  if facing right goto Round
  # VistaLighthouse_VolknerWalkToDoorNorth: the player stands south of him, so he goes round by the east
  move volkner right 3 down 6 left 3
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  waitmoves
  goto Leaves
# VistaLighthouse_VolknerWalkToDoorWestEast
label Round
  move volkner down 1 right 3 down 5 left 3
  wait 0.3
  face player down
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  waitmoves
label Leaves
  face volkner up
  sound "door_slide"
  wait 0.3
  walk volkner up 1
  hide volkner
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VISTA_LIGHTHOUSE_VOLKNER
  sound "lift"
  setflag FLAG_VOLKNER_RETURNED_TO_GYM
  endscene
  end


# VistaLighthouse_Binoculars
script Binoculars
  speaker none
  say "{player} looked through the binoculars."
  sound "select"
  fade out 0.4
  say "Far to the north, across the sea, a wall of rock climbs out of the water, with a waterfall white down its face."
  say "And on top of it, half lost in cloud, stand the towers of the Pokémon League."
  fade in
  end
