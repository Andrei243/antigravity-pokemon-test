# Circhester Stadium's pitch (plan 33 · GL5, its second session): the gym challenge's sixth Leader, Gordie in a Sword
# game (the Rock Badge) and Melony in a Shield one (the Ice Badge, in the same place on the card). Both stand on one spot,
# the version's shown by FLAG_GALAR_SHIELD_GAME, which the pitch's arrival keeps to VAR_GALAR_VERSION. They Dynamax their
# last Pokémon. VAR_GALAR_STORY 31 to 32 (postwick.txt's count). Our own lines.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_SHIELD_GAME
  end
label Shield
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_SHIELD_GAME
  end


script Gordie
  face gordie player
  speaker "Gordie"
  if badge rock goto Won
  scene "Gordie"
  say "You made it through the pits, then. I'm Gordie. Circhester's Leader, and yes, my mother's the Leader you've heard of."
  say "I'm not her. I'm the one standing in front of you. Let's see you move a mountain!"
  battle gordie as "galar_gordie_1"
  say "...Fair's fair. You moved it. Not many do."
  say "The Rock Badge is yours."
  givebadge rock
  text "{player} received the Rock Badge from Gordie!"
  call AfterTheBadge
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Rock doesn't move for anyone. Except you, apparently."
  end


script Melony
  face melony player
  speaker "Melony"
  if badge ice goto Won
  scene "Melony"
  say "Hello, dear! Out of the cold and onto my pitch. I'm Melony, and Circhester's ice is mine to look after."
  say "I'll warn you now, I've a son who'd like my job. That only makes me battle the harder. Ready?"
  battle melony as "galar_melony_1"
  say "My, my. You've thawed my whole team. That doesn't happen often."
  say "The Ice Badge. Wear it with pride, dear."
  givebadge ice
  text "{player} received the Ice Badge from Melony!"
  call AfterTheBadge
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Keep warm out there, dear. The bay is bitter this time of year."
  end


# What either Leader's badge leaves: the gym's trainers beaten, Hop and Sonia gone on, and the way west along Route 9
script AfterTheBadge
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_CIRCHESTER_GYM
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_carys"
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_tomos"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_CIRCHESTER
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_SONIA_CIRCHESTER
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 32
  objective galarBike
  text "Six badges. Route 9 runs west of the town, along the shore of Circhester Bay."
  return
