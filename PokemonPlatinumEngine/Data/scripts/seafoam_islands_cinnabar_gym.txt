# Blaine's Gym in the Seafoam Islands as HeartGold has it (plan 20 · J15, part g). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D11R0106).
#
# Cinnabar Island's volcano erupted and took the old Gym with it, so Blaine keeps his Gym in a hall of the Seafoam
# Islands' rock. He waits at its head and battles for the Volcano Badge, which is Kanto's (`givebadge volcano of kanto`,
# as the other Kanto Gyms of HeartGold's: the rulings' "HeartGold's Kanto, part b" to "part g"), and gives TM50,
# Overheat. Whether he is beaten here is FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_BLAINE, a flag of ours, so a FireRed Champion battles him
# all the same. The Gym has no trainers of its own: its volunteers only talk.


script Blaine
  face d11r0106_gsleader15 player
  speaker "Blaine"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_BLAINE goto Won
  scene "Blaine"
  say "So, you found my hideaway. The volcano swallowed my old Gym, and now I keep my fire in a cave of ice."
  say "But a Leader's fire doesn't go out with a building. A Champion from Johto, is it? Then let's turn up the heat!"
  battle self as "johto_leader_blaine_blaine"
  say "My flames are out, for now. You've earned this: the Volcano Badge."
  givebadge volcano of kanto
  text "{player} received the Volcano Badge from Blaine!"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_BLAINE
  endscene
label Tm
  say "And take this, too. It holds my hottest move."
  give "TM50"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM50_FROM_BLAINE
  say "TM50 is Overheat. It's fearsomely strong, but it leaves the user's Special Attack much lower afterwards."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM50_FROM_BLAINE goto Tm
  say "One day I'll build my Gym on Cinnabar again. Until then, the ice keeps my fire honest."
  end


# The Gym guide by the door.
script Guide
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_BLAINE goto Won
  say "Hey, champ in the making! Blaine's Pokémon burn hot. Water and Ground moves will cool them right down."
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Blaine! The ice in here nearly melted!"
  end
