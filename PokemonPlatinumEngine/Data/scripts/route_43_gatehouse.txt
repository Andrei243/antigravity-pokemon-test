# The gate house in the middle of Route 43 (plan 20 · J11, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_R43R0201).
#
# Until the Team Rocket Hideout is cleared (FLAG_ROCKET_HIDEOUT_CLEARED, team_rocket_headquarters_b2f.txt) two of
# Team Rocket's men stand in the gate house and make whoever comes in pay ¥1,000 to pass, or whatever they have
# left when that is less: HeartGold's room script asks it on every arrival, which is where this one asks it too. The
# guard is out of the way meanwhile (FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_43_GATE_GUARD, which HeartGold's new game sets and this
# arrival sets in its place), and once he is back he gives TM36, Sludge Bomb (FLAG_GOT_TM36_FROM_ROUTE_43_GUARD).


# R43R0201_002 and _001
script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_ROCKET_HIDEOUT_CLEARED end
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_43_GATE_GUARD
  scene "The toll on Route 43"
  emote r43r0201_rocketm exclaim
  emote r43r0201_rocketm_2 exclaim
  face r43r0201_rocketm player
  face r43r0201_rocketm_2 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Hold it right there, kid. This here's a toll gate. It's ¥1,000 to pass."
  if money >= 1000 goto Pays
  say "What's that? You haven't got that much? Fine. We'll take whatever you've got, then."
label Hundreds
  takemoney 100
  if result == 1 goto Hundreds
label Tens
  takemoney 10
  if result == 1 goto Tens
label Ones
  takemoney 1
  if result == 1 goto Ones
  say "Thank you kindly! Off you go."
  endscene
  end
label Pays
  takemoney 1000
  say "Thank you kindly! Pleasure doing business."
  endscene
  end


# R43R0201_000
script Rocket
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "You've paid. Now beat it before we change our minds about the price."
  end


# R43R0201_003
script Guard
  face r43r0201_counterm player
  speaker "Guard"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TM36_FROM_ROUTE_43_GUARD goto Given
  say "Those men locked me in the back room and charged everyone who came through. Thanks for running them off!"
  say "Here. They left this behind. I'd rather it went to someone who'll put it to good use."
  give "TM36"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM36_FROM_ROUTE_43_GUARD
label Given
  say "TM36 is Sludge Bomb. It's a strong Poison move, and it may poison the target too."
  end
