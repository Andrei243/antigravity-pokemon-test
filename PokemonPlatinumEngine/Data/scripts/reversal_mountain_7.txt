# Reversal Mountain's east tunnel (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black
# 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/reversal_mountain_7.s, read for the order alone). The count of the cave's
# errand is VAR_UNOVA_REVERSAL (reversal_mountain_4.txt has it whole).
#
# With Bianca along, our trigger at the foot of the way out to Undella Town (26, 25, at 1) has her say the town is just ahead and
# offer to go her own way; the player can't go on with her along without saying yes. Parted, she (0, hidden until she parts)
# stands further up the tunnel, wondering about the Magma Stone.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  call UnovaBiancaWaits
  end


script BiancaSaysGoodbye
  if var VAR_UNOVA_REVERSAL != 1 end
  if not partner end
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Oh! Undella Town is right through here! I'd like to look around a bit more in the cave, though. Shall we say goodbye for now?"
  call UnovaBiancaAsks
  if yes goto Gone
  walk player left 1
  end
label Gone
  fade out 0.4
  hide bianca_along
  fade in
  end


script BiancaAtTheExit
  face 0 player
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Reversal Mountain... I wonder... Could a Magma Stone be in there?"
  say "Have you heard of it? A Magma Stone was found in a volcano in Sinnoh, far away. It had something to do with Heatran!"
  end
