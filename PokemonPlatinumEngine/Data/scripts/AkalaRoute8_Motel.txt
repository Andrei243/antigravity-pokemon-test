# Route 8's motel (plan 23, the wave after the loose ends: a door Z5, Z6 or Z7 left shut, or a room of the islands' last
# places). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The rest of the islands: Hapu, the guardians' ruins, the
# last routes and the shut doors").


script Clerk
  speaker "Motel Clerk"
  ask "Need a nap? A room is 500 dollars, Pokémon included. Would you like to rest?"
  if no goto Later
  takemoney 500
  if result == 0 goto Poor
  fade out
  heal
  text "{player} and the Pokémon rested for a while."
  fade in
  say "Feeling fresh? Mind the grunts outside."
  end
label Poor
  say "A little short, are we? There's a Pokémon Center just down the road."
  end
label Later
  say "Come back if you change your mind."
  end
