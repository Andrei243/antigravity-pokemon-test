# Couriway Town (plan 23, Kalos's water; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's Gyms, its water and the Mamoswine
# ride"). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beat as it is publicly described: the fisherman by the falls gives the
# Super Rod.


script SuperRod
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD goto After
  say "Three rivers meet under the town and go over the falls together. The fishing's never dull."
  say "But the big ones sit at the bottom of the pool under the falls. You need a proper rod for those."
  say "I've worn out more rods than I can count. This one's still good, though. Take it."
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD
  say "That's a Super Rod. Whatever's down there, you'll meet it now."
  end
label After
  say "The falls carry everything down to Couriway sooner or later. Even the Pokémon."
  end
