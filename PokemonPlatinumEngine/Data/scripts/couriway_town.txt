# Couriway Town (plan 23, Kalos's water; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's Gyms, its water and the Mamoswine
# ride"). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beat as it is publicly described: the fisherman by the falls gives the
# Super Rod.


script SuperRod
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD goto After
  say "Three rivers meet under the town and go over the falls together. The fishing's never dull."
  say "But the big ones sit at the bottom of the pool under the falls. You need a proper rod for those."
  say "I've worn out more rods than I can count. This one's still good, though. Take it."
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD
  say "That's a Super Rod. Whatever's down there, you'll meet it now."
  end
label After
  say "The falls carry everything down to Couriway sooner or later. Even the Pokémon."
  end


# Volcanion on the falls pool's sandbank (plan 23, Kalos's last species; plan 29 · LS11's beat for Kalos's mythicals,
# docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's last species"). Volcanion, given by events alone in X and Y, keeps far from people
# in the mountains; ours stands in the steam at the foot of Couriway's falls once Kalos's Hall of Fame is entered (the
# overlay shows it by KalosHallOfFame), at level 70. Beaten or caught it is gone for good (FLAG_KALOS_VOLCANION_GONE);
# run from, it stays.
script Volcanion
  scene "Volcanion at the foot of the falls"
  music stop
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 655 377 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect geyser 655 377 2
  effect eruption 655 377 2
  camera shake 0.6
  cry "Volcanion"
  speaker none
  text "Steam bursts up off the pool! A Pokémon of fire and water stands in it and glares at {player}."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Volcanion" 70
  if result == 2 goto Stays
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_VOLCANION_GONE
  hide self
  music area
  end
label Stays
  music area
  text "Volcanion turns its back and lets the steam hide it again."
  end
