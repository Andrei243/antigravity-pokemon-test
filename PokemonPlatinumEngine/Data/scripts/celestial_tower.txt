# The Celestial Tower's summit (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/celestial_tower.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The great bell (read at 16, 10) may be rung by anyone. Black 2's bell is also the end of a wish of a couple of Humilau City
# (four people of the tower try to stop whoever goes to ring it), which is plan 22 · U8's, and its Mesprit, which hangs over
# the room until it is met, is plan 22 · U10's.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script RingTheBell
  text "It's the great bell of the Celestial Tower."
  ask "Will you ring the bell?"
  if no goto Left
  text "{player} rang the bell..."
  sound "lift_ding"
  text "The sound of the bell rolls out over the tower and the hills beyond, and slowly fades away."
  end
label Left
  text "The bell was left untouched..."
  end


# Mesprit over the summit (0; plan 22 · U10, part a): once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered it hangs here, Black 2's level 65
# (celestial_tower.s); caught or beaten it is gone for good (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_MESPRIT), run from, it is back the next time.
script Mesprit
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  face self player
  cry "Mesprit"
  speaker none
  text "Mesprit gave a soft cry!\nA warm feeling washes over {player}!"
  wildbattle "Mesprit" 65
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_MESPRIT
  hide self
  text "The bell hums faintly in the wind..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Mesprit drifted off into the sky..."
  end
