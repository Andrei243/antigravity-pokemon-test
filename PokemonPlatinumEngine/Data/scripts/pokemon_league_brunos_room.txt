# Bruno's room, the second of Kanto's Elite Four (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PokemonLeague_BrunosRoom/scripts.inc). As Lorelei's room
# (pokemon_league_loreleis_room.txt): spoken to, he battles; beaten, his door opens (FLAG_DEFEATED_BRUNO).


script Bruno
  face bruno player
  speaker "Bruno"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_BRUNO goto Beaten
  scene "Bruno"
  say "I am Bruno of the Elite Four! I have trained my body and my Pokémon together, through every kind of hardship."
  say "We will crush whatever stands before us with sheer strength. Hoo hah!"
  if flag FLAG_SYS_CAN_LINK_WITH_RS goto Stronger
  battle self as "kanto_elite_four_bruno"
  goto Won
label Stronger
  battle self as "kanto_elite_four_bruno_2"
label Won
  speaker "Bruno"
  say "Why? How could I lose?"
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_BRUNO
  sound "door_open"
  say "My job is done. Go face your next challenge."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "Go on! Show the next of the Elite Four what you showed me!"
  end
