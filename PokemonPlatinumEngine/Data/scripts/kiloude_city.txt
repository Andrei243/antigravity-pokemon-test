# Kiloude City (plan 23 · Z4, the loose ends). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described:
# after the Hall of Fame an old man called AZ waits at the landing across Kiloude's pier. Long ago he was a king whose
# war was ended by a weapon he made, and the weapon took the Floette he loved; he has waited three thousand years to
# be forgiven. He tells it all once, battles the player to see what the bond between a Trainer and a Pokémon is worth,
# and gives thanks. His team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_az_1. FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_AZ is clear; the overlay shows him
# from KalosHallOfFame (so no new game needs to hide him).


script Az
  face az player
  speaker "AZ"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_AZ_DONE goto Done
  scene "AZ"
  say "Hm? A young Trainer, out on the old landing. Not many walk this far."
  say "I am called AZ. I was a king, once. A very long time ago."
  say "My land was at war, and I could not stop it. So I built a machine that could. A weapon, the greatest ever made."
  say "It ended the war. It also took the life of a small Floette who had been my dearest friend."
  say "I could not bear it. I gave my own life to the machine to find her. I have waited here, in one form or another, ever since."
  say "They tell me you have walked all of Kalos and stood in the Hall of Fame. You have shown that Pokémon and people can be strong together."
  say "I would like to see it for myself. Not a weapon. A bond. Show me!"
  battle self as "kalos_az_1"
  say "Ah... yes. That is what I forgot. Power is not what the war was missing. It was this."
  say "Thank you, {player}. Perhaps the long wait has been worth it. Take these, with an old man's thanks."
  give "Rare Candy" 3
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_AZ_DONE
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "Some days I sit here and listen to the sea. It does not ask for anything. I am learning from it."
  end


# Plan 23 · Z4b: the neighbour on the city's northern hill once the TMV pass is had (FLAG_KALOS_TMV_PASS): a battle, the
# Absolite and the professor's message the first time won (FLAG_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE_BEATEN, VAR_KALOS_POSTGAME 2), and a
# battle once a day after (FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE). The teams are Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_rival_kiloude_*,
# named for the neighbour's own first Pokémon, the one strong against the player's.
script RivalKiloude
  face self player
  speaker "{rival}"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE_BEATEN goto Again
  scene "The neighbour in Kiloude"
  say "{player}! I came out on the first train. Kiloude's the end of the line, so I knew you'd turn up here sooner or later."
  say "Champion, huh? I'm not jealous. Much. But I haven't stopped chasing you. Battle me!"
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainstChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainstFennekin
  battle self as "kalos_rival_kiloude_chespin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstChespin
  battle self as "kalos_rival_kiloude_fennekin" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstFennekin
  battle self as "kalos_rival_kiloude_froakie" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto RivalWon
  say "Still the Champion. All right. I'll keep training up here, where the wind off the sea is strong."
  say "Take this. I found it on the beach, of all places. It's a Mega Stone, for an Absol. It'll do more good with you."
  give "Absolite"
  say "Oh, and the professor rang. He wants to meet you by the sundial in Anistar City. Something about your Key Stone."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE_BEATEN
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE
  if var VAR_KALOS_POSTGAME < 2 setvar VAR_KALOS_POSTGAME 2
  objective kalosSundial
  endscene
  end
label RivalWon
  say "I won? I won! Come back when you're ready. I'm not going anywhere."
  endscene
  end
label Again
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE goto Tomorrow
  ask "Another battle? I train up here every day."
  if no goto Later
  if starter "Chespin" goto AgainChespin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto AgainFennekin
  battle self as "kalos_rival_kiloude_chespin" canlose
  goto Tired
label AgainChespin
  battle self as "kalos_rival_kiloude_fennekin" canlose
  goto Tired
label AgainFennekin
  battle self as "kalos_rival_kiloude_froakie" canlose
label Tired
  heal
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RIVAL_KILOUDE
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Same time tomorrow?"
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you like. The sea's not going anywhere, and neither am I."
  end
label Tomorrow
  say "That's enough for one day. Come back tomorrow, and I'll be stronger!"
  end


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the Borrowed Masterpiece (plan 41 · EG8, provisional)
# The Open Door Agency's case in Kalos: the station's porter sees a long flat parcel on the last train in and the first
# train back, and a painter smells fresh varnish on her neighbour's canvases. Once Looker has the deductions the
# museum's restorer, Odile Ferrand, waits in Kiloude City's square (FLAG_CASE_KALOS_ODILE_WAITS).

script CaseKalosPorter
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_TAKEN goto Case
  say "I carry the bags off the train from Lumiose. Big ones, small ones, ones that bark."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLUE_TICKET goto Again
  say "A regular? There's one lady. Every night, the last train in, with a long flat parcel she won't let me carry."
  say "And every morning, the first train back to Lumiose, with the same parcel. Here's one of her old tickets."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLUE_TICKET
  text "{player} noted the ticket for the last train in and the first out."
  end
label Again
  say "Last train in, first train out, and a parcel as long as a door. She never misses."
  end
label Thanks
  say "The lady with the parcel comes by day now, and she lets me carry her easel. Progress!"
  end

script CaseKalosPainter
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_TAKEN goto Case
  say "Kiloude's light is the best in Kalos. I paint the sea every morning, and every morning it's different."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLUE_VARNISH goto Again
  say "Odd things at night? The house by the shore smells of fresh varnish every morning. Somebody paints there till dawn."
  say "Students, I think. I heard a teacher saying, look at the brushstrokes, see how the master did it."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLUE_VARNISH
  text "{player} noted the smell of fresh varnish at night."
  end
label Again
  say "Fresh varnish every morning. Whoever they are, they work hard."
  end
label Thanks
  say "There's a painting class in town now, by day. I've joined it! Never too old to learn."
  end

script CaseKalosOdile
  face self player
  speaker "Odile"
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CULPRIT end
  scene "OdileFaced"
  say "So Looker has found me out. Yes, I take the painting out of its frame each night. I bring it here, and I bring it back."
  say "My students can't learn from a postcard. They must see the real brushstrokes! Still, if you insist, we shall duel."
  battle self as "case_kalos_odile"
  say "...A masterful stroke. Very well. I'll ask the museum properly. Tell Looker I'll call at his agency."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CULPRIT
  clearflag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_ODILE_WAITS
  hide self
  endscene
  end
