# The Aspertia Gym (plan 22 · U5), behind the Trainers' School. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/aspertia_city_gym.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Cheren, its new Leader (the room's person 0), battles once both his pupils are beaten (1, the youngster Pedro; 2,
# the lass Serena); the Basic Badge is the region's first, `givebadge basic`, and he gives TM83 (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM83),
# which our data's table, Platinum's, fills with its own move. The guide by the door gives a bottle of Fresh Water
# once (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_FRESH_WATER). Black 2's TRAINER_LEADER_CHEREN is the Leader of the game's usual difficulty; his
# _2 is the Challenge Mode's, plan 13's. The Badge also lets the hiker on Route 20 battle (VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_20 1).


script Cheren
  face 0 player
  speaker "Cheren"
  if badge basic goto Won
  if not defeated "unova_youngster_pedro" goto Pupils
  if not defeated "unova_lass_serena" goto Pupils
  scene "Cheren"
  say "Welcome to the Aspertia Gym. I'm Cheren, its Leader. Well... I suppose I'm still a Leader in training."
  say "This is your first Gym challenge, and it's my very first battle as a Gym Leader."
  say "Let's both do our best!"
  battle self as "unova_leader_cheren"
  say "That battle made me really glad you were my first challenger."
  say "Here is your first Gym Badge, the Basic Badge. It's an important first step. Wear it with pride!"
  givebadge basic
  text "{player} received the Basic Badge from Cheren!"
  defeat "unova_youngster_pedro"
  defeat "unova_lass_serena"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_20 == 0 setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_20 1
label Tm
  say "Take this too. Use it however you think best."
  give "TM83"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM83
  say "There are eight Gyms in Unova, and eight Badges. Where you go from here is up to you, {player}."
  say "Don't worry about losing your way. You have your Pokémon beside you."
  endscene
  end
label Pupils
  say "We have a challenger! If you can beat my two pupils first, I'll be your opponent."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM83 goto Tm
  say "I hope you'll always remember the moment you received that Badge."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_FRESH_WATER goto Advice
  say "Hello! I'm Clyde, guide to every Trainer who takes on a Pokémon Gym. Thanks for taking this one on!"
  say "Here, have this to mark the occasion."
  give "Fresh Water"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_FRESH_WATER
label Advice
  if badge basic goto Won
  say "Gyms test what Trainers can do. Beat the Leader, and you'll have proved yourself a strong Trainer!"
  say "If every Pokémon you have faints, you lose. So it helps to bring a few along!"
  end
label Won
  say "I hope you'll always remember the moment you won that Badge!"
  end
