# A house in Sootopolis City (plan 21 · H8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/SootopolisCity_House1/scripts.inc). The black belt who
# made TM31 gives it once (FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_BRICK_BREAK).


script BlackBelt
  face self player
  speaker "Black Belt"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_BRICK_BREAK goto Given
  say "Thirty years I've spent in Sootopolis sharpening my skills. And I made a TM that shatters anything."
  say "I'm passing it on to you!"
  give "TM31"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_BRICK_BREAK
label Given
  say "TM31 is Brick Break! It smashes through walls of light. Words can't do it justice."
  end
