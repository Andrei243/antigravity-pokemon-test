# The rival's house in Pallet Town (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/PalletTown_RivalsHouse/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_PALLET_TOWN_RIVALS_HOUSE is 1 once Oak has given out the Pokédex: the rival's sister has heard of the
# errand and hands over the Town Map that lies on the table, whatever her brother said.


script Daisy
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TOWN_MAP goto Given
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_PALLET_TOWN_RIVALS_HOUSE >= 1 goto Errand
  sayown
  end
label Errand
  say "Hi, {player}! Grandpa asked you to help with his Pokédex? He asked my brother too."
  say "You'll be travelling all over Kanto, then. Here, this will help you find your way."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TOWN_MAP
  hide town_map
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TOWN_MAP
  give "Town Map"
  say "Don't tell my brother I gave you that. He wanted it all to himself."
  end
label Given
  say "Keep an eye on my little brother out there for me, would you? He's not as tough as he thinks."
  end


# The map on the table, until it is given.
script TownMap
  text "It's a big map of Kanto. {rival}'s sister keeps it on the table."
  end
