# The Masters' Hall (plan 14 · B15), off the Battle Frontier annex's lobby, and its titles. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the beats of Let's Go's Master Trainers: one of the team put forward, the master of
# its species steps up, one on one with the bag shut, and the title is the player's once the master is beaten.
#
# One hall calls the master of whichever species is put forward (decision 8): nobody stands in the imported world, and
# the master here carries no team on the map, so loading the room draws nothing. Both people are shown once the Hall of
# Fame is entered (SinnohHallOfFame). A title is a master beaten (StoryState's beaten trainers, master_<species>); the
# latest is the story's VAR_MASTER_TITLE_LATEST (Models/MasterTitles.cs), and the Trainer Card has a page of them.


script Receptionist
  face self player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "Welcome to the Masters' Hall! A master of every species waits here for a Trainer who has raised one of their own."
label Ask
  choose "Will you put one of your Pokémon forward?" "Yes" "The rules" "No"
  if result == 1 goto Rules
  if result == 2 goto Later
  say "Please choose the Pokémon you'll put forward."
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Later
  if titled goto Again
  say "{chosen}! The {master} will see you now. Please step onto the mark."
  goto Step
label Again
  say "You already hold the title of {master}. The master will gladly battle you again, but the title is yours already."
label Step
  fade out 0.4
  place player 5 8 up
  fade in
  walk master down 3
  speaker "Master"
  say "So this is your {chosen}. Every one of its kind I've met has taught me something. Let's see what yours knows."
  master master canlose
  if won goto Won
  say "Not this time. Raise {chosen} a little more, and the two of you come back to me."
  goto Home
label Won
  say "Well battled. {chosen} has a Trainer who understands it, and that is what a master looks for."
  text "{player} holds the title of {master}! Titles held: {number}."
label Home
  walk master up 3
  face master down
  end
label Later
  say "Please come again. The masters are always here."
  end
label Rules
  say "Choose one of your Pokémon, and the master of its species battles it, one on one. Nothing may be used from the Bag."
  say "Your own Pokémon battles, so what it learns here stays with it. There's no prize money, only the title."
  say "Win, and the title of that species' master is yours. Lose, and you may try again whenever you like."
  goto Ask


# The master, spoken to before anyone is put forward
script Master
  face self player
  say "The receptionist will ask which of your Pokémon you'll put forward. I am the master of whichever it is."
  end
