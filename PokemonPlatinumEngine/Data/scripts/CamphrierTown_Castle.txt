# Camphrier Town's castle (plan 23 · Z2, second part). The lines are our own: its keeper, Aubert, at home once he has
# met the player in the street (camphrier_town.txt; he is shown here by FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_CASTELLAN_STREET).


#
# The first time the player comes in once he is home (plan 23, after Kalos finished; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Parfum
# Palace's maze and fireworks, and the castle's keeper at home"), he comes down the hall to meet them and shows them
# the castle: its armour and its window over the town (FLAG_KALOS_CASTLE_TOUR). Once the Snorlax is woken he gives
# the Leftovers it was found clutching once (FLAG_KALOS_CASTLE_GIFT), a gift of ours.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_CASTELLAN_STREET goto Nobody
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_CASTLE_TOUR goto Nobody
  if var PLAYER_Y != 10 goto Nobody
  scene "The castle's keeper at home"
  letterbox on
  speaker "Castellan Aubert"
  say "Ah, my young friend! Come in, come in. Welcome to the castle of Camphrier."
  walk castellan down 5
  face castellan player
  say "It has stood here for some three hundred years. The town grew up round its walls, not the other way about."
  camera pan 4 6
  say "These suits of armour were made for the knights who rode out from here with their Pokémon at their sides."
  camera pan 7 2
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 16 goto TourWoken
  say "And from that window my forebears watched the road south. Today it watches a sleeping Snorlax, I am sorry to say."
  goto TourEnd
label TourWoken
  say "And from that window my forebears watched the road south. Thanks to you, it watches carts rolling again."
label TourEnd
  camera release
  say "Well, I mustn't keep you. Do make yourself at home whenever you pass through."
  walk castellan up 5
  face castellan down
  letterbox off
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_CASTLE_TOUR
  endscene
label Nobody
  end


script Castellan
  face castellan player
  speaker "Castellan Aubert"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 16 goto Woken
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 15 goto Flute
  say "The palace is at the head of the avenue north of town. Its butler keeps the gate. Mention my name!"
  end
label Flute
  say "You have the palace's flute! Then go and play it to our sleeper on Route 7. The whole town will thank you."
  end
label Woken
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_CASTLE_GIFT goto Thanked
  say "The road south is open again, and the carts are rolling to the coast. Camphrier owes you a great deal."
  say "The road menders found this where the Snorlax had lain. It must have been clutching it in its sleep. Take it, please."
  give "Leftovers"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_CASTLE_GIFT
  end
label Thanked
  say "The road south is open again, and the carts are rolling to the coast. Camphrier owes you a great deal."
  end
