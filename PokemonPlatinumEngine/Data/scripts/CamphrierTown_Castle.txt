# Camphrier Town's castle (plan 23 · Z2, second part). The lines are our own: its keeper, Aubert, at home once he has
# met the player in the street (camphrier_town.txt; he is shown here by FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_CASTELLAN_STREET).


script Castellan
  face castellan player
  speaker "Castellan Aubert"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 16 goto Woken
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 15 goto Flute
  say "The palace is at the head of the avenue north of town. Its butler keeps the gate. Mention my name!"
  end
label Flute
  say "You have the palace's flute! Then go and play it to our sleeper on Route 7. The whole town will thank you."
  end
label Woken
  say "The road south is open again, and the carts are rolling to the coast. Camphrier owes you a great deal."
  end
