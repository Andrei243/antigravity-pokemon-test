# Lorelei's house on Four Island (plan 19 · K9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/FourIsland_LoreleisHouse/scripts.inc).
#
# Lorelei is at home from the poachers' scene in Icefall Cave (four_island_icefall_cave_back.txt clears FLAG_HIDE_
# LORELEI_IN_HER_HOUSE) until Celio takes the Sapphire (one_island_pokemon_center_1f.txt sets it again: she goes back
# to the League). Before the scene she isn't home. What she says follows Team Rocket's warehouse (FLAG_DEFEATED_
# ROCKETS_IN_WAREHOUSE) and whether she has said it (FLAG_TALKED_TO_LORELEI_AFTER_WAREHOUSE). Her dolls are all on
# show: FireRed's draw of which stand where is left out.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ICEFALL_CAVE_BACK == 0 setflag FLAG_HIDE_LORELEI_IN_HER_HOUSE
  end


script Lorelei
  face lorelei player
  speaker "Lorelei"
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_LORELEI_AFTER_WAREHOUSE goto Later
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_ROCKETS_IN_WAREHOUSE goto Freed
  say "This island is where I was born. If anything happened to it, or to the Pokémon that live here, I'd never forgive myself."
  end
label Freed
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_LORELEI_AFTER_WAREHOUSE
  say "I heard you set free the Pokémon in Team Rocket's warehouse. Thank you, {player}. The island owes you a great deal."
  say "Before long I'll go back to the Pokémon League. When you come to challenge it, I'll be waiting, colder than ever."
  end
label Later
  say "For now I'll do what I can for the island. The League can spare me a little while longer."
  end


script Doll
  speaker none
  text "A Pokémon doll, plump and soft. There are dolls everywhere in this house!"
  end
