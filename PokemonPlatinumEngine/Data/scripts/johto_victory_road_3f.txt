# Victory Road's third floor (plan 20 · J12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D43R0103).
#
# The red-haired boy's last challenge: the first time the player comes up the corridor to the Indigo Plateau (the
# area's first trigger, on VAR_UNK_40C5 0), he comes up behind them and battles with the team that beats the
# player's starter, then goes on ahead. HeartGold keeps him off the floor by FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_RIVAL, which its
# new game sets; here the floor's arrival sets it, so a save of Johto from before this session needs no story version,
# and the scene brings him in where the player stands.


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_RIVAL
  end


# HeartGold's D43R0103_000: he catches up two tiles behind the player
script Rival
  scene "The rival in Victory Road"
  music "johto/rival"
  if var PLAYER_X == 37 place d43r0103_gsrivel 37 24 up
  if var PLAYER_X == 38 place d43r0103_gsrivel 38 24 up
  if var PLAYER_X == 39 place d43r0103_gsrivel 39 24 up
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_RIVAL
  show d43r0103_gsrivel
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hold it."
  face player down
  walk d43r0103_gsrivel up 2
  say "So you're going to take on the Pokémon League. Not before you've dealt with me."
  say "I've been thinking about what that dragon tamer said. I'm not saying he was right. But I'm not the same as I was."
  say "{player}! This time, I win!"
  if starter "Chikorita" goto VsTyphlosion
  if starter "Cyndaquil" goto VsFeraligatr
  battle d43r0103_gsrivel as "johto_rival_silver_5"
  goto Won
label VsTyphlosion
  battle d43r0103_gsrivel as "johto_rival_silver_9"
  goto Won
label VsFeraligatr
  battle d43r0103_gsrivel as "johto_rival_silver_13"
label Won
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...I lost. Again. But it doesn't feel the way it used to."
  say "My Pokémon fought for me today. Not because I told them to. I couldn't have done that before."
  say "Go on, then. Go and take the League. I'm going to train somewhere nobody bothers me, and when I come back I'll be stronger than all of you."
  walk d43r0103_gsrivel down 3 fast
  hide d43r0103_gsrivel
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_RIVAL
  setvar VAR_UNK_40C5 1
  music area
  endscene
  end


# Spoken to in the moment between his battle and his going (nobody stands here otherwise)
script Gone
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "What are you waiting for? The League's that way."
  end
