# Postwick, Galar's first place (plan 33 · GL1), on the open world's engine: its places are
# Data/world3d/galar/places/postwick.json, and a scene here names markers, never tiles. The lines are our own, on Sword
# and Shield's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Galar's first chapter").
#
# VAR_GALAR_STORY, the first chapter's count, kept here for every place of it:
#   0 a new game, in the bedroom          6 Mum's Poké Balls; Hop waits in Wedgehurst
#   1 Mum's word on the first morning      7 Hop's second battle won or lost
#   2 Hop at the door, off to the station  8 the Pokédex at the professor's house
#   3 Leon home, the three, Hop's battle   9 Hop's third battle in the garden; night falls
#   4 into the Weald after Hop's Wooloo   10 the Wishing Stars, the Dynamax Band and Leon's endorsement
#   5 the shadow in the fog; home again   11 aboard the train at Wedgehurst Station
# VAR_GALAR_VERSION is the version the game is (plan 33 · decision 2): 1 Sword, 2 Shield, asked by Mum on the first
# morning (Postwick_PlayersHouse_1F.txt).


# Out of the door on the first morning: Hop, off to meet his brother's train
script HopAtTheDoor
  scene "Hop at the door"
  faceto player hop_postwick
  speaker "Hop"
  say "There you are! You're never going to believe it, Leon's coming home today, on the train to Wedgehurst!"
  say "The Champion of all Galar, back in Postwick. Come on, we have to be at the station when it pulls in!"
  say "It's straight up Route 1. Stick to the path: we've got no Pokémon of our own yet, and the grass is full of them."
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_POSTWICK
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_STATION
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_LEON_STATION
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 2
  objective galarStation
  endscene
  end


# The gap in the hedge into the Slumbering Weald: latched until Hop's Wooloo slips through it, after the first battle
script WealdGate
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 3 goto Wooloo
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY < 3 goto Latched
  end
label Latched
  text "The gate into the Slumbering Weald is latched. Nobody in Postwick goes in there, and nobody says why."
  walkto player weald_back
  end
label Wooloo
  scene "Into the Weald"
  faceto player hop_gate
  speaker "Hop"
  say "{player}! My Wooloo squeezed through the gate and ran off into the Weald!"
  say "Everyone says to keep out of there. But it's my Wooloo, I can't just leave it. Come on, we'll find it together!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_GATE
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_WEALD
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 4
  objective galarWooloo
  endscene
  end
