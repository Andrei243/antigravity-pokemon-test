# The Pokémon Center at the foot of Mt. Moon on Route 4 (plan 19 · K4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route4_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc).


# The man who sells a Magikarp for ¥500, once (FLAG_BOUGHT_MAGIKARP).
script MagikarpSalesman
  face balding_man player
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_BOUGHT_MAGIKARP goto Bought
  say "Psst! Got a minute? I've got a very special Pokémon here, a real secret."
  ask "Just ¥500 and this Magikarp is yours. How about it?"
  if no goto Declined
  if not money >= 500 goto Poor
  takemoney 500
  givepokemon "Magikarp" 5
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} paid ¥500 and received a Magikarp!"
  setflag FLAG_BOUGHT_MAGIKARP
  say "Pleasure doing business with you. No refunds, mind!"
  end
label Declined
  say "No? Your loss. Pokémon like this don't come along every day."
  end
label Poor
  say "You don't have ¥500? Come back when you do, then."
  end
label Bought
  say "No refunds! I told you. A deal's a deal."
  end
