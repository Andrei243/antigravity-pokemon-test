# Ballonlea Stadium's pitch (plan 33 · GL5, its first session): Opal's battle for the Fairy Badge, the gym challenge's
# fifth (`givebadge fairy`). She Dynamaxes her last Pokémon. VAR_GALAR_STORY 29 to 30 (postwick.txt's count). Our own
# lines.


script Opal
  face opal player
  speaker "Opal"
  if badge fairy goto Won
  scene "Opal"
  say "Ah, a challenger who answers questions properly. How refreshing. I'm Opal, and this pink little gym is mine."
  say "I've been Leader here a very long time, dear. Let's see if you have the pink in you that my successor will need!"
  battle opal as "galar_opal_1"
  say "Hmm. Not bad. Not bad at all. You've plenty of pink in you, though you don't wear it."
  say "The Fairy Badge. Take it, and mind you look after it."
  givebadge fairy
  text "{player} received the Fairy Badge from Opal!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_BALLONLEA_GYM
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_posy"
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_luned"
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 30
  say "Five badges. The road on runs back through Hammerlocke and west. Off you go, dear. I've a successor to find."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Pink is the colour of a life well lived, dear. Remember that."
  end
