# Route 17, the Mamoswine Road (plan 23, Kalos's Mamoswine ride; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's Gyms, its water
# and the Mamoswine ride"). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beat as it is publicly described: the snow is too deep to
# walk, and the Mamoswine kept at the road let a Trainer ride them through the drifts. Four stand on the road (at each
# end and in each clearing), and any of them carries the player (`ride "Mamoswine"`, a ride the route lends:
# RidePager.LentRides); spoken to from its back, one lets the player down again. The ride ends by itself as the player
# leaves the route (GameEngine.DismountWhereNoRiding).


script Mamoswine
  speaker none
  cry own
  if var RIDING == 1 goto Off
  text "A Mamoswine stands in the snow, a saddle on its broad back. It snorts, ready to plough through the drifts."
  ask "Will you ride the Mamoswine?"
  if no goto Stay
  ride "Mamoswine"
  if var RESULT == 0 goto Refused
  text "{player} climbed onto the Mamoswine's back!"
  end
label Refused
  text "The Mamoswine shook its head. It won't leave the snow of its own road."
  end
label Stay
  text "The Mamoswine went back to snuffling in the snow."
  end
label Off
  ask "Get down from the Mamoswine's back?"
  if no end
  ride off
  text "{player} slid down into the snow, and the Mamoswine trundled back to its herd."
  end
