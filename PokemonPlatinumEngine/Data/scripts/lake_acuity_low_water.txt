# Lake Acuity as it is before Team Galactic comes (plan 02 · S11, part a): the map Acuity Lakefront leads to until
# Commander Mars is beaten at Lake Verity. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_lake_acuity_low_water.s).
#
# The rival, sent here from Canalave City's library to watch the lake, has nothing to report, and says so; spoken to
# again he sends the player off to the Gym they haven't beaten. Whoever stands here has climbed the lakefront's rock
# face, so has Snowpoint's Badge: Sunyshore's is the one left.


# The rival (the original's script 1).
script Rival
  face self player
  speaker "{rival}"
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_LAKE_ACUITY_LOW_WATER_RIVAL goto Again
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_LAKE_ACUITY_LOW_WATER_RIVAL
  say "{player}? What are you doing here? This lake's mine to watch, remember? You've got your own."
  say "There's nothing going on, anyway. And if anything does, I've got it covered. I've got my Pokémon with me, and you know how tough I am!"
  end
label Again
  say "What did I tell you? I'm fine here by myself!"
  say "If you haven't been yet, go and take on Sunyshore's Gym. Things are only going to get rougher from here, and you'll want every Badge you can get."
  end
