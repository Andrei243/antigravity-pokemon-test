# Professor Juniper's lab in Nuvema Town (plan 22 · U10, the last part). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own. Once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered she is back at her desk, glad of the Champion's visit; what she says
# follows what the game knows: whether the Pokédex is the National one, and whether her father's Cave of Being has been seen.


script Juniper
  face self player
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  # (plan 40 · GE11: the stones of Unova's own Megas of ours, once the bag holds a Key Stone)
  call OurMegaStones
  say "{player}! The new Champion, in my own lab! Come in, come in."
  if nationaldex goto National
  say "Keep filling in your Pokédex. Every page tells me a little more about where Pokémon came from."
  goto Cave
label National
  say "And the National Pokédex too! You're seeing Pokémon from regions I've only read about."
label Cave
  if var VAR_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING == 1 goto Seen
  say "My father has gone off to some cave on Route 20 again. He says something sleeps there. He always says that!"
  end
label Seen
  say "Dad says you saw three Pokémon fly out of his cave. Three! He hasn't stopped talking about it."
  end


# The stones of the Megas of our own whose line is Unova's (plan 40 · GE11; docs/plans/decisions/R20b-mega-proposals.md,
# "Where the stones are found", approved 2026-10-26): once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered (she is at her desk only then)
# and the bag holds a Key Stone, the professor hands over the Serperiorite and the Samurottite, once, and the talk ends
# there. (Emboar's stone is Legends: Z-A's, plan 23's to place.)
script OurMegaStones
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame return
  if not item "Key Stone" return
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_RECEIVED_JUNIPERS_MEGA_STONES return
  say "{player}! Is that a Key Stone you're wearing? Then I've been keeping these for exactly the right Trainer."
  say "They were dug out of the old ruins in Unova's south. Each one hums when a Pokémon of its own line comes near."
  give "Serperiorite"
  give "Samurottite"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_RECEIVED_JUNIPERS_MEGA_STONES
  say "One for Serperior and one for Samurott. Let one hold its stone in battle and watch it change. I can't wait!"
  end
