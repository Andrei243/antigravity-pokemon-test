# Professor Juniper's lab in Nuvema Town (plan 22 · U10, the last part). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own. Once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered she is back at her desk, glad of the Champion's visit; what she says
# follows what the game knows: whether the Pokédex is the National one, and whether her father's Cave of Being has been seen.


script Juniper
  face self player
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  say "{player}! The new Champion, in my own lab! Come in, come in."
  if nationaldex goto National
  say "Keep filling in your Pokédex. Every page tells me a little more about where Pokémon came from."
  goto Cave
label National
  say "And the National Pokédex too! You're seeing Pokémon from regions I've only read about."
label Cave
  if var VAR_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING == 1 goto Seen
  say "My father has gone off to some cave on Route 20 again. He says something sleeps there. He always says that!"
  end
label Seen
  say "Dad says you saw three Pokémon fly out of his cave. Three! He hasn't stopped talking about it."
  end
