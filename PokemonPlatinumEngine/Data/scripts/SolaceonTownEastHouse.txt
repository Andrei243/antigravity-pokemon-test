# Solaceon Town's east house (plan 06 · R17b), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_solaceon_town_east_house.s).
#
# The mother gives the Seal Case with five Seals in it (one Heart Seal, two Star Seals, a Foamy Seal and a Song Seal,
# each a draw) and says how Seals are used. Her boy, once the player has the case, gives ten Seals for the team's first
# Unown, the letter of its own shape; not for the shape he was shown last this visit, nor once the player has
# ninety-nine of the kind.


script PokefanF
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SOLACEON_TOWN_EAST_HOUSE_SEAL_CASE goto Explain
  say "Oh, a Trainer! You don't have a Seal Case, do you? I can tell by looking. Here, take this one. I've more than I'll ever use."
  give "Seal Case"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_SOLACEON_TOWN_EAST_HOUSE_SEAL_CASE
  say "And a case with nothing in it is a sad thing, so here are a few Seals to start you off."
  seal first
  fanfare item
  text "{player} put the Seals away in the Seal Case."
label Explain
  say "Seals go on a Ball Capsule, and a Ball Capsule goes on a Poké Ball. When the Pokémon comes out in battle, out they all burst!"
  say "You stick them on at a PC. Where you put each Seal changes how it goes off, so try them out."
  end


script NinjaBoy
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SOLACEON_TOWN_EAST_HOUSE_SEAL_CASE goto NoCase
  seal unown
  if result == 1 goto Given
  say "Show me an Unown and I'll give you Seals that look just like it! A different shape each time, mind."
  end
label Given
  say "Whoa! That {wearer} looks just like my Seals! Here, these are the ones. Take them!"
  fanfare item
  text "{player} received {seals} × {seal}!" "{player} put them away in the Seal Case."
  end
label NoCase
  say "I've got loads of Seals with letters on them. But you've nothing to keep them in... Go and talk to my mom!"
  end
