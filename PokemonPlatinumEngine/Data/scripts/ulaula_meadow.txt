# Ula'ula Meadow (plan 23, the wave after the loose ends). The lines are our own. The dancer explains the meadow's red
# nectar and hands one over, once (FLAG_ALOLA_MEADOW_NECTAR): an Oricorio that sips it takes the Baile Style.


script Dancer
  speaker "Dancer"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEADOW_NECTAR goto Done
  say "The Oricorio here dance the fiery dance. They get it from the red flowers. One sip of their nectar and whoosh!"
  say "Here, I gathered some this morning. Give it to an Oricorio from any other island and watch it change its tune."
  give "Red Nectar"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEADOW_NECTAR
  end
label Done
  say "Every island's flowers teach the Oricorio a different dance. This one is my favourite. Obviously."
  end
