# The Azalea Gym (plan 20 · J7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats
# (scr_seq_T23GYM0102).
#
# Bugsy battles, gives the Hive Badge (`givebadge hive`) and TM89, U-turn (FLAG_GOT_TM89_FROM_BUGSY), and his four
# trainers count beaten, as the original's script sets their trainer flags. The twelve docks of the Spinarak carts
# (the room's triggers) each ride the player across the web to the dock facing it (CartRide): HeartGold's carts run
# on tracks its two levers turn, which are left out here (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Johto's second chapter").


script Bugsy
  face t23gym0102_gsleader2 player
  speaker "Bugsy"
  if badge hive goto Won
  scene "Bugsy"
  say "I'm Bugsy! I never lose when it comes to Bug Pokémon. I've studied every one of them, inside and out."
  say "Let me show you what I've learned!"
  battle self as "johto_leader_bugsy_bugsy"
  say "Whoa, amazing! You really know your stuff. Okay, you win. This is yours: the Hive Badge!"
  givebadge hive
  text "{player} received the Hive Badge from Bugsy!"
  defeat "johto_bug_catcher_al"
  defeat "johto_bug_catcher_benny"
  defeat "johto_bug_catcher_josh"
  defeat "johto_twins_amy_and_mimi"
  say "With the Hive Badge, Pokémon up to level thirty will obey you, and you can use Cut outside of battle."
  endscene
label Tm
  say "Here, take this as well. It's a move my Bug Pokémon love."
  give "TM89"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM89_FROM_BUGSY
  say "TM89 is U-turn. Your Pokémon hits the foe, then swaps out for another. Hit and run, just like a bug!"
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_TM89_FROM_BUGSY goto Tm
  say "Bug Pokémon are so deep. There's always something new to learn about them. I'm going to keep studying!"
  end
