# Four of the road's trainers, beaten, swap numbers for the PokéNav's Match Call (Emerald's RegisterMatchCallAfterBattle;
# their rematches wait for Emerald's table of rematches, which the reader doesn't read). The Day-Care Man is common.DayCareMan.

# Isaac, beaten, swaps numbers for the PokéNav
script Isaac
  if not defeated self goto Fight
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_HOENN_ISAAC goto Known
label Ask
  speaker "Isaac"
  say "You raise your Pokémon well. Let's trade numbers and talk about raising them!"
  ask "Register Isaac in the PokéNav's Match Call?"
  if no goto Refused
  addcontact hoenn_isaac
  say "Wonderful. I'll call with tips from the Day Care."
  end
label Refused
  say "I see. Perhaps another time, then."
  end
label Known
  trainerline after
  end
label Fight
  trainerline before
  battle self
  goto Ask
