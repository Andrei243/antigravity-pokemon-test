# Team Aqua's hideout, its lower basement (plan 21 · H7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/AquaHideout_B2F/scripts.inc).
#
# Matt, Team Aqua's admin, waits by the submarine's pen. The first step onto one of the area's two triggers beside the
# pen makes him turn (VAR_TEMP_1, which every arrival sets back to 0 as Emerald's temporary variables are, and to 1
# once the submarine has gone). Spoken to, he battles as Team Aqua's admin; beaten, he laughs that he only had to keep
# the player busy: Archie has sailed off in Captain Stern's submarine to a cave under the sea. That is
# FLAG_TEAM_AQUA_ESCAPED_IN_SUBMARINE, the end of the eighth stretch: Team Aqua's grunts leave Lilycove City
# (FLAG_HIDE_LILYCOVE_CITY_AQUA_GRUNTS) and their Wailmer the channel east of it, so the sea to Route 124 is open.


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 0
  if flag FLAG_TEAM_AQUA_ESCAPED_IN_SUBMARINE setvar VAR_TEMP_1 1
  end


script MattNotices
  if var VAR_TEMP_1 != 0 end
  emote aqua_hideout_matt exclaim
  face aqua_hideout_matt player
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 1
  end


script Matt
  face self player
  speaker "Matt"
  if defeated "hoenn_matt" goto Beaten
  scene "Team Aqua's submarine sails"
  music "hoenn/eye_galactic"
  say "Hehehe... Here already, are you? We didn't think you'd get this far!"
  say "But this is where it ends. I'm no ordinary grunt. I'm not stalling you, either. I'm going to flatten you!"
  battle self as "hoenn_matt"
  speaker "Matt"
  say "Hehehe... So I lost too."
  face self left
  wait 0.4
  face self player
  say "Hehehe! But while you were playing with me, the boss finished getting ready!"
  face self left
  speaker none
  text "With a churn of water, the submarine slid out of its pen and was gone under the waves!"
  sound "dive"
  wait 0.6
  face self player
  setflag FLAG_TEAM_AQUA_ESCAPED_IN_SUBMARINE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LILYCOVE_CITY_AQUA_GRUNTS
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_AQUA_HIDEOUT_B2F_SUBMARINE_SHADOW
  call Gone
  music area
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  call Gone
  end


script Gone
  speaker "Matt"
  say "Hehehe! The boss is already on his way to a certain cave under the sea!"
  say "If you want to chase him, you'd better search the wide sea beyond Lilycove. Not that you'll ever find it! Hehehe!"
  return
