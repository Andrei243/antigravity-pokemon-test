# The Fishing Guru's house in Vermilion City (plan 19 · K4, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/VermilionCity_House1/scripts.inc): an Old Rod for a trainer who likes to
# fish (FLAG_GOT_OLD_ROD).


script FishingGuru
  speaker "Fishing Guru"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_OLD_ROD goto Biting
  ask "I'm the Fishing Guru! I simply love fishing. Do you like to fish?"
  if yes goto Give
  say "Oh... That's a shame. Fishing is such a peaceful way to pass the time."
  end
label Give
  say "Grand! Then take this, and give it a try."
  give "Old Rod"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_OLD_ROD
  say "Cast it wherever there's water. You never know what might bite! Fishing is a way of life, you know."
  end
label Biting
  say "Hello there! How are the fish biting?"
  end
