# The Battle Frontier island's Exchange Service Corner (plan 21 · H12, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/BattleFrontier_ExchangeServiceCorner/scripts.inc).
#
# The Frontier's Battle Points are one set (plan 06 · R18), so the island's counters trade the same prizes as the booths
# on Sinnoh's grounds (battle_frontier.txt): things to hold and vitamins at one, Technical Machines at the other. The two
# counters of Emerald's dolls and cushions have nothing to offer yet.


script ItemClerk
  face self player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome! This counter trades Battle Points for items your Pokémon can hold, and more."
  frontier prize items
  call ExchangeTrade
  end

script MachineClerk
  face self player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome! This counter trades Battle Points for Technical Machines."
  frontier prize machines
  call ExchangeTrade
  end

script DecorClerk
  face self player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "This counter will trade Battle Points for dolls and cushions for a secret base. Our stock hasn't arrived yet, I'm afraid."
  end


# What follows a counter's question: the prize chosen is traded for, if the points are there.
script ExchangeTrade
  if result == 0 goto Bye
  frontier buy
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  text "{player} traded {price} BP for the {item}."
  say "Thank you! Please come again."
  end
label Short
  say "I'm sorry. You don't have the Battle Points for that."
  end
label Bye
  say "Please come again."
  end
