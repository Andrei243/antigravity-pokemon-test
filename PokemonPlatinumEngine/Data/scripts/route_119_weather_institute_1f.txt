# The Weather Institute's ground floor on Route 119 (plan 21 · H6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route119_WeatherInstitute_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# While Team Aqua holds the institute (VAR_WEATHER_INSTITUTE_STATE 0) the little boy hides in the corner by the beds and
# two grunts keep the floor; the workers are upstairs, cornered. Once they are back down (state 2, route_119.txt) they
# talk about the weather. The beds give anyone a rest.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_WEATHER_INSTITUTE_STATE != 0 end
  place weather_institute_little_boy 0 5 right
  end


script LittleBoy
  face self player
  speaker "Boy"
  if var VAR_WEATHER_INSTITUTE_STATE != 0 goto Safe
  say "Some scary people in blue barged in and went upstairs. My dad's up there! Please, help him!"
  end
label Safe
  say "Dad says the people in blue wanted something from upstairs. You chased them off, didn't you? Thank you!"
  end


script InstituteWorker1
  face self player
  speaker "Worker"
  say "We watch the weather all over Hoenn from here. Lately there have been odd readings: sudden rain, sudden sun."
  say "Something out at sea, perhaps. It's as though the weather has a mind of its own."
  end


script InstituteWorker2
  face self player
  speaker "Worker"
  say "Thank you for chasing Team Aqua off. They took our data on rain and storms, but at least they're gone."
  end


# A bed by the wall: a rest for the whole team
script Bed
  speaker none
  text "There's a bed here... A little rest wouldn't hurt."
  fade out
  heal
  fanfare heal
  fade in
  text "{player}'s Pokémon are fully rested."
  end
