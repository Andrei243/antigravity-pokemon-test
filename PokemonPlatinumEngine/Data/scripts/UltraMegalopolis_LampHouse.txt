# The lamp keeper's house, Ultra Megalopolis (plan 23, Alola finished: a door Z8 left shut, a room of ours). The lines
# are our own. Once the light is back (FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED, MegaloTower.txt) the lamp keeper's wife gives the
# player a Star Piece once, a shard of the old light her husband kept (FLAG_ALOLA_MEGALOPOLIS_STAR_PIECE).


script Wife
  face self player
  speaker "Lamp Keeper's Wife"
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED goto Dark
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGALOPOLIS_STAR_PIECE goto Thanks
  say "You're the one who brought the light back! My husband has kept a shard of the old light in a jar for forty years."
  say "He says it belongs to you now. He's too proud to give it to you himself, so here."
  give "Star Piece"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGALOPOLIS_STAR_PIECE
  end
label Thanks
  say "My husband still lights the lamps every evening. Old habits. I don't have the heart to tell him to stop."
  end
label Dark
  say "My husband keeps the city's lamps. Without them we'd never find our own front doors."
  end
