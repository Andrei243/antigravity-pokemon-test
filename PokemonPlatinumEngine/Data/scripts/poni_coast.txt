# Poni Coast, Poni Island (plan 23, the wave after Ultra Space). The lines are our own. A fisherman on the beach below
# Poni Meadow hands over a Super Rod, once (FLAG_ALOLA_GOT_SUPER_ROD): Alola's waters are fished with it, Poni's deepest
# above all (rulings, "Poni's last places..."). The islet out at sea is shut by a rock only Sharpedo rams (common.SeaRock).


script Fisher
  face fisher player
  speaker "Fisherman"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_GOT_SUPER_ROD goto Done
  say "The fish of Poni are too strong for any old rod. They'd snap it like a twig!"
  say "You look like someone who could use a proper one. Here, take my spare, and mind you cast it somewhere deep."
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_GOT_SUPER_ROD
  end
label Done
  say "Ponds, rivers, the open sea: every water has its own fish. Try the meadow's pond, there's a dragon in it, they say."
  end
