# Petalburg Woods (plan 21 · H2, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/PetalburgWoods/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_PETALBURG_WOODS_STATE 0: a researcher of Devon is looking for Shroomish on the path north, and the first step
# past him (the area's triggers 0 and 1) brings on a grunt of Team Aqua after the papers he carries. Beaten, the grunt
# runs; the researcher gives a Great Ball and hurries on to Rustboro City. 1: the path is quiet.


script DevonResearcher
  scene "The Devon researcher in the woods"
  speaker "Researcher"
  say "Hmm... Not a single Shroomish anywhere. They're supposed to love these damp woods."
  face petalburg_woods_devon_employee player
  emote petalburg_woods_devon_employee exclaim
  walk petalburg_woods_devon_employee down 2
  say "Oh! Hello there. Have you seen a Shroomish around here? I study them, you see."
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_PETALBURG_WOODS_AQUA_GRUNT
  show petalburg_woods_grunt
  music "hoenn/eye_galactic"
  speaker "Team Aqua Grunt"
  say "Hold it right there, Devon man! I've been waiting all morning for you to wander off on your own."
  walk petalburg_woods_grunt down 2
  say "Hand over those papers you're carrying. Team Aqua has a use for them."
  speaker "Researcher"
  say "Eek! You there, trainer! Please, you have to help me! I'm no good at battling!"
  walk petalburg_woods_devon_employee left 1
  face petalburg_woods_devon_employee right
  speaker "Team Aqua Grunt"
  say "A kid playing hero? Get in Team Aqua's way and you'll be sorry!"
  battle petalburg_woods_grunt as "hoenn_grunt_petalburg_woods"
  say "Tch! Beaten by a kid. Fine, there are other ways into Rustboro. Remember this face!"
  walk petalburg_woods_grunt up 4 fast
  hide petalburg_woods_grunt
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PETALBURG_WOODS_AQUA_GRUNT
  music area
  face petalburg_woods_devon_employee player
  speaker "Researcher"
  say "Oh, my heart is still racing... Thank you! Please, take this as my thanks."
  give "Great Ball"
  say "That grunt said something about Rustboro. Team Aqua must be after something at Devon!"
  say "I must hurry back and warn everyone. Goodbye, and thank you again!"
  walk petalburg_woods_devon_employee up 6 fast
  hide petalburg_woods_devon_employee
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PETALBURG_WOODS_DEVON_EMPLOYEE
  setvar VAR_PETALBURG_WOODS_STATE 1
  endscene
  end


# The girl who gives a Miracle Seed (Emerald's PetalburgWoods_EventScript_Girl)
script Girl
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_MIRACLE_SEED goto Explain
  say "You've come all the way through the woods? Your Pokémon must be tired. Here, this might help!"
  give "Miracle Seed"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_MIRACLE_SEED
  end
label Explain
  say "A Pokémon that holds a Miracle Seed puts more power into its Grass moves. Isn't that something?"
  end
