# Shoal Cave's inner room (plan 21 · H8, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/ShoalCave_LowTideInnerRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# Two piles of Shoal Salt, bare only at low tide (the tide covers them while FLAG_SYS_SHOAL_TIDE is set), and four Shoal
# Shells in the rock, each found once a day (FLAG_DAILY_RECEIVED_SHOAL_..., which the day clears where Emerald clears
# its flags once the Shell Bell's old man is next spoken to after a new day). The room is laid at high tide by
# world.json's swaps on the same flag; it reads the tide the entrance room set and never sets it.


script ShoalSalt1
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_SYS_SHOAL_TIDE goto Tide
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_RECEIVED_SHOAL_SALT_1 goto Gone
  find "Shoal Salt"
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_RECEIVED_SHOAL_SALT_1
  end
label Tide
  text "The sea has come in over the sand. Whatever lay here is under the water."
  end
label Gone
  text "A little Shoal Salt was here, but there's none left now."
  end
