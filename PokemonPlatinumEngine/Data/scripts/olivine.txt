# Olivine City (plan 20 · J9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats
# (scr_seq_T26).
#
# The first time the player walks past the Gym's door (the area's trigger 0, while VAR_UNK_4078 is 0), the red-haired
# boy comes out of it: the Leader is up the lighthouse nursing a sick Pokémon, and he has no time for a Leader like
# that. He goes back the way he came and the trigger is spent (VAR_UNK_4078 1). Until then he is kept off the street
# (FLAG_HIDE_OLIVINE_RIVAL, which HeartGold's new game sets: here the town's own arrival does, so no save needs it).
#
# Warden Baoba's first call (plan 20 · J9, part b): the first time the player comes back down into the town once Amphy
# is cured (VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE 2, olivine_lighthouse_light_room.txt), Baoba rings to say his Safari Zone is
# open (FLAG_UNK_0F5), which sends the workmen away from the Cliff Edge Gate (FLAG_UNK_249) and starts his test
# (VAR_UNK_4057 1, safari_zone_entrance_interior.txt); his number goes into the Pokégear. HeartGold rings as the player
# leaves the lighthouse; the variable's 3 is ours, so the call comes once (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The Johto Safari
# Zone").
#
# With the takeover begun by the last of three Badges (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 1), Professor Elm rings as the player
# comes out into the town (common.JohtoTakeoverCall, plan 20 · J11, part b).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_UNK_4078 == 0 setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLIVINE_RIVAL
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 1 call JohtoTakeoverCall
  if var VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE == 2 goto Baoba
  end
label Baoba
  scene "Warden Baoba's call"
  sound "select"
  text "Brrrring! Brrrring! It's the Pokégear..."
  speaker "Baoba"
  say "Hello, {player}! Warden Baoba here. Sorry to keep you waiting, but my Safari Zone is open at last!"
  say "Do you know the gate in the cave behind Cianwood City? Go through it and follow the cliff path, and you'll be there in no time."
  say "We're a little out of the way, but do come and visit!"
  text "Click... The call has ended."
  addcontact baoba
  setflag FLAG_UNK_0F5
  setflag FLAG_UNK_249
  setvar VAR_UNK_4057 1
  setvar VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE 3
  endscene
  end


# T26_001
script RivalAtTheGym
  scene "The red-haired boy at Olivine's Gym"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_OLIVINE_RIVAL
  show t26_gsrivel
  walk t26_gsrivel down 1
  emote player exclaim
  music "johto/rival"
  face player left
  face t26_gsrivel player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...You again. If you're here for the Gym, don't bother. The Leader's not even in it."
  say "She's up that lighthouse, fussing over some sick Pokémon. A Gym Leader playing nurse!"
  say "A Pokémon too weak to stand isn't worth the time. You'd waste yours on it too, wouldn't you? That's why you'll never be strong."
  walk t26_gsrivel up 1 fast
  hide t26_gsrivel
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLIVINE_RIVAL
  setvar VAR_UNK_4078 1
  music area
  endscene
  end


# Plan 44 · SD3: a Lockwork agent of Johto's cell, one locked Pokémon in the team
script LockworkAgent1
  face self player
  speaker self
  if defeated "lockwork_johto_agent_1" goto Beaten
  say "Lockwork moves its cargo quietly. You've seen too much of it already."
  say "That one's heart is locked tight. It'll fight for me without a second thought."
  battle self as "lockwork_johto_agent_1"
  speaker self
  say "Tch. Doesn't matter. The cargo's already on its way."
  end
label Beaten
  say "Keep walking. Lockwork doesn't forget a face."
  end
