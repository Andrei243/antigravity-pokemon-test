# The Virbank Gym's way down (plan 22 · U5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/virbank_city_gym.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK_GYM (Black 2's 0x40aa): 0 the guide hasn't met the player; 1 he has, and given a bottle of Fresh
# Water. Black 2's guide gives the water only to someone who has it in room; ours always does.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


# The top of the landing (our trigger, on 0)
script GuideGreets
  face 0 player
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  say "Welcome! This is a Pokémon Gym, and a rock club too! Roxie and her band are rehearsing downstairs."
  say "Take on everyone in there! Oh, and stay hydrated. It gets hot under those stage lights."
  give "Fresh Water"
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK_GYM 1
  end


script GymGuide
  face 0 player
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge toxic goto Won
  say "The Trainers here all use Poison types. Grass and Bug moves don't work well against them, so watch out!"
  say "And if your Pokémon get poisoned, an Antidote or a Pecha Berry will sort them out."
  end
label Won
  say "That Badge shines because you and your Pokémon won it together. Never forget that!"
  end
