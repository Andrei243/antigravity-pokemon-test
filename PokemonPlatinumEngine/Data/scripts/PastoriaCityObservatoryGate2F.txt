# The Great Marsh's observatory in Pastoria City, upper floor (plan 01 · M11, part b), on the original's plan.
# docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_pastoria_city_observatory_gate_2f.s).

# Three pairs of binoculars look out over the marsh, $100 a look. The original swings the picture over the marsh and
# shows a Pokémon that lives where it comes to rest (GreatMarshBinoculars_GetMonSpecies); here a look names the
# Pokémon the day has brought to one of the marsh's six areas, drawn by the script's die ({marsh:1} to {marsh:6}:
# plan 06 · R13's daily Pokémon, which is what the binoculars are for). The view itself waits for plan 09's kit.


script Binoculars
  ask "A pair of binoculars, pointed out over the Great Marsh. Put $100 in?"
  if no end
  takemoney 100
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  fade out 0.3
  fade in 0.3
  random 6
  if result == 1 goto Area2
  if result == 2 goto Area3
  if result == 3 goto Area4
  if result == 4 goto Area5
  if result == 5 goto Area6
  text "The glass swings out over Area 1..."
  text "A {marsh:1} lifts its head out of the reeds and looks about."
  end
label Area2
  text "The glass swings out over Area 2..."
  text "A {marsh:2} is wading through the shallows."
  end
label Area3
  text "The glass swings out over Area 3..."
  text "A {marsh:3} peers out from the tall grass."
  end
label Area4
  text "The glass swings out over Area 4..."
  text "A {marsh:4} is splashing about in the mud."
  end
label Area5
  text "The glass swings out over Area 5..."
  text "A {marsh:5} darts across a patch of open ground."
  end
label Area6
  text "The glass swings out over Area 6..."
  text "A {marsh:6} is dozing in the sun by the tram's rails."
  end
label Short
  text "There isn't enough money for that..."
  end
