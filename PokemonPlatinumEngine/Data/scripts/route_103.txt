# Route 103 (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/Route103/scripts.inc).
#
# The rival, counting Pokémon at the water's edge: spoken to, the first battle between the two, with Emerald's team for
# the starter the player took (the rival's is the one strong against it: May's TRAINER_MAY_ROUTE_103_TREECKO is the
# battle of a player who took Treecko). Beaten, the rival heads back to the lab ahead of the player.


script Rival
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_RIVAL_ROUTE103 end
  scene "The rival on Route 103"
  face self player
  music "hoenn/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Hey! Dad told me you saved him. And he gave you a Pokémon? That's so cool!"
  say "I was just counting the Pokémon around here. But I've got my own partner too, you know."
  say "Let's have a battle! I'll show you what being a trainer is all about!"
  if girl goto Brendan
  if starter "Treecko" goto MayTreecko
  if starter "Torchic" goto MayTorchic
  battle self as "hoenn_may_route_103_mudkip"
  goto Beaten
label MayTreecko
  battle self as "hoenn_may_route_103_treecko"
  goto Beaten
label MayTorchic
  battle self as "hoenn_may_route_103_torchic"
  goto Beaten
label Brendan
  if starter "Treecko" goto BrendanTreecko
  if starter "Torchic" goto BrendanTorchic
  battle self as "hoenn_brendan_route_103_mudkip"
  goto Beaten
label BrendanTreecko
  battle self as "hoenn_brendan_route_103_treecko"
  goto Beaten
label BrendanTorchic
  battle self as "hoenn_brendan_route_103_torchic"
label Beaten
  say "Wow... You're really good! I can see why your Pokémon trusts you, {player}."
  say "I think I've seen enough for today. I'm heading back to the lab. Come on, Dad will want to hear about this!"
  walk self down 2 fast
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_103_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_RIVAL_ROUTE103
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_BIRCHS_LAB_RIVAL
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_OLDALE_TOWN_RIVAL
  setvar VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE 4
  setvar VAR_OLDALE_RIVAL_STATE 1
  music area
  endscene
  end
