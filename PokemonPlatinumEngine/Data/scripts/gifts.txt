# The gift box's gifts (plan 40 · GE7, GE9; Models/GiftBox.cs, Data/gifts.json; docs/scripts.md, "The gift box"). Ours,
# as the user answered the outline sheet (decisions/APPROVAL-plans-35-44.md, part D). Each is called by a Gift Runner's
# `gift give` once its terms hold and is noted taken first; it hands its gift over through the host's own commands and
# returns to the runner, who says goodbye. A gift that brings a Pokémon or an Egg waits for room on the team: the box
# doesn't offer it until there is (GiftBox.Refused). Seals stand in for the sheet's fireworks and snowflakes: Platinum's
# Party and Star Seals (rulings, "The gift box").


# 1. New Year's Day, from the Year-Turner Post
script NewYear
  seal give "Party Seal D" 5
  gift backdrop "Midnight Bloom"
  return
