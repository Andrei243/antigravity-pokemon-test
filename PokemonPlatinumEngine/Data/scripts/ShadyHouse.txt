# The Shady House, Po Town, Ula'ula Island: Nanu's grand trial (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's

# the kahuna of Ula'ula is a lazy man with a Meowth, and the island's grand trial is a battle with him. The Ula'ula
# stamp, the Darkinium Z, and word from Professor Kukui that Lusamine has taken Lillie (VAR_ALOLA_STORY 47).


script NanuInside
  face self player
  speaker "Nanu"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_NANU_DONE goto Done
  scene "Nanu's grand trial"
  say "Sit, sit. This is my house, so I make the rules. First rule: don't look at the Meowth."
  say "Ula'ula's grand trial is a battle with me. That's it. No totem, no dens, no tests. I can't be bothered."
  say "Do your best. I'll do mine, which is less, but it's enough."
  battle self as "alola_nanu_1"
  speaker "Nanu"
  say "Hmph. Not bad. You scrapped your way through Skull and now through me. The island has itself a decent one."
  givebadge UlaUla
  text "{player}'s Trainer card received Ula'ula's stamp!"
  give "Darkinium Z"
  say "That's the Darkinium Z. Dark-type moves, made bigger. Don't get used to being bigger."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_NANU_DONE
  hide self
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "...{player}! It's Kukui. Listen, Lillie went back to Aether Paradise, and Lusamine won't let her leave."
  say "Gladion called me. He says his mother isn't herself, and Lillie's in danger. Go to Aether Paradise. Take the ferry from Malie's pier."
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 47
  objective alolaRescue
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "Go. I'm napping."
  end
