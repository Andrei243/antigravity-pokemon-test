# The Abundant Shrine (plan 22 · U10, part d, second piece). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
# Black 2's Landorus waits before the shrine once the player has entered Unova's Hall of Fame (shown by UnovaHallOfFame), at
# level 70: caught or beaten it is gone for good (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_LANDORUS); run from, it flies off and is back the next
# time the wood is come to. Black 2 asks for Tornadus and Thundurus first (from the Dream Radar, which this game has no
# stand-in for), so ours asks only the Hall of Fame (the rulings' "The Abundant Shrine's Landorus").


script Landorus
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  scene "Landorus at the Abundant Shrine"
  music stop
  face player self
  face self player
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera path 22 14 22 15 3
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect beam 22 14 0.8
  shake self 8
  camera shake 0.8
  cry "Landorus"
  speaker none
  text "Landorus let out a thundering cry!\nThe soil of the whole wood seems to stir at its call!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Landorus" 70
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_LANDORUS
  hide self
  text "The wood settles. Only the wind over the pond is left..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Landorus soared away over the wood..."
  end
