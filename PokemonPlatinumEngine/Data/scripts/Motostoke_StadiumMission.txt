# Kabu's gym mission (plan 33 · GL4, its second session): three fire Pokémon loose on the course, each rounded up and
# handed back to its keeper, and two gym trainers beside the way. A Pokémon rounded up stays with its keeper (its own
# flag). The course is for challengers with the Water Badge. Our own lines, on Sword and Shield's beat of a course of
# Pokémon to catch; a round-up, not the original's points (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Route 5, Hulbury, Galar Mine
# No. 2 and Kabu").


# The first step onto the course: back to the lobby without the Water Badge
script MissionGate
  if badge water end
  speaker "Gym Trainer"
  say "Hold on! Kabu takes on challengers with two badges. Come back once you've won Nessa's Water Badge."
  walk player down 1
  end


script MissionVulpix
  text "The Vulpix yipped and dashed in circles. {player} crouched down and held out a hand..."
  text "It trotted over to be carried back to its keeper!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_MOTOSTOKE_CAUGHT_1
  end


script MissionGrowlithe
  text "The Growlithe barked and stood its ground. {player} stood just as still, and waited..."
  text "It wagged its tail and followed along to its keeper!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_MOTOSTOKE_CAUGHT_2
  end


script MissionSizzlipede
  text "The Sizzlipede glowed hot in the last gateway. {player} waited for it to cool, then scooped it up..."
  text "It curled up happily and went back to its keeper. The mission is cleared!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_MOTOSTOKE_CAUGHT_3
  end
