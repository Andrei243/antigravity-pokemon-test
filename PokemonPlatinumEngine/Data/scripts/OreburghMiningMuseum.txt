# The Oreburgh Mining Museum (plan 06 · R16b), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_mining_museum.s).
#
# The scientist at the desk brings a Fossil back to life: he has nothing to say of it until the player has the
# Explorer Kit (FLAG_RECEIVED_EXPLORER_KIT), takes one Fossil (asking which when the bag holds more than one kind),
# and has the Pokémon ready once the player has left the museum and come in again: the Fossil's species is kept in
# VAR_REVIVED_POKEMON_SPECIES, and FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_MUSEUM_REVIVING, which lasts only while the player stays, says the
# machine is still at work. A full team is told to make room, as in the original.


script FossilResearcher
  if flag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_MUSEUM_REVIVING goto Working
  say "I study Fossils. More than study them: with the machine behind me, I can bring them back to life!"
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_EXPLORER_KIT goto NotYet
  fossil ready
  if result == 1 goto Ready
  fossil count
  if result == 0 goto None
  ask "You have a Fossil with you, I can smell the stone dust! Shall I bring it back to life?"
  if no goto Declined
  fossil choose
  if result == 0 goto Declined
  fossil revive
  if result == 0 goto Declined
label Working
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_MUSEUM_REVIVING
  say "This takes a little while, and I work better unwatched." "Take a walk outside, then come and see me again."
  end
label Ready
  say "There you are! Look what your Fossil turned out to be: {thing}!"
  if not party < 6 goto Full
  fossil give
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received {thing}!"
  end
label Full
  say "But your team is full. Make room for one more, and it's yours."
  end
label None
  say "Bring me a Fossil, and I'll show you what once walked this land." "They turn up in the walls of the Underground, if you dig for them."
  end
label Declined
  say "No? If you change your mind, you know where the machine is."
  end
label NotYet
  say "...But you've never dug anything up yourself, have you? Come back when you have. It will mean more."
  end


# The collector before the cases. Plan 03 · D12a, ours: the Underground gives a Skull Fossil to a trainer whose number
# is odd and an Armor Fossil to one whose number is even (the original's weights, Models/Underground/MiningGame.cs),
# so one game could only ever have Cranidos or Shieldon. Once the Pokédex is the National one he hands over the two
# he dug in his own day, once.
script Collector
  if not nationaldex goto Talk
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_MINING_MUSEUM_COLLECTOR_FOSSILS goto Talk
  say "A National Pokédex! Then you'll want both of the old armoured ones in it, and the wall only ever gives a digger one kind." "I dug these two up in my own day, one of each. The scientist at the desk can bring them back."
  give "Skull Fossil"
  give "Armor Fossil"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_MINING_MUSEUM_COLLECTOR_FOSSILS
  end
label Talk
  say "Digging takes a strong arm, they say. Nonsense. It takes a strong mind." "One blow too many and the whole wall comes down on your find. Patience, patience."
  end
