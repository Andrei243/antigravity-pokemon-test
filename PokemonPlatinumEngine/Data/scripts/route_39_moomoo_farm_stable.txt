# The Moomoo Farm's barn (plan 20 · J9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's
# beats (scr_seq_R39R0201).
#
# The sick Miltank lies in the barn. Once the farmer has told the player what ails her (FLAG_UNK_0A8, set in the
# farmhouse), she may be fed Oran Berries one at a time; HeartGold counts the first six with six flags of its own and
# cures her with the seventh (FLAG_UNK_0AA). Here one variable of ours counts them (VAR_MOOMOO_FARM_BERRIES_FED), and
# the third and the fifth bring HeartGold's two words of cheer. Once she is well the farmer sells Moomoo Milk and his
# wife gives TM83 (route_39_moomoo_farm_house.txt). The girls' Seals are left out: Johto's game has no Seal Case.


# R39R0201_002
script SickMiltank
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0AA goto Well
  cry "Miltank"
  text "Miltank looks very weak. It hardly lifts its head."
  if not flag FLAG_UNK_0A8 end
  if not item "Oran Berry" goto NoBerry
  ask "Give Miltank an Oran Berry?"
  if no goto Later
  take "Oran Berry"
  text "{player} gave Miltank an Oran Berry. It munched it down."
  addvar VAR_MOOMOO_FARM_BERRIES_FED 1
  if var VAR_MOOMOO_FARM_BERRIES_FED == 3 text "Miltank seems a little happier."
  if var VAR_MOOMOO_FARM_BERRIES_FED == 5 text "Miltank is starting to look more lively."
  if var VAR_MOOMOO_FARM_BERRIES_FED < 7 end
  fanfare item
  text "Miltank is full of energy again! It's back on its feet!"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_0AA
  end
label NoBerry
  text "If only there were some Oran Berries to give it..."
  end
label Later
  text "{player} decided to come back later."
  end
label Well
  cry "Miltank"
  text "Moooo! Miltank looks happy and healthy."
  end


# R39R0201_000
script Girl
  face r39r0201_gsbabygirl1 player
  speaker "Girl"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0AA goto Well
  say "Our Miltank is poorly. She just lies there and won't eat her hay."
  end
label Well
  say "Miltank's better! Thank you! She's going to make lots of yummy milk again."
  end


# R39R0201_001
script Sister
  face r39r0201_gsbabygirl1_2 player
  speaker "Girl"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0AA goto Well
  if var VAR_MOOMOO_FARM_BERRIES_FED > 0 goto Feeding
  say "Grandpa says lots of Berries would make her better. Do you have any?"
  end
label Feeding
  say "She ate it! Keep going, please! Grandpa says she needs lots."
  end
label Well
  say "You made Miltank better with Berries! You're amazing!"
  end
