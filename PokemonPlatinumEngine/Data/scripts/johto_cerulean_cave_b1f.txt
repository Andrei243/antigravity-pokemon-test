# Cerulean Cave's lowest floor as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part o). docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D03R0103). FireRed's Kanto has its own floor and
# Mewtwo (cerulean_cave_b1f.txt), which this one does not touch.
#
# Mewtwo waits at the back until it has been fought (FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_MEWTWO, ours), at level 70 as HeartGold's: beaten
# or caught it is gone for good, run from it vanishes (HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_CERULEAN_CAVE_MEWTWO) and is back on the
# next visit, as Articuno and Moltres are.
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat for a Pokémon of the mind (style guide, "Legendary scenes"): the bars, the camera pushed
# in on it, sparkles and a ripple, Mewtwo there and not there, the cry and its will pressed on the player; the scene
# ends before the battle, so the hold never skips it.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_MEWTWO clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CERULEAN_CAVE_MEWTWO
  end

# D03R0103_000: Mewtwo
script Mewtwo
  scene "Mewtwo in Cerulean Cave"
  music stop
  face player self
  letterbox on 0.4
  # Met from below, it is behind the player's own head from the field's camera: a step back, facing it, shows it
  if facing up walk player back down 1
  camera pan 56 24 1.2
  camera zoom 1.8 1.4
  effect sparkles 56 24
  effect ripple 56 24
  flicker self 4
  cry "Mewtwo"
  camera shake 0.4
  emote player exclaim
  speaker none
  text "A cold, sharp will pressed against {player}'s mind!"
  if facing up walk player up 1
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Mewtwo" 70
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_MEWTWO
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CERULEAN_CAVE_MEWTWO
  hide self
  end
label Fled
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CERULEAN_CAVE_MEWTWO
  hide self
  text "Mewtwo vanished into the dark of the cave!"
  end
