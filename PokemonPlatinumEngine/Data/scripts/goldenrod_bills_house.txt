# Bill's family's house in Goldenrod City (plan 20 · J7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T25R0401): Bill, home once met in Ecruteak City (VAR_UNK_410D 1), gives an Eevee
# to a team with room for it (FLAG_GOT_EEVEE_FROM_BILL); his mother tells where he is.


script Bill
  face t25r0401_masaki player
  speaker "Bill"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_EEVEE_FROM_BILL goto Given
  say "Oh, hi, {player}! Thanks for coming. Remember the Eevee I told you about? It's right here."
  ask "It's a rare one, and it can evolve in all sorts of ways. Would you like to raise it?"
  if no goto No
  if var PARTY_COUNT >= 6 goto Full
  givepokemon "Eevee" 5
  setflag FLAG_GOT_EEVEE_FROM_BILL
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received Eevee!"
  say "Eevee's a funny one. Its genes are unstable, so it can turn into lots of different Pokémon. Take good care of it!"
  end
label No
  say "No? That's a shame. If you change your mind, come back. Eevee will be here."
  end
label Full
  say "Whoa, your team's full! Make some room, then come and see me."
  end
label Given
  say "How's Eevee doing? I can't wait to see what it turns into."
  end


script Mother
  if var VAR_UNK_410D == 1 goto Home
  say "My son Bill made the PC storage system you use. He's off in Ecruteak City just now."
  say "He calls whenever he wants feeding. Typical!"
  end
label Home
  say "Bill came home all of a sudden. He always rushes in like that, then off again!"
  end
