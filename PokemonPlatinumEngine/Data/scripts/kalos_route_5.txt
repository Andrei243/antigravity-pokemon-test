# Route 5, the Skaters' Lane (plan 23 · Z2, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Central Kalos, drawn by hand: Route 5 to Cyllage City"): out of
# Lumiose's west gate, Tierno and Trevor wait near the lane's west end; Tierno wants a battle to show off his team, and
# the two go on to Camphrier Town. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 12, Lysandre's café heard, becomes 13.
#
# The people: tierno and trevor (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_FRIENDS_ROUTE_5, set by their scene). Tierno's team is Kalos's
# trainers.json's kalos_tierno_2.


# Along the lane toward Camphrier (the trigger across it near its west end, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 12)
script Friends
  scene "Tierno and Trevor on Route 5"
  face tierno player
  face trevor player
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Hey, {player}! We skated all the way from Lumiose. Did you see the square back there? The skaters spin like dancers!"
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "I counted four kinds of Pokémon I'd never seen in the grass along here. Four!"
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "I caught a new partner on the way, and we've worked out a routine. Battle us and see!"
  battle tierno as "kalos_tierno_2" canlose
  heal
  speaker "Tierno"
  if lost goto TiernoWon
  say "You're getting really strong! I'll need a brand new routine to keep up with you."
  goto After
label TiernoWon
  say "Ta-da! But honestly, your team kept perfect time with ours. That was fun."
label After
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "Camphrier Town is just ahead. They say a big Pokémon has been asleep on the road south of it for days."
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Let's go and see it! Maybe it likes dancing."
  walk tierno left 8 fast
  move trevor left 8 fast
  waitmoves
  hide tierno
  hide trevor
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_FRIENDS_ROUTE_5
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 13
  objective kalosCamphrier
  endscene
  end


# Should they be spoken to while they stand there (they leave in the same scene)

  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Ready to see our routine?"
  end

  speaker trevor "Trevor"
