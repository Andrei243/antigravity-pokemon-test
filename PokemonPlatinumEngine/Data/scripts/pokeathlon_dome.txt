# The Pokéathlon Dome's square (Johto's leftovers). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beat.
#
# The first time the square is come to, the dome's owner sees the player and greets them (HeartGold's first-visit
# scene and its flag, FLAG_UNK_225); afterwards he stays by the way in to talk (the overlay's lines).


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_UNK_225 end
  scene "The dome's owner"
  emote d49_thlonowner exclaim
  face d49_thlonowner player
  speaker "Dome Owner"
  say "Oh! A new face! Welcome, welcome to the Pokéathlon Dome!"
  say "I built this place myself. In battle only the strongest shine, but here every Pokémon can show what it's good at."
  say "Speed, power, skill, stamina, jumps... Bring three Pokémon to the reception inside and enter a course. My Poliwrath and I will cheer you on!"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_225
  endscene
  end
