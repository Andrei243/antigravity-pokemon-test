# The Iron Chamber (plan 22 · U10, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/iron_chamber.s, read for the order alone).

# Registeel stands as a statue at the chamber's head; spoken to, it stirs and is battled at Black 2's level 65. Caught or beaten it is
# gone for good (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_REGISTEEL); run from, it is back the next time the chamber is come to.
script Registeel
  speaker none
  text "The statue of the iron giant began to move!"
  cry "Registeel"
  wildbattle "Registeel" 65
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_REGISTEEL
  hide self
  text "The chamber is still again..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Registeel sank away into the depths of the ruins..."
  end


script Plaque
  speaker none
  text "Iron Chamber"
  end


script Inscription
  speaker none
  text "Here sleeps the guardian of iron, who keeps watch over these ruins."
  end


script BackToTheDoor
  call UnovaRuinsWayBack
  end
