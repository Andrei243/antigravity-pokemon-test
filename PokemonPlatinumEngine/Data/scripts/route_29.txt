# Route 29 (plan 20 · J4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats
# (scr_seq_R29).
#
# The catching lesson (HeartGold's R29_001, the area's trigger 0, on VAR_UNK_408B 1, which the lab sets once the
# Poké Balls are handed over): the friend next door is waiting at the edge of the grass with their Marill, and shows
# the player how a wild Pokémon is caught. The lesson's battle plays itself (`catchinglesson`), with the Pokémon the
# friend was left (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Johto's first chapter").


script Lesson
  scene "The catching lesson"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_29_FRIEND
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_29_MARILL
  show r29_var_2
  show r29_tsure_poke_static_marill
  face player left
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "{player}! Over here! The professor told me you've got Poké Balls now."
  say "Have you ever caught a Pokémon? It's easy once you've seen it done. Watch me!"
  catchinglesson "Sentret" 2
  say "See? Wear it down first, then throw the ball. Now you try! Fill up that Pokédex."
  say "Marill and I are off home. See you around, {player}!"
  hide r29_var_2
  hide r29_tsure_poke_static_marill
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_29_FRIEND
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_29_MARILL
  setvar VAR_UNK_408B 2
  endscene
  end


# R29_000, the route's own script as it loads (plan 20 · J13): Tuscany of Tuesday is here on Tuesdays once the Zephyr
# Badge is won (FLAG_UNK_207)
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_UNK_207
  if not badge zephyr end
  if weekday tuesday clearflag FLAG_UNK_207
  end


# R29_003: Tuscany of Tuesday gives a Twisted Spoon once (FLAG_GOT_TWISTEDSPOON_FROM_TUSCANY) and counts herself among
# the siblings met (VAR_NUM_MET_WEEKDAY_SIBLINGS); once all seven are met she gives the ribbon of the day
script Tuscany
  face r29_gswoman2_2 player
  speaker "Tuscany"
  if var VAR_NUM_MET_WEEKDAY_SIBLINGS >= 7 goto All
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TWISTEDSPOON_FROM_TUSCANY goto Again
  if not weekday tuesday goto NotToday
  say "How do you do? I'm Tuscany of Tuesday. Since we've met on my day, please accept this little gift."
  give "Twisted Spoon"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TWISTEDSPOON_FROM_TUSCANY
  addvar VAR_NUM_MET_WEEKDAY_SIBLINGS 1
  say "A Pokémon that holds a Twisted Spoon hits harder with Psychic-type moves. Do look after it."
  end
label Again
  say "Have you met my younger brother Wesley? He keeps Wednesdays at the Lake of Rage."
  end
label NotToday
  say "Today isn't Tuesday, is it? How disappointing. Tuesday is my day."
  end
label All
  if not weekday tuesday goto NotToday
  call WeekSiblingRibbon
  end
