# The rival's room upstairs (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/LittlerootTown_MaysHouse_2F/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE, as Emerald counts it: 2 the room come into, 3 the rival met, 4 the Pokédex had. The rival
# comes in when the player looks at the Poké Ball on the floor, and goes out to help the professor.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE >= 2 end
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE 2
  end


script RivalsBall
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE != 2 goto JustABall
  scene "The rival next door"
  text "A Poké Ball sits on the floor. It's empty."
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MAYS_HOUSE_RIVAL_BEDROOM
  show rivals_house_2f_rival
  walk rivals_house_2f_rival down 1
  emote rivals_house_2f_rival exclaim
  face player rivals_house_2f_rival
  face rivals_house_2f_rival player
  music "hoenn/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Huh? Who are you? ...Oh! You're {player}, from next door! I heard you were moving in."
  say "I'm {rival}. My dad's Professor Birch. I help him count Pokémon out on the routes."
  say "I've always wanted a friend nearby who's into Pokémon too. Maybe someday you'll have one of your own!"
  say "Oops, I'd better get going. Dad's out on Route 101 and I said I'd help. See you, {player}!"
  walk rivals_house_2f_rival up 1
  hide rivals_house_2f_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MAYS_HOUSE_RIVAL_BEDROOM
  music area
  hide item_ball
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_MAYS_HOUSE_2F_POKE_BALL
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE 3
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE 1
  endscene
  end
label JustABall
  text "An empty Poké Ball."
  end


# The rival once back from Route 103 and in the lab, when they come home; and home again after the battle before
# Lilycove's Department Store (FLAG_MET_RIVAL_LILYCOVE, plan 21 · H7, part b), as Emerald puts them back in this room
script Rival
  face self player
  speaker "{rival}"
  if flag FLAG_MET_RIVAL_LILYCOVE goto Home
  say "Our house is right next to yours, {player}. Come over any time!"
  end
label Home
  say "{player}! I came home to help Dad sort through all the data from my journey."
  say "Hearing how far you've got makes me want to go back out there. Keep going, all right? I'm cheering for you!"
  end
