# The elder is home once Cyrus has left the ruins (celestic_town_cave.txt clears
# FLAG_HIDE_CELESTIC_TOWN_NORTH_HOUSE_ELDER). To a Champion (plan 02 · S15, part c) she reads from an old book she found
# in the shrine after the trouble on Mt. Coronet: whoever bears the Adamant Orb calls Dialga to Spear Pillar, and
# whoever bears the Lustrous Orb calls Palkia. Having heard it is what lets the mountain's way out lead to their
# pillars (FLAG_UNLOCKED_DIALGA_PALKIA_SPEAR_PILLAR, which mt_coronet_6f.txt asks), and she reads it again whenever
# asked, as the original's does.


# The old man by the table (the original's script 1).
script Mesprit
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "Where are you from? ...Twinleaf Town, you say? Why, that's near Lake Verity."
  say "They say the lake is home to Mesprit, the Being of Emotion. Perhaps it watches over you."
  ask "Would you like to hear more about Mesprit?"
  if no goto Not
  say "Mesprit is the legendary Pokémon that stands for the heart's feelings."
  say "Beside it are Uxie, the Being of Knowledge, and Azelf, the Being of Willpower."
  say "The three are said to have strong, kind spirits, and their strength keeps Sinnoh in balance."
  end
label Not
  say "Ha ha ha! No, I didn't think you would. It's a story from a very long time ago. No wonder it bores you."
  end


# The elder, home again (the original's script 2).
script Elder
  speaker "Elder"
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Research
  if flag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_CANALAVE_CITY goto Oldest
  say "Once one of your Pokémon knows Surf, you'll find you can reach all sorts of places you couldn't before."
  say "You may be surprised where the water takes you."
  end
label Oldest
  say "Celestic Town is the oldest town in all of Sinnoh. It was founded to honour Pokémon of the distant past."
  say "Or so the story goes, at any rate..."
  end
label Research
  # CelesticTownNorthHouse_IDidSomeResearch
  setflag FLAG_UNLOCKED_DIALGA_PALKIA_SPEAR_PILLAR
  say "Well, hello! You're looking well."
  say "I've been doing some reading of my own since that business in the ruins. The way Mt. Coronet was carrying on had me worried too."
  say "And this is what I found in the shrine: a book, and a very old one. Let me read you a little."
  say "\"Carry the Adamant Orb, bright as the day it was made, up to the Spear Pillar, and Dialga will come to it: the Pokémon that keeps watch over time.\""
  say "\"Carry the Lustrous Orb there in the same way, and Palkia will come: the Pokémon that holds all of space together.\""
  say "So it seems the people of long ago met them both: Dialga and Palkia, the Pokémon of time and of space."
  end
