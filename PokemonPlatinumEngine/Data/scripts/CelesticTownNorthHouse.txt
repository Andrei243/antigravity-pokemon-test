# The old man by the table (the original's script 1).
script Mesprit
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "Where are you from? ...Twinleaf Town, you say? Why, that's near Lake Verity."
  say "They say the lake is home to Mesprit, the Being of Emotion. Perhaps it watches over you."
  ask "Would you like to hear more about Mesprit?"
  if no goto Not
  say "Mesprit is the legendary Pokémon that stands for the heart's feelings."
  say "Beside it are Uxie, the Being of Knowledge, and Azelf, the Being of Willpower."
  say "The three are said to have strong, kind spirits, and their strength keeps Sinnoh in balance."
  end
label Not
  say "Ha ha ha! No, I didn't think you would. It's a story from a very long time ago. No wonder it bores you."
  end


# The elder, home again (the original's script 2).

  end
