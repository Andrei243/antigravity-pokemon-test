# Vermilion City's port hall (plan 20 · J15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_P01R0103).
#
# The sailor before the quay lets by anyone with passage on the S.S. Aqua (FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_PASSAGE, which Olivine's
# port hall gives a Champion of Johto), stepping aside for good the first time, as Olivine's does.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_PASSAGE end
  place p01r0103_seaman_2 8 10 left
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_JOHTO_VERMILION_PORT_SAILOR_ASIDE
  end


script Sailor
  face p01r0103_seaman_2 player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_SS_AQUA_PASSAGE goto Shut
  say "Bound back to Johto? The S.S. Aqua is at the quay, ready to sail for Olivine City."
  if flag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_JOHTO_VERMILION_PORT_SAILOR_ASIDE end
  walk self down 1 right 1
  face p01r0103_seaman_2 left
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_JOHTO_VERMILION_PORT_SAILOR_ASIDE
  end
label Shut
  say "The S.S. Aqua sails from here to Olivine City in Johto. Only those with passage may board."
  end
