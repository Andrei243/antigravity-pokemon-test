# The gate on Route 16 (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route16_NorthEntrance_1F/scripts.inc and data/scripts/cycling_road.inc).
#
# The gate's lower lane leads onto the Cycling Road. The guard calls back whoever crosses its middle without a
# Bicycle in the bag (FireRed's NeedBikeTrigger: FireRed asks the bag, not whether the player rides), and a step by
# the west doors notes that the player is going onto the road (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE16 1, FireRed's
# SetEnteringCyclingRoad), a step by the east doors that they have come off it (0). While the variable is 1, Route 16,
# 17 and 18 put the player on the Bicycle and keep them on it (route_16.txt, route_17.txt, route_18.txt).


script NeedBike
  if item "Bicycle" end
  speaker "Guard"
  face player up
  say "Hold on! No one goes onto the Cycling Road on foot. Come back with a Bicycle."
  walk player right 1
  end


script OntoTheRoad
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE16 1
  end


script OffTheRoad
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE16 0
  end
