# Veilstone's Department Store, ground floor (plan 01 · M11, part d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_veilstone_store_1f.s).
#
# Two clerks sell over its west counter (marts.json's veilstone_1f_right and veilstone_1f_left: the medicines, and the
# balls, repels and mail). The socialite gives the mask of a starter the player didn't meet, once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_STORE_1F_ACCESSORY_STARTER_MASK): Piplup's to whoever chose Turtwig, Turtwig's to whoever
# chose Chimchar, Chimchar's to whoever chose Piplup.


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_VEILSTONE_STORE_LIFT_FLOOR 2
  call VeilstoneStoreElevator.Stands
  end


# VeilstoneStore1F_Socialite
script Socialite
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_STORE_1F_ACCESSORY_STARTER_MASK goto Given
  say "I made an Accessory after the Pokémon of a Trainer I met once." "Would you like one? Here!"
  if starter "Turtwig" goto Piplup
  if starter "Chimchar" goto Turtwig
  giveaccessory "Chimchar Mask"
  goto After
label Piplup
  giveaccessory "Piplup Mask"
  goto After
label Turtwig
  giveaccessory "Turtwig Mask"
label After
  if result == 0 goto Full
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_STORE_1F_ACCESSORY_STARTER_MASK
  end
label Full
  say "Oh, your Fashion Case has no room for it. Do come back when it has!"
  end
label Given
  say "Put that mask on a Pokémon, and it will cut quite a different figure in a Contest."
  end
