# The Pewter Museum of Science, ground floor (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PewterCity_Museum_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# The steps past the desk (the area's triggers 0 to 2, on VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY_MUSEUM_1F 0, which the city sets
# back to 0 on every visit): a child's ticket is 50, and whoever won't pay or can't is turned back.


script Ticket
  face museum_scientist1 player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  ask "Welcome! A child's ticket is 50. Would you like to come in?"
  if no goto NotToday
  takemoney 50
  if result == 0 goto Short
  say "Thank you! Please enjoy the exhibits."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY_MUSEUM_1F 1
  end
label Short
  say "Oh dear, you don't have enough money. Please come back another time."
  walk player down 1
  end
label NotToday
  say "Do come again!"
  walk player down 1
  end


# The receptionist, spoken to from inside.
script Receptionist
  say "Amber is sap from ancient trees that hardened over time. Sometimes something is caught inside it."
  end
