# The Pewter Museum of Science, ground floor (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PewterCity_Museum_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# The steps past the desk (the area's triggers 0 to 2, on VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY_MUSEUM_1F 0, which the city sets
# back to 0 on every visit): a child's ticket is 50, and whoever won't pay or can't is turned back.


script Ticket
  face museum_scientist1 player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  ask "Welcome! A child's ticket is 50. Would you like to come in?"
  if no goto NotToday
  takemoney 50
  if result == 0 goto Short
  say "Thank you! Please enjoy the exhibits."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY_MUSEUM_1F 1
  end
label Short
  say "Oh dear, you don't have enough money. Please come back another time."
  walk player down 1
  end
label NotToday
  say "Do come again!"
  walk player down 1
  end


# The receptionist, spoken to from inside.
script Receptionist
  say "Amber is sap from ancient trees that hardened over time. Sometimes something is caught inside it."
  end


# The scientist in the back room, beside the Old Amber on its stand (plan 19 · K4, part b; FireRed's
# OldAmberScientist): he asks for it to be taken to a lab that can look into it, and gives it (FLAG_GOT_OLD_AMBER,
# FLAG_HIDE_OLD_AMBER). The lab that revives it is Cinnabar Island's, plan 19 · K7's.
script OldAmberScientist
  speaker "Scientist"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_OLD_AMBER goto Given
  say "Ssh! I think this chunk of amber holds the genes of an ancient Pokémon. Could you take it to a lab to be checked?"
  hide old_amber
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLD_AMBER
  give "Old Amber"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_OLD_AMBER
  end
label Given
  say "Ssh! Get that Old Amber looked at by someone who knows fossils. I'm sure there's a Pokémon inside!"
  end
