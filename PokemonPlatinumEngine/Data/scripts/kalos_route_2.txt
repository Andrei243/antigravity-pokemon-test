# Route 2, the Avance Trail (plan 23 · Z1, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos drawn by hand, its first chapter and its roller skates"): a little way up
# the trail the neighbour shows how a wild Pokémon is caught and hands over Poké Balls, and the friends at the café set
# off ahead. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 4, the skates had, becomes 5 here.
#
# The people: rival, the neighbour (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_2, shown for the scene alone).


# A save from before Santalune's part of the story was written finds its people as a new game does
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_SANTALUNE_BEGUN call KalosSantalune
  end


# A few steps up the trail with the skates had (the trigger across the trail, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 4)
script Lesson
  scene "The neighbour's lesson"
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_2
  place rival 255 632 down
  show rival
  face player up
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! I was hoping to catch you. Your first time in the tall grass?"
  say "Wild Pokémon live in there. If you want one on your team, wear it down a little, then throw a Poké Ball. Watch."
  if starter "Chespin" goto WithFennekin
  if starter "Fennekin" goto WithFroakie
  catchinglesson "Fletchling" 3 by "{rival}" "Chespin" 6
  goto Caught
label WithFennekin
  catchinglesson "Fletchling" 3 by "{rival}" "Fennekin" 6
  goto Caught
label WithFroakie
  catchinglesson "Fletchling" 3 by "{rival}" "Froakie" 6
label Caught
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Like that. A tired Pokémon gives in to a ball far more easily than a fresh one."
  say "Here, take some Poké Balls. The professor wants us to meet every Pokémon in Kalos, after all."
  give "Poké Ball" 10
  say "The forest is just north of here. The others went on ahead. I'll see you in Santalune City."
  walk rival up 6 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_ROUTE_2
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_FRIENDS_CAFE
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_SHAUNA_FOREST
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 5
  objective kalosForest
  endscene
  end


# Should the neighbour be spoken to before the scene moves on
script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Watch closely, {player}."
  end
