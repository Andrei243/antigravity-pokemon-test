# The Battle Frontier's annex (plan 14 · B8), a building of our own beside Platinum's five, and its lobby. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the beats of Emerald's Battle Dome (a tournament of sixteen, four wins
# to the title, the opponent's three seen before two of the player's are sent in).
#
# The annex has no model of its own on the Frontier's grounds: the guide before the lodge on the plaza's south side shows
# the player in (battle_frontier.txt, AnnexGuide), and the lobby's door leads back out to him. Its rooms are maps of ours
# (Data/maps/BattleFrontierAnnex.json, BattleDome.json). The Dome's desk is here; the tournament itself is the hall's
# arrival script (BattleDome.txt), round by round, on Models/Frontier/BattleDome.cs. Plan 14 · B15's Masters' Hall joins
# this lobby later.


# The Battle Dome's desk
script Receptionist
  face self player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "Welcome to the Battle Dome! Sixteen Trainers meet in a tournament, and four wins in a row make a champion."
  bracket streak
  if result == 0 goto Ask
  say "You've won {number} tournaments in a row. The field gets tougher with every one!"
label Ask
  choose "Would you like to enter a tournament?" "Enter" "The rules" "No"
  if result == 1 goto Rules
  if result == 2 goto Later
  bracket enough
  if result == 1 goto Choose
  say "You need three Pokémon that can take part, all of different species and holding different items."
  say "An Egg can't take part, and nor can {banned}."
  end
label Choose
  say "Please choose the three Pokémon you'll enter."
  bracket choose
  if result == 0 goto Later
  bracket check
  if result == 1 goto SameSpecies
  if result == 2 goto SameItem
  bracket begin
  say "Thank you! The bracket has been drawn. This way to the hall, please!"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "BattleDome" 8 10 up
  fade in
  end
label SameSpecies
  say "I'm sorry. The three entered must all be of different species."
  goto Later
label SameItem
  say "I'm sorry. No two of the three entered may hold the same item."
label Later
  say "Please come again. There's always another tournament about to begin."
  end
label Rules
  say "Your three battle at Level 50, or at their own level if it's lower. Nothing you do here changes them."
  say "Before every match you'll see your opponent's three, and you send two of yours into a battle of one on one."
  say "Win the final and you're the champion. Lose a match, and the tournament is over for you."
  end
