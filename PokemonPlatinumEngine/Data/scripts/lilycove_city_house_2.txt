# A house in Lilycove City (plan 21 · H7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/LilycoveCity_House2/scripts.inc): the man inside gives TM44, Rest, once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_REST).


script RestMan
  face self player
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_REST goto Given
  say "Huh? I was just about to have a lie-down. Nothing beats a good rest, you know. Here, take this. Sleep on it!"
  give "TM44"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_REST
  end
label Given
  say "Rest puts a Pokémon to sleep, but it wakes up good as new. Sleep is the best medicine there is."
  end
