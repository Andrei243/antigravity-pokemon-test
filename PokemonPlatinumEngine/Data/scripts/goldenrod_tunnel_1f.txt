# The Underground passage under Goldenrod City (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D37R0101).
#
# Its two people say what they make of Team Rocket while the takeover lasts (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 2 to 4), the
# man a word of his own for a player in a borrowed uniform (3).


# D37R0101_000
script Man
  face d37r0101_gsman1 player
  speaker "Man"
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 3 goto Uniform
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 2 goto Takeover
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 4 goto Takeover
  say "Somebody downstairs wanted to battle me. It's no place for the timid down there, so watch yourself."
  end
label Takeover
  say "Team Rocket again! Can't they ever leave people in peace?"
  end
label Uniform
  say "You seem a decent sort. However did you end up with Team Rocket?"
  end


# D37R0101_001
script Woman
  face d37r0101_gswoman1 player
  speaker "Woman"
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 3 goto Uniform
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 2 goto Takeover
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER == 4 goto Takeover
  say "There are shops down the stairs, but Trainers too. I'm too nervous to go down."
  end
label Takeover
  say "Everyone who worked down here has gone. Nobody wants trouble with Team Rocket."
  end
label Uniform
  say "Oh... Of course. Team Rocket can use the passage as well as anyone."
  end
