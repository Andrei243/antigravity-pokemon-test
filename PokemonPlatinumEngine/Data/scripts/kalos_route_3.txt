# Up out of the forest onto the road (the trigger across the way up, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 6)
script Friends
  scene "Tierno and Trevor on Route 3"
  face player up
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "There you are! We came out of the forest ages ago. Trevor wanted to stop and look at every bug."
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "Th-that's how you fill a Pokédex! Every one counts."
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Anyway! My partner and I have been practising our moves. Wanna see them? In a battle, I mean!"
  battle tierno as "kalos_tierno_1" canlose
  heal
  speaker "Tierno"
  if lost goto TiernoWon
  say "Whoa, that was some groove! You and your Pokémon really move together."
  goto After
label TiernoWon
  say "We did it! Don't worry, you and your partner looked great out there too."
label After
  speaker trevor "Trevor"
  say "Santalune City is just east of here. There's a Gym, and the Leader is supposed to be really good."
  speaker "Tierno"
  say "Last one to the fountain buys the pastries!"
  walk tierno right 8 fast
  move trevor right 8 fast
  waitmoves
  hide tierno
  hide trevor
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_FRIENDS_ROUTE_3
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_RIVAL_SANTALUNE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 7
  endscene
  end
