# Pewter City (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats
# (data/maps/PewterCity/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY, as FireRed counts it: 0 until the Boulder Badge, when the Gym's guide stops whoever heads
# east for Route 3 and brings them to the Gym (the area's triggers 0 to 3); 1 the Badge won, when Oak's aide waits on
# the same road with the Running Shoes (triggers 4 to 6); 2 the shoes given. FireRed's guide walks the player round to
# the Gym's door; here the picture goes dark and comes up again before it (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kanto's first
# chapter").


# FireRed's OnTransition: the museum asks for its ticket again on every visit to the city.
script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY_MUSEUM_1F 0
  end


# The guide on the road east, before the Badge (the area's triggers 0 to 3, and the guide spoken to).
script GymGuide
  scene "To the Gym"
  face pewter_gym_guide player
  emote pewter_gym_guide exclaim
  speaker "Boy"
  say "Hey, you're a trainer, right? Brock's always looking for someone new to battle! Come on, the Gym's this way!"
  fade out
  place player 63 77 up
  place pewter_gym_guide 64 77 up
  fade in
  face pewter_gym_guide player
  say "There, that's the Gym. Go on, show Brock what you can do!"
  fade out
  place pewter_gym_guide 90 80 down
  fade in
  endscene
  end


# Oak's aide on the road east once the Badge is won (triggers 4 to 6): the Running Shoes, with a note from Mom.
script RunningShoes
  scene "The Running Shoes"
  face pewter_aide player
  emote pewter_aide exclaim
  speaker "Aide"
  say "Oh, {player}! There you are. Professor Oak asked me to bring you something."
  say "Your mother sent these to the lab for you. A pair of Running Shoes!"
  setflag FLAG_HAS_RUNNING_SHOES
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} put on the Running Shoes!"
  say "Hold the run button down as you walk, and you'll go twice as fast. There's a note with them, too."
  text "\"Don't forget to rest when you're tired, and call home now and then. Love, Mom.\""
  say "Well, I'd better be getting back to the lab. Good luck out there!"
  walk pewter_aide right 4
  hide pewter_aide
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PEWTER_CITY_RUNNING_SHOES_GUY
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY 2
  endscene
  end


# The man by the museum, who asks whether the player has seen it.
script MuseumGuide
  ask "Have you been to the Museum of Science yet? It's full of fossils!"
  if yes goto Seen
  say "You haven't? You really must. It's just over there, by the big rocks."
  end
label Seen
  say "Weren't those fossils amazing? Imagine Pokémon that lived so long ago!"
  end
