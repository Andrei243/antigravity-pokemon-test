# The Azalea Gym's hall (plan 20 · J7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's
# beats (scr_seq_T23GYM0101): the guide, before and after the Hive Badge.


script GymGuy
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge hive goto Won
  if not flag FLAG_BEAT_AZALEA_ROCKETS goto Away
  say "Yo, challenger! Bugsy's Bug types are tough, but Fire and Flying moves will scorch and blow them away."
  say "Ride the Spinarak carts across the webs to reach him. Step onto a cart's dock and off you go!"
  end
label Away
  say "Bugsy's off looking into the Slowpoke Well. Something's wrong in town, and he can't sit still till it's sorted."
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Bugsy! Those bugs didn't stand a chance. On to Goldenrod City, champ!"
  end
