# The Hotel Grand Lake's west cottage at Valor Lakefront (plan 01 · M11, part b), on the original's plan.
# docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_grand_lake_valor_lakefront_west_house.s).
#
# The game director is staying here. He asks after the Pokédex, and has more to say once every Pokémon of Sinnoh
# has been seen (210 of them). The diplomas themselves are the Pokédex's own to hand out, the first time it is
# opened once complete (plan 03 · D10; docs/mechanics/rulings.md): he sends the player to look. Having said so, he
# talks of other things until the player has gone out and come back (a flag that lasts while the player stays, as
# the original's FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_0x01).
#
# Since plan 40 · GE5 he is also one of the designers who hand over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned (charm due,
# charm give; Models/Charms.cs): any regional Pokédex seen whole, whichever region's, and the National one caught whole.
# What is due is given before anything else he says, one charm after another.


script Director
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  if diploma Ours goto OursDiploma
  if diploma Sinnoh goto OwnDiploma
  if var DIPLOMAS >= 3 goto ManyDiplomas
  if var DIPLOMAS > 0 goto AnyDiploma
  if flag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_GAME_DIRECTOR_SPOKE goto Stories
  if var SEEN >= 210 goto Complete
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "Oh! You're filling in a Pokédex, I see!" "Do come and show me again once there's more in it!"
  end
label Complete
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_GAME_DIRECTOR_SPOKE
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "Oh! You've seen every single Pokémon of Sinnoh! That's marvellous!"
  sound "levelup"
  say "Work like that deserves a diploma. Open your Pokédex: you'll find it waiting for you inside."
  end
label Stories
  say "How did you come by all the Pokémon in your Pokédex, I wonder?" "Did you work long and hard to catch and raise each one? Were some of them gifts from friends? Did a few arrive in trades from far away?" "Every Pokédex may look the same in the end. The stories inside them never are."
  end
label Charms
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "Now, let me see that Pokédex... Page after page, every one of them filled in! That earns more than a diploma."
  say "Whoever makes it this far gets a little something from all of us who made the game. Here you are!"
label GiveCharm
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto GiveCharm
  say "Keep it with you wherever you go. Fill in another Pokédex and come and show me: I may have something more for you."
  end
# Plan 40 · GE4, ours: the director reads the diplomas the Pokédex has handed out (diploma, DIPLOMAS). They sit here, after
# the lines above, so that the lines' numbers in the translation files stay where they were.
label OursDiploma
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "A diploma for the Pokémon that aren't in any region's Pokédex, and you saw every one of them! Nobody asked us for those."
  end
label OwnDiploma
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "That diploma on your Pokédex is for all of Sinnoh, every page seen with your own eyes. I could pick that paper out of a hundred!"
  end
label ManyDiplomas
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "Diplomas from several Pokédexes! You've seen more of the world than all of us who made the game put together."
  end
label AnyDiploma
  say "Hello there! I'm the game director!" "I see a diploma on your Pokédex! Other regions have lists of their own, you know. Fill in another and show me."
  end
