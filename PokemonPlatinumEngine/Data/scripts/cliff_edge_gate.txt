# The Cliff Edge Gate (plan 20 · J8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D48R0101).
#
# The woman by the board: before the Safari Zone opens (FLAG_UNK_0F5, which Warden Baoba's first call sets in
# olivine.txt) she can't wait for it; once it is open she urges the player along; once the player has walked out onto
# Route 47 (VAR_UNK_40EB 1, route_47.txt) she can tell they enjoyed it.


# D48R0101_000
script Woman
  speaker "Woman"
  if var VAR_UNK_40EB == 1 goto Been
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0F5 goto Open
  say "Have you heard? They're building a Safari Zone just past this cliff. You can catch any Pokémon you find there!"
  say "I can hardly wait. Hurry up and finish it, workmen!"
  end
label Open
  ask "The Safari Zone's open at last, just around the corner! Any Pokémon you find, you can catch. Won't you go and look?"
  if no goto No
  say "That's the spirit! Up the stairs and out onto the cliff path. It's only a short walk from there."
  end
label No
  say "Really? It's such a bargain, though..."
  end
label Been
  say "You've been to the Safari Zone, haven't you? Don't tell me, I can see it on your face. It was wonderful!"
  end


# D48R0101_003: the board by the stairs
script Board
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0F5 goto Open
  text "Warden Baoba's Safari Zone\nCOMING SOON! Wild Pokémon to find around every corner!"
  end
label Open
  text "Warden Baoba's Safari Zone\nNOW OPEN! Wild Pokémon to find around every corner!"
  end
