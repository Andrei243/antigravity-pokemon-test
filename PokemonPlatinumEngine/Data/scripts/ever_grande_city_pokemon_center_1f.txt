# Ever Grande City's Pokémon Center (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/EverGrandeCity_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Scott waits here for a player with the Feather Badge, once (FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_EVERGRANDE, which going into Sidney's room
# sets too, as Emerald's does): he has watched the player all the way from Petalburg and is glad to see them here. He
# goes out of the door and on his way (VAR_SCOTT_STATE counts his meetings, as Emerald's does).


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_TWELVE call HoennChapterTwelve
  if flag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_EVERGRANDE end
  if not badge feather end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_EVER_GRANDE_POKEMON_CENTER_1F_SCOTT
  end


script Scott
  scene "Scott in Ever Grande"
  face ever_grande_scott player
  speaker "Scott"
  say "{player}! You made it all the way to Ever Grande City! I knew it the first time I saw you battle."
  say "I've watched a great many trainers in my time, but very few of them ever stood where you're standing now."
  say "Beyond Victory Road is the Pokémon League. Go and show the Elite Four what you showed me. I'll be cheering!"
  sound "door_slide"
  hide ever_grande_scott
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_EVER_GRANDE_POKEMON_CENTER_1F_SCOTT
  setflag FLAG_MET_SCOTT_IN_EVERGRANDE
  addvar VAR_SCOTT_STATE 1
  endscene
  end
