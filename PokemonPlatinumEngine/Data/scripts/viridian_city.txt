# Viridian City (plan 19 · K1). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats
# (data/maps/ViridianCity/scripts.inc).
#
# The Gym's door is a way like any other, and what keeps it shut is FireRed's own: the step before it (the map's
# coordinate event, trigger 1 of the area, on VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_GYM_DOOR 0) turns the player back, until
# the city is come to with the Cascade, Thunder, Rainbow, Soul, Marsh and Volcano Badges, when the variable is 1.
# The old man across the road north and the lesson in catching are the story's (plan 19 · K3); the Gym's Leader and
# his trainers K7's.


# FireRed's OnTransition: ViridianCity_EventScript_TryUnlockGym, while the door is still shut.
script OnEnter
  if not var VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_GYM_DOOR == 0 end
  if not badge cascade end
  if not badge thunder end
  if not badge rainbow end
  if not badge soul end
  if not badge marsh end
  if not badge volcano end
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_GYM_DOOR 1
  end


# The step before the Gym's door (ViridianCity_EventScript_GymDoorLocked): the player looks at the doors, finds them
# locked, and goes back down the way they came.
script GymDoorLocked
  face player up
  wait 0.33
  say "The Gym's doors are locked tight. Nobody seems to be inside."
  walk player down
  end


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ plan 19 · K3: the old man of the road north
#
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_OLD_MAN, as FireRed counts it: 0 the old man lying across the road north, grumpy for
# want of his coffee (the area's trigger 0 turns the player back); 1 his coffee had, once the Pokédex is given, and
# his lesson in catching waiting on the steps before him (triggers 2 and 3); 2 the lesson given. FireRed's lesson is a
# battle the old man fights against a Weedle while the player watches; here he tells it, since this game's
# watched lesson is the assistant's own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kanto's first chapter").


# The step before the old man while he blocks the road (ViridianCity_EventScript_RoadBlocked).
script RoadBlocked
  face viridian_woman player
  speaker "Woman"
  say "Oh, I'm so sorry! That's my grandfather lying across the road. He won't budge until he's had his coffee."
  say "Could you go round another way for now?"
  walk player down 1
  end


# The old man, by what he has had.
script OldMan
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_OLD_MAN >= 1 goto Awake
  say "Grumble... This is private property! No coffee, no passing. Go away!"
  end
label Awake
  say "Ahh, I've had my coffee and I feel like a new man. Off you go, the road north is all yours."
  end


# His granddaughter, by the same.
script Granddaughter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_OLD_MAN >= 1 goto Better
  sayown
  end
label Better
  say "Grandpa's had his coffee at last. He's much nicer now, isn't he?"
  end


# The steps before him once he is awake (ViridianCity_EventScript_TutorialTrigger): he stops the player and tells
# how a Pokémon is caught.
script Tutorial
  scene "The old man's lesson"
  face tutorial_man player
  emote tutorial_man exclaim
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "Ah, there you are! You look like a trainer in a hurry. Got a Pokédex, have you?"
  say "Then listen to an old man. You can't just throw a Poké Ball at any wild Pokémon and hope."
  say "Wear it down in battle first, without making it faint. The weaker it is, the easier it is to catch."
  say "A Pokémon that's asleep or paralysed is easier still. Now go and try it for yourself!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_OLD_MAN 2
  endscene
  end
