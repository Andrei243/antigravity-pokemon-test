# Once Jasmine has asked after a medicine for Amphy (VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE 1), the pharmacist gives the Secret
# Potion once (FLAG_GOT_SECRETPOTION); every other time he sells HeartGold's five medicines (the counter
# `cianwood_pharmacy`, src/scrcmd_mart.c's list 11, tools/DataImporter/Overrides/marts.json).


# T24R0501_000
script Pharmacist
  face t24r0501_sunglasses player
  speaker "Pharmacist"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_SECRETPOTION goto Shop
  if var VAR_SCENE_LIGHTHOUSE_JASMINE != 1 goto Shop
  say "The lighthouse's Ampharos is sick? Then you'll want this. It's our own medicine, made here for generations."
  give "Secret Potion"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_SECRETPOTION
  say "One sip of the Secret Potion will set it right. Take it to the lighthouse, and hurry!"
  end
label Shop
  say "Welcome to Cianwood's pharmacy, the oldest in Johto. What can I get for you?"
  shop "cianwood_pharmacy"
  end
