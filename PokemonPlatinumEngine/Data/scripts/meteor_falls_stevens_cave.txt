# Steven's cave behind Meteor Falls (plan 21 · H12, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/MeteorFalls_StevensCave/scripts.inc).
#
# Its mouth opens in the first room once Hoenn's Hall of Fame is entered. Steven stands at the far end looking at the
# rock; spoken to, he starts, turns and battles with his own team (hoenn_steven, Emerald's TRAINER_STEVEN), and after it
# says what he had foreseen (FLAG_DEFEATED_METEOR_FALLS_STEVEN). He stays, and says it again whenever spoken to.
#
# Jirachi (plan 29 · LS11c, ours: the games gave it only by events). Once Prof. Cozmo has his meteorite back
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_RETURN), he tells a Champion of a comet that passes once in a thousand years and of a stone here
# that hums at night (fallarbor_town_cozmos_house.txt). Steven, spoken to again by day, says he is waiting up for it;
# at night the stone's hum wakes Jirachi before it at level 50. Steven stands in the way of the stone at the end of a
# passage one tile wide, so the scene puts him back down the passage and the player three tiles from it behind a
# fade, and the beat is the mind's row of plan 29: the bars, the camera to the stone, sparkles and a ripple, Jirachi
# shown hovering a tile before Steven's old one, flickering awake (no "!" over the player, which would hide so small a
# Pokémon), its cry and a line, then the battle's own way in. Caught or beaten it is gone for good
# (FLAG_HOENN_JIRACHI_MET); run from, it comes again the next night Steven is spoken to. Jirachi is a person of ours
# (the overlay's npcs), shown only by the scene (FLAG_HOENN_SHOW_JIRACHI).


script Steven
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_METEOR_FALLS_STEVEN goto Afterwards
  emote self exclaim
  wait 0.8
  face self player
  speaker "Steven"
  say "Oh! {player}, you found me here? I wonder what you take me for. Just a man who loves stones?"
  say "We fought side by side in Mossdeep's Space Center. You know what I can do."
  say "If you mean to challenge me, then come at me with everything. I won't hold back!"
  battle self as "hoenn_steven"
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_METEOR_FALLS_STEVEN
  # (plan 40 · GE11, decision 11: the three Hoenn starters' Mega Stones, once he has been beaten here)
  call StartersStones
  goto Foreseen
label Afterwards
  face self player
  speaker "Steven"
  call StartersStones
  if flag FLAG_HOENN_JIRACHI_MET goto Granted
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_RETURN goto Foreseen
  if time night goto Humming
  goto Waiting
label Foreseen
  say "Ever since we met in Granite Cave, I had a feeling you would become the Champion."
  say "My hunches tend to come true. So where will you go from here?"
  say "...Ha! Even I can't see that far."
  end
# (Jirachi's lines come after the old ones, so that those keep their numbers for the translations)
label Waiting
  say "Prof. Cozmo tells me this stone hums at night, while the comet passes. I mean to stay up and hear it."
  say "Come back after dark, {player}. I'd like you to hear it too."
  end
label Granted
  say "A Pokémon that wakes once in a thousand years... and you were the one here to meet it. I'm almost jealous."
  end
label Humming
  say "Shh... Do you hear it? The stone is humming, just as Prof. Cozmo said it would."
  scene "Jirachi wakes in Steven's cave"
  music stop
  fade out 0.3
  place meteor_falls_steven 18 7 right
  place player 19 6 up
  fade in
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 19 4 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 19 4
  effect ripple 19 4
  wait 0.4
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_SHOW_JIRACHI
  show meteor_falls_jirachi
  flicker meteor_falls_jirachi 6
  effect sparkles 19 5
  cry "Jirachi"
  speaker none
  text "The stone's hum rose into a chime, and a small Pokémon blinked awake in its glow!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Jirachi" 50
  hide meteor_falls_jirachi
  clearflag FLAG_HOENN_SHOW_JIRACHI
  if result == 1 goto Gone
  if result == 3 goto Gone
  speaker none
  text "Jirachi drifted back into the stone's glow, and the humming stopped..."
  end
label Gone
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_JIRACHI_MET
  face meteor_falls_steven player
  speaker "Steven"
  goto Granted


# Jirachi looked at before its battle has begun (it is only shown in the scene)
script Jirachi
  speaker none
  text "Jirachi floats before the humming stone, half asleep."
  end


# The Hoenn starters' Mega Stones (plan 40 · GE11, decision 11). Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire give the starter's from Steven
# on Route 120 and the other two from a stone seller on Route 114; here Steven, beaten in his cave after the Hall of Fame,
# hands over all three, once, and goes on with what he was saying.
script StartersStones
  if not flag FLAG_DEFEATED_METEOR_FALLS_STEVEN return
  if flag FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_STARTERS_STONES return
  speaker "Steven"
  say "That was a battle worth having. Here, I want you to have these. I found them on my travels, and they've waited long enough."
  give "Sceptilite"
  give "Blazikenite"
  give "Swampertite"
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_STARTERS_STONES
  say "Mega Stones for Sceptile, Blaziken and Swampert, the three Pokémon Prof. Birch gives to new Trainers. With a Key Stone, they'll answer you."
  return
