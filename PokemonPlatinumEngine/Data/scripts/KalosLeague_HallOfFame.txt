# Kalos's Hall of Fame (plan 23 · Z4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as
# they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand").
#
# One scene, played as the new Champion comes in behind Diantha, as the other regions' are: her words, the team recorded
# (halloffame), Kalos's story ended (KalosHallOfFame, the region's flag, which opens the flight on to Alola from Lumiose
# City's Pokémon Center and the National Pokédex), the scene of the team's entry, the team healed, home to the player's
# room in Vaniville Town, the game saved there and the credits. ClearGame puts the League back for a second challenge.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DIANTHA end
  scene "Kalos's Hall of Fame"
  face diantha_hof player
  speaker "Diantha"
  say "This is the Hall of Fame. Every Champion of Kalos is kept here, with the Pokémon who stood beside them."
  say "Your journey began in a little town in the south, and look where it has brought you. Let's record your team."
  endscene
  wait 0.4
  fade out 0.8
  halloffame
  setflag KalosHallOfFame
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 31
  # Plan 23 · Z4b: Lumiose's last two roads open (Lumiose Station, Azure Bay), and the professor waits at the station
  setvar VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 3
  objective kalosStation
  call ClearGame
  halloffame show
  heal
  warp "VanivilleTown_PlayersHouse_2F" 4 6 down
  save
  if result == 0 goto NotSaved
  sound "save"
  text "{player} saved the game."
  goto Roll
label NotSaved
  text "The game could not be saved."
label Roll
  credits
  end


# What a victory leaves behind: the League's rooms put back for a second challenge
script ClearGame
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_MALVA
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_SIEBOLD
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_WIKSTROM
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DRASNA
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DIANTHA
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_LEAGUE_FOUR
  return


script DianthaWaits
  speaker "Diantha"
  say "Kalos is lucky to have you, {player}."
  end
