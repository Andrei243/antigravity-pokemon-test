# A guest who dreams of travelling: each time he names one of four places far from Sinnoh, by the original's die.


script Traveller
  say "Is that so? A traveller, are you? Good for you. Everybody ought to see other places!"
  random 4
  if result == 0 goto Moon
  if result == 1 goto Tower
  if result == 2 goto Space
  say "Have you heard of the Whirl Islands, off in the Johto region?" "A Pokémon of shining silver, they say... I mean to see it before I'm through."
  end
label Moon
  say "Do you know Mt. Moon, in the Kanto region?" "Clefairy dancing under the full moon... That is a sight I must see once in my life."
  end
label Tower
  say "Have you heard of the Tin Tower, in the Johto region?" "A Pokémon in all the colours of the rainbow... I mean to see it before I'm through."
  end
label Space
  say "Do you know the Space Center, in the Hoenn region?" "A rocket climbing all the way up into space... That is a sight I must see once in my life."
  end
