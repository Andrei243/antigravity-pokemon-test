# The Mahogany Gym's last room (plan 20 · J11, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T28GYM0101).
#
# Pryce battles, gives the Glacier Badge (`givebadge glacier`), counts the Gym's five skiers and boarders beaten as
# HeartGold's SetTrainerFlag does, and gives TM07, Hail (FLAG_GOT_TM07_FROM_PRYCE). HeartGold starts Team Rocket's
# takeover of the Radio Tower once the Storm, Mineral and Glacier Badges are all held (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 1,
# which it sets as its count of the three reaches three): here whichever Leader's script gives the last of them sets
# it, so Pryce's asks after the other two (plan 20 · J11, part b gives Chuck's and Jasmine's the same lines).


# T28GYM0101_000
script Pryce
  face t28gym0101_gsleader7 player
  speaker "Pryce"
  if badge glacier goto Won
  scene "Pryce"
  say "Pokémon have lived through a great deal, as I have. I have seen many winters, and they have taught me patience."
  say "I am Pryce, the winter's harsh cold made flesh. Let us see whether you can stand it."
  battle self as "johto_leader_pryce_pryce"
  say "Hmm. Your Pokémon carried you through the cold. You have earned this: the Glacier Badge."
  givebadge glacier
  text "{player} received the Glacier Badge from Pryce!"
  defeat "johto_boarder_deandre"
  defeat "johto_boarder_gerardo"
  defeat "johto_skier_jill"
  defeat "johto_skier_diana"
  defeat "johto_boarder_patton"
  if not badge storm goto Said
  if not badge mineral goto Said
  setvar VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 1
label Said
  say "With it, your Pokémon will use Whirlpool outside of battle, and every Pokémon's Speed rises a little."
  endscene
label Tm
  say "Take this as well. It is the winter itself, in a disc."
  give "TM07"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM07_FROM_PRYCE
  say "TM07 is Hail. It brings down a storm of ice that hurts every Pokémon not of the Ice type, for five turns."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_TM07_FROM_PRYCE goto Tm
  say "When the ice breaks, spring comes. Perhaps you are a spring of sorts for these old bones."
  end
