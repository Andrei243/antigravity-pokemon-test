# The Treasure Eatery (plan 34 · PD6): Larry's gym test is the secret order at its counter, three choices made as the
# hints about the town give them (medali.txt): the grilled rice balls, lemon on the side, extra crispy. Right, the test
# is done (FLAG_PALDEA_MEDALI_TEST_DONE, which Larry asks for: Medali_GymTest.txt); wrong anywhere, the dish comes as the
# menu has it and the order may be made again. Our own lines and our own order.


script Order
  speaker "Waiter"
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_MEDALI_TEST_DONE goto Done
  say "Welcome to the Treasure Eatery! What can I get you?"
  choose "What will you order?" "Grilled rice balls" "Seafood stew" "Nothing, thanks"
  if result == 2 goto Leave
  if result == 1 goto Plain
  choose "Anything on the side?" "Lemon" "Mustard" "No side"
  if result != 0 goto Plain
  choose "And how would you like them?" "Extra crispy" "Just as they come"
  if result != 0 goto Plain
  scene "The secret order"
  text "The waiter's eyebrows went up. He leaned in and lowered his voice."
  say "Grilled rice balls, lemon on the side, extra crispy. That's the secret order. You've passed Larry's test!"
  say "Head up to the gym when you've eaten. He'll be expecting you. Probably. He's always expecting something."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_MEDALI_TEST_DONE
  endscene
  end
label Plain
  say "Coming right up, just as it is on the menu. Enjoy your meal!"
  text "It was very good. But it wasn't the secret order."
  end
label Leave
  say "Take your time. Ask around town, if you're after something special."
  end
label Done
  say "The usual? Grilled rice balls, lemon on the side, extra crispy. You're a regular now!"
  end
