# The hostess stands on the tile inside the door. As the player comes in she asks for the team's ribbons
# (common.RibbonSyndicateEntry, plan 06 · R17b: ten different ones between them, and the door is theirs for good,
# FLAG_JOINED_RIBBON_SYNDICATE); whoever hasn't them is shown out, to the tile before the door. A member finds her a
# step to the east, out of the way. The cashier behind the counter is common.RibbonSyndicateCashier. The lift in the
# back wall goes to the spa on the floor above (plan 01 · M11, part c: ResortAreaRibbonSyndicateElevator, whose car
# leads out by FLAG_RIBBON_SYNDICATE_LIFT_UPSTAIRS, a flag of our own; whoever stands on this floor has the lift down).


# The original's OnTransition (the hostess beside the way for a member) and OnFrame_CheckEntry
script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_RIBBON_SYNDICATE_LIFT_UPSTAIRS
  if flag FLAG_JOINED_RIBBON_SYNDICATE goto Member
  speaker "Hostess"
  wait 0.3
  call RibbonSyndicateEntry
  if flag FLAG_JOINED_RIBBON_SYNDICATE goto Joined
  wait 0.3
  warp "Sinnoh" 824 454 down
  end
label Joined
  face beauty left
  walk beauty right 1
  face beauty left
  end
label Member
  place beauty 16 14 down
  end


script Hostess
  speaker "Hostess"
  say "{player}! We had so looked forward to seeing you again. Do make yourself at home."
  end


script Member
  speaker "Member"
  say "To belong here says something about a person. One does not simply walk in off the beach, you know."
  end


# She has one thing to say to a boy and another to a girl
script Lady
  speaker "Lady"
  if girl goto Girl
  say "I do wish gentlemen took more trouble over how they look. A fine Pokémon deserves a trainer fit to stand beside it."
  end
label Girl
  say "A lady ought to be worth seeing beside her Pokémon. I have always said so, and I see that you agree."
  end
