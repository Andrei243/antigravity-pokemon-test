# Kiloude Station, the TMV's end of the line (plan 23, Kalos's last places). The lines are our own. Z4b's attendant stood
# on Kiloude's street; X and Y's Kiloude has a station of its own, and so does ours since: the attendant takes a pass
# holder back to Lumiose Station (LumioseCity_Station.txt's Tmv brings them here).


script Tmv
  face tmv player
  speaker "TMV Attendant"
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_TMV_PASS goto NoPass
  ask "The next train leaves for Lumiose City. Will you be travelling?"
  if no goto Later
  say "Your pass, please... Thank you. All aboard!"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "LumioseCity_Station" 12 5 down
  fade in
  end
label Later
  say "The trains run all day. Enjoy the sea air in the meantime!"
  end
label NoPass
  say "I'm sorry, the TMV is for pass holders only. Passes are given out by Professor Sycamore's laboratory in Lumiose City."
  end


script Commuter
  face self player
  speaker "Commuter"
  say "I live in Lumiose and battle in Kiloude. The train is the only quiet half hour of my day."
  end
