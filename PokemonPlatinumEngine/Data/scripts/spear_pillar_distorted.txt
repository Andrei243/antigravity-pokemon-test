# Spear Pillar as the Distortion World tears it (plan 02 · S12): the original's second map of the summit, which Mt.
# Coronet's way out leads to from the moment Giratina takes Cyrus until the player comes out at Sendoff Spring
# (FLAG_SPEAR_PILLAR_IS_DISTORTED). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_spear_pillar_distorted.s).
#
# The scene plays as the player is brought here from the other map (the original's OnFrame,
# VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DISTORTED_STATE 1): Cynthia comes up the steps too late, the three Pokémon of the lakes come to
# hold the tear, and she asks whether the player is ready to go in after Cyrus. Yes leads into the Distortion World
# (its first floor, beside the portal back); no leaves her waiting by the tear, where she asks again. State 2 from
# then on. Charon leaves Team Galactic's control room with this scene, as the original has him
# (FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_CONTROL_ROOM_CHARON).
#
# Team Galactic's four stand where they stood, facing the dark (FLAG_HIDE_SPEAR_PILLAR_DISTORTED_TEAM_GALACTIC, set
# at Sendoff Spring), and the two rifts Dialga and Palkia came through are read where they hang.
#
# Stood in for (plan 09 · L12's set pieces have no part here): the original's cutscene of the three guardians over the
# tear (its sub-scenes 4 to 6) is the three as Pokémon of the map, each coming in sparkles, the air wavering as they
# hold the tear (plan 09 · L11's effects), their cries and the camera. Uxie and Azelf are people of our own beside the
# original's Mesprit, all three behind its flag.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DISTORTED_STATE != 1 end
  place cynthia 32 34 up
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SPEAR_PILLAR_DISTORTED_CYNTHIA
  show cynthia
  camera pan 31 26 0.1
  wait 0.4
  setvar VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DISTORTED_STATE 2
  music "common/eye_champion"
  walk cynthia up 4
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "No... I'm too late."
  wait 0.3
  # The three Pokémon of the lakes come to hold the tear
  sound "warp"
  effect sparkles 31 28
  show mesprit
  cry "Mesprit"
  wait 0.6
  effect sparkles 29 28
  show uxie
  cry "Uxie"
  wait 0.6
  effect sparkles 33 28
  show azelf
  cry "Azelf"
  wait 1.0
  effect ripple 31 26
  camera shake 0.6
  wait 0.6
  call Flicker
  call Flicker
  hide mesprit
  hide uxie
  hide azelf
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SPEAR_PILLAR_DISTORTED_MESPRIT
  camera release 0.8
  face player right
  face cynthia left
  say "Forgive me. I came as fast as I could, and I missed all of it. But I can see what happened."
  face cynthia right
  wait 0.5
  face cynthia down
  wait 0.3
  face cynthia up
  say "Dialga and Palkia are gone, and Cyrus with them. What took him is the Pokémon the old books barely dare to name: Giratina."
  say "That darkness is a way into its world, the other side of ours. And look at the pillars. The two worlds are bleeding into each other. Left alone, this one will come apart."
  say "The Pokémon of the lakes are holding the tear shut as best they can. They can't hold it for long. Somebody has to go in and calm Giratina."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_CONTROL_ROOM_CHARON
  music area
  face cynthia left
  call Cynthia
  end


# Cynthia by the tear, in the scene and whenever she is spoken to afterwards (the original's
# SpearPillarDistorted_AskEnterDistortionWorld and SpearPillarDistorted_Cynthia).
script Cynthia
  face cynthia player
  speaker "Cynthia"
  ask "I'm going in after it. Will you come with me? Are you ready?"
  if no goto Wait
  say "Then we have to hurry. Stay close. Nothing in there will be the way you expect it."
  sound "warp"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "DistortionWorld1F" 34 30 down
  end
label Wait
  say "Then get ready, and be quick about it. I'll wait for you here. I won't go in without you."
  end


# The three blink out of sight and back, as they do before they are gone.
script Flicker
  hide mesprit
  hide uxie
  hide azelf
  wait 0.07
  show mesprit
  show uxie
  show azelf
  wait 0.07
  return


# Team Galactic's four, left behind (the original's SpearPillarDistorted_Jupiter, _Mars, _GruntM and _GruntF).
script Jupiter
  speaker "Jupiter"
  say "What was that thing? The shadow that... No. Don't ask me. I don't know."
  end

script Mars
  speaker "Mars"
  say "Where did he go? Where did it take him? He's coming back. He has to come back!"
  end

script Grunt
  speaker "Galactic Grunt"
  say "The boss is gone and the sky's the wrong way up. What are we supposed to do? Nobody gave any orders for this!"
  end


# The two rifts over the altar (the original's SpearPillarDistorted_RiftDialga and _RiftPalkia).
script RiftDialga
  speaker none
  say "A rift hangs in the air. Standing near it, a moment seems to last too long, and then not long enough."
  end

script RiftPalkia
  speaker none
  say "A rift hangs in the air. Standing near it, the far wall seems close enough to touch, and your own hand a long way off."
  end
