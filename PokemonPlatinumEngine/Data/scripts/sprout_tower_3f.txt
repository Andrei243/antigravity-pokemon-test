# Sprout Tower's top floor (plan 20 · J4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D15R0103).
#
# The red-haired boy has just beaten the Elder as the player comes up (HeartGold's D15R0103_001, the area's trigger 0,
# on VAR_UNK_40A4 0): he scorns the Elder's lesson and leaves by an Escape Rope. The Elder battles for TM70, Flash,
# which brings the Gym's guide up the hall (FLAG_GOT_TM70; the guide's two flags).


script RivalLeaves
  scene "Sprout Tower's top"
  show d15r0103_gsrivel
  speaker "Elder Li"
  say "You are strong, young man. But you treat your Pokémon as tools. Strength like that has nowhere to grow."
  face d15r0103_gsrivel down
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Spare me the sermon, old man. Strength is all that counts. I won, didn't I?"
  emote d15r0103_gsrivel exclaim
  say "...You again. Don't get in my way. I've got no time for a weakling like you."
  text "{rival} used an Escape Rope!"
  hide d15r0103_gsrivel
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SPROUT_TOWER_RIVAL
  setvar VAR_UNK_40A4 1
  endscene
  end


# Elder Li at the top of the tower.
script Elder
  face d15r0103_chourou player
  speaker "Elder Li"
  if defeated "johto_elder_li" goto Beaten
  scene "Elder Li"
  say "Welcome to the top of Sprout Tower. You have climbed far, and your Pokémon climbed beside you."
  say "The pillar at the tower's heart sways with every wind, and so it never falls. Let me see if your bond is as supple."
  battle self as "johto_elder_li"
  say "Splendid. You and your Pokémon move as one. Take this, and may it light your way."
  give "TM70"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM70
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VIOLET_GYM_GYM_GUY_BEFORE_SPROUT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VIOLET_GYM_GYM_GUY_AFTER_SPROUT
  say "TM70 holds Flash. It lights a dark cave and dazzles a foe. Falkner awaits you at the Gym. Go to him with my blessing."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "Do not forget: the bond between you and your Pokémon is the strongest pillar of all."
  end
