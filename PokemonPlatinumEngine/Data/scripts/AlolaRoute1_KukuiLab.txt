# Professor Kukui's laboratory on the beach of the Hau'oli Outskirts (plan 23 · Z5). The lines are our own, on Sun and
# Moon's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Alola's first chapter"). VAR_ALOLA_STORY is
# alola_route_1.txt's count. The professor is hidden by FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_KUKUI_LAB until the kahuna's scene in Iki Town.


# The professor: the Pokédex and five Poké Balls once the first Pokémon is had, the way to Hau'oli City after
script Kukui
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 6 goto After
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY < 5 goto Before
  scene "The professor's gift"
  say "Alola! Welcome to my lab. Don't mind the hole in the roof. Moves get tested in here, and some of them hit hard!"
  say "Here, this is for you. A Pokédex: it writes down every Pokémon you meet, all over the islands."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex!"
  say "And you'll want these to catch the Pokémon you meet. Take five, on me."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "Hau'oli City is east along the shore. Have a look round, cousin. The whole island's open to you now!"
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 6
  objective alolaHauoli
  endscene
  end
label Before
  say "Alola! You'll want to see the kahuna in Iki Town first."
  end
label After
  say "Every move a Pokémon uses tells me something. Go and see as many as you can, yeah?"
  end
