# Professor Kukui's laboratory on the beach of the Hau'oli Outskirts (plan 23 · Z5). The lines are our own, on Sun and
# Moon's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Alola's first chapter"). VAR_ALOLA_STORY is
# alola_route_1.txt's count. The professor is hidden by FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_KUKUI_LAB until the kahuna's scene in Iki Town.


# The professor: the Pokédex and five Poké Balls once the first Pokémon is had, the way to Hau'oli City after
script Kukui
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 6 goto After
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY < 5 goto Before
  scene "The professor's gift"
  say "Alola! Welcome to my lab. Don't mind the hole in the roof. Moves get tested in here, and some of them hit hard!"
  say "Here, this is for you. A Pokédex: it writes down every Pokémon you meet, all over the islands."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex!"
  say "And you'll want these to catch the Pokémon you meet. Take five, on me."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "Hau'oli City is east along the shore. Have a look round, cousin. The whole island's open to you now!"
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 6
  objective alolaHauoli
  endscene
  end
label Before
  say "Alola! You'll want to see the kahuna in Iki Town first."
  end
label After
  if flag AlolaHallOfFame goto Champion
  say "Every move a Pokémon uses tells me something. Go and see as many as you can, yeah?"
  end
# Plan 23, Alola finished: after the Hall of Fame the professor hands over the three partners' own Z-Crystals, once
# (FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_STARTER_CRYSTALS), and, once the bag holds a Key Stone, the stones of the Megas of our own whose line
# is Alola's (docs/plans/decisions/R20b-mega-proposals.md, "Where the stones are found"; FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_MEGA_STONES)
label Champion
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_STARTER_CRYSTALS goto Stones
  say "Champion! Woo! I still can't get used to saying it. My lab's roof is in pieces from celebrating."
  say "These were made for the three partners of the islands. A Z-Crystal for each of their final moves, all in one go!"
  give "Decidium Z"
  give "Incinium Z"
  give "Primarium Z"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_STARTER_CRYSTALS
  say "Whichever partner you raised, it's got a move nobody else has now. Show me, yeah?"
label Stones
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_MEGA_STONES goto Done
  if not item "Key Stone" goto Done
  say "Hold on... a Key Stone? Mega Evolution, out here in Alola? Then these three stones finally make sense!"
  say "They washed up on the beach out front, one by one, each warm whenever a partner Pokémon came near."
  give "Decidueyite"
  give "Incineroarite"
  give "Primarinite"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_MEGA_STONES
  say "One for each of the three. Whichever you raised, it can go further still. I can't wait to take one of those hits!"
  end
label Done
  say "Every move a Pokémon uses tells me something. Even a Champion's. Especially a Champion's!"
  end
