# A house of Accumula Town (plan 40 · GE2: the Lass's two trades). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (accumula_town_2.s, read for the order). The Lass swaps her Ambipom for an Excadrill, and on the next
# talk her Alakazam for a Hippowdon, once each (NpcTrades "ambidexter" and "beardy"). The original's battles with the
# Pokémon she was given back are not built.


script TradeLass
  speaker "Lass"
  if flag FLAG_TRADED_FOR_BEARDY goto AllDone
  if flag FLAG_TRADED_FOR_AMBIDEXTER goto Second
  ask "Do you have an Excadrill? I'd swap my Ambipom for one. Let's trade!"
  if no goto Waiting1
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Waiting1
  trade ambidexter
  if result == 0 goto Wrong1
  setflag FLAG_TRADED_FOR_AMBIDEXTER
  say "Great! Ambidexter knows Nasty Plot. I'll make your Excadrill really strong."
  end
label Wrong1
  say "That isn't an Excadrill. It's an Excadrill I want."
  end
label Waiting1
  say "I see. That's your trusty partner. If you change your mind, let's trade."
  end
label Second
  ask "Oh! Could you give me a Hippowdon, too? I'd trade you my Alakazam for it!"
  if no goto Waiting2
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Waiting2
  trade beardy
  if result == 0 goto Wrong2
  setflag FLAG_TRADED_FOR_BEARDY
  say "Wonderful! Beardy knows Psycho Cut. I'll make your Hippowdon really tough."
  end
label Wrong2
  say "That isn't a Hippowdon. It's a Hippowdon I want."
  end
label Waiting2
  say "I see. Come back if you change your mind about swapping."
  end
label AllDone
  say "I'm so glad we got to trade Pokémon! Ambidexter and Beardy are lucky to have you."
  end
