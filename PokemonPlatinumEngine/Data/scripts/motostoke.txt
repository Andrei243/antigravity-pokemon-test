# Motostoke (plan 33 · GL4), the city of the opening ceremony, on the open world's engine: its places are
# Data/world3d/galar/places/motostoke.json. The lines are our own, on Sword and Shield's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Motostoke, the opening ceremony and Turffield"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is
# postwick.txt's count: 13 up the steps, 14 off to register, 15 registered, 16 the morning of the ceremony, 17 the gym
# challenge open, 18 Bede beaten in the Galar Mine, 19 the Grass Badge; and (GL4's second session) 22 Bede beaten in
# Galar Mine No. 2, 23 Marnie beaten by the east wall, 24 the Fire Badge.


# Up the steps for the first time: Hop, waiting at the top with the news
script HopInMotostoke
  scene "Hop in Motostoke"
  faceto player hop_motostoke
  speaker "Hop"
  say "There you are! Isn't it brilliant? Smoke and gears and stone, and the stadium sat on top of it all."
  say "Registration for the gym challenge is at the stadium. Up the ramp! Let's go and make it official!"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_IN_MOTOSTOKE
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_IN_LOBBY
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BEDE_IN_LOBBY
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 14
  endscene
  end


# The west gate onto Route 3: open to challengers once the ceremony is over
script WestGate
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 17 end
  faceto player west_gate_staffer
  speaker "League Staff"
  say "Route 3's for challengers, and the challenge doesn't open until after the ceremony. See you on the other side!"
  walkto player motostoke_west_gate_back
  end


# Plan 33 · GL4, its second session: Galar Mine No. 2's way out in the east wall, for challengers with the Water Badge
script MineTwoShut
  if badge water end
  faceto player mine_2_staffer_south
  speaker "League Staff"
  say "Galar Mine No. 2 runs north under the banks to Hulbury, but it's for challengers with the Water Badge. Turffield first!"
  walkto player galar_mine_2_back_south
  end


# Out of Galar Mine No. 2 with the Water Badge: Marnie, waiting by the east wall, and her battle
script MarnieInMotostoke
  scene "Marnie in Motostoke"
  walkto player marnie_spot
  faceto player marnie_motostoke
  speaker "Marnie"
  say "You're the one Team Yell keep moaning about. Sorry about them. They mean well. Mostly."
  say "I'm Marnie, from Spikemuth. I'm in the challenge to win it, same as you. Let's see if they're right to worry."
  battle marnie_motostoke as "galar_marnie_1" canlose
  speaker "Marnie"
  say "Hm. You're tougher than you look. Kabu's gym is up at the stadium. I'll see you there, or after."
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_MARNIE_IN_MOTOSTOKE
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 23
  objective galarKabu
  endscene
  end
