# Motostoke (plan 33 · GL4), the city of the opening ceremony, on the open world's engine: its places are
# Data/world3d/galar/places/motostoke.json. The lines are our own, on Sword and Shield's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Motostoke, the opening ceremony and Turffield"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is
# postwick.txt's count: 13 up the steps, 14 off to register, 15 registered, 16 the morning of the ceremony, 17 the gym
# challenge open, 18 Bede beaten in the Galar Mine, 19 the Grass Badge.


# Up the steps for the first time: Hop, waiting at the top with the news
script HopInMotostoke
  scene "Hop in Motostoke"
  faceto player hop_motostoke
  speaker "Hop"
  say "There you are! Isn't it brilliant? Smoke and gears and stone, and the stadium sat on top of it all."
  say "Registration for the gym challenge is at the stadium. Up the ramp! Let's go and make it official!"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_IN_MOTOSTOKE
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_IN_LOBBY
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BEDE_IN_LOBBY
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 14
  endscene
  end


# The west gate onto Route 3: open to challengers once the ceremony is over
script WestGate
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 17 end
  faceto player west_gate_staffer
  speaker "League Staff"
  say "Route 3's for challengers, and the challenge doesn't open until after the ceremony. See you on the other side!"
  walkto player motostoke_west_gate_back
  end
