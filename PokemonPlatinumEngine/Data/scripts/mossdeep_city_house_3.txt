# A house of Mossdeep City (plan 21 · H8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MossdeepCity_House3/scripts.inc). The fisherman gives the Super Rod once
# (FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD).


script SuperRod
  face self player
  speaker "Fisherman"
  if flag FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD goto Given
  ask "Hey there, trainer! A Super Rod is truly super, you know. Do you want one?"
  if no goto Declined
  say "Ha! A trainer after my own heart. Here you go!"
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_RECEIVED_SUPER_ROD
  say "Drop it into any water you like, and see what bites. The deeper the water, the bigger the catch!"
  end
label Given
  say "Go after the Pokémon of the sea floor with that rod of yours!"
  end
label Declined
  say "What, don't you like fishing?"
  end
