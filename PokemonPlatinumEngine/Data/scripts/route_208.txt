# The black belt by the Berry Master's fence (the original's script 4) has no use for the odd stone he dug up, and
# gives it to the player once (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_208_ODD_KEYSTONE). Its place is the tower of stones on Route 209.
script BlackBelt
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_208_ODD_KEYSTONE goto Given
  say "I dug up an odd stone on my travels. It's heavier than it looks, and it turns cold at night."
  say "It gives me the shivers. Go on, you have it!"
  give "Odd Keystone"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_208_ODD_KEYSTONE
  speaker self
label Given
  say "There's a little tower of stones on Route 209 with one missing. If you ask me, that stone belongs there."
  end
