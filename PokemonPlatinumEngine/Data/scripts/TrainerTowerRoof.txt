# The Trainer Tower's roof (plan 37 · FC6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the beats of
# FireRed's Trainer Tower (TrainerTower_Roof), read for the order alone. The owner tells the climb's time, gives the
# challenge's prize once (its first floor's, FireRed's GiveChallengePrize) and holds the time against the best.


script Owner
  face self player
  speaker trainer_tower_owner "Owner"
  facility trainertower climbing
  if result == 3 goto Climbed
  goto Visit
label Climbed
  say "You made it to the roof! All eight floors in {tower:TIME}."
  facility trainertower prize
  facility trainertower record
  if result == 0 goto Record
  if result == 2 goto Done
  say "A fine climb, though your best for this challenge still stands at {tower:BEST}."
  goto Done
label Record
  say "That's a new best for this challenge! I'll write it in the records downstairs."
label Done
  say "Take the stairs down when you're ready. The attendant will see to your Pokémon."
  end
label Visit
  say "The wind up here does a climber good. Come and see me after a climb!"
  end
