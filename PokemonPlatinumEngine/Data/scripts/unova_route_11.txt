  end


# Virizion on the road (0; plan 22 · U10, part a): once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered it waits there, Black 2's level 65
# (route_11.s), and is gone for good once caught or beaten (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_VIRIZION); run from, it is back next time.
script Virizion
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  face self player
  cry "Virizion"
  speaker none
  text "Virizion called out, light and sharp!\nIt watches {player} with calm, keen eyes!"
  wildbattle "Virizion" 65
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_VIRIZION
  hide self
  text "The grass by the road sways, and nothing more..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Virizion slipped away into the trees..."
  end
