# Lake Verity, as the game finds it before Team Galactic comes (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_lake_verity_low_water.s).
#
# The first visit with the rival: a man in grey stands by the shore and speaks to the lake, names himself and
# walks away; then something cries out over the water, and the rival, with no Poké Balls, races off to the
# professor's lab.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_VISITED_LAKE_VERITY_WITH_RIVAL != 0 end
  show rival
  place rival 47 53 up
  wait 0.4
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Huh? Who's that, down by the water?"
  camera pan 48 44 1.0
  wait 0.5
  speaker "???"
  say "......"
  say "Time flows on without end, and space stretches on without limit... And one day, both will answer to me."
  say "My name is Cyrus."
  say "You who sleep beneath this lake. Sleep on, while you still can."
  camera release 0.8
  walk cyrus down 5 left 1 down 3
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "Step aside."
  walk rival right 1
  walk cyrus down 2
  hide cyrus
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_CYRUS
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...What was that about? What a weird guy."
  say "Forget him! The legendary Pokémon! Let's find it and catch it!"
  cry "Mesprit"
  text "Something cried out, somewhere far out over the lake..."
  emote rival exclaim
  walk rival up 2 fast
  walk rival down 2 fast
  face rival player
  say "Did you hear that? That had to be it! ...Oh, no. We don't have any Poké Balls!"
  say "Right! We'll ask Professor Rowan for some. Race you to his lab in Sandgem! Last one there pays the fine!"
  walk rival left 1 down 1 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_RIVAL
  setvar VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE 4
  setvar VAR_VISITED_LAKE_VERITY_WITH_RIVAL 1
  end
