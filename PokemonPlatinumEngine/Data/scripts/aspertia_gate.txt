# The gate between Aspertia City and Route 19 (plan 22 · U5). The lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/aspertia_gate.s). Its guard stops whoever walks north through it the first time:
# with no Pokémon he sends them back into town, and with one he gives them a Potion for the road
# (VAR_UNOVA_GATE 1 once given).


# The row across the gate's floor (the room's trigger 0, on 0)
script GateGift
  face 0 player
  speaker "Guard"
  if var PARTY_COUNT == 0 goto NoPokemon
  scene "The guard's gift"
  say "Hold on, {player}! You can't go out there without... Oh? You've got a Pokémon with you!"
  say "Well then, have a going-away present. Don't be shy, take it!"
  give "Potion"
  say "When your Pokémon get hurt, take it easy and stop in at a Pokémon Center."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_GATE 1
  endscene
  end
label NoPokemon
  say "Hold on, {player}! Wild Pokémon live in the grass out there. Don't go without a Pokémon of your own!"
  walk player down 1
  end


script Guard
  speaker "Guard"
  say "Beyond this gate is Route 19. Floccesy Town is a short walk down the hill."
  end
