# Iono's battle for the Electric Badge. She Terastallizes her Mismagius into an Electric type. Her levels are fixed,
# whatever order the gyms are taken in (plan 34 · decision 3)
script Leader
  face iono player
  speaker "Iono"
  if badge electric goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  scene "Iono"
  say "Hey hey, you found every last one of my hiders! Welcome to the stream, everyone! I'm Iono, Levincia's gym leader and streamer!"
  say "Today's challenger is a real one, chat! Let's see if they can survive my electrifying team!"
  battle iono as "paldea_iono"
  say "No way! The views on this one are going to be off the charts. You've earned the Electric Badge!"
  givebadge electric
  text "{player} received the Electric Badge from Iono!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_LEVINCIA_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_volt"
  say "Don't forget to like and subscribe! And come back for a rematch some time!"
  endscene
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "A challenger who isn't a student yet? The chat says enrol at the academy first! Then come back, okay?"
  end
label Won
  say "My stream's still buzzing about our battle. Thanks for the views!"
  end
