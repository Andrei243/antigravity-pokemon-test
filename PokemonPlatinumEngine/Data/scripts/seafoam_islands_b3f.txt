# The Seafoam Islands, B3F (plan 19 · K6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the beats are FireRed's
# (data/maps/SeafoamIslands_B3F/scripts.inc).
#
# A current runs south through this floor and sweeps whoever lands in it down to the floor below (the GBA's currents
# are FieldMovement.Carries; the overlay's ways out at its foot are where FireRed's script warps the player). Once the
# two boulders dropped from B2F lie across its mouth, the current is still: FireRed lays the floor out again as calm
# water (FLAG_STOPPED_SEAFOAM_B3F_CURRENT, which world.json's swap reads). Two of the four boulders on the floor's
# west side go down its holes to B4F, and the first boulder there shows for the one by the western hole, the second
# for the one by the eastern; the other two only fall.


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_STOPPED_SEAFOAM_B3F_CURRENT end
  if flag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_B3F_BOULDER_1 end
  if flag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_B3F_BOULDER_2 end
  setflag FLAG_STOPPED_SEAFOAM_B3F_CURRENT
  end

script FallFromB3F
  hide self
  setflag own
  if subject "pushable_boulder_6" clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_B4F_BOULDER_1
  if subject "pushable_boulder_3" clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_B4F_BOULDER_2
  sound "bump"
  speaker none
  text "The boulder dropped through the hole to the floor below!"
  end
