# The gate between Eterna City and the Cycling Road (plan 01 · M11, part d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_route_206_cycling_road_north_gate.s).
#
# The Cycling Road lies south of the room. A row of the lane just past the gate keepers (the original's trigger at
# y 8, while VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 is 0) turns back anyone on foot; a rider is waved through and can't get off the
# Bicycle from there to the road (FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE, which BicycleRules asks). The row before it (y 6, while
# the variable is 1) lets a rider coming back off again. Whoever comes in from the road is riding and stays so until
# that row (the original's OnFrame script). The door to the road is for riders only as well (the map file's
# "cyclistsOnly"), which is what puts the player on the road.
#
# The professor's assistant by the door gives the Exp. Share once to whoever has seen 35 Pokémon
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_NORTH_GATE_EXP_SHARE), and is gone the next time the room is come into.


# Route206CyclingRoadNorthGate_OnTransition and _OnFrame_TryForceBiking
script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_NORTH_GATE_EXP_SHARE setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_NORTH_GATE_SCIENTIST_M
  if var PLAYER_Y >= 12 setflag FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE
  end


# Route206CyclingRoadNorthGate_CoordEvent_OnlyBicycles
script OnlyBicycles
  if var CYCLING == 1 goto Riding
  emote cashier_m_west exclaim
  speaker "Gate Keeper"
  say "Hold on! The Cycling Road is for Bicycles and nothing else. Come back riding one!"
  walk player up 1
  end
label Riding
  setflag FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 1
  end


# Route206CyclingRoadNorthGate_CoordEvent_ClearFlagForceBikingInGate
script OffTheRoad
  clearflag FLAG_FORCE_BIKING_IN_GATE
  setvar VAR_MAP_LOCAL_0x02 0
  end


# Route206CyclingRoadNorthGate_ScientistM
script Assistant
  speaker "Assistant"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_NORTH_GATE_EXP_SHARE goto Given
  say "Hello, {player}! I'm one of Professor Rowan's assistants. How is the Pokédex coming along?" "Let me see how many Pokémon you've met so far..."
  if var SEEN < 35 goto TooFew
  say "{var:SEEN} already! That's splendid. I have something here for a Trainer like you."
  give "Exp. Share"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_206_CYCLING_ROAD_NORTH_GATE_EXP_SHARE
  end
label TooFew
  say "{var:SEEN}... Hmm. Come and see me once you've met at least 35. I'll have something for you then."
  end
label Given
  say "A Pokémon holding the Exp. Share gets a share of the Exp. Points from a battle, even if it never set foot in it."
  end
