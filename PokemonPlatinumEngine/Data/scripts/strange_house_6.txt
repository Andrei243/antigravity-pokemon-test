# The Strange House's cellar (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/strange_house_6.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_STRANGE is the house's count: 1 after the girl's first walk (strange_house_3.txt), 2 after this one.
#
# As the player steps along the middle of the cellar (our trigger at 17, 19 to 19, at 1) the girl (0) is in the room a second time,
# nearer the stairs to the attic, saying her words again before she goes.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  end


script TheGirlComesBack
  scene "The girl comes back"
  wait 0.5
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_STRANGE_GIRL
  place 0 18 16 up
  show 0
  wait 0.4
  walk 0 up 3
  speaker "Girl"
  say "The house is so big, and so dark. Is anyone there? Is anyone looking for me?"
  say "Up there it's quiet. The quiet's where they leave the things they don't want to lose."
  wait 0.4
  hide 0
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_STRANGE_GIRL
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_STRANGE 2
  endscene
  end
