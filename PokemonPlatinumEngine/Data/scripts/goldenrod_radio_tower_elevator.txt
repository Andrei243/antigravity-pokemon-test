# The Radio Tower's lift (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D23R0107).
#
# The lift joins the fifth floor and the observation deck, and carries whoever comes in to the other end at once, as
# the Glitter Lighthouse's does (olivine_lighthouse_elevator.txt): the two ends each say so as they are come to
# (VAR_RADIO_TOWER_LIFT_FLOOR, a variable of our own: 0 set by the fifth floor's arrival script, 1 by the deck's).


script OnEnter
  walk player up 2
  face player down
  if var VAR_RADIO_TOWER_LIFT_FLOOR == 1 goto Down
  anim elevator up 2
  text "The lift has reached the observation deck."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Goldenrod_RadioTower_ObservationDeck" 8 13 down
  end
label Down
  anim elevator down 2
  text "The lift has reached the fifth floor."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Goldenrod_RadioTower_5F" 13 3 down
  end
