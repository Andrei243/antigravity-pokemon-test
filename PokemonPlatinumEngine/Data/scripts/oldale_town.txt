# Oldale Town (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/OldaleTown/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_OLDALE_TOWN_STATE 0: the man studying footprints on the west road keeps everyone off it until the Pokédex is had
# (FLAG_ADVENTURE_STARTED); 1 the road is free. VAR_OLDALE_RIVAL_STATE 1: the rival, back from Route 103, waits at the
# south edge of town and runs on ahead to the lab.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_ADVENTURE_STARTED end
  setvar VAR_OLDALE_TOWN_STATE 1
  end


# The west road before the Pokédex (the area's trigger 0, on VAR_OLDALE_TOWN_STATE 0)
script BlockedPath
  face footprints_man player
  emote footprints_man exclaim
  speaker "Man"
  say "Aaah! Stop right there! Don't step on these footprints!"
  say "I'm sketching them. A rare Pokémon must have come through here, and I won't have them smudged!"
  walk player right 1
  end


script FootprintsMan
  if flag FLAG_ADVENTURE_STARTED goto Done
  say "Look at these tracks! Three toes, a long stride... Whatever made them was in a hurry."
  end
label Done
  say "I've finished my sketches. You can go west now, toward Petalburg City. Mind the grass on Route 102!"
  end


# The clerk who walks new trainers to the Mart and hands them a Potion (Emerald's MartEmployee, without the walk)
script MartEmployee
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_POTION_OLDALE goto Explain
  say "Hi there! I work at the Poké Mart. That's the shop with the blue roof, right over there."
  say "Here's a little something to try. Think of it as a free sample!"
  give "Potion"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_POTION_OLDALE
  end
label Explain
  say "A Potion heals a Pokémon that's been hurt in battle. The Mart sells them, and lots else besides!"
  end


# The rival at the south edge of town, back from Route 103 (triggers 1 to 3, on VAR_OLDALE_RIVAL_STATE 1)
script RivalWaiting
  face oldale_rival player
  face player oldale_rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! I'm going on ahead to the lab. Dad's waiting for us both. Don't dawdle!"
  walk oldale_rival down 3 fast
  hide oldale_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLDALE_TOWN_RIVAL
  setvar VAR_OLDALE_RIVAL_STATE 2
  end


script Girl
  say "Oldale's a stop on the way to bigger places. Everyone passes through, but hardly anyone stays."
  end
