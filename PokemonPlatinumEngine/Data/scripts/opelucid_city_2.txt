# Drayden's house (plan 22 · U8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/opelucid_city_2.s, read for the order alone). Once the Legend Badge is won (VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID 2,
# FLAG_UNOVA_DRAYDEN_AT_HOME) Drayden waits here and tells the tale of the two dragons; the ice falls as he finishes (3) and the
# player is put at the door, where opelucid_city.txt plays the rest.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpC
  if var VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID == 2 call TheTale
  end


script TheTale
  scene "The tale of the two dragons"
  walk player up 3
  face 0 player
  speaker "Drayden"
  say "Let me tell you the story. It is a long one, so listen closely."
  say "Two years ago, two dragon Pokémon were awakened. The white one, Reshiram, sought what is true, with the desire to bring the world to its ideal."
  say "The black one, Zekrom, pursued what is ideal, with the desire to make that truth real. Each was chosen by a hero, and each hero was sure of his own way."
  say "You may wonder why there were two. The single dragon had helped twin heroes bring a new region into being. But the heroes quarrelled over truth and ideals, and the dragon split in two."
  say "As the story goes, a third dragon, Kyurem, also came into existence in that age: a void left behind when the one became two, hungry for what the others took."
  say "Oh, the DNA Splicers are stored very safely. I guard them because I don't know what kind of power may lie within them. But here, I'll show you..."
  camera shake 0.8
  sound "bump"
  text "Boom!"
  emote 0 exclaim
  say "Hm? What was that sound?"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_DRAYDEN_AT_HOME
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID 3
  warp "Unova" 418 160 down
  endscene
  end
