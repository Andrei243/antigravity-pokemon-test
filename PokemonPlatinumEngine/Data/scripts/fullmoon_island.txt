# Fullmoon Island (plan 01 · M10, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_fullmoon_island.s).
#
# Arriving sets what the original's does: the island counts as come to, and it shows on the maps from then on (the
# original's EnableHiddenLocation: VAR_HIDDEN_LOCATION_FULL_MOON_ISLAND holds its number for the island, 0x0208).
#
# The sailor on the pier sails back to Canalave City, to the pier by Sailor Eldritch's boat. He is Sailor Eldritch,
# who brought the player (canalave_city.Ferry, once his son lies in his nightmare: plan 02 · S15, part b; the
# original's TakeShipFromCanalave lands the player at 39, 277, facing west) and waits by his boat: whoever comes back
# to him carrying the Lunar Wing is thanked and sailed home without being asked.

script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_FULLMOON_ISLAND
  setvar VAR_HIDDEN_LOCATION_FULL_MOON_ISLAND 520
  end


script Ferry
  face self player
  # FullmoonIsland_ThatsTheLunarWing: he sails back at once for whoever carries it
  if item "Lunar Wing" goto Wing
  ask "Seen all you came to see? Shall we sail back to Canalave City?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Aye, aye! Anchors aweigh!"
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 44 750 left
  end
label Stay
  say "All right, then. Tell me when you've had enough of the quiet."
  end
label Wing
  say "So the Pokémon you met in there was the one they call the Lunar Pokémon. I've heard tell of it, flying from one end of Sinnoh to the other."
  say "And that shine in your bag... Then that's the Lunar Wing. Thank you. Thank you! It'll wake my boy. Aboard with you, quick!"
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Sinnoh" 44 750 left
  end
