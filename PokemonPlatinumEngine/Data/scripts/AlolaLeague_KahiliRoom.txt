# Kahili's room of the Alola Pokémon League (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ula'ula,
# Poni, Aether Paradise and the League"): the last of the Elite Four, Flying, a golfer of Hau'oli's links. Her team is
# alola_kahili_league; beaten, FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_KAHILI opens the door to the Champion's room.


script League
  face kahili player
  speaker "Kahili"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_KAHILI goto Won
  if flag AlolaHallOfFame goto Rematch
  scene "Kahili"
  say "Welcome, {player}. I'm Kahili, the last of the Elite Four. I fly, and so do my Pokémon, and so does my golf ball."
  say "Flying types are just a matter of freedom. See if you can keep up with mine!"
  battle self as "alola_kahili_league"
  say "A hole in one! ...For you, I mean. Not for me. Well played."
  say "The Champion's door is open. I think you'll recognise the Trainer waiting inside."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_KAHILI
  endscene
  end
# The second climb and every one after it (the wave after Ultra Space): the rematch team (alola_kahili_rematch)
label Rematch
  scene "KahiliRematch"
  say "The Champion on the tee again. My Pokémon have been flying higher since you beat us. Let's see how high."
  battle self as "alola_kahili_rematch"
  say "Another hole in one, and still not mine. The Champion's room is open. Someone is waiting to take your title."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_KAHILI
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Go on. The Champion is waiting for you."
  end
