# Sootopolis City's Poké Mart (plan 21 · H8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/SootopolisCity_Mart/scripts.inc). The clerk sells
# Emerald's stock for the town (marts.json's sootopolis). The man by the shelves won't go out while the titans are in
# the city.


script SootopolisClerk
  face self player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome! What can I get for you?"
  shop "sootopolis"
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end


script FatMan
  face self player
  speaker "Man"
  if not flag FLAG_LEGENDARIES_IN_SOOTOPOLIS goto Calm
  say "What... what's going on out there? I'd love to know, but it's too frightening to go and look."
  end
label Calm
  say "A PP Up is a wonderful thing. It raises how many times a Pokémon can use one of its moves!"
  end
