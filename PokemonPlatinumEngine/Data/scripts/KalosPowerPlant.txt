# and cut Lumiose City's power; two grunts guard the hall and Aliana, one of the team's scientists, the machines at its
# head. Beating her puts the power back: VAR_KALOS_STORY 23 becomes 24, and VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 2 opens the road

script Aliana
  face aliana player
  speaker "Aliana"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 23 goto Early
  scene "Aliana at the Power Plant"
  say "Oh? A child, all the way out here. Team Flare is borrowing this plant's power, and we don't share."
  say "Every watt goes to a far more beautiful purpose than lighting shop windows. Now run along."
  say "No? Then I'll have to make you."
  battle self as "kalos_aliana_1"
  say "Hmph. Fine. We've taken what we needed anyway. Enjoy your shop windows."
  hide aliana
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_ALIANA_PLANT
  defeat "kalos_flare_grunt_plant_1"
  defeat "kalos_flare_grunt_plant_2"
  speaker "Worker"
  say "You chased them off! I'll have the generators running again in no time. Lumiose will be lit by tonight!"
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 24
  setvar VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 2
  objective kalosClemont
  endscene
  end

  say "Run along, child. Team Flare has business here that has nothing to do with you."
  end
