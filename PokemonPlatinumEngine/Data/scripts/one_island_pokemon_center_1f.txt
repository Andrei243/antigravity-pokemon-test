# One Island's Pokémon Center (plan 19 · K7, K9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/OneIsland_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# The first time the player comes in with Bill (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_HARBOR 3, one_island.txt) the two meet Celio
# at his network machine: Bill brings him up to speed, sets to work on it with him, and Celio gives the player the
# Tri-Pass for the Seagallop (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 1). Since plan 19 · K9 Bill also hands over
# the Meteorite for the Joyful Game Corner on Two Island (FLAG_GOT_METEORITE_FROM_BILL, a flag of ours; a game that met
# Celio before is given it when Bill is next spoken to). Once the Meteorite has been delivered
# (two_island_joyful_game_corner.txt sets the variable to 2) the machine works, and the first step towards Bill sends
# the two of them home to Cinnabar Island by the Seagallop (3; cinnabar_island.txt plays the rest).
#
# Celio alone (plan 19 · K9, part a): with the National Pokédex he asks for the Ruby from Mt. Ember (4, which opens
# the Ruby Path and puts Team Rocket's two at Mt. Ember's foot, VAR_MAP_SCENE_MT_EMBER_EXTERIOR 1), and for it he
# gives the Rainbow Pass for all seven islands and asks after the Sapphire (5). The Sapphire won back from Team Rocket
# (plan 19 · K9, part b: six_island_dotted_hole_sapphire_room.txt, five_island_rocket_warehouse.txt) completes the
# machine, which links the islands with Hoenn (6, FLAG_SYS_CAN_LINK_WITH_RS: the Elite Four and the Champion take their
# second teams, Cerulean Cave's guard goes, Lorelei goes back to the League). FireRed's roaming beast is part c's.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 setflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_BILL
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_CELIO
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F >= 3 setflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_BILL
  if not var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_HARBOR == 3 end
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 goto Meet
  end
label Meet
  scene "Bill and Celio"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_BILL
  show one_island_pokemon_center_bill
  walk player right 5 up 1
  speaker "Bill"
  say "Hey there, Celio! Long time no see."
  face celio player
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Bill! You really came all the way out here? I can't believe it!"
  speaker "Bill"
  say "This is {player}. A real up-and-coming trainer, beat every Gym Leader in Kanto. Came along to keep me company."
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Pleased to meet you! I'm Celio. I'm trying to link the PCs here on the islands with the PCs back in Kanto."
  say "But the machine just won't talk to Kanto's network. I've been stuck on it for weeks."
  speaker "Bill"
  say "Leave it to me. Two heads are better than one, right? Let's see what's going on in there."
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Thanks, Bill! Oh, and {player}, you should see more of the islands while you're here. Please, take this."
  give "Tri-Pass"
  say "With the Tri-Pass, the Seagallop will take you between Kanto and the first three islands, whenever you like."
  speaker "Bill"
  say "We'll be at this for a while. Say, {player}, could you run an errand for me while you look around?"
  call GiveMeteorite
  say "Go and have a look around, {player}. The sailor at the harbour can take you back to Kanto too."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 1
  endscene
  end


# Bill's Meteorite (a flag of ours: FireRed gives it in the meeting, which a game of plan 19 · K7 has played without it)
script GiveMeteorite
  setflag FLAG_GOT_METEORITE_FROM_BILL
  give "Meteorite"
  say "That's a Meteorite. A fellow who runs the game corner on Two Island has been wanting one for ages."
  say "Would you take it to him? Celio's been meaning to send it over, but he can't leave the machine."
  return


# The machine works: the first step towards Bill on the column before it (the area's triggers 0 to 3, while the
# variable is 2). Bill comes over to the player, and the two of them sail for Kanto
script BillLeaves
  scene "Bill sails home"
  face player right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 7 walk one_island_pokemon_center_bill down 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 8 walk one_island_pokemon_center_bill down 2
  if var PLAYER_Y == 9 walk one_island_pokemon_center_bill down 3
  walk one_island_pokemon_center_bill left 1
  face one_island_pokemon_center_bill player
  speaker "Bill"
  say "{player}! You're back! Guess what? Celio and I got the machine talking to Kanto at last."
  say "The PCs on the islands are linked with the ones at home now. My work here's done."
  say "So I'm heading back to Kanto. Why don't you come along? I'll ride the Seagallop with you as far as Cinnabar."
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Bill, {player}, thank you both. I couldn't have done it without you."
  say "{player}, come back and see me anytime. There's still so much I want to try with this machine."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_BILL
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 3
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CINNABAR_BILL
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_CINNABAR_ISLAND 3
  call SeagallopToCinnabar
  endscene
  end


script Bill
  face one_island_pokemon_center_bill player
  speaker "Bill"
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_METEORITE_FROM_BILL goto Errand
  if flag FLAG_SEVII_DETOUR_FINISHED goto Stroll
  say "Hmm, what if I connect this here... No, that's not it either. Don't worry, we'll crack it!"
  if item "Meteorite" say "Did you take the Meteorite to the game corner on Two Island yet? The Seagallop will get you there."
  end
label Errand
  say "Oh, {player}! I almost forgot. Could you run an errand for me?"
  call GiveMeteorite
  end
label Stroll
  say "You've been all the way to Three Island? Then you've seen more of the islands than me! Take your time, the machine's nearly there."
  end


script Celio
  face celio player
  speaker "Celio"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 goto Busy
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F <= 2 goto Working
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 3 goto Alone
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 4 goto Ruby
  goto Sapphire
label Busy
  say "Sorry, I'm in the middle of something. This machine is giving me no end of trouble."
  end
label Working
  say "Bill's a genius. With his help, I'm sure the machine will link up with Kanto in no time."
  end
label Alone
  if not nationaldex goto Modifying
  say "{player}, your Pokédex... it's been upgraded to the National Pokédex! Then you'll be meeting Pokémon from far and wide."
  say "Here's the thing. I want to link this machine to networks much farther away than Kanto. But it needs more power."
  say "There's a gem called the Ruby that holds the power I need. It's said to lie somewhere deep inside Mt. Ember, at the north end of this island."
  say "I know it's a lot to ask, but could you look for it? I'd go myself, but I'm no match for the Pokémon on that mountain."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 4
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_MT_EMBER_EXTERIOR 1
  setflag FLAG_MT_EMBER_RUBY_PATH_OPEN
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_MT_EMBER_EXTERIOR_ROCKETS
  end
label Modifying
  say "Thanks to Bill, the islands' PCs are linked with Kanto. Now I'm trying to make the machine reach even farther."
  end
label Ruby
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_RUBY goto Seeking
  say "That glow... It's the Ruby! You found it on Mt. Ember? Thank you, {player}!"
  say "Now, there's one more thing I need. Could I ask one more favour of you?"
  ask "Will you help me again?"
  if no goto NotYet
  take "Ruby"
  say "There's said to be another gem, the Sapphire, somewhere on the islands farther south. With both, the machine will be complete."
  say "This pass will help. Take it in place of your Tri-Pass."
  take "Tri-Pass"
  give "Rainbow Pass"
  say "With the Rainbow Pass, the Seagallop will take you to all seven islands. I'm counting on you!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 5
  end
label NotYet
  say "Oh... Please think it over, {player}. You're the only one I can ask. Keep the Ruby safe until then."
  end
label Seeking
  say "The Ruby should be somewhere deep inside Mt. Ember. Please be careful on that mountain!"
  end
label Sapphire
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F >= 6 goto Linked
  if item "Sapphire" goto Complete
  say "The Sapphire should be on one of the islands farther south. The Seagallop will take you there with the Rainbow Pass."
  end
label Complete
  scene "The Sapphire"
  say "{player}! That blue light... It's the Sapphire! You really found it!"
  take "Sapphire"
  fanfare keyitem
  speaker none
  text "{player} handed the Sapphire to Celio."
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Thank you! Now, let me set it in beside the Ruby..."
  face celio up
  sound "select"
  wait 0.8
  say "That's it, it fits perfectly. Now if I just bring the power up slowly..."
  wait 0.8
  sound "pc_on"
  say "It's working! The machine's reaching out farther than ever... and something's answered! A PC far away, in a region called Hoenn!"
  face celio player
  say "{player}, thank you. With your help the islands are linked with Kanto and with Hoenn too. I'll never forget it."
  setflag FLAG_SYS_CAN_LINK_WITH_RS
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CERULEAN_CAVE_GUARD
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LORELEI_IN_HER_HOUSE
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 6
  endscene
  end
label Linked
  say "The machine hasn't missed a beat since you brought the Sapphire. I wish you the very best on your travels, {player}!"
  end
