# One Island's Pokémon Center (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/OneIsland_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# The first time the player comes in with Bill (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_HARBOR 3, one_island.txt) the two meet Celio
# at his network machine: Bill brings him up to speed, sets to work on it with him, and Celio gives the player the
# Tri-Pass for the Seagallop (VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 1). Bill stays to help from then on. FireRed's
# Meteorite, its errand on Two and Three Island, and Celio's later requests (the Ruby and the Sapphire) are plan 19 ·
# K9's; so is Bill's sailing home once the machine works (FLAG_SEVII_DETOUR_FINISHED).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 setflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_BILL
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_CELIO
  if not var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_HARBOR == 3 end
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 goto Meet
  end
label Meet
  scene "Bill and Celio"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ONE_ISLAND_POKECENTER_BILL
  show one_island_pokemon_center_bill
  walk player right 5 up 1
  speaker "Bill"
  say "Hey there, Celio! Long time no see."
  face celio player
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Bill! You really came all the way out here? I can't believe it!"
  speaker "Bill"
  say "This is {player}. A real up-and-coming trainer, beat every Gym Leader in Kanto. Came along to keep me company."
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Pleased to meet you! I'm Celio. I'm trying to link the PCs here on the islands with the PCs back in Kanto."
  say "But the machine just won't talk to Kanto's network. I've been stuck on it for weeks."
  speaker "Bill"
  say "Leave it to me. Two heads are better than one, right? Let's see what's going on in there."
  speaker "Celio"
  say "Thanks, Bill! Oh, and {player}, you should see more of the islands while you're here. Please, take this."
  give "Tri-Pass"
  say "With the Tri-Pass, the Seagallop will take you between Kanto and the first three islands, whenever you like."
  speaker "Bill"
  say "We'll be at this for a while. Go and have a look around, {player}. The sailor at the harbour can take you back to Kanto too."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 1
  endscene
  end


script Bill
  face one_island_pokemon_center_bill player
  speaker "Bill"
  say "Hmm, what if I connect this here... No, that's not it either. Don't worry, we'll crack it!"
  end


script Celio
  face celio player
  speaker "Celio"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ONE_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 goto Busy
  say "Bill's a genius. With his help, I'm sure the machine will link up with Kanto in no time."
  end
label Busy
  say "Sorry, I'm in the middle of something. This machine is giving me no end of trouble."
  end
