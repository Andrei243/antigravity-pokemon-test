# The gate before the Bell Tower (plan 20 · J11, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T27R0201).

# The Sage by the north door sends visitors away until the Kimono Girls have tested the holder of the Rainbow Wing
# (FLAG_JOHTO_KIMONO_GIRLS_BEATEN, ecruteak_dance_theater.txt), which opens the door; then he lets them by.


# T27R0201_000: the Sage by the north door
script Sage
  face self player
  speaker "Sage"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_KIMONO_GIRLS_BEATEN goto Pass
  if flag FLAG_UNK_093 goto Wing
  sayown
  end
label Wing
  say "A Rainbow Wing! Then the Kimono Girls of the Dance Theater must test you first. Go and show it to them."
  end
label Pass
  say "The Kimono Girls have told us of you. The way to the Bell Tower is open. May the bird of the rainbow know you."
  end
