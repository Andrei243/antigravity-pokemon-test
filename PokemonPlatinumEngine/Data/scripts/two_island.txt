# Two Island (plan 19 · K9, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/TwoIsland/scripts.inc).
#
# The island's little stall sells more as its story goes on, as FireRed's does: a couple of things at first, more once
# Lostelle is home (FLAG_RESCUED_LOSTELLE, three_island_berry_forest.txt), and the most once the player is Champion
# (FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR) as well (marts.json's two_island_1 to _3). The woman who comes for the new goods is there from
# the rescue on, the beauty from the Hall of Fame on. FireRed's last stock, and the Pokémaniac who comes for it, wait
# on a link with another game, which this one has no part of: he stays away.


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TWO_ISLAND_POKE_MANIAC
  if not flag FLAG_RESCUED_LOSTELLE setflag FLAG_HIDE_TWO_ISLAND_WOMAN
  if flag FLAG_RESCUED_LOSTELLE clearflag FLAG_HIDE_TWO_ISLAND_WOMAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TWO_ISLAND_BEAUTY
  if not flag FLAG_RESCUED_LOSTELLE end
  if flag FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR clearflag FLAG_HIDE_TWO_ISLAND_BEAUTY
  end


script Clerk
  face clerk player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome! Have a look at what we've got in today."
  if not flag FLAG_RESCUED_LOSTELLE goto First
  if flag FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR goto Third
  shop "two_island_2"
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end
label First
  shop "two_island_1"
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end
label Third
  shop "two_island_3"
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the Ferry's Extra Passenger (plan 41 · EG8)
# The Open Door Agency's case in Kanto: the harbour keeper of Two Island shows the duffel nobody claimed off the ferry.

script CaseKantoKeeper
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KANTO_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KANTO_TAKEN goto Case
  say "I keep the harbour tidy. Ropes coiled, nets dried, lost things kept safe until someone comes for them."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KANTO_CLUE_DUFFEL goto Again
  say "Lost things off the ferry? There's this duffel. It came off with the cargo, not the passengers, and nobody's claimed it."
  say "A change of clothes inside, and a timetable with seat thirteen circled. So somebody rode over, ghost or no ghost."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_KANTO_CLUE_DUFFEL
  text "{player} noted the duffel nobody claimed."
  end
label Again
  say "Off with the cargo, not the passengers. Whoever owned that duffel didn't ride with the rest."
  end
label Thanks
  say "The duffel's owner came back for it at last. Rode over in a proper seat this time, they said."
  end
