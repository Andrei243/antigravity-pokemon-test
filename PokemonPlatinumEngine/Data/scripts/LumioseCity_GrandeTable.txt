# La Grande Table in Lumiose City (plan 37 · FC5, the fourth of Lumiose's restaurants, and the one that charges). A
# restaurant of ours whose menu costs 15,000 and is three courses against its staff, one in each format: a double
# battle (Séverine), a triple (Évrard) and a rotation (Hippolyte), trainers of Kalos's table a step above the other
# three restaurants' staff. Without the money the menu isn't served and the day's menu is kept. Once paid, the menu is
# taken once a day (FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_4), and the dish is rarer than the others' and chosen by the turns the
# three courses took together (TURNS): six or fewer an Ability Capsule, ten or fewer a PP Max, fifteen or fewer two PP
# Ups, more two Rare Candies.


script Host
  face self player
  speaker "Ernestine"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_4 goto Served
  say "Welcome to La Grande Table. Our menu is three courses, a double, a triple and a rotation battle."
  ask "The menu is {money 15000}. Will you take it?"
  if no goto Later
  takemoney 15000
  if result == 0 goto Poor
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_4
  say "Thank you. Our kitchen's dishes are rare ones, and the quicker the meal, the rarer the dish."
  speaker "Séverine"
  say "The first course, served for two. Shall we begin?"
  battle severine as "kalos_restaurant_grande_severine" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Évrard"
  say "The main course comes in three. I hope you have room."
  battle evrard as "kalos_restaurant_grande_evrard" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Hippolyte"
  say "The house's dessert turns on the table. It is my favourite course to serve."
  battle hippolyte as "kalos_restaurant_grande_hippolyte" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Ernestine"
  say "Three courses in {var:TURNS} turns. The kitchen has prepared something worthy of it."
  if var TURNS <= 6 goto Best
  if var TURNS <= 10 goto Good
  if var TURNS <= 15 goto Fair
  say "An unhurried meal, as a grand one should be. Please accept this."
  give "Rare Candy" 2
  end
label Best
  say "Magnificent! The rarest thing our kitchen has ever sent out."
  give "Ability Capsule"
  end
label Good
  say "Splendid. The kitchen sends out one of its treasures."
  give "PP Max"
  end
label Fair
  say "A meal well eaten. Here is the dish of the day."
  give "PP Up" 2
  end
label Poor
  say "Oh dear, that is not quite enough. We shall keep your table for when you are ready."
  end
label Lost
  heal
  speaker "Ernestine"
  say "The meal is over for today, I'm afraid. Your Pokémon have been seen to. We hope to see you tomorrow."
  end
label Later
  say "Of course. La Grande Table will be here when you are ready."
  end
label Served
  say "You have dined with us today already. The kitchen will be ready again tomorrow."
  end


# The staff between meals
script Staff
  face self player
  if subject "severine" goto Severine
  if subject "evrard" goto Evrard
  speaker "Hippolyte"
  say "Two, then three, then the table turns. A guest must keep their wits about them here."
  end
label Severine
  speaker "Séverine"
  say "Each course here is served differently. Guests who change their plans quickly do well."
  end
label Evrard
  speaker "Évrard"
  say "Madame Ernestine takes the bill before the first course. That is how grand we are."
  end
