# The Celestial Tower's ground floor (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/celestial_tower_2.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Professor Juniper (0, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_TOWER, cleared by the scene in Mistralton City) is at her field work here.
# Before the Jet Badge she gives a Lucky Egg once (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_LUCKY_EGG) and carries on; once it is won she is ready to
# fly and goes back to Mistralton City, where she and Skyla wait in the Cargo Service's hall (VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON 3).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script JuniperInTheTower
  face 0 player
  speaker "Prof. Juniper"
  if badge jet goto Ready
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_LUCKY_EGG goto Egg
  say "Hi there, {player}! How did things go with Skyla? Oh? You haven't won her Badge yet?"
  say "Then I'll keep up my field work a little longer. Before you go, take this for your trip."
  give "Lucky Egg"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_LUCKY_EGG
  say "Give that Lucky Egg to a Pokémon to hold, and it earns a little more Experience from every battle! Strong Pokémon make a strong Trainer."
  end
label Egg
  say "The Celestial Tower... a giant memorial. I wonder whether it was built where Ghost and Psychic Pokémon gathered, or whether they came because of it."
  say "Either way it's fascinating. Come and fetch me when you've beaten Skyla!"
  end
label Ready
  say "Oh, that's right! How did things go with Skyla? You won the Jet Badge! Well done!"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_LUCKY_EGG goto Fly
  give "Lucky Egg"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_LUCKY_EGG
  say "Do take that Lucky Egg. Held by a Pokémon, it earns a little more Experience from every battle."
label Fly
  say "Then the plane should be ready to fly! Thank you for coming to fetch me."
  say "I'll be waiting for you in Mistralton City!"
  hide 0
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_TOWER
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_JUNIPER_HALL
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SKYLA_HALL
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON 3
  end
