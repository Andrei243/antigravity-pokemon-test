# Glacia's room, the third of Hoenn's Elite Four (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/EverGrandeCity_GlaciasRoom/scripts.inc). As Sidney's room: spoken to, she
# battles; beaten, the door behind her opens onto the hall to Drake's room (FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_GLACIA).


script Glacia
  face glacia player
  speaker "Glacia"
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_GLACIA goto Rematch
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_GLACIA goto Beaten
  scene "Glacia"
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  say "Welcome. I am Glacia of the Elite Four. I came to Hoenn from far away, hoping to meet a trainer worth my ice."
  say "So far, I have found only disappointment. I do hope you will be different."
  say "Show me the heat of your spirit. My Ice-type Pokémon and I will freeze it, if we can."
  battle self as "hoenn_glacia"
  speaker "Glacia"
  say "Such fire... You melted my ice completely. I have not felt this in a long time."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_GLACIA
  addcontact glacia
  text "Registered Glacia in the PokéNav."
  sound "door_open"
  say "Go on. The last of the Elite Four waits ahead, and he is not as gentle as I am."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "Keep that fire burning. You will need all of it for what lies ahead."
  end
label Rematch
  say "So you are here. I have trained my team to the edge of winter itself. Let us see you thaw it again."
  battle self as "hoenn_glacia_rematch"
  speaker "Glacia"
  say "Defeated again. Your warmth is formidable, {player}. I will come back stronger."
  clearflag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_GLACIA
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_GLACIA
  sound "door_open"
  end
