# Amity Square (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_amity_square.s).
#
# The square is for strolling with a Pokémon, and only with one of the kinds its rules allow: the receptionists at
# either way in turn back a Trainer who has none of them. With one, they ask whether the player would like a stroll,
# heal the team and let the player through (VAR_FOLLOWER_MON_ACTIVE 1); on the way out they ask how it went (0).
# The Pokémon walking at the player's side and what it finds on the paths are plan 06 · R16's, once Pokémon can
# follow the player (plan 10 · F3 and F4); until then it stays in its ball, as docs/mechanics/rulings.md says.


# The way in from the west gate (the original's trigger 0, state 0) and from the east gate (trigger 2, state 0).
script WestGate
  face player left
  call Welcome
  end

script EastGate
  face player right
  call Welcome
  end

# AmitySquare_CheckPartyHasSpecies: the kinds of Pokémon the square allows before the National Pokédex
script Welcome
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if has "Pikachu" goto Allowed
  if has "Clefairy" goto Allowed
  if has "Psyduck" goto Allowed
  if has "Pachirisu" goto Allowed
  if has "Drifloon" goto Allowed
  if has "Buneary" goto Allowed
  if has "Happiny" goto Allowed
  if has "Turtwig" goto Allowed
  if has "Grotle" goto Allowed
  if has "Torterra" goto Allowed
  if has "Chimchar" goto Allowed
  if has "Monferno" goto Allowed
  if has "Infernape" goto Allowed
  if has "Piplup" goto Allowed
  if has "Prinplup" goto Allowed
  if has "Empoleon" goto Allowed
  say "Welcome to Amity Square! The square is for strolling together with your Pokémon."
  say "But only some kinds may come in: Pikachu, Clefairy, Psyduck, Pachirisu, Drifloon, Buneary and Happiny..."
  say "...and Turtwig, Chimchar and Piplup, and the Pokémon they grow into. Do come back with one of them!"
  walk player down 1
  return
label Allowed
  ask "Welcome to Amity Square! Would you like to take a stroll here with your Pokémon?"
  if no goto NotToday
  heal
  setvar VAR_FOLLOWER_MON_ACTIVE 1
  say "Please enjoy your time together!"
  walk player up 1
  return
label NotToday
  say "I see. Please come back whenever you like."
  walk player down 1
  return


# The way out at either gate (the original's triggers 1 and 3, state 1).
script Leave
  speaker "Receptionist"
  setvar VAR_FOLLOWER_MON_ACTIVE 0
  say "Did you enjoy your stroll? Please come again soon!"
  walk player down 1
  end
