# Drake's room, the last of Hoenn's Elite Four (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/EverGrandeCity_DrakesRoom/scripts.inc). As Sidney's room: spoken to, he battles;
# beaten, the door behind him opens onto the long hall to the Champion's room (FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_DRAKE).


script Drake
  face drake player
  speaker hoenn_drake "Drake"
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_DRAKE goto Rematch
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_DRAKE goto Beaten
  scene "Drake"
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  say "I am Drake, the last of the Elite Four. I sailed the seas for many years before I came to the dragons."
  say "To command Dragon-type Pokémon, a trainer needs one thing above all. Do you know what it is?"
  say "If you don't, you will not get past me. Come! Show me what you have!"
  battle self as "hoenn_drake"
  speaker hoenn_drake "Drake"
  say "Superb. You have it, the thing my dragons and I look for in a trainer: a true bond with your Pokémon."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_DRAKE
  addcontact drake
  text "Registered Drake in the PokéNav."
  sound "door_open"
  say "Go on through. The Champion waits at the end of the hall. Give it everything you have."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "Why do you linger here? The Champion is waiting for you."
  end
label Rematch
  say "You've come back to my room. Good. A dragon's pride is a patient thing. Show me what you've grown into!"
  battle self as "hoenn_drake_rematch"
  speaker "Drake"
  say "Splendid. Beaten again, and I could not be prouder of the fight. My dragons will remember it."
  clearflag FLAG_PHONE_REMATCH_DRAKE
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_DRAKE
  sound "door_open"
  end
