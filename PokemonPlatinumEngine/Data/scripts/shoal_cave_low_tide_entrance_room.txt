# Shoal Cave's entrance room (plan 21 · H8, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/ShoalCave_LowTideEntranceRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# The tide is read behind the warp's fade as the player comes in from Route 125 (`tide`, Emerald's
# UpdateShoalTideFlag: FLAG_SYS_SHOAL_TIDE set at high tide, from midnight to three, nine to three and nine to
# midnight), and world.json's swaps lay the high tide's chunks over this room and the inner room while it is set. The
# old man makes a Shell Bell of four Shoal Salt and four Shoal Shells, as often as they are brought; the salt and the
# shells lie about the cave again each day (FLAG_DAILY_RECEIVED_SHOAL_SALT_1 to _4 and _SHELL_1 to _4, the inner,
# stairs and lower rooms' scripts).


script OnTransition
  tide
  end


script ShellBellExpert
  face self player
  speaker "Old Man"
  if item "Shoal Salt" >= 4 goto Shells
  goto Short
label Shells
  if item "Shoal Shell" >= 4 goto Offer
label Short
  if item "Shoal Salt" goto Some
  if item "Shoal Shell" goto Some
  say "Going in? The sea comes and goes in this cave twice a day. Some corners are dry only while the tide is out."
  end
label Some
  say "Bring me four Shoal Salt and four Shoal Shells, and I'll make you something you'll be glad of."
  end
label Offer
  ask "Four Shoal Salt and four Shoal Shells! Shall I make them into a Shell Bell for you?"
  if no goto Declined
  say "Leave it to me. It won't take a moment!"
  take "Shoal Salt" 4
  take "Shoal Shell" 4
  give "Shell Bell"
  say "Let a Pokémon hold the Shell Bell, and every blow it lands gives it back a little HP. Its ring is soothing, too."
  end
label Declined
  say "No? I'll be here whenever you change your mind."
  end
