# The Indigo Plateau's Pokémon Center (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/IndigoPlateau_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc and the League's rooms).
#
# Whoever comes in here faces the Elite Four from the first again: the four rooms' doors are shut and their masters
# unbeaten (FLAG_DEFEATED_LORELEI to FLAG_DEFEATED_CHAMP, FireRed's own, which its rooms set as each is beaten), and the
# rooms' arrival scenes wait (VAR_MAP_SCENE_POKEMON_LEAGUE 0). FireRed clears the same as its whiteout and its Hall of
# Fame leave the League; nobody comes back out through a room's door here, as there. The guard at the door wishes the
# player luck. Lorelei's absence once the National Pokédex is had, and the rematch teams, are plan 19 · K9's.


script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_LORELEI
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_BRUNO
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_AGATHA
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_LANCE
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_CHAMP
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_POKEMON_LEAGUE 0
  end


script DoorGuard
  face league_door_guard player
  speaker "Guard"
  say "Beyond this door wait the Elite Four, the strongest trainers in Kanto. Beat all four, and the Champion awaits."
  say "Once you step through, there's no coming back out until it's over. Good luck!"
  end
