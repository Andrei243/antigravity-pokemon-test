# The player's house in Cabo Poco, downstairs (plan 34 · PD1). The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats as
# they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Paldea's first chapter"). VAR_PALDEA_STORY is cabo_poco.txt's
# count. A Champion who crosses in from Galar lands inside the door with the story at 0, and has the same first morning.


# Coming down on the first morning (or in through the door from Galar): Mom, and the academy's director come to call
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY == 0 goto Morning
  end
label Morning
  scene "The director comes to call"
  face mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Morning, sweetheart! You'll never guess who's sitting in our kitchen. The director of the academy, in person!"
  face clavell_home player
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  say "Good morning, {player}. Forgive the early visit: I like to welcome every new student myself."
  say "The president of our student council lives just up the hill from you, and she has something for you. Shall we go up?"
  say "Her house is the one with the terrace over the sea. I'll meet you there."
  speaker "Mom"
  say "A Pokémon of your own, I'd bet! Go on, then. And take your good shoes, there's a lot of Paldea between here and school."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_RUNNING_SHOES
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_CLAVELL_HOME
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_CLAVELL_TERRACE
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_TERRACE
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 1
  objective paldeaTerrace
  endscene
  end


# Mom: a rest whenever the team has one
script Mom
  if party > 0 goto Rest
  speaker "Mom"
  say "The director is waiting for you up at the president's house. Don't keep him standing there!"
  end
label Rest
  sayown
  heal
  fanfare heal
  call NextHint
  end
