# The friendship rater's house in Verdanturf Town (plan 21 · H3, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/VerdanturfTown_FriendshipRatersHouse/scripts.inc): how fond of the
# player the first Pokémon of the team is, by Emerald's steps of friendship.


script FriendshipRater
  face self player
  speaker "Woman"
  say "Let me have a look at your Pokémon. I can always tell how much a Pokémon loves its trainer."
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP == 255 goto Max
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 200 goto Great
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 150 goto Good
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 100 goto Fond
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 50 goto Wary
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 1 goto Cold
  say "Hmm, it doesn't seem to care much for you at all. Spend more time together, and that will change."
  end
label Cold
  say "It's still a little wary of you. Be kind to it, and it will warm up."
  end
label Wary
  say "It's getting used to you. Keep at it, and you'll be good friends."
  end
label Fond
  say "It's quite fond of you! You two are getting along nicely."
  end
label Good
  say "It's very friendly toward you. It's happy to be with you!"
  end
label Great
  say "It adores you! You must take very good care of it."
  end
label Max
  say "It loves you with all its heart! I don't think I've ever seen a Pokémon so devoted."
  end
