# Lysandre's café in the north of Lumiose City (plan 23 · Z2). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Lumiose City and Route 4, drawn by hand"): the professor waits there
# with a tall man of the city who speaks of a better world and of Pokémon that change; the professor goes back to his
# laboratory after. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 11 becomes 12 here. The room's people: lysandre,
# sycamore (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_SYCAMORE_CAFE but while he waits there) and the café's waiter; Lysandre leaves for good
# after his broadcast (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_LYSANDRE_CAFE, plan 23 · Z4), and the waiter then shows the way down to his laboratory.


# Into the café with the professor waiting (VAR_KALOS_STORY 11)
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY != 11 end
  scene "Lysandre's cafe"
  face sycamore player
  speaker "Prof. Sycamore"
  say "Ah, {player}, here you are! Come, come. Let me introduce an old friend of mine."
  face lysandre player
  speaker "Lysandre"
  say "So this is the young Trainer the professor speaks of. My name is Lysandre."
  say "I want this world to be beautiful. To be beautiful, it must be fair, and to be fair, it must be shared by fewer hands."
  say "Some Pokémon change their very shape when a bond is strong enough. Power like that should go to those who would use it well."
  speaker "Prof. Sycamore"
  say "Lysandre helped pay for my research for years. He's a good man, if a little grand about it!"
  speaker "Lysandre"
  say "Tell me, {player}: will you use your strength for others, or only for yourself? I'll be watching to find out."
  speaker "Prof. Sycamore"
  say "Well! I should get back to the laboratory. Go and see Kalos, {player}. Its roads are opening up again soon!"
  hide sycamore
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_SYCAMORE_CAFE
  clearflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_SYCAMORE_LAB
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 12
  setvar VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 1
  objective kalosRoute5
  endscene
  end


script Lysandre
  face lysandre player
  speaker "Lysandre"
  say "The world grows more crowded every year. One day someone will have to choose who it is for."
  end

script Sycamore
  speaker "Prof. Sycamore"
  say "Isn't this café wonderful? The best in Lumiose, I'd say."
  end

script Waiter
  speaker "Waiter"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS goto Xerosic
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY == 27 goto Labs
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 28 goto Gone
  say "Good day. The gentleman in red owns this café. He takes his coffee very black and very hot."
  end
# Plan 23 · Z4: after Lysandre's broadcast (AnistarCity_Gym.txt, VAR_KALOS_STORY 27) the waiter shows the player the way
# down to the laboratory under the café (LumioseCity_LysandreLabs.txt)
label Labs
  scene "The way down to Lysandre's laboratory"
  say "Mr. Lysandre said a young Trainer would come asking for him. He is waiting for you below."
  say "This way, behind the counter. Mind the stairs; they are steeper than they look."
  endscene
  warp "LumioseCity_LysandreLabs" 8 14 up
  end
label Gone
  say "Mr. Lysandre hasn't been in since that broadcast. The café feels very empty without him."
  end
# Plan 23 · Z4b, Looker's fifth case: once Malva has told of Xerosic (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS), the waiter shows the way down
# again, until the laboratory is cleared
label Xerosic
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LABS_DONE goto Gone
  say "You've come about the people downstairs? They came in through the back last week, and I've been too frightened to say."
  say "The stairs are behind the counter, as before. Please be careful."
  warp "LumioseCity_LysandreLabs" 8 14 up
  end
