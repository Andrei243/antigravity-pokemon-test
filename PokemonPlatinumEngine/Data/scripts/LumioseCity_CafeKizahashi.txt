# Lumiose City's Café Kizahashi (plan 23, Kalos's last species: one of the thirteen doors left shut). A café of ours on X
# and Y's place as it is publicly described, a café for its regulars. Its owner serves only those the city knows: until
# Kalos's Hall of Fame he turns the player away kindly, and after it pours the Champion a cup.


script Kizahashi
  face self player
  speaker "Owner"
  if flag KalosHallOfFame goto Champion
  say "I'm sorry, young Trainer. Every stool in here belongs to a regular, and has for years."
  say "Make a name for yourself in Kalos, and there will be a cup waiting for you."
  end
label Champion
  say "The Champion of Kalos! Sit, sit. That stool by the window has been waiting for you."
  say "Your cup is on the house, today and every day after. That's what it means to be a regular here."
  end
