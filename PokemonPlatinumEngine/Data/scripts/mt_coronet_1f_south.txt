# Mt. Coronet, the way through from Route 207 to Route 208 (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_mt_coronet_1f_south.s).
#
# Cyrus stands in the passage. As the player comes by he walks down to them and speaks of the mountain where Sinnoh
# began, of the world as it was first made and of what became of it, then goes on his way west.
# VAR_MT_CORONET_1F_SOUTH_STATE: 1 once he has spoken.


# The passage's crossing (the original's trigger 0, a single tile at 14,23, state 0).
script Cyrus
  face player up
  walk cyrus down 6
  face cyrus left
  face player right
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "We meet again. Mt. Coronet... It is said that Sinnoh's history began on this very mountain."
  face cyrus up
  face player up
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  say "In the world as it was first made, there can have been no strife. There was nothing yet to fight over."
  face cyrus left
  say "And what became of that world? People came, with their hearts, and their hearts brought quarrels. What a waste."
  say "Excuse me. I have somewhere I must be."
  walk player down 1
  face player up
  wait 0.3
  face player left
  walk cyrus left 10
  hide cyrus
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MT_CORONET_1F_SOUTH_CYRUS
  setvar VAR_MT_CORONET_1F_SOUTH_STATE 1
  end
