# Mt. Coronet, the way in from Route 207 (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_mt_coronet_1f_south.s).
#
# A step into the hall (the original's trigger 0): Cyrus comes down from the north, speaks of the mountain as the place
# where Sinnoh began and of the world that came to be, and goes off west. VAR_MT_CORONET_1F_SOUTH_STATE 1 once he has.


script Cyrus
  face player up
  walk cyrus down 6
  face cyrus left
  face player right
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "We meet again. Do you know what this mountain is? It is said that Sinnoh began here, at Mt. Coronet."
  face cyrus up
  wait 0.3
  face player up
  wait 0.2
  face player right
  say "In a world that had only just been made, time began to flow and space began to spread. Everything was new."
  face cyrus left
  say "And what became of that world? It filled with conflict and with spirit, and it was never made whole. A pity."
  walk player down 1
  face player up
  wait 0.3
  face player left
  walk cyrus left 10
  hide cyrus
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MT_CORONET_1F_SOUTH_CYRUS
  setvar VAR_MT_CORONET_1F_SOUTH_STATE 1
  end
