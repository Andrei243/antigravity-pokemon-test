# Pokéstar Studios' studio building (plan 42 · PS4, part b; rulings, "The film studio"). Black 2's room, our people and
# our words: Director Wren of ours shoots the studio's four films, each opened by the studio's rank (the best stars of
# every film summed). A take is a battle of the film's own; nothing of the player's team goes in. The building's two
# inner doors (the sound stage and the back rooms) stay shut.


script StudioDesk
  face self player
  speaker "Director Wren"
  studio rank
  if result >= 1 goto Again
  say "Welcome to the lot! I'm Wren, and I direct films for the studio. Every take is a battle, played to a script."
  say "We lend you the cast. Say the script's lines or your own, and the take ends however you play it."
  goto Pick
label Again
  say "Back for another take? Splendid! The studio keeps your best take of every film for its premiere."
label Pick
  choose "Which film shall we shoot?" "THE EMPTY SEAT" "THE GILDED HOUR" "LETTERS IN THE FOG" "THUNDER AT MARLOWE PASS" "NOT TODAY"
  if result == 0 goto EmptySeat
  if result == 1 goto GildedHour
  if result == 2 goto Letters
  if result == 3 goto Thunder
  say "Come back whenever you feel like a star!"
  end
label EmptySeat
  studio shoot "emptySeat"
  goto Wrap
label GildedHour
  studio shoot "gildedHour"
  goto Wrap
label Letters
  studio open "lettersInTheFog"
  if result == 0 goto NotYet
  studio shoot "lettersInTheFog"
  goto Wrap
label Thunder
  studio open "thunderAtMarlowePass"
  if result == 0 goto NotYet
  studio shoot "thunderAtMarlowePass"
  goto Wrap
label NotYet
  say "That script is still in the drawer. Earn the studio a few more stars on the films we have, then ask again!"
  end
label Wrap
  speaker "Director Wren"
  if result >= 4 goto Great
  say "That's a wrap! Your best take of every film goes to the theater across the lot for its premiere."
  end
label Great
  say "Magnificent! That's the take the posters will be painted from. Go and see it at the theater!"
  end


script Casting
  face self player
  speaker "Casting Clerk"
  studio rank
  say "The studio's rank is {result} stars. Each film's best take counts toward it."
  say "At four stars the detective story opens, and at eight the race against the storm. Good luck!"
  end
