# Silph Co.'s 11F (plan 19 · K5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/SilphCo_11F/scripts.inc).
#
# Team Rocket holds the building. A door of the floor is shut until the Card Key (from the fifth floor) opens it
# for good: FireRed draws it into its tiles and changes them with setmetatile; here it is four things of ours on
# its tiles (a shutter each), gone once its flag is set (FLAG_SILPH_<FLOOR>F_DOOR_<N>, FireRed's).

script Door
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_SILPH_11F_DOOR end
  if not item "Card Key" goto Locked
  sound "door_slide"
  setflag FLAG_SILPH_11F_DOOR
  text "The Card Key opened the door!"
  end
label Locked
  text "The door is locked. It takes a card to open it."
  end


# Giovanni waits in the president's office and battles whoever steps up before him (the area's triggers on
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_SILPH_CO_11F 0). Beaten, he and his grunts leave, Team Rocket goes from the building and the city
# (FLAG_HIDE_SILPH_ROCKETS, FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_ROCKETS) and Saffron City's people come back out
# (FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CIVILIANS cleared). The president gives the Master Ball (FLAG_GOT_MASTER_BALL_FROM_SILPH).
script Giovanni
  scene "Giovanni at Silph"
  face silph_co_giovanni down
  face player up
  speaker "Giovanni"
  say "Ah, it's you again. We meet once more. The president and I are discussing a business deal."
  say "Silph Co. will hand over its Master Ball to Team Rocket, and you will keep out of it."
  say "Or will you? Very well. I'll make sure you never interfere again!"
  music "kanto/eye_galactic"
  battle silph_co_giovanni as "kanto_boss_giovanni_2"
  speaker "Giovanni"
  say "Arrgh! I lose again?! Blast it all... You ruined our plans for Silph Co."
  say "But Team Rocket will never fall! I'll be waiting for you, child. Remember that."
  fade out 0.4
  hide silph_co_giovanni
  hide silph_co_11f_grunt1
  hide silph_co_11f_grunt2
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SILPH_ROCKETS
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_ROCKETS
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CIVILIANS
  fade in 0.4
  music area
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_SILPH_CO_11F 1
  endscene
  end


script President
  speaker "President"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_MASTER_BALL_FROM_SILPH goto Given
  say "Thank you, my young friend! You saved Silph Co. from Team Rocket. I can't thank you enough."
  say "Please accept this as a token of our gratitude. You won't find another anywhere."
  give "Master Ball"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_MASTER_BALL_FROM_SILPH
  say "That's our secret prototype, the Master Ball. It will catch any wild Pokémon without fail."
  end
label Given
  say "The Master Ball is the only one of its kind. Use it wisely!"
  end
