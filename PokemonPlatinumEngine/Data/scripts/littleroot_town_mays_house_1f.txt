# The rival's house downstairs, the east house of Littleroot Town (plan 21 · H2): Professor Birch's family. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LittlerootTown_MaysHouse_1F/scripts.inc,
# RivalsHouse_1F_EventScript_RivalMom).


# The first visit (VAR_LITTLEROOT_HOUSES_STATE_MAY 1, Emerald's YoureNewNeighbor): the professor's wife spots the new
# neighbour at the door and sends them up to meet her child.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_HOUSES_STATE_MAY != 1 end
  scene "The new neighbour"
  emote rivals_house_1f_mom exclaim
  face rivals_house_1f_mom player
  speaker "Neighbour"
  say "Oh! Hello there. You must be {player}, from the house next door. Welcome to Littleroot!"
  say "I'm Professor Birch's wife. Our {rival} is about your age, and so excited to have someone new next door."
  say "{rival}'s upstairs. Go on up and say hello!"
  setflag FLAG_MET_RIVAL_MOM
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_HOUSES_STATE_MAY 2
  endscene
  end


script RivalMom
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_RIVAL_ROUTE103 goto Home
  if var VAR_ROUTE101_STATE >= 3 goto OnTheRoute
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE >= 3 goto Busy
  say "{rival}'s upstairs. Go on up, {player}. Don't be shy!"
  end
label Busy
  say "{rival} went off to help my husband with his fieldwork. That child is never still!"
  end
label OnTheRoute
  say "{rival}? Out on Route 103, I think, counting Pokémon. North of Oldale Town."
  end
label Home
  say "{rival} comes home every so often, usually covered in grass. I suppose I should be glad of the visits!"
  end


script RivalSibling
  say "My big sibling is going to be the best Pokémon trainer ever. You'll see!"
  end
