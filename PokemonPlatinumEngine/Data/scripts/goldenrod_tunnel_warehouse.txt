# The warehouse under Goldenrod City (plan 20 · J11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D37R0105).
#
# While the takeover is at its height (VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER 4) three grunts keep the warehouse
# (FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1), and the Radio Tower's real Director is shut up in it (_2). He hands the player his
# Card Key for the shutters on the tower's third floor, which is HeartGold's FLAG_UNK_1C1: the slot there asks that
# flag, not the bag, since a player who has been to Kanto may carry Silph Co.'s Card Key already.


# D37R0105_000
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_2
  if var VAR_SCENE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER != 4 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_1
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROCKET_TAKEOVER_2
  end


# D37R0105_001
script Director
  face d37r0105_gsgentleman player
  speaker "Director"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_1C1 goto Told
  say "Who's there? You've come to get me out? Thank you! But tell me, what's become of the Radio Tower?"
  say "Team Rocket have taken it? Then you'll need this. It's my Card Key."
  if not item "Card Key" give "Card Key"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_1C1
label Told
  say "It opens the shutters on the tower's third floor."
  say "Please, I'm begging you. With the transmitter in their hands, there's no knowing what they'll do. They might even control Pokémon with some special signal!"
  say "You're the only one I can turn to. Save the Radio Tower, and every Pokémon in the land!"
  end
