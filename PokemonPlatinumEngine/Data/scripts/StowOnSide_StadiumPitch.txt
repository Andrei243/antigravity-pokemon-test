# Stow-on-Side Stadium's pitch (plan 33 · GL5, its first session): the gym challenge's fourth Leader, Bea in a Sword game
# (the Fighting Badge) and Allister in a Shield one (the Ghost Badge, in the same place on the card). Both stand on one
# spot, the version's shown by FLAG_GALAR_SHIELD_GAME, which the pitch's arrival keeps to VAR_GALAR_VERSION. They
# Dynamax their last Pokémon. VAR_GALAR_STORY 27 to 28 (postwick.txt's count). Our own lines.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_SHIELD_GAME
  end
label Shield
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_SHIELD_GAME
  end


script Bea
  face bea player
  speaker "Bea"
  if badge fighting goto Won
  scene "Bea"
  say "So you kept your feet. Good. I am Bea, Stow-on-Side's Leader. I train my body and my Pokémon together, every day."
  say "Words are not needed. Show me your strength!"
  battle bea as "galar_bea_1"
  say "...I see. Your strength is real. I will train harder still."
  say "Take the Fighting Badge."
  givebadge fighting
  text "{player} received the Fighting Badge from Bea!"
  call AfterTheBadge
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Train your body. Your Pokémon will feel it."
  end


script Allister
  face allister player
  speaker "Allister"
  if badge ghost goto Won
  scene "Allister"
  say "...You found your way past the masks. I'm Allister, the Leader here. Ghosts aren't so scary, once you get to know them."
  say "Please... don't hold back. My Pokémon won't."
  battle allister as "galar_allister_1"
  say "...You weren't scared at all. That's... really something."
  say "Here. The Ghost Badge."
  givebadge ghost
  text "{player} received the Ghost Badge from Allister!"
  call AfterTheBadge
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "...Come back and visit sometime. My ghosts liked you."
  end


# What either Leader's badge leaves: the gym's trainers beaten, and Bede at the mural on the hill
script AfterTheBadge
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_STOW_ON_SIDE_GYM
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_cerys"
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_dai"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BEDE_AT_MURAL
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 28
  objective galarMural
  text "As {player} left the pitch, a deep rumble came down from the hill above the town."
  return
