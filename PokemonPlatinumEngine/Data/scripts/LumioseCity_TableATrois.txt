# La Table à Trois in Lumiose City (plan 37 · FC5, the second of Lumiose's restaurants). A restaurant of ours: its menu
# is three courses, each a triple battle against its staff (Raoul, Agnès and Héloïse, trainers of Kalos's table whose
# "format" is "triple"; a team of fewer than three is met in the double or single it can field), fought one after
# another on the player's own team. The menu is taken once a day (FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_2, set as it is served),
# and the dish is chosen by the turns the three courses took together (TURNS): six or fewer a PP Up, ten or fewer a Rare
# Candy, fifteen or fewer two Leppa Berries, more three Oran Berries.


script Host
  face self player
  speaker "Barnabé"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_2 goto Served
  say "Bienvenue to La Table à Trois! Every table seats three, and every battle sends out three."
  ask "Will you take today's menu? Three courses, each a triple battle against our staff."
  if no goto Later
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_2
  say "Wonderful. Remember, the kitchen rewards a guest who doesn't dawdle."
  speaker "Raoul"
  say "The first course, three plates at once. Don't let anything go cold!"
  battle raoul as "kalos_restaurant_trois_raoul" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Agnès"
  say "The main course! Left, middle and right, all at the same time."
  battle agnes as "kalos_restaurant_trois_agnes" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Héloïse"
  say "Dessert is mine. I have never once served it slowly."
  battle heloise as "kalos_restaurant_trois_heloise" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Barnabé"
  say "Three courses in {var:TURNS} turns! The kitchen will want to hear of it."
  if var TURNS <= 6 goto Best
  if var TURNS <= 10 goto Good
  if var TURNS <= 15 goto Fair
  say "A long and lovely lunch. A small dish to send you on your way."
  give "Oran Berry" 3
  end
label Best
  say "Astonishing! The kitchen sends out its rarest dish, just for you."
  give "PP Up"
  end
label Good
  say "Quick and clever. The kitchen sends out something special."
  give "Rare Candy"
  end
label Fair
  say "A fine meal at a fine pace. Here is the dish of the day."
  give "Leppa Berry" 2
  end
label Lost
  heal
  speaker "Barnabé"
  say "Ah, the meal is over for today. We have seen to your Pokémon. Come back tomorrow for another try."
  end
label Later
  say "As you wish. The table will be set for three tomorrow, too."
  end
label Served
  say "You have eaten with us today already. Come back tomorrow, the menu will be new."
  end


# The staff between meals
script Staff
  face self player
  if subject "raoul" goto Raoul
  if subject "agnes" goto Agnes
  speaker "Héloïse"
  say "Whoever stands in the middle can reach everyone. So can whoever brings dessert."
  end
label Raoul
  speaker "Raoul"
  say "Three at once is a lot of plates to carry. I dropped only two last week!"
  end
label Agnes
  speaker "Agnès"
  say "Those at the ends can't always reach each other. Think about who sits where."
  end
