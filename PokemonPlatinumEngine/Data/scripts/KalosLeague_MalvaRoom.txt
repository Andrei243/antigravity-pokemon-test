# Malva's room of the Pokémon League (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team
# Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): the first of the Elite Four's four doors, Fire. Her team is Kalos's
# trainers.json's kalos_malva_1; beaten, FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_MALVA, and the
# Champion's door opens once all four are (common.KalosLeagueFour).


script Malva
  face malva player
  speaker "Malva"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_MALVA goto Won
  if flag KalosHallOfFame goto Again
  scene "Malva"
  say "So you're the Trainer everyone on the news is talking about. I'm Malva of the Elite Four."
  say "I don't care how you got here. I care how hot you burn. Let's find out."
  battle self as "kalos_malva_1"
  say "Hmph. You burn hotter than I expected. Don't let it go out before the end."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_MALVA
  call KalosLeagueFour
  endscene
  end
label Again
  scene "MalvaAgain"
  say "The Champion, back in my room. Don't expect the same fire as last time. I've been stoking it."
  battle self as "kalos_malva_rematch"
  say "Tch. Still burning hotter than me. Fine. I'll be ready next time, Champion."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_MALVA
  call KalosLeagueFour
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Go on. The others won't wait all day."
  end
