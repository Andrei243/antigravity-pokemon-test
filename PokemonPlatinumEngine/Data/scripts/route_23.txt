# Route 23 (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats
# (data/maps/Route23/scripts.inc).
#
# Seven guards stand along the road to the Pokémon League, and each lets through only a trainer who shows the next of
# Kanto's Badges, in the order they are won: VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE23, as FireRed counts it, is 1 once Route 22's gate has
# seen the Boulder Badge (route_22_north_entrance.txt) and goes up by one at each guard, to 8 past the last. Each guard's
# line of tiles is the area's triggers, which wait for the guard's own value (FireRed's last guard has more of them in
# the forest at the road's west edge, which is trees here and nobody can stand in, and they are left out); a trainer
# without the Badge is turned back a step. Spoken to, a guard says which Badge he asks for, or that the way is open.
#
# Coming here puts Victory Road back as FireRed's Route23_OnTransition does: its boulders where they started and its
# three floor switches unpressed, with the rock barriers they lift standing again (victory_road_1f.txt and the rest).


script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_3F_BOULDER
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_2F_BOULDER
  clearflag FLAG_VICTORY_ROAD_1F_SWITCH_PRESSED
  clearflag FLAG_VICTORY_ROAD_2F_SWITCH_1_PRESSED
  clearflag FLAG_VICTORY_ROAD_2F_SWITCH_2_PRESSED
  clearflag FLAG_VICTORY_ROAD_3F_SWITCH_PRESSED
  end


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the guards' lines of tiles

script CascadeTrigger
  face cascade_badge_guard player
  speaker "Guard"
  if badge cascade goto Pass
  call Refused
  say "You need the Cascade Badge to go any further."
  walk player down 1
  end
label Pass
  say "That's the Cascade Badge, all right. Go on through!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE23 2
  end

script EarthTrigger
  face earth_badge_guard player
  speaker "Guard"
  if badge earth goto Pass
  call Refused
  say "You need the Earth Badge to go any further."
  # (the rock south of the step beside the guard sends the player back by the step west of it)
  if var PLAYER_X == 5 goto Aside
  walk player down 1
  end
label Aside
  walk player left 1 down 1
  end
label Pass
  say "That's the Earth Badge! You've won all eight. Victory Road is just ahead. Go on through!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE23 8
  end

script Refused
  say "Halt! Only trainers strong enough to face the Pokémon League may travel this road."
  return


# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the guards spoken to

script CascadeGuard
  face cascade_badge_guard player
  speaker "Guard"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE23 >= 2 goto Open
  say "I check for the Cascade Badge. No trainer passes me without it."
  end
label Open
  say "You've shown me the Cascade Badge. The way is open."
  end
