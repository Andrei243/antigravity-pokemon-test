# Alder's house in Floccesy Town (plan 22 · U5). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/floccesy_town_2.s, read for the order of the scene alone).
#
# Alder's lesson, played as the player is brought in on VAR_UNOVA_FLOCCESY 4: two of the Trainers' School's pupils
# battle the player in turn, each with one of the three elemental monkeys (Black 2's TRAINER_SCHOOL_KID_SEYMOUR and
# _CASSIE, _2 and _3 by the starter the player took: Seymour's is weak to it, Cassie's strong). Then Alder's talk of
# types, the team healed, five Oran Berries, and the player sent back up the hill to Cheren's Gym (5; Route 19's
# VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_19 3). The people: 0 the girl (Cassie), 1 the boy (Seymour), 2 Alder.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_UNOVA_FLOCCESY != 4 end
  scene "Alder's lesson"
  speaker "Girl"
  say "Oh! Are we going to help train this Trainer, sir?"
  speaker "Boy"
  say "Really? But they look really tough!"
  face 2 player
  speaker "Alder"
  say "Exactly! This Trainer may be tough, but there's as much to learn from losing as from winning."
  say "And haven't I always told you? What matters most is that you and your Pokémon enjoy yourselves!"
  say "So, {player}! Please be their opponent."
  speaker "Boy"
  face 1 player
  say "OK! I'll show you what's cool about my Pokémon!"
  if starter "Snivy" goto SeymourSnivy
  if starter "Tepig" goto SeymourTepig
  battle 1 as "unova_school_kid_seymour_3" canlose
  goto Seymoured
label SeymourSnivy
  battle 1 as "unova_school_kid_seymour" canlose
  goto Seymoured
label SeymourTepig
  battle 1 as "unova_school_kid_seymour_2" canlose
label Seymoured
  speaker "Alder"
  say "A fine battle, both of you! Now, before the next one, let's see to your Pokémon, {player}."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  speaker "Girl"
  face 0 player
  say "Some battles are decided by type matchups. Let me show you!"
  if starter "Snivy" goto CassieSnivy
  if starter "Tepig" goto CassieTepig
  battle 0 as "unova_school_kid_cassie_3" canlose
  goto Cassied
label CassieSnivy
  battle 0 as "unova_school_kid_cassie" canlose
  goto Cassied
label CassieTepig
  battle 0 as "unova_school_kid_cassie_2" canlose
label Cassied
  speaker "Alder"
  say "Now that was a rousing battle! I could tell every one of the Pokémon was enjoying itself."
  say "Well, {player}? Types matter a great deal in battle. Water beats Fire, Fire beats Grass, Grass beats Water."
  say "But listen! When a Pokémon uses a move of its own type, the move hits even harder. Remember that!"
  say "Let's heal these hard-working Pokémon. {starter}! You did a great job for {player}!"
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "One more thing before you go. Take these!"
  give "Oran Berry" 5
  say "Give a Pokémon an Oran Berry to hold, and it'll eat it by itself when its HP runs low in battle."
  say "Now, the Gym Leader in Aspertia is a very strong Trainer. But don't worry. You'll do just fine!"
  say "After that, take on stronger Trainers, and the Gym Leader of every city. Grow together with your Pokémon!"
  say "Meeting Pokémon and people you'd never have met otherwise. That's the best thing about travelling!"
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_FLOCCESY 5
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ROUTE_19 3
  endscene
  end


script Alder
  speaker "Alder"
  if badge basic goto Badge
  say "Go and show Aspertia's Gym Leader what you and your Pokémon can do!"
  end
label Badge
  say "Oho! You won a Gym Badge! That comes of understanding your Pokémon and drawing out their strength."
  end

script Girl
  speaker "Girl"
  say "My dream is to train my Pokémon here, and become the strongest Trainer in Unova!"
  end

script Boy
  speaker "Boy"
  say "I want to go to lots of places and learn all about Pokémon. Then I'll get even stronger!"
  end
