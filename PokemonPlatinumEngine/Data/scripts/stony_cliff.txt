# The Stony Cliff (plan 34 · PD4, its first session): the first Titan of the Path of Legends, Klawf, met in the gully's
# mouth, beaten and chased up the ramp to its den, beaten again there once the Herba Mystica has made it bigger, and the
# herbs it left shared with the mount, which can dash again. VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS is the path's count:
#   0  not begun
#   1  Arven met at Poco Path's lighthouse (FLAG_PALDEA_LEGENDS_BEGUN)
#   2  the Titan Klawf beaten in the gully's mouth and fled to its den (FLAG_PALDEA_KLAWF_FLED)
#   3  the Titan Klawf beaten at its den (FLAG_PALDEA_KLAWF_BEATEN), its herbs shared: the mount dashes (FLAG_PALDEA_MOUNT_DASH)
# The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats; the Titans are a totem's aura fought alone (`titan`).


# The Titan in the gully's mouth: the first battle, and its flight up the ramp
script Klawf
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS == 1 goto Titan
  end
label Titan
  scene "The Stony Cliff Titan"
  faceto titan_klawf player
  cry "Klawf"
  text "Rocks clatter down the gully. A Klawf the size of a cart scrambles out of the stones, its claws wide!"
  wildbattle "Klawf" 16 titan defense
  text "The Titan Klawf reels back... then turns and scuttles off up the gully toward the ledge at its head!"
  walkto titan_klawf klawf_den fast
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_KLAWF_FLED
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 2
  endscene
  end


# Up on the ledge: the Titan at its Herba Mystica, the second battle, and Arven come up the ramp to share the herbs
script KlawfDen
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS == 2 goto Den
  end
label Den
  scene "The Titan's Herba Mystica"
  faceto player titan_klawf_den
  text "The Titan Klawf is crouched at the back of the ledge, gulping down a clump of herbs that glow like embers."
  text "Its shell swells and shines. It turns on {player}, bigger than ever!"
  wildbattle "Klawf" 16 titan all
  text "The Titan Klawf is beaten. It shrinks back to an ordinary Klawf and slips away between the rocks."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_KLAWF_BEATEN
  faceto player arven_stony_cliff
  speaker "Arven"
  say "You did it! I heard the whole fight from the bottom of the ramp. And look: it didn't eat all of them."
  say "Herba Mystica. Five flavours, one in each Titan's den. Nothing in Paldea makes food stronger."
  say "Your ride's been limping since you met it, right? Hold on. Bread, a smear of this, a few herbs on top..."
  faceto arven_stony_cliff player
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT == 2 goto Miraidon
  text "Arven held out the sandwich. Koraidon wolfed it down in two bites and shook itself from head to tail."
  goto Strength
label Miraidon
  text "Arven held out the sandwich. Miraidon swallowed it whole and its lights blinked on, one after another."
label Strength
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_MOUNT_DASH
  text "Some of its old strength has come back. It can dash again! (Riding, hold the run button to sprint.)"
  say "That's the first. There are four more Titans out there, and four more piles of herbs."
  say "I'll find out where the next one's hiding. You keep riding. That thing's going to want to stretch its legs."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 3
  endscene
  end
