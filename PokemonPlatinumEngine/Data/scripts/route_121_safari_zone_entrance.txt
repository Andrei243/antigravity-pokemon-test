# The Safari Zone's gate on Route 121 (plan 21 · H6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route121_SafariZoneEntrance/scripts.inc).
#
# Whoever walks the one-tile passage before the counter steps onto Emerald's coordinate event there (the area's
# trigger 0) and is asked to play: for $500, thirty Safari Balls and Emerald's five hundred steps (`safari start 500`;
# Models/SafariGame.cs), then along the counter and in through the zone's door. Turned down, or short of the fee, they
# are sent a step back. Emerald asks for a Pokéblock Case first, which the Contest Lobby's receptionist gives (plan 21 ·
# H11, part b; before it the counter asked nothing); nor does it ask whether the boxes are full, as no gate of this game
# does.
# VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 2 tells the zone a player is coming in, and FLAG_GOOD_LUCK_SAFARI_ZONE is cleared, so its
# attendant wishes them luck once.
#
# A game that ended (the attendant inside the zone's door asked to let the player out, the last step, the last ball:
# common.SafariTimeUp and SafariOutOfBalls, which bring a player of Hoenn here with the variable 1) is walked out past
# the counter (Emerald's ExitSafariZone).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE != 1 end
  walk player right 7
  setvar VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 0
  end


# The counter, from Emerald's coordinate event before it (EntranceCounterTrigger)
script Counter
  if safari end
  face player up
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Safari Zone!"
  if item "Pokéblock Case" >= 1 goto HaveCase
  say "I'm sorry, but you can't come in without a Pokéblock Case. The Pokémon here are fond of Pokéblocks, and every visitor should carry some."
  say "The receptionist at the Contest Lobby in Lilycove City gives a Case to anyone who asks. Do come back with one!"
  walk player right 1
  end
label HaveCase
  ask "Catch as many Pokémon as you like for just $500! Would you like to play the Safari Game?"
  if no goto Away
  takemoney 500
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  safari start 500
  say "That's $500, thank you. Here are your Safari Balls."
  text "{player} received 30 Safari Balls!"
  say "We'll let you know when your game is over. Until then, off you go into the wild!"
  walk player left 6 down 1
  setvar VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 2
  clearflag FLAG_GOOD_LUCK_SAFARI_ZONE
  warp "HoennSafariZone" 72 75 down
  end
label Short
  say "I'm sorry, you don't have enough money."
label Away
  say "All right. Please come and play another time!"
  walk player right 1
  end


script CounterTalk
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Walk along the counter toward the zone's door, and I'll ask whether you'd like to play the Safari Game."
  end


script Welcome
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Safari Zone! Pokémon you'll hardly ever see in Hoenn live here in the wild."
  say "And trainers may catch them to keep. Come in and enjoy yourself!"
  end


script Explain
  speaker "Attendant"
  ask "Welcome to the Safari Zone! Is this your first visit?"
  if no goto Enjoy
  say "You go in with 30 Safari Balls, the only balls you may throw inside."
  say "The game is over when you've thrown your last ball, or when you've walked 500 steps."
label Enjoy
  say "Come in and enjoy the Safari Zone!"
  end
