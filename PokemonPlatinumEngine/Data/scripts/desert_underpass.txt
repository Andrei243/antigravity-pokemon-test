# The Desert Underpass (plan 21 · H12, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/DesertUnderpass/scripts.inc).
#
# The cavern the Fossil Maniac's digging broke into, behind his tunnel once Hoenn's Hall of Fame is entered. At its far
# east end lies the fossil Mirage Tower's fall left behind: the Claw Fossil for whoever took the Root Fossil there
# (FLAG_CHOSE_ROOT_FOSSIL), the Root Fossil for whoever took the Claw (FLAG_CHOSE_CLAW_FOSSIL), and nothing to take for
# whoever took neither. Taken, it is gone for good (FLAG_HIDE_DESERT_UNDERPASS_FOSSIL).


script Fossil
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_CHOSE_ROOT_FOSSIL goto Claw
  if flag FLAG_CHOSE_CLAW_FOSSIL goto Root
  text "A fossil is set fast in the rock. It won't come loose."
  end
label Claw
  find "Claw Fossil"
  goto Taken
label Root
  find "Root Fossil"
label Taken
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_DESERT_UNDERPASS_FOSSIL
  hide self
  end
