# The Big Stadium's stands (plan 42 · PS5; rulings, "Sports days"). Black 2's room, our words. While a game is on (the
# twilight hours) the crowd fills the seats (FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON, set behind the door's fade) and coming up the stairs
# plays the game's scene from the rail, once for each time the stands are come to.

script OnTransition
  if time twilight goto On
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON
  end
label On
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON
  end

script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON goto Quiet
  scene "StadiumGame"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera path 16 22 9 20 23 20 16 22 2
  sound "contest_cheer"
  text "The crowd is on its feet! The home side breaks down the field..."
  emote player exclaim 0.9
  text "And they score! The stands roar, and the scoreboard flickers with the new numbers."
  sound "contest_cheer"
  text "There's the final whistle! The players shake hands and wave to the crowd."
  camera release 0.6
  endscene
label Quiet
  end
