# Ultra Deep Sea (plan 23 · Z8), a world of Ultra Space. The lines are our own, on Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ultra Space and the Battle Royal Dome"): no picture, map, line or
# script of the games is read. It is come to through the Ultra Wormhole over the Altar's dais (poni_altar.txt) and left
# by the one it is come in by (common.UltraWayHome). Its Ultra Beast, Nihilego, waits at 32,16: met as a wild Pokémon
# after a staged beat, at level 60, and back each time the world is come to until it is caught
# (FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_NIHILEGO); FLAG_ALOLA_MET_NIHILEGO says it has been met, which the Lightseekers count.


script OnEnter
  effect rift 32 55 hold
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_NIHILEGO end
  text "Nothing here is water, yet everything drifts as if it were. Light falls like snow onto pale stone."
  end


script Beast
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "Nihilego"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 32 16 1.0
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 32 16 wait
  cry "Nihilego"
  text "Something like a jellyfish of glass hung over the stone, its tendrils trailing light. It turned toward {player}."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Nihilego" 60
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_NIHILEGO
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MET_NIHILEGO
  hide self
  end
