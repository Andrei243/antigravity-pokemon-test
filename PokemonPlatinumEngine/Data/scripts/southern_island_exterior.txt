# The sailor who brought the player stands on the sand and sails them back to Lilycove's harbour on request
# (Emerald's SouthernIsland_Exterior_EventScript_Sailor).


script Sailor
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  say "This is Southern Island. Nobody lives here, and I only come when the Champion's pass is shown."
  ask "Shall I take you back to Lilycove?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Aye. Hold on tight."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed back to Lilycove City..."
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Stay
  say "As you like. I'll be here when you're ready."
  end
