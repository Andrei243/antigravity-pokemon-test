# The gate between Route 7 and Saffron City (plan 19 · K5, parts a and b). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route7_EastEntrance/scripts.inc).
#
# The guard is thirsty on duty and turns everyone back from the three steps before him (the area's triggers 0 to 2,
# on VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE5_ROUTE6_ROUTE7_ROUTE8_GATES 0). Whoever brings the Tea that Celadon City's old lady gives
# (FLAG_GOT_TEA, celadon_city_condominiums_1f.txt) hands it over: he drinks, shares it with the other three gates'
# guards, and lets everyone through from then on (the variable 1, which ends the four gates' triggers).


script Guard
  face player up
  speaker "Guard"
  if item "Tea" goto Tea
  say "I'm on guard duty, and I'm parched! I'm not letting anyone through until I've had a drink."
  walk player left 1
  end
label Tea
  scene "Tea"
  say "I'm on guard duty, and I'm parched! Hey, is that tea you've got there?"
  take "Tea"
  text "{player} gave the guard the Tea."
  say "Glug, glug... That's better! I'll pass some along to the other gates. On you go!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE5_ROUTE6_ROUTE7_ROUTE8_GATES 1
  endscene
  end


script GuardTalk
  face policeman player
  speaker "Guard"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE5_ROUTE6_ROUTE7_ROUTE8_GATES == 1 goto Open
  say "Hi there. Saffron City's closed to travellers for now. Sorry about that."
  end
label Open
  say "That tea was delicious. Enjoy Saffron City!"
  end
