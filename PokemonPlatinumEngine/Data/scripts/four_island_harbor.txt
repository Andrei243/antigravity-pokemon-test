# Four Island's harbour (plan 19 · K9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/FourIsland_Harbor/scripts.inc).
#
# The sailor sails a holder of the Rainbow Pass to any port (common.SeagallopRainbow); nobody comes this far without
# it. The ship stays out of sight, as One Island's does (FLAG_HIDE_FOUR_ISLAND_HARBOR_SEAGALLOP, a flag of ours).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FOUR_ISLAND_HARBOR_SEAGALLOP
  end


script Sailor
  face sailor player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if not item "Rainbow Pass" goto NoPass
  say "Welcome aboard the Seagallop! Have your pass ready."
  setvar VAR_SEAGALLOP_HERE 5
  call SeagallopRainbow
  end
label NoPass
  say "The Seagallop sails only for holders of a pass, I'm afraid."
  end
