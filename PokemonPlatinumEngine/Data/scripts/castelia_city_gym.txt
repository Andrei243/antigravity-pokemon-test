# The Castelia Gym (plan 22 · U5, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/castelia_city_gym.s and castelia_city_gym_2.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Clyde (4) gives a challenger Fresh Water at the door (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_CASTELIA). Burgh (burgh) waits at the
# head of the hall once he is back from the sewers: his battle (Black 2's TRAINER_LEADER_BURGH; _2 is the Challenge
# Mode's, plan 13's), the Insect Badge, the region's third (`givebadge insect`), and TM76 (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM76), which
# our data's table, Platinum's, fills with its own move; the city's count moves on (VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 10), and
# Colress waits on the plaza (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_PLAZA cleared).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script GymGuide
  face 4 player
  speaker "Clyde"
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_CASTELIA goto Water
  if badge insect goto Won
  say "Burgh waits at the far end of the hall. His Bug types are tough, but Fire, Flying and Rock moves hit them hard!"
  end
label Water
  say "Hey there, future champ! Welcome to the Castelia Gym. Burgh's taste in decoration is... sticky. Mind the honey!"
  say "Burgh's Bug types fold to Fire, Flying and Rock moves. Here, have a drink before you start!"
  give "Fresh Water"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_CASTELIA
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Burgh! Your battle was a work of art. I might even ask him to paint it!"
  end


script Burgh
  face burgh player
  speaker "Burgh"
  if badge insect goto Won
  scene "Burgh"
  say "Welcome! I've just got back from the sewers, and do you know what? My muse came back with me!"
  say "I'm Burgh, Leader of this Gym and painter of Bug Pokémon. I'll paint our battle onto the canvas of your memory!"
  battle burgh as "unova_leader_burgh"
  say "Oh ho... You're strong, all right. I suppose losing was no surprise. Here, the Insect Badge! I think it suits you!"
  givebadge insect
  text "{player} received the Insect Badge from Burgh!"
  defeat "unova_harlequin_clarence"
  defeat "unova_harlequin_gary"
  defeat "unova_harlequin_anders"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA < 10 setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 10
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_PLAZA
label Tm
  say "And, well, have this too. Take it as thanks for the inspiration!"
  give "TM76"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM76
  say "The Insect Badge looks even better on you than I imagined. Route 4 is north of the plaza, through the gate. Off you go!"
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM76 goto Tm
  say "Your Pokémon and you painted something wonderful together. Come back and show me how you've grown!"
  end
