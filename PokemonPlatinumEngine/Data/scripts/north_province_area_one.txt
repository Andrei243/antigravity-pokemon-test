# The Zero Gate (plan 34 · PD8, its second session): Arven, Nemona and Penny wait before it once the League is won
# (PaldeaHallOfFame shows them). Spoken to, any of them begins the meeting when the Path of Legends and Starfall Street
# are done too (VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 12, VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL 6), or says which is still to do. The meeting: Penny owns up to
# being Cassiopeia, Arven tells of the professor's message, and the professor's voice opens the gate
# (FLAG_PALDEA_ZERO_GATE_OPEN, which the door waits for; VAR_PALDEA_ZERO 1, AreaZero.txt's count). Our own lines.
script ZeroGate
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_ZERO_GATE_OPEN goto Open
  if flag PaldeaHallOfFame goto Champion
  end
label Champion
  speaker "Arven"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS < 12 goto Legends
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STARFALL < 6 goto Starfall
  scene "The friends at the Zero Gate"
  say "There you are. A Champion now, huh? Nemona hasn't stopped talking about it the whole way up the valley."
  speaker "Nemona"
  say "Because it's amazing! But Arven says this is even bigger than the League, so here I am."
  speaker "Penny"
  say "...Hi. You know my voice, I think. I'm Penny. I'm the one who called herself Cassiopeia."
  say "Team Star were my friends. I wanted them to stop before the academy threw them out, and you made them stop. Thank you."
  speaker "Arven"
  say "The professor sent me a message: come down to the Zero Lab at the bottom of the crater, and bring the strongest Trainers I know."
  say "Yeah, that professor. The one I grew up waiting for. I'm not going down there alone, so... this is the team."
  text "The speaker beside the gate crackles into life."
  call Voice
  say "You've come, all four of you. The gate is open. Take the lift down, and follow the research stations to the lab."
  text "With a deep hum, the Zero Gate unlocks."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_ZERO_GATE_OPEN
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ZERO 1
  endscene
  end
label Legends
  say "Not yet. The Herba Mystica come first, and there's still a Titan out there guarding one."
  end
label Starfall
  speaker "Penny"
  say "Um... not yet. Team Star still has a boss on Starfall Street. I'd like that to be over before we go down."
  end
label Open
  end


# The professor's voice, by the version (VAR_PALDEA_VERSION: 1 Scarlet's Sada, 2 Violet's Turo)
script Voice
  if var VAR_PALDEA_VERSION == 2 goto Turo
  speaker "Professor Sada"
  return
label Turo
  speaker "Professor Turo"
  return
