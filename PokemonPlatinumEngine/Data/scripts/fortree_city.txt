# Fortree City (plan 21 · H6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/FortreeCity/scripts.inc).
#
# The city in the treetops: houses built round the trunks, joined by rope bridges. A Kecleon nobody can see stands on
# the bridge to the Gym until the player looks at it through the Devon Scope, when it shows itself and runs off for
# good (FLAG_KECLEON_FLED_FORTREE, FLAG_HIDE_FORTREE_CITY_KECLEON).


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_VISITED_FORTREE_CITY
  end


# The woman who wants to go to the Gym
script Woman
  face self player
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_KECLEON_FLED_FORTREE goto Open
  say "I want to go to the Gym, but something's in the way on the bridge. I can't see anything, yet I can't get past!"
  end
label Open
  say "Whatever was on the bridge has gone. This time I'll beat Winona, you'll see!"
  end


# What stands on the bridge to the Gym: it flees once seen
script Kecleon
  call HoennKecleonLook
  effect sparkles 345 8 wait
  cry "Kecleon"
  text "The Pokémon was startled and fled!"
  hide self
  setflag own
  setflag FLAG_KECLEON_FLED_FORTREE
  end
