# The Trainer Hill's roof (plan 21, part l). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the beats
# of Emerald's Trainer Hill (TrainerHill_Roof), read for the order alone: the owner holds the climb's time against the
# challenge's best and gives Emerald's prize for it (Models/Frontier/TrainerHill.cs, PrizeFor). The lift in the south
# wall goes back down to the lobby, where the climb ends.


script Owner
  face self player
  speaker "Owner"
  trainerhill active
  if result == 3 goto Climbed
  say "Quite a view from up here, isn't it? Climb all four floors and you'll find me waiting with a prize."
  end
label Climbed
  trainerhill record
  if result == 2 goto Again
  say "You made it to the roof! All four floors in {hill:TIME}."
  if result == 0 goto Record
  say "A fine climb, though your best for this challenge still stands at {hill:BEST}."
  goto Prize
label Record
  say "That's a new record for this challenge! I'll have it put up on the board downstairs."
label Prize
  say "Here, this is for your trouble. The faster you climb, the better the prize."
  trainerhill prize
label Again
  say "Take the lift down whenever you're ready, and come and climb again!"
  end
