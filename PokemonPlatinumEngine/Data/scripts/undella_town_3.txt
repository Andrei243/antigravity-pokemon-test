# The visitors' house of Undella Town (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language. The holiday-makers who are not
# there every day are hidden by FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_UNDELLA_SPARE, which the chapter's catch-up sets (common.UnovaLacunosa).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  end


# Cynthia (1; plan 22 · U10, part h): once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered she spends her holidays here, in the socialite's (6)
# place, and battles once a day with Black 2's team (unova_cynthia, levels 76 and 78), as Black 2's does in her villa
# (undella_town_3.s, read for the order alone). Won or lost, she battles again the next day (FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_CYNTHIA).
script Cynthia
  face 1 player
  speaker "Cynthia"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_CYNTHIA goto Tomorrow
  say "Oh, it's you. The new Champion of Unova! I come to Undella every year to rest, but I never really stop thinking about battles."
  ask "Would you battle me? I'd like to see how far you and your Pokémon have come."
  if no goto Later
  battle self as "unova_cynthia" canlose
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_CYNTHIA
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Thank you. A battle like that clears the mind better than any holiday."
  say "Come back tomorrow, if you like. I'll be here, looking at the sea."
  end
label Later
  say "Of course. The sea isn't going anywhere, and neither am I."
  end
label Tomorrow
  say "One good battle a day is plenty. Let's have another tomorrow."
  end
