# The Plasma Frigate's deck (plan 22 · U6, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/plasma_frigate.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The first boarding is one scene, played as the player comes aboard from the gangway at the Pokémon World
# Tournament's pier while VAR_UNOVA_PWT is 1 (pwt.txt): Hugh (0) and Cheren (1) are on deck, two grunts (15, 14) stand in
# the way and battle the player in turn (Black 2's TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_GRUNT_5 and _6), Zinzolin (16) comes down from
# the bow, and the Shadow Triad (17 to 19) put all three back ashore on the pier, where the grounds' arrival goes on.
# Black 2 also has Cheren and the player clear a second band of grunts before Zinzolin comes; ours leaves them standing.
# Come aboard at any other time, the grunts at the rail see the player straight back down the gangway, but at anchor off Route
# 21 (plan 22 · U8, part b: FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_AT_ROUTE_21), where Marlon's gangplank is the way aboard.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  if var VAR_UNOVA_PWT == 1 goto Boarding
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_AT_ROUTE_21 end
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Hey! This is a private ship! Off you go, back down the gangway!"
  warp "Unova" 198 494 left
  end
label Boarding
  scene "Aboard the Plasma Frigate"
  face 0 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! You came! This ship's freezing... why is it so cold up on deck?"
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "It's dressed up as a cargo ship, but look at the crew. This is Team Plasma's ship, all right."
  face 15 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Team Plasma? Correct answer! And there are a lot more of us here than there are of you!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Then just tell us where you're keeping the Pokémon you've stolen! My sister's Purrloin first!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Ha! Beat us first. And you, the quiet one, you're up first!"
  battle 15 as "unova_team_plasma_grunt_5"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Next! Somebody, next!"
  face 14 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "My turn! You won't get past me so easy!"
  battle 14 as "unova_team_plasma_grunt_6"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "What kind of Trainer is this?! Fall back, fall back!"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_FRIGATE_GRUNTS
  hide 14
  hide 15
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "Good work. The way to the bow is open. Let's see who's giving the orders on this ship."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZINZOLIN_FRIGATE
  show 16
  walk 16 down 4
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "Oh, my. The Gym Leader and a pair of children. Once again, we are made to feel unwelcome."
  say "No matter. This ship will soon cover all of Unova in ice. The Shadow Triad will see you off."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_FRIGATE
  show 17
  show 18
  show 19
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "By order of Lord Ghetsis, you leave this ship. Now."
  fade out 0.6
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZINZOLIN_FRIGATE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_FRIGATE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_FRIGATE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_FRIGATE
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_PWT_PIER
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_PWT_PIER
  speaker none
  text "There is a rush of wind, and the deck is gone from under your feet..."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_PWT 2
  endscene
  warp "Unova" 197 490 up
  fade in
  end
