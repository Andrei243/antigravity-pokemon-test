# Bebe's house in Hearthome City (plan 01 · M11, part a), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_hearthome_city_northwest_house.s).
#
# Bebe, who looks after the Pokémon Boxes, offers an Eevee at level 20, once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_EEVEE; FLAG_MET_BEBE once she has said who she is). Only a team with
# room takes it, as in the original. The nickname the original offers afterwards waits for the naming prompt
# (plan 12 · Q19).


script Bebe
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_EEVEE goto After
  if flag FLAG_MET_BEBE goto Again
  setflag FLAG_MET_BEBE
  say "Hiya! Oh, you're a Trainer! Then you use the Pokémon Boxes! I love meeting the people who use my work!" "... Why the blank look? Oh, right, sorry! There I go, rattling on again." "I'm Bebe! You know the Boxes on the PC? I'm the one who keeps them running!"
  ask "This is out of the blue, I know, but would you like a Pokémon called Eevee?"
  goto Answer
label Again
  ask "Bebe: So, how about it? Would you like that Eevee?"
label Answer
  if no goto Declined
  if party >= 6 goto Full
  say "Bebe: Here you go! A good friend of mine in Johto gave it to me. He has more Eevee than he can count." "Sorry, that won't mean a thing to you. Just be good to it, please!"
  givepokemon "Eevee" 20
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_NORTHWEST_HOUSE_EEVEE
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received the Eevee!"
  end
label Full
  say "Bebe: Here you go! ... No, wait, I can't! You haven't room for another Pokémon!" "Now that's exactly what my Boxes are for!"
  end
label Declined
  say "Bebe: Oh, OK... Then I suppose I'll raise it myself."
  end
label After
  if nationaldex goto National
  say "Bebe: My friend Bill built the first Pokémon storage system." "I've tinkered with it here and there since, to make it easier to use."
  end
label National
  say "Bebe: Oh? You've filled the whole Sinnoh Pokédex? Good for you! You'll be needing my Boxes more than ever." "Mind you, I always did think a good Trainer juggles Boxes cleverly. Not that I'm one to say so."
  end
