# Team Magma's hideout, its top floor (plan 21 · H7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/MagmaHideout_4F/scripts.inc).
#
# Maxie stands before Groudon, asleep in the magma, with the Blue Orb taken from Mt. Pyre. Spoken to, he holds the orb
# up to it: its light wakes Groudon, which rises, shakes the hall and is gone into the mountain. There is no battle with
# Groudon, as Emerald has none here: it is taken off the map (FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_4F_GROUDON_ASLEEP and
# FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_4F_GROUDON both set). Maxie, at a loss, turns on the player and battles; beaten, he goes after
# Groudon with all of Team Magma (FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_GRUNTS). What Emerald sets with it is set here: the
# awakening (FLAG_GROUDON_AWAKENED_MAGMA_HIDEOUT), Captain Stern back in Slateport City and interviewed outside its
# harbour by Gabby and Ty (VAR_SLATEPORT_CITY_STATE 1, FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_CAPTAIN_STERN and
# FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_GABBY_AND_TY cleared) and Team Aqua ready to take his submarine from the harbour
# (VAR_SLATEPORT_HARBOR_STATE 1; slateport_city.txt, slateport_city_harbor.txt).


script Maxie
  if flag FLAG_GROUDON_AWAKENED_MAGMA_HIDEOUT end
  scene "Groudon wakes in Team Magma's hideout"
  music "hoenn/eye_magma"
  speaker "Maxie"
  say "Groudon... Asleep for so long in your bed of magma, where nothing could reach you."
  say "This is the Blue Orb. It is what you were waiting for, isn't it? I have brought it to you."
  say "Let its light wake you! And show me... show me all that your power can do!"
  wait 0.3
  effect sparkles 16 17 wait
  music "hoenn/awaken_legend"
  effect burst 16 17
  face player up
  wait 2.5
  hide magma_hideout_4f_groudon_sleeping
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_4F_GROUDON_ASLEEP
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_4F_GROUDON
  show magma_hideout_4f_groudon
  cry "Groudon"
  wait 1
  walk magma_hideout_4f_groudon down 2
  camera shake 1.2
  wait 1.2
  walk magma_hideout_4f_groudon up 2
  hide magma_hideout_4f_groudon
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_4F_GROUDON
  camera shake 1.2
  wait 1.4
  face magma_hideout_4f_maxie left
  wait 0.3
  face magma_hideout_4f_maxie right
  wait 0.3
  face magma_hideout_4f_maxie left
  wait 0.3
  face magma_hideout_4f_maxie up
  wait 0.5
  music "hoenn/eye_magma"
  speaker "Maxie"
  say "Groudon! What is the matter with you?"
  say "Wasn't the Blue Orb the key? Groudon! Where have you gone?"
  face player magma_hideout_4f_maxie
  wait 0.5
  face magma_hideout_4f_maxie player
  emote magma_hideout_4f_maxie exclaim
  say "So it was you. I've seen you nosing about wherever Team Magma has business..."
  say "Now I see it. You must have played some cheap trick on us!"
  battle self as "hoenn_maxie_magma_hideout"
  speaker "Maxie"
  say "How can a child handle Pokémon so well?"
  say "There must be some reason Groudon fled. That's what you would tell me, isn't it?"
  say "......"
  say "Hmph. Do you think I don't know that already?"
  say "With Groudon gone, I have no more use for this wretched volcano. I am going after Groudon. This is where we part."
  setflag FLAG_GROUDON_AWAKENED_MAGMA_HIDEOUT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_CAPTAIN_STERN
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SLATEPORT_CITY_GABBY_AND_TY
  setvar VAR_SLATEPORT_CITY_STATE 1
  setvar VAR_SLATEPORT_HARBOR_STATE 1
  fade out
  hide magma_hideout_4f_maxie
  hide magma_hideout_4f_grunt_1
  hide magma_hideout_4f_grunt_2
  hide magma_hideout_4f_grunt_3
  hide magma_hideout_4f_tabitha
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_GRUNTS
  music area
  fade in
  endscene
  end


# Groudon asleep, looked at before Maxie has woken it
