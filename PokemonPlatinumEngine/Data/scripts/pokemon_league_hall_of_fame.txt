# Kanto's Hall of Fame (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats
# (data/maps/PokemonLeague_HallOfFame/scripts.inc, and its special EnterHallOfFame).
#
# One scene, played as the new Champion comes in with Oak ahead of them: his words, the team recorded (halloffame),
# Kanto's story ended (KantoHallOfFame, the region's flag, and FireRed's own FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR), the scene of the
# team's entry, the team healed, home to the player's room in Pallet Town, the game saved there, and the credits. The
# room's floor is FireRed's polished one (MB_ICE, which slides whoever walks on it), so nobody walks here: Oak turns to
# the player at the door. What a Champion of Kanto finds afterwards (the sailor at Vermilion's harbour, the Sevii
# Islands' errand and the National Pokédex) is plan 19 · K8's and K9's.


script OnEnter
  scene "The Hall of Fame"
  face hall_of_fame_prof_oak down
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "Here we are. This is the Hall of Fame, where the names of the League's Champions are kept for all time."
  say "Every trainer who has stood where you're standing came here with the Pokémon who carried them to victory."
  say "{player}, you and your Pokémon have earned your place among them. Let's record your team, so nobody ever forgets."
  endscene
  wait 0.4
  fade out 0.8
  halloffame
  setflag KantoHallOfFame
  setflag FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR
  halloffame show
  heal
  warp "PalletTown_PlayersHouse_2F" 6 6 up
  save
  if result == 0 goto NotSaved
  sound "save"
  text "{player} saved the game."
  goto Roll
label NotSaved
  text "The game could not be saved."
label Roll
  credits
  end
