# Route 207 (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_route_207.s).

# Just before Mt. Coronet, the professor's assistant catches the player up with two things from the lab: a Vs. Seeker,
# hidden in one hand or the other, and the Dowsing Machine for the Pokétch. VAR_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART_TRIGGER_STATE:
# 1 once they have been given (Eterna City's bug catcher goes back to talking about the wind then).


# The way into Mt. Coronet (the original's trigger 0, a column three tiles deep at x 340, state 0): the assistant
# comes running along the road from the west, stops beside the player and holds out two closed hands.
script Assistant
  if var PLAYER_Y == 712 place counterpart 331 712 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 713 place counterpart 331 713 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 714 place counterpart 331 714 right
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART
  show counterpart
  walk counterpart right 3
  emote counterpart exclaim
  music "common/assistant"
  walk counterpart right 5
  face player left
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "{player}! Phew, I caught you before the mountain! The professor sent me after you with something."
  choose "I've got it in one of my hands. Which one?" "Right" "Left"
  say "Ta-da! A Vs. Seeker! Charge it up by walking, then use it, and Trainers you've already beaten will want a rematch."
  setflag FLAG_UNLOCKED_VS_SEEKER_LVL_1
  give "Vs. Seeker"
  say "And this is for your Pokétch. You'll love it, I promise."
  poketchapp DowsingMachine
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Dowsing Machine was added to the Pokétch!"
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Tap it on the ground and it shows where hidden items are buried. Try it all over Sinnoh!"
  say "Right, I'd better get back to the lab. Mt. Coronet is a maze inside, so don't get lost, {player}!"
  walk counterpart left 8
  hide counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART_TRIGGER_STATE 1
  end


# The cyclist at the foot of the muddy slope (the original's script 4): the slope needs a Bicycle in its fast gear.
script Cyclist
  speaker "Cyclist"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_BICYCLE goto Bike
  say "That muddy slope is far too slippery to walk up. You'll only slide straight back down."
  end
label Bike
  say "Put your Bicycle in its fast gear, take a run at the slope and don't stop pedalling till you're at the top!"
  end
