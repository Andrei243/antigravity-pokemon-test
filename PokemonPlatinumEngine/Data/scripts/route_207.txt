# Route 207 (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_route_207.s).
#
# At the foot of Mt. Coronet (the original's trigger 0, a column three tiles deep at x 340), the assistant comes running
# up the route with a present from the professor: a guessing game of which hand, then the Vs. Seeker either way, and
# the Dowsing Machine for the Pokétch. VAR_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART_TRIGGER_STATE: 0 not met, 1 met.


script Assistant
  # She comes up the route from the west, level with the player (the original's SetObjectEventPos 331, z)
  if var PLAYER_Y == 712 place counterpart 331 712 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 713 place counterpart 331 713 right
  if var PLAYER_Y == 714 place counterpart 331 714 right
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART
  show counterpart
  emote counterpart exclaim
  music "common/assistant"
  walk counterpart right 8
  face player left
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "{player}! Phew, I caught you before the mountain. The professor asked me to bring you something."
  choose "Here's a game first. Which hand do you think it's in?" "Right" "Left"
  say "...Ha ha! It doesn't matter which, actually. You win either way!"
  # The first level of rematches comes with it (the original's Route207_GiveVsSeeker; set since plan 02 · S9)
  setflag FLAG_UNLOCKED_VS_SEEKER_LVL_1
  give "Vs. Seeker"
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "It's a Vs. Seeker. Use it, and any trainer you've beaten who wants a rematch will let you know."
  say "Oh, and one more thing, since you've got a Pokétch. Have this too!"
  fanfare keyitem
  poketchapp DowsingMachine
  text "{player} added the Dowsing Machine app to the Pokétch!"
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "It finds items hidden in the ground nearby. Tap it now and then on your way, and you'll be surprised what turns up!"
  say "Right, I'm off back to the lab. Good luck on Mt. Coronet, {player}!"
  walk counterpart left 8
  hide counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_ROUTE_207_COUNTERPART_TRIGGER_STATE 1
  end
