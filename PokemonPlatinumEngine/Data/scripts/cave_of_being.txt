# The Cave of Being on Route 20 (plan 22 · U10, the last part). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on Black 2's beats (the three of the lakes rising out of the cave and away, with a Juniper there to see it).
#
# Once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered Cedric Juniper waits in the cave. The first step into its heart plays the scene once
# (VAR_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING 0 to 1): Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf rise, cry and fly off, and he tells where they have gone, which
# is where they are met: Azelf on Route 23 and Mesprit over the Celestial Tower (plan 22 · U10, part a), and Uxie, which no
# place of ours holds yet, nowhere the player can follow. Before the Hall of Fame the cave is quiet.


script TheThree
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  scene "The lakes' three in the Cave of Being"
  music stop
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING_RISING
  show uxie
  show mesprit
  show azelf
  letterbox on 0.4
  effect sparkles 17 7
  cry "Uxie"
  effect sparkles 9 16
  cry "Mesprit"
  effect sparkles 27 18
  cry "Azelf"
  speaker none
  text "Three small Pokémon rise out of the cave's dark, glowing!"
  effect burst 18 14
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING_FLOWN
  hide uxie
  hide mesprit
  hide azelf
  text "In a flash of light they are gone!"
  letterbox off 0.3
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING 1
  speaker "Prof. Cedric Juniper"
  say "Did you see that? Uxie, Mesprit and Azelf! They have slept in this cave since long before any of us."
  say "They've scattered over Unova now. Azelf toward Route 23, Mesprit up to the Celestial Tower, and Uxie... somewhere far off."
  say "If you go looking, be gentle with them. They feel everything people feel."
  endscene
  end


script Cedric
  face self player
  speaker "Prof. Cedric Juniper"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_CAVE_OF_BEING == 1 goto After
  say "Shh! Something lives deep in this cave. Walk in slowly and see."
  end
label After
  say "My daughter will never believe I saw all three at once. Good thing you were here to back me up!"
  end
