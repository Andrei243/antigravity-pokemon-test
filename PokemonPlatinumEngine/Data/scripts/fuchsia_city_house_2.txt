# The fishing brother's house in Fuchsia City (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/FuchsiaCity_House2/scripts.inc): the Fishing Guru's brother gives
# a Good Rod to a trainer who likes to fish (FLAG_GOT_GOOD_ROD).


script FishingGurusBrother
  speaker "Fisherman"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_GOOD_ROD goto Biting
  ask "I'm the Fishing Guru's older brother. I bet you like fishing too, eh?"
  if yes goto Give
  say "Oh. That's a pity. You don't know what you're missing!"
  end
label Give
  say "Ha! A fellow angler! Here, this is a better rod than my little brother's."
  give "Good Rod"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_GOOD_ROD
  say "A Good Rod lands better Pokémon than an Old Rod. Try it on the sea!"
  end
label Biting
  say "Are the fish biting for you? Keep at it!"
  end
