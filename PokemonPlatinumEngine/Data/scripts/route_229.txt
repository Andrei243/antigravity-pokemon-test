# The man by the pond (the original's script 1) is sure the player has walked off with the Nuggets he dropped, and
# gives two of them away to be rid of the argument, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_229_NUGGET).

script Pokefan
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_229_NUGGET goto Mine
  say "You! You've been picking things up off the ground round here, haven't you? I can tell by the look of you!"
  say "Oh, don't make that face. Here. Let this settle it between us."
  give "Nugget"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_229_NUGGET
  say "Still sulking? Then take another, and let that be the end of it!"
  give "Nugget"
label Mine
  say "Every Nugget you find lying about on this route is one of mine. I dropped the lot. Finders keepers, I suppose!"
  end
