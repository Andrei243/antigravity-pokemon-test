# Cyllage City's bicycle shop (plan 23, Kalos finished; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Parfum Palace, the bicycle shop
# and the rest"). The lines are our own. X and Y's shop gives the player a bicycle of the colour chosen; ours waits for its
# parts until the power is back and Lumiose's roads open (VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 2, the Power Plant's), then gives a
# Bicycle to a bag without one, as a Champion of an earlier region already rides one (plan 23's rule for a region's key
# items), and only talks to a bag that has one.


script Owner
  face owner player
  speaker "Shop Owner"
  if var VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS < 2 goto Waiting
  if item "Bicycle" goto Riding
  say "My parts came through from Lumiose at last! And you were the first through my door all those empty weeks."
  say "So the first bicycle off the bench is yours. No charge. Just tell everyone where you got it!"
  give "Bicycle"
  end
label Riding
  say "A fine bicycle you have there. Keep the chain oiled and it will carry you round Kalos and back."
  end
label Waiting
  say "Welcome! I am afraid the shelves are empty. The parts for my bicycles are stuck in Lumiose until the roads open."
  end
