# The Driftveil Gym's floor, a mine of ours (plan 22 · U6, part b; Data/maps/DriftveilCity_Gym.json). docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's
# res/field/scripts/driftveil_city_gym.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Black 2's Gym is a mine of lifts on rails, which the reader leaves out: ours is a hall of the mine with rock walls
# zigzagging up it and Black 2's seven workers on its shifts (their trainers of the data). Clay (clay) waits at its head:
# his battle (Black 2's TRAINER_LEADER_CLAY; _2 is the Challenge Mode's, plan 13's), the Quake Badge, the region's
# fifth (`givebadge quake`), and TM78 (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM78), which our data's table, Platinum's, fills with its own move.
# Driftveil's count moves on (VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL 4), and Clay and Hugh wait outside the Gym.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script Clay
  face clay player
  speaker "Clay"
  if badge quake goto Won
  scene "Clay"
  say "So you dug your way down here. I'm Clay, boss of these mines and Leader of this Gym. I don't go easy on anybody!"
  say "Let's see if your team's got the grit to strike it rich against mine!"
  battle clay as "unova_leader_clay"
  say "Well, I'll be. You struck a seam I never saw comin'. Here, you earned it fair and square."
  givebadge quake
  text "{player} received the Quake Badge from Clay!"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL < 4 setvar VAR_UNOVA_DRIFTVEIL 4
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CLAY_DRIFTVEIL
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_GYM
label Tm
  say "Your fifth Badge, is it? Then take this along with it. A move that shakes the ground and slows down whoever it hits."
  give "TM78"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM78
  say "Now get on up out of my mine. I've got work to do, and so have you!"
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM78 goto Tm
  say "Hard work never lies, kid. Not to you, and not to your Pokémon."
  end
