# New Mauville's entrance (plan 21 · H12, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/NewMauville_Entrance/scripts.inc).
#
# The plant under Route 110's Cycling Road is reached by surfing to its door. The door is locked until Wattson's
# Basement Key opens it (FLAG_GOT_BASEMENT_KEY_FROM_WATTSON, his errand once Norman is beaten): the step before it asks,
# and the key used sets VAR_NEW_MAUVILLE_STATE 1, Emerald's, which world.json's swap reads to lay the shut door's
# blocks, and FLAG_HOENN_NEW_MAUVILLE_OPENED, a flag of ours the overlay's door is opened by. A Basement Key of another
# region's (HeartGold's) is not Wattson's, so the script asks his flag and not the bag.


script Door
  face player up
  speaker none
  text "The door is locked."
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_BASEMENT_KEY_FROM_WATTSON end
  ask "Use the Basement Key?"
  if no end
  text "{player} used the Basement Key. The door opened!"
  sound "door_open"
  setvar VAR_NEW_MAUVILLE_STATE 1
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_NEW_MAUVILLE_OPENED
  end
