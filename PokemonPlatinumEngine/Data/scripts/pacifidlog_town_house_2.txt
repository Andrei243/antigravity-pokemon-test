# A house of Pacifidlog Town (plan 21 · H8, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/PacifidlogTown_House2/scripts.inc).
#
# The Pokémon Fan Club chairman's younger brother reads how fond of the player the first of the team is
# (LEAD_FRIENDSHIP, Emerald's GetLeadMonFriendshipScore): 150 or more gives TM27 Return, under 50 TM21 Frustration, and
# between he gives nothing. Emerald gives one a week (VAR_PACIFIDLOG_TM_RECEIVED_DAY); here one a day
# (FLAG_DAILY_PACIFIDLOG_RECEIVED_TM), as the plan has it. His two Azurill cry: one loves its trainer, one doesn't.


script FanClubYoungerBrother
  face self player
  speaker "Gentleman"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_PACIFIDLOG_RECEIVED_TM goto Tomorrow
  if flag FLAG_MET_FANCLUB_YOUNGER_BROTHER goto Look
  say "I'm the younger brother of the Pokémon Fan Club's chairman. I've come out to Pacifidlog for a long holiday."
  setflag FLAG_MET_FANCLUB_YOUNGER_BROTHER
label Look
  say "Let me have a look at the Pokémon you're with..."
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 150 goto Loving
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 50 goto Between
  say "Oh dear. It hasn't taken to you at all. A Pokémon like that should have this."
  give "TM21"
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_PACIFIDLOG_RECEIVED_TM
  goto Explain
label Loving
  say "My, my! It simply adores you. A Pokémon like that deserves this."
  give "TM27"
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_PACIFIDLOG_RECEIVED_TM
label Explain
  say "Return hits harder the more a Pokémon loves its trainer. Frustration hits harder the less it does."
  end
label Between
  say "Hmm, neither here nor there. Spend more time together, and come back to show me."
  end
label Tomorrow
  say "Come back tomorrow, and I'll have another look at your Pokémon."
  end


script HappyAzurill
  cry own
  speaker "Azurill"
  say "Rurii!"
  speaker none
  text "It looks very fond of its trainer."
  end


script UnhappyAzurill
  cry own
  speaker "Azurill"
  say "...Rii."
  speaker none
  text "It doesn't seem very fond of its trainer."
  end
