# The Musical Theater's lobby in Nimbasa City (plan 22 · U6, part b; the room's people are its overlay's). docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own. Black 2 has nobody to meet here; Meloetta is ours, after Black and White's
# event (plan 29 · LS11d): after dark, once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered, it sings before the stage's door.


# Meloetta is there only at night (FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS, kept here on every arrival)
script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  if time night setflag FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS
  if time latenight setflag FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS
  end


# Meloetta before the stage's door (meloetta; the recipe's mind and dreams row): a room, so the zoom alone; level 65, as
# Unova's other legends after the League; caught or beaten it is gone for good (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_MELOETTA), run from it is
# back the next night the lobby is come to
script Meloetta
  scene "Meloetta in the Musical Theater"
  music stop
  face player meloetta
  face meloetta player
  letterbox on 0.4
  # (one tile south of it, the player would hide it: they step back first, and the "!" over their head, which hid it
  # too, comes once it is in sight)
  if var PLAYER_X != 14 goto Look
  if var PLAYER_Y != 4 goto Look
  walk player down 2
  face player up
  emote player exclaim
label Look
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 14 3
  wait 0.4
  effect ripple 14 3
  flicker meloetta 6
  cry "Meloetta"
  speaker none
  text "A soft melody fills the quiet lobby, and a Pokémon flickers into sight!\nIt stops singing and turns to {player}!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Meloetta" 65
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_UNOVA_CAUGHT_MELOETTA
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_MELOETTA
  hide meloetta
  text "The lobby is silent. The last note seems to hang in the air..."
  end
label Fled
  hide meloetta
  text "Meloetta's song faded away behind the stage's door..."
  end
