# The Musical Theater's lobby in Nimbasa City (plan 22 · U6, part b; the room's people are its overlay's). docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own. Black 2 has nobody to meet here; Meloetta is ours, after Black and White's
# event (plan 29 · LS11d): after dark, once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered, it sings before the stage's door.


# Meloetta is there only at night (FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS, kept here on every arrival)
script OnEnter
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  if time night setflag FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS
  if time latenight setflag FLAG_UNOVA_MELOETTA_SINGS
  end


# Meloetta before the stage's door (meloetta; the recipe's mind and dreams row): a room, so the zoom alone; level 65, as
# Unova's other legends after the League; caught or beaten it is gone for good (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_MELOETTA), run from it is
# back the next night the lobby is come to
script Meloetta
  scene "Meloetta in the Musical Theater"
  music stop
  face player meloetta
  face meloetta player
  letterbox on 0.4
  # (one tile south of it, the player would hide it: they step back first, and the "!" over their head, which hid it
  # too, comes once it is in sight)
  if var PLAYER_X != 14 goto Look
  if var PLAYER_Y != 4 goto Look
  walk player down 2
  face player up
  emote player exclaim
label Look
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 14 3
  wait 0.4
  effect ripple 14 3
  flicker meloetta 6
  cry "Meloetta"
  speaker none
  text "A soft melody fills the quiet lobby, and a Pokémon flickers into sight!\nIt stops singing and turns to {player}!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Meloetta" 65
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_UNOVA_CAUGHT_MELOETTA
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_MELOETTA
  hide meloetta
  text "The lobby is silent. The last note seems to hang in the air..."
  end
label Fled
  hide meloetta
  text "Meloetta's song faded away behind the stage's door..."
  end


# The stage show (plan 42 · PS1, PS2; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The stage show"): the owner gives the trunk and a first
# handful of Props at the first word, the desk (the socialite, person 6) opens the dressing room and the stage, and the
# three fans of the lobby (persons 1, 4 and 18) each wait with a Prop after a show (their own lines otherwise)
script Owner
  face self player
  stageshow has
  if result == 1 goto Again
  say "Ah, a new face! I am the owner of this theater, and every Pokémon deserves a turn in the spotlight."
  say "Take this trunk. It holds a few Props to start with: hats, glasses, a cape and more."
  stageshow trunk
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Prop Trunk and {number} Props!"
  say "Dress a Pokémon of yours at the desk, then take the stage against our three performers. The crowd decides the rest!"
  end
label Again
  say "A musical is music, dance and costume all at once. Pokémon shine in all three!"
  say "Win the crowd's eyes with tricks on the beat, and wear Props that suit the show's mood."
  end

script Desk
  face self player
  stageshow has
  if result == 0 goto NoTrunk
  choose "Welcome, darling. What will it be today?" "DRESSING ROOM" "TAKE THE STAGE" "NOTHING"
  if result == 0 goto Dress
  if result == 1 goto Perform
  say "Do come back when you feel like shining."
  end
label Dress
  stageshow dress
  say "Divine! Your Pokémon will remember that look on stage."
  end
label Perform
  say "Splendid! Choose a show and a performer. Press on the beat when the spotlight finds you!"
  stageshow perform
  if result == 0 goto NotToday
  if result == 1 goto Won
  say "What a performance! The crowd will be talking about it all evening."
  end
label Won
  say "First place! The whole theater was looking at your Pokémon!"
  end
label NotToday
  say "Another time, then. The stage will wait for you."
  end
label NoTrunk
  say "The dressing room is through there. Only performers and their Pokémon may go in, darling."
  say "Speak to the owner first. He has something for every new performer."
  end
