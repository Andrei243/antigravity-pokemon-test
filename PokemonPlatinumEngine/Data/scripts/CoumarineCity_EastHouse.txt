# Coumarine City's east house (plan 23, Kalos's water; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's Gyms, its water and the
# Mamoswine ride"). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beat as it is publicly described: a fisherman of the port gives
# the Good Rod. The house stands in for the port, which the city's drawing doesn't have.


script GoodRod
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_GOOD_ROD goto After
  say "I fish the harbour every morning before the flower sellers are up. The bigger ones won't look at an old rod."
  say "You've got the look of an angler. Take my spare Good Rod and see what the deeper water holds."
  give "Good Rod"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_GOOD_ROD
  say "Out by the coast you'll pull in things with claws. Mind your fingers!"
  end
label After
  say "A Good Rod reaches further down. Further down is where the strange ones live."
  end
