# The Poké Ball Factory on Laverre City's east side (plan 23, Kalos's last places). The lines are our own, on X and Y's
# beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's last places"): once the Fairy Badge is won the
# factory's door opens on Team Flare, who have taken the works over to fill their own Poké Balls; three grunts stand among
# the machines (Kalos's trainers.json, kalos_flare_grunt_factory_1 to _3), and the president, held in his own office at
# the back, gives the Master Ball to whoever sends them off. X and Y have the neighbour beside the player and two of Team
# Flare's scientists at the back; ours is the player's alone, and those two are met under Geosenge (plan 23 · Z4). The
# factory is no step of the story, so nothing waits on it. FLAG_KALOS_FACTORY_DONE (ours) sends the grunts away.


script President
  face self player
  speaker "President"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_FACTORY_DONE goto Done
  if not defeated "kalos_flare_grunt_factory_1" goto Held
  if not defeated "kalos_flare_grunt_factory_2" goto Held
  if not defeated "kalos_flare_grunt_factory_3" goto Held
  scene "The factory president"
  say "They've gone? All three? You've no idea how long they've had me sitting at this desk, signing orders for their balls."
  say "Team Flare wanted every Poké Ball we could make, and they wanted the best of them for themselves."
  say "This one they never found. It catches any Pokémon there is, without fail. We make very few, and I'd like you to have it."
  give "Master Ball"
  say "Use it on the one Pokémon you never want to lose the chance of meeting again."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_FACTORY_DONE
  endscene
  end
label Held
  say "Please, be careful! Team Flare are all over the works. I can't get past them, and they won't let anyone in."
  end
label Done
  say "The lines are running again. Every ball you buy in Kalos started its life right here."
  end


script Worker
  face self player
  speaker "Worker"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_FACTORY_DONE goto Done
  say "They switched off half the lines and told us to make only their balls. Red ones, with an F. Hideous things."
  end
label Done
  say "Back to proper balls. You'd be amazed how many bad Poké Balls it takes to make one good one."
  end
