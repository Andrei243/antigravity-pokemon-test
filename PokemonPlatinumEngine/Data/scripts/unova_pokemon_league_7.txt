# Marshal's room of the Elite Four (plan 22 · U9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league_7.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_MARSHAL is 0 until Marshal is beaten in this challenge, 1 after; the plaza's arrival puts it back to 0, as a new
# challenge begins there. Marshal battles with Black 2's first team (unova_elite_four_marshal) and, once Unova's Hall of Fame has been
# entered, with the team of the second round (unova_elite_four_marshal_2). Once beaten, the light in the corner (Pad) carries the
# player back down to the League's hall, beside the arm to this room.


script Marshal
  face 0 player
  speaker "Marshal"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_MARSHAL == 1 goto Beaten
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Again
  say "I am Marshal of the Elite Four. I learned all I know from Alder, our former Champion."
  say "My Fighting-type Pokémon and I have trained our bodies and our spirits to the limit. Show me yours!"
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_marshal"
  goto Won
label Again
  say "You return to train with me again? Then I hold nothing back. Come!"
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_marshal_2"
label Won
  speaker "Marshal"
  say "Your strength... is the real thing. Your spirit, and your Pokémon's, would make my master proud."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_MARSHAL 1
  speaker none
  text "A light began to glow in the corner of the room."
  end
label Beaten
  say "Alder taught me that strength means nothing without care for others. You have both. Go on."
  end


# The light in the corner, once Marshal is beaten: back down to the League's hall
script Pad
  speaker none
  text "The light carried {player} back down to the hall."
  warp "UnovaPokemonLeague_Hall" 38 39 left
  end
