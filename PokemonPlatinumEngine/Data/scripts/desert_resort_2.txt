# The archaeologist (0) tells of the Relic Castle and gives Soft Sand once (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_SOFT_SAND).


script SoftSand
  face 0 player
  speaker "Archaeologist"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_SOFT_SAND goto Given
  say "They say the Relic Castle was a city once, built long ago by a hero and the dragon Pokémon at his side."
  say "Now the sand has nearly swallowed it. Here, take a little of the desert with you."
  give "Soft Sand"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_SOFT_SAND
label Given
  say "A Pokémon that holds Soft Sand hits harder with Ground-type moves. Fitting, for a place like this!"
  end
