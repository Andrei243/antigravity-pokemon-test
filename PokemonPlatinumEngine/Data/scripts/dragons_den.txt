# The Dragon's Den (plan 20 · J11, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D44R0102).
#
# Once Clair has given the Rising Badge in the shrine (VAR_UNK_40C4 1, dragons_den_shrine.txt) she waits by the way
# out; the first steps onto the row before her (trigger 0) bring her over to give TM59, Dragon Pulse
# (FLAG_GOT_TM59_FROM_CLAIR), and she goes back to her Gym (VAR_UNK_40C4 2; blackthorn.txt does the same for a player
# who leaves the Den another way, and blackthorn_gym.txt gives the TM there). The Den's own arrival hides her
# (FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR, HeartGold's own) unless she is waiting with the TM.
#
# After the Hall of Fame (plan 20 · J13, part c): HeartGold begins the Den's post-game with the rival's battle in Mt.
# Moon (VAR_UNK_40FC 1), which this game has not yet (Kanto's part of Johto is J15's), so the first arrival here once
# JohtoHallOfFame is set begins it instead (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The Dragon's Den after the League"). The
# red-haired boy waits by the lake's shrine and the first step onto the row before him (trigger 1) brings Lance and Clair
# for a double battle, the player beside the boy (VAR_UNK_40FC 2). From then on the boy trains here on Tuesdays and
# Thursdays, and Clair walks the island on her mornings until her number is in the Pokégear and then on her afternoons
# (HeartGold's hours 6 to 9 and 16 to 18, the time of day here), to swap numbers. Her photograph and her rematch hours
# (the Pokégear's, J3) are not part of this.


script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CHAMPIONS_ROOM_RETREAT
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_DRAGONS_DEN_LANCE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_DRAGONS_DEN_CLAIR_TAG
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_DRAGONS_DEN_RIVAL
  if var VAR_UNK_40C4 == 1 end
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame end
  if var VAR_UNK_40FC == 0 setvar VAR_UNK_40FC 1
  if var VAR_UNK_40FC != 1 goto Later
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_DRAGONS_DEN_RIVAL
  end
label Later
  if weekday tuesday clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CHAMPIONS_ROOM_RETREAT
  if weekday thursday clearflag FLAG_HIDE_CHAMPIONS_ROOM_RETREAT
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_CLAIR goto Registered
  if time morning clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR
  end
label Registered
  if time day clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR
  end


# D44R0102_000: Clair stops the player on the way out
script Clair
  scene "Clair in the Dragon's Den"
  walk player left 1
  if var PLAYER_X == 26 walk d44r0102_gsleader8 right 1
  if var PLAYER_X == 27 walk d44r0102_gsleader8 right 2
  if var PLAYER_X == 28 walk d44r0102_gsleader8 right 3
  face d44r0102_gsleader8 player
  face player d44r0102_gsleader8
  speaker "Clair"
  say "Wait!"
  say "I'm sorry about before. Here. Take this as my apology."
  give "TM59"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM59_FROM_CLAIR
  say "That's Dragon Pulse. It's... a sort of great surge of energy. You don't have to use it if you don't want to."
  say "If you're heading for the Pokémon League, go back to New Bark Town and Surf east along Route 27."
  say "Something in your face says you've unfinished business. Whatever comes next, don't you dare give up!"
  if var PLAYER_X == 26 move d44r0102_gsleader8 up 1 right 3 up 7
  if var PLAYER_X == 27 move d44r0102_gsleader8 up 1 right 2 up 7
  if var PLAYER_X == 28 move d44r0102_gsleader8 up 1 right 1 up 7
  face player up
  waitmoves
  hide d44r0102_gsleader8
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR
  setvar VAR_UNK_40C4 2
  endscene
  end


# D44R0102_003: Clair, spoken to while she waits with the TM, and after the Hall of Fame (her number)
script ClairTalk
  face d44r0102_gsleader8 player
  speaker "Clair"
  if var VAR_UNK_40C4 == 1 goto Waiting
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_CLAIR goto Registered
  say "Hm? You're up early, with nothing to do already? I may have time to listen to you."
  ask "If you insist, we can swap numbers, too! What do you say?"
  if no goto Declined
  say "Fine. Here's my number, {player}."
  addcontact clair
  text "Registered Clair in the Pokégear."
  say "I can battle you on Friday nights, if you insist!"
  fade out 0.6
  hide d44r0102_gsleader8
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR
  wait 0.4
  fade in 0.6
  end
label Declined
  say "..."
  end
label Registered
  say "You again? Friday nights, remember, if it's a battle you're after. Until then, let me train."
  end
label Waiting
  say "Hm? What is it? I'm on my way back to the Gym."
  end


# D44R0102_001: the red-haired boy, training on the island's west bank
script RivalTalk
  face d44r0102_gsrivel player
  speaker "{rival}"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0E2 goto Again
  setflag FLAG_UNK_0E2
  say "...What? Yes, I'm training my Pokémon here. You can laugh at me now, but it has to be done."
  say "My Pokémon aren't ready to beat you yet. I can't push them too hard. I have to be strict with myself to become the greatest Trainer."
  end
label Again
  say "...Whew. Learn to stay out of my way."
  end


# D44R0102_004: the player's first step onto the row before the boy after the Hall of Fame. Lance and Clair come down
# behind the player, and the boy is dared into a double battle beside the player against the two of them (the partner
# is HeartGold's by the starter: TRAINER_PARTNER_RIVAL_2 for Chikorita, _3 for Cyndaquil, _1 otherwise). Won, Lance
# leaves, then Clair, then the boy is left alone with his pledge (VAR_UNK_40FC 2)
script Rival
  scene "The Dragon's Den's double battle"
  emote d44r0102_gsrivel_2 exclaim
  face d44r0102_gsrivel_2 up
  if var PLAYER_X == 24 walk player down 2
  if var PLAYER_X != 24 walk player down 1 left 1 down 1
  face player down
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...What? Have you come all this way to laugh at my training? Humph!"
  say "I came here to learn the Master's secrets, and he makes a mystery of everything. So I'm training my Pokémon here myself."
  place d44r0102_wataru 28 34 down
  place d44r0102_gsleader8_2 28 33 down
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_DRAGONS_DEN_LANCE
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_DRAGONS_DEN_CLAIR_TAG
  show d44r0102_wataru
  show d44r0102_gsleader8_2
  move d44r0102_wataru down 7 left 3 down 3
  move d44r0102_gsleader8_2 down 7 left 4 down 4
  waitmoves
  face d44r0102_gsrivel_2 up
  speaker "Lance"
  say "Hello, {player}! And you, too. We met at the Team Rocket hideout."
  emote d44r0102_gsrivel_2 exclaim
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "You! Where have you been all this time? Prepare to lose. I challenge you!"
  speaker "Lance"
  say "Calm down. I was in a hurry then, and I'm sorry for it." "But I never turn down a challenger! How about a Double Battle?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Humph! You can't beat me one on one, can you?"
  speaker "Lance"
  say "Come on, a Double Battle isn't so bad. {player}, you'll be on my side!"
  speaker "Clair"
  say "Well, then. You over there, will you team up with me?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "You can't be serious! Me, with that show-off in the strange costume? No way!"
  say "{player}! Come to my side! We'll beat Lance together!"
  walk player right 1 down 2
  face player up
  if starter "Chikorita" goto WithSecond
  if starter "Cyndaquil" goto WithThird
  battle d44r0102_wataru and d44r0102_gsleader8_2 with "johto_partner_rival_1"
  goto Won
label WithSecond
  battle d44r0102_wataru and d44r0102_gsleader8_2 with "johto_partner_rival_2"
  goto Won
label WithThird
  battle d44r0102_wataru and d44r0102_gsleader8_2 with "johto_partner_rival_3"
label Won
  speaker "Lance"
  say "Just as I expected. You two make quite a team." "Going alone is one way to aim for the top. But that isn't all there is to a Pokémon battle, though I don't suppose either of you needs reminding." "See you another time, {player}!"
  move d44r0102_wataru up 4 right 3 up 7
  waitmoves
  hide d44r0102_wataru
  speaker "Clair"
  say "I must excuse myself. ...{player}, that was a fun battle. And that goes for you too, wise guy."
  move d44r0102_gsleader8_2 up 3 right 4 up 7
  face d44r0102_gsrivel_2 right
  face player left
  waitmoves
  hide d44r0102_gsleader8_2
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}... It wasn't supposed to go this way. Anyway, I'm not giving up on beating you!"
  say "I'll train my Pokémon, and my own heart too. I'll come back as the toughest challenge you've ever had!"
  fade out 0.8
  hide d44r0102_gsrivel_2
  wait 0.5
  fade in 0.8
  setvar VAR_UNK_40FC 2
  endscene
  end
