# Porto Marinada on the open map (plan 34 · PD6). Kofu's gym test is an errand, as Scarlet and Violet's is: he sends a
# challenger west over the hills to the port's auction for the morning's best crate, and the auctioneer sells it to
# whoever bids last. Raise the paddle while the chef can still answer and he outbids every time; wait for the last call
# and the crate is the challenger's (FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_TEST_DONE, which Kofu asks for: Cascarrafa_GymTest.txt). A
# lot lost can be bid for again. Our own lines.


script Auctioneer
  speaker "Auctioneer"
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_TEST_DONE goto Sold
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_ERRAND goto Errand
  say "Bids on every crate, all morning long! Fish, shellfish, sea salt by the sack. Come back when you've something to buy."
  end
label Errand
  scene "The auction"
  say "Kofu sent you, did he? Then you'll want the next lot: one crate of the morning's finest sea urchins, fresh off the boat!"
  say "Paddles ready. The bidding is open!"
  choose "The chef across the quay is watching you." "Raise the paddle" "Wait"
  if result == 0 goto TooSoon
  say "Do I hear a bid? Going once!"
  choose "The chef is fiddling with his paddle." "Raise the paddle" "Wait"
  if result == 0 goto TooSoon
  say "Going twice! Last call for the urchins!"
  choose "The chef has looked away." "Raise the paddle" "Wait"
  if result == 1 goto Missed
  text "{player} raised the paddle at the very last moment!"
  say "Sold! To Kofu's runner, before the chef could blink. Here you are, one crate, packed in ice."
  text "{player} received a crate of sea urchins for Kofu."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_CASCARRAFA_TEST_DONE
  say "Take it straight up to Cascarrafa. Kofu doesn't like his urchins kept waiting."
  endscene
  end
label TooSoon
  text "The chef raised his paddle at once. {player} raised again, and so did he, and again, and again..."
  say "Sold, to the chef! The price got away from you, I'm afraid. Another crate comes in soon. Try again!"
  endscene
  end
label Missed
  text "Nobody raised a paddle... until the chef did, at the very last second."
  say "Sold, to the chef! You've got to bid before the hammer falls. Another crate comes in soon. Try again!"
  endscene
  end
label Sold
  say "Kofu's runner! That crate made the whole morning. Come back any time you want to bid."
  end
