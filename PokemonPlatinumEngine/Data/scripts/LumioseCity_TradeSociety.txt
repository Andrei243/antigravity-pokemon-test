# The Cercle des Échanges in Lumiose City (plan 37 · FC5's wave, beside the restaurants): a society of ours for those who
# trade Pokémon. Its host gives the player a Linking Cord once (FLAG_KALOS_TRADE_SOCIETY_CORD), which is how Kalos's
# Pokémon that grow when traded grow with nobody else, and its counter sells more (marts.json's lumiose_trades).


script Host
  face self player
  speaker "Gustavine"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_TRADE_SOCIETY_CORD goto Given
  say "Welcome to the Cercle des Échanges! Our members trade Pokémon with friends all over Kalos."
  say "Some Pokémon only grow when they are traded. With this cord, no trade is needed at all."
  give "Linking Cord"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_TRADE_SOCIETY_CORD
  say "Use it on one of them from your bag. Our counter sells more, should you need another."
  end
label Given
  say "A trade is a friendship. So, in its own way, is a cord. Do make good use of yours."
  end
