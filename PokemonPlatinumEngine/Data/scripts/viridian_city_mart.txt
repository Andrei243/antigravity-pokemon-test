# Viridian City's Poké Mart (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/ViridianCity_Mart/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_MART, as FireRed counts it: 0 the Parcel waiting at the counter, 1 the Parcel carried
# to Pallet Town, 2 the Pokédex had and the counter open.


# The first time in (FireRed's OnFrame at 0): the clerk knows where the player is from and has something for Oak.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_MART != 0 end
  scene "Oak's Parcel"
  face player up
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Oh! You're from Pallet Town, aren't you? Then you know Professor Oak!"
  say "His order came in this morning. Would you be a dear and take it to him for me?"
  give "Parcel"
  say "Thank you ever so much! Give him my regards."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_MART 1
  endscene
  end


# The clerk: only the counter once the Parcel has gone to Oak.
script MartClerk
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_VIRIDIAN_CITY_MART >= 2 goto Open
  say "Professor Oak's waiting on that parcel. Off you go, quick as you can!"
  end
label Open
  call Clerk
  end
