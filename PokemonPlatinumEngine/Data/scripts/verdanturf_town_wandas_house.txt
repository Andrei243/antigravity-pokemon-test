# Wanda's house in Verdanturf Town (plan 21 · H3, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/VerdanturfTown_WandasHouse/scripts.inc). Wally speaks of getting stronger once
# (FLAG_WALLY_SPEECH) and shortly after; his aunt and Wanda speak of the tunnel and of him.


script Wally
  face self player
  speaker "Wally"
  if flag FLAG_WALLY_SPEECH goto Short
  say "{player}... I lost to you, but I'm not giving up. I'm going to get stronger, as strong as you!"
  say "The air here is so clean, I feel better already. Ralts and I are going to train every single day."
  setflag FLAG_WALLY_SPEECH
  end
label Short
  say "I'll get stronger, {player}. Just you wait!"
  end


script WallysUncle
  face self player
  speaker "Wally's Uncle"
  say "Wally's in the next room, so to speak. He's been training with his Gardevoir from morning till night!"
  end


script WallysAunt
  face self player
  speaker "Wally's Aunt"
  if flag FLAG_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_OPENED goto Open
  say "My daughter's young man is so devoted. He's been digging a tunnel from Rustboro just to see her!"
  end
label Open
  say "So it was you who helped break through the tunnel? My daughter's young man was digging for weeks!"
  end


script Wanda
  face self player
  speaker "Wanda"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_WALLY_MAUVILLE goto Wally
  say "Don't worry about my cousin Wally. He's frail, but he's tougher than he looks."
  end
label Wally
  say "You must be {player}! Wally hasn't stopped talking about you since he came back from Mauville."
  end
