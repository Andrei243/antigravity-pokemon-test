# Pal Park's gate in Fuchsia City (plan 20 · J15, part o). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T08R0201).
#
# HeartGold's gate leads to a Pal Park beyond it. This game has one Pal Park, Sinnoh's on Route 221 (plan 06 · R16b), and
# a warp never crosses regions, so the attendant in the gate's doorway takes the player there: `travel to` is the crossing every
# region uses (GameEngine.CrossTo: the whole team along, the region's Pokédex, rival, music and name sign), as Route 40's
# guide takes them to the Battle Frontier. The way back is the Kanto attendant in Pal Park's lobby (PalParkLobby.txt,
# KantoAttendant). As Sinnoh's own doors do, the gate lets through only a Trainer with the National Pokédex.


script Attendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  if not nationaldex goto Shut
  say "Welcome to Pal Park's gate! The park itself is far away in Sinnoh, but we can take you straight there."
  ask "Would you like to go to Pal Park?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Right this way, please. Mind the step!"
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} was shown through the gate on the long way to Pal Park..."
  travel to "PalParkLobby" 8 18 up
  fade in 0.8
  end
label Stay
  say "Whenever you're ready. The park's Pokémon aren't going anywhere."
  end
label Shut
  say "Pal Park is a place for Pokémon from far away. Only a Trainer with the National Pokédex may go through."
  end
