# Pokéstar Studios' theater (plan 42 · PS4, part b; rulings, "The film studio"). Black 2's room, our people and our
# words: the usher shows a film's best take at its premiere (`studio premiere`), its lines said again, its ending, the
# crowd and the box office. A film never shot has nothing to show.


script Premiere
  face self player
  speaker "Usher"
  say "Welcome to the studio's theater! Tonight we screen the best take of any film you've shot."
  choose "Which premiere would you like to see?" "THE EMPTY SEAT" "THE GILDED HOUR" "LETTERS IN THE FOG" "THUNDER AT MARLOWE PASS" "NOT TONIGHT"
  if result == 0 goto EmptySeat
  if result == 1 goto GildedHour
  if result == 2 goto Letters
  if result == 3 goto Thunder
  say "The house is open whenever you are. Enjoy the lot!"
  end
label EmptySeat
  studio premiere "emptySeat"
  goto After
label GildedHour
  studio premiere "gildedHour"
  goto After
label Letters
  studio premiere "lettersInTheFog"
  goto After
label Thunder
  studio premiere "thunderAtMarlowePass"
  goto After
label After
  speaker "Usher"
  if result == 0 goto NoTake
  say "What a night! The whole lot will be talking about that one."
  end
label NoTake
  say "Hm, we have no take of that film to show. Shoot one at the studio building first!"
  end
