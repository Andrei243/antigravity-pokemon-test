# The Blackthorn Gym (plan 20 · J11, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T30GYM0101).
#
# The floor is lava, crossed on three slabs of stone that turn and slide at the press of their buttons (the room's
# puzzle, BlackthornPlatforms, laid out afresh each time the player comes in, as HeartGold's BlackthornGymInit lays
# them out). Three panels at the far ends of the ways across carry whoever steps on them back to the door (triggers 0 to
# 2, T30GYM0101_002, a warp into the same room, which lays the slabs out again). The guide comes over the first time the player walks in (trigger 3, FLAG_UNK_138).
#
# Clair battles and will not give her Badge: she sends the player to the Dragon Shrine's elder instead
# (FLAG_UNK_0D1, which also moves the old man before the Dragon's Den aside, blackthorn.txt). She gives the Rising Badge
# in the shrine (dragons_den_shrine.txt, FLAG_UNK_0EA) and TM59, Dragon Pulse, in the Dragon's Den or here
# (FLAG_GOT_TM59_FROM_CLAIR). HeartGold hides her in the Gym only for her rematches after the Hall of Fame, which this
# game has not yet: she is always home.


# T30GYM0101_004: the guide comes over to the player at the door, the first time
script Guide
  if flag FLAG_UNK_138 end
  scene "The Blackthorn Gym's guide"
  emote t30gym0101_sunglasses exclaim
  if var PLAYER_X == 12 walk t30gym0101_sunglasses left 2
  if var PLAYER_X == 13 walk t30gym0101_sunglasses left 1
  face t30gym0101_sunglasses player
  face player t30gym0101_sunglasses
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  say "Hey, champ in the making! Careful: the floor in here is lava, so you cross it on the stone slabs. Press the buttons on a slab to turn it or slide it."
  say "Clair's Pokémon are dragons, and dragons shrug off most attacks. Ice moves are what they hate."
  if var PLAYER_X == 12 walk t30gym0101_sunglasses right 2
  if var PLAYER_X == 13 walk t30gym0101_sunglasses right 1
  face t30gym0101_sunglasses down
  setflag FLAG_UNK_138
  setvar VAR_UNK_4120 1
  endscene
  end


# T30GYM0101_003: the guide
script GuideTalk
  face t30gym0101_sunglasses player
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0D1 goto Beaten
  say "Clair's Pokémon are dragons, and dragons shrug off most attacks. Ice moves are what they hate. And mind the lava!"
  end
label Beaten
  say "You beat Clair! Whatever she says, you're a real trainer already. I'd still do what she asks, though."
  end


# T30GYM0101_002: a panel at the far end of a way across carries the player back to the door
script BackToTheDoor
  walk player onspot down onspot left onspot up onspot right
  sound "warp"
  warp "Blackthorn_Gym" 8 83 up
  end


# T30GYM0101_000: Clair
script Clair
  face t30gym0101_gsleader8 player
  speaker "Clair"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0EA goto Badge
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0D1 goto Waiting
  scene "Clair"
  say "I am Clair, the finest master of Dragon-type Pokémon there is. I can stand up to the Elite Four themselves."
  say "You still want to battle me? ...Fine. A Gym Leader holds nothing back, whoever comes. Here I come!"
  battle self as "johto_leader_clair_clair"
  say "I won't accept this. I lost, yes, but you're still not ready for the Pokémon League."
  say "I know. Take the dragon tamers' test. Behind this Gym is the Dragon's Den, and a shrine stands at its heart. Go there."
  say "Show the elder you've left your soft ideas behind, and I'll admit you deserve a Badge."
  defeat "johto_ace_trainer_m_paulo"
  defeat "johto_ace_trainer_m_cody"
  defeat "johto_ace_trainer_m_mike"
  defeat "johto_ace_trainer_f_fran"
  defeat "johto_ace_trainer_f_lola"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_0D1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_DEN_GUARD_INFRONT
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHRON_DEN_GUARD_ASIDE
  endscene
  end
label Waiting
  say "What's the matter? Is the shrine too much to ask of you?"
  end
label Badge
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TM59_FROM_CLAIR goto Said
  say "You've kept me waiting! Here, take this."
  give "TM59"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM59_FROM_CLAIR
  say "That's Dragon Pulse. It's... a sort of great surge of energy. You don't have to use it if you don't want to."
  end
label Said
  say "Aren't you off to the Pokémon League? Something in your face says you've unfinished business first."
  say "You came from New Bark Town, didn't you, where Professor Elm's lab is? Maybe you should go back there."
  say "Whatever comes next will be harder. Don't you dare give up, or I'll feel even worse about losing to you!"
  end
