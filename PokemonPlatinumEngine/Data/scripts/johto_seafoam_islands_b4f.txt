# The Seafoam Islands' lowest floor as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part m). docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D11R0105). FireRed's Kanto has its own
# floor and script (seafoam_islands_b4f.txt), which this one does not touch.
#
# Articuno waits on its ledge until it has been fought (FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_ARTICUNO, ours), as Moltres does in Mt. Silver:
# beaten or caught it is gone for good, run from it flies off (HeartGold's FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_ISLAND_ARTICUNO) and is back
# on the next visit.
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes"): the bars, the camera to Articuno, sparkles of cold and
# the air wavering, the cave shaken with its cry as the sea's birds are; the scene ends before the battle, so the hold
# never skips it.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_ARTICUNO clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_ISLAND_ARTICUNO
  end

# D11R0105_000: Articuno
script Articuno
  scene "Articuno in the Seafoam Islands"
  music stop
  face player self
  letterbox on 0.4
  # Met from below, it is behind the player's own head from the field's camera: a step back, facing it, shows it
  if facing up walk player back down 1
  camera pan 27 22 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 27 22
  effect ripple 27 22
  wait 1
  cry "Articuno"
  camera shake 0.5
  emote player exclaim
  speaker none
  text "The air turned bitterly cold as Articuno spread its icy wings!"
  if facing up walk player up 1
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Articuno" 50
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_FOUGHT_ARTICUNO
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_ISLAND_ARTICUNO
  hide self
  end
label Fled
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SEAFOAM_ISLAND_ARTICUNO
  hide self
  text "Articuno flew off into the depths of the caves!"
  end
