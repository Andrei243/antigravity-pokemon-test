# Motostoke Riverbank (plan 33 · GL2): the Wild Area's northern edge under Motostoke. The steps up to the city are
# closed until the opening ceremony (plan 33 · GL4 opens them, at VAR_GALAR_STORY 13, postwick.txt's count).


# The foot of the steps: the League's steward turns a Trainer back
script MotostokeSteps
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 13 end
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 12 goto Endorsed
  faceto player league_steward
  speaker "League Steward"
  say "Sorry, the steps are closed! Motostoke is busy getting ready for the opening ceremony of the gym challenge."
  say "You'll hear when they open. The Wild Area's all yours until then!"
  walkto player motostoke_steps_back
  end
# Plan 33 · GL4: an endorsed challenger, come across the Wild Area on foot as Hop did, is let up the steps
label Endorsed
  scene "Up the steps to Motostoke"
  faceto player league_steward
  speaker "League Steward"
  say "Hold on, is that a letter from the Champion? You're one of the endorsed challengers! Up you go."
  say "Your friend went up ahead of you. Registration's at the stadium, right at the top of the city."
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_RIVERBANK
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_IN_MOTOSTOKE
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 13
  objective galarRegister
  endscene
  end
