# Fallarbor's Battle Tent (plan 21 · H3's last part). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# beats of Emerald's (data/maps/FallarborTown_BattleTentLobby/scripts.inc): a round of three Trainers on the Battle Arena's
# rules, three against three with no switching and every bout that lasts three turns judged, and a Hyper Potion for the
# three won.
#
# The round is the Arena's venue in a tent (Models/Frontier/BattleVenue.cs, `venue tent fallarbor`): the three entered go in
# as copies at their own levels, made whole for every battle, and the Trainers' are at the highest of them, never under
# thirty as Hoenn's levels map it. The attendant walks the player through the corridor
# (fallarbor_town_battle_tent_corridor.txt) to the battle room, whose arrival script is the round.


script Attendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Fallarbor Battle Tent! Every bout here is fought to a finish, or to the judge's verdict."
label Ask
  choose "Would you like to take the Battle Tent challenge?" "Enter" "The rules" "No"
  if result == 1 goto Rules
  if result == 2 goto Later
  venue enough
  if result == 1 goto Choose
  say "You need three Pokémon that can take part, all of different species and holding different items."
  say "An Egg can't take part, and nor can {banned}."
  end
label Choose
  say "Please choose the three Pokémon you'll enter."
  venue choose
  if result == 0 goto Later
  venue check
  if result == 1 goto SameSpecies
  if result == 2 goto SameItem
  venue tent fallarbor
  say "Thank you! Three Trainers are waiting for you. Please follow me."
  walk self up 3
  fade out 0.6
  warp "FallarborTown_BattleTentCorridor" 2 7 up
  fade in
  end
label SameSpecies
  say "I'm sorry. The three entered must all be of different species."
  goto Later
label SameItem
  say "I'm sorry. No two of the three entered may hold the same item."
label Later
  say "Please come again. The judges never tire of a good bout."
  end
label Rules
  say "Your Pokémon fight one on one, and nobody switches out. If neither falls in three turns, the judge decides."
  say "The judge looks at how boldly each one fought, how well it aimed and how much HP it kept."
  say "Your three keep their own levels and are made whole after every battle. Win three in a row for a prize!"
  goto Ask
