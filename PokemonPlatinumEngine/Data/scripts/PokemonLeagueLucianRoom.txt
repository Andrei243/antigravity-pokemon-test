# Lucian's room, the last of the Elite Four's (plan 01 · M9, part 2d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_pokemon_league_lucian_room.s).
#
# As Aaron's room: the door shuts behind the challenger, and the door on to the Champion's passage opens once Lucian
# is beaten. He is drawn as a gentleman until plan 11 makes his look. Once Charon has been arrested at Stark Mountain
# he brings his rematch team (plan 02 · S15, part b).


# PokemonLeagueLucianRoom_OnFrame_EnterRoom, by the door's own flag (see Aaron's room)
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_LUCIAN_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR end
  walk player up 2
  sound "lift_stop"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_LUCIAN_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  show entrance_door
  end


script Lucian
  speaker "Lucian"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_LUCIAN goto Beaten
  scene "Lucian"
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  say "...One moment, please. I'm at the last page of the chapter."
  say "There. Thank you for waiting. I am Lucian, the last of the Elite Four, and I battle with Psychic Pokémon."
  say "Three of my colleagues have already lost to you, so I know how this is likely to end. I intend to enjoy finding out all the same. Shall we?"
  # PokemonLeagueLucianRoom_StartLucianRematchBattle: the stronger team, once Charon is caught at Stark Mountain
  if flag FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN goto Rematch
  battle self as "elite_four_lucian"
  goto Fought
label Rematch
  battle self as "elite_four_lucian_rematch"
label Fought
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_LUCIAN
  sound "lift_stop"
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_LUCIAN_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  hide exit_door
  say "I see. So that is how it ends. Congratulations: you have beaten all four of us."
  say "But there is one more room. The Champion is far stronger than any of us. Rest your mind a moment, then go through that door."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "I have a great many books left to read, and you have one battle left to fight."
  say "The Champion is waiting beyond that door. Go on."
  end
