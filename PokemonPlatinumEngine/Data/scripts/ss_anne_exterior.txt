# The S.S. Anne at Vermilion's pier (plan 19 · K4, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/SSAnne_Exterior/scripts.inc).
#
# Coming off the ship with HM01 (VAR_MAP_SCENE_VERMILION_CITY 1, ss_anne_captains_office.txt), the player sees her
# sail from the gangway and is back on Vermilion's quay with the scene's variable at 3 (FireRed's
# DoSSAnneDepartureCutscene, then its 2 and its walk up the pier, both done here at once). From then on the sailor at
# the head of the pier turns everyone back (vermilion_city.txt).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_VERMILION_CITY != 1 end
  scene "The ship sets sail"
  music stop
  face player down
  camera pan 32 20 1
  wait 0.6
  text "Bwooo-oooh!\nThe S.S. Anne sounded her horn."
  camera shake 0.4
  text "The great ship slipped away from the pier and out to sea..."
  fade out
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_VERMILION_CITY 3
  setvar VAR_VERMILION_CITY_TICKET_CHECK_TRIGGER 0
  camera release 0
  warp "Kanto" 287 232 up
  endscene
  end
