# Oreburgh Gate (plan 02 · S5). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_oreburgh_gate_1f.s).
#
# A hiker gives the player HM06, Rock Smash, the first time they pass him. VAR_OREBURGH_GATE_1F_HIKER_STATE: 1 he
# waits (set by every new game), 2 the HM given.


# The tile in front of him (the original's trigger 3).
script HikerStops
  emote hiker exclaim
  face hiker player
  face player hiker
  call GiveHm
  end

script Hiker
  face hiker player
  if not flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM06 goto Give
  call Explain
  end
label Give
  call GiveHm
  end

script GiveHm
  speaker "Hiker"
  say "Hey there, young Trainer! Going through the Gate? Then you'll want this."
  say "Plenty of cracked rocks in these tunnels. Smash 'em, and you'll find all sorts of things!"
  give "HM06"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM06
  setvar VAR_OREBURGH_GATE_1F_HIKER_STATE 2
  call Explain
  return

script Explain
  speaker "Hiker"
  say "That HM holds Rock Smash. Teach it to a Pokémon and it can break cracked rocks out here in the field."
  say "Mind you, it only works outside battle once you've earned the Badge from Oreburgh's Gym."
  return
