# Flower Paradise (plan 01 · M10, part b; Shaymin plan 29 · LS11a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_flower_paradise.s).
#
# Arriving sets what the original's does for the place itself, and decides whether Shaymin is there: from the moment
# it ran up the Seabreak Path from Route 224's white rock (VAR_SHAYMIN_EVENT_STATE 2, route_224.txt) until it is caught
# or beaten (3); run from, it stays. Platinum's own level, 30. Its meeting is plan 29's beat by the row of grass and
# woods: the bars, the camera on it, sparkles among the flowers and the camera following it as it turns about, its
# cry and a line, then the battle's own way in (plan 09 · L14's Mythical).

script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_FLOWER_PARADISE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FLOWER_PARADISE_SHAYMIN
  if var VAR_SHAYMIN_EVENT_STATE == 2 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_FLOWER_PARADISE_SHAYMIN
  end


# FlowerParadise_Shaymin
script Shaymin
  scene "Shaymin in Flower Paradise"
  music stop
  face player shaymin
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 911 203 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 911 203
  camera follow shaymin 0.6
  walk shaymin onspot left 1 onspot right 1 onspot down 1
  cry "Shaymin"
  speaker none
  text "Shaymin pops up out of the flowers with its petals all a-quiver. It seems to want to play!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Shaymin" 30
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setvar VAR_SHAYMIN_EVENT_STATE 3
  hide shaymin
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FLOWER_PARADISE_SHAYMIN
label Fled
  music area
  end
