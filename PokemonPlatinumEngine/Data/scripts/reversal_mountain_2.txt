# Reversal Mountain's first floor (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black
# 2's beats. The count of the cave's errand is VAR_UNOVA_REVERSAL (reversal_mountain_4.txt has it whole).
#
# With Bianca along, our trigger across the foot of the way out to the slopes (24 to 26, 26, at 1) has her offer to go her own way:
# the player can't leave the cave with her along, since she can't follow onto the slopes.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  call UnovaBiancaWaits
  end


script BiancaOffersToPart
  if var VAR_UNOVA_REVERSAL != 1 end
  if not partner end
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Oh! We're nearly outside! I want to look for more traces of Heatran in here by myself. Shall we split up for now?"
  call UnovaBiancaAsks
  if yes goto Gone
  walk player up 1
  end
label Gone
  fade out 0.4
  hide bianca_along
  fade in
  end
