# The Pokémon League's Pokémon Center (plan 01 · M8), rebuilt to the original's plan with the door to the Elite Four
# and its guard (plan 01 · M9, part 2d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_pokemon_league_north_pokecenter_1f.s).
#
# The rival's battle before the door (plan 02 · S14): the first step onto the row before the guard, from any of its
# three tiles, brings him running in through the way out. He means to take on the League too, and battles to see which
# of the two is ready (his team by the player's starter, rival_pokemon_league_*). Beaten, he owns that he isn't, swears
# to come back tougher and runs out; VAR_RIVAL_BEAT_SUNYSHORE_GYM is the scene's variable, as the original's, and it
# is what the Sunyshore Gym's statues wait for before they name him (SunyshoreGym.txt). His flag stays set throughout:
# the scene shows him and takes him off again by itself, so a battle lost (the player wakes at this room's nurse)
# leaves nobody standing in the doorway, and he comes running in again at the next step onto the row.


# PokemonLeagueNorthPokecenter1F_OnTransition. Coming in lets Fly come here, and puts the Elite Four's rooms back as
# a challenger finds them: the doors behind open, the doors on shut, and all four waiting to battle again.
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_POKEMON_LEAGUE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_AARON_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_BERTHA_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_FLINT_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_LUCIAN_ROOM_ENTRANCE_DOOR
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_AARON_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_BERTHA_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_FLINT_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_LUCIAN_ROOM_EXIT_DOOR
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_AARON
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_BERTHA
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_FLINT
  clearflag FLAG_DEFEATED_LUCIAN
  # PokemonLeagueNorthPokecenter1F_SetPositionDoorGuardAwayFromDoor
  if flag FLAG_POKEMON_LEAGUE_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY place door_guard 12 3 down
  end


# PokemonLeagueNorthPokecenter1F_DoorGuard: he looks the Badges over and steps out of the doorway, to the side away
# from the player. The original asks nothing: nobody reaches its League without all eight (Victory Road needs
# Waterfall, and Waterfall the Beacon Badge). Here he counts them, and stays where he is for fewer.
script DoorGuard
  speaker "Guard"
  if flag FLAG_POKEMON_LEAGUE_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY goto Moved
  say "Beyond this door the Elite Four are waiting. Only a Trainer with all eight of Sinnoh's Gym Badges goes through. Let me have a look at yours."
  if badges < 8 goto Short
  scene "The guard at the door"
  # PokemonLeagueNorthPokecenter1F_Text_ExamineGymBadges: he counts them off, town by town
  say "Oreburgh City's Coal Badge. Eterna City's Forest Badge. Hearthome City's Relic Badge. Veilstone City's Cobble Badge."
  say "Pastoria City's Fen Badge. Canalave City's Mine Badge. Snowpoint City's Icicle Badge. And... Sunyshore City's Beacon Badge!"
  fanfare badge
  if facing left goto FromTheEast
  walk self right 1
  if facing right goto FromTheWest
  face self down
  goto Away
label FromTheWest
  face self left
  goto Away
label FromTheEast
  walk self left 1
  face self right
label Away
  setflag FLAG_POKEMON_LEAGUE_DOOR_GUARD_MOVED_AWAY
  say "All eight, and every one of them earned. You may go in."
  say "Make sure your Pokémon are rested first. Once you are through that door there is no coming back until you have won, or lost."
  endscene
  end
label Moved
  say "You will face the Elite Four one after another, and then the Champion. Nobody will heal your Pokémon between the rooms but you."
  say "Good luck in there."
  end
label Short
  say "...You don't have all eight yet. I can't let you through. Come back when you have every Gym Badge in Sinnoh."
  end


# PokemonLeagueNorthPokecenter1F_CoordEvent_Rival (plan 02 · S14): the row before the guard, while
# VAR_RIVAL_BEAT_SUNYSHORE_GYM is nought. He comes in by the way out and up beside the carpet to the tile below the
# player's, as the original's three movements have him. The original stands him on its exit mat, which is its warp;
# here the way out is the door in the row below the mat, so that is his own tile, a step further to come.
script RivalBeforeTheDoor
  scene "The rival before the door"
  show rival
  music "common/rival"
  if var PLAYER_X == 10 walk rival up 4 left 1 up 3 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 11 walk rival up 7 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 12 walk rival up 4 right 1 up 3 fast
  face player down
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hold it right there, {player}! You're not going through that door without me!"
  say "You've come to take on the Pokémon League, haven't you? Well, so have I! I didn't win eight Badges to stand and watch you go in first."
  say "So we'll settle it the way we always do. Whoever wins this battle is the one who's ready for the Elite Four!"
  if starter "Turtwig" goto VsTurtwig
  if starter "Chimchar" goto VsChimchar
  battle rival as "rival_pokemon_league_piplup"
  goto After
label VsTurtwig
  battle rival as "rival_pokemon_league_turtwig"
  goto After
label VsChimchar
  battle rival as "rival_pokemon_league_chimchar"
label After
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...If I'm still losing to you, then I'm not ready for the Pokémon League. Not yet. Argh, I can't stand it!"
  say "But you just watch! I'm going to get a whole lot tougher, and then I'm coming back to beat every last one of them."
  say "The Champion is the toughest Trainer there is, and that's going to be me!"
  say "So don't you dare lose to anybody in there before I've had my turn, {player}!"
  if var PLAYER_X == 10 walk rival down 3 right 1 down 4 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 11 walk rival down 7 fast
  if var PLAYER_X == 12 walk rival down 3 left 1 down 4 fast
  sound "stairs"
  hide rival
  music area
  setvar VAR_RIVAL_BEAT_SUNYSHORE_GYM 1
  endscene
  end
