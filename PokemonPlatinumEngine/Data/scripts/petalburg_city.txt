# Petalburg City (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/PetalburgCity/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE, as Emerald counts it: 0 the city not yet come to (a boy at the west road points out the
# Gym), 1 come to, 2 out with Wally for his catch, 3 the catch made. The catch (Emerald's WallyTutorial) is the
# city's arrival from the Gym: in the tall grass east of town, Wally shows how a Pokémon is caught, with his father's
# Zigzagoon lent by Norman (`catchinglesson ... by`), and catches his Ralts.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE != 2 end
  scene "Wally's catch"
  fade out
  place player 71 249 right
  place petalburg_wally 72 249 right
  show petalburg_wally
  fade in
  face petalburg_wally player
  speaker "Wally"
  say "{player}... the grass is so tall here. Is a Pokémon really in there somewhere?"
  say "I'm a little scared. But I'll do my best! Please, watch me!"
  face petalburg_wally right
  catchinglesson "Ralts" 5 by "Wally" "Zigzagoon" 7
  face petalburg_wally player
  say "I did it! I caught a Ralts! It's mine... my very own Pokémon!"
  say "Thank you, {player}! Let's go back to the Gym and tell your dad!"
  fade out
  hide petalburg_wally
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PETALBURG_CITY_WALLY
  setvar VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE 3
  setvar VAR_PETALBURG_GYM_STATE 1
  warp "PetalburgCity_Gym" 4 108 up
  endscene
  end


# The west road on first coming to town (the area's triggers 0 to 3, on VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE 0): the boy who
# waits there for the Gym to open tells the newcomer where it is
script ShowGym
  emote gym_boy exclaim
  face gym_boy player
  speaker "Boy"
  say "Hey! You're new here, aren't you? Have you seen our Gym? It's that big building just up the road!"
  say "The Leader's name is Norman. He's moved here from Johto, and he's really, really strong!"
  setvar VAR_PETALBURG_CITY_STATE 1
  end


script GymBoy
  if var VAR_PETALBURG_GYM_STATE >= 2 goto Open
  say "The Gym's that big building up the road. Norman's the Leader. Isn't that your dad?"
  end
label Open
  say "Norman won't take on just anyone. You need four Badges from other Gyms before he'll battle you!"
  end


script WallysMom
  say "My boy Wally has always been frail. We're sending him to stay with relatives in Verdanturf, where the air is clean."
  end
