# Silph Co.'s 9F (plan 19 · K5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/SilphCo_9F/scripts.inc).
#
# Team Rocket holds the building. A door of the floor is shut until the Card Key (from the fifth floor) opens it
# for good: FireRed draws it into its tiles and changes them with setmetatile; here it is four things of ours on
# its tiles (a shutter each), gone once its flag is set (FLAG_SILPH_<FLOOR>F_DOOR_<N>, FireRed's).

script Door1
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_SILPH_9F_DOOR_1 end
  if not item "Card Key" goto Locked
  sound "door_slide"
  setflag FLAG_SILPH_9F_DOOR_1
  text "The Card Key opened the door!"
  end
label Locked
  text "The door is locked. It takes a card to open it."
  end

# The woman in the west room puts the team to rest while Team Rocket still holds the building.
script HealWoman
  speaker "Woman"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_SILPH_CO_11F >= 1 goto Free
  say "You look worn out! Your Pokémon should take a quick nap. Here, let them rest a moment."
  fade out 0.4
  heal
  fanfare heal
  fade in 0.4
  say "There. Don't give up now!"
  end
label Free
  say "Thank you so much! Team Rocket's gone, and we can all go home at last."
  end
