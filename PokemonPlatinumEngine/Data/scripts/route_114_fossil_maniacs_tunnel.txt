# The Fossil Maniac's tunnel on Route 114 (plan 21 · H4, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route114_FossilManiacsTunnel/scripts.inc). Emerald's maniac asks after
# the desert's fossils once Mirage Tower has given one; Mirage Tower is not open yet, so he only talks of them.


script FossilManiac
  speaker "Fossil Maniac"
  if flag HoennHallOfFame goto CaveIn
  say "I'm digging for fossils! I dig and dig, every day. Pokémon from ancient times lie hidden in the rock."
  say "They say fossils turn up in the desert, too. If you ever find one, I'd love to see it!"
  end
label CaveIn
  say "Oh... careful back there. I was digging away when the whole wall gave in. There's a great cavern under the desert now."
  say "I've let it be. I doubt there are any fossils down there..."
  end


# Plan 21 · H12, part d: once Hoenn's Hall of Fame is entered, the cave-in behind him has opened onto the Desert Underpass
# (the overlay's door, opened by HoennHallOfFame) and he stands aside from it, facing the way he came (Emerald's
# Route114_FossilManiacsTunnel_EventScript_MoveFossilManiac, which puts him on 6, 5)
script OnEnter
  if not flag HoennHallOfFame end
  place fossil_maniac 6 5 down
  end
