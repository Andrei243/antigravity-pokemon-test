# Mt. Ember's summit (plan 19 · K9, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/MtEmber_Summit/scripts.inc).
#
# Moltres waits in the crater until it has been fought (FLAG_FOUGHT_MOLTRES), as Zapdos in the Power Plant: beaten or
# caught it is gone for good, run from it flies off and is back on the next visit.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_FOUGHT_MOLTRES clearflag FLAG_HIDE_MOLTRES
  end

script Moltres
  scene "Moltres"
  speaker none
  cry "Moltres"
  text "Gyaoo!\nFlames swirled round the great bird as it spread its wings!"
  wildbattle "Moltres" 50
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_FOUGHT_MOLTRES
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MOLTRES
  hide self
  endscene
  end
label Fled
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_MOLTRES
  hide self
  text "Moltres flew off over the crater's rim!"
  endscene
  end
