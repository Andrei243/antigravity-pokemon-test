# Jagged Pass (plan 21 · H7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/JaggedPass/scripts.inc).
#
# Team Magma has dug its hideout into the mountain's side and hidden the way in behind the rock. Coming here with the
# Magma Emblem in the bag readies the rock (VAR_JAGGED_PASS_STATE 1, as Emerald's arrival does); the first step onto
# one of the area's five triggers near the spot shakes the mountain and the rock gives way (VAR_JAGGED_PASS_STATE 2
# and FLAG_HOENN_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_OPEN, a flag of ours that opens the way in, where Emerald sets its metatiles). Until Mt.
# Pyre's old woman has given the emblem a grunt stands on guard by the spot and battles once
# (FLAG_BEAT_MAGMA_GRUNT_JAGGED_PASS); the summit's scene takes him away (FLAG_HIDE_JAGGED_PASS_MAGMA_GUARD).


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_EIGHT_B call HoennChapterEightB
  if var VAR_JAGGED_PASS_STATE != 0 end
  if not item "Magma Emblem" end
  setvar VAR_JAGGED_PASS_STATE 1
  end


script OpenHideout
  if var VAR_JAGGED_PASS_STATE != 1 end
  scene "The rock opens on Jagged Pass"
  camera shake 0.6
  wait 0.6
  speaker none
  text "The Magma Emblem is shaking in the bag! The rock nearby seems to be shaking in answer!"
  camera shake 1
  sound "rock_smash"
  wait 1
  setflag FLAG_HOENN_MAGMA_HIDEOUT_OPEN
  setvar VAR_JAGGED_PASS_STATE 2
  text "A hole has opened in the rock face!"
  endscene
  end


script Guard
  if flag FLAG_BEAT_MAGMA_GRUNT_JAGGED_PASS goto Beaten
  emote self exclaim
  face self player
  music "hoenn/eye_magma"
  speaker "Team Magma Grunt"
  say "Huh? What are you doing up here? There's nothing to see on this bit of the mountain. Nothing at all!"
  say "Hey, don't go poking at the rock! I said there's nothing there! Fine, then. You'll have to get past me!"
  battle self as "hoenn_grunt_jagged_pass"
  setflag FLAG_BEAT_MAGMA_GRUNT_JAGGED_PASS
  speaker "Team Magma Grunt"
  say "Ugh... I'm still not moving. Standing guard is the one thing I'm good at."
  music area
  end
label Beaten
  face self player
  speaker "Team Magma Grunt"
  say "Go wherever you want. Without the right thing on you, there's nothing on this mountain for you to find."
  end
