# The Seashore House on Route 109 (plan 21 · H3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route109_SeashoreHouse/scripts.inc): its owner gives six Soda Pops to whoever beats the
# three trainers resting inside (FLAG_RECEIVED_6_SODA_POP), and sells Soda Pop afterwards (marts.json's seashore_house).


script Owner
  face self player
  speaker "Owner"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_6_SODA_POP goto Shop
  if not defeated "hoenn_dwayne" goto Challenge
  if not defeated "hoenn_simon" goto Challenge
  if not defeated "hoenn_johanna" goto Challenge
  say "You beat every trainer in my house? Now that's the hottest thing I've seen all summer!"
  say "Here, have a cold drink on me. Have six, actually!"
  give "Soda Pop" 6
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_6_SODA_POP
  say "If you want more, I'll sell you some. Just come and ask."
  end
label Challenge
  say "Welcome to the Seashore House! The trainers in here are hot to battle."
  say "Beat all of them, and I'll treat you to something cool to drink."
  end
label Shop
  say "Want a Soda Pop? Nothing beats the heat like a fizzy drink!"
  shop "seashore_house"
  say "Come back whenever you're thirsty!"
  end
