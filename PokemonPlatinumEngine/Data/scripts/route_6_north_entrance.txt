# The gate between Route 6 and Saffron City (plan 19 · K5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route6_NorthEntrance/scripts.inc).
#
# The guard is thirsty on duty and turns everyone back from the three steps before him (the area's triggers 0 to 2,
# on VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE5_ROUTE6_ROUTE7_ROUTE8_GATES 0). Whoever brings the Tea that Celadon City's old lady gives
# (FLAG_GOT_TEA, celadon_city_condominiums_1f.txt) hands it over: he drinks, shares it with the other three gates'
# guards, and lets everyone through from then on (the variable 1, which ends the four gates' triggers).


script Guard
  face player right
  speaker "Guard"
  if item "Tea" goto Tea
  say "Sorry, no one gets past. I've been on duty all day without a drop to drink!"
  walk player down 1
  end
label Tea
  scene "Tea"
  say "Sorry, no one gets... Is that tea? You're a lifesaver!"
  take "Tea"
  text "{player} gave the guard the Tea."
  say "Much better! I'll pass the rest round the other gates. Go right ahead!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE5_ROUTE6_ROUTE7_ROUTE8_GATES 1
  endscene
  end


script GuardTalk
  face policeman player
  speaker "Guard"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE5_ROUTE6_ROUTE7_ROUTE8_GATES == 1 goto Open
  say "Saffron City's closed to everyone for the time being. I don't know why."
  end
label Open
  say "That tea really hit the spot. Off you go to Saffron City!"
  end
