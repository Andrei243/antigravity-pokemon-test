# A room of the Solaceon Ruins (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_solaceon_ruins_room_2.s).
#
# The hiker has been looking for HM05, which lies deeper in the ruins. Once the player has it he asks to borrow it
# and gives a Green Shard for the favour (FLAG_RECEIVED_SOLACEON_RUINS_ROOM_2_GREEN_SHARD); turned down, he asks again
# straight away the next time (FLAG_DID_NOT_LOAN_HM_DEFOG). The HM stays the player's, as in the original.


script Hiker
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SOLACEON_RUINS_ROOM_2_GREEN_SHARD goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_DID_NOT_LOAN_HM_DEFOG goto Ask
  if item "HM05" goto Found
  say "They say a Hidden Machine called Defog is tucked away somewhere in these ruins." "I've been round and round these rooms all day. Every one of them looks the same!"
  end
label Found
  say "They say a Hidden Machine called Defog is tucked away somewhere in these ruins..."
label Ask
  emote hiker exclaim
  ask "Wait, you've got it? That's HM05! Would you let me borrow it, just for a moment?"
  if no goto Refused
  say "You're a lifesaver! Here, take this for your trouble."
  give "Green Shard"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_SOLACEON_RUINS_ROOM_2_GREEN_SHARD
  end
label Refused
  setflag FLAG_DID_NOT_LOAN_HM_DEFOG
  say "Aw, come on. It's not as if an HM wears out when you lend it!"
  end
label Thanks
  say "Defog, at last! Now no fog in Sinnoh can stand in my way."
  end
