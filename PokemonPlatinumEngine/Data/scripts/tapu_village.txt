# Tapu Village, Ula'ula Island (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ula'ula, Poni, Aether
# Paradise and the League"). VAR_ALOLA_STORY 43, Sophocles' trial passed, brings Acerola out to meet the player on the
# road in; she opens the old Thrifty Megamart for her trial (FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_OPEN) and the village's rancher lets
# the player ride his Mudsdale (Overworld/RidePager.cs: no field move of the grid's, a flag alone).
#
# The people: acerola is shown by FLAG_ALOLA_TAPU_ACEROLA until she has been met.


script AcerolaArrival
  scene "Acerola in Tapu Village"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_TAPU_ACEROLA
  walk player right 16
  waitmoves
  face player right
  speaker "Acerola"
  say "Hi hi! I'm Acerola, and you're the island challenger Sophocles told me about! He said you were scary. You don't look scary at all."
  say "I've got a trial for you in the old Thrifty Megamart, just there. Don't mind the dust. Or the ghosts."
  say "I'll open the door. Go on in whenever you like. My Mimikyu is waiting in the dark, and he's very shy."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_MEGAMART_OPEN
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ACEROLA_MET
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 44
  objective alolaMegamart
  endscene
  end


script AcerolaMeet
  speaker "Acerola"
  say "The Megamart's door is open. Mind the dark, and mind the ghosts. They like you already."
  end


script Rancher
  speaker "Rancher Moke"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 44 goto Ride
  say "This ridge is no road for the weak-kneed. Come back when you've been to the observatory and I'll lend you a Mudsdale."
  end
label Ride
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_MUDSDALE goto Have
  say "You're the trial-goer I've heard about! Take my Mudsdale. She can trot over rubble that would break your ankles."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_MUDSDALE
  text "{player} registered Mudsdale in the Ride Pager! Mudsdale can gallop over rough ground."
  end
label Have
  say "Mudsdale is a good horse. Strong, steady and not the least bit clever."
  end
