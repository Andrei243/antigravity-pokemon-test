# The craftswoman's workshop in Sunyshore City (plan 39 · CR1): a room of ours behind the town's door 8, which the Hall of
# Fame opens (FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED, the overlay's openedBy). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# since nothing of this is the original's.
#
# She gives the Crafting Kit once, to whoever comes in (`craft give kit` puts it in the bag and sets FLAG_GOT_CRAFTING_KIT,
# which the rest of plan 39 waits for), and afterwards looks into it with the player, keeps the pantry's counter (CR6: the
# goods for the camp's pot, `craft pantry`), or says how it goes. Her apprentice (CR3) trades three of a type's common
# material for one of its rare (`craft tradable`, `craft trade`).


script Craftswoman
  speaker "Craftswoman"
  if item "Crafting Kit" goto Again
  say "Well, look at that! A Champion, and on my doorstep. Come in, come in, and mind the shavings."
  say "This is where I make things. Every Pokémon sheds something as it battles, and the countryside gives a good deal more to anyone who knows where to look."
  say "I can't turn you into a craftsman in an afternoon. But I can hand you a kit. Here, it's yours."
  craft give kit
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the {item}!"
  say "Keep it in your bag. When a wild Pokémon is beaten or caught, whatever it leaves behind goes into the kit's pouch by itself, and you'll be told so once the battle is over."
  say "Open it whenever you like. Its MAKE page turns what you've gathered into balls, medicine and the like, wherever you stand."
  say "And with the kit along you can make camp anywhere. There's a pot in it, and I keep a pantry here for whatever you want to cook."
  end
label Again
  say "Back again! Your kit's still in good order, I hope."
  choose "What can I do for you?" "LOOK IN THE KIT" "THE PANTRY" "HOW IT WORKS" "NOTHING"
  if result == 0 goto Open
  if result == 1 goto Pantry
  if result == 2 goto How
  say "Then I'll get on. Mind the shavings on your way out."
  end
label Open
  craft open
  end
label Pantry
  say "Flour, rice, beans and the rest, fresh in this morning. Take what you need for the pot."
  craft pantry
  say "Cook it at your camp. A good meal does a team a world of good, and it brings the wild ones round, or keeps them off."
  end
label How
  say "Every wild Pokémon leaves something of its own kind behind: a common thing almost every time, a rare one now and then. The higher its level, the more there is."
  say "Trainers' Pokémon leave nothing, nor do the ones in a contest or a park. Those are sport, not the wild."
  say "The kit's MAKE page lists what it can make and what each one takes. Nothing it makes comes cheaper than the shop's price, mind. You're paying in work, not money."
  say "Make camp from the menu and put the pot on. Stir when the heat is right and the dish comes out the better for it."
  end


script Apprentice
  speaker "Apprentice"
  if not item "Crafting Kit" goto NoKit
  craft tradable
  if result == 0 goto Nothing
  say "I'm learning to press the rare stuff out of the common. Three of your {material} would make one of the good kind."
  ask "Shall I have a go?"
  if no goto No
  craft trade
  say "There! One {material}, good as any I've seen. Well, nearly."
  end
label No
  say "Another time, then. I need the practice."
  end
label Nothing
  say "Bring me three of any Pokémon's leavings and I'll press them into one of the rare kind. I need the practice."
  end
label NoKit
  say "I'm only the apprentice. Talk to the craftswoman first."
  end
