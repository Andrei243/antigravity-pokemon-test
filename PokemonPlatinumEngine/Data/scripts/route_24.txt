# Route 24, Nugget Bridge (plan 19 · K4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/Route24/scripts.inc).
#
# Whoever beats the bridge's five trainers is met at its north end by a man with a Nugget for them, who turns out to
# be recruiting for Team Rocket. VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE24, as FireRed counts it: 0 until he is beaten (the area's
# triggers 0 and 1, and the man spoken to), 1 after. Bill's S.S. Ticket takes him off the bridge
# (FLAG_HIDE_NUGGET_BRIDGE_ROCKET).


# The step west of him (trigger 0): he walks up to the player. The step beside him (trigger 1): he turns.
script RocketLeft
  walk route24_rocket left 1
  call Prize
  end

script RocketRight
  face route24_rocket left
  call Prize
  end

script Rocket
  face route24_rocket player
  speaker "Man"
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE24 == 1 goto Beaten
  call Prize
  end
label Beaten
  say "With your talent, you'd climb right to the top of Team Rocket. Think about it!"
  end


script Prize
  scene "The prize at Nugget Bridge"
  face player right
  speaker "Man"
  say "Congratulations! You beat all five of our trainers. That makes you the winner of the Nugget Bridge challenge!"
  say "Here's your prize: a solid gold Nugget!"
  give "Nugget"
  say "And there's more. How'd you like to join Team Rocket? We're always looking for strong trainers like you."
  say "What, you won't? Then I'll have to change your mind the hard way!"
  battle route24_rocket as "kanto_team_rocket_grunt_6"
  speaker "Man"
  say "Argh! With a talent like yours, you'd climb right to the top of Team Rocket. Think about it!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_ROUTE24 1
  endscene
  return
