# The Virbank Complex (plan 22 · U5, part b). The lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's
# res/field/scripts/virbank_complex_2.s). Black 2's foreman asks the player to rouse three new workers, who battle
# only once asked; ours battle when spoken to, and the foreman thanks whoever has beaten all three. The scientist
# tells of the distillation tower, the stacks and the gas holders in turn (VAR_UNOVA_COMPLEX_TALK, Black 2's 0x40dd),
# and gives an Ether the third time (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_COMPLEX_ETHER).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script Foreman
  face 3 player
  speaker "Foreman"
  if not defeated "unova_worker_isaac" goto Ask
  if not defeated "unova_worker_mitchell" goto Ask
  if not defeated "unova_worker_nathan" goto Ask
  say "Ha! You got all three of my new workers fired up. Nothing like a battle to get the blood pumping!"
  end
label Ask
  say "Hey there, Trainer! Your Pokémon look full of beans. My new workers, not so much."
  say "There are three of them dawdling round the complex. Battle them and get them going, would you?"
  end


script Scientist
  face 7 player
  speaker "Scientist"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_COMPLEX_TALK == 1 goto Stacks
  if var VAR_UNOVA_COMPLEX_TALK == 2 goto Holders
  say "Heat crude oil, and it separates into all its parts. That's what the tall tower over there is for!"
  say "Oh! You heard all that? My knowledge just overflows sometimes."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_COMPLEX_TALK 1
  end
label Stacks
  say "The smokestacks rise right into the sky! Breathtaking, aren't they? They burn off the waste gas, you know."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_COMPLEX_TALK 2
  end
label Holders
  say "Those round gas holders are so harmonious! A sphere stands up best to the pressure inside it."
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_COMPLEX_ETHER goto Buddy
  say "You keep listening to me! Does that make us complex buddies? Here, a present for my new friend!"
  give "Ether"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_COMPLEX_ETHER
label Buddy
  say "The foreman wanted to hire me, but I'm happy just looking at the place!"
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_COMPLEX_TALK 0
  end
