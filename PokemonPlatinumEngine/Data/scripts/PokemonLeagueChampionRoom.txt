# The Champion's room (plan 01 · M9, part 2d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_pokemon_league_champion_room.s).
#
# The whole room is one scene, played as the challenger comes in: four steps up to Cynthia, her greeting, the battle,
# and then she steps aside, the challenger walks onto the platform behind her, it rises fifteen tiles to the gallery,
# and six steps on is the door to the hallway of the Hall of Fame. Nobody walks here by themselves. A battle lost ends
# it there, in the Pokémon Center, as every battle lost does.
#
# The original sets FLAG_ALT_MUSIC_CHAMPION_ROOM before the battle and clears it when the battle is lost; here it is
# set once the battle is won, which comes to the same (a script ends where a battle is lost). The room has no song of
# its own for after the battle yet (plan 05 · A6). Cynthia's rematch team waits for plan 02 · S15.


# PokemonLeagueChampionRoom_OnFrame_EnterRoom. The original runs it while a local variable is nought, since its frame
# table is asked every frame; here a place's script runs once as the player comes in, and nobody can save in this room.
script OnEnter
  walk player up 4
  music "common/eye_champion"
  speaker "Cynthia"
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Again
  say "So it's you, {player}. Somehow I thought it would be."
  say "On Mt. Coronet, and in that world behind this one, you never once let go of your Pokémon, whatever stood in the way. I have wondered ever since how far that would carry you."
  say "And here is the answer: all the way to this room. I couldn't be happier."
  say "Then let me welcome you properly. I am Cynthia, Champion of the Pokémon League, and I accept your challenge!"
  goto Fight
label Again
  say "Welcome back, {player}. I was hoping you would come."
  say "A Champion's room is a quiet place, you know. There is nothing I look forward to more than a challenger worth every one of my Pokémon."
  say "Come, then. As Champion of the Pokémon League, I accept your challenge!"
label Fight
  battle cynthia as "champion_cynthia"
  setflag FLAG_ALT_MUSIC_CHAMPION_ROOM
  say "...It has been a long time since I lost a battle. I had forgotten how it feels. It feels like the start of something."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_CYNTHIA
  say "That was superb, {player}. You knew just when to change, just which move to choose. You and your Pokémon fought as one."
  say "From this moment you are the Champion. Come with me. We'll take the lift."
  # She steps aside, and the challenger walks onto the platform behind where she stood
  walk cynthia left 1
  face cynthia down
  walk player up 6
  face cynthia up
  wait 1
  platformlift
  wait 1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_HALLWAY_TO_HALL_OF_FAME_CYNTHIA
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_HALLWAY_TO_HALL_OF_FAME_PROF_ROWAN
  walk player up 6
  sound "stairs"
  warp "PokemonLeagueHallwayToHallOfFame" 5 23 up
  end
