# The clearing in Fullmoon Island's wood (plan 02 · S15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_fullmoon_island_forest.s).
#
# Cresselia rests by the pool in the middle of the wood. Nobody battles it here: spoken to, it cries, shimmers and is
# gone, to roam Sinnoh (plan 06 · R13's roamers: met in the grass of the routes at level 50, fleeing, keeping what a
# battle did to it; VAR_ROAMING_CRESSELIA_STATE tells what became of it). Where it rested something glitters: the
# Lunar Wing, in its ball, which the original keeps hidden from the start of the game
# (FLAG_OBTAINED_FULLMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_LUNAR_WING, set by common.ChapterTwelve) and shows only now
# (FLAG_MET_CRESSELIA). It is what wakes Sailor Eldritch's son (CanalaveCitySailorEldritchHouse.txt).
#
# Whoever speaks to it from the south would have the ball behind their own head where it rested, so the original has
# Cresselia draw back a step first and leaves the ball there, a tile further off; so does this.
#
# A Cresselia knocked out on the routes is back here once the Hall of Fame is next entered
# (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt: VAR_ROAMING_CRESSELIA_STATE 3): it flies off again, and that time it leaves nothing.
#
# Its going is the original's own set piece (plan 09 · L12: FlickerObject, six times), staged by plan 29's beat
# (style guide, "Legendary scenes"): the bars, the camera on Cresselia, sparkles and the air wavering, and its shadow
# sent away north once it is gone. The scene ends before the line about the glitter, which the player reads with the
# camera home. Not here: the original shows
# Cresselia's picture over the field before its cry (DrawPokemonPreview), which no chapter has a screen for yet.


# FullmoonIslandForest_Cresselia
script Cresselia
  scene "Cresselia on Fullmoon Island"
  music stop
  face player cresselia
  letterbox on 0.4
  # Met from below, it is behind the player's own head from the field's camera: a step back, facing it, shows it
  if facing up walk player back down 1
  camera pan 16 15 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 16 15
  cry "Cresselia"
  wait 0.6
  # FullmoonIslandForest_CresseliaMoveAwayForLunarWing
  if facing up call DrawsBack
  # FlickerObject LOCALID_CRESSELIA, 6: it is gone and back three times over, and then gone for good, its shadow
  # going away north over the trees (a shadow is lit in the middle of its sixteen tiles: lit eight north of
  # Cresselia's tile it sets off from Cresselia, not from behind the player)
  effect ripple 16 15
  flicker cresselia 6
  hide cresselia
  effect shadow 16 7 2 up
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FULLMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_CRESSELIA
  roamer start "Cresselia"
  if facing up walk player up 1
  if var VAR_ROAMING_CRESSELIA_STATE == 3 goto Again
  # The ball shows by its flag alone: shown by the script it would stay, picked up or not, until the wood was come to again
  clearflag FLAG_OBTAINED_FULLMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_LUNAR_WING
  setflag FLAG_MET_CRESSELIA
  speaker none
  say "The Pokémon rose over the trees and flew away..."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  music area
  endscene
  say "Where it was resting, something is glittering."
  end
# FullmoonIslandForest_ResetCresseliaRoamingState: set loose a second time, after the Hall of Fame
label Again
  setvar VAR_ROAMING_CRESSELIA_STATE 0
  speaker none
  say "The Pokémon rose over the trees and flew away..."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  music area
  endscene
  end


script DrawsBack
  place item_lunar_wing 16 14
  walk cresselia up 1
  face cresselia down
  return
