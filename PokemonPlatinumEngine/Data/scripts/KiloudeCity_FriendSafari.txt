# The Friend Safari's gate in Kiloude City (plan 23, Kalos finished; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The Friend Safari").
# The lines are our own. X and Y's safari draws a friend's grounds from the friend's code; this game has no friend list,
# so every Trainer has three grounds of their own, drawn from their trainer number (Overworld/FriendSafari.cs):
# {friendsafari:1} to {friendsafari:3} name their types. The guide sends the player to the one chosen
# (VAR_KALOS_FRIEND_SAFARI 0 to 2), and the grounds' grass is laid with its three species as the player arrives
# (GameEngine.LayFriendSafari). The door opens once Kalos's Hall of Fame is entered.


script Guide
  face guide player
  speaker "Safari Guide"
  say "Welcome to the Friend Safari! Here every Trainer has grounds of their own, kept by the friends they've made on the road."
  say "Yours are three, and they stay as they are however often you come. Each holds Pokémon of one type."
  choose "Which of your grounds will you visit today?" "{friendsafari:1} grounds" "{friendsafari:2} grounds" "{friendsafari:3} grounds" "Not today"
  if result == 3 goto Later
  setvar VAR_KALOS_FRIEND_SAFARI RESULT
  say "Off you go, then. Walk back down the path whenever you've seen enough!"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "KiloudeCity_FriendSafariGrounds" 11 16 up
  fade in
  end
label Later
  say "Your grounds will be waiting. Come back any time."
  end


script Visitor
  face birder player
  speaker "Bird Keeper"
  say "My grounds are full of Flying types. A friend of yours would find quite different ones. That's what makes the safari fun!"
  end
