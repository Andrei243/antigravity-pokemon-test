# The workshop's berry garden in Sunyshore City (plan 39 · CR8): a map of ours behind the workshop's back door, two rows
# of twelve soils (the save's patches from BerryPatches.GardenFirst), each tended through common.BerryPatch as any soil
# of Sinnoh's is, and crossing with the soils beside it along its row (Overworld/BerryCrossing.cs) while the crafting
# kit is in the bag. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, since nothing of this is the
# original's.
#
# Her keeper sells the four mulches (`shop "workshop_garden"`, marts.json) and shows the kit's table of crosses
# (`craft crosses`, the kit's CROSSES tab), or says how the garden goes. Every answer ends the talk.


script GardenKeeper
  speaker "Garden Keeper"
  say "Welcome to the garden! The craftswoman lets me keep it, and I let anyone plant in it."
  choose "What would you like?" "MULCH" "CROSSES" "HOW IT GROWS" "NOTHING"
  if result == 0 goto Mulch
  if result == 1 goto Crosses
  if result == 2 goto How
  say "Then take your time. The soil isn't going anywhere."
  end
label Mulch
  say "Spread it before you plant. Each kind changes how a Berry grows."
  shop "workshop_garden"
  say "Happy growing! Remember, mulch goes down before the seed."
  end
label Crosses
  craft crosses
  if result == 0 goto NoKit
  say "Two different Berries side by side, and now and then one of the fruit comes up as something new."
  end
label NoKit
  say "Crosses? You'd want the craftswoman's kit for that. Without it you'd never see which Berries took to each other."
  say "Ask her inside. She gives one to anyone who has stood in the Hall of Fame."
  end
label How
  say "Each soil here is yours to plant, just like the soft soil out on the routes. Face it from the path below."
  say "Plant two different Berries next to each other in a row. With the kit in your bag, they may cross when you pick them."
  say "Keep them watered and come back in a day or so. The rows on either side don't touch, so plan each row on its own."
  end
