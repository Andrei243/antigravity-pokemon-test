# The road from Virbank City into the Virbank Complex (plan 22 · U5, part b). The lines are our own, on Black 2's beats
# (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/virbank_complex.s). The roughneck by the road (person 2) swaps a Poké Ball
# for a Great Ball once a day (FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_BALL_SWAP, cleared each new day).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script BallTrade
  speaker "Roughneck"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_BALL_SWAP goto Done
  say "Yo! Here's a good deal for you. Swap me a Poké Ball for my Great Ball?"
  ask "Trade a Poké Ball for a Great Ball?"
  if no goto Refused
  if not item "Poké Ball" goto NoBall
  take "Poké Ball"
  additem "Great Ball"
  text "{player} traded a Poké Ball for a Great Ball!"
  say "Heh, thanks! Enjoy that Great Ball. We can trade again tomorrow if you like!"
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_BALL_SWAP
  end
label Refused
  say "Seriously? Most people jump at a deal like this!"
  end
label NoBall
  say "Seriously? You haven't got a single Poké Ball on you?"
  end
label Done
  say "Good deal, right? Come back tomorrow and we'll trade again!"
  end
