# The Lumiose Museum on the north boulevard (plan 23 · Z4b; its door opens with the Hall of Fame). The lines are our own,
# on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos after its Hall of Fame"): a hall of
# paintings, fossils and a model of the city. In Looker's first case one of his tickets lies by a statue; in his fourth
# (VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 4) the curator tells of the night's break-in and the Poké Balls stolen in the alleys, which sets the
# three suspects in the streets (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_STING, lumiose_city.txt); and in his farewell (6) Looker waits here.
#
# The people: curator, a visitor, looker_farewell (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_FAREWELL until FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LEFT).


script Curator
  face curator player
  speaker "Curator"
  if var VAR_KALOS_LOOKER == 4 goto BreakIn
  say "Welcome to the Lumiose Museum. Every work here was made in Kalos, or found in its ground."
  end
label BreakIn
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_STING goto Sting
  scene "The museum's break-in"
  say "You're from the bureau? Thank goodness. Last night someone broke in and smashed three of our cases."
  say "Nothing was taken from them, but a guard saw a figure in a dark suit running off toward the alleys."
  say "And all week, Trainers have had Poké Balls snatched in the alleys of this city. Three suspects keep being seen there."
  say "One in the alley beside the bureau, one in the alley beside this museum, and one near the restaurant to the west."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_STING
  endscene
  end
label Sting
  say "The alley beside the bureau, the alley beside this museum, and the street near the restaurant. Please, be careful."
  end


# Looker's first case: one of his tickets, by the statue among the old things
script Ticket
  if var VAR_KALOS_LOOKER != 1 goto Statue
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKET_MUSEUM goto Statue
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_TICKET_MUSEUM
  fanfare item
  text "A card is propped against the statue's foot, with an eye drawn on it in ink." "{player} found one of Looker's tickets!"
  end
label Statue
  text "A statue of a Trainer and a Pokémon, carved so long ago that nobody knows which Pokémon it was."
  end


# Looker's farewell (VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 6, after the fifth case): he hands the bureau to Emma, and his name to the player
script LookerFarewell
  face looker_farewell player
  speaker "Looker"
  scene "Looker's farewell"
  say "You came. Of course you did. You always find what you are looking for, {player}."
  say "The city is safe. Xerosic is in custody, and Emma is free. My work in Lumiose is finished."
  say "The International Police want me somewhere else. A detective's life is a suitcase and a train ticket."
  say "Emma will run the bureau now. She knows the streets, and she has a good heart. Look in on her from time to time."
  say "And you... You have earned something. From today, you may call yourself Looker too. A secret name, for a secret job."
  say "Farewell, partner. Look after this city of light."
  text "Looker turned up his collar and walked out of the museum without looking back."
  hide looker_farewell
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_LEFT
  setvar VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 7
  objective kalosFree
  endscene
  end
