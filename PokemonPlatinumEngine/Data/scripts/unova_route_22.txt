# Route 22 (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/route_22.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Colress (7) steps out to meet the player in the pass below the beach (our trigger, 752, 146 to 148, VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 4, and
# his own script while he stands there): Black 2 has Terrakion cross the road first, which is plan 22 · U10's. He asks whether the
# player means to stand against Team Plasma, gives the Colress Machine (FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE, a flag as the Frontier Pass
# is in Hoenn: the item isn't in our data) and tells of the great boulder in the Seaside Cave on Route 21 (5).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  end


script Colress
  if var VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU != 4 goto Later
  scene "Colress on Route 22"
  music "unova/colress"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_ROUTE_22
  place 7 750 147 right
  show 7
  face player left
  speaker "Colress"
  say "Well, well! It's been a while, {player}. The Pokémon of these hills are restless today. They can smell danger on the wind, I think."
  say "Danger in the shape of Team Plasma, no doubt. Tell me..."
  ask "Do you plan to stand against Team Plasma?"
  if no goto NotSure
  say "I see. Then you'll need the strength to protect your Pokémon yourself. A Trainer who is only protected by Pokémon isn't really a Trainer."
  say "It's because Trainers care for their Pokémon that their Pokémon can grow so strong. Here, take this."
  goto Machine
label NotSure
  say "I see! That's an answer too. You and your Pokémon can go your own way."
  say "What matters is that you care for your Pokémon and draw out everything they have. Here, take this."
label Machine
  text "{player} received the Colress Machine!"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_COLRESS_MACHINE
  say "A prototype of a device of mine that energises Pokémon. It does nothing in battle, but you may find it useful elsewhere."
  say "Come to think of it! In the Seaside Cave on Route 21 there's a great boulder that isn't quite a boulder. My machine might wake it up."
  say "Well then. I wish you and your Pokémon a safe journey!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 7
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_COLRESS_ROUTE_22
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 5
  music area
  fade in
  endscene
  end
label Later
  speaker "Colress"
  say "The Seaside Cave is on Route 21, south of Humilau. Good luck!"
  end


# Terrakion in the hills (6; plan 22 · U10, part a): once Unova's Hall of Fame is entered it waits by the road, Black 2's level
# 65 (route_22.s), and is gone for good once caught or beaten (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_TERRAKION); run from, it is back next time.
script Terrakion
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame end
  face self player
  cry "Terrakion"
  speaker none
  text "Terrakion bellowed!\nThe ground shakes as it stamps!"
  wildbattle "Terrakion" 65
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_TERRAKION
  hide self
  text "The hills are still once more..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Terrakion thundered off over the hills..."
  end
