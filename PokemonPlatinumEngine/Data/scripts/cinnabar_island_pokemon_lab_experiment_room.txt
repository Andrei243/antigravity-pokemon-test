# The Pokémon Lab's testing room on Cinnabar Island (plan 19 · K6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/CinnabarIsland_PokemonLab_ExperimentRoom/scripts.inc).
#
# The scientist takes a Fossil (the Helix or Dome Fossil from Mt. Moon, the Old Amber from Pewter's museum), asking
# which when the bag holds more than one, and has the Pokémon ready once the player has gone out and come back: the
# game's own Fossils (plan 06 · R16b: VAR_REVIVED_POKEMON_SPECIES, and a local flag that lasts while the player stays,
# as Oreburgh's museum keeps it). FireRed hands the Pokémon over at level 5, and so does this room.


script FossilScientist
  if flag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_CINNABAR_LAB_REVIVING goto Working
  fossil ready
  if result == 1 goto Ready
  say "Hiya! I'm working on bringing Pokémon back from ancient Fossils."
  fossil count
  if result == 0 goto None
  ask "Oh! You have a Fossil there, don't you? Shall I bring it back to life for you?"
  if no goto Declined
  fossil choose
  if result == 0 goto Declined
  fossil revive
  if result == 0 goto Declined
  say "Right! Leave it with me. This will take a little while."
label Working
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_CINNABAR_LAB_REVIVING
  say "Go for a walk round the island, then come back. It'll be ready by then!"
  end
label Ready
  say "There you are! Your Fossil came back to life as {thing}!"
  if not party < 6 goto Full
  fossil give 5
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received {thing}!"
  say "Look after it. It's been waiting a very long time to see the world."
  end
label Full
  say "But your team is full! Make room, and come back for it."
  end
label None
  say "If you ever find a Fossil, bring it to me. I'll show you what it used to be!"
  end
label Declined
  say "No? Well, the machine will be here if you change your mind."
  end
