# Chargestone Cave's second floor (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/chargestone_cave_3.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Bianca (0) is at work here once she has met the player at the cave's mouth (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_CHARGESTONE_HEART
# cleared by chargestone_cave_2.txt). The Nugget man (2) and the Nugget boy (1) each give a Nugget, once
# (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_NUGGET_MAN and FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_NUGGET_BOY).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script BiancaAndTynamo
  face 0 player
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Know what? I'm here because Professor Juniper asked me to be. I'm researching a Pokémon called Tynamo!"
  say "There aren't very many of them, and they don't look very strong... They must get stronger when they evolve. I'll find out how!"
  end


script NuggetMan
  face 2 player
  speaker "Nugget Man"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_NUGGET_MAN goto Given
  say "Hi! I'm the Nugget man. Glad you showed up! I'd like you to have this."
  give "Nugget"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_NUGGET_MAN
  say "That's a Nugget! How did it get so golden without being deep-fried? That's a trade secret!"
  end
label Given
  say "Nuggetaboutit! Come back whenever you like."
  end


script NuggetBoy
  face 1 player
  speaker "Nugget Boy"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_NUGGET_BOY goto Given
  say "And I'm the Nugget boy! Here, I want you to have one, too."
  give "Nugget"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_NUGGET_BOY
  say "Nuggets sell for a lot, but I'd keep it. Shiny things bring good luck!"
  end
label Given
  say "Dad says nothing beats a nugget in the hand."
  end
