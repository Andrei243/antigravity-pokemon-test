# Exeggutor Island, Poni Island: Hapu's grand trial (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beats as
# they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ula'ula, Poni, Aether Paradise and the League"). The
# Poni stamp, the Groundium Z, and the road to the Altar opened (VAR_ALOLA_STORY 51).


script HapuGrand
  face hapu player
  speaker "Hapu"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_HAPU_DONE goto Done
  scene "Hapu's grand trial"
  say "There you are! I'm Hapu, the kahuna of Poni, and I hate waiting. So let's get to it."
  say "Poni's grand trial is a battle with me. That's all. Every captain says yes. Every trial says yes. Now I decide."
  say "My Pokémon and I know the island's sand, the island's stone, and the island's sky. Show me yours!"
  battle self as "alola_hapu_1"
  speaker "Hapu"
  say "Hah! Nothing held back, and nothing left behind. You and your Pokémon are a team."
  givebadge Poni
  text "{player}'s Trainer card received Poni's stamp!"
  give "Groundium Z"
  say "The Groundium Z. Earth-type moves, grown. And the guard at the Altar's road will let you through."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_HAPU_DONE
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ALTAR_OPEN
  hide self
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 51
  objective alolaAltar
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "Go on. The Altar's up the northern road."
  end
