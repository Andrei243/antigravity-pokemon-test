# The fishing house on Route 12 (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route12_FishingHouse/scripts.inc): the youngest of the three fishing brothers
# gives a Super Rod to a trainer who likes to fish (FLAG_GOT_SUPER_ROD). FireRed's measure of the biggest Magikarp
# brought him waits for one of Kanto's own.


script FishingGurusBrother
  speaker "Fisherman"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_SUPER_ROD goto Biting
  ask "I'm the youngest of the fishing brothers! Do you like to fish, too?"
  if yes goto Give
  say "No? Then I've nothing to say to you. Hmph."
  end
label Give
  say "I knew it! You've got the look of a true angler. This is the best rod there is."
  give "Super Rod"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_SUPER_ROD
  say "With a Super Rod, you can hook Pokémon that the other rods could only dream of!"
  end
label Biting
  say "Caught anything big with that Super Rod yet?"
  end
