# The Small Court's stands (plan 42 · PS5; rulings, "Sports days"). Black 2's room, our words. While a match is on
# (the twilight hours) the crowd fills the seats round the courts (FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON, set behind the door's fade) and
# coming up the stairs plays the match's scene from the rail, once for each time the stands are come to.

script OnTransition
  if time twilight goto On
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON
  end
label On
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON
  end

script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GAME_ON goto Quiet
  scene "CourtMatch"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera path 16 22 9 20 23 20 16 22 2
  sound "contest_cheer"
  text "The crowd is on its feet! A long rally runs on and on..."
  emote player exclaim 0.9
  text "A winning shot! The stands roar, and the scoreboard flickers with the new numbers."
  sound "contest_cheer"
  text "That's the match! The players shake hands and wave to the crowd."
  camera release 0.6
  endscene
label Quiet
  end
