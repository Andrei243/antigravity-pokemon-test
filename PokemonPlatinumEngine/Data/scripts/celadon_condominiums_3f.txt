# The Celadon Condominiums' third floor in HeartGold's Kanto (plan 20 · J15, part k): the office of the people who make
# games. docs/scripts.md describes the language. It is come to only by the left-hand lift.
#
# The director is one of those who hand over the charms a Pokédex filled has earned (plan 40 · GE5; charm due, charm
# give; Models/Charms.cs): any regional Pokédex seen whole, whichever region's, and the National one caught whole. With
# nothing due he says his own line (the overlay's).


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_JOHTO_CELADON_CONDO_FLOOR 3
  end

script Director
  face self player
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Charms
  if diploma Ours goto OursDiploma
  if diploma Johto goto OwnDiploma
  if var DIPLOMAS >= 3 goto ManyDiplomas
  if var DIPLOMAS > 0 goto AnyDiploma
  sayown
  end
label Charms
  say "Ah, a Trainer! May I see your Pokédex? ...Every page of it, filled in. Splendid! Simply splendid!"
  say "The Pokédex keeps its own diplomas, but this office keeps its charms. Please, take one with our thanks."
label Give
  charm give
  charm due
  if var RESULT > 0 goto Give
  say "Carry it with you, and fill in another Pokédex if you can. We may have something more for you."
  end
# Plan 40 · GE4, ours: he reads the diplomas the Pokédex has handed out (diploma, DIPLOMAS). They sit after the lines
# above so that the lines' numbers in the translation files stay where they were.
label OursDiploma
  say "The diploma for the Pokémon outside every region's list! Splendid! I'd hardly believe it if it weren't printed here." "Nobody planned those Pokémon, you know. They simply turned up, and you found them all."
  end
label OwnDiploma
  say "A diploma for the whole Johto Pokédex! Every page seen, from the first Route to the last cave. Splendid!" "The Pokédex hands out that paper itself, but it's nice to see someone carry it as far as this office."
  end
label ManyDiplomas
  say "Diplomas from several Pokédexes! You've been farther than any of our maps." "I'd love to ask which region was your favourite, but I suspect you can't choose."
  end
label AnyDiploma
  say "A diploma on your Pokédex! Splendid! It's a real accomplishment." "Other regions have Pokédexes of their own, and a diploma waits in each. Take your time."
  end
