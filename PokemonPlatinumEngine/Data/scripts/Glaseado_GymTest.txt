# Grusha's hall at the top of Glaseado Mountain, the end of his gym test (plan 34 · PD6, its second session): the test
# is the snowslope run outside (glaseado_mountain.txt, FLAG_PALDEA_GLASEADO_TEST_DONE); a gym trainer stands in the
# hall, and Grusha at its far end battles a challenger who has finished the run. Our own lines.


# Grusha's battle for the Ice Badge. He Terastallizes his last into an Ice type. Plan 34 · decision 3 at its
# recommendation, as the leaders' before him: his team is scaled to the badges the challenger holds, its best at the cap
# of that many badges, so in the academy's order (seven held) it is the original's (Data/world/paldea/trainers.json:
# paldea_grusha, paldea_grusha_b0 to _b6)
script Leader
  face grusha player
  speaker "Grusha"
  if badge ice goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_GLASEADO_TEST_DONE goto NotYet
  scene "Grusha"
  say "You finished the run. Clean lines, too. I'm Grusha. I used to ride that slope for a living, before I kept this gym."
  say "My Ice types don't do warm welcomes. Let's get this over with before the snow gets in."
  if badges >= 7 goto Held7
  if badges >= 6 goto Held6
  if badges >= 5 goto Held5
  if badges >= 4 goto Held4
  if badges >= 3 goto Held3
  if badges >= 2 goto Held2
  if badges >= 1 goto Held1
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b0"
  goto Beaten
label Held1
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b1"
  goto Beaten
label Held2
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b2"
  goto Beaten
label Held3
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b3"
  goto Beaten
label Held4
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b4"
  goto Beaten
label Held5
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b5"
  goto Beaten
label Held6
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha_b6"
  goto Beaten
label Held7
  battle grusha as "paldea_grusha"
label Beaten
  say "...Huh. That actually warmed me up. Here. The Ice Badge. You've earned it."
  givebadge ice
  text "{player} received the Ice Badge from Grusha!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_GLASEADO_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_yuki"
  say "Don't let the cold get to you on the way down. It's a long way to the bottom."
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "The run first. The coach outside will set you off. Three gates, in order. I'll know if you skip one."
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "Not a student at the academy? Then go back down the mountain. I don't battle outside the rules."
  end
label Won
  say "You again. The badge is yours already. Go and get warm somewhere."
  end
