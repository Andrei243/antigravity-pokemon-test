# Lance's room, the last of Kanto's Elite Four (plan 19 · K7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PokemonLeague_LancesRoom/scripts.inc). As Lorelei's room
# (pokemon_league_loreleis_room.txt): spoken to, he battles; beaten, the door behind him opens onto the Champion's room
# (FLAG_DEFEATED_LANCE).


script Lance
  face lance player
  speaker "Lance"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_LANCE goto Beaten
  scene "Lance"
  say "Ah! I've heard of you. I lead the Elite Four. My name is Lance, and I'm a dragon trainer."
  say "Dragons are mighty Pokémon, hard to catch and harder still to raise. Their strength is beyond question."
  say "There's no running away now. Prepare to lose!"
  battle self as "kanto_elite_four_lance"
  speaker "Lance"
  say "I can't believe it. My dragons have been beaten..."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_LANCE
  sound "door_open"
  say "You're the one who beat the Elite Four. But there's still the Champion to face. You know who that is, don't you?"
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "The Champion waits through that door. Go. I'd like to know how it ends."
  end
