# Laverre City's Gym, inside its great tree (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): Valerie, a
# designer of dresses, battles for the Fairy Badge, Kalos's sixth (`givebadge fairy`), once the Voltage Badge is won.
# VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 25 becomes 26 here, and the objective points east along Routes 15 and 17
# to Anistar City. Her team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_valerie_1.


script Valerie
  face valerie player
  speaker kalos_valerie "Valerie"
  if badge fairy goto Won
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 25 goto Later
  scene "Valerie"
  say "Oh... a visitor. I am Valerie. Some say I am not quite of this world. Perhaps they are right."
  say "My Pokémon are as delicate as lace, and as strong as the thread that holds it. Shall we see?"
  battle self as "kalos_valerie_1"
  say "How lovely. You battle like a butterfly that has decided to be a storm."
  say "Please take the Fairy Badge. It suits you."
  givebadge fairy
  text "{player} received the Fairy Badge from Valerie!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_LAVERRE_GYM
  defeat "kalos_lass_josette"
  defeat "kalos_lady_eleonore"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY != 25 goto Tm
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 26
  objective kalosOlympia
label Tm
  say "And this. A charm so sweet a foe forgets to fight at all."
  give "TM45"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_VALERIE
  say "Anistar City lies far to the east, past Dendemille and the snow. Its Leader reads the stars."
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "The Leader of Lumiose's tower has not yet met you, I think. Go to him first. Then come back to me."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_VALERIE goto Tm
  say "Do come back. I am making a dress the colour of your battle."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge fairy goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Valerie's Fairy types shrink from poison and steel. Choose well!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Valerie! She's sketching a new dress already."
  end
