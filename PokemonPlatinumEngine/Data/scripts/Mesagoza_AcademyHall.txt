# The academy's entrance hall at the top of Mesagoza's stairs (plan 34 · PD1). VAR_PALDEA_STORY is cabo_poco.txt's
# count. The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats. The Tera Orb is given only to a bag without one (plan 34,
# "The region's file", kept from plan 23's design), as Galar's Dynamax Band is.


# In through the doors the first time: the director's welcome and the Tera Orb
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY == 6 goto Welcome
  end
label Welcome
  scene "The director's welcome"
  face clavell_hall player
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  say "{player}! You found your way up all those stairs. Welcome, officially, to the academy."
  say "Every student here carries one of these. Pokémon in Paldea can shine like gemstones in battle, and this is how."
  if item "Tera Orb" goto HasOrb
  give "Tera Orb"
  goto TreasureHunt
label HasOrb
  say "Ah, but you have a Tera Orb already. Then you know what it can do. Keep it charged."
label TreasureHunt
  say "Tomorrow the treasure hunt begins: every student goes out into Paldea to find what matters to them."
  say "The gyms, the Titans, whatever calls you. Settle in tonight. Paldea will still be there in the morning."
  say "Oh, and a student called Arven is waiting at the lighthouse on Poco Path. Something about Titans and herbs."
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_ARVEN_LIGHTHOUSE
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 7
  objective paldeaFree
  endscene
  end


# Plan 34 · PD9: after the Way Home the professor is gone, and the academy's director stands in for the region's
# professor of decisions/R20b-mega-proposals.md ("Where the stones are found"): once the bag holds a Key Stone he hands
# over the stones of the Megas of our own whose line is Paldea's, once (FLAG_PALDEA_CLAVELL_MEGA_STONES)
script Clavell
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY >= 7 goto Hunt
  sayown
  end
label Hunt
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_WAY_HOME goto Talk
  if flag FLAG_PALDEA_CLAVELL_MEGA_STONES goto Champion
  if not item "Key Stone" goto Champion
  say "A Key Stone! Then perhaps you can make sense of what the professor left in the lab's last crate."
  say "Three stones, each labelled with the name of one of the academy's first partners. I believe they were meant for you."
  give "Meowscaradite"
  give "Skeledirgite"
  give "Quaquavalite"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_CLAVELL_MEGA_STONES
  say "Whichever partner walked with you from Cabo Poco, it can go further still. Do show me one day."
  end
label Champion
  say "Our Champion, back in the halls! Sign up for the Academy Ace Tournament at the desk whenever you are ready."
  end
label Talk
  say "The treasure hunt has no wrong road. Gyms, Titans, or something nobody has thought of yet: choose for yourself."
  end


# The Academy Ace Tournament's desk (plan 34 · PD9): open once the Way Home is behind the player. Entering draws the
# three rounds' opponents (a gym leader, one of the Elite Four, the final Geeta's or Nemona's) and walks the player onto
# the battle court (Mesagoza_AceCourt.txt). A tournament left unfinished is taken up where it stood
script AceDesk
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_WAY_HOME goto Closed
  if var VAR_PALDEA_ACE >= 1 goto Resume
  ask "This is the desk for the Academy Ace Tournament: three rounds, and only the strongest at the academy enter. Sign up?"
  if no goto Later
  random 8
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ACE_FIRST RESULT
  random 4
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ACE_SECOND RESULT
  random 2
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ACE_FINAL RESULT
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_ACE 1
  heal
  say "You're signed up! Your Pokémon are rested and ready. The court's this way."
  warp "Mesagoza_AceCourt" 7 12 up
  end
label Resume
  say "Your tournament is still under way! Back to the court with you."
  warp "Mesagoza_AceCourt" 7 12 up
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you're ready. The tournament runs every day."
  end
label Closed
  say "The Academy Ace Tournament is for students who've finished their treasure hunt. Come back once yours is done!"
  end
