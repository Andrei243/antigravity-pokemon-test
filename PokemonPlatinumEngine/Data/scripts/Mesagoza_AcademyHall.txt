# The academy's entrance hall at the top of Mesagoza's stairs (plan 34 · PD1). VAR_PALDEA_STORY is cabo_poco.txt's
# count. The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats. The Tera Orb is given only to a bag without one (plan 34,
# "The region's file", kept from plan 23's design), as Galar's Dynamax Band is.


# In through the doors the first time: the director's welcome and the Tera Orb
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY == 6 goto Welcome
  end
label Welcome
  scene "The director's welcome"
  face clavell_hall player
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  say "{player}! You found your way up all those stairs. Welcome, officially, to the academy."
  say "Every student here carries one of these. Pokémon in Paldea can shine like gemstones in battle, and this is how."
  if item "Tera Orb" goto HasOrb
  give "Tera Orb"
  goto TreasureHunt
label HasOrb
  say "Ah, but you have a Tera Orb already. Then you know what it can do. Keep it charged."
label TreasureHunt
  say "Tomorrow the treasure hunt begins: every student goes out into Paldea to find what matters to them."
  say "The gyms, the Titans, whatever calls you. Settle in tonight. Paldea will still be there in the morning."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 7
  objective paldeaFree
  endscene
  end


script Clavell
  speaker "Director Clavell"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY >= 7 goto Hunt
  sayown
  end
label Hunt
  say "The treasure hunt has no wrong road. Gyms, Titans, or something nobody has thought of yet: choose for yourself."
  end
