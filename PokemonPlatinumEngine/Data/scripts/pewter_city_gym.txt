# The Pewter City Gym (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/PewterCity_Gym/scripts.inc).
#
# Brock battles for the Boulder Badge (FLAG_BADGE01_GET is the badge itself, `givebadge boulder`) and gives TM39, Rock
# Tomb. His Badge sends the Gym's guide home from the road east, brings Oak's aide with the Running Shoes into the
# city (VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY 1) and counts his trainer beaten (FireRed's set_gym_trainers).


script Brock
  face brock player
  speaker "Brock"
  if badge boulder goto Won
  scene "Brock"
  say "So you're the new challenger. I'm Brock, Pewter's Gym Leader."
  say "I believe in rock-hard defence and unshakable will. My Pokémon are Rock types, solid to the core."
  say "Show me what you've got!"
  battle self as "kanto_leader_brock"
  say "...I misjudged you. You and your Pokémon fought as one. That deserves the Boulder Badge."
  givebadge boulder
  text "{player} received the Boulder Badge from Brock!"
  say "With it, Flash can be used outside battle, and Pokémon you get in trades will listen to you a little better."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_BROCK
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PEWTER_CITY 1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PEWTER_CITY_GYM_GUIDE
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_PEWTER_CITY_RUNNING_SHOES_GUY
  defeat "kanto_camper_liam"
label Tm
  say "Wait, take this as well. It's a TM of my own choosing."
  give "TM39"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TM39_FROM_BROCK
  say "TM39 holds Rock Tomb. It drops rocks on the foe and slows it down. A move worthy of a Pewter trainer."
  say "There are other Gyms all over Kanto, each tougher than the last. Go and test yourself against them."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_GOT_TM39_FROM_BROCK goto Tm
  say "The world is big, and there are trainers far stronger than me out there. Keep at it!"
  end


# The guide by the door: advice before the Badge, praise after.
script GymGuy
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge boulder goto Won
  say "Hey, future Champion! Brock's Pokémon are Rock types, hard as stone."
  say "Normal moves bounce right off them. Water and Grass moves are what they hate."
  say "If your Pokémon can't do either, train up a bit more before you take him on!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Brock! I knew you had what it takes. Champion material, that's you!"
  end
