# The charcoal kiln in Azalea Town (plan 20 · J7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T23R0201): the master's word before the well, while his apprentice is lost in Ilex Forest
# and once HM01 is given; the apprentice's Charcoal once he is home (FLAG_GOT_CHARCOAL_FROM_AZALEA_TOWN_MAN).


script Master
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM01 goto Home
  if flag FLAG_BEAT_AZALEA_ROCKETS goto Lost
  say "With Team Rocket in town and the Slowpoke gone, nobody's got the heart for work. Even the kiln's gone cold."
  end
label Lost
  say "My apprentice took our two Farfetch'd into Ilex Forest to cut wood, and he still isn't back."
  say "That boy. Without wood there's no charcoal, and without charcoal there's no work. I'll go and fetch him myself."
  end
label Home
  say "Thanks to you, the boy's back with both birds, and the kiln's burning again. Azalea's charcoal is the best there is!"
  end


script Apprentice
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM01 goto Home
  say "I'll find wood. Just watch me!"
  end
label Home
  if flag FLAG_GOT_CHARCOAL_FROM_AZALEA_TOWN_MAN goto Given
  say "You found both our Farfetch'd! I'd never have got them back on my own. Please, take this. I made it myself."
  give "Charcoal"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_CHARCOAL_FROM_AZALEA_TOWN_MAN
  say "Charcoal makes Fire-type moves stronger when a Pokémon holds it."
  end
label Given
  say "The Farfetch'd are much better at finding good wood than I am. Don't tell my master I said so."
  end
