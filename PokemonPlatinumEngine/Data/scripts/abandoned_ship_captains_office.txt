# The Abandoned Ship's captain's office (plan 21 · H12, part i). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/AbandonedShip_CaptainsOffice/scripts.inc).
#
# Captain Stern's assistant is looking into the wreck for him. He has been asked to find a Scanner too: once the player
# has it he asks them to take it to Stern in Slateport's harbour (slateport_city_harbor.txt), and once it is given
# he talks about the ship.


script SternsAide
  face self player
  speaker "Scientist"
  if flag FLAG_EXCHANGED_SCANNER goto Ship
  if item "Scanner" goto Deliver
  say "I'm going over this wreck for Captain Stern. He asked me to look for a Scanner, too, but I haven't had any luck..."
  end
label Deliver
  say "Is that a Scanner? Wonderful! Could you take it to Captain Stern in Slateport for me?"
  say "I'd like to stay and look around this ship a little longer."
  end
label Ship
  say "This ship was called the S.S. Cactus. It seems to be from long before my time."
  end
