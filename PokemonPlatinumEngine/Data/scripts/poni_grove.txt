# Poni Grove, Poni Island (plan 23, the wave after Ultra Space). The lines are our own, on Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon's beats
# as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Poni's last places, Blush Mountain, Route 11 and the rides of
# the rough ground and the sea"). A ranger keeps the grove's one-tile way in from Poni Wilds until Alola has its Champion
# (hidden by AlolaHallOfFame). Once the light is back in Ultra Megalopolis (FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED, MegaloTower.txt) a
# Stakataka stands in the ring of stones of the grove's east clearing and a Blacephalon among the flowers of its south-west,
# each met after a staged beat at 60 and back each time the grove is come to until it is caught. Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon
# keep one to a version here; ours has both.


script GroveRanger
  face ranger player
  speaker "Ranger"
  say "Sorry, the grove is closed. Something strange has been seen among the trees, and the rangers are keeping watch."
  say "Only the island's Champion may go in. That's the rule the kahunas made, and I'm sticking to it!"
  end


# A wall of stones in the ring of the east clearing, that is one creature
script Stakataka
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "Stakataka"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 182 206 1.0
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  shake self 3
  cry "Stakataka"
  text "One of the stones of the ring shifted. Its bricks were looking at {player}, every one of them."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Stakataka" 60
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_STAKATAKA
  hide self
  end


# A showman of Ultra Space among the flowers, its head a fuse
script Blacephalon
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "Blacephalon"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 145 213 1.0
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect burst 145 213 wait
  cry "Blacephalon"
  text "Something like a clown made of fireworks juggled its own head among the flowers, and bowed to {player}."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Blacephalon" 60
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_BLACEPHALON
  hide self
  end
