# The Lilycove Museum's ground floor (plan 21 · H7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LilycoveCity_LilycoveMuseum_1F/scripts.inc): the curator stands before the stairs
# and, asked, leads the player up to the gallery, whose frames wait for the Contest Hall's winners (plan 21 · H11),
# and stays up there with it (his flag is set; the gallery has a curator of its own).


script Curator
  face self player
  speaker "Curator"
  ask "Ah, a visitor! Our second floor is a gallery for paintings of Pokémon from the Contest Hall. Would you like to see it?"
  if no goto Later
  say "Splendid! Do come this way."
  walk self up 1
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LILYCOVE_MUSEUM_CURATOR
  warp "LilycoveCity_LilycoveMuseum_2F" 13 2 down
  end
label Later
  say "Of course. Enjoy the works down here, and ask me whenever you like."
  end
