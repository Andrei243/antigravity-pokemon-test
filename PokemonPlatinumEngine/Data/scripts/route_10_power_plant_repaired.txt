# The Power Plant on Route 10 as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part n). docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_R10R0201 and R10R0202).
#
# A part has been stolen from the generator. The manager tells the first Champion of Johto who speaks to him
# (FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_ROBBED); that brings Team Rocket's grunt to the Cerulean Gym (cerulean_gym.txt), who runs
# off to Nugget Bridge (johto_route_24.txt), whose battle tells where the part was hidden: the Gym's pool, where it is
# found (FLAG_JOHTO_FOUND_MACHINE_PART). Brought back, it sets the generator turning again
# (FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED), which puts the Kanto Radio Tower back on the air (lavender_radio_station.txt) and
# brings Zapdos to the plant's door (johto_route_10.txt), and the manager gives TM73, Thunder Wave. All the flags are
# ours. HeartGold shows the plant broken (R10R0202) on a map of its own while the part is gone; here the room is the
# plant at work throughout (the rulings' "HeartGold's Kanto, part j") and its people say whether the generator turns.


script Manager
  face r10r0201_gsbigman player
  speaker "Manager"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED goto Thanks
  if item "Machine Part" goto Returned
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_ROBBED goto Waiting
  scene "The Power Plant robbed"
  emote r10r0201_gsbigman exclaim
  say "Grr! Who's there? Oh, a Trainer. Sorry, I'm not myself today. Someone has robbed my plant!"
  say "A vital part of the generator was pulled right out of it. Without it, the generator won't turn."
  say "No power for the Magnet Train's lights, none for the radio... Kanto is going dark, bit by bit!"
  say "My men saw a man in black running off toward Cerulean City. If you see him, get that part back!"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_ROBBED
  endscene
  end
label Waiting
  say "The thief ran toward Cerulean City. Please, find that Machine Part and bring it back to me!"
  end
label Returned
  scene "The Machine Part returned"
  say "Is that... my Machine Part? You found it! Hand it over, quick!"
  take "Machine Part"
  text "{player} handed over the Machine Part."
  say "Right, back in its place it goes... There! Listen to that hum. The generator's turning again!"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED
  say "You've saved all of Kanto from the dark. Here, take this as my thanks. It's electrifying!"
  give "TM73"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM73_FROM_MANAGER
  say "TM73 is Thunder Wave. It paralyses the target, so it may not be able to move."
  endscene
  end
label Thanks
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM73_FROM_MANAGER goto Tm
  say "The generator's humming like it never stopped. The Radio Tower in Lavender is back on the air, too."
  end
label Tm
  say "I still owe you my thanks for that Machine Part. Here you go."
  give "TM73"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM73_FROM_MANAGER
  end


# The engineer by the generator's panels, who says whether it turns.
script Engineer
  speaker "Engineer"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED goto Turning
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_ROBBED goto Stopped
  say "I check every dial on these panels twice a day. Everything reads fine."
  end
label Stopped
  say "Every dial reads nothing. The generator won't turn a single time without that part."
  end
label Turning
  say "Every dial's back in the green. The generator's turning as sweetly as ever!"
  end
