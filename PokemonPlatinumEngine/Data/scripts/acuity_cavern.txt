# Acuity Cavern (plan 02 · S13). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_acuity_cavern.s).
#
# The cave on the island in the middle of Lake Acuity, reached over the water. Uxie is back in it once the professor
# has heard of the Distortion World (RowanLab.txt clears FLAG_HIDE_ACUITY_CAVERN_UXIE), and battles whoever speaks to
# it, at level 50; the battle may be run from. Lost, the player wakes in a Pokémon Center and Uxie waits here still.
# Caught, it is gone for good (FLAG_CAUGHT_UXIE). Knocked out or run from, it is gone into the cavern (the original
# hides it as the cavern loads again after the battle) until the Hall of Fame is next entered, whose script brings it
# back while it hasn't been caught (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt).
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes"): the bars, the camera to Uxie, sparkles and the
# air wavering round it, its cry, a line, and then the battle's own way in (plan 09 · L14's Legendary).


# AcuityCavern_OnTransition
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_ACUITY_CAVERN
  end


# AcuityCavern_Uxie
script Uxie
  scene "Uxie in Acuity Cavern"
  music stop
  face player uxie
  letterbox on 0.4
  # Met from below, it is behind the player's own head from the field's camera: a step back, facing it, shows it
  if facing up walk player back down 1
  camera pan 14 10 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect sparkles 14 10
  wait 0.6
  effect ripple 14 10
  cry "Uxie"
  emote player exclaim
  speaker none
  say "Uxie's eyes stay shut, yet {player} feels it looking straight through them..."
  if facing up walk player up 1
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Uxie" 50
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ACUITY_CAVERN_UXIE
  hide uxie
  if result == 3 goto Caught
  say "Uxie slipped away into the depths of its cavern..."
  end
label Caught
  setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_UXIE
  end
