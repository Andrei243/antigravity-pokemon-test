# Artazon Gym's lobby (plan 34 · PD3): Brassius's gym, the second of Victory Road in the academy's suggested order. His
# test, the hidden Sunflora, is through the doors behind the counter (Artazon_GymTest.txt). Our own lines.


script Receptionist
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if badge grass goto Won
  say "Welcome to Artazon Gym! Brassius's gym test is behind me: find the Sunflora hiding in his garden."
  say "They stand perfectly still among the flowers. Find all three and Brassius will see you."
  end
label Won
  say "Congratulations on the Grass Badge! Levincia's gym is up the road north, and Cortondo's is west of Mesagoza."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge grass goto Won
  say "Brassius's Grass types can't stand fire, ice, flying, poison or bug moves. Plenty to choose from!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Brassius! He's probably sculpting your victory as we speak."
  end
