# The Fishing Guru's house by the Lake of Rage (plan 20, Johto's leftovers). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T29R0201).
#
# He measures any Magikarp of the team he is shown and keeps the biggest (Models/SizeContest.cs, the original's own
# arithmetic) as a record of his own, VAR_JOHTO_MAGIKARP_SIZE_RECORD, which holds nothing until he has measured one, so
# the first Magikarp shown is always a record, as with Kanto's fishing brother. A record earns an Elixir, every time,
# as Gold and Silver's guru gives one. Sizes are told in centimetres (docs/mechanics/rulings.md).


# T29R0201_000
script Guru
  speaker "Fishing Guru"
  if not has "Magikarp" goto Bring
  say "Oh! You've brought a Magikarp! Let me see how it measures up."
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Denied
  team size "Magikarp" VAR_JOHTO_MAGIKARP_SIZE_RECORD
  if result == 3 goto Other
  if result == 0 goto Smaller
  if result == 1 goto Same
  say "Wow! This one is {size}! That's a new record for this lake!"
  say "You've earned this. It's my prize for the record holder."
  give "Elixir"
  team size keep VAR_JOHTO_MAGIKARP_SIZE_RECORD
  say "I'll write it on my wall. Come back if you ever land a bigger one!"
  end
label Bring
  team record "Magikarp" VAR_JOHTO_MAGIKARP_SIZE_RECORD
  if result == 1 goto Record
  say "I've fished this lake for forty years. The Magikarp here grow bigger than anywhere else."
  say "Catch a big one and bring it to me. I'll measure it and keep the record on my wall!"
  end
label Record
  say "The biggest Magikarp anyone has shown me measured {record}."
  say "Catch a bigger one and bring it here. I'll see to it you're rewarded!"
  end
label Same
  say "{size}... The same as the record, to the millimetre! Close, but not quite enough."
  end
label Smaller
  say "Hmm, {size}. A fine Magikarp, but the record is {record}. Keep fishing!"
  end
label Other
  say "That's no Magikarp! I only measure Magikarp, you know."
  end
label Denied
  say "Not today? The tape will be here when you're ready."
  end
