# Hammerlocke Stadium's pitch (plan 33 · GL5, its second session): Raihan's double battle for the Dragon Badge, the gym
# challenge's eighth and last (`givebadge dragon`). He Dynamaxes his last Pokémon. VAR_GALAR_STORY 35 to 36 (postwick.txt's
# count). Our own lines.


script Raihan
  face raihan player
  speaker "Raihan"
  if badge dragon goto Won
  scene "Raihan"
  say "Seven badges and a straight face. Nice. I'm Raihan, Hammerlocke's Leader, and the one who's going to beat Leon one day."
  say "Here, everything's two at a time. Two Pokémon each, and the weather on my side. Let's see your plan!"
  battle raihan as "galar_raihan_1"
  say "...That's a plan. Every gym in Galar beaten. Leon's going to like you, and I'm going to hate that."
  say "The Dragon Badge. Eight of eight. Go on and show the Champion Cup what you're made of."
  givebadge dragon
  text "{player} received the Dragon Badge from Raihan!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_HAMMERLOCKE_GYM
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_nia"
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_osian"
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 36
  say "The Cup's in Wyndon, up north by train. I'll see you there. In the stands, this time."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Next time it's me and Leon. Don't you dare miss it."
  end
