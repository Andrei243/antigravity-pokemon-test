# Hammerlocke Stadium's pitch (plan 33 · GL5, its second session): Raihan's double battle for the Dragon Badge, the gym
# challenge's eighth and last (`givebadge dragon`). He Dynamaxes his last Pokémon. VAR_GALAR_STORY 35 to 36 (postwick.txt's
# count). Our own lines.


script Raihan
  face raihan player
  speaker "Raihan"
  if badge dragon goto Won
  scene "Raihan"
  say "Seven badges and a straight face. Nice. I'm Raihan, Hammerlocke's Leader, and the one who's going to beat Leon one day."
  say "Here, everything's two at a time. Two Pokémon each, and the weather on my side. Let's see your plan!"
  battle raihan as "galar_raihan_1"
  say "...That's a plan. Every gym in Galar beaten. Leon's going to like you, and I'm going to hate that."
  say "The Dragon Badge. Eight of eight. Go on and show the Champion Cup what you're made of."
  givebadge dragon
  text "{player} received the Dragon Badge from Raihan!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_DEFEATED_HAMMERLOCKE_GYM
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_nia"
  defeat "galar_gym_trainer_osian"
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 36
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_LEON_WYNDON
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_WYNDON
  objective galarWyndon
  say "The Cup's in Wyndon, up north by train from the station by the south gate. I'll see you there."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Next time it's me and Leon. Don't you dare miss it."
  end


# After the Hall of Fame (plan 33 · GL7, part b; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Sordward and Shielbert"): Sordward on
# Hammerlocke's pitch once his brother has been beaten at Motostoke, another of the stadium's Pokémon Dynamaxed and
# raided, and his battle. Our own lines.
script Sordward
  face sordward_pitch player
  speaker "Sordward"
  if flag FLAG_GALAR_SORDWARD_BEATEN end
  scene "Sordward at Hammerlocke Stadium"
  say "So you beat my brother. No matter. The heroes of the legend were never what Galar was told, and Hammerlocke's own vault says so."
  text "The pitch shudders. The stadium's Duraludon rears up to a giant's height, roaring at the stands!"
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Not again! {player}, we're with you. Hop, Sonia, positions!"
  raid boss "Duraludon" 63 stars 5 cannotfail with "galar_raid_post_hop" "galar_raid_post_sonia" "galar_raid_post_leon"
  speaker "Sordward"
  say "Then it falls to me. Let us settle this, Champion!"
  battle sordward_pitch as "galar_sordward_1" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Sordward"
  say "Beaten, both of us... Perhaps the true kings were only ever stories. We will trouble the stadiums no more."
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_SORDWARD_BEATEN
  speaker "Hop"
  say "Every stadium's quiet again! Champion, you were brilliant. Nobody's doing that to Galar's Pokémon again."
  objective galarFree
  endscene
  end
label Lost
  speaker "Sordward"
  say "As I thought. Come back when you are worthy of the sword, Champion."
  endscene
  end
