# The Mistralton Gym (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/mistralton_city_gym.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The guide (5) gives a challenger Fresh Water at the door (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_MISTRALTON). Black 2's Gym is a wind tunnel
# whose cannons fling a challenger from platform to platform; ours is the maze of its corridors with five pilots
# (0, 1, 2, 4, 6) standing in them, and Skyla (3, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SKYLA_GYM, shown by the scene in Mistralton City) on the
# stage at the top. Her battle (Black 2's TRAINER_LEADER_SKYLA; _2 is the Challenge Mode's, plan 13's) gives the Jet Badge,
# the region's sixth (`givebadge jet`), and TM62 (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM62), which our data's table, Platinum's, fills with its
# own move; the city's count moves on (VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON 2).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script GymGuide
  face 5 player
  speaker "Guide"
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_MISTRALTON goto Water
  if badge jet goto Won
  say "The corridors are a maze, and a pilot waits round every bend. Skyla is at the very top, past them all."
  say "Her Pokémon fly, so Electric, Ice and Rock moves will bring them down to earth."
  end
label Water
  say "Whoosh! Hold on to your hat in here. This Gym is a wind tunnel!"
  say "The propellers at the back are what make the gale. Here, have a drink to keep you going."
  give "Fresh Water"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_MISTRALTON
  say "The pilots stand guard along the corridors, and Skyla waits on the stage. Flying Pokémon are weak to Electric, Ice and Rock moves!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Skyla! That was a flight to remember."
  end


script Skyla
  face 3 player
  speaker "Skyla"
  if badge jet goto Won
  scene "Skyla"
  say "Hee-hee! I've been waiting for you. You're a tough Trainer who can face the wind and not be blown off your feet!"
  say "I'm kind of excited about this battle! Shall we? You and me, wings spread!"
  battle 3 as "unova_leader_skyla"
  say "You're an amazing Trainer. My Pokémon and I are happy: for the first time in a long while, we battled with everything we had!"
  say "Here, take this. It's the Jet Badge, proof that you beat me!"
  givebadge jet
  text "{player} received the Jet Badge from Skyla!"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON < 2 setvar VAR_UNOVA_MISTRALTON 2
label Tm
  say "And I want you to have this TM, so that you'll always remember the Pokémon who flies the highest."
  give "TM62"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM62
  say "With a Badge from each of the winds, who knows how high you'll soar? I wonder what Professor Juniper is up to. I did promise her a ride in my plane..."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM62 goto Tm
  say "Are you and your Pokémon well? Our battle was a ton of fun. My Pokémon want to train again just thinking about it."
  say "Two things I love: flying my own plane, and having my Pokémon take me up using Fly!"
  end
