# Nimbasa City (plan 22 · U6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/nimbasa_city.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_NIMBASA is the city's count: 0 until the Bolt Badge, 1 with Hugh and Team Plasma waiting on the street to
# Route 5 (nimbasa_city_gym.txt sets it), 2 once their scene is played.
#
# Before the Gear Station the Subway Bosses, Ingo (6) and Emmet (7), wait for a two-on-two battle beside a young
# Trainer, the hero the player isn't (Black 2's TRAINER_ROSA or TRAINER_NATE, with the starter the player and Hugh
# didn't take): Rosa (5) for a boy, Nate (nate) for a girl, shown by the OnEnter below. Spoken to, they ask the player
# to battle beside them (Black 2's multi battle, 732 and 733); won, the Bosses go back into the station and the
# partner gives a Vs. Recorder (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SUBWAY_BOSSES). Once the Bolt Badge is won, Hugh (3) faces four of Team
# Plasma (0, 1, 2, 4) on the street west to Route 5; our trigger (409, 437 to 440, at 1) plays it: the player battles
# two of them (Black 2's TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_GRUNT_4 and _47), they flee, and Hugh tells of his sister's Purrloin,
# heals the team and goes on.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SUBWAY_BOSSES goto Done
  if girl clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_NATE_NIMBASA
  if boy clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROSA_NIMBASA
label Done
  end


# The young Trainer before the Gear Station: Rosa (5) or Nate (nate)
script Partner
  face self player
  scene "The Subway Bosses"
  if girl goto NateAsks
  speaker "Rosa"
  say "I came to try the Battle Subway, and guess who turned up? The Subway Bosses themselves!"
  say "They'll battle out here, but only two on two. I need a partner!"
  ask "Will you battle the Subway Bosses beside me?"
  if no goto Later
  say "Really? Thanks! I'm Rosa. Let's be the best partners ever!"
  if starter "Snivy" partner 5 "unova_rosa"
  if starter "Tepig" partner 5 "unova_rosa_2"
  if starter "Oshawott" partner 5 "unova_rosa_3"
  goto Battle
label NateAsks
  speaker unova_nate "Nate"
  say "I came to try the Battle Subway, and guess who turned up? The Subway Bosses themselves!"
  say "They'll battle out here, but only two on two. I need a partner!"
  ask "Will you battle the Subway Bosses beside me?"
  if no goto Later
  say "Really? Thanks! I'm Nate. You and me together, we'll be better than perfect!"
  if starter "Snivy" partner nate "unova_nate"
  if starter "Tepig" partner nate "unova_nate_2"
  if starter "Oshawott" partner nate "unova_nate_3"
label Battle
  face 6 player
  speaker "Ingo"
  say "A battle in the open air instead of aboard our trains? Most irregular! Still, new scenery may teach me something."
  say "Emmet, have you anything to add?"
  speaker "Emmet"
  say "Two on two, rules are rules! Destination: victory. All aboard!"
  battle 6 and 7 with partner
  speaker "Ingo"
  say "Bravo! You and your Pokémon make a truly splendid team!"
  speaker "Emmet"
  say "I'm Emmet. We lost, but I had fun! Let's battle again sometime!"
  speaker "Ingo"
  say "Next time, ride the Battle Subway and challenge us aboard! Now we must be off. All aboard!"
  walk 6 up 1
  hide 6
  walk 7 up 1
  hide 7
  if girl goto NateThanks
  speaker "Rosa"
  say "Even holding back, they were so strong! That's the Subway Bosses for you. Thanks, {player}! This is for you!"
  goto Gift
label NateThanks
  speaker unova_nate "Nate"
  say "Even holding back, they were so strong! That's the Subway Bosses for you. Thanks, {player}! This is for you!"
label Gift
  partner off
  give "Vs. Recorder"
  say "With a Vs. Recorder you can keep your battles and watch them again. I had a great time battling beside you. See you!"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_SUBWAY_BOSSES
  if girl goto NateLeaves
  walk 5 up 3
  hide 5
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROSA_NIMBASA
  endscene
  end
label NateLeaves
  walk nate up 3
  hide nate
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_NATE_NIMBASA
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "Not ready yet? That's OK! I'll keep them talking. Come back when you are!"
  endscene
  end


script Ingo
  face 6 player
  speaker "Ingo"
  say "I am Ingo, a Subway Boss. Usually you'll find me battling aboard the trains that leave from the Gear Station."
  end


script Emmet
  face 7 player
  speaker "Emmet"
  say "I'm Emmet, a Subway Boss! I love Double Battles. Two Pokémon working as one, that's the best!"
  end


script Hugh
  face 3 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Cover me!"
  end


# The street west to Route 5 once the Bolt Badge is won (our trigger on 409, 437 to 440, at 1)
script HughAndPlasma
  scene "Hugh and Team Plasma in Nimbasa"
  place player 409 439 left
  face 3 left
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hold it! What are you lot up to here?"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Up to? Nothing. We're just standing about. So why are you bothering us?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Maybe you haven't done anything to me. But I'll never forgive Pokémon thieves like you. Never!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Kids these days. You think your Pokémon's strength is your own? I don't like being pushed around!"
  face 3 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Cover me!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Heh heh! I stole this Pokémon two years ago and I've trained it hard ever since. It's tough!"
  battle 0 as "unova_team_plasma_grunt_4"
  say "What?! I toughened it up for two whole years!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "My turn! And I show no mercy!"
  battle 2 as "unova_team_plasma_grunt_47"
  say "We give up! Beaten by two kids... Fine. Since you're so strong, I'll tell you this much."
  say "Team Plasma is searching for something. Once we find it, our secret weapon will show its true power!"
  say "Farewell!"
  walk 4 left 11 fast
  hide 4
  walk 1 left 11 fast
  hide 1
  walk 0 left 11 fast
  hide 0
  walk 2 left 11 fast
  hide 2
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_NIMBASA_PLASMA
  face 3 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Five years ago, Team Plasma stole my little sister's Purrloin. It was a present."
  say "I was just a kid. I couldn't do a thing. That's why I have to get stronger!"
  say "Good work back there. I knew you had good instincts! Here, let me see to your team."
  heal
  text "{rival} healed your Pokémon."
  say "Listen. Fill up that Pokédex, keep getting stronger, and keep backing me up. I'm counting on you!"
  walk 3 right 13 fast
  hide 3
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_NIMBASA
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_NIMBASA 2
  endscene
  end
