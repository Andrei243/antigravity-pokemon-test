# The Dragonspiral Tower's open top (plan 22 · U10, part e). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
#
# Plan 40 · GE3: White 2's dragon. Once Zekrom has been met in N's Castle (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_ZEKROM), Reshiram waits at
# the tower's top, where White 2 puts it, and is battled at White 2's level 70. Caught or beaten it is gone for good
# (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_RESHIRAM, ours); run from, it is back the next time the top is come to.


script Reshiram
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_ZEKROM end
  scene "Reshiram at the Dragonspiral Tower"
  music stop
  face player self
  letterbox on 0.4
  effect burst 16 11
  cry "Reshiram"
  speaker none
  text "Reshiram's tail blazes white!\nThe air at the tower's top shimmers with its heat!"
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Reshiram" 70
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_RESHIRAM
  hide self
  text "The wind whistles over the empty tower top..."
  end
label Fled
  text "Reshiram looks down on {player} from the tower's top..."
  end
