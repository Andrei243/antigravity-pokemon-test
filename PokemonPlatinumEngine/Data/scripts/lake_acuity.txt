# Lake Acuity as Team Galactic leaves it (plan 02 · S11, part a): the original's other map of the lake, which Acuity
# Lakefront leads to once Commander Mars is beaten at Lake Verity. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_lake_acuity.s).
#
# The scene plays as the player comes in (the original's OnFrame, VAR_LAKE_ACUITY_STATE 1, which Mars's defeat sets):
# at the water's edge the rival has lost to Commander Jupiter, and the lake's Pokémon is already taken. She mocks him,
# comes down the shore to the player, whom she knows from Eterna City, warns them off Team Galactic's headquarters in
# Veilstone City, and leaves; nobody battles. The player goes up to the rival, who could do nothing, saw the Pokémon
# suffer, and goes off resolved to grow stronger. The player comes in a step inside the way out (14 or 15, 49), a tile
# further in than the original's, so every walk here is a step shorter than its own.
#
# What the scene leaves (the original's own): VAR_LAKE_ACUITY_STATE 2; the grunt who drops the Storage Key shown in
# Veilstone City (FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GRUNT_M_STORAGE_KEY cleared: plan 02 · S11, part b, writes him); the
# professor back in his lab and gone from Lake Verity with the assistant, on both of its maps; the tremor no longer
# the talk of Canalave City (FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED cleared); the lake's own theme from now on
# (FLAG_ALT_MUSIC_LAKE_ACUITY). The original's RemoveObject sets the flag that hides whoever it takes away, so Jupiter
# and the rival are hidden by their flags too.
#
# Acuity Cavern on the island, and Uxie in it, come back with the lake guardians (plan 02 · S13).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_LAKE_ACUITY_STATE != 1 end
  camera pan 16 45 1.0
  wait 0.25
  face rival up
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...! You're not getting away with this, Team Galactic!"
  face jupiter down
  speaker "Jupiter"
  say "Oh? Is that all? Finished already?"
  say "Your Pokémon aren't bad. You, though, are so weak it's almost funny."
  say "Did you honestly think you could save the Pokémon of the lake? And be Champion one day, too? Dream on, kid."
  say "Ugh, this cold gets into everything. We're done here. Back to headquarters in Veilstone."
  if var PLAYER_X == 15 goto East
  walk jupiter left 2 down 5
  face jupiter left
  face player right
  goto Met
label East
  walk jupiter left 2 down 4
label Met
  camera release 0.8
  say "Hm? Don't I know you? Yes... Eterna City."
  say "Listen. What Team Galactic is about to do is for everyone's sake. Something far too big for a child to grasp."
  say "So keep out of our way. And spare me the tears over a few poor Pokémon. It's all so tiresome."
  say "Whatever you do, don't waste your time coming to our headquarters in Veilstone City. Now, if you'll excuse me."
  if var PLAYER_X == 15 goto LeaveEast
  walk jupiter down 1
  goto Gone
label LeaveEast
  walk jupiter left 1 down 2
label Gone
  sound "stairs"
  hide jupiter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_ACUITY_JUPITER
  if var PLAYER_X == 15 goto WalkEast
  walk player up 4 right 2
  goto Beaten
label WalkEast
  walk player up 4 right 1
label Beaten
  face rival left
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "......" "...Yeah. That's right. I couldn't do a thing against Team Galactic. Not one thing!"
  face rival up
  say "That Pokémon they call Uxie... It was hurting. And I just stood here and watched."
  wait 0.5
  face rival left
  say "I'm going to get tougher. Not so I can win. Winning and losing isn't good enough any more."
  say "Saying I want to be the strongest Trainer there ever was doesn't make me strong. It takes real work. And a heart that doesn't give up."
  move rival down 2 left 2 down 3
  wait 0.4
  face player down
  waitmoves
  sound "stairs"
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_ACUITY_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_ALT_MUSIC_LAKE_ACUITY
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GRUNT_M_STORAGE_KEY
  setvar VAR_LAKE_ACUITY_STATE 2
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_PROF_ROWAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_COUNTERPART
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_LOW_WATER_PROF_ROWAN
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_COUNTERPART
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VERITY_PROF_ROWAN
  clearflag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED
  end
