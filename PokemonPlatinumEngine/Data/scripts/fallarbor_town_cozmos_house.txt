# Professor Cozmo's house in Fallarbor Town (plan 21 · H4, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/FallarborTown_CozmosHouse/scripts.inc). His wife says where he went, and
# how he is once Team Magma has been beaten at Mt. Chimney; Cozmo himself is home only after that (plan 21 · H4's next
# part), and asks for the meteorite back for TM27 (FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_RETURN).


script Wife
  speaker "Cozmo's Wife"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_RETURN goto Happy
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_EVIL_TEAM_MT_CHIMNEY goto Sad
  say "My husband went off to Meteor Falls with some people in red. They said they wanted to see his meteorite..."
  say "He can't say no to anyone. I hope he's all right."
  end
label Sad
  say "My husband's been so down since his meteorite was taken. I do feel sorry for him."
  end
label Happy
  say "My husband's so happy to have his meteorite back! Thank you, truly."
  end


script Cozmo
  speaker "Prof. Cozmo"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_RETURN goto Thanks
  if item "Meteorite" goto Ask
  say "My meteorite... It will never be mine again now. Team Magma made off with it."
  end
label Ask
  say "Is that... my meteorite? You got it back! May I have it, please?"
  ask "Give the Meteorite to Prof. Cozmo?"
  if no goto Refused
  take "Meteorite"
  say "Thank you! Please, take this TM in return."
  give "TM27"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_RETURN
label Thanks
  say "With the meteorite back, my research can go on. You've helped me more than you know!"
  end
label Refused
  say "Oh... I see. I'm crushed, but I understand."
  end
