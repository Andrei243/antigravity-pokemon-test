# The Kanto Radio Tower in Lavender Town as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part n). docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T05R0701).
#
# The tower runs on the Power Plant's power. While the generator on Route 10 stands still for its stolen part
# (route_10_power_plant_repaired.txt), the station is off the air and the director has nothing to give. Once the
# plant runs again (FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED, a flag of ours), he gives the Expn Card, which lets the Pokégear's
# radio hear Kanto's stations: the Poké Flute's among them, which wakes Route 12's Snorlax (johto_route_12.txt). The
# card is the bag's only memory of it.


script Director
  face t05r0701_gsgentleman_2 player
  speaker "Director"
  if item "Expn Card" goto Given
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED goto Give
  say "Welcome to the Kanto Radio Tower. I'm the director, and I'm afraid you've caught us at a bad time."
  say "The Power Plant on Route 10 has stopped. Without its power, we can't send a single song across Kanto."
  end
label Give
  scene "The Expn Card"
  say "Welcome to the Kanto Radio Tower! We're back on the air, thanks to the Power Plant running again."
  say "I hear you're the one who brought its missing part back. Then you deserve this."
  give "Expn Card"
  say "Slip that card into your Pokégear, and its radio will pick up every station we broadcast here in Kanto."
  say "Try the Poké Flute channel. Its tune is said to wake even the soundest sleeper."
  endscene
  end
label Given
  say "Is your Pokégear picking us up all right? The Poké Flute channel is a favourite of ours."
  end


script Receptionist
  face t05r0701_gswoman6 player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED goto OnAir
  say "Welcome to the Kanto Radio Tower. We're off the air for now, I'm sorry to say. There's no power."
  end
label OnAir
  say "Welcome to the Kanto Radio Tower. We're on the air all day, every day!"
  end


script Listener
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_POWER_PLANT_FIXED goto OnAir
  say "I came to hear the station's music in person, and there's nothing on! Something about the Power Plant."
  end
label OnAir
  say "Ah, the Poké Flute's tune. They play it on the radio here, you know. It's enough to wake anyone."
  end
