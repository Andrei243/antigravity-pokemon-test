# The fisherman gives away the Old Rod once (FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD), to whoever agrees that it is a fine thing, and
# after that offers to tell how to fish with it.


script Fisherman
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD goto Tips
  ask "Yes, indeed! There's nothing like an Old Rod! You think so too, don't you?"
  if no goto Refused
  say "Ha! The right answer! You and I are going to get along." "Here. This one's my own, and now it's yours!"
  give "Old Rod"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_OLD_ROD
label Tips
  say "Wherever there's water, there's something to fish for."
  ask "Come to think of it, would you like a tip or two on using that rod?"
  if no goto Twice
  say "Stand facing the water and use the rod from your bag. Then keep your eyes open!" "When a Pokémon bites, an exclamation mark pops up. That's your moment to reel it in!"
  end
label Twice
  say "Getting a bite is the first hurdle. Then you've got to battle whatever you reeled in." "Two thrills for the price of one. That's fishing!"
  end
label Refused
  say "Oh. Is that so? Not one for fishing, eh?"
  end
