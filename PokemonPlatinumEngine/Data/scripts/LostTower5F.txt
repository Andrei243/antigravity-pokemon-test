# The Lost Tower's top floor (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_route_209_lost_tower_5f.s).
#
# The floor is lost in fog until Defog clears it (the room's weather, FLAG_DEFOG_ACTIVE while the player stays). The
# two old women by the graves can't see who is there until then; once it has been cleared before them
# (FLAG_USED_DEFOG_IN_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F) the one gives the Spell Tag and the other the Cleanse Tag, once each.


script OldWoman1
  if flag FLAG_DEFOG_ACTIVE goto Cleared
  if not flag FLAG_USED_DEFOG_IN_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F goto Fog
label Cleared
  setflag FLAG_USED_DEFOG_IN_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F_SPELL_TAG goto Gave
  say "Oh! The fog has gone, and there you are. It must have been you who cleared it." "Take this. It's a little thanks from an old woman."
  give "Spell Tag"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F_SPELL_TAG
label Gave
  say "Fantina of the Hearthome Gym comes up here often, you know." "She says the Ghost-type Pokémon here are like old friends to her."
  end
label Fog
  say "Is somebody there? This fog is so thick I can't see my own hand."
  end


script OldWoman2
  if flag FLAG_DEFOG_ACTIVE goto Cleared
  if not flag FLAG_USED_DEFOG_IN_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F goto Fog
label Cleared
  setflag FLAG_USED_DEFOG_IN_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F_CLEANSE_TAG goto Gave
  say "My, how clear it is now! I can see every stone." "Here. Something to keep you safe on your travels."
  give "Cleanse Tag"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_209_LOST_TOWER_5F_CLEANSE_TAG
label Gave
  say "The Pokémon who rest here are at peace." "I come up to keep them company, and they keep me company in turn."
  end
label Fog
  say "Ohh... Who's there? I can hardly see a thing in this fog."
  end
