# Bianca's tour: the room's own person 8 (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_CENTER), shown as the outlook's scene warps the player in on
# VAR_UNOVA_ASPERTIA 5. The nurse heals the team, Bianca shows the PC and the Poké Mart's counters and gives ten Poké
# Balls, and goes on ahead (6), so that Mom and Hugh's sister are waiting outside (aspertia_city.txt).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_UNOVA_ASPERTIA != 5 end
  scene "The Center's tour"
  face 8 player
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "OK! I'll show you round the Pokémon Center!"
  fade out
  place player 7 5 up
  place 8 6 5 right
  fade in
  say "Pokémon Centers heal your Pokémon for free! Bring them here whenever they're worn out."
  speaker "Nurse"
  say "Welcome! I'll take your Pokémon for a moment."
  anim heal
  heal
  say "All done! Your Pokémon are full of energy again."
  speaker "Bianca"
  face player left
  say "Next, the PC! That's the square machine by the wall. Any Trainer can use it for free."
  say "You can leave Pokémon in it when your team is full, and take them out again whenever you like."
  say "And the counters on the other side are the Poké Mart! You can buy and sell all kinds of items there."
  say "The Poké Balls you catch Pokémon with are sold there too. Here, {player}, I'll give you some to start!"
  give "Poké Ball" 10
  say "Next up, I'll show you how to use them! Meet me on Route 19, past the gate at the top of town!"
  fade out
  hide 8
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_CENTER
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MOM_ASPERTIA
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGHS_SISTER
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MOM_HOME
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_ASPERTIA 6
  fade in
  endscene
  end


# Bianca spoken to before the tour begins (she starts it at once on arrival, so this is only the save's case)

  speaker "Bianca"
