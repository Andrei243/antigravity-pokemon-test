# Maylene, who ran up Route 217 barefoot on her way to train with Candice, is thawing out by the counter. She stays
# until the player has the Icicle Badge and comes to Acuity Lakefront again, where the original sends her home to her
# own Gym (FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_CITY_POKECENTER_1F_MAYLENE, acuity_lakefront.txt). The room is the common Pokémon

# Maylene (the original's script 5).
script Maylene
  face self player
  speaker "Maylene"
  if badge icicle goto Badge
  say "Brrr... Oh! Hi, {player}! Are you taking on the Gym here now?"
  say "I can't wait to see Candice, but I'm not moving from this spot until I can feel my toes again."
  say "You should know that Candice uses Ice-type Pokémon. I really look up to her. Nobody studies as hard as she does!"
  say "She asked me to train with her so she could learn all about the Fighting type. Ice doesn't do well against Fighting, you see."
  end
label Badge
  say "Oh! That's the Icicle Badge! Then you've beaten Candice!"
  say "Doesn't that Badge let your Pokémon use Rock Climb outside of battle? Rock types and Fighting types are the ones to teach it to, I think."
  end
