# Eterna City's Pokémon Center (plan 02 · S6). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_eterna_city_pokecenter_1f.s).

# A boy whose Pokémon Team Galactic took, until it is back with him, and a woman with a Pokétch app that tells how
# close a Pokémon feels to its Trainer (the Friendship Checker: given and kept, and shown once the Pokétch runs it).
# The boy's Buneary, home again (FLAG_HIDE_ETERNA_CITY_POKECENTER_1F_BUNEARY), waits for Pokémon that stand in the field.


# The boy (the original's script 3).
script Schoolboy
  speaker "Schoolboy"
  if flag FLAG_TEAM_GALACTIC_LEFT_ETERNA_BUILDING goto Back
  say "Team Galactic took my Pokémon away. They've taken other people's too."
  say "Somebody ought to do something, but everyone's too scared of them... What are they doing to my Pokémon?"
  end
label Back
  say "I've got my Pokémon back! I'm so happy I want to ride my Bike down the Cycling Road with it right now!"
  end


# The woman with the app (the original's script 5).
script FriendshipChecker
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ETERNA_FRIENDSHIP_CHECKER goto Given
  if not poketch goto Given
  say "Hello there, little Trainer! What Pokémon have you brought along with you today?"
  say "Here, this Pokétch app shows how close your Pokémon feel to you."
  poketchapp FriendshipChecker
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ETERNA_FRIENDSHIP_CHECKER
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Friendship Checker was added to the Pokétch!"
  end
label Given
  say "Spend enough time together, and any Pokémon will open its heart to you."
  end
