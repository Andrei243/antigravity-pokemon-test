# The Bistro des Deux in Lumiose City (plan 37 · FC5, the first of Lumiose's restaurants). A restaurant of ours: its menu
# is three courses, each a double battle against its staff (Odette, Fabien and Bertrand, trainers of Kalos's table with
# "doubleBattle"), fought one after another on the player's own team and paid as any trainer's battle. The menu is
# taken once a day (FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_1, set as it is served, so a meal lost is the day's meal too), and the
# dish the kitchen sends out at the end is chosen by the turns the three courses took together (TURNS: the fewer, the
# better): four or fewer a Rare Candy, eight or fewer two Lum Berries, twelve or fewer two Sitrus Berries, more three
# Oran Berries. The dishes are items and nothing more; plan 39 · CR7's meals with powers are served elsewhere.


script Host
  face self player
  speaker "Clothilde"
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_1 goto Served
  say "Welcome to the Bistro des Deux. Here everything is served in pairs, the battles too."
  ask "Will you take today's menu? Three courses, each a double battle against our staff."
  if no goto Later
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KALOS_RESTAURANT_1
  say "Splendid. The quicker you finish, the finer the dish the kitchen sends out."
  speaker "Odette"
  say "The first course! Two of mine against two of yours."
  battle odette as "kalos_restaurant_bistro_odette" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Fabien"
  say "The main course, still steaming. Mind the plates!"
  battle fabien as "kalos_restaurant_bistro_fabien" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Bertrand"
  say "And to finish, something from my own stove."
  battle bertrand as "kalos_restaurant_bistro_bertrand" canlose
  if lost goto Lost
  speaker "Clothilde"
  say "The whole menu in {var:TURNS} turns! Let us see what the kitchen sends out for you."
  if var TURNS <= 4 goto Best
  if var TURNS <= 8 goto Good
  if var TURNS <= 12 goto Fair
  say "A leisurely meal. The kitchen sends a little something all the same."
  give "Oran Berry" 3
  end
label Best
  say "So quick the soup was still steaming! The chef's finest, with his compliments."
  give "Rare Candy"
  end
label Good
  say "Brisk and stylish. The kitchen is quite pleased with you."
  give "Lum Berry" 2
  end
label Fair
  say "A good, honest meal. Here is the dish of the day."
  give "Sitrus Berry" 2
  end
label Lost
  heal
  speaker "Clothilde"
  say "The kitchen has closed for today, I'm afraid. Your Pokémon have been seen to. Do come back tomorrow."
  end
label Later
  say "Another time, then. The menu is new every day."
  end
label Served
  say "You have dined with us today already. The kitchen opens again tomorrow."
  end


# The staff between meals
script Staff
  face self player
  if subject "odette" goto Odette
  if subject "fabien" goto Fabien
  speaker "Bertrand"
  say "A dish is like a battle. Too long on the fire and it is spoiled."
  end
label Odette
  speaker "Odette"
  say "Two plates in each hand, two Pokémon on each side. You get used to it!"
  end
label Fabien
  speaker "Fabien"
  say "Ask Madame Clothilde for the menu. I'll be waiting with the main course."
  end
