# The Pokémon Tower's sixth floor (plan 19 · K5, parts a and b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PokemonTower_6F/scripts.inc).
#
# A spirit waits before the stairs up (the area's triggers on VAR_MAP_SCENE_POKEMON_TOWER_6F 0): Cubone's mother,
# whom Team Rocket struck down. Without the Silph Scope nobody can see what it is, and FireRed's battle with a ghost
# nobody can see can only be run from, so here it says its piece and pushes the player back
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kanto's third chapter, first part"). With the Scope (part b, from Giovanni in the
# Rocket Hideout) it is seen for the Marowak it is and battled (level 30, FireRed's StartMarowakBattle); beaten, it
# is at peace and the way up is open (VAR_MAP_SCENE_POKEMON_TOWER_6F 1). Run from, it pushes the player back as
# before. FireRed's Marowak can't be caught; a battle here has no such rule yet, and a Marowak caught is taken for a
# spirit at rest (the rulings, "Kanto's third chapter, second part").


script MarowakGhost
  speaker "Ghost"
  say "Be... gone... Intruders... be... gone..."
  speaker none
  if item "Silph Scope" goto Seen
  text "The ghost can't be made out. Without some way of seeing it, there's no going on."
  walk player up 1
  end
label Seen
  scene "Marowak"
  text "The Silph Scope shows the ghost for what it is: a Marowak!"
  wildbattle "Marowak" 30
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  text "The ghost was the spirit of Cubone's mother, Marowak."
  cry "Marowak"
  text "The mother's spirit was calmed. It went away to rest at last..."
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_POKEMON_TOWER_6F 1
  endscene
  end
label Fled
  walk player up 1
  endscene
  end
