# A cabin of the Plasma Frigate (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/plasma_frigate_9.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The grunt (0) shows the pictures of Reshiram and Zekrom on the cabin's wall, which the Pokédex notes as seen; the switch on the
# wall at the head of the middle passage (6, 2) is the first of the hold's barrier's two (FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SWITCH_A,
# common.UnovaFrigateBarriers).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  end


script Grunt
  face 0 player
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "See these pictures? The legendary Pokémon that made the Unova region. This one's Reshiram..."
  seen "Reshiram"
  say "...and this one's Zekrom. With Pokémon like these, ruling Unova would be easy, right? Too bad we don't have either. Yet."
  seen "Zekrom"
  end


script Switch
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SWITCH_A goto Off
  ask "There's a switch on the wall marked BARRIER. Press it?"
  if no end
  text "Click! From behind the wall came the sound of a barrier switching off."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SWITCH_A
  call UnovaFrigateBarriers
  end
label Off
  text "The switch marked BARRIER is off."
  end
