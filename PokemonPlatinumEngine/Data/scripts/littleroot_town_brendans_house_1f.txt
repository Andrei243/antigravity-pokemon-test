# The player's house downstairs, the west house of Littleroot Town (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LittlerootTown_BrendansHouse_1F/scripts.inc and
# data/scripts/players_house.inc). Emerald gives the player the east house when they are the girl; this game keeps the
# family in the west one either way (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hoenn's first chapter").


# In from the van (VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 3): the movers' Vigoroth at work, and Mom sends the player upstairs to set
# the clock. Back down with the clock set (6): Dad's Gym on the television, just missed, and next door to say hello.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE == 3 goto MovingIn
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE == 6 goto OnTheTelevision
  end
label MovingIn
  scene "Moving in"
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "See, {player}? The movers' Vigoroth are doing all the heavy lifting. Isn't that something?"
  say "Your room is upstairs. Go and have a look! And Dad bought you a new clock. Set it for me, would you?"
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 4
  endscene
  end
label OnTheTelevision
  scene "Dad on the television"
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  emote players_house_1f_mom exclaim
  speaker "Mom"
  say "{player}! Quick, come here! It's Dad's Gym on the television! Petalburg City!"
  text "...the Gym's Leader, Norman, has kept his team in fine form this season. That's all from Petalburg..."
  say "Oh! It's over already. We missed him. What a shame!"
  say "Your father has his Gym in Petalburg City now, so we'll see more of him. You should go and visit him."
  say "But first, go next door and say hello. Professor Birch lives there, and his child is just about your age!"
  hide vigoroth_carrying_box
  hide vigoroth_facing_away
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_PLAYERS_HOUSE_VIGOROTH_1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_PLAYERS_HOUSE_VIGOROTH_2
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 7
  endscene
  end


# Out of the door before the clock is set (the area's trigger 0, on VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 4)
script GoSeeRoom
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Wait, {player}! You haven't even seen your room yet. And don't forget to set the clock!"
  walk player up 1
  end


# Mom, once the moving is done: she sees to the team, as every Mom does
script Mom
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE < 7 goto Busy
  speaker "Mom"
  say "You look tired out, sweetie. Sit down for a minute and catch your breath."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "There, all better! Your Pokémon look happier already. Don't forget to come home now and then."
  end
label Busy
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Go on upstairs and see your room, {player}. The clock won't set itself!"
  end
