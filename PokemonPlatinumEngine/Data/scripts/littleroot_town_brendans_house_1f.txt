# The player's house downstairs, the west house of Littleroot Town (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/LittlerootTown_BrendansHouse_1F/scripts.inc and
# data/scripts/players_house.inc). Emerald gives the player the east house when they are the girl; this game keeps the
# family in the west one either way (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hoenn's first chapter").


# In from the van (VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 3): the movers' Vigoroth at work, and Mom sends the player upstairs to set
# the clock. Back down with the clock set (6): Dad's Gym on the television, just missed, and next door to say hello.
# Down from the bedroom as the Champion (plan 21 · H9, part b: HoennHallOfFame, VAR_HOENN_CHAMPION_HOME 0, a variable of
# ours): the television's report of the Pokémon flying over Hoenn, Mom's question of its colour, which sets Latias or
# Latios roaming (Emerald's VAR_ROAMER_POKEMON, 0 for red and 1 for blue, as its MULTI_TV_LATI), and Dad home from
# Petalburg with the S.S. Ticket, who goes back to his Gym. A save of Hoenn from before this was written is given Dad's
# hiding here (common.HoennChapterThirteen).
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_THIRTEEN call HoennChapterThirteen
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE == 3 goto MovingIn
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE == 6 goto OnTheTelevision
  if not flag HoennHallOfFame end
  if var VAR_HOENN_CHAMPION_HOME != 0 end
  goto ChampionHome
label MovingIn
  scene "Moving in"
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "See, {player}? The movers' Vigoroth are doing all the heavy lifting. Isn't that something?"
  say "Your room is upstairs. Go and have a look! And Dad bought you a new clock. Set it for me, would you?"
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 4
  endscene
  end
label OnTheTelevision
  scene "Dad on the television"
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  emote players_house_1f_mom exclaim
  speaker "Mom"
  say "{player}! Quick, come here! It's Dad's Gym on the television! Petalburg City!"
  text "...the Gym's Leader, Norman, has kept his team in fine form this season. That's all from Petalburg..."
  say "Oh! It's over already. We missed him. What a shame!"
  say "Your father has his Gym in Petalburg City now, so we'll see more of him. You should go and visit him."
  say "But first, go next door and say hello. Professor Birch lives there, and his child is just about your age!"
  hide vigoroth_carrying_box
  hide vigoroth_facing_away
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_PLAYERS_HOUSE_VIGOROTH_1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_PLAYERS_HOUSE_VIGOROTH_2
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 7
  endscene
  end
label ChampionHome
  scene "Home as the Champion"
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  emote players_house_1f_mom exclaim
  speaker "Mom"
  say "{player}! You're up! The whole of Littleroot is talking about you. My child, the Champion of Hoenn!"
  say "Oh, but come and look, quick! There's a special report on the television."
  text "...we interrupt this programme for a report from across Hoenn. A Pokémon nobody can name has been seen flying over the routes."
  text "It flies faster than a jet, they say. But no two people who saw it can agree on what colour it was..."
  say "I only caught a glimpse of it out of the window this morning. What colour did it look like to you, {player}?"
  choose "What colour was the Pokémon?" "RED" "BLUE"
  if result == 1 goto Blue
  roamer start "Latias"
  setvar VAR_ROAMER_POKEMON 0
  say "Red? Then it might be the one the old stories call Latias. Fancy that, flying about Hoenn right now!"
  goto DadComesHome
label Blue
  roamer start "Latios"
  setvar VAR_ROAMER_POKEMON 1
  say "Blue? Then it might be the one the old stories call Latios. Fancy that, flying about Hoenn right now!"
label DadComesHome
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_PLAYERS_HOUSE_DAD
  place players_house_1f_dad 5 6 left
  show players_house_1f_dad
  face players_house_1f_dad player
  speaker norman "Dad"
  say "{player}! I came as soon as I heard. The Champion! I always knew you'd go further than me one day."
  say "I've brought you something. Think of it as a present from a proud father."
  give "S.S. Ticket"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_SS_TICKET
  say "That ticket lets you board the S.S. Tidal at Slateport or Lilycove. And ships out of Lilycove go much further still."
  say "Well, a Gym Leader can't stay away from his Gym for long. Look after your mother, {player}. I'll see you soon!"
  walk players_house_1f_dad right 2
  walk players_house_1f_dad down 2
  walk players_house_1f_dad right 1
  hide players_house_1f_dad
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PLAYERS_HOUSE_DAD
  speaker "Mom"
  say "That's your father for you. He was so proud he could hardly stand still!"
  setvar VAR_HOENN_CHAMPION_HOME 1
  endscene
  end


# Out of the door before the clock is set (the area's trigger 0, on VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 4)
script GoSeeRoom
  face players_house_1f_mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Wait, {player}! You haven't even seen your room yet. And don't forget to set the clock!"
  walk player up 1
  end


# Mom, once the moving is done: she sees to the team, as every Mom does
script Mom
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE < 7 goto Busy
  speaker "Mom"
  say "You look tired out, sweetie. Sit down for a minute and catch your breath."
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "There, all better! Your Pokémon look happier already. Don't forget to come home now and then."
  end
label Busy
  speaker "Mom"
  say "Go on upstairs and see your room, {player}. The clock won't set itself!"
  end
