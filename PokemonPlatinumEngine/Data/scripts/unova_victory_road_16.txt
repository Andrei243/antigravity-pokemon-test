# Victory Road's last tunnel (plan 22 · U9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/victory_road_16.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_HUGH_TUNNEL is 0 until Hugh has caught the player up in the tunnel, 1 after his battle. As the player comes along the
# passage towards the way out (our trigger across it, 12, 6 to 9) Hugh runs in from the summit wanting one more battle before the
# League: Black 2's TRAINER_RIVAL_16, _17 and _18 are his team by the player's starter (the one strong against it). He thanks the
# player for the journey, gives TM24 and runs back the way he came.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpF
  end


script Hugh
  scene "Hugh in Victory Road"
  place player 12 7 right
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_TUNNEL
  place hugh_tunnel 20 7 left
  show hugh_tunnel
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Wait up, {player}!"
  walk hugh_tunnel left 7
  say "Before you take on the Pokémon League, battle me one more time. Every battle makes us stronger, right?"
  if starter "Snivy" goto AgainstSnivy
  if starter "Tepig" goto AgainstTepig
  battle hugh_tunnel as "unova_rival_18"
  goto Fought
label AgainstSnivy
  battle hugh_tunnel as "unova_rival_16"
  goto Fought
label AgainstTepig
  battle hugh_tunnel as "unova_rival_17"
label Fought
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}... Thanks to you, I did what I set out to do on this journey. My sister's Purrloin is home."
  say "I wish you could have seen her face. This is my thanks!"
  give "TM24"
  say "You're seriously amazing. So go and become the Champion! Show everyone the kind of Trainer your Pokémon are proud of!"
  say "See you!"
  walk hugh_tunnel right 7 fast
  hide hugh_tunnel
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_TUNNEL
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_HUGH_TUNNEL 1
  endscene
  end
