# The Pokémon Fan Club in Vermilion City (plan 19 · K4, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/VermilionCity_PokemonFanClub/scripts.inc).
#
# The chairman, heard out about his Pokémon, gives the Bike Voucher (FLAG_GOT_BIKE_VOUCHER), which Cerulean City's
# Bike Shop takes for a Bicycle (cerulean_city_bike_shop.txt). The two members each think their own Pokémon the
# finest, and say so the more after the other has had their say (FireRed's FLAG_TEMP_2 and FLAG_TEMP_3: flags of the
# place here, FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_WOMAN and ..._FAT_MAN).


script Chairman
  speaker "Chairman"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_BIKE_VOUCHER goto Heard
  ask "Did you come to hear about my Pokémon? I'm the chairman of the Pokémon Fan Club!"
  if yes goto Story
  say "Oh, how sad. Come back when you have time to listen, won't you?"
  end
label Story
  scene "The chairman's Rapidash"
  say "Splendid! My favourite is my Rapidash. Its mane is a blaze, and it runs like the wind!"
  say "When it gallops, the whole field lights up. And it nuzzles me like a little Ponyta still. Simply darling!"
  say "...Oh, I've gone on far too long again. Thank you for listening! Please, take this."
  give "Bike Voucher"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_BIKE_VOUCHER
  say "Take that voucher to the Bike Shop in Cerulean City. It's good for one free bicycle!"
  endscene
  end
label Heard
  say "Hello, {player}! Did you come to hear about my Pokémon again? ...No? What a shame."
  end


# The woman with the Seel.
script Woman
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_FAT_MAN goto After
  say "Isn't my Seel adorable? That little nose! That sleek coat!"
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_WOMAN
  end
label After
  say "Hmph! Whatever he says about his Pikachu, Seel is far more charming!"
  clearflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_FAT_MAN
  end


# The man with the Pikachu.
script FatMan
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_WOMAN goto After
  say "Look at my Pikachu's tail! The way it twitches when it's happy... I could watch it all day."
  setflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_FAT_MAN
  end
label After
  say "A Seel? Please. My Pikachu is twice as cute, any day of the week!"
  clearflag FLAG_MAP_LOCAL_FAN_CLUB_SPOKE_TO_WOMAN
  end
