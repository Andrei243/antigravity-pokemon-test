# Larry's office, the end of his gym test (plan 34 · PD6): the secret order is made at the Treasure Eatery
# (Medali_TreasureEatery.txt, from the hints about the town in medali.txt), a gym trainer by the desks, and Larry at the
# far end with the Normal Badge, who battles a challenger who has made the order. Our own lines.


# Larry's battle for the Normal Badge. He Terastallizes his last into a Normal type. Plan 34 · decision 3 at its
# recommendation: his team is scaled to the badges the challenger holds as Kofu's is (Cascarrafa_GymTest.txt), its best
# at the cap of that many badges, so in the academy's order (four held) it is the original's
# (Data/world/paldea/trainers.json: paldea_larry, paldea_larry_b0 to _b7)
# Plan 34 · PD9: after Paldea's Hall of Fame the leader asks for a rematch once a day (FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_LARRY_REMATCH;
# docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "After Paldea's Hall of Fame"), with the team of paldea_larry_rematch
script Leader
  face larry player
  speaker paldea_larry "Larry"
  if badge normal goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_MEDALI_TEST_DONE goto NotYet
  scene "Larry"
  say "Ah. The challenger who ordered the secret menu. I'm Larry. I run this gym, among the other things I'm asked to run."
  say "Let's get this done. Normal types, nothing fancy. Ordinary isn't the same as easy, though."
  if badges >= 7 goto Held7
  if badges >= 6 goto Held6
  if badges >= 5 goto Held5
  if badges >= 4 goto Held4
  if badges >= 3 goto Held3
  if badges >= 2 goto Held2
  if badges >= 1 goto Held1
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b0"
  goto Beaten
label Held1
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b1"
  goto Beaten
label Held2
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b2"
  goto Beaten
label Held3
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b3"
  goto Beaten
label Held4
  battle larry as "paldea_larry"
  goto Beaten
label Held5
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b5"
  goto Beaten
label Held6
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b6"
  goto Beaten
label Held7
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_b7"
label Beaten
  say "Well. That was efficient. Here, the Normal Badge. Please don't make a fuss about it."
  givebadge normal
  text "{player} received the Normal Badge from Larry!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_MEDALI_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_dina"
  say "I have a meeting. I always have a meeting. Good luck out there."
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "The test is at the Treasure Eatery on the square. Order the secret menu, then come back. I'll be here. Working."
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "You're not enrolled at the academy, are you. Then I can't battle you. Rules. Come back once you are."
  end
label Won
  if not flag PaldeaHallOfFame goto Chat
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_LARRY_REMATCH goto Chat
  ask "You again. Fine. My manager says I should battle the Champion. Officially. Shall we?"
  if no goto Chat
  say "Right. Let's keep this efficient."
  battle larry as "paldea_larry_rematch"
  say "...That'll go in the report. Come back tomorrow. I'll still be here. Unfortunately."
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_LARRY_REMATCH
  end
label Chat
  say "Back again? I'm on my break. Technically. My breaks are also meetings."
  end
