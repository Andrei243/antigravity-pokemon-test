# Santalune City's Gym (plan 23 · Z1, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos drawn by hand, its first chapter and its roller skates"): Viola, a
# photographer of Bug-type Pokémon, battles for the Bug Badge, Kalos's first (`givebadge bug`), and gives a TM. The
# hall is a web over the dark (the map's puzzle, SantaluneWeb: plan 23, Kalos's Gyms): its strands hold while the dew lies
# on them, and a knot at a ring's end shakes the dew from one set to the other. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's
# count: the Badge makes it 9. Her team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_viola_1; her two trainers are the hall's own.


script Viola
  face viola player
  speaker "Viola"
  if badge bug goto Won
  scene "Viola"
  say "Welcome to the Santalune Gym! I'm Viola, its Leader. When I'm not battling, I'm behind a camera."
  say "Through a lens you learn to wait for the right moment. Bug-type Pokémon taught me that."
  say "Let's see if you know when to strike. I'll capture this battle in a single shot!"
  battle self as "kalos_viola_1"
  say "What a picture! You and your Pokémon were perfectly in focus the whole time."
  say "Here, this is yours. The Bug Badge, proof that you won at the Santalune Gym."
  givebadge bug
  text "{player} received the Bug Badge from Viola!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_SANTALUNE_GYM
  defeat "kalos_lass_mireille"
  defeat "kalos_bug_catcher_luc"
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 9
  objective kalosRoute4
label Tm
  say "And take this as well. My bugs use it to strike and slip away before anyone can blink."
  give "TM89"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_VIOLA
  say "The road to Lumiose City starts north of town, past the garden. There's a lot more of Kalos to see through your lens!"
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_VIOLA goto Tm
  say "I still can't stop thinking about our battle. I wish I'd taken more photos of it!"
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge bug goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Viola's Bug types don't care for fire or wind. Pick your partner well!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Viola! Now that's a photo finish I'd have liked to see."
  end
