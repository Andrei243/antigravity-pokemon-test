# The Safari Zone's gate (plan 20 · J8, part b, and J9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_D47).
#
# The workmen by the gate are kept off the plaza until Warden Baoba has set his second test (VAR_UNK_4057 5,
# safari_zone_entrance_interior.txt), where HeartGold's new game sets their flag.
#
# Baoba's second call: once the player has shown him a Geodude from the zone (VAR_UNK_4057 3), he works on his second
# test until the next day (FLAG_DAILY_BAOBA_THINKING, a flag of ours that the new day clears); the next time the player
# comes to the plaza after that he rings to say it is ready (VAR_UNK_4057 4), where HeartGold rings wherever the player
# is once the hours have passed (UpdateSafariZoneIGT, docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The Johto Safari Zone").


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_UNK_4057 < 5 setflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFARI_ZONE_WORKERS
  if var VAR_UNK_4057 != 3 end
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_BAOBA_THINKING end
  scene "Warden Baoba's second call"
  sound "select"
  text "Brrrring! Brrrring! It's the Pokégear..."
  speaker "Baoba"
  say "Hello, {player}! Warden Baoba here. Sorry to keep you waiting, but the second test is ready at last!"
  say "Come and see me at the Safari Zone and I'll tell you all about it."
  text "Click... The call has ended."
  setvar VAR_UNK_4057 4
  endscene
  end

# D47_005: a song one time, a jingle the next
script Singer
  speaker "Boy"
  if var VAR_TEMP_SAFARI_SINGER == 1 goto Jingle
  say "Safari, safari, oh what fun! Safari, safari, catch everyone! Pokémon are waiting for us all!"
  say "...I made that song up. I sing it whenever I come here."
  setvar VAR_TEMP_SAFARI_SINGER 1
  end
label Jingle
  say "Up goes the Safari Ball into the blue! Out comes a friend for me and you! I love the Safari Zone!"
  say "...That's my new jingle. Think anyone would want it for an advert?"
  setvar VAR_TEMP_SAFARI_SINGER 0
  end
