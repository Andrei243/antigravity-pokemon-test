# The clearing in Newmoon Island's wood (plan 29 · LS11a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_newmoon_island_forest.s).
#
# Darkrai stands over the dark pool in the clearing's middle for whoever the Harbor Inn's dream brings here
# (CanalaveHarborInn.txt). The wood's own arrival script keeps its flag, as the original's does
# (FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_DARKRAI): it stands there until it is caught or beaten (FLAG_NEWMOON_ISLAND_DARKRAI_GONE,
# ours), and a Darkrai run from is back the next time the wood is come to. Coming here at all ends the dream's hold on
# the island's pier: its sailor is there again once the player walks back out (FLAG_ENTERED_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST, ours;
# newmoon_island.txt). Platinum's own level, 50.
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes"), the row of time, space and the other world: the bars,
# the camera on it, a dream's dark mist held round it (plan 29 · LS10's `mist`, in the rift's place) over a ripple under
# slow motion, and the Pokémon there and not there,
# as a dream is (flicker); its cry with the camera shaken, a line, and the battle's own way in (plan 09 · L14's
# Mythical).


# NewmoonIslandForest_OnTransition
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_ENTERED_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_DARKRAI
  if flag FLAG_NEWMOON_ISLAND_DARKRAI_GONE end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_DARKRAI
  end


# NewmoonIslandForest_Darkrai
script Darkrai
  scene "Darkrai on Newmoon Island"
  music stop
  face player darkrai
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 16 13 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  if facing up call DrawsBack
  effect mist 16 12 hold
  slowmo 0.5 1.5
  effect ripple 16 13
  flicker darkrai 4
  cry "Darkrai"
  camera shake 0.5
  speaker none
  text "A shadow stands over the dark pool. Something in it is looking straight at {player}."
  effect stop 16 12
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Darkrai" 50
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_NEWMOON_ISLAND_DARKRAI_GONE
  hide darkrai
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST_DARKRAI
  music area
  speaker none
  text "The shadow is gone. Only the pool is left, still and dark."
  end
label Fled
  music area
  end


# Spoken to from the south it would stand behind the player's own head: it drifts back a step first, into the mist
script DrawsBack
  walk darkrai up 1
  face darkrai down
  return
