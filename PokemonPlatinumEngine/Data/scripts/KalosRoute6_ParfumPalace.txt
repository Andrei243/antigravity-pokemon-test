# Parfum Palace's hall, at the head of Route 6 (plan 23, Kalos finished; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Parfum Palace,
# the bicycle shop and the rest"). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beat as it is publicly described: the palace's
# owner, fond of his Furfrou, which has run off from its grooming. Its gate opens once the butler has lent the player the
# palace's flute (FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_HM01, set with it). Found in the east wing, the Furfrou trots back to its master
# (FLAG_KALOS_PALACE_FURFROU_FOUND), who gives a Sun Stone once (FLAG_KALOS_PALACE_GIFT).


script Owner
  face owner player
  speaker "Palace Owner"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_PALACE_GIFT goto Done
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_PALACE_FURFROU_FOUND goto Thanks
  say "Welcome to my palace! You are the Trainer my butler lent the flute to, I believe. Do look around."
  say "Though I confess I am distracted. My Furfrou slipped away halfway through its grooming. Have you seen it?"
  end
label Thanks
  scene "The palace's thanks"
  say "My Furfrou, home again and half groomed! You found it in the east wing? You have my deepest thanks."
  say "Please, accept this. It came from the palace's own sun garden, and it is far more use to a Trainer than to me."
  give "Sun Stone"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_PALACE_GIFT
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "My Furfrou has not left my side since. I think it rather enjoyed its adventure."
  say "Do try the gardens behind the hall. Find the fountain in the evening, and I shall show you something splendid."
  end


script Maid
  face maid player
  speaker "Maid"
  say "The master spends more on his Furfrou's grooming than on the whole palace's dusting. Not that I'm complaining."
  end


script FurfrouAway
  face furfrou_away player
  cry "Furfrou"
  say "Furfrou is hiding in a corner of the east wing, its coat only half trimmed. It gives you a guilty look."
  say "It seems happy to be found, and trots off toward its master."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_PALACE_FURFROU_FOUND
  end


script FurfrouHome
  face furfrou_home player
  cry "Furfrou"
  say "Furfrou sits proudly beside its master, waiting for the rest of its grooming."
  end
