# Cinnabar Island as HeartGold has it three years on (plan 20 · J15, part g). docs/scripts.md describes the language;
# the lines are our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T09).
#
# Blue, the Leader of the Viridian Gym, stands by the ruins looking out to sea. Spoken to, he says what the volcano did
# and that his Gym waits for a challenger who holds Kanto's other seven Badges; with seven he goes home to
# Viridian (plan 20 · J15, part h): FLAG_JOHTO_BLUE_BACK_IN_GYM, a flag of ours, takes him off the island and opens the
# Gym's door, where he battles for the Earth Badge (viridian_gym.txt).


script Blue
  face t09_gsleader16 player
  speaker "Blue"
  if badges of kanto >= 7 goto Seven
  say "Huh. A Champion from Johto, all the way out here. Look at this place. The volcano blew and the whole town went with it."
  say "I'm the Leader of the Viridian Gym. Funny, right? I'm never there. I don't take on just anyone."
  say "Come back when you've got Kanto's other seven Badges. Then we'll see if you're worth my time."
  end
label Seven
  say "Seven of Kanto's Badges, huh? Not bad. Not bad at all."
  say "Right, I'm heading back to Viridian. Come and find me at my Gym, and don't keep me waiting."
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_BLUE_BACK_IN_GYM
  hide t09_gsleader16
  end
