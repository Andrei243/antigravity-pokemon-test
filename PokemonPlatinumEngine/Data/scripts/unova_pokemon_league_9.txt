# The Champion's room (plan 22 · U9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats
# (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league_9.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_IRIS is 0 until Iris is beaten in this challenge, 1 after; the plaza's arrival puts it back to 0. As the player
# comes to the head of the walk (our trigger across it, 15 to 17, 43) Iris comes down from her dais and battles, with Black 2's
# team (unova_champion_iris) or, once Unova's Hall of Fame has been entered, the second round's (unova_champion_iris_2). Won, she
# leads the new Champion to the Hall of Fame (unova_pokemon_league_10.txt). Lost, the challenge is over and starts again.


script Iris
  scene "Iris the Champion"
  place player 16 43 up
  walk 0 down 8
  face 0 player
  speaker "Iris"
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Again
  say "Hi! You made it all the way here, past the whole Elite Four!"
  say "I'm Iris. I'm the Champion of the Pokémon League. Can you believe it? Sometimes I can't either."
  say "I know how much your Pokémon trust you. I can tell just by looking at them. So let's have a battle we'll both remember!"
  battle 0 as "unova_champion_iris"
  goto Won
label Again
  say "You came back! I've been training every day, waiting for this. Let's go!"
  battle 0 as "unova_champion_iris_2"
label Won
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Wow... You're amazing! Your Pokémon fought their very hardest for you, and you fought for them."
  say "That means you're the Champion now! Come with me. There's a room where every Champion is remembered."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_IRIS 1
  endscene
  fade out 0.8
  warp "UnovaPokemonLeague_HallOfFame" 8 14 up
  end


# Iris on her dais, spoken to before the challenger has come up the walk
script IrisTalk
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Come on up! I'm waiting for you!"
  end
