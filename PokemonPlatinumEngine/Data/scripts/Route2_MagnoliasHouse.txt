# Professor Magnolia's house at the end of Route 2 (plan 33 · GL1): the professor and her granddaughter Sonia, and the
# Pokédex put on the player's phone. The lines are our own, on Sword and Shield's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Galar's first chapter"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is postwick.txt's count.


# In at the door for the first time: the professor, Sonia and the Pokédex
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY != 7 end
  scene "The professor and the Pokedex"
  face magnolia player
  speaker "Prof. Magnolia"
  say "Ah, the young Trainer from Postwick. Do come in. Hop has been here ten minutes and has eaten all the biscuits."
  speaker "Sonia"
  say "Hi! I'm Sonia, Gran's assistant. Well, sort of. Can I see your phone? It needs something every Trainer needs."
  text "Sonia tapped at the phone for a moment and handed it back."
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "The Pokédex was added to {player}'s phone!"
  speaker "Sonia"
  say "There! It records every Pokémon you meet. Leon never filled his in, so don't you follow his example."
  speaker "Prof. Magnolia"
  say "Hop is waiting in the garden, I believe, rather impatiently. Go on, then. We'll talk more this evening."
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_GARDEN2
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 8
  objective galarGarden
  endscene
  end


script Magnolia
  speaker "Prof. Magnolia"
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 10 goto Band
  say "A Pokémon that grows to the size of a house. Galar is the only place it happens, and I mean to learn why."
  end
label Band
  say "Look after that band. The power in a Wishing Star is no toy, but in a good Trainer's hands it is a wonder."
  end


script Sonia
  speaker "Sonia"
  say "Gran's been studying Dynamaxing since before I was born. I keep telling her to write it all down somewhere sensible."
  end
