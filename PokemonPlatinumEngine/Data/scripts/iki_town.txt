# Iki Town, Melemele Island (plan 23 · Z5). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Alola's first chapter"): the kahuna found away, and his return with the three
# Pokémon, his grandson Hau and the first battle. VAR_ALOLA_STORY is alola_route_1.txt's count.
#
# The people: kukui (FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_KUKUI_IKI, cleared at the door on Route 1), hala and hau (FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_HALA_IKI,
# FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_HAU_IKI, cleared as the kahuna comes home). Hau's teams are Alola's trainers.json's alola_hau_1 to _3:
# the Pokémon he takes is the one strong against the player's (Litten against Rowlet, Popplio against Litten, Rowlet
# against Popplio).


# Up from Route 1 into the village for the first time: the professor finds the kahuna out
script KahunaAway
  scene "The kahuna away"
  walk kukui down 6
  waitmoves
  face kukui player
  face player up
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Welcome to Iki Town! This is where the island's festival is held, right on that stage."
  say "Bad news, though: the kahuna's gone off somewhere. He does that. He's a busy man."
  say "Oh, and a girl I know just ran up the Mahalo Trail, north of the village, in an awful hurry. Would you go and check on her for me?"
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_LILLIE_TRAIL
  walk kukui up 6
  waitmoves
  face kukui down
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 3
  objective alolaTrail
  endscene
  end


# Back down from the trail: the kahuna comes home, with three Pokémon for a new Trainer
script KahunaHome
  scene "The kahuna comes home"
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_HALA_IKI
  place hala 249 116 up
  place kukui 251 116 up
  show hala
  face player down
  speaker "Hala"
  say "Ho ho! So you're the new face Kukui told me about. And it seems the island's guardian has taken an interest in you as well!"
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Hala's the kahuna of Melemele, the one who watches over the whole island. Kahuna, this is {player}."
  speaker "Hala"
  say "Every child in Alola goes out into the world with a partner. Here are three who have been waiting. Choose the one you like!"
  starter
  text "{player} chose {starter}!"
  speaker "Hala"
  say "A fine choice. May the two of you grow strong together."
  speaker "Hau"
  say "Tutu! Wait for me! Did I miss it?"
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_HAU_IKI
  place hau 249 125 up
  show hau
  walk hau up 8 fast
  waitmoves
  face hau player
  say "Alola! I'm Hau. That's my grandpa, the kahuna! You got your Pokémon already? Then this one's mine!"
  speaker "Hala"
  say "You were off eating again, weren't you? Very well, very well."
  speaker "Hau"
  say "Hey, {player}! We've both got Pokémon now, so let's battle! The first battle's the best one!"
  if starter "Rowlet" goto AgainstRowlet
  if starter "Litten" goto AgainstLitten
  battle hau as "alola_hau_3" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstRowlet
  battle hau as "alola_hau_1" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstLitten
  battle hau as "alola_hau_2" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "Hau"
  if lost goto HauWon
  say "Aw, you're really good! That was so much fun. Your Pokémon looked like it was having fun too!"
  goto Lab
label HauWon
  say "We won! But that was so close. Let's battle again sometime, okay?"
label Lab
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Woo! What a battle! Now, {player}, come by my laboratory on the beach south of your house. I've got something for you."
  walk kukui down 4 fast
  hide kukui
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_KUKUI_IKI
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_KUKUI_LAB
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 5
  objective alolaLab
  endscene
  end


# The people of the village, after their scenes
script Kukui
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "The kahuna might be back soon. Have a look around the village while you wait."
  end

script Hala
  speaker "Hala"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 13 goto Won
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 9 goto Challenge
  say "The island watches over all of us, and the guardian watches over the island. Take good care of your partner."
  end
label Challenge
  say "Pass Ilima's trial, and every captain's on Melemele that follows, and I will be waiting for you on this stage for your grand trial."
  end
label Won
  say "Ho ho! Akala's kahuna is a fierce one. Show her the same fire you showed me."
  end

script Hau
  speaker "Hau"
  say "Have you tried the malasadas in Hau'oli City? They're the best thing in Alola! Well, after Pokémon."
  end


# Up from Route 1 after meeting Team Skull and Ilima in Hau'oli: the island's festival, Hau's battle on the stage and
# the kahuna's blessing, a Z-Ring for the island challenge (plan 23 · Z5, second part; ours: Sun and Moon hold the
# festival on the first evening, before Hau'oli City)
script Festival
  scene "The festival"
  face player up
  speaker "Hala"
  say "Ah, there you are! Tonight we hold the festival for Tapu Koko, the guardian of Melemele."
  say "And in Iki Town, a festival is never complete without a battle on the stage. Hau has been waiting all day!"
  speaker "Hau"
  say "Finally! I've been practising with my new Pokémon. And I caught a new friend too! Let's give the guardian a good show!"
  if starter "Rowlet" goto AgainstRowlet
  if starter "Litten" goto AgainstLitten
  battle hau as "alola_hau_festival_3" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstRowlet
  battle hau as "alola_hau_festival_1" canlose
  goto Fought
label AgainstLitten
  battle hau as "alola_hau_festival_2" canlose
label Fought
  heal
  speaker "Hau"
  if lost goto HauWon
  say "Whoa, you got even stronger since this morning! The guardian must have liked that one."
  goto Blessing
label HauWon
  say "We did it! But only just. That was the best festival battle ever!"
label Blessing
  speaker "Hala"
  say "A fine battle, both of you. Tapu Koko is surely pleased."
  say "{player}, the guardian took an interest in you on the bridge. So I give you this, for the island challenge."
  # The Z-Ring only to a bag without one (plan 23 · Z6: a Champion who brought one from elsewhere keeps the one)
  if not item "Z-Ring" give "Z-Ring"
  say "Pass the trials of the island's captains, and the crystals they give you will let you and your Pokémon unleash Z-Moves through that ring."
  say "Your first trial is Captain Ilima's, in Verdant Cavern at the end of Route 2. Go with the guardian's blessing!"
  speaker "Hau"
  say "I'll go on ahead to Route 2! See you there, {player}!"
  hide hau
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_HIDE_HAU_IKI
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_FESTIVAL_DONE
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 9
  objective alolaVerdant
  endscene
  end


# Back up from Route 3 with Ilima's crystal: Hala's grand trial on the stage, the Melemele stamp, the Fightinium Z,
# and Tauros registered in the Ride Pager (plan 23 · Z5, second part; Overworld/RidePager.cs)
script GrandTrial
  scene "Hala's grand trial"
  face player up
  speaker "Hala"
  say "Ho ho! Ilima's crystal, and the look of a Trainer who has walked every road of Melemele. Then the time has come."
  say "I am Hala, kahuna of this island. As kahuna, I will face you with everything I have. Show me the strength you have found!"
  battle hala as "alola_hala_1"
  speaker "Hala"
  say "Ha! Splendid, splendid! The guardian chose well."
  givebadge Melemele
  text "{player}'s Trainer card received Melemele's stamp!"
  give "Fightinium Z"
  say "And take this, too. With it, the Tauros of the island will come when you call."
  give "Ride Pager"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_TAUROS
  text "{player} registered Tauros in the Ride Pager! Tauros can charge through cracked rocks."
  give "TM54"
  say "Kukui is waiting at Hau'oli's marina with his boat. Akala Island is next, and its kahuna will not go easy on you!"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_MARINA
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 13
  objective alolaSail
  endscene
  end
