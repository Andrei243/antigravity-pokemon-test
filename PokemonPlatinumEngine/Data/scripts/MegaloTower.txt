# Megalo Tower's top floor, Ultra Megalopolis (plan 23 · Z8). The lines are our own, on Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon's beats
# as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ultra Space and the Battle Royal Dome"). The Lightseekers
# wait here once the seven Ultra Beasts have been met (FLAG_ALOLA_ULTRA_AT_TOWER, set at the Altar), before Necrozma,
# the being that took the city's light long ago. Necrozma is battled at 65 after a staged beat and back each time the
# tower is come to until it is caught; the first battle, whatever comes of it, gives the light back (VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA 4,
# FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED), which draws Blacephalon to the city and Stakataka to the Altar.


script Necrozma
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA >= 4 goto Again
  speaker "Veyra"
  say "There. That prism is what took our light. It has slept on it for longer than anyone in this city has been alive."
  say "We can't wake it and hope to stand. You can. Please, {player}."
label Again
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "Necrozma"
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 7 4 1.0
  camera zoom 1.5 0.8
  effect beam 7 4 wait
  slowmo 0.4 1.2
  cry "Necrozma"
  text "The prism cracked, and light poured out of it as a shape of black crystal unfolded, hungry for more!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Necrozma" 65 nofleeing
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_ALOLA_CAUGHT_NECROZMA
  hide self
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA >= 4 end
  effect burst 7 4 wait
  speaker "Veyra"
  say "The light... It's going back. All of it, back to the sky. Look, Oskin. Look at the windows!"
  speaker "Oskin"
  say "I can see my own hands without a lamp. {player}, everyone in the city will want to thank you."
  speaker "Veyra"
  say "The wormholes will close now, mostly. The ones that stay open, we'll watch. Thank you for everything."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA 4
  objective alolaRoyalDome
  end


script Veyra
  face veyra player
  speaker "Veyra"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA >= 4 goto Lit
  say "Necrozma has never let anyone this close. It knows you are different."
  end
label Lit
  say "We were the Lightseekers. I suppose we'll need a new name now. Come back whenever you like, {player}."
  end


script Oskin
  face oskin player
  speaker "Oskin"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA >= 4 goto Lit
  say "Stay behind us if you like. No? Ha. Of course not."
  end
label Lit
  say "Back home on Alola, they say there's a dome where four Trainers battle at once. You should see it."
  end
