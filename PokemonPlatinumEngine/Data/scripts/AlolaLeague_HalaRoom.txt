# Aether Paradise and the League"): the first of the Elite Four, Fighting. His team is alola_hala_league; beaten,
# FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_HALA opens the door to Olivia's room.


script League
  face hala player
  speaker "Hala"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_HALA goto Won
  if flag AlolaHallOfFame goto Rematch
  scene "Hala"
  say "Ho ho! Here you are at last, {player}, and look how far the island's champion has come since Iki Town."
  say "I am Hala, once your kahuna and now the first of the Alola League. I will not hold back, as you must not hold back."
  say "The sea is wide and the sun is hot. Show me everything!"
  battle self as "alola_hala_league"
  say "Ha ha! Magnificent! My heart is as full as the first time I saw you stand up to Tapu Koko."
  say "Go on. Olivia is next. She has the strength of the rock beneath our islands."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_HALA
  endscene
  end
# The second climb and every one after it (the wave after Ultra Space): the rematch team (alola_hala_rematch)
label Rematch
  scene "HalaRematch"
  say "Ho ho! The Champion comes back to the bottom of the mountain to climb it again. That is the island way!"
  say "My Pokémon have trained every morning since. Let us see whose sun shines hotter today!"
  battle self as "alola_hala_rematch"
  say "Ha! Still magnificent. Go on, Champion. The others are waiting to be beaten too."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_DEFEATED_HALA
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Olivia waits through the door. Go with my blessing."
  end
