# Route 222 (plan 06 · R14a). docs/scripts.md describes the language.
#
# The original's arrival script: at night the policemen battle, and from morning to twilight their doubles on the
# same tiles only keep watch. The swap is made as the player comes, so it holds until they come again.


script OnEnter
  if time night goto Battle
  if time latenight goto Battle
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_222_POLICEMAN_THOMAS_NO_BATTLE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_222_POLICEMAN_THOMAS
  end
label Battle
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_222_POLICEMAN_THOMAS
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_222_POLICEMAN_THOMAS_NO_BATTLE
  end


# The rich boy by the road gives TM56 away, once (plan 02 · S13; the original's Route222_RichBoy).
script RichBoy
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_222_TM56 goto Explains
  say "Everyone thinks a Poké Ball is the only thing a Trainer ever throws. But is it? I want people to stop and ask themselves that!"
  say "Which is why I hand this Technical Machine to anyone who passes."
  give "TM56"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_222_TM56
label Explains
  say "With Fling, a Pokémon hurls whatever it is holding straight at the foe, and what happens next depends on what it threw."
  say "Do remember: a Pokémon holding nothing has nothing to fling, and the move simply fails."
  end
