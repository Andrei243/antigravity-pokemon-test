# Team Galactic's headquarters in Veilstone City, the ground floor (plan 02 · S11, part b), rebuilt to the original's
# plan. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_galactic_hq_1f.s).
#
# The building's three doors are open from the start, as the original's are. The two on the west lead into the lobby,
# where a receptionist and three members talk and a shutter keeps the stairs behind it until the Galactic Key
# (FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_1F_DOOR). The third leads into a room with nothing but a green pad in it: the way out of
# the headquarters ends there (the fourth floor's green panel), and it carries nobody the other way. Everything else
# on this floor is reached from the basement or by warp panel: the way in is the warehouse's.
#
# What the lobby's four say changes once Team Galactic has left Lake Valor for good (the original asks
# FLAG_GALACTIC_LEFT_LAKE_VALOR, which Sendoff Spring sets as the player comes out of the Distortion World, plan 02 ·
# S12), and Commander Saturn stands in the lobby from then on (FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_1F_SATURN, which the same scene
# clears).


# The original's OnTransition.
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_FIRST_ARRIVAL_GALACTIC_HQ
  end


# The receptionist behind her counter (the original's script 2).
script Receptionist
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "Welcome to Team Galactic's headq... Ahem. Welcome to the Galactic Veilstone Building."
  if flag FLAG_GALACTIC_LEFT_LAKE_VALOR goto Afterwards
  say "We study the energy of the cosmos here. Power without end, enough for everybody! Wouldn't that be something?"
  end
label Afterwards
  say "Whether it's for the better or the worse I couldn't tell you, but nothing whatever is planned for today."
  end


# The scientist in the lobby (script 5).
script Scientist
  if flag FLAG_GALACTIC_LEFT_LAKE_VALOR goto Afterwards
  say "Team Galactic's research goes on day and night in this building."
  say "New sources of energy: that is what we are after."
  end
label Afterwards
  say "This is where Team Galactic works. On what, now? On our dream, I suppose."
  say "A wonderful new world... Not that anybody has told me how."
  end


# The grunt who wanders the lobby (script 3).
script GruntM
  say "Whatever can be taken, Team Galactic takes! Whatever isn't needed, Team Galactic throws out!"
  if flag FLAG_GALACTIC_LEFT_LAKE_VALOR goto Afterwards
  say "That's how we do things!"
  end
label Afterwards
  say "...That's how we did things, anyway. There's nothing left to take now."
  end


# The grunt in the lobby's corner (script 4).
script GruntF2
  if flag FLAG_GALACTIC_LEFT_LAKE_VALOR goto Afterwards
  say "You? You're far too young for us."
  say "Come back when you've done some growing."
  end
label Afterwards
  say "Where has our boss got to, I wonder..."
  end


# Commander Saturn, in the lobby once it is all over (script 8).
script Saturn
  speaker "Saturn"
  say "So. It's you. I heard what happened on Spear Pillar."
  say "Master Cyrus despised the heart for being unfinished. And yet it was hearts he stirred, with every speech he gave, to make people follow him."
  say "The thing he hated most was the tool he used. I found that fascinating. I wanted to see where he would take it, and I did, at his side."
  say "It's over now. A world with no heart in it... Who would want such a thing?"
  say "So what becomes of Team Galactic? I have learned this much: going to extremes solves nothing."
  say "Perhaps we really should look for new sources of energy. Instead of only saying so in our advertisements."
  end


# The shutter before the stairs (script 6): it opens to the Galactic Key, for good.
script Door
  speaker none
  if item "Galactic Key" goto HasKey
  say "The shutter is locked. It wants a special key."
  end
label HasKey
  ask "The shutter is locked. It wants a special key. Use the Galactic Key?"
  if no goto Leave
  sound "door_slide"
  hide galactic_hq_door_west
  hide galactic_hq_door_east
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_1F_DOOR
  say "The shutter slid open."
label Leave
  end

# The creed on the wall, read with no name on the box.
script Notice
  speaker none
  sayown
  end
