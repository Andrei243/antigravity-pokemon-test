# Route 6, the Palace Avenue (plan 23 · Z2, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Central Kalos, drawn by hand: Route 5 to Cyllage City"): the palace's
# butler keeps the gate at the head of the avenue and, once the castle's keeper has sent the player, lends them the
# palace's flute, which wakes the Snorlax on Route 7. The palace itself and its owner are a later session's.
# VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 14, the castle's keeper met, becomes 15 here.


script Butler
  face butler player
  speaker "Butler"
  if item "Poké Flute" goto Lent
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 14 goto Flute
  say "Good day. The palace is closed to visitors this week. The master is entertaining, you understand."
  end
label Flute
  scene "The palace's flute"
  say "Good day. The palace is closed to visitors this week... Oh? Monsieur Aubert sent you?"
  say "A Snorlax on the south road, again. The last one slept there for a whole summer, I recall."
  say "Very well. The master would want the road open. Take the palace's flute, and do bring it back one day."
  give "Poké Flute"
  call GiveCut
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 15
  objective kalosSnorlax
  endscene
  end
label Lent
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_HM01 goto LentCut
label LentSay
  say "Play the flute close to the sleeper and it will wake. It has never failed the palace yet."
  end
label LentCut
  call GiveCut
  goto LentSay


# HM01 (plan 23, Kalos's HMs; X and Y find it in the hedge maze behind the palace, which this Kalos doesn't draw): the butler
# hands it over with the flute, or to a save already past the flute when spoken to again. A small tree on the avenue's
# north side stands before a gap in the hedge.
script GiveCut
  say "And this, from the palace gardeners. They keep the hedges with it, and they will not miss one."
  give "HM01"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_HM01
  say "A Pokémon that knows Cut can clear a small tree out of your way. There is one by the hedge, I believe."
  return
