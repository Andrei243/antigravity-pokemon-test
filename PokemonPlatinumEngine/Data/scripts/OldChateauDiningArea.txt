# The Old Chateau's dining room (plan 01 · M11, part a), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language (scripts_old_chateau_dining_area.s).
#
# Now and then an old man stands at the head of the long table as the player comes in: the original rolls a die of
# a hundred and one for it and shows him on ten or less (CheckShouldShowGhost 10). He turns, glides off along the
# table and is gone before anyone can speak to him. His flag (FLAG_HIDE_OLD_CHATEAU_DINING_AREA_OLD_MAN) hides him
# the rest of the time.


script OnEnter
  random 101
  if result <= 10 goto Ghost
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLD_CHATEAU_DINING_AREA_OLD_MAN
  end
label Ghost
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_OLD_CHATEAU_DINING_AREA_OLD_MAN
  place old_man 19 4 down
  show old_man
  walk player up 1
  wait 0.5
  face old_man left
  wait 0.3
  walk old_man left 10
  hide old_man
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_OLD_CHATEAU_DINING_AREA_OLD_MAN
  end
