# Katy's gym test, the olive roll (plan 34 · PD3): three giant olives in the course's three gaps (cards of our own, PD3's
# second session), each rolled on with a push into the goal, a gym trainer beside the course, and Katy at its end with
# the Bug Badge. An olive rolled away stays gone (its own flag). The roll is a nudge, not a puzzle, as Galar's herding
# is (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The South and East Provinces and the first three gyms"). Our own lines, on Scarlet and
# Violet's beat of rolling a giant olive into a goal.


script Test1
  text "A giant olive, taller than a Smoliv, sits in the gap. {player} gave it a push..."
  text "It rolled away down the course and into the goal!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_CORTONDO_TEST_1
  end


script Test2
  text "Another giant olive, glossy and green. {player} gave it a push..."
  text "It bumped off the rail and trundled into the goal!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_CORTONDO_TEST_2
  end


script Test3
  text "The last olive was the roundest of all. {player} gave it a good push..."
  text "It rolled straight into the goal. The olive roll is cleared!"

# Katy's battle for the Bug Badge. She Terastallizes her Teddiursa into a Bug type (the trainer AI's own use, plan 06 ·
# R30, with the Tera Type her team gives it). Her levels are fixed, whatever order the gyms are taken in (plan 34 ·
# decision 3: the first three gyms keep the original's levels)
# Plan 34 · PD9: after Paldea's Hall of Fame the leader asks for a rematch once a day (FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_KATY_REMATCH;
# docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "After Paldea's Hall of Fame"), with the team of paldea_katy_rematch
script Leader
  face katy player
  speaker "Katy"
  if badge bug goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  scene "Katy"
  say "Oh my, you rolled every one of them in! You must be a challenger from the academy. I'm Katy, Cortondo's gym leader."
  say "My Bug types are small, but sweet things can sting. Let's see how your team takes the heat of my oven!"
  battle katy as "paldea_katy"
  say "Well, I never! That was baked to perfection. You've earned this: the Bug Badge."
  givebadge bug
  text "{player} received the Bug Badge from Katy!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_CORTONDO_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_olive"
  say "Come by the bakery any time. And good luck with the rest of Victory Road!"
  endscene
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "A challenger? Oh, but you aren't enrolled at the academy yet. Come back once term has started, dear."
  end
label Won
  if not flag PaldeaHallOfFame goto Chat
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_KATY_REMATCH goto Chat
  ask "Our new Champion, in my bakery's gym! I've been training a new batch. Care to taste-test it?"
  if no goto Chat
  say "Wonderful! Fresh out of the oven, and every bit as tough as it looks!"
  battle katy as "paldea_katy_rematch"
  say "Delicious work, Champion. Come back tomorrow and I'll have something new rising."
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_PALDEA_KATY_REMATCH
  end
label Chat
  say "Come back any time. I'll save you a pastry."
  end
