# Lavaridge Town's herb shop (plan 21 · H4, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/LavaridgeTown_HerbShop/scripts.inc): its counter sells Emerald's four herbs
# (marts.json's lavaridge_herb_shop), and the old man gives Charcoal once (FLAG_RECEIVED_CHARCOAL).


script HerbClerk
  face self player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome to the herb shop! Our medicine works wonders, and it costs next to nothing."
  shop "lavaridge_herb_shop"
  say "Please come again!"
  end


script OldMan
  face self player
  speaker "Old Man"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_CHARCOAL goto Given
  say "Come all the way to Lavaridge to look at herbal medicine, have you? Good for you. I like that. Here!"
  give "Charcoal"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_CHARCOAL
  end
label Given
  say "That Charcoal goes into our medicine. Let a Pokémon hold it, and its Fire moves burn all the hotter."
  end
