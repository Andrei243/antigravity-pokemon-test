# A lounge of the Battle Frontier island (plan 21 · H12, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/BattleFrontier_Lounge1/scripts.inc): the old breeder judges a Pokémon's potential
# by its six IVs, as the Battle Tower's judge does in Sinnoh (BattleTower.txt, StatsJudge; `frontier judge`).


script Breeder
  face self player
  speaker "Breeder"
  say "I've raised Pokémon all my life. Show me one of yours, and I'll tell you what it could become."
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Cancel
  frontier judge
  if result == 0 goto Cancel
  if result == 3 goto Egg
  frontier judged total
  if result <= 90 goto Plain
  if result <= 150 goto Good
  say "{wearer} has the makings of a true champion. Raise it with care!"
  goto Best
label Plain
  say "{wearer} is an ordinary sort. Love it all the same, and it'll serve you well."
  goto Best
label Good
  say "{wearer} has a good deal in it. You chose well."
label Best
  say "Its greatest gift is its {thing}."
  end
label Egg
  say "An Egg? Bring it back once it has hatched."
  end
label Cancel
  say "Come back if you change your mind."
  end
