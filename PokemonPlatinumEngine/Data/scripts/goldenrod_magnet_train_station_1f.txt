# Goldenrod City's Magnet Train station (plan 20 · J15, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T25R0501).
#
# The officer at the gate to the platform makes the ride to Saffron City in Kanto. HeartGold asks for the Magnet Train
# Pass, which the Copycat gives once the Power Plant is running again; here, as on the S.S. Aqua, the Hall of Fame is
# the ticket (the rulings' "HeartGold's Kanto, part c"). The ride is told over the dark and ends before the officer
# at Saffron's gate.


script Officer
  face t25r0501_policeman player
  speaker "Officer"
  if not flag JohtoHallOfFame goto Shut
  ask "The Magnet Train to Saffron City in Kanto is about to leave. Would you like to board?"
  if no goto Later
  say "All aboard! Please stand clear of the doors."
  fade out 0.6
  text "The Magnet Train glided out of Goldenrod, floating silently east toward Kanto..."
  warp "Saffron_MagnetTrainStation" 14 10 down
  fade in
  end
label Later
  say "The trains run all day. Come back whenever you're ready."
  end
label Shut
  say "The Magnet Train runs between Goldenrod City and Saffron City in Kanto."
  say "For now its seats are kept for those the League has named Champion. Win at the Indigo Plateau, and you can ride."
  end
