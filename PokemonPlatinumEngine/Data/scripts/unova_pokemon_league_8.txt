# Caitlin's room of the Elite Four (plan 22 · U9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league_8.s, read for the order alone).
#
# VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_CAITLIN is 0 until Caitlin is beaten in this challenge, 1 after; the plaza's arrival puts it back to 0, as a new
# challenge begins there. Caitlin battles with Black 2's first team (unova_elite_four_caitlin) and, once Unova's Hall of Fame has been
# entered, with the team of the second round (unova_elite_four_caitlin_2). Once beaten, the light in the corner (Pad) carries the
# player back down to the League's hall, beside the arm to this room.


script Caitlin
  face 0 player
  speaker "Caitlin"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_CAITLIN == 1 goto Beaten
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Again
  say "Mm... You woke me. Not many challengers make it this far before I've had my rest."
  say "I am Caitlin of the Elite Four. My Psychic-type Pokémon are waiting. Let me see what you're made of."
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_caitlin"
  goto Won
label Again
  say "You've returned. How delightful. Let us see how much you have grown."
  battle 0 as "unova_elite_four_caitlin_2"
label Won
  speaker "Caitlin"
  say "Splendid. Your Pokémon fought with grace, and you guided them with a clear mind."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_CAITLIN 1
  speaker none
  text "A light began to glow in the corner of the room."
  end
label Beaten
  say "I shall return to my rest now. Do go on. The Champion awaits."
  end


# The light in the corner, once Caitlin is beaten: back down to the League's hall
script Pad
  speaker none
  text "The light carried {player} back down to the hall."
  warp "UnovaPokemonLeague_Hall" 37 37 down
  end
