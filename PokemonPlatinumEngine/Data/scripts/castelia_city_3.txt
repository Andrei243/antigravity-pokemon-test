# Castelia City's Gym Street (plan 22 · U5, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/castelia_city_3.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA is the city's count (castelia_city_10.txt has it whole).
#
# Clyde, the Gym's guide (0), stands at its door: Burgh has gone off after some trouble in the city, and as he says so
# Iris (iris, ours, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_GYM_STREET) comes up the street looking for him, hears that the player is
# after Team Plasma and runs off to help (4). Once the sewers are behind the player and Iris has had her word on Thumb
# Pier (9), Clyde has gone in (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CLYDE_STREET) and the Gym is open.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script Clyde
  face 0 player
  speaker "Clyde"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA >= 4 goto After
  scene "Burgh away from his Gym"
  say "Here to challenge Burgh? I'm sorry, he isn't here. He said there might be trouble somewhere in the city, and off he went!"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_GYM_STREET
  place iris 20 58 up
  show iris
  speaker "???"
  say "Huh? Burgh's gone wandering off again?"
  walk iris up 9
  face iris right
  face 0 iris
  speaker "Clyde"
  say "Oh! Hello, Iris. Something came up, and Burgh isn't in right now."
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Hmm... But isn't Burgh always disappearing? He says he can't think of anything to paint and goes wandering out of his Gym."
  face iris player
  say "Hi there! Who are you? ...You're looking for Team Plasma? But Team Plasma broke up two years ago!"
  say "Oh, never mind that! You've got a problem, so I'll help you look!"
  face iris up
  say "Hmm... Now where would shady people go to hide?"
  face iris player
  emote iris exclaim
  say "I've got it! I bet that's where they are!"
  walk iris down 9 fast
  hide iris
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_GYM_STREET
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_AVENUE
  face 0 player
  speaker "Clyde"
  say "Good grief... Burgh and Iris are as bad as each other. She ran off toward the avenue, by the look of it."
  say "Head down to the avenue and go west past the Pokémon Center. You're sure to catch her up."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 4
  endscene
  end
label After
  say "Iris ran off toward the avenue. Go west past the Pokémon Center and you're sure to catch her up."
  end


script IrisGymStreet
  face iris player
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Where would shady people hide...?"
  end
