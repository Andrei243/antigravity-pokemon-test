# HeartGold's beats: the woman who lives here lets a trainer on the way to the League rest the team, behind a fade.


script Woman
  speaker "Woman"
  ask "The road to the League is a long one. Would your Pokémon like a rest?"
  if no goto NotNow
  say "Make yourselves at home."
  fade out
  fanfare heal
  heal
  fade in
  say "There, everyone looks lively again. Do your best at the League!"
  end
label NotNow
  say "All right. Come back if you get tired on the way."
  end
