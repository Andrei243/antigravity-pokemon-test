# Hammerlocke (plan 33 · GL5, its first session), the walled city at the heart of Galar, on the open world's engine: its
# places are Data/world3d/galar/places/hammerlocke.json. The lines are our own, on Sword and Shield's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hammerlocke, Route 6, Stow-on-Side and Ballonlea"). VAR_GALAR_STORY is
# postwick.txt's count: 24 the Fire Badge, 25 Leon and Hop at the gate.


# Through the south gate with three badges: Leon and Hop, waiting under the walls
script LeonInHammerlocke
  scene "Leon in Hammerlocke"
  faceto player leon_hammerlocke
  speaker "Hop"
  say "{player}! You made it! Three badges each, and here we are in Hammerlocke!"
  speaker "Leon"
  say "Welcome to Hammerlocke, the two of you. This city's walls have stood longer than the League has, and Raihan's gym is the last before the Champion Cup."
  say "He won't see a challenger with fewer than seven badges, though. Your road runs west from here, out of the gate and up Route 6 to Stow-on-Side."
  say "And if you've a moment, look in at the vault. Sonia's been up there all week with the old tapestries. She'd like to see you, I'd bet."
  speaker "Hop"
  say "I'm going straight to Route 6! I've been training the whole way from Motostoke. See you on the road, {player}!"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_LEON_HAMMERLOCKE
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_HAMMERLOCKE
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_SONIA_VAULT
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 25
  objective galarVault
  endscene
  end


# The east gate onto Route 7 (plan 33 · GL5, its second session): open to challengers with the fifth badge
script EastShut
  if badge fairy end
  text "A League staffer's sign stands in the east gate: Route 7 is for challengers with five badges."
  walkto player hammerlocke_east_back
  end


# Hammerlocke Stadium's door (plan 33 · GL5, its second session): Raihan sees challengers with seven badges and not one fewer
script StadiumShut
  if badges >= 7 end
  speaker "League Staff"
  say "Sorry! Raihan's gym is the last of the challenge. Come back with seven badges and the door's yours."
  walkto player hammerlocke_stadium_back
  end


# The station by the south gate (plan 33 · GL6): the train north to Wyndon, for challengers with all eight badges
script TrainToWyndon
  speaker "Station Staff"
  if badges >= 8 goto Board
  say "Trains north run to Wyndon, for the Champion Cup. Only challengers with all eight badges may ride them this season."
  end
label Board
  say "Eight badges! The Champion Cup is waiting for you in Wyndon."
  ask "Take the train north to Wyndon?"
  if no goto Later
  warpto "Galar" wyndon_station
  end
label Later
  say "The line north runs all day. Come back when you're ready."
  end
