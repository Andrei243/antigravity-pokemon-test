# Giratina's room at the end of Turnback Cave (plan 01 · M8; scripts_turnback_cave_giratina_room.s). Its doors
# are never aimed: every one of them leads back to the entrance.
#
# Giratina waits here after the Hall of Fame if it got away in the Distortion World (the Hall of Fame clears its
# flag, plan 02 · S14). Until then a new game keeps it hidden: a Pokémon can't be drawn standing in the field
# until plan 10 · F1. Once it is caught, a ball lies here each time the player comes back through Sendoff
# Spring, and what is in it hangs on how few rooms it took to get here.

script Giratina
  cry "Giratina"
  speaker none
  say "A roar shakes the whole cave!"
  wildbattle "Giratina" 47 nofleeing
  if var RESULT == 3 goto Caught
  hide self
  say "Giratina sank back into the shadows and was gone..."
  end
label Caught
  setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_GIRATINA
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TURNBACK_CAVE_GIRATINA_ROOM_GIRATINA
  hide self
  end

script Item
  if var VAR_TURNBACK_CAVE_ROOMS_VISITED == 3 goto ReaperCloth
  if var VAR_TURNBACK_CAVE_ROOMS_VISITED < 15 goto RareBone
  find "Stardust"
  goto Taken
label ReaperCloth
  find "Reaper Cloth"
  goto Taken
label RareBone
  find "Rare Bone"
label Taken
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TURNBACK_CAVE_GIRATINA_ROOM_ITEM
  hide self
  end

script Inscription
  speaker none
  say "Words are cut into the stone: \"Here the world we know and the world beyond it lie one over the other.\""
  end
