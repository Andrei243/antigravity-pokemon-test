# Giratina's room at the end of Turnback Cave (plan 01 · M8, plan 02 · S12; scripts_turnback_cave_giratina_room.s).
# Its doors are never aimed: every one of them leads back to the entrance.
#
# Giratina waits here, in its Altered Forme, for whoever did not catch it in the Distortion World: nobody comes to
# Turnback Cave before the Distortion World lets them out at Sendoff Spring, so its flag is clear from the start, as
# the original's is. Caught there or here it is gone for good; beaten here it is gone until the Hall of Fame is next
# entered (the original hides it as its room loads again after the battle, and the Hall of Fame clears the flag while
# FLAG_CAUGHT_GIRATINA is unset). Once it is caught, a ball lies here each time the player comes back through Sendoff
# Spring, and what is in it hangs on how few rooms it took to get here.
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes"): the bars, the camera to Giratina, its shadow
# sweeping across the room, the roar with the cave shaken and the picture wavering, and then the battle's own way in
# (plan 09 · L14's Mythical).
#
# Where Giratina stood a portal hangs once it is gone, into the corner of the Distortion World where the Griseous Orb
# lies (the original's background event on its tile).

script Giratina
  scene "Giratina in Turnback Cave"
  music stop
  face player self
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 11 14 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect shadow 11 14 2 left
  wait 1
  cry "Giratina"
  camera shake 0.8
  effect ripple 11 14
  speaker none
  say "A roar shakes the whole cave!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Giratina" 47 nofleeing
  if var RESULT == 3 goto Caught
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TURNBACK_CAVE_GIRATINA_ROOM_GIRATINA
  hide self
  say "Giratina sank back into the shadows and was gone..."
  end
label Caught
  setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_GIRATINA
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TURNBACK_CAVE_GIRATINA_ROOM_GIRATINA
  hide self
  end

script Item
  if var VAR_TURNBACK_CAVE_ROOMS_VISITED == 3 goto ReaperCloth
  if var VAR_TURNBACK_CAVE_ROOMS_VISITED < 15 goto RareBone
  find "Stardust"
  goto Taken
label ReaperCloth
  find "Reaper Cloth"
  goto Taken
label RareBone
  find "Rare Bone"
label Taken
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_TURNBACK_CAVE_GIRATINA_ROOM_ITEM
  hide self
  end

script Inscription
  speaker none
  say "Words are cut into the stone: \"Here the world we know and the world beyond it lie one over the other.\""
  end

# The portal where Giratina stood (the original's TurnbackCaveGiratinaRoom_Portal).
script Portal
  speaker none
  ask "The air is torn here, and somewhere else shows through the tear. Leap into the portal?"
  if no end
  say "{player} leapt into the portal!"
  sound "warp"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "DistortionWorldTurnbackCaveRoom" 46 45 down
  end
