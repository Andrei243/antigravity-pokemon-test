# Spear Pillar with Dialga's rift over its altar (plan 02 · S15, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_spear_pillar_dialga.s).
#
# The summit as a Champion who holds the Adamant Orb finds it once the elder of Celestic Town has told what she read in
# the shrine (mt_coronet_6f.txt decides which Spear Pillar the mountain's way out leads to). Nobody is here: only the
# rift, read from below it. Through it Dialga can be seen, and asked, it comes out to battle at level 70; the battle
# may be run from. Whatever its end but a loss, the rift is quiet from then on (VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE 1), and
# the mountain's way leads to Palkia's pillar or to the pillar as it stands; a Dialga that wasn't caught is behind its
# rift again once the Hall of Fame has next been entered (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt puts the state back to 0). Caught,
# it is gone for good (FLAG_CAUGHT_DIALGA).
#
# The Hall of Origin's step before the way down (VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE 1, for whoever carries the Azure Flute of an
# event) plays the flute: the summits' AzureFlute (plan 29 · LS11a). Its meeting is plan 29's beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes"): the bars, the
# camera up to the rift (the pillar's own model, d5_ana_d, standing on 29, 17: a rift effect there would hide behind
# it) wavering under slow motion, a burst out of it with the cry, a line, and then the battle's own way in (plan 09 ·
# L14's Legendary).


# SpearPillarDialga_Rift
script Rift
  speaker none
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE >= 1 goto Quiet
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_DIALGA goto Quiet
  text "Beyond the rift lies a place where nothing is the way it ought to be..." "And in it, looking back: Dialga!"
  ask "Will you challenge Dialga?"
  if no end
  scene "Dialga's rift"
  music stop
  face player up
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 29 15 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  slowmo 0.5 1.5
  effect ripple 29 17
  wait 1
  effect burst 29 17
  cry "Dialga"
  camera shake 0.6
  emote player exclaim
  text "Dialga pushes out of the rift, and time itself stumbles round it!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Dialga" 70
  setvar VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE 1
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_DIALGA
  end
label Quiet
  text "Time itself seems to pour out of the rift, more of it than anyone could stand in for long." "Where does it lead?"
  end


# The Azure Flute played on the top step (plan 29 · LS11a): the original's trigger before the way down, at 31, 52 of
# each of the summit's three maps, while VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE is 1, which Celestic Town's elder sets as she gives
# the flute. Coming up from the mountain is stepping onto it, so the question is asked as the summit is come to. Yes is
# a stair of light up the pillar's steps (plan 29 · LS10's `stair`, held, climbing away north from the steps' foot as
# the camera goes up after it) and the climb to the Hall of Origin (hall_of_origin.txt). The three summits' files each
# hold this script.
script AzureFlute
  speaker none
  ask "The Azure Flute is humming softly in {player}'s bag. Play it?"
  if no end
  scene "The stair of light"
  music stop
  face player up
  letterbox on 0.4
  sound "use_grass_whistle"
  wait 0.8
  camera path 31 48 3 nowait
  effect stair 31 50 hold
  effect sparkles 31 50
  wait 1.2
  text "A stair of light rises from the pillar's steps, up and up into the sky!"
  fade out 0.6
  effect stop 31 50
  camera release 0.1
  letterbox off 0.1
  warp "HallOfOrigin" 31 52 up
  fade in
  endscene
  end
