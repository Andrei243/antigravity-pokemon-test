# Spear Pillar with Dialga's rift over its altar (plan 02 · S15, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_spear_pillar_dialga.s).
#
# The summit as a Champion who holds the Adamant Orb finds it once the elder of Celestic Town has told what she read in
# the shrine (mt_coronet_6f.txt decides which Spear Pillar the mountain's way out leads to). Nobody is here: only the
# rift, read from below it. Through it Dialga can be seen, and asked, it comes out to battle at level 70; the battle
# may be run from. Whatever its end but a loss, the rift is quiet from then on (VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE 1), and
# the mountain's way leads to Palkia's pillar or to the pillar as it stands; a Dialga that wasn't caught is behind its
# rift again once the Hall of Fame has next been entered (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt puts the state back to 0). Caught,
# it is gone for good (FLAG_CAUGHT_DIALGA).
#
# The Hall of Origin's step before the way down (VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE 1, for whoever carries the Azure Flute of an
# event) is left dead: plan 03 · D12c. Stood in for until plan 09 · L14 gives the legendary Pokémon their ways in: the
# cry, a shake of the picture and the battle's own opening.


# SpearPillarDialga_Rift
script Rift
  speaker none
  if var VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE >= 1 goto Quiet
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_DIALGA goto Quiet
  text "Beyond the rift lies a place where nothing is the way it ought to be..." "And in it, looking back: Dialga!"
  ask "Will you challenge Dialga?"
  if no end
  cry "Dialga"
  camera shake 0.6
  text "Dialga: Gyugguh-gwaah!"
  wildbattle "Dialga" 70
  setvar VAR_SPEAR_PILLAR_DIALGA_STATE 1
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_DIALGA
  end
label Quiet
  text "Time itself seems to pour out of the rift, more of it than anyone could stand in for long." "Where does it lead?"
  end
