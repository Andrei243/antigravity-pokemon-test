# The lady by the shelves (the original's script 3) hands over a Scope Lens, once
# (FLAG_RECEIVED_FIGHT_AREA_MART_SCOPE_LENS), and frets over what her Pokémon should hold ever after.

script Socialite
  face self player
  speaker "Socialite"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_FIGHT_AREA_MART_SCOPE_LENS goto Dilemma
  say "Tell me, what do you give your Pokémon to hold in battle? I simply cannot make up my mind."
  say "I bought far too many of these. Do take one off my hands."
  give "Scope Lens"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_FIGHT_AREA_MART_SCOPE_LENS
label Dilemma
  say "One item for each Pokémon, and so many to choose from! It is the same dilemma before every single battle."
  end
