# Route 126 (plan 21 · H8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's beats
# (data/maps/Route126/scripts.inc). Pablo, the triathlete, swaps numbers for the PokéNav once beaten.


script Pablo
  if not defeated self goto Fight
  if flag FLAG_PHONE_CONTACT_HOENN_PABLO goto Known
label Ask
  speaker "Pablo"
  say "You're tougher than a double race! Let's keep in touch and push each other on."
  ask "Register Pablo in the PokéNav's Match Call?"
  if no goto Refused
  addcontact hoenn_pablo
  say "Great! I'll call you between laps."
  end
label Refused
  say "Oh well. Back to my laps, then!"
  end
label Known
  trainerline after
  end
label Fight
  trainerline before
  battle self
  goto Ask
