# Birth Island's harbour (plan 21 · H12, part f). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/BirthIsland_Harbor/scripts.inc).
#
# The S.S. Tidal's sailor, who brought the player on the ferry desk's pass, waits on the quay to sail them back to
# Lilycove's harbour (Emerald's BirthIsland_Harbor_EventScript_Sailor). The ship itself is not drawn.


script Sailor
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  say "This is Birth Island. Odd place. The sky over it hums some nights."
  ask "Shall we sail back to Lilycove?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Aye. Hold on tight."
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed back to Lilycove City..."
  warp "LilycoveCity_Harbor" 8 11 down
  end
label Stay
  say "As you like. I'll be here when you're ready."
  end
