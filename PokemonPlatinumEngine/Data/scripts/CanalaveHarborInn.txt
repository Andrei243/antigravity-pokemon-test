# The Harbor Inn in Canalave City (plan 29 · LS11a), on the original's plan: the same room as Sailor Eldritch's house,
# as the original builds both from one model. docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_canalave_city_harbor_inn.s).
#
# The door is shut until the sailor's boy gives the Member Card (canalave_city's overlay opens it on
# FLAG_RECEIVED_CANALAVE_CITY_SAILOR_ELDRITCH_HOUSE_MEMBER_CARD). The innkeeper keeps one room for whoever carries the
# card: a night there is the dream that sets the player down on Newmoon Island's pier, with the island's sailor away
# until the island's wood has been come to (FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_SAILOR, cleared by newmoon_island.txt once
# FLAG_ENTERED_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST, ours, is set). Ours: the original's card came by an event of its own, and its
# innkeeper is hidden until it is held; here he stands at the door for everyone.


# Whoever a night here sent off is put back at the door
script OnEnter
  place innkeeper 4 7 down
  end


script Innkeeper
  face self player
  if item "Member Card" goto Member
  say "Welcome to the Harbor Inn. I'm afraid every room is taken tonight."
  say "We do keep one room aside, but only for members. Without a card, I can't let it."
  end
label Member
  say "Ah, a Member Card! Then the room by the window is yours, for as long as you like."
  ask "Will you stay the night?"
  if no goto Later
  scene "A night at the Harbor Inn"
  say "Right this way. Sleep well... and mind your dreams."
  fade out
  speaker none
  text "{player} lay down and drifted off to the sound of the sea..."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_NEWMOON_ISLAND_SAILOR
  clearflag FLAG_ENTERED_NEWMOON_ISLAND_FOREST
  warp "Sinnoh" 152 277 down
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "Whenever you're ready. The room will keep."
  end
