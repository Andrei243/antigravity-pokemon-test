# Hau'oli City, Melemele Island (plan 23 · Z5). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Alola's first chapter" and "The rest of Melemele"): no script of the original is
# read. VAR_ALOLA_STORY is alola_route_1.txt's count.
#
# The people: grunt and grunt_2 on the marina's pier (shown by FLAG_ALOLA_SKULL_MARINA for their scene), ilima beside
# them (FLAG_ALOLA_ILIMA_MARINA), kukui on the beach by the pier (shown by FLAG_ALOLA_KUKUI_MARINA from the grand trial
# until FLAG_ALOLA_SAILED_TO_AKALA), and the sailor at the pier's end, whose ferry crosses to Akala's Heahea City.


# Into the city for the first time with the Pokédex: the first chapter's road is walked
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY != 6 end
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 7
  objective alolaMarina
  end


# Onto the marina's pier for the first time: two of Team Skull, and Captain Ilima
script Marina
  scene "Team Skull at the marina"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SKULL_MARINA
  show grunt
  show grunt_2
  face player down
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Yo yo yo! Look who's wandered onto our pier. A little island challenger, huh?"
  say "Team Skull says this pier's ours today. Wanna argue? Then let's battle!"
  battle grunt as "alola_grunt_marina"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Tch! You got lucky. Come on, let's split. This place is boring anyway."
  hide grunt
  hide grunt_2
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_SKULL_MARINA
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ILIMA_MARINA
  show ilima
  face player left
  speaker "Ilima"
  say "Well handled! I was about to step in, but you didn't need me at all. I'm Ilima, a trial captain of this island."
  say "A trial-goer who battles like that... Let me see it for myself. Your Pokémon are tired, so allow me."
  heal
  battle ilima as "alola_ilima_1"
  speaker "Ilima"
  say "Splendid! You and your partner understand each other well."
  say "My trial waits in Verdant Cavern, up Route 2. But first you need the kahuna's blessing: the festival in Iki Town is tonight, and Hala will be expecting you."
  hide ilima
  clearflag FLAG_ALOLA_ILIMA_MARINA
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 8
  objective alolaFestival
  endscene
  end


# The grunts and the captain, should they be spoken to while they stand there
script Grunt
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "What're you looking at?"
  end

script Ilima
  speaker "Ilima"
  say "Verdant Cavern is north up Route 2. I'll be waiting."
  end


# After the grand trial: the professor's boat to Akala
script SailToAkala
  scene "The boat to Akala"
  face player left
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Woo! There's the island's newest grand trial winner! Hala told me all about it, cousin."
  say "Melemele's done, so Akala's next. My boat's tied up right here. Hop in and I'll take you across!"
  fade out
  text "The professor's boat skimmed out of the marina and across the bright sea to Akala Island..."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_SAILED_TO_AKALA
  hide kukui
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 14
  objective alolaPaniola
  warp "Akala" 125 328 up
  fade in
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Welcome to Akala! Heahea City's just up the beach. The nearest trial is north, past Route 4 and Paniola Town. Have fun out there!"
  endscene
  end

script Kukui
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "Whenever you're ready, step onto the pier and we'll set sail for Akala."
  end


# The ferry to Akala, once the professor has taken the player across the first time
script Ferry
  speaker "Sailor"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 14 goto Sail
  say "This ferry crosses to Akala Island. It's for trial-goers who've finished Melemele's grand trial, so come back once the kahuna's seen what you can do!"
  end
label Sail
  ask "All aboard for Akala Island! Shall we sail?"
  if no end
  fade out
  text "The ferry pulled out of Hau'oli Marina..."
  warp "Akala" 125 328 up
  fade in
  end
