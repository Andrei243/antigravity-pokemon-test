# Poco Path (plan 34 · PD1): the way north from Cabo Poco along the cliffs, the beach under them and the lighthouse on
# its knoll. VAR_PALDEA_STORY is cabo_poco.txt's count. The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats.


# Down the track onto the beach after the battle: the cry again, out of the cave in the cliff
script ARoar
  scene "A roar on the beach"
  text "The cry comes again, louder now, out of a cave in the foot of the cliff. Something in there sounds hurt."
  text "There is another sound under it: snarling, and claws on rock."
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_GROTTO_LEGENDS
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 3
  objective paldeaGrotto
  endscene
  end


# At the lighthouse's foot with the legendary along: Nemona's lesson in catching, and the way north
script NemonasLesson
  scene "Nemona at the lighthouse"
  faceto player nemona_lighthouse
  speaker "Nemona"
  say "There you are! Wait... what is that? It's huge! Where did you find it?"
  say "In the cave under the cliffs? And it just followed you out? I've never seen a Pokémon like it, not even in books."
  say "Well, it obviously likes you. Hang on to it! Now, catching. Weaken a wild Pokémon in battle, then throw one of these."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "The fields past Los Platos are full of Pokémon. Catch a few on the way and you'll have a real team by Mesagoza."
  say "I'll meet you at the city's south gate. Last one there battles the other! Well, we battle either way."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_LIGHTHOUSE
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_GATE
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 5
  objective paldeaMesagoza
  endscene
  end


# Arven at the lighthouse once term has begun (plan 34 · PD4): the Path of Legends begins. VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS is
# stony_cliff.txt's count
script ArvenLighthouse
  speaker "Arven"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto Busy
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 1 goto Begun
  scene "Arven at the lighthouse"
  faceto arven_lighthouse player
  say "You're the transfer student with the strange ride, aren't you? The director said you might come out here."
  say "I'm Arven. I'm looking for Herba Mystica: herbs so strong that one leaf makes any dish a cure."
  say "The trouble is, they only grow where the Titans live. Huge Pokémon, bigger than anything you've seen, and furious."
  say "One of them has dug in at the Stony Cliff: the gully north of the road from Mesagoza to Artazon. A Klawf."
  say "I can cook, but I can't beat a Titan alone. Help me, and whatever herbs we find, your ride gets its share."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEGENDS_BEGUN
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 1
  endscene
  end
label Busy
  say "Hm? I'm busy. Go on, the academy's waiting for you."
  end
label Begun
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 12 goto Done
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 11 goto Mabosstiff
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 9 goto FalseDragon
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 7 goto Quaking
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 5 goto LurkingSteel
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS >= 3 goto OpenSky
  say "The Stony Cliff's the gully north of the road east out of Mesagoza, before Artazon. Be careful of the rocks."
  end
label OpenSky
  say "Next Titan's a bird, they say, in a gully off the West Province's road, east of Asado Desert."
  end
label LurkingSteel
  say "The next one's east: a cutting off the road north from Artazon to Levincia. Something steel's under the sand."
  end
label Quaking
  say "The next Titan's in the dusty basin north of Cortondo. Mind your footing when it starts to run."
  end
label FalseDragon
  say "Last one's at the lake in the hollow east of the road to Levincia. A small dragon and a huge friend."
  end
label Done
  say "Mabosstiff's been bouncing off the walls since. Come back any time. I'll cook."
  end


# Back at the lighthouse after the fifth Titan (plan 34 · PD4, its second session): why Arven wanted the herbs, his
# Mabosstiff well again, and his battle. VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 11 to 12 (FLAG_PALDEA_LEGENDS_DONE)
label Mabosstiff
  scene "Arven's Mabosstiff"
  faceto arven_lighthouse player
  say "You came. I owe you the truth about the herbs. They were never really for cooking."
  say "My Mabosstiff got hurt protecting me, years back. Nothing the doctors did helped. I've been feeding it herbs since."
  say "I don't know if it was the fifth batch or all five together, but... watch."
  cry "Mabosstiff"
  text "A Mabosstiff bounds out from behind the lighthouse and slams into Arven's legs, its tail going like a drum."
  say "Ha! Easy, easy! See? It's back. It's really back. And it wants to battle, I can tell. So do I."
  say "Show me what you and your ride's friends can do. I won't hold back. My partner wouldn't let me."
  battle arven_lighthouse as "paldea_arven"
  say "You're strong. Stronger than a Titan. Thanks for walking the Path of Legends with me."
  say "Say thanks too, buddy. ...That's a lick. That counts."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEGENDS_DONE
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 12
  endscene
  end
