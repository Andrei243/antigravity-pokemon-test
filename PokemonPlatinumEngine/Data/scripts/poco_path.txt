# its knoll. VAR_PALDEA_STORY is cabo_poco.txt's count. The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats.


# Down the track onto the beach after the battle: the cry again, out of the cave in the cliff
script ARoar
  scene "A roar on the beach"
  text "The cry comes again, louder now, out of a cave in the foot of the cliff. Something in there sounds hurt."
  text "There is another sound under it: snarling, and claws on rock."
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_GROTTO_LEGENDS
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 3
  objective paldeaGrotto
  endscene
  end


# At the lighthouse's foot with the legendary along: Nemona's lesson in catching, and the way north
script NemonasLesson
  scene "Nemona at the lighthouse"
  faceto player nemona_lighthouse
  speaker "Nemona"
  say "There you are! Wait... what is that? It's huge! Where did you find it?"
  say "In the cave under the cliffs? And it just followed you out? I've never seen a Pokémon like it, not even in books."
  say "Well, it obviously likes you. Hang on to it! Now, catching. Weaken a wild Pokémon in battle, then throw one of these."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "The fields past Los Platos are full of Pokémon. Catch a few on the way and you'll have a real team by Mesagoza."
  say "I'll meet you at the city's south gate. Last one there battles the other! Well, we battle either way."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_LIGHTHOUSE
  clearflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_NEMONA_GATE
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 5
  objective paldeaMesagoza
  endscene
  end
