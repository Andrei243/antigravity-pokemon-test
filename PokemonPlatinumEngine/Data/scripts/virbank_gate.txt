# The gate between Route 20 and Virbank City (plan 22 · U5, part b). The lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/virbank_gate.s). Its guard stops whoever first walks east through it and gives
# them two Great Balls, saying a word about how many Pokémon they have (VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK_GATE 1 once given; Black
# 2's 0x4151).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


# The column across the gate's floor by the guard (our trigger, on 0)
script GateGift
  face 0 player
  speaker "Guard"
  say "Hello there! Oh, you're a Trainer, aren't you?"
  if var PARTY_COUNT >= 2 goto Several
  say "Just the one Pokémon with you? You two must be close. But you'll want more friends when the going gets tough!"
  goto Gift
label Several
  say "You've got a few Pokémon with you. The more friends you travel with, the more fun the road is!"
label Gift
  say "Here, these will help you make some new ones."
  give "Great Ball" 2
  say "You know what they say. Good company makes the road shorter!"
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK_GATE 1
  end


script Guard
  speaker "Guard"
  say "Good company makes the road shorter! Virbank City is just through here."
  end
