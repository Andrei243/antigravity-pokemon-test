# A house of Fortree City (plan 21 · H6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/FortreeCity_House1/scripts.inc).
#
# The boy trades his Plusle, Pluses, for a Volbeat, once (NpcTrades "pluses", which carries Emerald's contest
# condition and sheen for the Plusle). FLAG_FORTREE_NPC_TRADE_COMPLETED, Emerald's name: the trade is done.


script Trader
  speaker "Boy"
  if flag FLAG_FORTREE_NPC_TRADE_COMPLETED goto Done
  ask "I've got a Plusle, but what I really want is a Volbeat. Will you trade me one?"
  if no goto Waiting
  say "Which Pokémon will you trade?"
  choosepokemon
  if result == 255 goto Waiting
  trade pluses
  if result == 0 goto NotWanted
  setflag FLAG_FORTREE_NPC_TRADE_COMPLETED
  say "A Volbeat! Thanks! Look after Pluses for me, OK?"
  end
label NotWanted
  say "Huh? That's not a Volbeat. I'm only after a Volbeat."
  end
label Waiting
  say "No? If you ever catch a Volbeat, come back and see me."
  end
label Done
  say "Volbeat and I go out every night to watch the lights in the trees. How's my old Pluses doing?"
  end
