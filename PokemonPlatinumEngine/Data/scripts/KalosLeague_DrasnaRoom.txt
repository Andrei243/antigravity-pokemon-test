# Drasna's room of the Pokémon League (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team
# Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): one of the Elite Four's four doors, Dragon, its master a cheerful old woman. Her
# team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_drasna_1; beaten, FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DRASNA, and the
# Champion's door opens once all four are (common.KalosLeagueFour).


script Drasna
  face drasna player
  speaker "Drasna"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DRASNA goto Won
  if flag KalosHallOfFame goto Again
  scene "Drasna"
  say "Oh my, what a lovely young Trainer! I'm Drasna. Don't let my age fool you, dear."
  say "My dragons are as fierce as ever. Shall we?"
  battle self as "kalos_drasna_1"
  say "Oh, wonderful! It's been an age since anyone made my dragons work so hard."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DRASNA
  call KalosLeagueFour
  endscene
  end
label Again
  scene "DrasnaAgain"
  say "Back again, Champion? How lovely! My dragons have been looking forward to this all week, dear."
  battle self as "kalos_drasna_rematch"
  say "Oh, splendid! You never let up, do you? My dragons will be talking about this one for days."
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_DRASNA
  call KalosLeagueFour
  endscene
  end
label Won
  say "Off you go, dear. Give the others my love."
  end
