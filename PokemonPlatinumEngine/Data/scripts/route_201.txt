# Route 201 (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_route_201.s).
#
# The rival waits at the edge of the grass; the professor stops the two of them, asks whether they love Pokémon,
# and trusts them with the Pokémon in his briefcase, which his assistant brings back from the lake. The player
# chooses first; the rival takes the one strong against it and battles at once (the game's first battle, which
# may be lost), and the player is sent home. Back on the road, the rival is off to Lake Verity to look for its
# legendary Pokémon. VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE: 1 choosing, 2 home again, 3 the rival gone to the lake, 4 back.
#
# In the original the rival walks behind the player to the lake; here he runs on ahead and waits at the lakefront
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The first chapter").


# Back on the road while the briefcase waits (VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE 1). The professor, the assistant and the
# briefcase are there: a game saved before the scene cleared their flags (it only showed them) had lost them at the
# first door, and finds them again here. And the rival stands beside the briefcase, as at the end of the scene:
# whoever comes to a map again stands where the import puts them, and his own tile is the one in front of the
# briefcase, which his back hid from the player.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE != 1 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_PROF_ROWAN
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_COUNTERPART
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_BRIEFCASE
  place rival 113 855 left
  end


# The rival's trigger at the top of the road out of Twinleaf Town (the original's trigger 2).
script RowanAppears
  face rival player
  music "common/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "There you are, {player}! Way too slow! That's a ten-million fine!"
  say "Listen. Professor Rowan's back, right? So he's got to have loads of Pokémon. We ask him, he gives us one each. Easy!"
  say "But the tall grass is in the way... So here's the plan: we run through it so fast the wild Pokémon can't catch us!"
  say "Ready? On three. One... two..."
  speaker "???"
  say "Wait! Stay right where you are!"
  music "common/rowan"
  # Each of the three is cleared off its flag as well as shown, as the original does (ClearFlag, then AddObject):
  # shown alone, they were gone again the next time the player came out of a door, the briefcase with them
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_PROF_ROWAN
  show prof_rowan
  walk prof_rowan right 4 down 1
  face rival prof_rowan
  face player prof_rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "What do you think you are doing? Walking into tall grass without a Pokémon of your own is how people get hurt!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "......Huh? Hey! You're Professor Rowan! From the TV!"
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  walk prof_rowan left 2
  say "Hmm... Should I...? No. But then again..."
  walk prof_rowan right 2
  face prof_rowan player
label Ask
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  ask "Tell me truthfully. Do you love Pokémon?"
  if yes goto Loves
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey! What kind of answer is that? Tell him the truth!"
  goto Ask
label Loves
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "See? We both love Pokémon! And that's never, ever going to change. Right, {player}?"
  ask "Right?"
  if yes goto Agreed
  say "Ha ha, very funny. That joke's getting old already! Let's try that again."
  goto Ask
label Agreed
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Hmm... Two children charging into tall grass. What would they do with Pokémon of their own, I wonder?"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Then give one to my friend, at least! Running into the grass was my idea, not theirs."
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "...You would speak up for your friend. Very well. I will trust you both with a Pokémon."
  say "But promise me you will never be so reckless again. Now, where has my briefcase got to...?"
  music "common/assistant"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_COUNTERPART
  show counterpart
  walk counterpart right 3 fast
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Professor! You left your briefcase by the lake! ...Wait. Are you giving those Pokémon to these two?"
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "I am. Today is the day these two set out."
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_BRIEFCASE
  show briefcase
  say "{player}, the Pokémon are in that briefcase. Open it and choose the one you want."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Whoa, for real? You go first, {player}! I'll take whichever's left. Well, the best one that's left!"
  # He steps aside: from where he stood his back hid the briefcase, the tile behind him, from the player
  walk rival right 1
  face rival left
  music area
  setvar VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE 1
  end


# Walking off while the briefcase waits (the original's trigger 4, twice, toward the grass; and ours on the other
# ways out, overlays/route_201.json: the grass's edge north of it, the road to the lake and the road home). Nobody
# leaves here without a Pokémon.
script ChooseFirst
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey, where are you going? Open the briefcase and pick your Pokémon first!"
  walk player left 1
  end

script ChooseFirstWest
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Hey, the lake can wait! Open the briefcase and pick your Pokémon first!"
  walk player right 1
  end

script ChooseFirstSouth
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Whoa, you're not going home now, are you? Pick your Pokémon from the briefcase first!"
  walk player up 1
  end


script Briefcase
  fade out
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_BRIEFCASE
  hide self
  fade in
  starter
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received the {starter}!"
  face player prof_rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "And {rival} has chosen {rivalstarter}. Hm. You both chose well. Like you, those Pokémon have never seen the world."
  say "If anything troubles you, come and see me at my lab in Sandgem Town."
  walk prof_rowan right 9
  hide prof_rowan
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_PROF_ROWAN
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "Professor, wait for me! ...Sorry, coming through!"
  walk counterpart right 9 fast
  hide counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_COUNTERPART
  face rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Professor Rowan's actually kind of nice, huh? He looks so scary on TV."
  say "Hey, {player}! Let's see what our Pokémon can do!"
label Battle
  ask "You're up for a battle, right?"
  if yes goto Fight
  say "Come on, don't be like that! Just one battle!"
  goto Battle
label Fight
  if starter "Turtwig" goto VsChimchar
  if starter "Chimchar" goto VsPiplup
  battle rival as "rival_route_201_piplup" first canlose
  goto After
label VsChimchar
  battle rival as "rival_route_201_turtwig" first canlose
  goto After
label VsPiplup
  battle rival as "rival_route_201_chimchar" first canlose
label After
  speaker "{rival}"
  if lost goto Lost
  say "Whew... That wore me out! I'm going home to rest. My Pokémon too!"
  goto Home
label Lost
  say "Whoa, I won? I actually won! Battling with Pokémon is the best!"
label Home
  setvar VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE 2
  setvar VAR_PLAYER_HOUSE_STATE 3
  fade out
  heal
  warp "PlayerHouse" 2 6 up
  end


# Back on the road (the original's trigger 16): the rival is off to the lake.
script OffToTheLake
  face rival player
  music "common/rival"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Took you long enough! I want to go and thank Professor Rowan properly."
  say "But first! You know the lake past the western end of this road? They say a legendary Pokémon lives in it!"
  say "If we caught it, the professor would be so impressed! Race you there!"
  walk rival left 4 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_201_RIVAL
  setflag FLAG_RIVAL_WAITS_AT_LAKEFRONT
  music area
  setvar VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE 3
  end


# While the rival waits at the lake, the road home and the road to Sandgem Town turn the player back (the
# original's triggers 3 and 14, where he pulls the player back himself).
script NotHome
  text "{rival} ran off toward the lake at the western end of this road. Better catch up with him!"
  walk player up 1
  end

script NotSandgem
  text "{rival} ran off toward the lake at the western end of this road. Better catch up with him!"
  walk player left 1
  end


script Rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  if var VAR_FOLLOWER_RIVAL_STATE == 1 goto Choose
  say "My Pokémon and I are going to be the strongest ever. Just watch!"
  end
label Choose
  say "Go on, you choose first! Then I'll take the best one that's left!"
  end


script Rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "The Pokémon are in the briefcase. Go on, choose one."
  end


# The Mart's worker hands out a sample, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_201_POTION).
script MartWorker
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_201_POTION goto Done
  say "I work at the Poké Mart in Sandgem Town. Here's a free sample to try out!"
  give "Potion"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_201_POTION
label Done
  sayown
  end
