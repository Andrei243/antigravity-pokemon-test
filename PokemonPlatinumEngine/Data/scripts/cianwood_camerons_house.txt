# Cianwood City's photo studio (plan 20, part q). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T24R0601).
#
# Cameron's father keeps the player's photo album (Models/PhotoAlbum.cs; HeartGold's album is looked at here too). The
# first word with him sends Cameron out to his places (FLAG_JOHTO_MET_CAMERONS_FATHER, ours: every Cameron of an overlay
# is shown by it); after that he offers the album.


script Father
  face t24r0601_fishing_2 player
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_MET_CAMERONS_FATHER goto Album
  say "Welcome to my studio! My son Cameron takes photos of Trainers all over Johto, and Kanto too."
  say "I'll tell him to look out for you. Every photograph he takes of you comes here, and I keep them in an album."
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_MET_CAMERONS_FATHER
  end
label Album
  photo count
  if result == 0 goto Empty
  ask "Would you like to look through your album?"
  if no goto Later
  photo album
  end
label Empty
  say "Your album's still empty. Find my son Cameron out on his travels, and he'll take your picture."
  end
label Later
  say "Any time you like. The album isn't going anywhere."
  end
