# Route 2's motel (plan 23, the wave after the loose ends: a door Z5, Z6 or Z7 left shut, or a room of the islands' last
# places). The lines are our own (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The rest of the islands: Hapu, the guardians' ruins, the
# last routes and the shut doors").


script Clerk
  speaker "Motel Clerk"
  ask "A room for a nap is 500 dollars. Your Pokémon get a bed too. Would you like to rest?"
  if no goto Later
  takemoney 500
  if result == 0 goto Poor
  fade out
  heal
  text "{player} and the Pokémon rested for a while."
  fade in
  say "All rested? Come back any time."
  end
label Poor
  say "Oh dear. You're a little short. The Pokémon Center down the road is free, you know."
  end
label Later
  say "Suit yourself. The beds will be here."
  end
