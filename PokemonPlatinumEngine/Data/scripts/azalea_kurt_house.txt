# Kurt's house in Azalea Town (plan 20 · J7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T23R0501).
#
# Kurt hears of Team Rocket in the well and runs there himself (FLAG_UNK_19E takes him from the house, FLAG_UNK_19F
# the guard from the well's mouth, FLAG_UNK_077 says he went). Once the well is cleared its scene brings the player
# here (VAR_UNK_4080 2), where Kurt thanks them with a Lure Ball (FLAG_UNK_07C). Making balls of Apricorns is plan 20
# · J6's.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_UNK_4080 != 2 end
  scene "Kurt's thanks"
  face t23r0501_gantetsu player
  speaker "Kurt"
  say "Ah, there you are! The Slowpoke are safe and Team Rocket are gone, all thanks to you."
  say "I make Poké Balls, you know, from Apricorns. Here, take this one. It's one of my own."
  give "Lure Ball"
  setflag FLAG_UNK_07C
  say "A Lure Ball is just the thing for a Pokémon you've hooked with a rod. Bring me Apricorns and I'll make you more."
  setvar VAR_UNK_4080 3
  endscene
  end


script Kurt
  face t23r0501_gantetsu player
  speaker "Kurt"
  if flag FLAG_BEAT_AZALEA_ROCKETS goto Thanked
  scene "Kurt runs to the well"
  say "Hm? A trainer? I make Poké Balls, the old way, from Apricorns. But I've no heart for it today."
  say "Team Rocket are down in the Slowpoke Well, cutting off the Slowpoke's tails to sell! I won't stand for it."
  say "I'll give those thugs a piece of my mind myself. Stand aside!"
  walk t23r0501_gantetsu down 3 fast
  hide t23r0501_gantetsu
  setflag FLAG_UNK_077
  setflag FLAG_UNK_19E
  setflag FLAG_UNK_19F
  # Whoever had not yet seen the shove by the well finds the street clear of it too
  if var VAR_UNK_4080 == 0 setvar VAR_UNK_4080 1
  setflag FLAG_AZALEA_ROCKET_HARASSING_CIVILIAN
  setflag FLAG_AZALEA_HARASSED_CIVILIAN
  endscene
  end
label Thanked
  say "Bring me Apricorns and I'll turn them into Poké Balls. Every colour makes a ball of its own."
  end


# T23R0501_002: the granddaughter, before Kurt goes, while he is gone and once he is back
script Granddaughter
  if flag FLAG_BEAT_AZALEA_ROCKETS goto Back
  if flag FLAG_UNK_077 goto Gone
  say "Grandpa's been grumpy all morning. He keeps looking out of the window at the well."
  end
label Gone
  say "Grandpa ran off to the well. He said he'd sort out Team Rocket on his own. I hope he's all right..."
  end
label Back
  say "The Slowpoke are home again! Grandpa says he'll get back to making Poké Balls now."
  end
