# Undella Town (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/undella_town.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_UNDELLA is the town's count (Black 2's 0x40cd): 0 before Hugh's battle at the east end of the street, 1 after it.
#
# As the player walks up the street towards the Pokémon Center (our trigger across it, 751 to 755, 299, at 0) Hugh (hugh_undella,
# hidden until then) runs up from the beach wanting a battle: Black 2's TRAINER_RIVAL_10, _11 and _12 are his team by the player's
# starter (the one strong against it), and he is off again to look for his sister's Purrloin. Black 2's later scenes here
# (Hugh back from the Abyssal Ruins at 6, Zinzolin reading the ruins' script at 7) are plan 22 · U10's and stay hidden.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  end


script HughAtTheEast
  scene "Hugh at Undella Town"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_UNDELLA
  place hugh_undella 753 304 up
  show hugh_undella
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Wait up, {player}!"
  walk hugh_undella up 4
  face hugh_undella player
  say "Let's see how well we've raised our Pokémon!"
  if starter "Snivy" goto AgainstSnivy
  if starter "Tepig" goto AgainstTepig
  battle hugh_undella as "unova_rival_12"
  goto Fought
label AgainstSnivy
  battle hugh_undella as "unova_rival_10"
  goto Fought
label AgainstTepig
  battle hugh_undella as "unova_rival_11"
label Fought
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Great! If we're this strong, Team Plasma will run screaming the moment they see us!"
  say "I won't let them get away this time, though. Her Purrloin... I'll get it back, for sure!"
  say "So, {player}, keep helping me out! And keep working on the Pokédex, too! You're the one who was officially asked to complete it, after all!"
  walk hugh_undella down 4 fast
  hide hugh_undella
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_UNDELLA
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_UNDELLA 1
  endscene
  end
