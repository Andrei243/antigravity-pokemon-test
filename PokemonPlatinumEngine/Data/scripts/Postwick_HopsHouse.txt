# Hop's house in Postwick (plan 33 · GL1). The lines are our own; VAR_GALAR_STORY is postwick.txt's count.


script HopsMum
  speaker "Hop's Mum"
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 10 goto Endorsed
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 3 goto Partner
  say "Hop's been up since dawn waiting for Leon's train. I don't think he slept a wink."
  end
label Partner
  say "So you've a partner of your own now! Hop won't stop talking about your battle."
  end
label Endorsed
  say "Both of you endorsed by the Champion himself! Look after Hop out there for me, won't you?"
  end
