# Reversal Mountain's lair (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's

# errand's count (reversal_mountain_4.txt has it whole): 1 as the player comes up to the lair with Bianca along, 2 once she has
# had her say.

# Black 2's Heatran waits in this room for the Magma Stone and the post-game (plan 22 · U10). Its person (0) stays hidden
# (FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HEATRAN) until then.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpB
  call UnovaBiancaWaits
  end


# The passage up to the lair (our trigger across it, 9 to 11, 18, at 1)
script BiancaInTheLair
  if var VAR_UNOVA_REVERSAL != 1 end
  scene "Bianca in the lair"
  face bianca_along player
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "What is this place? It feels very strange."
  say "Could this be where Reversal Mountain started from? The lair of the Pokémon Heatran?"
  say "They say Heatran is a Pokémon with magma-like blood flowing through it! Just imagine!"
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_REVERSAL_LAIR 2
  endscene
  end
