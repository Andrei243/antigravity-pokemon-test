# The psychic asks what kind of Trainer the player would be taken for. In the original the answer is how others see
# the player in the Union Room; with the wireless link down (decision 06 · 7) he asks and changes nothing (plan 13).
# The gentleman wonders about Team Galactic, and wonders differently once the game is completed.


# OreburghCityPokecenter1F_Psychic
script Psychic
  say "Hmmm...! Tell me, what kind of Trainer do you like best of all?"
  say "No, no, don't answer straight away. It is the sort of thing a person ought to think over."
  say "The way you see yourself is the way others will come to see you. Tell me when you have made up your mind!"
  end


# OreburghCityPokecenter1F_Gentleman
script Gentleman
  if flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Afterwards
  say "Hmmm! Team Galactic... What are they? Who are they?"
  say "They promise the world a marvellous new kind of energy. And yet people whisper that they take Pokémon away by force."
  say "A mystery! A mystery, I tell you! Is nobody looking into them?"
  end
label Afterwards
  say "Hmmm! Team Galactic... What were they, in the end?"
  say "I hear they have sworn off taking other people's Pokémon. They still work on that energy of theirs, but honestly these days."
  end
