# A house in Mossdeep City (plan 21 · H11, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MossdeepCity_House1/scripts.inc): a black belt who can tell at a glance the colour of
# Pokéblock the first Pokémon of the team likes best (`pokeblock taste`: the one flavour its nature likes, or none).


script BlackBelt
  face self player
  speaker "Black Belt"
  say "Hmm! Your {member:0}..."
  pokeblock taste
  if result == 0 goto Neutral
  say "It likes {taste}s, doesn't it? No, I'm positive of it! It definitely likes {taste}s!"
  end
label Neutral
  say "It doesn't appear to like or dislike any Pokéblocks."
  end
