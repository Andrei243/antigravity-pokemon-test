  battle self as "johto_elite_four_karen_karen"

  say "Well, aren't you good. I like that in a trainer."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_KARENS_ROOM_EXIT

  say "People love to sort Pokémon into the strong and the weak. I've never had much patience for that. The best trainers win with the ones they love."
  say "You fight like someone who knows it. Go on. The Champion is waiting."
  endscene
  end
