# NimbasaCity_FootballField: Nimbasa City's football field (plan 42 · PS5; rulings, "Sports days"). Black 2's place, our words.
# Its three athletes battle once a day each (FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_*), with the team the story calls for: the first
# under five of Unova's Badges, the second from five, the third with all eight, the fourth after the Hall of Fame
# (SportsDays.TierOf; SportsDayTests hold these scripts to SportsDays).

script Athlete1
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_FOOTBALL_1 goto Done1
  say "I've been working on my footwork. Let's see if it works in battle!"
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Fourth1
  if badges >= 8 goto Third1
  if badges >= 5 goto Second1
  battle self as "unova_striker_tony"
  goto Won1
label Second1
  battle self as "unova_striker_tony_2"
  goto Won1
label Third1
  battle self as "unova_striker_tony_3"
  goto Won1
label Fourth1
  battle self as "unova_striker_tony_4"
label Won1
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_FOOTBALL_1
label Done1
  say "You got that one past the keeper. Well played."
  end
