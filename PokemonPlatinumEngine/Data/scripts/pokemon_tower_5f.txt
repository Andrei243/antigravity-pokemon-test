# The Pokémon Tower's fifth floor (plan 19 · K5, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/PokemonTower_5F/scripts.inc).
#
# A ring of tiles in the floor's middle is kept clean of evil spirits: stepping in heals the team (the area's triggers
# on VAR_TEMP_1 0), and stepping out of it lets the next step in heal again (those on VAR_TEMP_1 1). FireRed's
# VAR_TEMP_1 is forgotten on every arrival; here the floor's own arrival forgets it.


script OnEnter
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 0
  end


script PurifiedZone
  fade out 0.3
  heal
  fade in
  speaker none
  text "This ground is purified, and no evil spirit can come near. Your Pokémon are fully healed."
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 1
  end


script ExitPurifiedZone
  setvar VAR_TEMP_1 0
  end
