# Snowbelle City's Gym (plan 23 · Z4). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The mountain, Team Flare and the League, drawn by hand"): Wulfric, a big laughing man of the
# north, battles for the Iceberg Badge, Kalos's eighth (`givebadge iceberg`), once the Psychic Badge is won. VAR_KALOS_STORY
# is vaniville_town.txt's count: 29, Team Flare stopped, becomes 30 here, and the objective points on through the woods
# and the village to Victory Road. His team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_wulfric_1.


script Wulfric
  face wulfric player
  speaker "Wulfric"
  if badge iceberg goto Won
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 29 goto Later
  scene "Wulfric"
  say "Ha! Another one comes in out of the cold! I'm Wulfric, and this Gym is the coldest place in Kalos. Ha ha!"
  say "Ice is patient. It waits, it grows, and one day it moves mountains. Let's see if you can move it!"
  battle self as "kalos_wulfric_1"
  say "Ha ha ha! You melted the lot of us! That's the best fun I've had all winter."
  say "The Iceberg Badge. Eight! Now you're ready for the road to the League."
  givebadge iceberg
  text "{player} received the Iceberg Badge from Wulfric!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_SNOWBELLE_GYM
  defeat "kalos_skier_agathe"
  defeat "kalos_skier_basile"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY != 29 goto Tm
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 30
  objective kalosLeague
label Tm
  say "Take this too. A storm of hail and wind that freezes everything in its way!"
  give "TM13"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_WULFRIC
  say "North of town, through the woods and past the village, Victory Road waits. Go on, then!"
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "Ha! You've come a long way, but not far enough. Kalos has bigger trouble than me just now: go and stop it first."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_WULFRIC goto Tm
  say "Ha ha! Show the League what a little cold can do!"
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge iceberg goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Wulfric's Ice types crack under fire, fists, rock and steel. Choose well!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Wulfric! I could hear him laughing from the door."
  end
