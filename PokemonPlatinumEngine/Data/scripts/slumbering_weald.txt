# The Slumbering Weald's first visit (plan 33 · GL1): Hop's Wooloo found deep in the fog, and the shadow that stands
# in it. The lines are our own; VAR_GALAR_STORY is postwick.txt's count. The shadow is the version's legendary
# (VAR_GALAR_VERSION: Zacian's in Sword, Zamazenta's in Shield), met in a staged battle (plan 33 · GL6; docs/mechanics/
# rulings.md, "Staged battles"): its level hidden, nothing the team does touches it, it does nothing, and after three
# turns it is gone. Its true battle is GL7's. And the second visit (GL6): the altar on the Darkest Day.


script Shadow
  scene "The shadow in the fog"
  faceto player hop_weald
  speaker "Hop"
  say "There! I can hear my Wooloo bleating just ahead. Wait... where did all this fog come from?"
  text "The fog rolls in thick and cold until the trees are only shapes. Something huge stands in it, watching."
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  seen "Zacian"
  text "Its eyes burn through the white like the edge of a sword."
  goto Fog
label Shield
  seen "Zamazenta"
  text "Its eyes burn through the white over a mane like a shield."
label Fog
  speaker "Hop"
  say "It's coming this way! Everyone, out, now! Go!"
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto ShieldBattle
  wildbattle "Zacian" 70 hidelevel untouchable passive endafter 3
  goto Gone
label ShieldBattle
  wildbattle "Zamazenta" 70 hidelevel untouchable passive endafter 3
label Gone
  speaker "Hop"
  say "Our Pokémon threw everything at it, and nothing they did even touched it..."
  text "The fog closes over everything."
  fade out
  speaker "Leon"
  say "{player}! Hop! There you are. What were you two thinking, going into the Weald on your own?"
  say "Both of you, home. Now. And the Wooloo too."
  heal
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_HIDE_HOP_WEALD
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 5
  objective galarMum
  warp "Postwick_PlayersHouse_2F" 4 6
  fade in
  endscene
  end


# The Darkest Day (plan 33 · GL6): the altar deep in the fog, the rusted sword and shield, and the two of the legend
# seen in the white; back to Wyndon by Hop's word. VAR_GALAR_STORY 40 to 41
script Altar
  scene "The altar in the Weald"
  faceto player hop_altar
  speaker "Hop"
  say "This is it, the old altar. The stories say the heroes laid their sword and shield down here when the Darkest Day was over."
  text "On the stone lie a sword and a shield, both eaten through with rust."
  say "They're just... rusty. That's all that's left of them? Then how are we meant to stop what's happening over Wyndon?"
  text "The fog thickens. Two shapes stand in it, one with the eyes of a sword and one with a mane like a shield."
  seen "Zacian"
  seen "Zamazenta"
  say "It's them! The two from the fog! They're... they're looking at the sky. Over Wyndon."
  say "They know. They're going to help, I'm sure of it! Come on, {player}, back to Wyndon. The tower!"
  text "When the fog thins, the two are gone, and so are the rusted sword and shield."
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_HOP_ALTAR
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 41
  objective galarRoseTower
  warpto "Galar" rose_tower_back
  endscene
  end
