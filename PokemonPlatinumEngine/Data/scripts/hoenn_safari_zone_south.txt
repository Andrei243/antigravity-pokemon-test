# The Safari Zone's south part, inside its door (plan 21 · H6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/SafariZone_South/scripts.inc, data/scripts/safari_zone.inc).
#
# The zone's six parts are one map (HoennSafariZone), each its own area. The attendant stands before the door back to
# the gate: the first time in a game he wishes the player luck (FLAG_GOOD_LUCK_SAFARI_ZONE, cleared at the counter),
# then asks whether to leave early, which ends the game and takes the player back through the door to be walked out
# (Emerald's ExitEarly and SafariZone_EventScript_Exit, VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 1). The counter brings a player in a
# step south of him (VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 2, which the arrival clears, as Emerald's EnterSafariZone does once its
# attendant has stepped back before the door). The two builders east of the door keep the zone's expansion shut until
# Hoenn's Hall of Fame (plan 21 · H9) sets their flag.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE == 2 setvar VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 0
  end


script ExitAttendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  if not safari goto Out
  if not flag FLAG_GOOD_LUCK_SAFARI_ZONE goto GoodLuck
  ask "You still have time left. Would you like to leave the Safari Zone now?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Very well. I'll take back the Safari Balls you have left. Thank you for playing, and do come again!"
  safari end
  setvar VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 1
  warp "Route121_SafariZoneEntrance" 2 4 right
  end
label Stay
  say "Then enjoy the rest of your time in the wild!"
  end
label GoodLuck
  setflag FLAG_GOOD_LUCK_SAFARI_ZONE
  say "Good luck out there! If there's anything you need, just come and ask me."
  end
label Out
  say "Your game is over, but the way out is right behind me. This way, please!"
  setvar VAR_SAFARI_ZONE_STATE 1
  warp "Route121_SafariZoneEntrance" 2 4 right
  end
