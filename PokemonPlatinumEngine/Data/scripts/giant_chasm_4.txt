# The Giant Chasm's crater as the story finds it, with the Plasma Frigate at rest in its frozen middle (plan 22 · U9, part a).
# docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's
# res/field/scripts/giant_chasm_4.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Rood (2) and two of the old Team Plasma's sages (0, 1) stand against five grunts of the new (4, 5, 7, 8, 12) on the grass north
# of the cave's mouth. As the player comes up from the cave (Black 2's trigger, 25 to 27, 72; VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 1) Hugh (6) is
# already there: the grunts won't listen to Rood, Hugh takes one of them on and the player another (Black 2's
# TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_GRUNT_46), Rood's sages see to the team, and Rood sends both on to the ship's ramp (2). The ramp hall is
# plasma_frigate_6.txt; the passage north to the cavern where Ghetsis waits is giant_chasm_5, the cavern giant_chasm_6.txt.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpE
  end


script RoodAndTheGrunts
  scene "Rood's stand in the crater"
  speaker "Rood"
  say "Listen to me! Ghetsis never cared for Pokémon. Freeing them was only a tale to win Unova for himself!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Save your speeches, old man. You walked out on Team Plasma. Traitors don't get a say!"
  face 6 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Well, talking didn't work. {player}, you're just in time!"
  face 6 up
  say "Out of the way! I'm here for a Pokémon you people stole, and I'm not leaving without it!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "A couple of kids? Don't make us laugh. Nobody gets near the Plasma Frigate!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Fine! I'll take this one. {player}, you take the other!"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "We'll flatten you both, and the traitors with you!"
  battle 4 as "unova_team_plasma_grunt_46"
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Ugh... Retreat! Back to the ship, everyone!"
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CRATER_GRUNTS
  hide 4
  hide 5
  hide 7
  hide 8
  hide 12
  fade in
  face 2 player
  speaker "Rood"
  say "Are your Pokémon hurt? Here, let my friends see to them."
  heal
  speaker none
  text "Rood's sages tended the team. Everyone is full of energy again!"
  speaker "Rood"
  say "We believed, once, that we fought for a world kinder to Pokémon. We were blind to what Ghetsis truly wanted."
  say "Now we must make amends. We will hold this ground. You two, go aboard by the ramp to the north-east and stop him!"
  say "And you, young man. The Purrloin you seek is most likely held by the Shadow Triad, Ghetsis's own shadows."
  face 6 2
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Then I'll get it back from them myself. If it helps you old Plasma folks make up for things, all the better!"
  face 6 player
  say "{player}, I'll go first. Don't be long!"
  fade out 0.3
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_CRATER
  hide 6
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GHETSIS_RAMP
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_RAMP
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 2
  endscene
  fade in
  end

  speaker "Rood"

  end

  end
