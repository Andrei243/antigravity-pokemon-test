# The player's room upstairs (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/LittlerootTown_BrendansHouse_2F/scripts.inc and data/scripts/players_house.inc).
#
# The wall clock: set once Mom has asked (VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 4), after which she comes up to see it. This game's
# clock is the computer's own (GameClock), so setting it is a line and no screen (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hoenn's
# first chapter").


script WallClock
  if var VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE != 4 goto Ticking
  scene "The wall clock"
  text "It's the clock Dad sent from Petalburg. Its hands are still at twelve."
  text "{player} set the clock to the right time."
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_PLAYERS_BEDROOM_MOM
  show players_house_2f_mom
  walk players_house_2f_mom down 1
  face players_house_2f_mom player
  speaker "Mom"
  say "{player}, how's your room? Oh, you've set the clock! It looks lovely up there."
  say "Come back downstairs for a minute, would you? I've got something to tell you."
  walk players_house_2f_mom up 1
  hide players_house_2f_mom
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_PLAYERS_BEDROOM_MOM
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_INTRO_STATE 6
  endscene
  end
label Ticking
  text "The clock ticks away quietly. It keeps good time."
  end
