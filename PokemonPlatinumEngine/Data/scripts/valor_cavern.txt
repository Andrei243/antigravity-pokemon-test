# Valor Cavern (plan 02 · S10). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats
# (scripts_valor_cavern.s).
#
# The cave on the island in the middle of Lake Valor, reached over the lake's dry bed once Team Galactic's bomb has
# emptied it. Commander Saturn stands alone where the lake's Pokémon slept: it has been taken already. Spoken to, he
# is reporting to himself that the mission goes to plan, notices the player and battles; beaten, he says Commander
# Mars is at Lake Verity and leaves, and Team Galactic's grunts go from the lake with him
# (FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VALOR_GALACTIC, which the original's RemoveObject sets as it takes him away;
# FLAG_DEFEATED_COMMANDER_SATURN_VALOR_CAVERN, which turns Verity Lakefront's way in to the lake Team Galactic holds).
#
# Azelf itself is back in the cavern after the Distortion World (plan 02 · S13).


script Saturn
  speaker "Commander Saturn"
  say "...The mission is proceeding without a hitch. The boss will be pleased with this."
  face self player
  emote self exclaim
  wait 0.5
  say "Hm? A visitor. You must be the child our grunts have been complaining about."
  say "I am Saturn, a Commander of Team Galactic. You have come a long way to find an empty cave. The least I can do is see what all the fuss was about."
  battle saturn as "commander_saturn_valor_cavern"
  speaker "Commander Saturn"
  say "...So that is why they complain. You are strong. And you are far too late."
  say "The Pokémon of this lake is already ours. By now Mars will have what she went to Lake Verity for, as well."
  say "Run there if you like. It will change nothing."
  fade out
  hide saturn
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_LAKE_VALOR_GALACTIC
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_COMMANDER_SATURN_VALOR_CAVERN
  setflag FLAG_ALT_MUSIC_LAKE_VALOR
  fade in
  end
