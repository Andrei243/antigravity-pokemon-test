# The house on Route 16 (plan 19 · K5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/Route16_House/scripts.inc).
#
# The woman who has found her way here gives HM02, Fly (FLAG_GOT_HM02), which the Thunder Badge lets a Pokémon use
# outside battle: Kanto's towns once come to are on its map.


script Woman
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM02 goto Given
  say "Oh! You found my little hideaway. Not many people make it this far."
  say "My Fearow and I fly everywhere together. You ought to try it. Here, take this!"
  give "HM02"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_HM02
  say "HM02 is Fly. A Pokémon that knows it can carry you to any town you've been to before."
  end
label Given
  say "Flying over Kanto is wonderful. Every town looks so small from up there!"
  end
