# The Altar of the Sunne and Moone, Poni Island (plan 23 · Z7). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ula'ula, Poni, Aether Paradise and the League"): Lillie carries her
# Cosmog up to the dais, where it calls the legendary Pokémon of the sun. Ours is Solgaleo (Sun's): battled at 55 and
# gone once it is caught or beaten (FLAG_ALOLA_ALTAR_DONE). VAR_ALOLA_STORY 51 is the way in, 52 the Altar passed.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY != 51 end
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_ALTAR_DONE end
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_AT_ALTAR
  end


script LillieAtAltar
  face lillie player
  speaker "Lillie"
  say "{player}... I'm so glad you're here. Nebby changed, on the way. Look."
  say "It wants to go home, and I think the dais is where home is. The sun is out, and the dais lights when the sun is."
  say "I'm afraid. But I'm not going to hide in my bag any more. Please, stay with me."
  text "Lillie held her bag up to the dais, and the light in it rose into the cavern like a second sun!"
  end


script Legend
  face self player
  speaker none
  scene "The legendary of the sun"
  # Its beat (plan 29 · LS, wave 91): the bars, the camera on the lion and the lens closing, the dais shaking under it
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera follow self 0.8
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  text "The light on the dais gathered into a great white shape: a lion of the sun, its mane ablaze!"
  shake self 6
  cry "Solgaleo"
  camera release 0.4
  wildbattle "Solgaleo" 55 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ALTAR_DONE
  hide self
  hide lillie
  speaker "Lillie"
  say "Nebby... you were Solgaleo all along. You're free to go home, now, if that's what you want."
  say "...{player}, thank you. For everything. Whatever happens, I'm going to be braver."
  speaker "Prof. Kukui"
  say "{player}! Hello, hello! Lillie told me what happened. The Pokémon League is finally open on Mount Lanakila, cousin."
  say "Hau's already there, I bet. Take the Route 13 road east from Tapu Village. And try not to enjoy yourself too much."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_LANAKILA_OPEN
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 52
  objective alolaLanakila
  endscene
  end
