# A house in Mauville City (plan 21 · H3, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/MauvilleCity_House1/scripts.inc).
#
# The man who loves smashing rocks gives HM06, Rock Smash (FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_ROCK_SMASH), which the Dynamo Badge lets be
# used outside battle.


script RockSmashMan
  face self player
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_ROCK_SMASH goto Given
  say "Rocks! Cracked rocks! Nothing beats the feeling of smashing one to bits!"
  say "You look like you'd enjoy it too. Here, take this. It'll let your Pokémon smash rocks to rubble."
  give "HM06"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_HM_ROCK_SMASH
  say "That's Rock Smash. Use it on a rock with cracks in it, and boom! Once you've got Mauville's Badge, that is."
  end
label Given
  say "Have you found a cracked rock yet? Smash it! Wild Pokémon sometimes hide in the rubble, too."
  end
