# The Plasma Frigate's stern room (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black
# 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/plasma_frigate_4.s, Colress's room, read for the order alone).
#
# Colress (colress_stern) is Team Plasma's scientist, not its servant: he battles the player to see the strength they draw out
# of their Pokémon (Black 2's TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_COLRESS), asks how they think that strength is found, and switches off the
# second of the barrier's switches himself (FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SWITCH_B, common.UnovaFrigateBarriers). Black 2's Master Ball
# from him, after Team Plasma is gone, is plan 22 · U10's.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  end


script Colress
  face colress_stern player
  speaker "Colress"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SWITCH_B goto After
  scene "Colress aboard the Plasma Frigate"
  music "unova/colress"
  say "Welcome! I was asked by an acquaintance to help with his research. What I want is to draw out the whole of a Pokémon's potential."
  say "If that strength comes from the bond between Pokémon and Trainer, so be it. If it takes something cold and mechanical, so be that too."
  say "I've travelled all over Unova battling Trainers to test the one idea against the other. You're the best test yet. Shall we?"
  battle colress_stern as "unova_team_plasma_colress"
  say "So strong! You really are a remarkable Trainer. So tell me..."
  ask "Do you think you can reach even greater heights by understanding your Pokémon?"
  if no goto Doubt
  say "I see! To me, that's the ideal answer. You bring out your Pokémon's power by respecting them. How wonderful!"
  goto Switch
label Doubt
  say "I see... An honest answer. Don't lose heart. The reason you're so strong is that you treat your Pokémon well, whether you know it or not."
label Switch
  say "You've given me plenty to think about. As thanks, I'll switch off one of the barrier's switches myself. The other is your problem!"
  speaker none
  text "Colress pressed a switch on the wall. Somewhere in the ship, something hummed and went quiet."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_SWITCH_B
  call UnovaFrigateBarriers
  music area
  endscene
  end
label After
  say "Whether Team Plasma wins or you do will decide what the bond between people and Pokémon becomes. I'm looking forward to seeing which."
  end
