# Solaceon Town (plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_solaceon_town.s).
#
# The rival comes running down the street as the player first walks into town from Route 209 (the original's trigger,
# a row seven tiles wide at y 669, while VAR_SOLACEON_TOWN_STATE is 0): he has been in the ruins, where he found HM05,
# and goes off north again. The Ruin Maniac gives the Pokémon History app to a trainer who has seen fifty species of
# the Sinnoh Pokédex.


script Rival
  emote player exclaim
  music "common/rival"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SOLACEON_TOWN_RIVAL
  if var PLAYER_X == 557 place rival 557 661 down
  if var PLAYER_X == 558 place rival 558 661 down
  if var PLAYER_X == 559 place rival 559 661 down
  if var PLAYER_X == 560 place rival 560 661 down
  if var PLAYER_X == 561 place rival 561 661 down
  if var PLAYER_X == 562 place rival 562 661 down
  if var PLAYER_X == 563 place rival 563 661 down
  show rival
  face player up
  walk rival down 7 fast
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Woo-hoo!"
  face rival left
  wait 0.2
  face rival right
  wait 0.2
  face rival down
  say "Hey, {player}! I've got something brilliant. Since it's you, I'll let you have a look!"
  emote rival exclaim
  say "Huh? Wait a second... Where did they go? I had some Seals, and I never even stuck them on anything." "Oh, that's right. It's because I was in the ruins!"
  say "The ruins here are amazing. You've got to go and see. I even found a Hidden Machine in there. Defog!" "Anyway, we'll battle another time. See you!"
  walk rival up 7 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SOLACEON_TOWN_RIVAL
  music area
  setvar VAR_SOLACEON_TOWN_STATE 1
  end


script RuinManiac
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_POKETCH_POKEMON_HISTORY goto Gave
  if var SEEN < 50 goto TooFew
  say "Every Pokémon you meet becomes part of your story, and you part of theirs." "This is a Pokétch app called Pokémon History. It keeps a record of the Pokémon you've caught."
  poketchapp PokemonHistory
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_POKETCH_POKEMON_HISTORY
  text "{player}'s Pokétch now has the Pokémon History app!"
  end
label TooFew
  say "A travelling trainer, are you? Then how many kinds of Pokémon have you come across so far?" "Any trainer worth the name should have met fifty at least. If you haven't, go further and look harder!"
  end
label Gave
  say "You have a history of your own, and so does every Pokémon." "Where the two cross, a new story begins."
  end
