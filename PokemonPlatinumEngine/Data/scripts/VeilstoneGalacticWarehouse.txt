# Team Galactic's warehouse in Veilstone City (plan 02 · S8), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_veilstone_city_galactic_warehouse.s).
#
# Looker brings the player in once the assistant and the player have beaten its guards (veilstone_city.txt,
# VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE 1). The way on is shut by a rusty door that wants a special key (the
# Storage Key and the Galactic HQ behind it are plan 02 · S11's); Looker finds HM02 in its ball by the crates and tells
# the player to keep it, and has overheard in the Game Corner that Team Galactic has sent something to Pastoria City
# (VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 1, which brings the rival there). He waits in here until the explosion in Pastoria
# (FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_LOOKER).


# The original's OnTransition (Looker by the door until he has looked round) and OnFrame (state 1).
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE < 2 place looker 8 10 up
  if var VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE >= 2 place looker 12 8 down
  if var VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE == 1 goto WithLooker
  end
label WithLooker
  walk looker up 2
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Hmm. This door, it does not budge. Without the proper key, here our road ends."
  say "Whoever fled through it is beyond our reach... for now."
  walk looker down 2
  face looker left
  wait 0.4
  face looker right
  wait 0.4
  walk looker right 5
  face looker up
  emote looker exclaim
  face looker left
  face player right
  say "What is this? A Hidden Machine, for the move Fly!"
  say "Take it, {player}. Team Galactic will make no use of it if it is in your bag."
  walk looker left 5
  face looker down
  face player up
  setvar VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 1
  setvar VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE 2
  say "Well. The warehouse has told us little. Never mind."
  say "In the Game Corner, I caught a whisper: a crate of Team Galactic's has gone off to Pastoria City."
  say "What is in it, I cannot say. But my nose for trouble, it is twitching."
  end


# Looker afterwards (the original's script 3).
script Looker
  speaker "Looker"
  say "A crate of Team Galactic's, on its way to Pastoria City... I do not like it, my friend. Not one bit."
  say "If you go that way, keep your eyes open for me."
  end
