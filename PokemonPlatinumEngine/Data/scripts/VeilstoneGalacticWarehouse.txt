# Team Galactic's warehouse in Veilstone City (plan 02 · S8), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_veilstone_city_galactic_warehouse.s).
#
# Looker brings the player in once the assistant and the player have beaten its guards (veilstone_city.txt,
# VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE 1). The way on is shut by a rusty door that wants a special key (the
# Storage Key and the Galactic HQ behind it are plan 02 · S11's); Looker finds HM02 in its ball by the crates and tells
# the player to keep it, and has overheard in the Game Corner that Team Galactic has sent something to Pastoria City
# (VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 1, which brings the rival there). He waits in here until the explosion in Pastoria
# (FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_LOOKER).
#
# Plan 02 · S11, part b: the door is the original's, two leaves across the corridor (the map file's
# galactic_hq_door_west and _east, behind FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_GALACTIC_HQ_DOOR), and behind it
# the stairs go down to the headquarters' basement (GalacticHQB2F). Once Looker has the Storage Key and the player has
# agreed to go in with him (veilstone_city.txt, VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE 3), a step up to the door
# (the original's trigger, the two tiles before it) brings him in behind the player: he unlocks it, goes on ahead down
# the stairs and is gone from Veilstone City (state 4). The player never holds the key in Platinum; the branch of the
# original's door script that asks to use it is Diamond and Pearl's, and nothing gives the item.


# The original's OnTransition (Looker by the door until he has looked round) and OnFrame (state 1).
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE < 2 place looker 8 10 up
  if var VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE >= 2 place looker 12 8 down
  if var VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE == 1 goto WithLooker
  end
label WithLooker
  scene "Looker in the warehouse"
  walk looker up 2
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Hmm. This door, it does not budge. Without the proper key, here our road ends."
  say "Whoever fled through it is beyond our reach... for now."
  walk looker down 2
  face looker left
  wait 0.4
  face looker right
  wait 0.4
  walk looker right 5
  face looker up
  emote looker exclaim
  face looker left
  face player right
  say "What is this? A Hidden Machine, for the move Fly!"
  say "Take it, {player}. Team Galactic will make no use of it if it is in your bag."
  walk looker left 5
  face looker down
  face player up
  setvar VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 1
  setvar VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE 2
  say "Well. The warehouse has told us little. Never mind."
  say "In the Game Corner, I caught a whisper: a crate of Team Galactic's has gone off to Pastoria City."
  say "What is in it, I cannot say. But my nose for trouble, it is twitching."
  endscene
  end


# The door, read while it stands (the original's script 1, without the key nobody is given).
script Door
  speaker none
  say "Rust has crept all over this heavy door. It won't open without some special key."
  end


# Up to the door with Looker's word given (the original's coordinate event, a row of two tiles): he comes in by the
# way the player did, round them to the tile beside theirs, and opens the door.
script LookerOpensDoor
  scene "Looker opens the door"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_LOOKER
  place looker 8 11 up
  show looker
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto BesideOnTheWest
  walk looker up 2 right 1 up 1
  face looker left
  face player right
  goto Opens
label BesideOnTheWest
  walk looker up 3
  face looker right
  face player left
label Opens
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Ah, there you are! I have kept you waiting."
  face looker up
  say "Now, the Storage Key that Team Galactic so kindly let fall... In it goes, and..."
  sound "door_slide"
  hide galactic_hq_door_west
  hide galactic_hq_door_east
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_GALACTIC_HQ_DOOR
  setflag FLAG_USED_STORAGE_KEY
  wait 0.3
  say "...clunk! There! It is open!"
  face looker player
  say "I go on ahead, my friend. Do not put yourself in the way of harm!"
  face player up
  walk looker up 5
  face looker left
  wait 0.2
  face looker right
  wait 0.3
  if var PLAYER_X == 9 goto DownFromTheWest
  walk looker right 4 fast
  goto Gone
label DownFromTheWest
  walk looker right 5 fast
label Gone
  sound "stairs"
  hide looker
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_LOOKER
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VEILSTONE_CITY_LOOKER
  setvar VAR_VEILSTONE_CITY_GALACTIC_WAREHOUSE_STATE 4
  endscene
  end


# Looker afterwards (the original's script 3).
script Looker
  speaker "Looker"
  say "A crate of Team Galactic's, on its way to Pastoria City... I do not like it, my friend. Not one bit."
  say "If you go that way, keep your eyes open for me."
  end
