# The Driftveil Gym's lobby (plan 22 · U6, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
# The guide (0) gives a challenger Fresh Water (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_DRIFTVEIL). The lift's car at the head of the path
# (7, 3) takes the player down to the Gym's floor (the overlay's exit to DriftveilCity_Gym).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script GymGuide
  face 0 player
  speaker "Guide"
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_DRIFTVEIL goto Water
  if badge quake goto Won
  say "Clay waits at the bottom of the mine, past his workers. Water and Grass moves will wear his Ground types down!"
  end
label Water
  say "Hey there, future champ! This Gym is a working mine. The lift takes you down, and the workers will dig in for a battle!"
  say "Here, have a drink before you go down!"
  give "Fresh Water"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_WATER_DRIFTVEIL
  say "Clay's Ground types don't care for Water or Grass moves. And Electric moves won't touch them at all!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Clay! You dug deep, champ. Even the workers are cheering down there!"
  end
