# The Unknown Dungeon behind the Pokémon Village (plan 23 · Z4b). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are
# publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos after its Hall of Fame"): at the far end of the last chamber
# Mewtwo waits alone, at level 70. Beaten or caught it is gone for good (FLAG_KALOS_MEWTWO_GONE) and the two Mewtwonites lie
# where it stood (X's and Y's both, since the game has no versions); run from, it stays.


script Mewtwo
  speaker none
  scene "Mewtwo in the Unknown Dungeon"
  text "A Pokémon stands alone at the end of the cave, with its back to the wall. Slowly, it turns its head toward {player}."
  cry "Mewtwo"
  wildbattle "Mewtwo" 70
  if result == 2 goto Stays
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_MEWTWO_GONE
  hide self
  text "Where Mewtwo stood, two stones glimmer on the cave floor."
  give "Mewtwonite X"
  give "Mewtwonite Y"
  goto Done
label Stays
  text "Mewtwo watches {player} go, without moving."
label Done
  endscene
  end
