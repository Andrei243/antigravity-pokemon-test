# Opal's gym mission (plan 33 · GL5, its first session): a quizmaster at each of the course's three gates, stepping aside
# for a right answer, and two gym trainers beside the way. A gate passed stays open (its own flag); a wrong answer only
# asks again next time. Our own questions, on Sword and Shield's beat of Opal's quiz; the quiz changes nothing in the
# battle (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Hammerlocke, Route 6, Stow-on-Side and Ballonlea"). Our own lines.


script MissionQuiz1
  speaker "Quizmaster"
  choose "Question one! Which type has no effect on a Fairy type at all?" "Dragon" "Fighting" "Dark"
  if result == 0 goto Right
  say "Oh dear, not quite! Have a think and come back to me."
  end
label Right
  say "Quite right! Dragon moves can't touch a fairy. On you go!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BALLONLEA_QUIZ_1
  end


script MissionQuiz2
  speaker "Quizmaster"
  choose "Question two! What is the name of the town where you won your first badge?" "Hulbury" "Turffield" "Motostoke"
  if result == 1 goto Right
  say "Oh dear, not quite! Have a think and come back to me."
  end
label Right
  say "Quite right! Milo's town, and all those Wooloo. On you go!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BALLONLEA_QUIZ_2
  end


script MissionQuiz3
  speaker "Quizmaster"
  choose "Last question! What is Opal's favourite colour?" "Blue" "Green" "Pink"
  if result == 2 goto Right
  say "Oh dear, not quite! Look around you, dear, and come back to me."
  end
label Right
  say "Quite right! Pink, pink and more pink. The mission is cleared!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_BALLONLEA_QUIZ_3
  end
