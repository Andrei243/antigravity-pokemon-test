# A house in Dewford Town (plan 21 · H3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/DewfordTown_House2/scripts.inc): the boy who hands out a Silk Scarf (FLAG_RECEIVED_SILK_SCARF).


script Boy
  face self player
  speaker "Boy"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_SILK_SCARF goto Given
  say "Grandpa gave me this scarf, but it's way too smooth for me. You have it!"
  give "Silk Scarf"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_SILK_SCARF
  say "If a Pokémon holds it, its Normal-type moves get stronger. That's what Grandpa says."
  end
label Given
  say "A sailor needs a rough scarf, not a silky one. That's what I think."
  end
