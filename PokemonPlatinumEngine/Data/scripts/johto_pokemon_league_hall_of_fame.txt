# Johto's Hall of Fame (plan 20 · J12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on HeartGold's
# beats (scr_seq_T10R0701, and its Hall of Fame's ending).
#
# One scene, played as the new Champion comes in behind Lance: his words, the team recorded (halloffame), Johto's
# story ended (JohtoHallOfFame, the region's flag, which opens the way on to Hoenn from Olivine's port and the gate's
# door to Route 22), the scene of the team's entry, the team healed, home to the player's room in New Bark Town, the
# game saved there, and the credits. Professor Elm's call comes once the player is downstairs
# (new_bark_player_house_1f.txt).


script OnEnter
  scene "Johto's Hall of Fame"
  face t10r0701_wataru player
  speaker "Lance"
  say "This is the Hall of Fame. Every trainer who has beaten the League is honoured here, with the Pokémon who stood beside them."
  say "Every one of them came here as you have, not alone, but with partners they believed in."
  say "{player}, today your name and your Pokémon's join theirs. Let's record your team, so the League never forgets it."
  endscene
  wait 0.4
  fade out 0.8
  halloffame
  setflag JohtoHallOfFame
  # Johto's swarms begin with the Champion (plan 20 · J13, part c; HeartGold turns them on in Professor Oak's lab)
  swarms on
  # Red is back on Mt. Silver's summit for every new entry (plan 20 · J15)
  clearflag FLAG_UNK_25F
  halloffame show
  heal
  warp "NewBark_PlayersHouse_2F" 4 9 up
  save
  if result == 0 goto NotSaved
  sound "save"
  text "{player} saved the game."
  goto Roll
label NotSaved
  text "The game could not be saved."
label Roll
  credits
  end
