# A couple who wanted a cottage to themselves. He gives away the Pokétch's Coin Toss app, which chose this one for
# them; both have something else to say once the player has it. A Max Revive is hidden in the bin.


script CoinToss
  if poketchapp CoinToss goto Have
  say "The hotel was full, but they managed to squeeze the two of us in here." "So at last we're alone together. Or we would be, if you weren't standing there." "Look, here's something we have no more use for. Take it, would you?"
  fanfare keyitem
  poketchapp CoinToss
  text "{player} added the Coin Toss app to the Pokétch!"
label Have
  say "I've always left my decisions to the toss of a coin." "It makes me brave. Nothing I decide is ever my fault!"
  end


script Lady
  if poketchapp CoinToss goto Have
  say "It was his coin toss that landed us in this musty, stuffy old cottage..."
  end
label Have
  say "We came here to be alone and talk, with nobody to disturb us." "We need this time together, just the two of us."
  end
