# Grayfall Keep, the old sea fort on Grayfall Islet (plan 41 · EG9, "The gathering"). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own.
#
# The outline the user chose (2026-11-10, the outline sheet's 12a, 13b, 14b, 15a, 16b, 59a, 60a): Ismene Quill, keeper of
# the Laurel Gallery's records, spread a rumour to each villain team's leader that their old machine had come back, and
# every one of them sailed here angry to claim it. A leader stands in their team's bay only where the player finished
# their region (shownBy its Hall of Fame), behind their admins, whose battles open the way (each hidden once beaten).
# With every leader there beaten, Ismene puts on her show: her battle, then a showman's finale (curtains, bows, and
# tickets for next season). Afterwards each leader keeps their bay for a battle a day (FLAG_DAILY_KEEP_<WHO>). Every team
# here is ours (Data/our-trainers.json, <who>_gathering), lifted to the Laurel Circuit's level by GameEngine.StartTrainerBattle.


script Looker
  face self player
  speaker "Looker"
  if flag FLAG_GATHERING_DONE goto After
  say "Every leader in one fort, each sure their old machine is here. I have never seen so many angry people in one place!"
  goto Sail
label After
  say "What a performance. I may buy a ticket for next season myself. In disguise, naturally."
label Sail
  ask "Shall we sail back to the harbour?"
  if no end
  fade out 0.8
  text "{player} sailed back with Looker..."
  warp "Hub" 40 42 up
  fade in 0.8
  end

  say "There is no machine. Only you. Then let us finish what was left unfinished."

  say "...Again. A world without spirit would never have lost to you. Perhaps that is the answer I came for."

  battle self as "cyrus_gathering"
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_KEEP_CYRUS
  say "Tomorrow, then."
  end
label Rested
  say "Come back tomorrow."
  end

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the one who gathered them

script Ismene
  face self player
  speaker "Ismene"
  if not flag SinnohHallOfFame goto Kanto
  if not flag FLAG_KEEP_CYRUS_BEATEN goto NotYet
label Kanto
  if not flag KantoHallOfFame goto Hoenn
  if not flag FLAG_KEEP_GIOVANNI_BEATEN goto NotYet
label Hoenn
  if not flag HoennHallOfFame goto Unova
  if not flag FLAG_KEEP_MAXIE_BEATEN goto NotYet
  if not flag FLAG_KEEP_ARCHIE_BEATEN goto NotYet
label Unova
  if not flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Kalos
  if not flag FLAG_KEEP_GHETSIS_BEATEN goto NotYet
label Kalos
  if not flag KalosHallOfFame goto Show
  if not flag FLAG_KEEP_LYSANDRE_BEATEN goto NotYet
label Show
  scene "TheShow"
  say "Bravo! Bravo! Every act played, every villain beaten, and in front of a full house. Well, a full fort."
  say "Yes, it was me. I keep the Laurel Gallery's records. I've read every plan those leaders ever made, and every way you foiled them."
  say "So I wrote each of them a little note: your old machine has come back. And here they all came, cross as cats!"
  say "I only wanted to see the whole story on one stage. And now, the finale. Mine!"
  battle self as "ismene_gathering"
  say "Oh, wonderful! What an ending! Places, everyone, places!"
  fade out 0.8
  text "The keep's old curtains swept shut across the stage bay..."
  text "...and opened on every leader in a row. Somewhat against their will, they bowed."
  text "Ismene curtsied, and a little sign appeared at the door: TICKETS FOR NEXT SEASON, PLEASE ENQUIRE WITHIN."
  fade in 0.8
  say "Thank you, thank you! The leaders have agreed to stay on. Well, they didn't disagree. They'll battle anyone, once a day."
  say "And you, Champion, have earned a title of your own. Do come back next season!"
  setflag FLAG_GATHERING_DONE
  clearflag FLAG_KEEP_ISMENE_AWAY
  givemoney 50000
  give "Rare Candy" 3
  hide self
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "Not yet, not yet! The show isn't over until every act has played. Go and see the leaders in their bays."
  end
