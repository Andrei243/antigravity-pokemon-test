# The Plasma Frigate's ramp hall, the ship at rest in the Giant Chasm's crater (plan 22 · U9, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/plasma_frigate_6.s, read for the order
# alone).
#
# As the player comes up the ramp (our trigger, 10 to 12, 14; VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 2) Ghetsis (ghetsis_ramp) is at the head of the hall
# with the Shadow Triad (triad_ramp_a to _c) before him. He tells of his plan for Kyurem and leaves for the place where its power is
# strongest; Hugh (hugh_ramp) runs in and asks after his sister's Purrloin, and the first of the Triad shows a Liepard (liepard) that
# answers only to him now. The three battle the player one after another (Black 2's TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_SHADOW, _2 and _3), give the
# Liepard back and go; Hugh stays with it, and the player goes on alone to the cavern north of the crater (3).


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpE
  end


script GhetsisAboard
  scene "Ghetsis aboard the Frigate"
  place player 11 10 up
  speaker "???"
  say "So Colress has gone his own way, has he? He cares more for his questions than for the cause that fed them."
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "Well, well. A child, here? Then you shall have the rare honour of hearing my plan from my own lips."
  say "Kyurem is a hollow thing, what was left behind when Reshiram and Zekrom were made. Hollow things can be filled."
  say "With our science, its power will be wound to the very limit, and all of Unova will be buried in ice."
  say "The people will beg us to stop it, and they will give up their Pokémon to save themselves. Unova will be mine!"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Lord Ghetsis. Kyurem has been moved to the place you chose."
  speaker "Ghetsis"
  say "At last! Then the age of my rule begins. I leave this child to you."
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GHETSIS_RAMP
  hide ghetsis_ramp
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_RAMP
  show hugh_ramp
  fade in
  face player hugh_ramp
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! I saw a man in a long robe leave... Never mind him. You! You're one of the Shadow Triad, aren't you?"
  face hugh_ramp triad_ramp_a
  say "Tell me what happened to the Purrloin that was stolen in Aspertia City five years ago!"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Very well. Perhaps this is the one you mean."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_LIEPARD_RAMP
  show liepard
  cry "Liepard"
  say "I took it as a Purrloin, five years ago. It has grown, and now it obeys no one but me."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "No... That Pokémon belongs to my little sister! Give it back!"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Pity the Pokémon, bound to Poké Balls and to whatever their Trainers want. But enough. You will not hinder Lord Ghetsis."
  battle triad_ramp_a as "unova_team_plasma_shadow"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Hm. Then try all three of us."
  battle triad_ramp_b as "unova_team_plasma_shadow_2"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "Even if we fall, Lord Ghetsis need only win."
  battle triad_ramp_c as "unova_team_plasma_shadow_3"
  speaker "Shadow Triad"
  say "It makes no difference. You can only watch as Lord Ghetsis uses Kyurem to freeze Unova."
  face triad_ramp_a hugh_ramp
  say "You may have the Liepard back. After what Lord Ghetsis is about to do, I have no more need of it."
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_TRIAD_RAMP
  hide triad_ramp_a
  hide triad_ramp_b
  hide triad_ramp_c
  fade in
  face hugh_ramp player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...It's glaring at me. I finally found my sister's Pokémon, and it doesn't know me at all."
  say "{player}... If Team Plasma get their way, there'll be more Pokémon like this one, and like Kyurem. Go after that man!"
  say "I'll stay here with Liepard for now. I'll catch you up, I promise."
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GHETSIS_CAVERN
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_KYUREM_CAVERN
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CHASM 3
  endscene
  end
