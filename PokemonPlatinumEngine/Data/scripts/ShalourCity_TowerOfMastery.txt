# The Tower of Mastery in Shalour City (plan 23 · Z3, first part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they
# are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Route 10 to Shalour City and the Key Stone, drawn by hand"): the
# old master of the tower, Gurkinn, keeps the stones that let a Trainer's Pokémon Mega Evolve. On the first visit he
# and his granddaughter Korrina, Shalour's Gym Leader, send the player to her Gym first; once the Rumble Badge is won
# he hands over a Key Stone, only to a bag without one, and Korrina shows Mega Evolution in a battle of her Lucario,
# then gives the player a Lucario of their own holding its Lucarionite, once. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's
# count: 19 becomes 20 at the first visit, 21 is the Rumble Badge (ShalourCity_Gym.txt) and 22 the Key Stone's scene.
#
# The people: gurkinn, and korrina (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_KORRINA_TOWER: set as she leaves for her Gym, cleared once the
# Badge is won). Korrina's tower team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_korrina_tower, with a Key Stone of her own.


script Guru
  face gurkinn player
  speaker "Gurkinn"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 22 goto After
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY == 21 goto Mastery
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY == 20 goto Waiting
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY == 19 goto First
  say "Welcome to the Tower of Mastery. Look around as long as you like, young Trainer. The statue has stood here a very long time."
  end

label First
  scene "The Tower of Mastery"
  say "Ho! Another young Trainer climbs my stairs. I am Gurkinn, the master of this tower."
  say "This statue shows a Pokémon in a shape it can only take when its heart and its Trainer's beat as one. We call it Mega Evolution."
  say "Two stones make it happen: a Mega Stone held by the Pokémon, and a Key Stone carried by the Trainer."
  speaker "Korrina"
  face korrina player
  say "Grandpa, can I? I'm Korrina, the Leader of Shalour's Gym. Grandpa only gives Key Stones to Trainers who've earned them."
  say "So come to my Gym first and show me what you've got! If you beat me, I'll put in a good word."
  walk korrina down 9 fast
  hide korrina
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_KORRINA_TOWER
  speaker "Gurkinn"
  say "Ho ho. She skates everywhere, that one. Her Gym is east of the main street. Go on!"
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 20
  objective kalosKorrina
  endscene
  end

label Waiting
  say "Korrina waits at her Gym, east of the main street. Win her Badge, then come back to me."
  end

label Mastery
  scene "Mega Evolution"
  say "Ho! Korrina's Rumble Badge. Then you are ready to carry what the tower keeps."
  if item "Key Stone" goto HasStone
  say "This Key Stone is yours. Wear it close, and let it listen to your heart."
  give "Key Stone"
  goto Shown
label HasStone
  say "You carry a Key Stone already, I see. Then you need no other from me. Keep it close."
label Shown
  speaker "Korrina"
  face korrina player
  say "My turn! Watch closely: this is what a Key Stone and a Mega Stone can do together."
  battle korrina as "kalos_korrina_tower" canlose
  heal
  speaker "Korrina"
  if lost goto KorrinaWon
  say "You beat a Mega Evolved Lucario! Grandpa, did you see that?"
  goto Gift
label KorrinaWon
  say "That's the power of Mega Evolution! And now you have a Key Stone too."
label Gift
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_LUCARIO goto Done
  say "Here, take this Lucario. It was raised at the tower, and it holds its own Mega Stone, the Lucarionite."
  givepokemon "Lucario" 32 holding "Lucarionite"
  text "{player} received a Lucario holding a Lucarionite!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_LUCARIO
  say "In battle, choose its move with the Key Stone ready, and it'll Mega Evolve. Take good care of it!"
label Done
  call Surf
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 22
  objective kalosFree
  endscene
  end

label After
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_HM03 goto Owed
  say "The bond between you and your Pokémon is the real stone. The rest is only rock."
  end
label Owed
  call Surf
  end

# Plan 23, Kalos's water: HM03 Surf, which X and Y hand over in Shalour City once the Rumble Badge and the Key Stone are
# the player's (the neighbour gives it there; here the guru does, the neighbour being on the road ahead: rulings,
# "Kalos's Gyms, its water and the Mamoswine ride"). A save already past the tower is given it by talking to him again
script Surf
  speaker "Gurkinn"
  say "One thing more. Kalos is a land of rivers and coasts, and a Trainer who can't cross water sees only half of it."
  give "HM03"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_HM03
  say "That is Surf. Teach it to a Pokémon that swims, and face the water to ride out on its back."
  return


script Korrina
  face korrina player
  speaker "Korrina"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY >= 22 goto After
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY == 21 goto Ready
  say "Grandpa talks to that statue more than to me. I think it listens better!"
  end
label Ready
  say "You won my Badge! Go and talk to Grandpa. He's been waiting all morning."
  end
label After
  say "Train that Lucario well. Next time we battle, it'll be Mega against Mega!"
  end
