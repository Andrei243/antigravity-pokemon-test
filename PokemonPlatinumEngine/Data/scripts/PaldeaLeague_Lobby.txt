# The Pokémon League's lobby by Mesagoza's south wall (plan 34 · PD8, its first session): its nurse and its shop, the
# staff at the counter, and Rika's desk, where a challenger with Paldea's eight badges is interviewed before the Elite
# Four. The interview asks three questions; every answer is heard and none is wrong (VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 1 once it is
# done, PaldeaLeague_Court.txt's count). Our own lines and questions.


# Rika's interview, the Champion assessment's first step
script Interview
  face rika player
  speaker "Rika"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 1 goto Done
  if badges >= 8 goto Interview
  say "Welcome to the Pokémon League. I hold the interviews for the Champion assessment, and I can only see challengers who carry all eight of Paldea's badges."
  say "Come back when your case is full. I'll keep the chair warm for you."
  end
label Interview
  scene "The League's interview"
  say "Eight badges, all present and polished. So you're here for the Champion assessment. Sit down. I'm Rika, and before anybody battles, I ask the questions."
  say "Don't look so worried. There aren't any wrong answers. Only dull ones."
  choose "First: what brings you to the League today?" "To become a Champion" "To see how strong my team is" "Nemona sent me"
  if result == 2 goto SentByNemona
  if result == 1 goto ToMeasure
  say "Straight to the point. I like that in a challenger."
  goto Second
label ToMeasure
  say "Sensible. The four of us make a very good measuring stick."
  goto Second
label SentByNemona
  say "Ha! She sends us somebody every year. Usually she just turns up herself."
label Second
  choose "Which gym gave you the hardest time?" "Cortondo's" "Levincia's" "Medali's" "Glaseado's" "None of them"
  if result == 2 goto Medali
  if result == 4 goto NoneOfThem
  say "That leader will be glad to hear it. Hard is the whole point of a gym."
  goto Third
label Medali
  say "Larry's? Don't tell him I said so, but he'll be pleased. Very quietly pleased."
  goto Third
label NoneOfThem
  say "None at all? Bold words, said to the person who's about to battle you."
label Third
  choose "Last one. Your team, in a single word?" "Trusted" "Strong" "Fun"
  say "Noted, in my very best handwriting."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE 1
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_LEAGUE_INTERVIEWED
  say "That's the interview over. Now the practical part. The battle court is through the door at the back, and the first of the Elite Four you'll meet there is... me."
  say "Go on through. I'll be right behind you."
  hide rika
  endscene
  end
label Done
  say "The court's through the door at the back. The Elite Four are waiting for you."
  end


# The staff at the counter
script Staff
  face league_staff player
  speaker "League Staff"
  if flag PaldeaHallOfFame goto Champion
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEAGUE >= 1 goto Interviewed
  say "Welcome to the Pokémon League. Challengers for the Champion assessment see Rika at the desk for their interview first."
  end
label Interviewed
  say "The battle court is through the door at the back. Good luck. Truly."
  end
label Champion
  say "Welcome back, Champion. Your name is on the League's register now, beside Nemona's."
  end
