# The Safari Zone's gate in Fuchsia City (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/FuchsiaCity_SafariZone_Entrance/scripts.inc).
#
# Whoever walks up to the zone's door steps onto one of FireRed's three coordinate events before it (the area's
# triggers 0 to 2) and is asked to play: for $500, thirty Safari Balls and FireRed's six hundred steps
# (`safari start 600`; Models/SafariGame.cs), then in through the door to the zone's centre. Turned down, or short of
# the fee, they are sent a step back.
#
# Coming back through the zone's door with a game still on, the player is asked whether to leave early: yes ends the
# game, no leads back in (FireRed's ExitEarly, VAR_MAP_SCENE_FUCHSIA_CITY_SAFARI_ZONE_ENTRANCE 2). A game that ended
# in the zone (its last step, its last ball: common.SafariTimeUp and SafariOutOfBalls, which bring a player of Kanto
# here with the variable 1) is seen off at the counter (FireRed's ExitWarpIn).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_FUCHSIA_CITY_SAFARI_ZONE_ENTRANCE == 1 goto Over
  if not safari end
  speaker "Attendant"
  ask "Leaving the Safari Zone already? You still have time left. Are you sure?"
  if no goto Back
  say "Very well. Please hand back your Safari Balls. We hope to see you again!"
  safari end
  walk player down 3
  end
label Back
  say "Then off you go. Good luck out there!"
  warp "SafariZone_Center" 26 29 up
  end
label Over
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Did you catch your fair share? Come again any time!"
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_FUCHSIA_CITY_SAFARI_ZONE_ENTRANCE 0
  walk player down 2
  end


# The counter, from FireRed's coordinate events before the door (EntryTriggerLeft, Mid and Right)
script Counter
  if safari end
  face player right
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Safari Zone!"
  ask "For just $500, you can catch all the Pokémon you like! Would you like to play the Safari Game?"
  if no goto Away
  takemoney 500
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  safari start 600
  say "That'll be $500, thank you. Here you are: we use only these special balls in the zone."
  text "{player} received 30 Safari Balls!"
  say "We'll call you over the speakers when your game is up. Enjoy yourself!"
  walk player up 1
  warp "SafariZone_Center" 26 29 up
  end
label Short
  say "I'm sorry, you don't have enough money."
label Away
  say "Very well. Please come again!"
  walk player down 1
  end


script CounterTalk
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Walk up to the door behind me, and I'll ask whether you'd like to play the Safari Game."
  end


script Explain
  speaker "Attendant"
  ask "The Safari Zone has four parts, each with its own Pokémon. Would you like to hear how the Safari Game works?"
  if no goto Enjoy
  say "You go in with 30 Safari Balls. They're the only balls you may throw inside."
  say "You can't battle the Pokémon you meet, but you can throw things to calm them or rile them up. Try it and see!"
  say "The game is over when you've walked 600 steps, or when your last ball is thrown."
  end
label Enjoy
  say "Then enjoy the Safari Zone!"
  end
