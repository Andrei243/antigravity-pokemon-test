# The island's sailor takes the player back to Canalave City, to the pier by Sailor Eldritch's boat.

script Ferry
  face self player
  say "Seen enough of the island? The tunnels under it go a long way down."
  ask "Shall I sail you back to Canalave City?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Off we go, then!"
  warp "Sinnoh" 44 750 left
  end
label Stay
  say "Take your time. I'll be here with the boat."
  end
