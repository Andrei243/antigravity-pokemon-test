# Iron Island (plan 01 · M7, plan 02 · S10). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_iron_island.s).
#
# The island's sailor takes the player back to Canalave City, to the pier by Sailor Eldritch's boat.
#
# Riley stands before the way into the island's tunnels (plan 02 · S10). Spoken to, he gives HM04 (Strength) whether or
# not the player has Byron's Badge, says what the Badge has to do with it, and goes inside, where he waits two floors
# down (iron_island_b2f_left_room.txt). The original's RemoveObject sets the flag that hides him, so he is gone from
# the door for good (FLAG_HIDE_IRON_ISLAND_RILEY).

script Ferry
  face self player
  say "Seen enough of the island? The tunnels under it go a long way down."
  ask "Shall I sail you back to Canalave City?"
  if no goto Stay
  say "Off we go, then!"
  warp "Sinnoh" 44 750 left
  end
label Stay
  say "Take your time. I'll be here with the boat."
  end


# Riley before the tunnels' door (the original's script 3).
script Riley
  face self player
  speaker "Riley"
  say "Hello there. I don't think we've met. My name is Riley, and I'm a Trainer, same as you."
  say "You've come to the island to train, {player}? Then here, a small gift from one Trainer to another."
  give "HM04"
  if badge mine goto Badge
  say "That HM holds Strength. A Pokémon that knows it can push the heaviest boulders out of the way."
  say "Outside of battle, though, it answers only to the Gym Badge of Canalave City. Something to aim for!"
  goto In
label Badge
  say "That HM holds Strength. With Canalave's Gym Badge on you already, your Pokémon can shove boulders aside with it."
  say "I'm going back inside to train. Come and find me down there, if you like."
label In
  face riley up
  sound "stairs"
  hide riley
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_IRON_ISLAND_RILEY
  end
