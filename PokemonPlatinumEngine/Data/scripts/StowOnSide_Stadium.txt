# Stow-on-Side Stadium's lobby (plan 33 · GL5, its first session): the fourth gym of the challenge, Bea's in a Sword game
# and Allister's in a Shield one (VAR_GALAR_VERSION). The mission is through the back (StowOnSide_StadiumMission.txt),
# and the pitch beyond it (StowOnSide_StadiumPitch.txt). Our own lines.


script Receptionist
  speaker "Receptionist"
  if badge fighting goto Won
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  say "Welcome to Stow-on-Side Stadium! Bea's gym mission is through the doors behind me. Keep your footing!"
  say "A gym trainer stands at each of the course's gates. Pass each one's test, then Bea awaits on the pitch."
  end
label Shield
  say "Welcome to Stow-on-Side Stadium! Allister's gym mission is through the doors behind me. Don't be frightened!"
  say "A masked gym trainer stands at each of the course's gates. Pass each one's test, then Allister awaits on the pitch."
  end
label Won
  say "Congratulations on your fourth badge! Ballonlea is through Glimwood Tangle, east of town."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge fighting goto Won
  if var VAR_GALAR_VERSION == 2 goto Shield
  say "Bea's Fighting types hit hard. Flying, psychic and fairy moves will take them down a peg!"
  end
label Shield
  say "Allister's Ghost types play tricks. Dark and ghost moves will see right through them!"
  end
label Won
  say "Four badges! Halfway to the Champion Cup. Ballonlea is through the glowing wood to the east."
  end
