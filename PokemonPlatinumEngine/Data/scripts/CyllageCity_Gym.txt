# Cyllage City's Gym (plan 23 · Z2, second part). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Central Kalos, drawn by hand: Route 5 to Cyllage City"): Grant, a climber of
# rock walls, battles for the Cliff Badge, Kalos's second (`givebadge cliff`), and gives a TM. The hall is a climbing wall
# in two pitches (the map's puzzle, CyllageWall: plan 23, Kalos's Gyms), climbed up and sideways by its grips, and gone
# down by its ropes. VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: the Badge makes it 18.
# His team is Kalos's trainers.json's kalos_grant_1; his two trainers are the hall's own.


script Grant
  face grant player
  speaker kalos_grant "Grant"
  if badge cliff goto Won
  scene "Grant"
  say "So you made the climb. I'm Grant, Leader of the Cyllage Gym."
  say "On a rock face you look for the next hold, and then the one after it. One mistake and you start again from the bottom."
  say "A battle is the same. Show me how you climb!"
  battle self as "kalos_grant_1"
  say "You never once lost your grip. That's a climb I'd be proud of."
  say "Here. The Cliff Badge is yours, proof that you reached the top of the Cyllage Gym."
  givebadge cliff
  text "{player} received the Cliff Badge from Grant!"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_DEFEATED_CYLLAGE_GYM
  defeat "kalos_hiker_bruno"
  defeat "kalos_hiker_armand"
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 18
  objective kalosRoute10
label Tm
  say "Take this too. My Pokémon use it to bury a foe's feet in stone and slow it right down."
  give "TM39"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_GRANT
  say "There's more of Kalos to climb. Keep looking for the next hold."
  endscene
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_TM_GRANT goto Tm
  say "Every morning I climb this wall and think of our battle. I still haven't found where I slipped."
  end


script GymGuide
  speaker "Gym Guide"
  if badge cliff goto Won
  say "Hello there, future Champion! Grant's Rock types shrug off fire and flight. Bring water, grass or a good fist!"
  end
label Won
  say "You beat Grant! You must have hands of iron."
  end
