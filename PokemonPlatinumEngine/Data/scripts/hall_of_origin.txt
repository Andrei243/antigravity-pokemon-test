# The Hall of Origin (plan 29 · LS11a), the original's own map above Spear Pillar. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_hall_of_origin.s).
#
# Nothing leads here but the stair of light the Azure Flute calls from the top of Spear Pillar's steps
# (the summits' AzureFlute, for whoever Celestic Town's elder gave the flute once Dialga, Palkia and Giratina were all caught:
# VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE 1). Arceus waits at the head of the hall: the wide step across it (the original's trigger,
# 30 to 32, 35) is its meeting. Whatever the battle's end but a loss the hall is quiet from then on (the state 2, and
# Arceus off its dais); a caught Arceus is gone for good (FLAG_CAUGHT_ARCEUS, ours), one beaten or run from is back once
# the Hall of Fame has next been entered (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt puts the state back to 1). Platinum's own level,
# 80. The way down at the hall's foot leads back into Mt. Coronet, as the original's does.
#
# Its meeting is plan 29's beat (style guide, "Legendary scenes"), the row of time and space without the rift (Arceus
# is no tear): the bars, the camera up the hall to it, a burst of light and the air wavering under slow motion, the
# hall shaken, its cry and a line, then the battle's own way in (plan 09 · L14's Legendary).


# HallOfOrigin_OnTransition
script OnEnter
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_ARCEUS
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_ARCEUS end
  if var VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE != 1 end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_ARCEUS
  end


# HallOfOrigin_Arceus: the step across the hall, or Arceus itself
script Arceus
  scene "Arceus in the Hall of Origin"
  music stop
  face player up
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 31 27 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect burst 31 27
  slowmo 0.5 1.5
  effect ripple 31 27
  camera shake 0.8
  wait 0.6
  cry "Arceus"
  emote player exclaim
  speaker none
  text "Arceus looks down from the head of the hall, and the light round it hums like the first morning of the world."
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Arceus" 80
  setvar VAR_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_STATE 2
  if result == 3 setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_ARCEUS
  hide arceus
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_HALL_OF_ORIGIN_ARCEUS
  music area
  end
