# Route 21 (plan 22 · U8, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/route_21.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The beach east of the Seaside Cave's second mouth, where the Plasma Frigate lies at anchor: Hugh (9) finds the player there
# and Marlon (10) swims up and throws down the gangplank (our trigger, 796, 239 to 242, VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 5; the way aboard is
# FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_AT_ROUTE_21); Hugh goes aboard first (6). Put back ashore by the Shadow Triad (8), the player finds Hugh and
# Cheren on the beach as the Frigate flies off toward the Giant Chasm (FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_LEFT_ROUTE_21, 9); Cheren stays to say
# where the chasm lies, which is plan 22 · U9's.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpD
  if var VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU != 8 end
  scene "The Plasma Frigate flies off"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_ROUTE_21
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CHEREN_ROUTE_21
  show 9
  show 11
  face 9 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Team Plasma! Wherever you go, I won't let you get away!"
  speaker none
  text "With a roar of engines, the Plasma Frigate rose from the water and flew off toward the west..."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_LEFT_ROUTE_21
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...It flies?! Where's it going?"
  face 11 player
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "Sorry I'm late. If I had to guess, they're making for the Giant Chasm."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "The Giant Chasm! That's at the far end of Route 22, right? Then that's where I'm going!"
  say "That Pokémon in the ship... Kyurem. Its cry sounded so lonely."
  speaker "Cheren"
  say "Kyurem! So Team Plasma means to use Kyurem's power for themselves."
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "How do we stop something like that? Drayden said only Reshiram or Zekrom could stand up to it. And neither of them is anywhere!"
  say "...It doesn't matter. I'm going. {player}, follow when you're ready!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 9
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_ROUTE_21
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 9
  fade in
  endscene
  end


# Marlon and the gangplank (our trigger on the beach, at 5)
script Gangplank
  scene "Marlon's gangplank"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_ROUTE_21
  show 9
  face 9 player
  emote 9 exclaim
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! You found them! I knew you could do it!"
  say "That's Team Plasma's ship, anchored right off the beach. But... how do we get aboard?"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MARLON_ROUTE_21
  show 10
  speaker marlon "Marlon"
  say "Hold up!"
  say "Eh, this should do it!"
  speaker none
  text "A heavy gangplank came down from the ship's rail with a solid thud..."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FRIGATE_AT_ROUTE_21
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Marlon! I thought you didn't care about Team Plasma?"
  speaker marlon "Marlon"
  say "Hey, friends! Team Plasma's not bothering me. They might be up to no good for real, but it's not my style to decide that without thinking it through."
  say "But you're in trouble, so I've got to help. That's what I want to do!"
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "...Thanks."
  speaker marlon "Marlon"
  say "Listen, 'K? Believe in something. Searching for a stolen Pokémon is fine. Keeping Unova from freezing is fine too. Just think about why you're doing it."
  say "I made a bit of noise, so some of them are bound to come out. Be careful, 'K!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 10
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_MARLON_ROUTE_21
  fade in
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "What was that?! The guy doesn't stick around, does he?"
  say "Well... I guess he was being kind in his own way. All right, I'm going in!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 9
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_ROUTE_21
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_HOLD
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_HUMILAU 6
  fade in
  endscene
  end
