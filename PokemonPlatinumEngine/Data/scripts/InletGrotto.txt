# Inlet Grotto, the cave in the foot of the cliffs under Poco Path (plan 34 · PD1). VAR_PALDEA_STORY is cabo_poco.txt's
# count. The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beat of the hungry legendary in the cave; the choice of the two is
# plan 30 · decision 14's scene of ours, and the one chosen is the version the game is (plan 34 · decision 2): both
# plans' answers in one choice (the rulings' "Paldea's first chapter").


# A step into the cavern after the roar: a pack of Houndour scattered, two worn-out Pokémon left, one chosen
script TheTwo
  scene "The two in the grotto"
  text "A pack of Houndour bolts past, out of the cave and away along the beach. They had cornered something in here."
  text "Two strange Pokémon lie at the back of the cavern, too tired to stand: one red and armoured, one violet and sleek."
  text "Their stomachs rumble, loudly. They look at the sandwich in {player}'s bag, then at {player}."
  choose "Which of the two do you share your sandwich with?" "The red one" "The violet one"
  if result == 1 goto Violet
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_VERSION 1
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT 1
  seen "Koraidon"
  text "The red one wolfs the sandwich down, and gets to its feet. It gives a happy roar that shakes the cave."
  text "The violet one watches for a moment, then slips out past them both and is gone."
  goto Follows
label Violet
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_VERSION 2
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT 2
  seen "Miraidon"
  text "The violet one eats the sandwich in three neat bites, and gets to its feet with a pleased hum."
  text "The red one watches for a moment, then shoulders past them both and is gone."
label Follows
  text "It nudges {player} with its head. It seems it means to come along. Nemona will be waiting at the lighthouse."
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_HIDE_GROTTO_LEGENDS
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_STORY 4
  objective paldeaLighthouse
  endscene
  end


# Spoken to before the choice: too weak to do more than look up
script Legends
  text "It is too worn out to stand. It looks up hungrily."
  end
