# The Ruins of Alph's northwest chamber (plan 20 · J5). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beat: the stone panel in the floor, its picture of a great bird broken into sixteen pieces, to be put back
# together. What solving it opens (the floor giving way to the rooms below, the Unown) is a later chapter's.


# The panel (HeartGold's background event at 6, 6)
script Panel
  if flag FLAG_ALPH_HOOH_PANEL_SOLVED goto Whole
  text "A stone panel is set in the floor. It shows a great bird, broken into sixteen pieces that seem to slide."
  ask "Try to put the picture back together?"
  if no goto Left
  puzzle hooh
  if result == 0 goto Left
  setflag FLAG_ALPH_HOOH_PANEL_SOLVED
  sound "boulder"
  text "The last piece slid home with a deep click. Somewhere under the Ruins, something rumbled."
  end
label Left
  text "The pieces lie where they were."
  end
label Whole
  text "The panel shows a great bird, whole again. The stone around it is warm to the touch."
  end
