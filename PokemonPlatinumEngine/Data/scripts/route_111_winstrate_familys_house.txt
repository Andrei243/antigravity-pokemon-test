# The Winstrate family's house on Route 111 (plan 21 · H4, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines
# are our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/Route111_WinstrateFamilysHouse/scripts.inc). Victoria gives the Macho
# Brace once to whoever beat the whole family (FLAG_RECEIVED_MACHO_BRACE).


script Victor
  speaker "Victor"
  say "Every one of us trains Pokémon. It's what keeps this family close!"
  end


script Victoria
  speaker "Victoria"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_MACHO_BRACE goto Given
  if not defeated "hoenn_vicky" goto Unbeaten
  say "You beat all four of us! We don't see that very often. Here, I'd like you to have this."
  give "Macho Brace"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_MACHO_BRACE
  say "A Pokémon that holds it grows stronger faster, though it slows down while it wears it."
  end
label Unbeaten
  say "We battle as a family, one after another. Have you tried us all yet?"
  end
label Given
  say "Is the Macho Brace working out? A little training goes a long way."
  end


script Vivi
  speaker "Vivi"
  say "I'm going to be stronger than Grandma one day. Just you wait!"
  end


script Vicky
  speaker "Vicky"
  say "I've been battling longer than anyone in this house. But you, dear, have a real gift."
  end
