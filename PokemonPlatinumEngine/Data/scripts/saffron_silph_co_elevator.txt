# Silph Co.'s lift in HeartGold's Kanto (plan 20 · J15, part n). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T11R0703).
#
# The lift joins the lobby and the research floor, Rotom's room, and carries whoever comes in to the other end at once,
# as the Glitter Lighthouse's does: the two ends each say so as they are come to (VAR_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_FLOOR, a
# variable of ours: 0 set by the lobby's arrival script, 1 by Rotom's room's).


script OnEnter
  walk player up 2
  face player down
  if var VAR_JOHTO_SILPH_LIFT_FLOOR == 1 goto Down
  anim elevator up 4
  text "The lift has reached the research floor."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Saffron_SilphCo_RotomRoom" 7 3 down
  end
label Down
  anim elevator down 4
  text "The lift has reached the lobby."
  sound "stairs"
  warp "Saffron_SilphCo" 3 3 down
  end
