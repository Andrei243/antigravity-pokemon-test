# The Pokémon League's hall (plan 22 · U9, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's
# beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/pokemon_league_2.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The statue in the well at the hall's heart is read from the floor south of it. Until the Elite Four are all beaten in this
# challenge (VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_SHAUNTAL, _GRIMSLEY, _MARSHAL and _CAITLIN all 1) it only says so; then it sinks and carries the
# challenger down to the way to the Champion's room (Black 2's own lift, played over a fade).


script Statue
  speaker none
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_SHAUNTAL != 1 goto Waiting
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_GRIMSLEY != 1 goto Waiting
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_MARSHAL != 1 goto Waiting
  if var VAR_UNOVA_LEAGUE_CAITLIN != 1 goto Waiting
  text "The statue is glowing. All four of the Elite Four have been beaten!"
  ask "Go down to the Champion?"
  if no end
  text "The statue sank slowly into the ground, carrying {player} with it..."
  warp "UnovaPokemonLeague_Descent" 31 84 up
  end
label Waiting
  text "A statue of a Pokémon stands in the well. It won't move until all four of the Elite Four have been beaten."
  end
