# Wedgehurst Station (plan 33 · GL1): the attendant sells a ticket north only to a Trainer endorsed for the gym
# challenge. Boarding is the end of Galar's first chapter; the train's run north and its stop at the Wild Area's
# station are plan 33 · GL2's, so for now it waits at the platform. VAR_GALAR_STORY is postwick.txt's count.


script Attendant
  speaker "Station Attendant"
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY >= 11 goto Waiting
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY == 10 goto Board
  say "Trains north to the Wild Area and Motostoke. Gym challengers travel free, with an endorsement."
  end
label Board
  say "An endorsement from the Champion himself! Then you'll be on your way to the opening ceremony in Motostoke."
  ask "Board the train north?"
  if no goto Later
  scene "Aboard the train north"
  text "{player} stepped aboard. Hop is already at a window seat, waving like mad."
  speaker "Station Attendant"
  say "We'll be off as soon as the line ahead is clear. There's a flock of Wooloo on it again, I'm told."
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 11
  objective galarFree
  endscene
  end
label Later
  say "Come back when you're ready. The trains wait for gym challengers."
  end
label Waiting
  say "Still waiting on those Wooloo up the line, I'm afraid. The train won't be long."
  end
