# The Canalave Library, first floor (plan 02 · S10), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_canalave_library_1f.s).
#
# The librarian behind her desk and a girl reading at the shelves, who both have something else to say once Lake Valor
# has blown (FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED). Lucian of the Elite Four reads at the shelves after the Hall of Fame (plan 02 ·
# S15, part a; FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_1F_LUCIAN, set from the start by common.ChapterSeven and cleared by every
# victory at the League, PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.Victory): spoken to, he says which of five books he has in hand
# (the original's roll of five), that he will see the player at the League, and goes out by the door.


script Receptionist
  speaker "Librarian"
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  say "Welcome to the Canalave Library. Three floors of books, and the oldest stories are at the top."
  say "Please keep your voice down while you're with us."
  end
label After
  say "Please keep your voice... Oh! That shriek just now? That wasn't me. It really wasn't!"
  end


script Lass
  speaker "Lass"
  if flag FLAG_LAKE_VALOR_EXPLODED goto After
  say "......"
  face self player
  say "Shh! I'm in the middle of a chapter. Please don't disturb me."
  face self up
  end
label After
  face self player
  say "I can't take in a single word any more. That tremor has left me all in a flutter!"
  end


# CanalaveLibrary1F_Lucian
script Lucian
  scene "Lucian in the library"
  face self player
  speaker "Lucian"
  say "Ah, the new Champion. Forgive me, I was miles away. Aren't books wonderful? An hour in here and I forget there is a League at all."
  random 5
  if result == 0 goto Letters
  if result == 1 goto Humankind
  if result == 2 goto Observations
  if result == 3 goto Essays
  say "This one is a book of philosophy. It asks what a Pokémon and a person owe one another, and has the grace not to answer."
  goto Leaves
label Letters
  say "This one is a bundle of letters, written long ago by someone who crossed Sinnoh on foot. Every page smells of rain."
  goto Leaves
label Humankind
  say "This one is a learned tome on humankind: why we gather, why we quarrel, why we keep Pokémon by us through it all."
  goto Leaves
label Observations
  say "This one is a record of wild Pokémon, watched for years by somebody too patient ever to catch one."
  goto Leaves
label Essays
  say "This one is a collection of essays on worlds that might be. Fancies, most of them. The best fancies are half true."
label Leaves
  say "But I have read long enough. Come and challenge us again, Champion. I shall be waiting at the Pokémon League."
  # He goes round the player to the door (CanalaveLibrary1F_LucianLeaveNorth, West and East)
  if facing right goto East
  walk self left 5 down 6
  goto Out
label East
  walk self down 1 left 5 down 5
label Out
  sound "stairs"
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_CANALAVE_LIBRARY_1F_LUCIAN
  endscene
  end
