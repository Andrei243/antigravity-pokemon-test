# The P2 Laboratory's hall (plan 22 · U10, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own. Black 2
# leaves the lab empty; Genesect is ours (plan 29 · LS11d, the user's answer 10a: in the P2 Laboratory once U10 opens it),
# met by the recipe's five beats on the "lightning" row, and the researcher of the hall gives its Shock Drive afterwards.


# Genesect at the back of the hall (ours: the recipe's lightning row, two bursts 0.4 s apart with a shake each): level 65, as
# Unova's other legends after the League; caught or beaten it is gone for good (FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_GENESECT), run from it is
# back the next time the hall is come to
script Genesect
  scene "Genesect in the P2 Laboratory"
  music stop
  face player self
  face self player
  letterbox on 0.4
  # (one tile south of it, the player would hide it: they step back first)
  if var PLAYER_X != 5 goto Look
  if var PLAYER_Y != 3 goto Look
  emote player exclaim
  walk player down 2
  face player up
label Look
  camera pan 5 2 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  effect burst 5 2
  camera shake 0.4
  wait 0.4
  effect burst 5 2
  camera shake 0.4
  cry "Genesect"
  emote player exclaim
  speaker none
  text "Sparks race along the old pod's casing, and the Pokémon inside it unfolds!\nIts cannon swings round to lock on to {player}!"
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  endscene
  wildbattle "Genesect" 65
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_GENESECT
  hide self
  text "The pod stands open and empty, still humming faintly..."
  end
label Fled
  hide self
  text "Genesect blasted off through the hall's broken skylight..."
  end


# The researcher by the window: what was made here, and the Shock Drive for whoever has met it
script Scientist
  face self player
  speaker "Scientist"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_SHOCK_DRIVE goto Given
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_FOUGHT_GENESECT goto Give
  say "Team Plasma rebuilt an ancient Pokémon here. They called it a weapon. I call it a crime."
  say "It still sleeps in its pod at the back. I don't dare go near it."
  end
label Give
  say "You faced Genesect? Then you should have this. It was made to fit the cannon on its back."
  give "Shock Drive"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_SHOCK_DRIVE
  say "The other drives were taken away when Team Plasma left. Where to, nobody knows."
  end
label Given
  say "A Pokémon made to be a weapon, set free at last. Treat it kindly."
  end
