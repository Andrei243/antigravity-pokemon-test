# Verdanturf's Battle Tent (plan 21 · H3's last part). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# beats of Emerald's (data/maps/VerdanturfTown_BattleTentLobby/scripts.inc): a round of three Trainers on the Battle Palace's
# rules, every Pokémon choosing its moves by its nature, and a Nest Ball for the three won.
#
# The round is the Palace's venue in a tent (Models/Frontier/BattleVenue.cs, `venue tent verdanturf`): the three entered go
# in as copies at their own levels, made whole for every battle, and the Trainers' are at the highest of them, never under
# thirty as Hoenn's levels map it. The attendant walks the player through the corridor
# (verdanturf_town_battle_tent_corridor.txt) to the battle room, whose arrival script is the round.


script Attendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Verdanturf Battle Tent! Here, your Pokémon battle by their own judgement."
label Ask
  choose "Would you like to take the Battle Tent challenge?" "Enter" "The rules" "No"
  if result == 1 goto Rules
  if result == 2 goto Later
  venue enough
  if result == 1 goto Choose
  say "You need three Pokémon that can take part, all of different species and holding different items."
  say "An Egg can't take part, and nor can {banned}."
  end
label Choose
  say "Please choose the three Pokémon you'll enter."
  venue choose
  if result == 0 goto Later
  venue check
  if result == 1 goto SameSpecies
  if result == 2 goto SameItem
  venue tent verdanturf
  say "Thank you! Three Trainers are waiting for you. Please follow me."
  walk self up 3
  fade out 0.6
  warp "VerdanturfTown_BattleTentCorridor" 2 7 up
  fade in
  end
label SameSpecies
  say "I'm sorry. The three entered must all be of different species."
  goto Later
label SameItem
  say "I'm sorry. No two of the three entered may hold the same item."
label Later
  say "Please come again. Your Pokémon are welcome to show us their hearts."
  end
label Rules
  say "You give no orders here. Each Pokémon picks its own moves, the way its nature leans."
  say "Your three keep their own levels, and they're made whole after every battle."
  say "Beat three Trainers in a row and a prize is yours. Lose once, and the challenge ends."
  goto Ask


# The girl who hands out Attract (Emerald's AttractGiver, FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_ATTRACT, TM45)
script AttractGiver
  face self player
  speaker "Girl"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_ATTRACT goto Given
  say "A Pokémon's feelings can run deeper than any move. Here, I want you to have this."
  give "TM45"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_ATTRACT
label Given
  say "Attract can leave a foe too smitten to fight. Feelings go both ways, though!"
  end
