# Lumiose City (plan 23 · Z2). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Lumiose City and Route 4, drawn by hand"): the professor's two assistants meet the
# player at the south gate and send them up the avenue to the laboratory (LumioseCity_SycamoreLab.txt), the professor
# battles and gives one of Kanto's first three, and then takes the player to Lysandre's café in the north
# (LumioseCity_LysandreCafe.txt). VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 9, the Bug Badge won, becomes 10 at the
# gate, 11 in the laboratory and 12 in the café.
#
# The people: sina and dexio (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_ASSISTANTS_LUMIOSE once their scene is over). The roads out of the city
# wait for later sessions: VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS is 1 once Lysandre's café is heard (LumioseCity_LysandreCafe.txt),
# which opens the road west to Route 5 (plan 23 · Z2, second part), and 2 once the Power Plant is freed
# (KalosPowerPlant.txt), which opens the road north to Route 14 (plan 23 · Z4); the others keep turning the player back.


# A save from before this part of the story was written finds the people of Lumiose's part of the story as a new game does
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_LUMIOSE_BEGUN call KalosLumiose
  # Plan 23 · Z4b: a game saved after the Hall of Fame, before its last two roads were opened (the east gate to Lumiose
  # Station, the south-east one to Azure Bay: VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 3), finds them open
  if flag KalosHallOfFame goto Champion
  # Plan 23 · Z2, second part: a game saved after Lysandre's café, before the road west was opened, finds it open
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 12 end
  if var VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS == 0 setvar VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 1
  end
label Champion
  if var VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS < 3 setvar VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 3
  end


# The Prism Tower's lift operator at the foot of its south face (plan 23 · Z4): the lift has no power until the Power
# Plant is freed (KalosPowerPlant.txt, VAR_KALOS_STORY 24), then it takes the player up to Clemont's Gym
script PrismTower
  speaker "Lift Operator"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 24 goto Dark
  say "The power's back! Up you go: the Gym is at the very top of the tower."
  warp "LumioseCity_PrismTowerGym" 6 16 up
  end
label Dark
  say "Sorry, the lift has no power. The whole city's gone dark, and the tower with it."
  end


# Into the city from Route 4 (the trigger across the south gate, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 9): the professor's assistants
script Assistants
  scene "The professor's assistants"
  face sina player
  face dexio player
  speaker "Sina"
  say "There you are! You must be {player}. We've been watching the gate all morning!"
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "Welcome to Lumiose City. We assist Professor Sycamore with his research. I'm Dexio, and this is Sina."
  speaker "Sina"
  say "The professor is waiting for you at his laboratory. It's just up this avenue, on the right. You can't miss it!"
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "We'll go ahead and tell him you've arrived. Don't get lost among the shops, now."
  walk sina up 4 fast
  walk dexio up 4 fast
  hide sina
  hide dexio
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_ASSISTANTS_LUMIOSE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 10
  objective kalosLab
  endscene
  end

script Sina
  speaker "Sina"
  say "The professor's laboratory is up the avenue on the right!"
  end

script Dexio
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "Lumiose is a big city. The avenues all lead back to the tower, if you ever lose your way."
  end

# Plan 23 · Z4b, Looker's second case (LumioseCity_LookerBureau.txt, VAR_KALOS_LOOKER 2): the children who play at battles
# in the little park south of the bureau, and Emma, whose Espurr has run off (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_ALLEY until
# FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_ALLEY_DONE). Each child battles once (a flag of their own, counted by VAR_KALOS_ALLEY); with all four
# beaten, Emma asks where her Espurr went
script AlleyNanette
  face self player
  speaker "Nanette"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_NANETTE goto Played
  say "You're the Champion? Then you have to play with me! That's the rule of the park!"
  battle self as "kalos_preschooler_nanette"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_NANETTE
  addvar VAR_KALOS_ALLEY 1
  say "Aww. Okay, you can play again tomorrow. That's the rule too."
  end
label Played
  say "The girl with the pink hair? She's looking for her cat. It's a very grumpy cat."
  end

script AlleyLou
  face self player
  speaker "Lou"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_LOU goto Played
  say "I'm guarding the park! Nobody gets past without a battle!"
  battle self as "kalos_preschooler_lou"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_LOU
  addvar VAR_KALOS_ALLEY 1
  say "Okay, you can get past. But only a little bit."
  end
label Played
  say "My Pokémon are sleepy now. Shh."
  end

script AlleyCerise
  face self player
  speaker "Cerise"

  setflag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_CERISE

  say "Okay, that wasn't fair. You're the Champion, aren't you?"
  end

  end

script AlleyBastien
  face self player
  speaker "Bastien"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_BASTIEN goto Played
  say "My Pokémon is very old and very spooky. Are you scared? You should be!"
  battle self as "kalos_school_kid_bastien"
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_ALLEY_BASTIEN
  addvar VAR_KALOS_ALLEY 1
  say "Not scared at all? Not even a little?"
  end

  end

script AlleyEmma
  face self player
  speaker "Emma"
  if var VAR_KALOS_ALLEY < 4 goto Kids
  scene "Emma and Mimi"
  say "You battled all four of them? The noise must have scared Mimi half to death. My Espurr, I mean. She's run off."
  say "She hides somewhere small and dark when she's frightened. There are three alleys near here she likes."
label Where
  choose "Where would a frightened Espurr hide?" "The alley beside the bureau" "The alley beside the museum" "The street by the restaurant"
  if result == 0 goto Found
  say "No, she wouldn't go that far. Somewhere close, and quiet."
  goto Where
label Found
  speaker none
  text "{player} looked into the narrow alley beside the bureau. Two wide eyes stared back out of the dark."
  text "{player} kept very still. After a while a small grey Pokémon crept out and sniffed {player}'s hand."
  speaker "Emma"
  say "Mimi! Oh, thank you! Nobody's ever been that patient with her. Not even me."
  say "You work with the detective, don't you? He asked me to help him. I said no, because of Mimi."
  say "But if Mimi likes you, maybe she'll like the bureau too. Tell him Emma's coming. Both of us."
  hide alley_nanette
  hide alley_lou
  hide alley_cerise
  hide alley_bastien
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_ALLEY_DONE
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_EMMA_BUREAU
  endscene
  end
label Kids
  say "Shh! My Espurr's hiding around here somewhere, and those children won't stop battling. Can you quiet them down?"
  end


# Plan 23 · Z4b, Looker's fourth case: once the museum's curator has told of the thefts (FLAG_KALOS_LOOKER_STING), three

# The Open Door Agency's case in Kalos: the guard by the museum shows the frame's screws worn bright. The porter and the
# painter are in Kiloude City (kiloude_city.txt).

script CaseKalosGuard
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLOSED goto Thanks
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_TAKEN goto Case
  say "I guard the museum by night. Nothing ever goes missing on my watch. Well. Nothing that isn't back by morning."
  end
label Case
  if flag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLUE_SCREWS goto Again
  say "The empty frame? It's still on the wall, look. Nobody moves it. But the screws on its back are worn bright."
  say "Screws only shine like that when they're turned, and turned often. Somebody takes the canvas out every night."
  setflag FLAG_CASE_KALOS_CLUE_SCREWS
  text "{player} noted the frame's screws, worn bright from turning."
  end
label Again
  say "Bright screws on an old frame. The canvas comes out, and the frame stays put."
  end
label Thanks
  say "The painting stays on its wall all night now. My watch has never been so dull. Or so peaceful."
  end
