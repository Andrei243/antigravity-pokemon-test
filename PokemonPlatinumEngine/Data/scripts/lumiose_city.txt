# Lumiose City (plan 23 · Z2). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Lumiose City and Route 4, drawn by hand"): the professor's two assistants meet the
# player at the south gate and send them up the avenue to the laboratory (LumioseCity_SycamoreLab.txt), the professor
# battles and gives one of Kanto's first three, and then takes the player to Lysandre's café in the north
# (LumioseCity_LysandreCafe.txt). VAR_KALOS_STORY is vaniville_town.txt's count: 9, the Bug Badge won, becomes 10 at the
# gate, 11 in the laboratory and 12 in the café.
#
# The people: sina and dexio (FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_ASSISTANTS_LUMIOSE once their scene is over). The roads out of the city
# wait for later sessions: VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS is 1 once Lysandre's café is heard (LumioseCity_LysandreCafe.txt),
# which opens the road west to Route 5 (plan 23 · Z2, second part), and 2 once the Power Plant is freed
# (KalosPowerPlant.txt), which opens the road north to Route 14 (plan 23 · Z4); the others keep turning the player back.


# A save from before this part of the story was written finds the people of Lumiose's part of the story as a new game does
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_LUMIOSE_BEGUN call KalosLumiose
  # Plan 23 · Z2, second part: a game saved after Lysandre's café, before the road west was opened, finds it open
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 12 end
  if var VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS == 0 setvar VAR_KALOS_LUMIOSE_ROADS 1
  end


# The Prism Tower's lift operator at the foot of its south face (plan 23 · Z4): the lift has no power until the Power
# Plant is freed (KalosPowerPlant.txt, VAR_KALOS_STORY 24), then it takes the player up to Clemont's Gym
script PrismTower
  speaker "Lift Operator"
  if var VAR_KALOS_STORY < 24 goto Dark
  say "The power's back! Up you go: the Gym is at the very top of the tower."
  warp "LumioseCity_PrismTowerGym" 6 16 up
  end
label Dark
  say "Sorry, the lift has no power. The whole city's gone dark, and the tower with it."
  end


# Into the city from Route 4 (the trigger across the south gate, on VAR_KALOS_STORY 9): the professor's assistants
script Assistants
  scene "The professor's assistants"
  face sina player
  face dexio player
  speaker "Sina"
  say "There you are! You must be {player}. We've been watching the gate all morning!"
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "Welcome to Lumiose City. We assist Professor Sycamore with his research. I'm Dexio, and this is Sina."
  speaker "Sina"
  say "The professor is waiting for you at his laboratory. It's just up this avenue, on the right. You can't miss it!"
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "We'll go ahead and tell him you've arrived. Don't get lost among the shops, now."
  walk sina up 4 fast
  walk dexio up 4 fast
  hide sina
  hide dexio
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_HIDE_ASSISTANTS_LUMIOSE
  setvar VAR_KALOS_STORY 10
  objective kalosLab
  endscene
  end

script Sina
  speaker "Sina"
  say "The professor's laboratory is up the avenue on the right!"
  end

script Dexio
  speaker "Dexio"
  say "Lumiose is a big city. The avenues all lead back to the tower, if you ever lose your way."
  end


# The roads out of the city, which later sessions open (plan 23 · Z2's Routes 5 to 7 and Z4's)
script NotYetNorth
  text "The road beyond is closed for repairs. It will open again soon."
  walk player down 1
  end

script NotYetSouth
  text "The road beyond is closed for repairs. It will open again soon."
  walk player up 1
  end

script NotYetWest
  text "The road to Route 5 is closed for repairs. It will open again soon."
  walk player right 1
  end

script NotYetEast
  text "The road beyond is closed for repairs. It will open again soon."
  walk player left 1
  end
