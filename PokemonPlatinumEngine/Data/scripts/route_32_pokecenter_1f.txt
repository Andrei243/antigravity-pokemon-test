# Route 32's Pokémon Center (plan 20 · J4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_R32PC0101): the fisherman by the window gives the Old Rod to whoever wants to fish.


script Fisherman
  speaker "Fishing Guru"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_OLD_ROD goto After
  say "This is a fine stretch of water for fishing. Do you fish? No? Then you're missing out!"
  ask "Would you like a rod of your own?"
  if no goto Refused
  say "That's the spirit! Here you are, my old rod. It's caught many a Magikarp in its day."
  give "Old Rod"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_OLD_ROD
  say "Face the water and use it from your bag. Fishing is all about patience!"
  end
label Refused
  say "Oh. Well, the fish will still be here if you change your mind."
  end
label After
  say "How's the fishing? Remember: patience, patience, and more patience."
  end
