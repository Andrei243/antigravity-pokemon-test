# The Safari Zone Warden's house in Fuchsia City (plan 19 · K6, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the
# lines are our own, on FireRed's beats (data/maps/FuchsiaCity_WardensHouse/scripts.inc).
#
# The Warden has lost his false teeth in the Safari Zone, and nobody can make out a word he says: he asks a question
# that is no question and mumbles back whatever the answer. With the Gold Teeth in the bag (found in the zone's west
# part, safari_zone_west's item ball) he takes them, puts them in and gives HM04, Strength (FLAG_GOT_HM04).


script Warden
  face old_man_2 player
  speaker "Warden"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_HM04 goto Strength
  if item "Gold Teeth" goto Teeth
  ask "Hif fuff hefifoo! Ha lof ha feef ee hafahi ho. Heff hee fwee!"
  if yes goto Yes
  say "Heh? Oh hoo. Hee hah hee ho hah..."
  end
label Yes
  say "Ah, howhee! Ho hoo hah! Hee hee!"
  end
label Teeth
  scene "The Gold Teeth"
  speaker none
  take "Gold Teeth"
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} gave the Gold Teeth to the Warden!"
  text "The Warden popped in his teeth."
  speaker "Warden"
  if girl say "Thanks, young lady! You're a real treasure. Nobody understood a word I said all week!"
  if boy say "Thanks, young man! You're a real treasure. Nobody understood a word I said all week!"
  say "Here, take this. It's the least I can do."
  give "HM04"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_HM04
  endscene
label Strength
  say "HM04 is Strength. A Pokémon that knows it can push big boulders out of the way, once you have the Rainbow Badge."
  end
