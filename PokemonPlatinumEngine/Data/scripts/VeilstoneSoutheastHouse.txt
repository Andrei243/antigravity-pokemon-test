# The clown spins a coin and asks which hand it is in. The answer makes no difference: half the time the player
# wins the Coin Case (FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_CITY_SOUTHEAST_HOUSE_COIN_CASE), as the original's GetRandom decides.


script Clown
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_CITY_SOUTHEAST_HOUSE_COIN_CASE goto Tearful
  say "Watch this coin. Watch it closely!" "And... spin!"
  face self left
  wait 0.13
  face self up
  wait 0.13
  face self right
  wait 0.13
  face self down
  wait 0.13
  choose "Now then! Which hand is the coin in?" "The right hand" "The left hand"
  gamecorner cointrick
  if result == 0 goto Wrong
  say "Right you are! And for our winner: a case to keep coins in!"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_VEILSTONE_CITY_SOUTHEAST_HOUSE_COIN_CASE
  give "Coin Case"
  end
label Wrong
  say "Wrong! What a poor show. The other hand, it was. Come, try again!"
  end
label Tearful
  say "I gave away my case, and now I've nowhere to keep my coin. I am a tearful clown."
  end
