# Brooklet Hill, Akala Island: Captain Lana's trial (plan 23 · Z5, second part). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's
# beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Akala, as far as Brooklet Hill"): Lana registers
# Lapras in the Ride Pager, the splashes on the first lake's shores are Wishiwashi, and the totem, a Wishiwashi that
# schools as it battles, waits on the far shore of the second lake. Ours: the totem is met as a wild battle that can't
# be run from; its aura and its allies are plan 23 · Z6's. VAR_ALOLA_STORY is alola_route_1.txt's count.
#
# The people: lana at the way in until the trial is passed (FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_TRIAL_DONE); the two splashes and the
# totem shown by FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_TRIAL while the trial is under way.


script Lana
  if var VAR_ALOLA_STORY >= 17 goto Begin
  speaker "Lana"
  say "...Hello. I'm Lana, the captain here. My trial is ready, but you look like you've somewhere else to be first. Route 5, maybe?"
  end
label Begin
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_TRIAL goto During
  scene "Lana's trial begins"
  speaker "Lana"
  say "...Hello. I'm Lana. This is Brooklet Hill, and my trial."
  say "Something has been splashing in the lakes. Something big. Would you find out what it is?"
  say "You'll need to cross the water. Here, I'll register my Lapras in your Ride Pager. It's a very good swimmer."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_LAPRAS
  text "{player} registered Lapras in the Ride Pager! Lapras can carry you across water."
  say "Look for the splashes on the shore of the first lake, then the far shore of the second. I'll be watching."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_TRIAL
  endscene
  end
label During
  speaker "Lana"
  say "The splashes are on the first lake's shores. Then cross the second lake to the far shore."
  end


script Splash1
  text "Something splashed! A Wishiwashi leapt out!"
  wildbattle "Wishiwashi" 17 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_SPLASH_1
  hide self
  end

script Splash2
  text "Something splashed! A Wishiwashi leapt out!"
  wildbattle "Wishiwashi" 18 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_SPLASH_2
  hide self
  end


script Totem
  speaker none
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_SPLASH_1 goto NotYet
  if not flag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_SPLASH_2 goto NotYet
  scene "The totem Wishiwashi"
  text "A little Wishiwashi peeked out of the shallows... then hundreds more rose up around it!"
  cry "Wishiwashi"
  wildbattle "Wishiwashi" 20 nofleeing
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_BROOKLET_TRIAL_DONE
  hide self
  hide lana
  speaker "Lana"
  say "...You did it. You passed my trial. The whole school, all at once!"
  say "This is the Waterium Z. With it, a Water-type move becomes a Z-Move. Keep Lapras too; you'll need it on Akala."
  give "Waterium Z"
  give "Dive Ball" 10
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_STORY 18
  objective alolaFree
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  text "The water by the far shore ripples strangely... Perhaps the splashes on the first lake come first."
  end
