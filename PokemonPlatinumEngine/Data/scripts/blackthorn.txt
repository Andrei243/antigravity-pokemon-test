# Blackthorn City (plan 20 · J11, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T30).
#
# Two pairs of men stand at two doors, one of each pair before the door and one beside it, HeartGold's own objects:
# the Gym's (FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_GYM_GUARD_INFRONT, set once Team Rocket is out of the Radio Tower,
# goldenrod_radio_tower_observation_deck.txt, which also lets the one beside it show) and the Dragon's Den's
# (FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_DEN_GUARD_INFRONT, set as Clair is beaten, blackthorn_gym.txt; HeartGold spells the other's
# flag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHRON_DEN_GUARD_ASIDE). HeartGold hides the two beside the doors as a new game begins; here the
# city's own arrival does, until what moves them aside has happened, so no save needs a step. And once Clair has
# given the Rising Badge in the shrine (VAR_UNK_40C4 1), she waits in the Dragon's Den to give TM59; coming back to
# the city without meeting her sends her home (HeartGold's T30_005).


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_BEAT_RADIO_TOWER_ROCKETS setflag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHORN_GYM_GUARD_ASIDE
  if not flag FLAG_UNK_0D1 setflag FLAG_HIDE_BLACKTHRON_DEN_GUARD_ASIDE
  if var VAR_UNK_40C4 != 1 end
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VICTORY_ROAD_CLAIR
  setvar VAR_UNK_40C4 2
  end


# T30_000: the man before the Gym's door
script GymGuard
  face t30_gsman1 player
  speaker "Man"
  say "I'm sorry. Clair, our Leader, has gone into the Dragon's Den behind the Gym, and nobody knows when she'll be back."
  end


# T30_001: the man beside it once the way is open
script GymGuardAside
  face t30_gsman1_2 player
  speaker "Man"
  if flag FLAG_UNK_0D1 goto Beaten
  say "Clair is waiting for challengers. Just don't expect an ordinary trainer to stand a chance against her."
  end
label Beaten
  say "You beat Clair? That's incredible! I've never heard of anyone doing that except Lance."
  end


# T30_002: the old man before the Dragon's Den
script DenGuard
  face t30_gsoldman1 player
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "No, no. Only trainers who have been chosen may go into the Den. Please turn back."
  end


# T30_003: the old man beside it once Clair has been beaten
script DenGuardAside
  face t30_gsoldman1_2 player
  speaker "Old Man"
  say "If Clair agrees to it, so will her grandfather, our elder. Go on in."
  end
