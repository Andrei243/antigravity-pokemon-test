# Konikoni City, Akala Island (plan 23 · Z6). The lines are our own, on Sun and Moon's beats as they are publicly
# described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "The rest of Akala: trials, totems and Olivia's grand trial"): Kahuna Olivia
# meets the player at the city's gate and sends them to the Ruins of Life, then holds Akala's grand trial. Ours: the
# grand trial is fought in the street before her shop. VAR_ALOLA_AKALA is royal_avenue.txt's count.
#
# The people: olivia before her shop, always. Her team is alola_olivia_1, her ace holding the Rockium Z.


script OliviaMeets
  scene "Olivia at Konikoni's gate"
  speaker kahuna_olivia "Olivia"
  say "Alola! You must be the trial-goer Mallow keeps messaging me about. I'm Olivia, kahuna of Akala."
  say "Before my grand trial, go up to the Ruins of Life past Memorial Hill. Your friend Lillie is waiting there."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_OLIVIA_MET
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_AKALA 5
  objective alolaRuins
  endscene
  end


script Olivia
  if var VAR_ALOLA_AKALA >= 7 goto After
  if var VAR_ALOLA_AKALA >= 6 goto GrandTrial
  speaker kahuna_olivia "Olivia"
  if var VAR_ALOLA_AKALA >= 5 goto Ruins
  say "Welcome to Konikoni! Mind the jewelry. It's all for sale."
  end
label Ruins
  say "The Ruins of Life are past Memorial Hill, east of Route 9. Lillie's waiting, so off you go!"
  end
label GrandTrial
  scene "Olivia's grand trial"
  speaker kahuna_olivia "Olivia"
  say "You've been to the guardian's shrine. Then you're ready. I'm Akala's kahuna, and I don't do anything halfway!"
  battle olivia as "alola_olivia_1"
  speaker kahuna_olivia "Olivia"
  say "Well! That was a gem of a battle. Akala's stamp is yours."
  givebadge Akala
  text "{player}'s Trainer card received Akala's stamp!"
  give "Rockium Z"
  say "And take Machamp along. With it in your Ride Pager, heavy boulders won't stand in your way."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_RIDE_MACHAMP
  text "{player} registered Machamp in the Ride Pager! Machamp can shove heavy boulders."
  say "Lillie and Hau went down to the Hano Grand Resort. Go south from Royal Avenue and say hello."
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_OLIVIA_DONE
  setvar VAR_ALOLA_AKALA 7
  objective alolaHano
  endscene
  end
label After
  speaker kahuna_olivia "Olivia"
  say "Two stamps down, two to go! The other kahunas won't go easy on you either."
  end
