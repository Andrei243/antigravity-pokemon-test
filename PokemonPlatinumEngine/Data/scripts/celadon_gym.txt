# The Celadon Gym as HeartGold has it (plan 20 · J15, part d). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T07GYM0101).
#
# Erika waits at the top of a garden whose ways three small trees close (Cut opens them, as anywhere). She battles for
# the Rainbow Badge, which is Kanto's (`givebadge rainbow of kanto`, as Lt. Surge's Thunder Badge and Sabrina's Marsh
# Badge: the rulings' "HeartGold's Kanto, part b" to "part d"), counts her four trainers beaten and gives TM19, Giga
# Drain. A trainer who already holds the Badge from FireRed's Kanto battles her all the same: whether she has been
# beaten here is FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_ERIKA, a flag of ours.


script Erika
  face t07gym0101_gsleader12 player
  speaker "Erika"
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_ERIKA goto Won
  scene "Erika"
  say "Oh... good afternoon. I must have dozed off among the flowers. The warm air in here is so very soothing."
  say "A Champion from Johto, you say? How lovely. I teach flower arranging, but my Grass Pokémon are no mere decoration."
  say "If you wish for my Badge, you shall have to earn it. I shan't hold back."
  battle self as "johto_leader_erika_erika"
  say "Oh my... I concede. You are every bit as strong as they say. Please, accept the Rainbow Badge."
  givebadge rainbow of kanto
  text "{player} received the Rainbow Badge from Erika!"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_ERIKA
  defeat "johto_twins_jo_and_zoe"
  defeat "johto_lass_michelle"
  defeat "johto_picnicker_tanya"
  defeat "johto_beauty_julia"
  endscene
label Tm
  say "And this, if you would. It holds a move my Pokémon are fond of."
  give "TM19"
  setflag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM19_FROM_ERIKA
  say "TM19 is Giga Drain. It drains the foe's strength and gives half of it back to the user."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_JOHTO_GOT_TM19_FROM_ERIKA goto Tm
  say "Losing to someone so strong is rather refreshing. I feel I could arrange a whole garden of flowers today."
  end


# The statues by the door.
script Statue
  if flag FLAG_JOHTO_DEFEATED_ERIKA goto Won
  text "Celadon City Pokémon Gym\nLeader: Erika"
  end
label Won
  text "Celadon City Pokémon Gym\nLeader: Erika\nWinning trainers: {player}"
  end
