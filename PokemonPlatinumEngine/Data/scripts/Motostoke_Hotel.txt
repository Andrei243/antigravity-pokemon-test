# Motostoke's hotel (plan 33 · GL4): Team Yell crowding the counter on the night before the ceremony, sent off by
# Chairman Rose, and a night's rest. VAR_GALAR_STORY 15 to 16 (postwick.txt's count). Our own lines.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALAR_STORY != 15 end
  scene "Team Yell at the hotel"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_YELL_AT_HOTEL
  speaker "Team Yell Grunt"
  say "Oi! Another challenger? Team Yell's backing Marnie, and Marnie only. You'd best go home!"
  say "We're not moving from this counter till every room's ours. Yell, yell, yell!"
  setflag FLAG_GALAR_ROSE_AT_HOTEL
  speaker "Chairman Rose"
  say "Now then! The gym challenge is a celebration. I won't have challengers kept from their rest by shouting."
  speaker "Team Yell Grunt"
  say "The chairman? Right, we're off. This isn't over!"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_YELL_AT_HOTEL
  face rose_hotel player
  speaker "Chairman Rose"
  say "My apologies for that. Rose, chairman of the Pokémon League. And you must be one of Leon's endorsed challengers."
  say "Rest well. Tomorrow the whole of Galar will be watching you. I look forward to the show!"
  clearflag FLAG_GALAR_ROSE_AT_HOTEL
  fade out
  text "{player} checked in and slept soundly. The morning of the opening ceremony came bright and loud."
  setvar VAR_GALAR_STORY 16
  objective galarCeremony
  fade in
  endscene
  end

  end
