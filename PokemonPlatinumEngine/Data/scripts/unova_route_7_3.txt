# The old man (1) tells of Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus and shows the Pokédex all three (once for the telling,
# FLAG_UNOVA_LANDORUS_TALE); the nursery aide (3) heals the team whenever asked.


script LandorusTale
  face 1 player
  speaker "Old Man"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_LANDORUS_TALE goto Told
  say "Long ago I was a sharp-looking young man, and my wife was a fine-looking girl. This is a story from those days."
  say "Right around here, a certain Pokémon once put Tornadus and Thundurus in their place, after they'd stirred up storms and thunder across the whole region."
  say "I'll show you a picture of each. This is Tornadus!"
  seen "Tornadus"
  say "And this is Thundurus!"
  seen "Thundurus"
  text "Tornadus and Thundurus have been registered in the Pokédex."
  say "The Pokémon that quieted the pair is known as Landorus. Feast your eyes!"
  seen "Landorus"
  text "Landorus has been registered in the Pokédex."
  say "I heard there's a shrine to Landorus somewhere along Route 14. Maybe you'll go and see it."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_LANDORUS_TALE
  end
label Told
  say "Tornadus and Thundurus were wild, and Landorus brought them back to order. A good story, I always think."
  end


script RestForTheTeam
  face 3 player
  speaker "Nursery Aide"
  say "Oh, dear. Your team looks a bit sluggish. Rest for just a moment!"
  heal
  fanfare heal
  say "There! All better. Walking the raised walkways is more tiring than you'd think, isn't it?"
  end
