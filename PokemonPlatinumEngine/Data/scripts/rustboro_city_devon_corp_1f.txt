# Devon Corporation's lobby (plan 21 · H2, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/RustboroCity_DevonCorp_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Until the Devon Goods are back (FLAG_RETURNED_DEVON_GOODS) the guard stands before the stairs, which only staff may
# take; afterwards he goes back to his round of the floor.


script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_RETURNED_DEVON_GOODS goto Round
  place devon_corp_stair_guard 14 2 down
  end
label Round
  place devon_corp_stair_guard 15 5 down
  end


script StairGuard
  if flag FLAG_RETURNED_DEVON_GOODS goto Welcome
  if flag FLAG_DEVON_GOODS_STOLEN goto Stolen
  say "I'm sorry, the floors upstairs are for Devon staff only."
  end
label Stolen
  say "Something's been stolen? Oh dear. I can't leave my post, though. Nobody goes upstairs without permission!"
  end
label Welcome
  say "Our president is expecting you any time. Go right on up!"
  end
