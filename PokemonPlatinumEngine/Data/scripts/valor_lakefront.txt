# Valor Lakefront (plan 02 · S8). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's
# beats (scripts_valor_lakefront.s).
#
# The grunt who carried the Galactic Bomb has run here from Route 213 (route_213.txt clears his flag). Caught talking
# to himself, he runs up the road toward the lake; caught there, he battles, and gives up. Then Cynthia comes down
# from the lake, which is closed to visitors (its scene is plan 02 · S10's), and the rival runs up after the grunt and
# off again; Cynthia asks about the Psyduck on Route 210 and gives the Secret Potion for them (route_210_south.txt).
# Crasher Wake is back in his Gym and the rival gone from Pastoria (VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 6).
#
# The road east to Sunyshore City is closed after the city's blackout: a man turns the player back (the original's
# trigger 0, VAR_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_BLOCK_SUNYSHORE_STATE 0) until the professor's lab sees to it (plan 02 · S13).


# The original's OnTransition: the grunt by the road to the lake once he has run from the player.
script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_GRUNT_M place grunt_m 723 769 up
  end


# The grunt (the original's script 3).
script Grunt
  speaker "Galactic Grunt"
  if flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_GRUNT_M goto Cornered
  say "Gasp... wheeze... Remind me why I'm doing all this running?"
  say "Oh, right. The brand-new world the boss has promised us... A whole universe made over, just for us..."
  say "And once this thing's been set off..."
  face self player
  emote self exclaim
  say "Not you AGAIN?! Can't you see I'm busy having private thoughts?!"
  say "You just don't quit, do you? I'm completely worn out... but I'm still not... battling... you..."
  if facing down goto RoundThePlayer
  walk self up 10 fast
  goto Moved
label RoundThePlayer
  walk self right 1 up 11 fast
label Moved
  place self 723 769 up
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_GRUNT_M
  end
label Cornered
  say "Ugh... still behind me? And with the lake so close I can almost touch it..."
  face self player
  say "Fine! Have it your way! My Pokémon will knock you flat!"
  battle self as "galactic_grunt_valor_lakefront"
  speaker "Galactic Grunt"
  say "That's it... I've got nothing left... not one more step..."
  say "The Commander can have this thing, then. I really wanted to be the one who used it at the lake..."
  if facing down goto Round
  walk self up 10
  goto Gone
label Round
  walk self right 1 up 9
label Gone
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_GRUNT_M
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GRAND_LAKE_ROUTE_213_LOBBY_LOOKER
  clearflag FLAG_BLOCK_PASTORIA_CITY_CROAGUNK_EVENT
  # Cynthia comes down from the lake to where the grunt stood
  if facing up place cynthia 723 761 down
  if facing down place cynthia 723 759 down
  if facing left place cynthia 724 760 down
  if facing right place cynthia 722 760 down
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_CYNTHIA
  show cynthia
  walk cynthia down 4
  face cynthia up
  wait 0.6
  walk cynthia down 4
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Why, it's you! We meet again. Has your Pokédex grown since I saw you last?"
  say "I came hoping to study the tales told about this lake. But it seems nobody's being let near it at the moment."
  say "Do you know the story? People say an island lies out in the middle of the water..."
  say "...and on it a Pokémon that shimmers like a mirage. Its chamber is meant to be left alone, always."
  say "Oh, but that's not what I wanted to ask you about..."
  # The rival runs up the road after the grunt
  if facing up place rival 724 780 up
  if facing down place rival 724 778 up
  if facing left place rival 723 779 up
  if facing right place rival 723 779 up
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_RIVAL
  show rival
  music "common/rival"
  walk rival up 10 fast
  if facing left face rival right
  if not facing left face rival left
  face player rival
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! Which way did that Galactic creep go?"
  face rival up
  say "Huh? Who's this? A relative of yours? ...No? Never mind, then."
  face rival player
  say "Oh, and Mr. Wake says the bang in the Great Marsh didn't do any real harm. He wanted you to know."
  say "But Team Galactic, honestly! They're going way too far. If they cross my path again, they'll be sorry!"
  say "Later!"
  face player up
  walk rival up 8 fast
  hide rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_RIVAL
  music area
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "A friend of yours? He's certainly in a hurry, isn't he?"
  face cynthia down
  face player up
  ask "Now, what I meant to ask... Have you come across the Psyduck on Route 210?"
  if no goto NotSeen
  say "Yes, those! The little crowd by the café on Route 210, all of them clutching their heads."
  goto Medicine
label NotSeen
  say "You haven't? Then look for them by the café on Route 210: a little crowd of Psyduck, all clutching their heads."
label Medicine
  say "Give them this medicine. It should ease their aching heads, and you'll want to see them for your Pokédex."
  give "Secret Potion"
  say "I travelled with a Pokédex of my own once, when I was about your age."
  say "Meet as many Pokémon as you can. Who knows? It may lead you to the mirage Pokémon's secret."
  say "Take care!"
  walk cynthia up 9
  hide cynthia
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_CYNTHIA
  # The rival leaves Pastoria's marsh door, and Crasher Wake is back in his Gym
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_PASTORIA_CITY_RIVAL
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_PASTORIA_CITY_GYM_CRASHER_WAKE
  setvar VAR_PASTORIA_CITY_STATE 6
  end


# The road to Sunyshore City (trigger 0): the man by it steps across and turns the player back.
script BlockSunyshore
  emote collector exclaim
  if var PLAYER_Y == 789 walk collector right 1 down 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 790 walk collector right 1 down 2
  if var PLAYER_Y == 791 walk collector right 1 down 3
  move collector left 1
  walk player left 1
  waitmoves
  face player right
  speaker "Collector"
  say "Hold it right there! You can't go this way."
  say "Sunyshore City's had a blackout, and the road's shut while the crews get the power back. Try again later!"
  if var PLAYER_Y == 789 walk collector up 1
  if var PLAYER_Y == 790 walk collector up 2
  if var PLAYER_Y == 791 walk collector up 3
  face collector down
  end
