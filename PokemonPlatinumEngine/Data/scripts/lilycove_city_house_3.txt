# Emerald's beats (data/maps/LilycoveCity_House3/scripts.inc): the mother of four tells what Pokéblocks are, if asked.


script PokeblockMother
  face self player
  speaker "Woman"
  ask "Would you like to learn about Pokéblocks from a master of the Berry Blender?"
  if no goto Declined
  say "Pokéblocks are treats made by blending berries. Each has a flavour that brings out a Pokémon's coolness, beauty, cuteness, smartness or toughness."
  say "A Pokémon that likes the flavour shines all the more on a contest stage. My four blend berries every day!"
  end
label Declined
  say "Oh, are you sure? Well, ask me any time."
  end
