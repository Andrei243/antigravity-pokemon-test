# Professor Birch's lab (plan 21 · H2). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/LittlerootTown_ProfessorBirchsLab/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE, as Emerald counts it: 2 the starter chosen on Route 101, 3 told of the rival on Route 103, 4 the
# rival beaten there, 5 the Pokédex had. Each scene is the lab's arrival.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE == 2 goto Thanks
  if var VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE == 4 goto Pokedex
  end
# In from the rescue: the Pokémon is the player's to keep, and the rival is out on Route 103
label Thanks
  scene "The professor's thanks"
  face birchs_lab_birch player
  speaker "Prof. Birch"
  say "So! I'm Birch, the Pokémon professor. And you're Norman's child, {player}, aren't you? I thought so!"
  say "I'd just got back from the field when that Zigzagoon came at me. You and that {starter} were wonderful."
  say "The way it listened to you... I'd say that {starter} has taken a shine to you already."
  say "Why don't you keep it? Consider it a thank-you from me."
  fanfare pokemon
  text "{player} received the {starter}!"
  say "You know, my child {rival} is out on Route 103 right now, north of Oldale Town, studying Pokémon."
  say "Why not go and see {rival}? I'm sure you two will get along. You might even learn a thing or two!"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_101_BOY
  setvar VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE 3
  endscene
  end
# Back from Route 103 (4): the Pokédex from the professor, and five Poké Balls from the rival
label Pokedex
  scene "The Pokedex given"
  face birchs_lab_birch player
  speaker "Prof. Birch"
  say "Ah, {player}! {rival} tells me you two had a battle. And you held your own, did you? Well done!"
  say "I've a feeling you've got what it takes to go far. So I'd like you to have this. It's my Pokédex."
  say "It records every Pokémon you meet. Fill it with as many as you can, and you'll be helping my research no end!"
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex from Professor Birch!"
  face birchs_lab_rival player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "Oh, wow! Dad gave you a Pokédex too? Then we're rivals now, {player}!"
  say "Here, you'll need these to catch Pokémon. I've got loads."
  give "Poké Ball" 5
  say "Weaken a wild Pokémon first, then throw the ball. It's easy once you get the hang of it!"
  say "I'm going to keep studying here for a bit. Go on, {player}, there's a whole region out there!"
  setflag FLAG_ADVENTURE_STARTED
  setvar VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE 5
  setvar VAR_OLDALE_TOWN_STATE 1
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_RIVAL_STATE 4
  setvar VAR_LITTLEROOT_TOWN_STATE 3
  endscene
  end


script Birch
  if flag HoennHallOfFame goto Champion
  if var VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE >= 5 goto Dex
  if var VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE >= 3 goto Rival
  say "Phew, what a morning! Thank you again, {player}."
  end
label Rival
  say "{rival} is on Route 103, north of Oldale Town. Go and say hello!"
  end
label Dex
  say "Pokémon live in every corner of Hoenn. Fill that Pokédex for me, {player}, and tell me all about them!"
  end
# The Champion back home (plan 21 · H9; Emerald's LittlerootTown_ProfessorBirchsLab_EventScript_UpgradeToNationalDex,
# which waits for the Hall of Fame and nothing else, as the region's file's nationalUnlock has it): the Pokédex made the
# National one, once
label Champion
  if nationaldex goto Wide
  scene "The National Pokedex"
  face birchs_lab_birch player
  say "{player}! The Champion of Hoenn, in my own lab! I've been waiting for you to come home."
  say "You've seen so much of Hoenn that I think it's time your Pokédex saw the rest of the world too."
  say "Let me have it for a moment... There! I've upgraded it to the National Pokédex."
  nationaldex
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player}'s Pokédex was upgraded to the National Pokédex!"
  say "Now it can record Pokémon from far beyond Hoenn. There are so many more out there, {player}. Go and find them!"
  endscene
  end
label Wide
  say "The world is a lot bigger than Hoenn, {player}. Fill that National Pokédex for me!"
  end


script Rival
  say "Dad's always running off into the grass without his Pokémon. One day I'll be a better field worker than him!"
  end


script Aide
  if var VAR_BIRCH_LAB_STATE >= 3 goto Starter
  say "The professor? He's out on Route 101 again, I'm afraid."
  say "He says you can't learn how Pokémon live by sitting indoors. So I sit indoors and do his paperwork."
  end
label Starter
  say "The professor's Pokémon from his bag! Take good care of it, all right? It's been looked after since it hatched."
  end
