# The old painter has hunted Cobalion through these caves for most of his life. Brought a Cobalion on the team, he sees it at last
# and is glad of what it says of people and Pokémon; afterwards he wonders what it thinks of the world now.
script Painter
  face self player
  speaker "Artist"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_PAINTER_SAW_COBALION goto After
  if has "Cobalion" goto Sees
  say "I have spent my whole life painting caves, hoping one day to paint Cobalion in one. It has never come."
  end
label Sees
  say "That Pokémon of yours... it can't be! Cobalion, here, standing before me!"
  say "It walks beside you of its own will. So it has found a person it can trust after all."
  say "That is what I'll paint, then: not the Pokémon alone, but the two of you, side by side."
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_PAINTER_SAW_COBALION
  end
label After
  say "My painting of Cobalion is nearly done. I wonder what it would make of it, if it ever came to look."
  end

# The holes of this floor a boulder pushed with Strength fills (Black 2's StrengthHole tiles, 2 by 2; plan 22 · U10, part b), as the
# Relic Passage's are: each pit's script lets the boulder fall in and the hole is floor from then on
script FillHole1
  call BoulderFallsIn
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GUIDANCE_CHAMBER_HOLE_1
  hide self
  end

script FillHole2
  call BoulderFallsIn
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GUIDANCE_CHAMBER_HOLE_2
  hide self
  end
