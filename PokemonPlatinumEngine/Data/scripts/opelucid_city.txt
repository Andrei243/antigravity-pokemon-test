# Opelucid City (plan 22 · U8, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (the
# decompilation's res/field/scripts/opelucid_city.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID is the chapter's count: 0 before Iris has met the player at the east gate, 1 once she has (the Gym is open), 2 once
# the Legend Badge is won and Drayden has asked the player to his house, 3 once his tale has been told and the Plasma Frigate's ice has
# fallen on the city, 4 once Zinzolin has made his demand and the grunts are about the streets, 5 once Zinzolin is beaten at the Gym's
# door, 6 once the splicers are taken and the Shadow Triad waits at the east end, 7 once the Triad is beaten and Cheren has spoken.
# FLAG_UNOVA_OPELUCID_ICE is what lays the ice: world.json swaps the city's five chunks for their frozen twins (made ground ice, bare
# ground snow) while it is set, and the overlay's zone lays snow over the air. It is set behind a black screen at 3 and cleared at 7.
# Black 2's townspeople go indoors under the ice (the hiddenBy of the street's people is the flag). The Frigate itself is never drawn.
# Black 2's Triple and Rotation battles are fought as single ones until plan 14 (B6, B7): the beats are marked in the Gym's script.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUpC
  if var VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID == 3 call TheIceComes
  end


# Iris at the east gate (our trigger down the road's end, 440, 173 to 177; VAR 0): she was in Castelia, and now she is home
script IrisGreets
  scene "Iris at Opelucid"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_OPELUCID
  place 11 436 175 right
  show 11
  face player left
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Yahoo! It's you! I haven't seen you since we ran into each other in Castelia City!"
  walk 11 right 3
  face 11 player
  say "Are you here to challenge my grandpa? Yep! Drayden, the Gym Leader of Opelucid City, is my grandpa."
  say "We're not really related, though! Hee hee!"
  face 11 up
  say "Oh! The Gym is up that way, past the street's end! But before you take it on, you might want to see Route 9 first."
  face 11 player
  say "Still, your Pokémon really respect you! I can feel how much fun they're having, even from inside their Poké Balls!"
  say "Good luck!"
  fade out 0.3
  hide 11
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_OPELUCID
  fade in
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID 1
  endscene
  end

# The sound after the tale (the house's script warps the player to its door): the Plasma Frigate's ice falls on the whole city

  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_DRAYDEN_OPELUCID
  place player 418 161 down
  place 0 419 161 down
  show 0
  face 0 player
  speaker "Drayden"
  say "What was that sound? It came from up in the sky!"
  camera shake 0.8
  sound "bump"
  effect ripple 418 165 1.2 wait
  fade out 0.4
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_OPELUCID_ICE
  wait 0.4
  fade in
  face 0 up
  say "This world of ice... In one moment the whole city has frozen over!"
  say "Haxorus! Use Dragon Tail on the ice!"
  cry "Haxorus"
  text "Haxorus slammed its tail down on the ice. Not even a scratch showed."
  say "Not only did the ice stay unbroken, there isn't even a mark on it. What's going on?"
  speaker "???"
  say "I don't think that will work."
  place 1 419 167 up
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZINZOLIN_OPELUCID
  show 1
  emote 0 exclaim
  speaker "Drayden"
  say "I remember you from N's Castle."
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "It's bitter cold. I'm shivering. I'm suffering, but I'm alive! This is what the essence of life feels like!"
  say "Let me explain our purpose here. Drayden, hand over the DNA Splicers!"
  say "Opelucid City is a city where the past and the future are entwined. Surely it is the right place to keep something that bridges the two."
  speaker "Drayden"
  say "Do you think a man who knows what you did two years ago will hand them over quietly?"
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  say "Humph. That's what I expected you to say. I would threaten you with another volley of ice, but we can't loose it so soon."
  say "No matter! I have my people searching your city already. I won't leave without what I came for."
  fade out 0.3
  place 1 405 146 down
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GRUNTS_OPELUCID
  fade in
  speaker "Drayden"
  say "Those foul villains!"
  face 0 player
  say "{player}! I want you to help me! We're going to drive Team Plasma out of Opelucid City!"
  say "They will go for the Gym sooner or later. I'll wait there. Clear the streets of their grunts and I'll meet you at my Gym's door."
  fade out 0.3
  hide 0
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_DRAYDEN_OPELUCID
  fade in
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID 4
  endscene
  end

# Zinzolin at the Gym's door (his object, 1, stood there at VAR 4 and 5): his battle is Black 2's TRAINER_TEAM_PLASMA_ZINZOLIN_3
script ZinzolinAtTheGym
  if var VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID != 4 return
  face 1 player
  speaker "Zinzolin"
  scene "Zinzolin at the Gym"
  say "Oh, for crying out loud... I didn't expect to have to fight, hampered by cold like this."
  say "Well, no matter! The fact that you've come this far means the splicers must be inside!"
  battle 1 as "unova_team_plasma_zinzolin_3"
  say "You're a strong Trainer. You are adept at handling Pokémon. I believe I'll take my leave, simply because I'm a little tired."
  fade out 0.3
  hide 1
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ZINZOLIN_OPELUCID
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_GRUNTS_OPELUCID
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_DRAYDEN_OPELUCID
  place 0 405 149 up
  show 0
  fade in
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_OPELUCID 5
  call TheSplicers
  endscene
  end
