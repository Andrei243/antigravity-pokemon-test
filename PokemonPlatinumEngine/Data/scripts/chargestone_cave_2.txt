# Chargestone Cave's first floor (plan 22 · U7, part a). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/chargestone_cave_2.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_CHARGESTONE is the cave's count (Black 2's 0x40d2 and 0x40d3): 0 as the player first comes in, 1 once Bianca
# has had her say, 2 once N has gone.
#
# As the player comes in from Route 6 our trigger on the first corridor (8 to 11, 50, at 0) lets Bianca (bianca_cave) run
# up from deeper in with a word of the cave and her errand and go off again; she is then at work on the second floor
# (chargestone_cave_3.txt). Further along our second trigger (47, 41 to 42, at 1) catches N (11) standing alone in the
# long corridor: he says what he likes about the place and that he must go, and is gone.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script BiancaAtTheMouth
  scene "Bianca at Chargestone Cave"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_CHARGESTONE
  place bianca_cave 11 48 down
  show bianca_cave
  speaker "Bianca"
  say "Heeey! {player}, is that you? Hi there!"
  walk bianca_cave down 1
  face bianca_cave player
  say "Isn't this place something? The whole cave is charged with electricity, and Pokémon just love it."
  say "The charge jumps from one rock to the next. That's why some of the stones in here glow, and why there are sparks underfoot."
  say "Oh, that's right! I came here to research something for Professor Juniper. I'd better get back to it. See you, {player}!"
  walk bianca_cave up 4
  hide bianca_cave
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_CHARGESTONE
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_BIANCA_CHARGESTONE_HEART
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CHARGESTONE 1
  endscene
  end


script NAlone
  scene "N in Chargestone Cave"
  speaker "N"
  say "Chargestone Cave... I like it here. The stones hum, and the Pokémon hum with them."
  say "Someone could write all of it down in formulas: the charge, the Pokémon, the people. But a formula never asks how a Pokémon feels."
  say "I have to go. There are Pokémon who need someone to speak for them, and there is a person I must stop."
  hide 11
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_N_CHARGESTONE
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CHARGESTONE 2
  endscene
  end


script NInTheCave
  face 11 player
  speaker "N"
  say "Please, let me be. I'm listening to the stones."
  end
