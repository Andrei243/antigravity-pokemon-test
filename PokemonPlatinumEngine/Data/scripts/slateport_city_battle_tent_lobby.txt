# Slateport's Battle Tent (plan 21 · H3's last part). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# beats of Emerald's (data/maps/SlateportCity_BattleTentLobby/scripts.inc): a round of three Trainers on the Battle Factory's
# rules, on three Pokémon rented from six offered, a swap after each win, and a Full Heal for the three won.
#
# The round is a tent's venue on rentals (Models/Frontier/BattleVenue.cs, `venue tent slateport`): the six offered and the
# Trainers' three are made at thirty as Hoenn's levels map it, and the player's own team stays out of it. The rentals are
# chosen in the corridor (slateport_city_battle_tent_corridor.txt) on the Factory's own screen; the battle room's arrival
# script is the round.


script Attendant
  face self player
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Welcome to the Slateport Battle Tent! Here you battle with Pokémon we lend you."
label Ask
  choose "Would you like to take the Battle Tent challenge?" "Enter" "The rules" "No"
  if result == 1 goto Rules
  if result == 2 goto Later
  venue tent slateport
  say "Thank you! Your rental Pokémon are ready. Please follow me."
  walk self up 3
  fade out 0.6
  warp "SlateportCity_BattleTentCorridor" 2 7 up
  fade in
  end
label Later
  say "Please come again. There's always a new rental waiting to surprise you."
  end
label Rules
  say "You'll choose three of six rental Pokémon, and battle three Trainers in a row with them."
  say "After each win, you may swap one of yours for one of the Trainer you just beat."
  say "Your own Pokémon stay out of it. Win all three battles for a prize!"
  goto Ask


# The sailor who hands out Torment (Emerald's TormentGiver, FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_TORMENT, TM41)
script TormentGiver
  face self player
  speaker "Sailor"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_TORMENT goto Given
  say "Hey, you! Don't look so worried. I've got something for you."
  give "TM41"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_TM_TORMENT
label Given
  say "Torment keeps a foe from using the same move twice in a row. Drives them up the wall!"
  end
