# Slateport City's Poké Mart (plan 21 · H3, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own.
# The clerk keeps the common counter and, as Emerald's Slateport Mart sells it, the Harbor Mail that Mauville's Coin
# Case is traded for (marts.json's slateport_mail).


script Clerk
  sayown
  choose "What are you looking for today?" "Supplies" "Mail" "Nothing"
  if result == 0 goto Supplies
  if result == 1 goto Mail
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end
label Supplies
  shop
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end
label Mail
  shop "slateport_mail"
  say "Thank you! Do come again."
  end
