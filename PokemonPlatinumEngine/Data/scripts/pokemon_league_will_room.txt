# our own, on HeartGold's beats (scr_seq_T10R0201).
#
# The way back to the hall is shut behind the challenger (the overlay locks it, and the doorway at the room's foot
# carries whoever steps into it back up). Spoken to, Will battles; beaten, he opens the door behind him
# (FLAG_HIDE_WILLS_ROOM_EXIT, HeartGold's, which hides its door there and opens the overlay's door here). The hall's
# arrival puts him and the doors back (pokemon_league_entrance.txt). His rematch team is plan 20 · J15's.


script Will
  face t10r0201_gsbigfour1 player
  speaker "Will"
  if flag FLAG_HIDE_WILLS_ROOM_EXIT goto Beaten
  scene "Will"
  say "Welcome to the Pokémon League. I am Will of the Elite Four."
  say "My Psychic-type Pokémon and I have honed our minds in every corner of the world, and the League called us here at last."
  say "I will not lose to you. Prepare yourself!"
  battle self as "johto_elite_four_will_will"
  speaker "Will"
  say "I... can't believe it. My psychic power was not enough."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_WILLS_ROOM_EXIT
  sound "door_open"
  say "Go on to the next room. You will learn there what the Elite Four truly are."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "Even having lost, I won't change my course. I'll keep training until I can see where your strength comes from."
  end
