# The Abandoned Ship's hidden floor (plan 21 · H12, part i). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/AbandonedShip_HiddenFloorCorridors/scripts.inc).
#
# The doors of Rooms 1, 2, 4 and 6 are locked until their keys, hidden in the other cabins, are turned in them. Each key
# stays in its lock (Emerald's removeitem) and its door stays open (FLAG_USED_ROOM_1_KEY and the rest, which open the way
# through each door by the overlay's switches). Rooms 3 and 5 are never locked.


script Room1
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_USED_ROOM_1_KEY goto Open
  if not item "Room 1 Key" goto Locked
  text "{player} put the Room 1 Key in the lock and turned it."
  sound "door_open"
  take "Room 1 Key"
  setflag FLAG_USED_ROOM_1_KEY
  text "The key stuck fast in the lock, but the door swung open."
  end
label Locked
  text "The door is locked. \"RM. 1\" is painted on it."
  end
label Open
  text "The door stands open."
  end
