# Saffron City (plan 19 · K5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's
# beats (data/maps/SaffronCity/scripts.inc).
#
# Team Rocket holds the city until Giovanni is beaten at the top of Silph Co. (FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_ROCKETS,
# silph_co_11f.txt), and its own people stay indoors until then (FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CIVILIANS, which FireRed's new
# game sets: here the city's arrival sets it for a game that hasn't freed the city). The grunt at Silph Co.'s door
# stands in it until Mr. Fuji is rescued (FireRed's setobjectxyperm on FLAG_RESCUED_MR_FUJI), and dozes beside it
# afterwards.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_ROCKETS setflag FLAG_HIDE_SAFFRON_CIVILIANS
  if not flag FLAG_RESCUED_MR_FUJI place saffron_door_guard_grunt 287 144 down
  end


script DoorGuard
  speaker "Grunt"
  if flag FLAG_RESCUED_MR_FUJI goto Asleep
  say "Silph Co. is closed today. Team Rocket's orders! Now beat it, kid!"
  end
label Asleep
  speaker none
  text "Zzz... Zzz... The grunt has dozed off at his post."
  end
