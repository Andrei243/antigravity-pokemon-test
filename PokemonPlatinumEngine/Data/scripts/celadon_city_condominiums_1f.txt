# The old lady gives the Tea (FLAG_GOT_TEA), which the four guards of Saffron City's gates are thirsty for
# (route_5_south_entrance.txt and its three fellows).


script TeaWoman
  speaker "Old Woman"
  if flag FLAG_GOT_TEA goto Given
  say "My, you look parched. Here, have some of my tea. It's my own blend!"
  give "Tea"
  setflag FLAG_GOT_TEA
  say "Do share it with anyone who looks thirsty, won't you?"
  end
label Given
  say "Nothing like a cup of tea, is there? It puts the world to rights."
  end
