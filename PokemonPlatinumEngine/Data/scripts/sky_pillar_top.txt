# The top of Sky Pillar (plan 21 · H8, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/SkyPillar_Top/scripts.inc).
#
# Rayquaza sleeps coiled at the middle of the top. The first step onto the tile before it (the area's trigger 0, while
# VAR_SKY_PILLAR_RAYQUAZA_CRY_DONE is 0) wakes it, as Emerald's SkyPillar_Top_EventScript_AwakenRayquaza: the camera looks
# up at it, it stirs and roars, the tower shakes twice, and it flies off toward Sootopolis (FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_TOP_RAYQUAZA,
# Emerald's removeobject). VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE goes to 5 and VAR_SKY_PILLAR_STATE to 1, which Sootopolis's own
# arrival reads to play Rayquaza's coming (sootopolis_city.txt). Rayquaza back on the top to be battled
# (FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_TOP_RAYQUAZA_STILL) is the post-game's, plan 21 · H12.


script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_HOENN_CHAPTER_ELEVEN call HoennChapterEleven
  if var VAR_SKY_PILLAR_STATE < 2 end
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_RAYQUAZA end
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_TOP_RAYQUAZA_STILL
  end


# Rayquaza back on the top (plan 21 · H12, part c): once it has calmed Sootopolis (VAR_SKY_PILLAR_STATE 2, Emerald's
# SkyPillar_Top_EventScript_TryShowRayquaza) it waits here, awake, until beaten or caught, and is battled at level 70
# (Emerald's SkyPillar_Top_EventScript_Rayquaza). Run from, it flies off and is back the next time the top is come to,
# as Emerald's OnTransition shows it again. The floors below are cracked meanwhile (FLAG_HOENN_SKY_PILLAR_CRACKED).
script Rayquaza
  cry "Rayquaza"
  speaker none
  text "Rayquaza glares down from the top of the pillar!"
  wildbattle "Rayquaza" 70
  hide self
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_TOP_RAYQUAZA_STILL
  if result == 2 goto Fled
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_RAYQUAZA
  end
label Fled
  text "Rayquaza flew off into the sky..."
  end


script Awaken
  if var VAR_SKY_PILLAR_RAYQUAZA_CRY_DONE != 0 end
  scene "Rayquaza wakes"
  music stop
  face player up
  camera pan 14 6 1.5
  wait 0.5
  walk rayquaza_sleeping onspot left 1
  wait 0.6
  cry "Rayquaza"
  camera shake 0.8
  wait 0.8
  cry "Rayquaza"
  camera shake 1
  wait 1.2
  walk rayquaza_sleeping up 1 fast
  sound "warp"
  hide rayquaza_sleeping
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SKY_PILLAR_TOP_RAYQUAZA
  speaker none
  text "The awakened Rayquaza flew off..."
  camera release 1.5
  music area
  setvar VAR_SOOTOPOLIS_CITY_STATE 5
  setvar VAR_SKY_PILLAR_STATE 1
  setvar VAR_SKY_PILLAR_RAYQUAZA_CRY_DONE 1
  endscene
  end


# Rayquaza asleep, looked at before the step that wakes it (Emerald gives it no script: its tile is in the way)
script Sleeping
  speaker none
  text "A huge green Pokémon lies coiled here, fast asleep."
  end
