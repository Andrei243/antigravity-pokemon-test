# Pallet Town (plan 19 · K3). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on FireRed's beats
# (data/maps/PalletTown/scripts.inc).
#
# The two steps at the top of the road north (the area's triggers 0 and 1, on VAR_MAP_SCENE_PALLET_TOWN_OAK 0): Oak
# calls out from behind, the player turns, he hurries up from the town, says the grass is no place to be without a
# Pokémon and takes the player to his lab. In FireRed the two walk there together and he opens the lab's door; here
# the picture goes dark at his word and comes up again inside, where the lab's own arrival plays the rest
# (pallet_town_professor_oaks_lab.txt; docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kanto's first chapter").


script OakStops
  scene "Oak in the grass"
  music "kanto/oak"
  speaker "???"
  say "Hold it! Don't take another step out there!"
  face player down
  emote player exclaim
  show pallet_prof_oak
  if var PLAYER_X == 73 goto FromTheRight
  walk pallet_prof_oak up 6 right 2
  goto Talk
label FromTheRight
  walk pallet_prof_oak up 6 right 3
label Talk
  face pallet_prof_oak player
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "Phew, I caught you in time! Wild Pokémon hide in that grass, and they don't take kindly to visitors."
  say "Without a Pokémon of your own to keep you safe, you'd be in real danger. Come along to my lab. Hurry, now!"
  # FireRed's MUS_FOLLOW_ME as he leads the way (plan 19 · K4); the lab plays its own once there
  music "kanto/follow_me"
  wait 0.8
  fade out
  hide pallet_prof_oak
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PALLET_TOWN_OAK 1
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_PALLET_TOWN_PROFESSOR_OAKS_LAB 1
  objective kantoStarter
  warp "PalletTown_ProfessorOaksLab" 6 12 up
  endscene
  end


# FireRed's roaming beast (plan 29 · LS9; pokefirered's src/roamer.c, InitRoamer): once the player is Kanto's Champion
# and holds the National Pokédex, the next arrival in Pallet Town sets one of the three beasts loose over Kanto's
# routes at level 50, by FireRed's rule of the starter chosen (Bulbasaur Entei, Charmander Suicune, anything else
# Raikou: a game begun in another region falls to Raikou, FireRed's default). FireRed sets it loose without a word as
# Oak upgrades the Pokédex; here the player sees it go by, plan 29's beat for a roamer: the bars, the camera over the
# town, its shadow racing across the road, its cry and a line, and nothing more until it is met in the grass (plan 09 ·
# L14's way in). It keeps a slot of its own (Roamers.Slots' last three, roamer start … kanto) and its own story's
# variable, VAR_ROAMING_KANTO_<SPECIES>_STATE, apart from Johto's Raikou and Entei.
script OnEnter
  if not flag FLAG_SYS_GAME_CLEAR end
  if not nationaldex end
  if flag FLAG_KANTO_ROAMER_LOOSE end
  scene "A beast over Pallet Town"
  music stop
  letterbox on 0.4
  camera pan 71 276 1.2
  camera zoom 1.4 0.8
  sound "thunder_rumble"
  effect shadow 71 276 2 right
  wait 0.8
  emote player exclaim
  speaker none
  if starter "Bulbasaur" goto Entei
  if starter "Charmander" goto Suicune
  cry "Raikou"
  camera shake 0.4
  text "A crackle of lightning raced down the road and was gone over the hills!" "Raikou is loose in Kanto!"
  roamer start "Raikou" kanto
  goto Gone
label Entei
  cry "Entei"
  camera shake 0.6
  text "A blast of hot wind tore down the road and was gone over the hills!" "Entei is loose in Kanto!"
  roamer start "Entei" kanto
  goto Gone
label Suicune
  cry "Suicune"
  text "A cool, clear wind swept down the road and was gone over the hills!" "Suicune is loose in Kanto!"
  roamer start "Suicune" kanto
label Gone
  setflag FLAG_KANTO_ROAMER_LOOSE
  camera release 0.4
  letterbox off 0.3
  music area
  endscene
  end
