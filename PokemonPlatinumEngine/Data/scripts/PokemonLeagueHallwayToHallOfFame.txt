# The hallway to the Hall of Fame (plan 01 · M9, part 2d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_pokemon_league_hallway_to_hall_of_fame.s).
#
# One scene, played as the new Champion comes in: seventeen steps up the hall, Cynthia coming up on the right and the
# professor on the left, their words, and all three through the door at the head of the hall. Both are off the map
# until then (the Champion's room sets their flags as the player leaves it), and nobody walks here by themselves.


# PokemonLeagueHallwayToHallOfFame_OnFrame_EnterHallOfFame
script OnEnter
  scene "The way to the Hall of Fame"
  walk player up 17
  wait 0.25
  face player down
  place cynthia 6 14 up
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_HALLWAY_TO_HALL_OF_FAME_CYNTHIA
  show cynthia
  walk cynthia up 8
  face cynthia left
  face player right
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "The room at the end of this hall is the Hall of Fame. The Trainers who have beaten the League are remembered there, with the Pokémon who stood beside them."
  place prof_rowan 4 14 up
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_POKEMON_LEAGUE_HALLWAY_TO_HALL_OF_FAME_PROF_ROWAN
  show prof_rowan
  walk prof_rowan up 8
  face prof_rowan right
  face player left
  speaker "Professor Rowan"
  say "{player}! I watched your last battle from beginning to end. It was splendid. Truly splendid!"
  face cynthia left
  emote cynthia exclaim
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "Professor Rowan! It's been far too long."
  speaker "Professor Rowan"
  say "Hm! So it has. {player}, when I handed you a Pokédex I hoped you would fill a few pages of it. I did not expect to be standing here. You have grown into a real Champion."
  face cynthia left
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "You haven't changed at all, Professor. I'm glad of it."
  if girl goto Girl
  say "Now, {player}. This way, young man: the Hall is waiting for you."
  goto In
label Girl
  say "Now, {player}. This way, young lady: the Hall is waiting for you."
label In
  say "Professor, please come in with us."
  walk cynthia up 2 left 1 up 2
  hide cynthia
  walk prof_rowan up 2 right 1 up 2
  hide prof_rowan
  face player up
  walk player up 4
  sound "stairs"
  warp "PokemonLeagueHallOfFame" 8 15 up
  endscene
  end
