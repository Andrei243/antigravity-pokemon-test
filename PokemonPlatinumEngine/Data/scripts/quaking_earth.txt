# Quaking Earth (plan 34 · PD4, its second session): the fourth Titan of the Path of Legends, the version's own: Great
# Tusk in a game with Koraidon (Scarlet), Iron Treads in one with Miraidon (Violet), as the version is told by the mount
# (VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT 1 or 2, set with the version in Inlet Grotto). Met charging across the dusty basin north of Cortondo,
# beaten and chased to its den on the rise at the basin's north end, beaten again there once the Herba Mystica has made
# it bigger, and the herbs it left shared with the mount, which can glide again. A stand-in for the original's place in
# Asado Desert, until plan 34 · PD6 builds it. VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS (stony_cliff.txt's header has the whole count) goes on:
#   7  the Titan Orthworm beaten: the version's Titan charges about the basin (FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_TUSK or _TREADS)
#   8  the Titan beaten in the basin and fled to its den (FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_FLED, and FLAG_PALDEA_GREAT_TUSK_FLED or
#      FLAG_PALDEA_IRON_TREADS_FLED for which of the two stands at the den)
#   9  the Titan beaten at its den (FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_BEATEN), its herbs shared: the mount glides (FLAG_PALDEA_MOUNT_GLIDE)
# The lines are our own, on Scarlet and Violet's beats.


# The Titan in the basin: the first battle, and its flight up onto the rise
script Quaking
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS == 7 goto Titan
  end
label Titan
  scene "The Quaking Earth Titan"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT == 2 goto Treads
  faceto titan_great_tusk player
  cry "Great Tusk"
  text "The ground shudders. A Great Tusk bigger than a house curls into a ball and comes rolling across the basin!"
  wildbattle "Great Tusk" 45 titan attack
  text "The Titan Great Tusk uncurls, stamps hard enough to crack the ground, and pounds off up onto the rise!"
  walkto titan_great_tusk quaking_earth_ramp_foot fast
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_GREAT_TUSK_FLED
  goto Fled
label Treads
  faceto titan_iron_treads player
  cry "Iron Treads"
  text "The ground shudders. An Iron Treads bigger than a house comes spinning across the basin, throwing up dust!"
  wildbattle "Iron Treads" 45 titan defense
  text "The Titan Iron Treads sparks and whirs, then spins away up onto the rise at the basin's end!"
  walkto titan_iron_treads quaking_earth_ramp_foot fast
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_IRON_TREADS_FLED
label Fled
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_FLED
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 8
  endscene
  end


# Up on the rise: the Titan at its Herba Mystica, the second battle, and Arven come up after it to share the herbs
script QuakingDen
  if var VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS == 8 goto Den
  end
label Den
  scene "The Quaking Earth Titan's Herba Mystica"
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT == 2 goto Treads
  faceto player titan_great_tusk_den
  text "The Titan Great Tusk is gulping down a clump of glowing herbs, dust pouring off its hide."
  text "Its tusks gleam and its back swells. It lowers its head at {player}!"
  wildbattle "Great Tusk" 45 titan all
  text "The Titan Great Tusk is beaten. It shrinks back to an ordinary size and lumbers off into the hills."
  goto Beaten
label Treads
  faceto player titan_iron_treads_den
  text "The Titan Iron Treads has rolled over a clump of glowing herbs. Its plates hum louder and louder."
  text "Light runs along its seams and its wheel spins up. It turns on {player}!"
  wildbattle "Iron Treads" 45 titan all
  text "The Titan Iron Treads is beaten. It shrinks back to an ordinary size and rolls off into the hills."
label Beaten
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_QUAKING_BEATEN
  placeat arven_quaking_earth quaking_earth_ramp_foot
  walkto arven_quaking_earth quaking_earth_herbs fast
  faceto player arven_quaking_earth
  speaker "Arven"
  say "Four! I've never seen anything that size move that fast. These herbs are salty, like the desert's in them."
  faceto arven_quaking_earth player
  if var VAR_PALDEA_MOUNT == 2 goto Miraidon
  text "Koraidon ate the sandwich and spread the fins along its sides wide, like wings catching the wind."
  goto Strength
label Miraidon
  text "Miraidon ate the sandwich and opened the fins along its sides, which lit up like a glider's wings."
label Strength
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_MOUNT_GLIDE
  text "Some of its old strength has come back. It can glide again! (Riding, press jump again in the air to glide.)"
  say "There's one more. Way out east, past the road to Levincia, there's a lake in a hollow."
  say "People say a dragon lives on its shore. A small one, bossing around something huge. We'll see."
  setvar VAR_PALDEA_LEGENDS 9
  endscene
  end
