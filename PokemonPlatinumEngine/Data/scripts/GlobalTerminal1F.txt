# The Global Terminal in Jubilife City, ground floor (plan 01 · M11, part d), on the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_global_terminal_1f.s).
#
# Everything the building is for goes over the wireless link, which this game leaves out (decision 06 · 7: the GTS,
# the rankings, the studios for Box Data and Dress-Up Data on 2F and the Battle Video Gallery on 3F): its rooms and
# its people are here, its receptionists say that the link is down, and its machines show no signal
# (docs/mechanics/rulings.md). The three floors are joined by warp panels, as the headquarters of Team Galactic are
# (Overworld/WarpPanels.cs).
#
# The beauty guesses the player's favourite Pokémon: the first of the team, as the original's does.


# GlobalTerminal1F_Beauty2
script Favourite
  if var PARTY_COUNT == 0 goto Nobody
  ask "Your favourite is {lead}, isn't it?"
  if no goto Really
  say "There, I was right! I knew it!"
  end
label Really
  say "...Oh? But your favourite really is {lead}, isn't it?"
  end
label Nobody
  say "I want to know what kind of Pokémon everybody likes best."
  end


# The GTS's own receptionist. Plan 03 · D12a, ours: with no link to trade over, she hands out a Linking Cord in its
# place whenever the bag holds none, so that every Pokémon that evolves by a trade can evolve with nobody else (a
# cord used on a Pokémon counts as a trade, Models/Evolution.cs).
script Gts
  say "Welcome! This is the Global Trade Station: the GTS, for short. Here Pokémon are traded with people all over the world." "Or they were. Sinnoh's link to the wider world has gone quiet, and no trade can leave this desk until it is back."
  if item "Linking Cord" goto Holding
  say "But a Pokémon that grows only through a trade needn't wait for the link. Here: a Linking Cord does the same for it."
  give "Linking Cord"
  say "Use it on the Pokémon like a stone. Come back for another whenever yours is used up!"
  end
label Holding
  say "You still have a Linking Cord, I see. Use it on a Pokémon that grows through a trade, and come back for another."
  end
