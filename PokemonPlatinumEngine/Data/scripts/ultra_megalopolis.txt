# Ultra Megalopolis (plan 23 · Z8), the Lightseekers' city in Ultra Space. The lines are our own, on Ultra Sun and Ultra
# Moon's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Ultra Space and the Battle Royal Dome"): a
# city whose light was taken long ago by the being at the top of Megalo Tower, lit by lamps alone. It is open through
# the Ultra Wormhole once the seven Ultra Beasts have been met (VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA 3), and after Necrozma's battle the
# light comes back (FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED, VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA 4). The Blacephalon Z8 stood on the square went to Poni
# Grove with the grove (the wave after Ultra Space), where Ultra Sun and Ultra Moon keep it.


script OnEnter
  effect rift 32 55 hold
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED end
  if var VAR_ALOLA_ULTRA >= 4 end
  text "A city of towers, and not one window lit. Only the street lamps burn, and the dark presses on them from above."
  end


script Resident1
  face self player
  speaker "Old Man"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED goto Lit
  say "My grandmother told me the sky here was bright once. I've only ever seen it like this."
  say "The Lightseekers say the one who took our light still sits at the top of Megalo Tower."
  end
label Lit
  say "Look at it. Look at the sky! I didn't know it had a colour."
  end


script Resident2
  face self player
  speaker "Lamp Keeper"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED goto Lit
  say "I keep the lamps. Every lamp in this city burns on power we make ourselves, every hour of every day."
  say "If they ever went out, nobody could find their way home. So they never go out."
  end
label Lit
  say "I turned a lamp off this morning, just to see what it was like. I could still see. I nearly cried."
  end


script Resident3
  face self player
  speaker "Woman"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_LIGHT_RETURNED goto Lit
  say "You came through the wormhole? From a world with a sun? ...Is it warm? I'm sorry. I just wondered."
  end
label Lit
  say "So this is warm. You were right. It's wonderful."
  end
