# Six Island's Pokémon Center (plan 19 · K9, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# FireRed's beats (data/maps/SixIsland_PokemonCenter_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# The first time the player comes in (VAR_MAP_SCENE_SIX_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 0) the rival is here, grumbling that
# the Pokémon of these islands are hard to come by; he leaves (1), and won't wait on Four Island after it either
# (VAR_MAP_SCENE_FOUR_ISLAND 1), as FireRed's scene sets. FireRed walks him to the player and out; here he speaks
# where he stands and a dark screen takes him away.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_MAP_SCENE_SIX_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F == 0 goto Rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SIX_ISLAND_POKECENTER_RIVAL
  end
label Rival
  scene "The rival on Six Island"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SIX_ISLAND_POKECENTER_RIVAL
  show six_island_rival
  music "kanto/rival"
  emote six_island_rival exclaim
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}? You again! You keep turning up wherever I go."
  say "I came all this way for the Pokémon of these islands, and half of them won't show their faces. Catching them all from here is hopeless!"
  say "Well, I'm off to look somewhere else. Smell you later!"
  fade out 0.4
  hide six_island_rival
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SIX_ISLAND_POKECENTER_RIVAL
  music area
  fade in 0.4
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_SIX_ISLAND_POKEMON_CENTER_1F 1
  setvar VAR_MAP_SCENE_FOUR_ISLAND 1
  endscene
  end
