# The Battle Maison's lobby in Kiloude City (plan 23, Kalos's last places; plan 37's streak house). The lines are our own,
# and so are the people: none of X and Y's hosts is here. X and Y's Maison is an endless streak of single, double, triple
# and rotation battles; ours is the first part of it on the frame the Battle Frontier's annex already has
# (Models/Frontier/BattleVenue.cs, `venue ... maison`): a round of seven single battles, three entered as copies at level 50,
# whole again after every battle, against trainers of the later sets (Data/facility-sets.json) who grow stronger with the
# rounds won in a row. The round itself is KiloudeCity_BattleMaisonHall.txt's arrival script. Battle Points go into the
# Frontier's one purse, and the counter beside the desk trades them for the later games' items (Data/facility-prizes.json).
# Plan 37 · FC2 builds the rest: the other three rooms, the hosts at the 20th and 50th battles, putting a round down.


script Desk
  face self player
  speaker "Receptionist"
  say "Welcome to the Battle Maison! Trainers from every corner of the world come to Kiloude to test themselves here."
  venue streak maison
  if result == 0 goto Ask
  say "You've won {number} battles in a row in our hall. The regulars have started to talk about you!"
label Ask
  choose "Would you like to take on the Battle Maison?" "Enter" "The rules" "No"
  if result == 1 goto Rules
  if result == 2 goto Later
  venue enough
  if result == 1 goto Choose
  say "You need three Pokémon that can take part, all of different species and holding different items."
  say "An Egg can't take part, and nor can {banned}."
  end
label Choose
  say "Please choose the three Pokémon you'll enter."
  venue choose
  if result == 0 goto Later
  venue check
  if result == 1 goto SameSpecies
  if result == 2 goto SameItem
  venue begin maison
  say "Thank you! Seven Trainers are waiting in the hall. This way, please!"
  fade out 0.6
  warp "KiloudeCity_BattleMaisonHall" 8 10 up
  fade in
  end
label SameSpecies
  say "I'm sorry. The three entered must all be of different species."
  goto Later
label SameItem
  say "I'm sorry. No two of the three entered may hold the same item."
label Later
  say "We'll be here. The hall never really empties."
  end
label Rules
  say "Your three battle at Level 50, or at their own level if it's lower. They are made whole after every battle."
  say "Beat seven Trainers in a row to win a round. Lose once, and the round is over, and so is your streak."
  say "The longer your streak, the stronger the Trainers who come to meet you."
  end


# The counter of prizes: the later games' items for Battle Points, the Frontier's one purse
script Prizes
  face self player
  speaker "Clerk"
  say "Welcome! Battle Points won here, or anywhere in the world's battle halls, are good at this counter."
  frontier prize maison
  if result == 0 goto Bye
  frontier buy
  if result == 0 goto Short
  sound "select"
  text "{player} traded {price} BP for the {item}."
  say "Thank you! Use it well."
  end
label Short
  say "I'm sorry. You don't have the Battle Points for that."
  end
label Bye
  say "Come back when you've won a few more!"
  end


script Fan
  face self player
  speaker "Fan"
  say "I come every afternoon just to watch. You can tell a Trainer's whole journey from how they send out their first Pokémon."
  end

script Veteran
  face self player
  speaker "Veteran"
  say "Seven in a row sounds easy until the fifth. Then you start to hear your own heartbeat."
  end
