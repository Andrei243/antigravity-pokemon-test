# Julia's house in Sunyshore City (plan 06 · R17b), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes the
# language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_sunyshore_city_northeast_house.s).
#
# Julia asks for a story every day and gives the first of the team that is no Egg the day's ribbon for it: Alert on
# Monday, Shock on Tuesday, Downcast on Wednesday, Careless on Thursday, Relax on Friday, Snooze on Saturday and Smile
# on Sunday. The first visit only makes the acquaintance (she asks to be visited tomorrow); a Pokémon that has the
# day's ribbon already gets none; and one story a day is all she hears (the two daily flags the new day clears).
# In the original the story is a word chosen from the easy-chat words; here the player only answers yes or no.


script Julia
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_TOLD_STORY_SUNYSHORE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE goto Tomorrow
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_VISIT_TOMORROW_SUNYSHORE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE goto VisitTomorrow
  if not flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_JULIA_SUNYSHORE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE goto First
  ribbon has today
  if result == 1 goto GaveBefore
  if weekday monday ask "It's Monday, and I want a story that wakes me right up. Have you one about a time you had to be on your guard?"
  if weekday tuesday ask "It's Tuesday. Tell me about something that gave you a real shock! Will you?"
  if weekday wednesday ask "Wednesdays always get me down. Have you a story of a time you felt low, to keep mine company?"
  if weekday thursday ask "Thursday! Tell me about a slip you made without thinking. Go on, will you?"
  if weekday friday ask "Friday at last. Have you a story about putting your feet up and taking it easy?"
  if weekday saturday ask "It's a sleepy sort of Saturday. Will you tell me about the best sleep you ever had?"
  if weekday sunday ask "Sunday! I'd like a story that makes me smile. Have you one?"
  if no goto Nothing
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_TOLD_STORY_SUNYSHORE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE
  say "...Oh, that was lovely. Thank you! I shan't forget it, and neither should your {wearer}. Please, give it this."
  ribbon give today
  fanfare item
  text "{player} received the {ribbon}!" "{player} put the {ribbon} on {wearer}."
label Tomorrow
  say "Come and tell me another story tomorrow, won't you? I've a different ribbon for every day of the week."
  end
label First
  setflag FLAG_TALKED_TO_JULIA_SUNYSHORE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_VISIT_TOMORROW_SUNYSHORE_CITY_NORTHEAST_HOUSE
  say "My husband is a sailor, so I'm often on my own here. A traveller like you must have stories. Would you come and tell me one?"
  say "Not today, I'm all out of sorts today. Tomorrow! Do come tomorrow."
  end
label VisitTomorrow
  say "Do come and visit tomorrow. I'll be ready for a story then."
  end
label GaveBefore
  say "Your {wearer} has today's ribbon already. Bring another Pokémon along first, or come back on another day."
  end
label Nothing
  say "Nothing comes to mind? That happens. Come back when you've thought of one."
  end
