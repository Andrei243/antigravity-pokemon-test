# The geothermal power plant at the top of Blush Mountain, Ula'ula Island (plan 23, the rest of the islands; rulings,
# "The Battle Tree, the power plant, Zygarde's cells and the rest of the islands"). The lines and the people are our own.
# Its chief shows the plant and gives an Electric Seed once (FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_GIFT); a worker and a scientist
# battle a visitor once each, and a Zygarde Cell hides in its south-west corner, as Sun and Moon keep one here.


script Chief
  face self player
  speaker "Plant Chief"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_GIFT goto Again
  say "Welcome to Blush Mountain's geothermal power plant! Hot water rises from deep under the mountain, right here."
  say "Its steam turns our turbines, and the turbines light half of Ula'ula. Clean power, straight from the island's heart!"
  say "Visitors who climb all this way get a souvenir. It's charged with the same energy as the plant itself."
  give "Electric Seed"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_GIFT
  end
label Again
  say "On clear nights the plant's lights draw Minior down from the sky. They say the mountain blushes red at dawn because of them."
  end


script Kanoa
  face self player
  speaker "Kanoa"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_KANOA goto After
  say "A visitor! The chief says I should be welcoming. My Pokémon say I should be battling. They usually win the argument."
  battle self as "alola_worker_kanoa"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_KANOA
  speaker "Kanoa"
  say "Overloaded. Totally overloaded. I'll go and check the fuses."
  end
label After
  say "Every gauge in here reads a little higher when a strong Trainer walks in. Funny, that."
  end


script Ola
  face self player
  speaker "Ola"
  if flag FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_OLA goto After
  say "I study how the mountain's pull changes Pokémon. Magnemite evolve here that never would anywhere else. Care to see why?"
  battle self as "alola_scientist_ola"
  setflag FLAG_ALOLA_POWER_PLANT_OLA
  speaker "Ola"
  say "Fascinating. Your team's output exceeds the plant's. I'll be writing that down."
  end
label After
  say "Bring a Magneton or a Nosepass up here and train it. The magnetic field does the rest."
  end


script Engineer
  face self player
  speaker "Engineer"
  say "The pipes run hot enough to cook an egg. Ask me how I know. Actually, don't."
  end


# The plant's own Zygarde Cell (Sun and Moon keep one here): common.ZygardeCell, its flag named for the check of flags
script PlantCell
  if item "Zygarde Cube" setflag FLAG_ALOLA_ZYGARDE_CELL_PLANT
  call ZygardeCell
  end
