# Virbank City's ship terminal (plan 22 · U5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/virbank_city_5.s, read for the order alone).
#
# The captain (2, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_CAPTAIN_TERMINAL) and Hugh (3, FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_HUGH_TERMINAL) are here once the
# captain has gone back to his ship (VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK 8, virbank_city.txt). Black 2's next beat is the voyage itself:
# Hugh asks whether the player is coming, the captain takes them both aboard and the ship sails into Castelia City
# (Black 2's 0x40ae 1). Castelia's streets stand on rails the importer doesn't read yet, so the voyage is plan 22 · U5,
# part c's: until then the captain is still warming his engines.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script Hugh
  face 3 player
  speaker "{rival}"
  say "{player}! You're heading to Castelia City too, right? I'm going after Team Plasma. I'll never let them get away!"
  face 3 2
  say "Hey, Captain! Show us this ship you're so proud of!"
  face 2 3
  call CaptainLines
  end


script Captain
  face 2 player
  call CaptainLines
  end


script CaptainLines
  speaker "Captain"
  say "Ha ha! She's raring to go, but her engines went cold while I was off at the studios."
  say "Give me a little while to warm them through, and we'll charge across to Castelia City!"
  return


script OldWoman
  speaker "Old Woman"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK >= 8 goto Life
  if var VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK >= 2 goto Gone
label Life
  say "Your life is your own, dear. Work hard at one thing, or try your hand at many. Just mind the people waiting on you."
  end
label Gone
  say "Looking for the captain? He's gone off to Pokéstar Studios, of all places! Thinks he can be a star like his daughter."
  end
