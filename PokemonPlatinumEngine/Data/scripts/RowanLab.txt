# Professor Rowan's lab in Sandgem Town (plan 02 · S4). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our
# own, on the original's beats (scripts_sandgem_town_pokemon_research_lab.s).
#
# The first visit: the professor looks at the player's Pokémon, sees the bond already there, and asks the player
# to fill in a Pokédex of every Pokémon in Sinnoh. The assistant, who has one too, introduces themself.
#
# Back from the Distortion World (plan 02 · S13; Sendoff Spring leaves VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE 2): the professor and
# the assistant wait just inside the door, and the scene plays as the player comes in (the original's OnFrame). He
# has hardly slept, she says how he worried, and he has news: the three Pokémon of the lakes have gone home. With
# seven Badges, only Sunyshore's Gym is left, and the Pokémon League after it. The scene leaves what the original's
# leaves: VAR_EXITED_DISTORTION_WORLD_STATE 3, the road to Sunyshore City open (FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_COLLECTOR,
# VAR_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_BLOCK_SUNYSHORE_STATE 1), the guardians back in their caverns (FLAG_HIDE_VERITY_CAVERN_MESPRIT,
# FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_CAVERN_AZELF, FLAG_HIDE_ACUITY_CAVERN_UXIE cleared) and the state 3. The two stand by the door
# until the player has been to Sunyshore City, whose arrival sends the assistant off and makes the state 4
# (sunyshore_city.txt); the professor is back behind his desk from then on.
#
# After the Hall of Fame (plan 02 · S15, part a): spoken to behind his desk, the professor looks at the Pokédex. With
# every Pokémon of Sinnoh seen he asks to be shown it, and as he praises it Professor Oak walks in: an old colleague,
# who upgrades the Pokédex to the National one (nationaldex), says Pal Park is open and goes; and Professor Rowan
# gives the Poké Radar for the Sinnoh Pokédex finished. The upgrade is had as plan 03's decision 5 has it
# (Pokedex.CanUnlockNational: the Hall of Fame entered and all 210 seen), where the original's script asks only that
# Eterna City has been reached. Until then he rates the Pokédex by how many have been seen, as the original's
# professor does (PokedexRatings_CheckRowan, its thirteen steps). If the tag battle before the Battle Frontier's gate
# is already won, the upgrade is what lifts the Fight Area's blockades (FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_BLOCKADE).
#
# A guardian is shown only where it hasn't been caught or set roaming already: the original's scene clears the three
# flags whatever has happened, since nothing can have, and a save from before the chapter may have met one
# (Story/StoryMigration.cs, version 16).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 2 goto Returned
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 3 goto ByTheDoor
  # Afterwards he is behind his desk again (a scene's place holds while the game runs)
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE >= 4 place prof_rowan 5 2 down
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE != 0 end
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE == 0 end
  walk counterpart up 3
  walk player up 4
  face counterpart prof_rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Ah, there you are. Let me have a look at that Pokémon of yours."
  say "...Hm. It seems happy to be with you."
  say "Then trusting it to you was no mistake."
  say "When I first saw you two charging into the tall grass, I was shocked. But a bond is already growing between you and your Pokémon."
  say "Cherish that Pokémon, and cherish that bond."
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I'm glad! I could tell straight away that you're kind to Pokémon."
  music "common/rowan"
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Now. As you know, I am Rowan, and I study Pokémon. There is something I would ask of you."
label Ask
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  ask "Will you take this Pokédex, and record every kind of Pokémon you meet in Sinnoh for me?"
  if yes goto Takes
  say "...I see. Well, I am a patient man. I will ask you again."
  goto Ask
label Takes
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex!"
  say "Go everywhere you can and meet every kind of Pokémon there is. That is what the Pokédex is for."
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I'm helping the professor fill in a Pokédex too!"
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Tell me, {player}: how did it feel, walking along Route 201 with a Pokémon at your side?"
  say "I have lived for sixty years, and I still feel that thrill. Your great adventure begins now."
  face counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I'm {assistant}, by the way. I help the professor out. My first Pokémon is {assistantstarter}!"
  say "We're both working on the Pokédex now, so let's do our best. See you outside!"
  walk counterpart down 3
  hide counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_COUNTERPART
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE 1
  end
# SandgemTownLab_OnTransition in states 2 and 3
label ByTheDoor
  call TheyWait
  end
# SandgemTownLab_OnFrame_ReturnedFromDistortionWorld
label Returned
  call TheyWait
  if var VAR_EXITED_DISTORTION_WORLD_STATE == 2 setvar VAR_EXITED_DISTORTION_WORLD_STATE 3
  # A step in from the door, so that the player stands clear of the text
  walk player up 1
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "{player}! You are back!"
  say "I... Words fail me. You saw it through. All of it!"
  say "Sixty years I have lived, and I have never spent so many days with my heart in my mouth."
  face counterpart right
  wait 0.4
  face counterpart down
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "The professor has been asking after you everywhere since you left, {player}. He was beside himself."
  if girl goto Lucas
  say "He wrote to everyone he knows, trying to find someone who could go and help you... I'm so glad you're safe. Welcome back!"
  goto News
label Lucas
  say "He rang round everyone he knows to scrape up some help. Not that you needed it. I'm glad you're back in one piece, too."
label News
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Now, I have news for you. Word has come from all three lakes: their Pokémon are home again, each in its own cave."
  say "And how do things stand with you? The Pokédex... Hm! {var:SEEN} seen! And your Badges?"
  if badges >= 8 goto Eight
  if badges < 7 goto Fewer
  say "Seven! Only Sunyshore City's Gym is left, then. Having come all this way, you may as well have its Badge too."
  goto League
label Fewer
  say "{var:BADGE_COUNT}, I see. Sunyshore City's Gym is open to you now: why not see how many you can win?"
  goto League
label Eight
  say "All eight! Then you have nothing left to prove to the Gyms of Sinnoh."
label League
  say "The Pokémon League is what lies beyond. Go and try yourself against it: you will see more of the world on that road than on all the others put together, and more Pokémon too."
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_COLLECTOR
  setvar VAR_VALOR_LAKEFRONT_BLOCK_SUNYSHORE_STATE 1
  if var VAR_ROAMING_MESPRIT_STATE == 0 clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VERITY_CAVERN_MESPRIT
  if not flag FLAG_CAUGHT_AZELF clearflag FLAG_HIDE_VALOR_CAVERN_AZELF
  if not flag FLAG_CAUGHT_UXIE clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ACUITY_CAVERN_UXIE
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE 3
  end


# SandgemTownLab_SetProfRowanAndCounterpartPositions: the two inside the door, facing it (the original stands them at
# 7, 14 and 6, 14 of its own, larger room).
script TheyWait
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_PROF_ROWAN
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_COUNTERPART
  place prof_rowan 5 5 down
  place counterpart 4 5 down
  show counterpart
  return


script Rowan
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 0 goto Before
  face self player
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  # SandgemTownLab_GiveCynthiaMyBestRegards: between his scene and the player's arrival in Sunyshore City
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 3 goto Regards
  # SandgemTownLab_ProfRowanReactToPokedex
  if nationaldex goto ManyMore
  if not flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Rate
  if var SEEN >= 210 goto Complete
label Rate
  # SandgemTownLab_CheckShouldRatePokedex: once the assistant has shown the player round the town
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE >= 2 goto Ratings
  say "Your adventure is waiting, {player}. Go and see the Pokémon of Sinnoh for yourself."
  end
label Ratings
  ask "Ah, {player}. Have you come to show me how your Pokédex is getting on?"
  if no goto Afraid
  call Rating
  end
label Afraid
  say "Hm? Are you afraid to show me? I don't bite, you know. Not as a rule."
  end
label Regards
  say "One thing more, {player}. Should your road cross Cynthia's, tell her that an old professor remembers her kindly."
  end
label ManyMore
  say "The world holds many, many more kinds of Pokémon than Sinnoh does, {player}. Go and meet them. I envy you!"
  end
label Complete
  # SandgemTownLab_ShowCompleteLocalDex
  ask "{player}! You have the look of someone with something to show me. Is it your Pokédex?"
  if yes goto Shown
  say "No? Then what is the matter? Speak up!"
  end
label Shown
  say "Let me see... Hm! So you have seen {var:SEEN} kinds of Pokémon in Sinnoh... Why, that is every one of them!"
  say "Excellent work, {player}! I could not have asked for more. In sixty years I have not..."
  call OakVisits
  end
label Before
  sayown
  end


# PokedexRatings_RateLocalDex: what the professor makes of the Pokédex, by how many of Sinnoh's Pokémon it has seen
# (the original's Pokedex_GetRatingMessageID_Local: its steps at 15, 30, 45, 60, 80, 100, 120, 140, 160, 180, 200,
# all but the last, and every one)
script Rating
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Hm... {var:SEEN} kinds of Pokémon seen in Sinnoh so far."
  if var SEEN <= 15 goto Upto15
  if var SEEN <= 30 goto Upto30
  if var SEEN <= 45 goto Upto45
  if var SEEN <= 60 goto Upto60
  if var SEEN <= 80 goto Upto80
  if var SEEN <= 100 goto Upto100
  if var SEEN <= 120 goto Upto120
  if var SEEN <= 140 goto Upto140
  if var SEEN <= 160 goto Upto160
  if var SEEN <= 180 goto Upto180
  if var SEEN <= 200 goto Upto200
  if var SEEN <= 209 goto Upto209
  say "Every Pokémon of Sinnoh, and so soon! Remarkable. Make your name at the Pokémon League, {player}, and then show me this again. I shall have something for you."
  return
label Upto15
  say "You have hardly begun. Walk into tall grass wherever you find it: that is where Pokémon live."
  return
label Upto30
  say "A start. Remember that some Pokémon show themselves only in the morning, and others only after dark."
  return
label Upto45
  say "It is coming along. Have you tried the trees that smell of honey? Some Pokémon are met nowhere else."
  return
label Upto60
  say "Good. A fishing rod would show you what lives under the water. There is more of it than you would think."
  return
label Upto80
  say "You are putting your back into it. A Pokémon a Trainer sends out counts too, so battle everyone you meet."
  return
label Upto100
  say "Now it grows interesting. Look in the caves, and out on the sea routes, and wherever the weather is foul."
  return
label Upto120
  say "More than a hundred! You have a real eye for this, {player}. Keep at it."
  return
label Upto140
  say "Splendid. Fewer and fewer people could tell me anything about Sinnoh's Pokémon that you could not."
  return
label Upto160
  say "Most of Sinnoh, in one Pokédex. The ones you lack are the ones that hide well. Think where you have not been."
  return
label Upto180
  say "Superb! You are close now. The strongest Trainers keep the rarest Pokémon: seek them out."
  return
label Upto200
  say "Hardly any are left! I find I am counting them with you. A professor should not be so excited."
  return
label Upto209
  say "All but a few! Do not stop now, {player}. Legends are Pokémon too: where the stories lead, go and look."
  return


# SandgemTownLab_EnableNationalDex: Professor Oak's visit. The player stands before the desk, across it from
# Professor Rowan.
script OakVisits
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_PROF_OAK
  place prof_oak 5 8 up
  show prof_oak
  sound "door_open"
  face player down
  wait 0.5
  walk player left 1
  face player right
  walk prof_oak up 3
  emote prof_rowan exclaim
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "Good day to you, Professor Rowan! I hope I am not interrupting."
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Well, I'll be! Professor Oak! My old colleague, all the way from Kanto. It has been far too long!"
  face prof_rowan left
  wait 0.3
  face prof_rowan down
  say "Let me introduce {player}, who has been helping me with the Pokédex. Every Pokémon of Sinnoh is in it. Every one!"
  walk prof_oak left 1
  face prof_oak up
  face player down
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "Is that so? Then you are just the person I hoped to meet. Allow me, {player}: I should like to upgrade that Pokédex of yours."
  # The Fight Area's blockades wait for this where the battle before the Frontier's gate is already won
  if var VAR_FIGHT_AREA_STATE >= 2 setflag FLAG_HIDE_FIGHT_AREA_BLOCKADE
  nationaldex
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player}'s Pokédex was upgraded. It now has a National Mode!"
  say "It can now record the Pokémon of every land, not only Sinnoh's. Filling it will be no small task, mind."
  # The PC grows with the Pokédex (plan 03 · D13: nationaldex gives it the boxes too)
  say "You will want room to keep them, too. I have seen to it that your PC has a hundred boxes now."
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Hmph! If anyone can do it, {player} will. I would stake my moustache on it."
  speaker "Prof. Oak"
  say "Ha! I believe you. One more piece of news: Pal Park, on Route 221, is open at last. Pokémon from far away are let loose there to be caught again."
  say "Do pay it a visit, {player}. I shall be there myself for a while. Well, I must be going!"
  walk prof_oak down 3 right 1
  sound "door_close"
  hide prof_oak
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_PROF_OAK
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "And off he goes. He never could stay in one place for more than five minutes."
  wait 0.25
  walk player right 1
  face player up
  say "Now, {player}. Finishing the Sinnoh Pokédex deserves more than an old man's praise. This is for you."
  give "Poké Radar"
  say "That is the Poké Radar. Use it in tall grass, and it finds Pokémon that would never show themselves otherwise. You will need it for that new Pokédex."
  return


# The assistant by the door (the original's scripts_counterpart_talk.s, between the professor's scene and the
# player's arrival in Sunyshore City).
script Assistant
  face self player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I keep turning it over, {player}. What you saw on the mountain..."
  say "Do you suppose those were the Pokémon the legends mean? The ones Sinnoh began with?"
  end
