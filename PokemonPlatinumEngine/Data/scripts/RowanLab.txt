# The first visit: the professor looks at the player's Pokémon, sees the bond already there, and asks the player
# to fill in a Pokédex of every Pokémon in Sinnoh. The assistant, who has one too, introduces themself.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE != 0 end
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_STATE == 0 end
  walk counterpart up 3
  walk player up 4
  face counterpart prof_rowan
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Ah, there you are. Let me have a look at that Pokémon of yours."
  say "...Hm. It seems happy to be with you."
  say "Then trusting it to you was no mistake."
  say "When I first saw you two charging into the tall grass, I was shocked. But a bond is already growing between you and your Pokémon."
  say "Cherish that Pokémon, and cherish that bond."
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I'm glad! I could tell straight away that you're kind to Pokémon."
  music "common/rowan"
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Now. As you know, I am Rowan, and I study Pokémon. There is something I would ask of you."
label Ask
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  ask "Will you take this Pokédex, and record every kind of Pokémon you meet in Sinnoh for me?"
  if yes goto Takes
  say "...I see. Well, I am a patient man. I will ask you again."
  goto Ask
label Takes
  setflag FLAG_HAS_POKEDEX
  fanfare keyitem
  text "{player} received the Pokédex!"
  say "Go everywhere you can and meet every kind of Pokémon there is. That is what the Pokédex is for."
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I'm helping the professor fill in a Pokédex too!"
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Tell me, {player}: how did it feel, walking along Route 201 with a Pokémon at your side?"
  say "I have lived for sixty years, and I still feel that thrill. Your great adventure begins now."
  face counterpart player
  speaker "{assistant}"
  say "I'm {assistant}, by the way. I help the professor out. My first Pokémon is {assistantstarter}!"
  say "We're both working on the Pokédex now, so let's do our best. See you outside!"
  walk counterpart down 3
  hide counterpart
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_COUNTERPART
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SANDGEM_TOWN_COUNTERPART
  music area
  setvar VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE 1
  end


script Rowan
  if var VAR_SANDGEM_TOWN_LAB_STATE == 0 goto Before
  speaker "Prof. Rowan"
  say "Your adventure is waiting, {player}. Go and see the Pokémon of Sinnoh for yourself."
  end
label Before
  sayown
  end
