# A house on Route 13 (plan 22 · U7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Black 2's beats.
# The woman (2) gives a Power Lens once, her Wingull's find.


script WingullsFind
  face 2 player
  speaker "Lady"
  if flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_POWER_LENS goto Done
  say "This Wingull used to carry the mail to everyone in the neighbourhood!"
  say "I know! I'll give you the Power Lens it brought me!"
  give "Power Lens"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_POWER_LENS
  say "It doesn't matter to Wingull that it isn't much of a fighter. It's the best postman in Unova."
  end
label Done
  say "Wingull doesn't mind that it can't battle very well. It delivers the post all the same."
  end
