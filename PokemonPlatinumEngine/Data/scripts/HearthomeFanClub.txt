# The Pokémon Fan Club in Hearthome City (plan 06 · R14c), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md describes
# the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_hearthome_city_pokemon_fan_club.s).
#
# The chairman gives the Poffin Case, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_POKEMON_FAN_CLUB_POFFIN_CASE); the woman by
# the shelves reads how fond of the player the first of the team is (LEAD_FRIENDSHIP), on the original's seven steps.
# The club's Psyduck, Buneary and Clefairy are Pokémon of the field (common.Pokemon). The reporter of the TV's
# programme waits for plan 08.


script Chairman
  speaker "Chairman"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_POKEMON_FAN_CLUB_POFFIN_CASE goto Done
  say "Welcome, welcome! I am the chairman of the Pokémon Fan Club!"
  say "You love your Pokémon too, I can tell. Then you simply must have one of these!"
  give "Poffin Case"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_HEARTHOME_CITY_POKEMON_FAN_CLUB_POFFIN_CASE
  say "Fill it with Poffins and treat your Pokémon. The Poffin House is just next door, you know!"
  end
label Done
  say "We are, without question, the finest fan club in all of Sinnoh!"
  end


script Beauty
  speaker "Woman"
  say "Oh, is that your Pokémon? Let me have a look..."
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 255 goto Adores
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 200 goto Fond
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 150 goto Friendly
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 100 goto GettingUsed
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 50 goto NotUsed
  if var LEAD_FRIENDSHIP >= 1 goto Wary
  say "Oh dear... {lead} looks thoroughly fed up with you. Please be kinder to it."
  end
label Adores
  say "My, my! {lead} simply adores you. I can't imagine a happier Pokémon!"
  end
label Fond
  say "{lead} is very fond of you. You can see it in its eyes."
  end
label Friendly
  say "{lead} is quite friendly with you. Keep it up!"
  end
label GettingUsed
  say "{lead} is warming to you. Give it a little more time."
  end
label NotUsed
  say "{lead} isn't quite used to you yet."
  end
label Wary
  say "{lead} seems a bit wary of you. Try spending more time together."
  end
