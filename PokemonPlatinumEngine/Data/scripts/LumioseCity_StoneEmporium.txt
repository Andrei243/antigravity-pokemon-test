# Lumiose City's stone shop by Rouge Plaza (plan 23 · Z2, second part; its counter and its owner since the wave after
# Z4b). The lines are our own, on X and Y's beats as they are publicly described (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "Kalos's
# last places: the stone shop, the Battle Maison, the Chamber of Emptiness and the rest"): the shopkeeper's counter sells
# evolution stones (lumiose_stones in marts.json), and once a Champion of Kalos comes in the owner hands over the Mega
# Stone of the Kanto Pokémon the professor gave, which X and Y give before the League. FLAG_KALOS_KANTO_STONE_GIVEN is ours.


script Owner
  face self player
  speaker "Owner"
  if flag FLAG_KALOS_KANTO_STONE_GIVEN goto Given
  if not flag KalosHallOfFame goto Early
  if not flag FLAG_KALOS_RECEIVED_KANTO_STARTER goto Early
  say "The Champion, in my little shop! Professor Sycamore left something here for you before the League, and you never came by."
  say "A Mega Stone, for the Pokémon he brought from Kanto. Which one is yours?"
  choose "Which stone would you like?" "Venusaurite" "Charizardite X" "Charizardite Y" "Blastoisinite"
  if var RESULT == 0 goto Venusaur
  if var RESULT == 1 goto CharizardX
  if var RESULT == 2 goto CharizardY
  give "Blastoisinite"
  goto Done
label Venusaur
  give "Venusaurite"
  goto Done
label CharizardX
  give "Charizardite X"
  goto Done
label CharizardY
  give "Charizardite Y"
label Done
  setflag FLAG_KALOS_KANTO_STONE_GIVEN
  say "Hold it with your Key Stone, and see what that Pokémon really looks like."
  end
label Given
  say "The stones on the shelf are for sale. The one I kept for you was a gift, and it's yours now."
  end
label Early
  say "Every stone in this shop has a story. The best ones I keep behind the counter, for the right Trainer."
  end
