# the garden's end with the Grass Badge, who battles a challenger who has found all three. Our own lines.


# Brassius's battle for the Grass Badge. He Terastallizes his Sudowoodo into a Grass type. His levels are fixed,
# whatever order the gyms are taken in (plan 34 · decision 3)
script Leader
  face brassius player
  speaker "Brassius"
  if badge grass goto Won
  if var VAR_PALDEA_STORY < 7 goto NotEnrolled
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_ARTAZON_TEST_1 goto NotYet
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_ARTAZON_TEST_2 goto NotYet
  if not flag FLAG_PALDEA_ARTAZON_TEST_3 goto NotYet
  scene "Brassius"
  say "You found them all! Your eye for art is keen. I am Brassius, Artazon's gym leader, and an artist of the grass."
  say "A battle is a sculpture made in a moment. Let us shape something magnificent together!"
  battle brassius as "paldea_brassius"
  say "Bravo! A masterpiece of a battle. Take this, the Grass Badge, as a frame for your triumph."
  givebadge grass
  text "{player} received the Grass Badge from Brassius!"
  setflag FLAG_PALDEA_DEFEATED_ARTAZON_GYM
  defeat "paldea_gym_trainer_hedge"
  say "Go forth and find your art, young challenger. Victory Road has much more to show you."
  endscene
  end
label NotYet
  say "Ah, a challenger! But my Sunflora are still hiding about Artazon. Find all three, and then we shall make art."
  end
label NotEnrolled
  say "A challenger who is not yet a student? Enrol at the academy first, and then we shall make art."
  end
label Won
  say "Your battle still inspires me. I have begun a new sculpture in its honour."
  end
