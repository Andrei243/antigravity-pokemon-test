# Team Aqua's hideout, its entrance hall (plan 21 · H7, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/AquaHideout_1F/scripts.inc).
#
# Two grunts block the way in until Team Aqua has taken Captain Stern's submarine at Slateport (their flags set by
# slateport_city_harbor.txt). Each lets slip where Archie is by how far the story has gone: before the orbs are taken,
# after the Magma Emblem (FLAG_RECEIVED_RED_OR_BLUE_ORB) and after Groudon has woken (FLAG_GROUDON_AWAKENED_MAGMA_HIDEOUT).


script Guard1
  face self player
  speaker "Team Aqua Grunt"
  if flag FLAG_GROUDON_AWAKENED_MAGMA_HIDEOUT goto Submarine
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_RED_OR_BLUE_ORB goto MagmaHideout
  say "What? What do you want with Team Aqua? The boss isn't here. He's off to grab something important!"
  say "Where? Wahaha! As if I'd tell you something like that!"
  end
label MagmaHideout
  say "What? Are you one of Team Magma's? I heard they're trying to wake some mighty Pokémon in their hideout."
  say "But where is their hideout, anyway?"
  end
label Submarine
  say "What? What do you want with Team Aqua? The boss isn't here. He's gone to take a submarine for himself!"
  say "Where? Wahaha! As if I'd tell you something like that!"
  end


script Guard2
  face self player
  speaker "Team Aqua Grunt"
  if flag FLAG_GROUDON_AWAKENED_MAGMA_HIDEOUT goto Slateport
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_RED_OR_BLUE_ORB goto MtChimney
  say "What? What do you want with Team Aqua? The boss isn't here. He's on his way to Mt. Pyre, on Route 122!"
  say "Why? Wahaha! As if I'd tell you something like that!"
  end
label MtChimney
  say "What? Are you one of Team Magma's? I heard they're after some mighty Pokémon at Mt. Chimney."
  say "What's that Pokémon like, I wonder?"
  end
label Slateport
  say "What? What do you want with Team Aqua? The boss isn't here. He's on his way to Slateport City!"
  say "Why? Wahaha! As if I'd tell you something like that!"
  end
