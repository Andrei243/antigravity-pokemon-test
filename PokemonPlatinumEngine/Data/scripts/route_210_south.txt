# Route 210, south of the fog (plan 06 · R14a, plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language.
#
# The original's arrival script: in the morning the joggers battle, and at any other time their doubles on the same
# tiles only jog past. The swap is made as the player comes, so it holds until they come again.


script OnEnter
  if time morning goto Battle
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT_NO_BATTLE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT
  end
label Battle
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT_NO_BATTLE
  end


# The Psyduck across the road north (the original's script 1). They hold their heads and won't budge until the
# SecretPotion cures them; then they go off and stay gone (FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_PSYDUCK). The SecretPotion, and
# Cynthia, who comes up the road after they leave, are the next chapter's (plan 02 · S9).
script Psyduck
  cry "Psyduck"
  if item "Secret Potion" goto Potion
  text "The Psyduck are clutching their heads and won't budge an inch." "They don't look like they'll be moving any time soon."
  end
label Potion
  ask "The Psyduck are clutching their heads and won't budge. Use the Secret Potion?"
  if no end
  text "{player} used the Secret Potion!" "The Psyduck's headaches seem to have cleared up!"
  cry "Psyduck"
  emote psyduck_1 exclaim
  emote psyduck_2 exclaim
  emote psyduck_3 exclaim
  emote psyduck_4 exclaim
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_PSYDUCK
  hide psyduck_1
  hide psyduck_2
  hide psyduck_3
  hide psyduck_4
  end
