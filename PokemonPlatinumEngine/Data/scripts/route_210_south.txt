# Route 210, south of the fog (plan 06 · R14a, plan 02 · S7). docs/scripts.md describes the language.
#
# The original's arrival script: in the morning the joggers battle, and at any other time their doubles on the same
# tiles only jog past. The swap is made as the player comes, so it holds until they come again.


script OnEnter
  if time morning goto Battle
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT_NO_BATTLE
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT
  end
label Battle
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_JOGGER_WYATT_NO_BATTLE
  end


# The Psyduck across the road north (the original's script 1). They hold their heads and won't budge until the
# SecretPotion cures them; then they waddle off up the road and stay gone (FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_PSYDUCK). Cynthia
# gives the SecretPotion at Valor Lakefront (plan 02 · S8, valor_lakefront.txt). Once they have gone she comes up the
# road behind the player (plan 02 · S9): the player can only face the Psyduck from the two tiles below them, and she
# stops on the tile below that. She asks, until the answer is yes, that the Old Charm be taken to her grandmother, the
# elder of Celestic Town (celestic_town.txt), and goes back the way she came (FLAG_USED_SECRETPOTION). The Secret
# Potion stays in the bag, as in the original.
script Psyduck
  cry "Psyduck"
  if item "Secret Potion" goto Potion
  text "The Psyduck are clutching their heads and won't budge an inch." "They don't look like they'll be moving any time soon."
  end
label Potion
  ask "The Psyduck are clutching their heads and won't budge. Use the Secret Potion?"
  if no end
  text "{player} used the Secret Potion!" "The Psyduck's headaches seem to have cleared up!"
  cry "Psyduck"
  emote psyduck_3 exclaim 0.5
  emote psyduck_4 exclaim 0.5
  emote psyduck_2 exclaim 0.5
  emote psyduck_1 exclaim 0.5
  # Off up the road they go, the ones at the back first
  move psyduck_3 up 9
  move psyduck_4 up 9
  wait 0.3
  move psyduck_1 up 9
  move psyduck_2 up 9
  waitmoves
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_PSYDUCK
  hide psyduck_3
  hide psyduck_4
  hide psyduck_1
  hide psyduck_2
  wait 0.8
  # Cynthia comes up the road behind the player (the original clears her flag and adds her)
  if var PLAYER_X == 560 place cynthia 560 596 up
  if var PLAYER_X != 560 place cynthia 561 596 up
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_CYNTHIA
  show cynthia
  walk cynthia up 7
  face player down
  speaker "Cynthia"
  say "So you used the Secret Potion I gave you. I'm glad it worked."
  say "Psyduck are prone to terrible headaches, you know. Nobody has ever worked out what sets them off."
  say "Oh, while I think of it... could I ask a big favour of you?"
  say "I'd like you to take this Old Charm to my grandmother in Celestic Town."
  say "There are some rare Pokémon living between here and there, so the trip should be worth your while."
  ask "Will you take it to her for me?"
label Ask
  if yes goto Yes
  say "I know it's a lot to ask. But I'd really like it to be you."
  ask "Will you go to Celestic Town for me?"
  goto Ask
label Yes
  give "Old Charm"
  say "My grandmother has a rather commanding air about her. You'll know her the moment you see her."
  say "She's the elder of Celestic Town. As for where it is, look at your Town Map. You can't miss it."
  say "I'm counting on you! I'm sure we'll meet again along the way."
  walk cynthia down 9
  hide cynthia
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_CYNTHIA
  setflag FLAG_USED_SECRETPOTION
  end


# The trainer resting by the café (the original's script 3) gives TM51, Roost, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_TM51).
script Roost
  speaker "Ace Trainer"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_TM51 goto Given
  say "Whew! I'm taking a breather before the climb. If I were a bird Pokémon, you'd call this roosting. Ha ha!"
  say "...You look like you could do with a rest yourself. Here, this one's for you."
  give "TM51"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_ROUTE_210_SOUTH_TM51
label Given
  say "Even bird Pokémon need to land now and then. Nobody can stay up in the air forever!"
  end
