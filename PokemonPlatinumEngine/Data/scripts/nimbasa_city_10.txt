# NimbasaCity_TennisCourt: Nimbasa City's tennis field (plan 42 · PS5; rulings, "Sports days"). Black 2's place, our words.
# Its three athletes battle once a day each (FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_*), with the team the story calls for: the first
# under five of Unova's Badges, the second from five, the third with all eight, the fourth after the Hall of Fame
# (SportsDays.TierOf; SportsDayTests hold these scripts to SportsDays).

script Athlete1
  face self player
  if flag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_TENNIS_1 goto Done1
  say "Love, fifteen, thirty... Let's battle and keep score!"
  if flag UnovaHallOfFame goto Fourth1
  if badges >= 8 goto Third1
  if badges >= 5 goto Second1
  battle self as "unova_smasher_elena"
  goto Won1
label Second1
  battle self as "unova_smasher_elena_2"
  goto Won1
label Third1
  battle self as "unova_smasher_elena_3"
  goto Won1
label Fourth1
  battle self as "unova_smasher_elena_4"
label Won1
  setflag FLAG_DAILY_UNOVA_TENNIS_1
label Done1
  say "Match point to you. Good game!"
  end
