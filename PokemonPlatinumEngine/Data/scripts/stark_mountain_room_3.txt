# The heart of Stark Mountain (plan 02 · S15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own,
# on the original's beats (scripts_stark_mountain_room_3.s).
#
# A short hall with the Magma Stone on its place at the head of it. The first time anyone comes in
# (VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_STATE 0) Charon stands before the stone between his two grunts, telling himself how the
# others have thrown Team Galactic away, and Buck is inside the way in. The mountain trembles; Buck and the player go
# up the hall; Charon takes the stone and says what he means to do with it (wake Heatran, hold the volcano over
# Sinnoh's head and be paid for it). A voice shouts, a Croagunk darts out and snatches the stone from his hands, and
# the cracked rock in the corner turns out to have been Looker all day. The grunts take one look at the International
# Police and run; two policemen come in and Charon is led off to somewhere cooler; and Looker, who finds it far too
# hot, takes everyone outside, where stark_mountain_outside.txt goes on.
#
# What the scene leaves is what the original's does: Charon arrested (FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN: the Elite
# Four and the Champion bring their stronger teams from now on, and the Battleground's door is the story's to open),
# the state 1, Looker and Buck shown outside (VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_STATE 2), Buck and the rival shown before the
# Battleground in the Survival Area, Buck shown inside it, and Looker back at Veilstone's Game Corner.
#
# Heatran waits at the head of the hall afterwards, but only once Buck has been spoken to at his place
# (FLAG_TALKED_TO_BATTLEGROUND_BUCK: plan 02 · S15, part c), for a Champion with the National Pokédex, while the state
# is 1: the room's own arrival decides, as the original's OnTransition does. It battles whoever speaks to it, at level
# 50, and the battle may be run from. Lost, the player wakes in a Pokémon Center and Heatran waits here still. Caught,
# it is gone for good (FLAG_CAUGHT_HEATRAN). Knocked out or run from, it sinks back into the volcano (state 2) until
# the Hall of Fame is next entered, whose script brings it back while it hasn't been caught
# (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt). Any battle not lost unlocks the Vs. Seeker's last level of rematches.
#
# The tremor is the picture's shake and a rumble, as it is in the original (its ScrCmd_29F shakes the camera from side
# to side for two seconds; nobody in the room is shaken, so plan 09 · L12's shake has no part in it). Stood in for:
# Looker's tune (plan 05 · A6); Buck's and the policemen's looks (plan 11).


# StarkMountainRoom3_OnTransition, and the frame table's scene
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_STATE >= 1 setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_BUCK
  call HeatranOrNot
  if var VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_STATE == 0 call Charon
  end


# StarkMountainRoom3_TryHideHeatran
script HeatranOrNot
  if flag FLAG_CAUGHT_HEATRAN goto Hide
  if not flag FLAG_GAME_COMPLETED goto Hide
  if not nationaldex goto Hide
  if not flag FLAG_TALKED_TO_BATTLEGROUND_BUCK goto Hide
  if var VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_STATE != 1 goto Hide
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_HEATRAN
  return
label Hide
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_HEATRAN
  return


# StarkMountainRoom3_OnFrame_Charon: the player stands a step inside the way in (7, 16), Buck beside them (6, 16)
script Charon
  # A game saved further up the hall before there was anyone here is brought down to the way in
  if var PLAYER_Y != 16 place player 7 16 up
  scene "Charon"
  place buck 6 16 up
  face player up
  # The hall is longer than the picture is tall from the way in: the camera looks at its middle, where the stone and
  # Charon are seen from the first word, and the two who come up the hall stand clear of the text box
  camera pan 7 12 0.8
  speaker "Charon"
  say "...Cyrus has vanished. Mars and Jupiter have walked out. And Saturn says he will mind what is left of Team Galactic, and will have nothing to do with me."
  say "Fools! Fools, every one of them, to throw Team Galactic away when there is still so much to be made of it!"
  sound "thunder_rumble"
  camera shake 0.9
  wait 0.3
  move buck up 2
  walk player up 2
  waitmoves
  face buck up
  speaker "Buck"
  say "Hey! You lot! Team Galactic! What do you think you're up to in my mountain?!"
  walk charon up 2
  wait 0.4
  sound "select"
  hide magma_stone
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_MAGMA_STONE
  face charon down
  speaker "Charon"
  say "Eh? What's this, now? Followed me all the way in, have you? I do like to see children trying their hardest."
  say "A pity you are too late. With this Magma Stone I shall wake the Pokémon Heatran, and with Heatran the volcano will do as I tell it."
  say "All Sinnoh will pay me to keep it quiet. Such a lot of money! And I shall be the boss of a new Team Galactic. Tee hee hee!"
  speaker "???"
  say "Go! Go! Go!"
  cry "Croagunk"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_CROAGUNK
  show croagunk
  walk croagunk left 2 up 3 fast
  face croagunk up
  sound "bump"
  wait 0.2
  walk croagunk down 3 right 2 fast
  face charon left
  wait 0.2
  face charon right
  wait 0.2
  face charon down
  face grunt_m_1 right
  face grunt_m_2 right
  speaker "Charon"
  say "No! The Magma Stone! What...? What is going on here?!"
  wait 0.5
  speaker "???"
  say "Excellent work, Croagunk!"
  hide croagunk
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_CROAGUNK
  hide rock_smash_looker
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_ROCK_SMASH_LOOKER
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_LOOKER
  show looker
  emote buck exclaim
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Pah! To pass a whole day as a rock, without one sneeze, without one scratch of the nose: it was no small thing!"
  walk looker left 3 up 3
  face grunt_m_1 up
  face grunt_m_2 up
  say "Now, old man. The International Police would be most grateful for your help. You have a great deal to tell us about this new Team Galactic."
  say "Cyrus is gone, and Mars, and Jupiter. There is nobody left to ask but you."
  face grunt_m_1 right
  speaker "Grunt"
  say "Hey! That's the International Police!"
  face grunt_m_2 left
  say "It is! It's the International Police, all right! Then there's only one thing for it!"
  wait 0.5
  say "Run for it!"
  move grunt_m_2 down 5 left 1 fast
  wait 0.15
  move grunt_m_1 right 2 down 5 left 1 fast
  wait 0.4
  face player right
  wait 0.4
  face player up
  waitmoves
  sound "stairs"
  hide grunt_m_1
  hide grunt_m_2
  face charon down
  speaker "Charon"
  say "Gah... What a mess... Er. International Police, did you say? I can't think what you mean."
  say "A new Team Galactic? Squeezing money out of Sinnoh with Heatran? The ramblings of a harmless old man! A joke! All of it, a joke!"
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Then you shall tell us the joke from the beginning. We have all the time in the world."
  say "Ah, no, no. No struggling. Three against one, you would not enjoy it. Officers! This way, if you please!"
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_POLICEMEN
  place policeman_1 7 17 up
  place policeman_2 7 17 up
  hide policeman_2
  show policeman_1
  sound "stairs"
  walk policeman_1 up 2 right 1 up 3 left 2
  face policeman_1 up
  show policeman_2
  walk policeman_2 right 1 up 4 left 1
  face policeman_2 up
  speaker "Looker"
  say "He is an old man, and this heat can do him no good. Quickly: take him somewhere cool. A cell would do very nicely!"
  # Looker goes round behind him and sees him down the hall
  move looker left 1 up 1 right 1 down 2
  wait 0.35
  move charon down 3
  waitmoves
  # In file round the player and out: a policeman, Charon, a policeman
  move policeman_2 right 1 down 3 left 1 down 1
  wait 0.35
  move charon down 1 right 1 down 3 left 1 down 1
  wait 0.35
  move policeman_1 right 1 down 1 right 1 down 3 left 1 down 1
  wait 0.3
  face player right
  face buck right
  wait 0.6
  face player down
  face buck down
  waitmoves
  sound "stairs"
  hide charon
  hide policeman_1
  hide policeman_2
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_TEAM_GALACTIC
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_POLICEMEN
  face player up
  face buck up
  face looker down
  camera release 0.6
  speaker "Looker"
  say "{player}! And you, the boy I have not met! There is something I must say to you both."
  say "First of all: it is far too hot in here! Let us get out of this oven!"
  setvar VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_STATE 2
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_BUCK
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_LOOKER
  setvar VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_STATE 1
  setflag FLAG_ARRESTED_CHARON_STARK_MOUNTAIN
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_GAME_CORNER_LOOKER
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SURVIVAL_AREA_RIVAL
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_BATTLEGROUND_BUCK
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_SURVIVAL_AREA_BUCK
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_BUCK
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_OUTSIDE_LOOKER
  warp "Sinnoh" 751 233 up
  endscene
  return


# StarkMountainRoom3_Heatran
script Heatran
  setflag FLAG_BATTLED_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_HEATRAN
  cry "Heatran"
  speaker none
  say "Gwooh... grrrohh!"
  wildbattle "Heatran" 50
  setvar VAR_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_STATE 2
  setflag FLAG_UNLOCKED_VS_SEEKER_LVL_5
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_STARK_MOUNTAIN_ROOM_3_HEATRAN
  hide heatran
  if result == 3 goto Caught
  say "Heatran sank back into the depths of the volcano..."
  end
label Caught
  setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_HEATRAN
  end


# Whoever of the scene's people is spoken to (nobody is: it has them from the first step in)
script Galactic
  speaker none
  text "Nobody here has a word to spare."
  end

# Buck, inside the way in until the scene begins
script BuckWaits
  face self player
  speaker "Buck"
  say "Shh! Look. That's them, right up at the top of the hall."
  end

  end
