# Roxie's club, the Virbank Gym's hall (plan 22 · U5, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Black 2's beats (the decompilation's res/field/scripts/virbank_city_gym_2.s, read for the order alone).
#
# Roxie (the room's person 0) can't hear a challenger over her band until both bandmates are beaten (1, the roughneck
# Nicky; 2, the guitarist Billy Jo); then she battles for the Toxic Badge, the region's second (`givebadge toxic`),
# and gives TM09 (FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM09), which our data's table, Platinum's, fills with its own move, and the town's
# count moves on (VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK 3). On the way out the studios' scout (3) stops the player, and Roxie (4, the
# scene's own, hidden by FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROXIE_SCENE) runs off after her father; Team Plasma is waiting on the dock
# (VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK 4, virbank_city.txt). Black 2's TRAINER_LEADER_ROXIE is the Leader of the game's usual
# difficulty; her _2 is the Challenge Mode's, plan 13's.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


script Roxie
  face self player
  speaker "Roxie"
  if badge toxic goto Won
  if not defeated "unova_roughneck_nicky" goto TooLoud
  if not defeated "unova_guitarist_billy_jo" goto TooLoud
  scene "Roxie"
  say "Hey, you made it through my band! Bet my poison's still ringing in your ears!"
  say "I'm Roxie, Leader of this Gym and singer of this band. Get ready, 'cause I'm gonna knock some sense into you!"
  battle self as "unova_leader_roxie"
  say "Aaargh! What am I doing, losing like that?! ...Guess that means you're the real deal."
  say "That stank, but I gave it everything I had, and now I feel totally refreshed! Here, the Toxic Badge!"
  givebadge toxic
  text "{player} received the Toxic Badge from Roxie!"
  defeat "unova_roughneck_nicky"
  defeat "unova_guitarist_billy_jo"
  if var VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK < 3 setvar VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK 3
label Tm
  say "And take this TM too. Use it however you like, just make it loud!"
  give "TM09"
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM09
  say "Doing what you love, the way you want to do it, with the Pokémon you love. That's the best, right?"
  say "Just don't go causing trouble for other people while you're at it. Got it?"
  endscene
  end
label TooLoud
  say "...!"
  text "The music's too loud! She can't hear a word over the band."
  end
label Won
  if not flag FLAG_UNOVA_GOT_TM09 goto Tm
  say "Your Pokémon WANTED that win! Keep going like that, and do all sorts of amazing stuff!"
  end


# The way back from the stage after the Toxic Badge (our trigger, on 3): the studios' scout, and Roxie remembering her
# father
script TheScout
  scene "The scout and Roxie"
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROXIE_SCENE
  place 3 2 7 up
  place 4 5 7 up
  show 3
  show 4
  face 3 player
  speaker "Scout"
  say "Hey, you there! Watching you battle Roxie, I just knew it. You've got star quality!"
  say "I'm a talent scout for Pokéstar Studios. Come and see us! Well... once the auditions are done. They're shooting nonstop right now."
  face 4 3
  speaker "Roxie"
  say "Pokéstar Studios?! Oh, no, I forgot about the old man! I have to drag him back to his ship!"
  walk 4 right 3 fast
  hide 4
  speaker "Scout"
  say "Oh dear. I hope the captain's audition is over before she gets there... See you at the studios one day!"
  walk 3 down 1
  hide 3
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROXIE_SCENE
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_VIRBANK_SCOUT
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_ROXIE_GYM
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_VIRBANK_DOCK
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_POKESTAR_GUARD
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_VIRBANK 4
  endscene
  end


script Scout
  face 3 player
  speaker "Scout"
  say "Roxie puts on a show, doesn't she? If only she'd come to the studios... She's always too busy with her band."
  end
