# Solaceon Town's Pokémon Day Care (plan 06 · R15). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# the original's beats (scripts_pokemon_day_care.s). The Day-Care Lady at her counter runs common.DayCareLady.
#
# Once the player has left a Pokémon with the couple (FLAG_STORED_POKEMON_AT_DAY_CARE), a fan of theirs waits by the
# table and shares the Pokétch Company's Day-Care Checker app, which shows the two left here and any Egg they found.
# He is hidden by the original's own flag, FLAG_HIDE_DAY_CARE_GYM_GUIDE (plan 02 · S14): a new game sets it
# (common.ChapterEleven), the Day-Care Lady's script clears it as the first Pokémon is left, and so does the Hall of
# Fame's, so a Champion finds him here whether or not they ever left one.


# PokemonDayCare_OnTransition
script OnEnter
  if flag FLAG_STORED_POKEMON_AT_DAY_CARE clearflag FLAG_HIDE_DAY_CARE_GYM_GUIDE
  end


script AppFan
  if poketchapp DayCareChecker goto Have
  say "Are you a fan of the Day-Care couple too? I've got hold of something rather handy." "It's the Pokétch Company's newest app, made with the couple's help. Here, you have it!"
  poketchapp DayCareChecker
  text "{player}'s Pokétch now has the Day-Care Checker app!"
  end
label Have
  say "With the Day-Care Checker you can see how the Pokémon you left here are getting on, wherever you are." "As for me, I can't bring myself to leave mine with them... So here I stay."
  end
