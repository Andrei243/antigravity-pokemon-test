# Castelia City's avenue along the bay (plan 22 · U5, part c), a map of ours (Data/maps/CasteliaCity.json): Black 2
# lays the avenue out on rails, which its data keeps outside the decompilation's text, so the reader leaves it out
# and this map stands in its place, the streets' and the piers' mouths in the order Black 2's rails have them. The
# lines are our own, on Black 2's beats (res/field/scripts/castelia_city.s, read for the order alone).
# VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA is the city's count (castelia_city_10.txt has it whole).
#
# The people: the Harlequin (harlequin), who welcomes a first-time visitor with a Bicycle (Black 2's Castelia
# Harlequin Hunt, whose medals are plan 13 · V9's, is left out: its Bicycle is the beat kept); Iris (iris,
# FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_AVENUE), who runs on to Thumb Pier once she has heard where Team Plasma might hide.


script OnEnter
  call UnovaCatchUp
  end


# The pier's mouth as the player first comes up from Prime Pier (our trigger on 38 to 41, 11, at 2)
script HarlequinScene
  scene "The Harlequin's welcome"
  face harlequin player
  emote harlequin exclaim
  speaker "Harlequin"
  say "Welcome, welcome! Is this your very first time in Castelia City? I can always tell!"
  say "Every first-time visitor gets a gift from the city, and yours is a Bicycle! These avenues go on and on, you know."
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 3
  if item "Bicycle" goto Already
  give "Bicycle"
  say "Ride it anywhere outside! Up Gym Street to the north-east, there's a Pokémon Gym worth a visit, too."
  endscene
  end
label Already
  say "Oh, you've brought a Bicycle of your own? Splendid! Then ride on, and enjoy our city!"
  say "Up Gym Street to the north-east, there's a Pokémon Gym worth a visit, too."
  endscene
  end


script Harlequin
  face harlequin player
  speaker "Harlequin"
  say "Castelia City never sleeps! Five streets, four piers and more towers than anyone has ever counted."
  end


# The avenue west of Castelia Street, once Iris has heard of Team Plasma on Gym Street (our trigger on 11, 6 to 10, at
# 4): she calls the player on and runs down to Thumb Pier
script IrisCalls
  scene "Iris on the avenue"
  face iris player
  emote iris exclaim
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Hey, over here! Come on, come on!"
  say "If Team Plasma were hiding in Castelia, there are only two places I can think of: Narrow Street, and Thumb Pier, right down there!"
  say "Narrow Street's always crowded, so... Thumb Pier! Let's go have a look!"
  walk iris down 4 fast
  hide iris
  setflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_AVENUE
  clearflag FLAG_UNOVA_HIDE_IRIS_PIER
  setvar VAR_UNOVA_CASTELIA 5
  endscene
  end


script IrisAvenue
  face iris player
  speaker "Iris"
  say "Thumb Pier's just down there. Hurry!"
  end
