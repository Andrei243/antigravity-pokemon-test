# The deepest room of Snowpoint Temple (plan 02 · S15, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on the original's beats (scripts_snowpoint_temple_b5f.s).
#
# Regigigas stands on its dais in the middle of the ice, as still as a statue, and reads as one: the words cut under
# it say that the king rises when the three are gathered. With Regirock, Regice and Registeel all in the team it
# wakes (FLAG_AWAKENED_REGIGIGAS, for good) and battles, at level 1, as in Platinum; the battle may be run from. Lost,
# the player wakes in a Pokémon Center and Regigigas waits here still, awake. Caught, it is gone for good
# (FLAG_CAUGHT_REGIGIGAS). Knocked out or run from, it is gone from the room (the original hides it as the room loads
# again behind the battle) until the Hall of Fame is next entered, whose script brings it back while it hasn't been
# caught, for whoever has the National Pokédex (PokemonLeagueHallOfFame.txt).
#
# The three it asks for are not to be had in Sinnoh by the story alone: Platinum opens their rooms (the Rock Peak
# Ruins on Route 228, the Iceberg Ruins in Mt. Coronet, the Iron Ruins on Iron Island) only to whoever travels with a
# Regigigas from one of its events, and here those rooms stay shut (docs/mechanics/rulings.md, "After the Hall of
# Fame, part b"). A team that has the three by trade or from another region wakes it all the same.
#
# Stood in for until plan 09 lands (L12, L14): the original shakes the statue itself as it wakes; here the picture
# shakes, with a rumble.


# SnowpointTempleB5F_Regigigas
script Regigigas
  speaker none
  if flag FLAG_AWAKENED_REGIGIGAS goto Wakes
  say "It has the shape of a Pokémon, and the stillness of a statue. No statue ever felt so heavy with strength."
  say "{player} read the words cut into the dais: \"One of rock. One of ice. One of steel. Bring the three together before the king, and the king shall rise.\""
  if not has "Regirock" end
  if not has "Regice" end
  if not has "Registeel" end
  setflag FLAG_AWAKENED_REGIGIGAS
  wait 0.4
  sound "thunder_rumble"
  camera shake 1.2
  wait 0.3
label Wakes
  cry "Regigigas"
  say "Zu... zut... gigigi!"
  wildbattle "Regigigas" 1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_SNOWPOINT_TEMPLE_B5F_REGIGIGAS
  hide regigigas
  if result == 3 goto Caught
  say "Regigigas stood still as stone again, and then it was gone from sight..."
  end
label Caught
  setflag FLAG_CAUGHT_REGIGIGAS
  end
