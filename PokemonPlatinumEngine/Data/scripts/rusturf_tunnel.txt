# Rusturf Tunnel (plan 21 · H2, part b). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on Emerald's
# beats (data/maps/RusturfTunnel/scripts.inc).
#
# VAR_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_STATE 2: the grunt with the Devon Goods has fled into the tunnel with Mr. Briney's Peeko and stands
# at its dead end; the first step toward him (the area's triggers 1 and 2) has him back away; 3 cornered. Beaten, he
# gives the goods back and runs, and Mr. Briney comes for his Peeko (FLAG_RECOVERED_DEVON_GOODS,
# VAR_RUSTBORO_CITY_STATE 4). Once the PokéNav is had, Wanda's young man digs at the rocks, with Wanda on their far side:
# her cousin Wally is to stay with her family in Verdanturf. Breaking through is plan 21 · H3's, with Rock Smash.


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_STATE != 2 end
  place rusturf_tunnel_peeko 13 4 left
  place rusturf_tunnel_grunt 13 5 left
  end


script GruntBacksUp
  scene "The grunt cornered"
  face rusturf_tunnel_grunt player
  emote rusturf_tunnel_grunt exclaim
  speaker "Team Aqua Grunt"
  say "Huh? You followed me all the way in here? Stay back! Don't come any closer!"
  walk rusturf_tunnel_grunt back right 1
  setvar VAR_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_STATE 3
  endscene
  end


script Peeko
  cry "Wingull"
  text "Peeko: Pihyoo..."
  text "The Wingull looks frightened."
  end


script Grunt
  scene "The Devon Goods recovered"
  face rusturf_tunnel_grunt player
  music "hoenn/eye_galactic"
  speaker "Team Aqua Grunt"
  say "Grr... What a dead end! Whoever dug this tunnel gave up halfway. And now I'm stuck with you!"
  say "Fine! If it's a battle you want, it's a battle you'll get!"
  battle rusturf_tunnel_grunt as "hoenn_grunt_rusturf_tunnel"
  say "Ugh, I lost? This job was supposed to be easy!"
  say "Take your stupid parts, then! I was told to grab them, not to fight kids over them!"
  give "Devon Goods"
  say "Team Aqua won't forget this. Out of my way!"
  walk rusturf_tunnel_grunt left 8 fast
  hide rusturf_tunnel_grunt
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_AQUA_GRUNT
  music area
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_BRINEY
  place rusturf_tunnel_briney 5 4 right
  show rusturf_tunnel_briney
  walk rusturf_tunnel_briney right 6
  cry "Wingull"
  speaker "Mr. Briney"
  say "Peeko! Oh, my dear Peeko, you're safe! I was so worried about you!"
  face rusturf_tunnel_briney player
  say "And you got her back for me? Thank you, thank you! I'm Briney. I live in the cottage on Route 104's beach."
  say "If ever there's anything I can do for you, come and see me. I'll never forget this!"
  walk rusturf_tunnel_briney left 6
  hide rusturf_tunnel_briney
  hide rusturf_tunnel_peeko
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_BRINEY
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_RUSTURF_TUNNEL_PEEKO
  clearflag FLAG_DEVON_GOODS_STOLEN
  setflag FLAG_RECOVERED_DEVON_GOODS
  setvar VAR_RUSTBORO_CITY_STATE 4
  setvar VAR_BRINEY_HOUSE_STATE 1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_ROUTE_116_MR_BRINEY
  endscene
  end


script WandasBoyfriend
  say "Wanda! Can you hear me? I'll dig through to you, I promise! Nothing's going to keep us apart!"
  say "Oh, sorry. Wanda's my girlfriend. She's just on the other side of these rocks, but they're too hard to move."
  say "If only someone had a Pokémon strong enough to smash them..."
  end


script Wanda
  say "Is somebody there? My cousin Wally is coming to stay with us in Verdanturf. I hope this tunnel is open by then!"
  end
