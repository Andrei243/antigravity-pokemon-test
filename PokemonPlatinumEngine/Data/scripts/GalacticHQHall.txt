# Team Galactic's headquarters, the hall (plan 02 · S11, part b), rebuilt to the original's plan. docs/scripts.md
# describes the language; the lines are our own, on the original's beats (scripts_galactic_hq_hall.s).
#
# The hall where Cyrus speaks to Team Galactic, reached by two warp panels of the second floor. Its grunts stand in
# ranks across the middle of it, so the panel in the west wall leads nowhere while they are there; the one in the east
# brings the player down a passage to where Looker is watching from the back (the original's trigger, a column of two
# tiles; VAR_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_STATE). He waves the player over, Cyrus comes onto the dais by the panel from his own
# floor, speaks of the unfinished world he hates and the new one Team Galactic now has the power to make, and goes as
# he came (the player watches from beside Looker, where the original stands them a tile south of him: under the text
# box, in this game's picture). The grunts file out to their posts (FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_GRUNTS), and Looker, who makes the matter
# the International Police's, goes off by the west panel (FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_LOOKER). The original's
# RemoveObject sets those flags and Cyrus's (FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_CYRUS) as it takes each away.
#
# The original moves its camera up the hall behind a fade and plays a fanfare of the crowd at each part of the
# speech; here the camera goes to the dais and the hall shakes with the roar (the story's effects: plan 09 · L11).


# Arriving: Looker is back where he watches from until the speech has been heard.
script OnEnter
  if var VAR_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_STATE == 0 place looker 19 12 left
  end


# Down the passage to the back of the hall (the original's coordinate event).
script Speech
  scene "The speech"
  face looker right
  emote looker exclaim
  speaker "Looker"
  say "Psst! Here! Over here, my friend!"
  say "All of Team Galactic, gathered in a single hall... What can it mean?"
  face looker left
  # The player comes to stand beside him (the original puts them a tile south of him, which here is under the text)
  if var PLAYER_Y == 13 walk player up 1
  face player left
  camera pan 13 8 0.8
  wait 0.6
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_CYRUS
  place cyrus 17 3 down
  show cyrus
  sound "warp"
  walk cyrus down 3 left 4 down 1
  wait 0.8
  speaker "Cyrus"
  say "Members of Team Galactic! Hear me! I stand before you today as Cyrus."
  say "This world of ours is a crude thing. In a word: unfinished. To live in it has always been a struggle, and it always will be."
  say "We humans are just as unfinished, and so are Pokémon. Because there is so much we lack, we fight, and we hurt one another... It is ugly."
  say "I hate it. That nothing is whole, that none of us is whole: I hate it with everything I am."
  say "The world ought to be whole. The world must change."
  camera shake 0.4
  wait 0.8
  say "Then who will change it? I will. I, Cyrus, and Team Galactic. Yes: every one of you."
  say "Together we dug through the myths and laid their secrets bare. Together we took the Pokémon of legend."
  say "And now Team Galactic holds the energy to change the world! The power of our dream is in our hands!"
  wait 0.8
  say "Understand me, all of you! The world I have dreamed of for so long is about to become real."
  say "Those of you who go to Mt. Coronet, and those who stay behind: our tasks may differ, but our hearts beat as one."
  say "Glory to Team Galactic!"
  camera shake 0.6
  wait 0.4
  speaker "Galactic Grunts"
  say "RAAAAAH!!"
  speaker "Galactic Grunt"
  say "Master Cyrus! There's nobody like him!"
  wait 0.4
  walk cyrus up 1 right 4 up 3
  sound "warp"
  hide cyrus
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_CYRUS
  fade out
  camera release 0.1
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_GRUNTS
  face looker right
  face player left
  wait 0.3
  fade in
  wait 0.4
  speaker "Looker"
  say "...Phew. So that was Cyrus, the boss of Team Galactic. Quite a performance, no? And the man is only twenty-seven!"
  say "But a new world, made for Team Galactic and for nobody else? It is absurd. My head, it spins."
  say "This \"new world\": what is it? This \"unfinished\" world: what is that?"
  say "Very well. To look into such things is the work of the International Police. And I am of the International Police, in good standing. So the work is mine."
  say "Here we part, my friend. Take every care!"
  walk looker left 1
  face player left
  face looker up
  wait 0.3
  face looker down
  wait 0.2
  face looker left
  wait 0.3
  walk looker left 17 fast
  sound "warp"
  hide looker
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_LOOKER
  setvar VAR_GALACTIC_HQ_HALL_STATE 1
  endscene
  end
