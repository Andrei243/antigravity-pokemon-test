# The corridor of Slateport's Battle Tent (plan 21 · H3's last part): the attendant walks a challenger to the battle room's
# door, on the beats of Emerald's SlateportCity_BattleTentCorridor, where the six rentals are chosen first. A
# game closed in the middle of a round finds none here (the round is the host's Venue, not saved), and nothing happens.


script OnEnter
  venue active
  if result == 0 end
  speaker "Attendant"
  say "Here are six rental Pokémon. Please choose the three you'll battle with."
  venue rent
  if result == 1 goto Walk
  say "No rentals? Then the challenge is over. Please come again."
  venue end
  fade out 0.6
  warp "SlateportCity_BattleTentLobby" 6 6 up
  fade in
  end
label Walk
  say "Right this way, please."
  walk slateport_tent_corridor_attendant up 4
  walk player up 4
  fade out 0.6
  warp "SlateportCity_BattleTentBattleRoom" 4 8 up
  fade in
  end
