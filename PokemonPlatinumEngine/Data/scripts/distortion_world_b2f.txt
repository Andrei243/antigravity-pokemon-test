# The Distortion World, B2F (plan 02 · S12). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on the
# original's beats (scripts_distortion_world_b2f.s). distortion_world_1f.txt says what VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS
# counts.
#
# Cynthia stands by the wall that leads up to the upper stones (on it, in the original) from the moment Mesprit has
# been seen on B1F (progress 4). Spoken to, she proposes that the two search apart (progress 5), and after that only
# says the two need not go together. She has gone on once Cyrus has been met on B3F (progress 6).


script OnEnter
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS < 4 hide cynthia
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS > 5 hide cynthia
  end


# The original's DistortionWorldB2F_OnFrame_FirstEntry (her object's script) and DistortionWorldB2F_Cynthia.
script Cynthia
  face cynthia player
  speaker "Cynthia"
  if var VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS >= 5 goto Apart
  say "The stones here don't lie in one plane. Some hang over the others, and a wall is as good a floor as any."
  say "We'll cover more of it apart. You take whichever way opens to you, and I'll take another. Call out if you find Cyrus, or Giratina."
  setvar VAR_DISTORTION_WORLD_PROGRESS 5
  face cynthia left
  end
label Apart
  say "We needn't keep together. This place lets each of us through by a road of our own. I'll find you further down."
  end
