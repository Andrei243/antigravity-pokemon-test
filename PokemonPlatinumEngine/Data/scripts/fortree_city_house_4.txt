# A house of Fortree City (plan 21 · H6, part c). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are our own, on
# Emerald's beats (data/maps/FortreeCity_House4/scripts.inc).
#
# The boy sends his Wingull off on an errand the first time he is spoken to (FLAG_WINGULL_SENT_ON_ERRAND): it runs out
# of the door and is gone (FLAG_HIDE_FORTREE_CITY_HOUSE_4_WINGULL), and the Wingull of Mossdeep City's second house,
# which Emerald shows at that moment (FLAG_HIDE_MOSSDEEP_CITY_HOUSE_2_WINGULL), is the other half of the errand. Once
# the player has seen it deliver its letter there (FLAG_WINGULL_DELIVERED_MAIL, plan 21 · H8, mossdeep_city_house_2.txt)
# it is home again, and the boy gives a Mental Herb for the player's trouble, once (FLAG_RECEIVED_MENTAL_HERB). Until
# then he says the bird is still away, as Emerald's does.


script Boy
  face self player
  speaker "Boy"
  if flag FLAG_RECEIVED_MENTAL_HERB goto Friends
  if flag FLAG_WINGULL_DELIVERED_MAIL goto Returned
  if flag FLAG_WINGULL_SENT_ON_ERRAND goto Away
  say "There! Off you go, bird Pokémon!"
  setflag FLAG_WINGULL_SENT_ON_ERRAND
  clearflag FLAG_HIDE_MOSSDEEP_CITY_HOUSE_2_WINGULL
  walk fortree_house_wingull down 2 right 1 fast
  hide fortree_house_wingull
  setflag FLAG_HIDE_FORTREE_CITY_HOUSE_4_WINGULL
  end
label Away
  say "Heh-heh, I've asked my Wingull to run an errand for me."
  end
label Returned
  say "My Wingull's back, and it says you saw it deliver my letter! Thanks for keeping an eye on it. Here!"
  give "Mental Herb"
  setflag FLAG_RECEIVED_MENTAL_HERB
  end
label Friends
  say "Thanks to my Wingull, my friend far away feels close by. Letters are great!"
  end


# The Wingull waiting for its errand
script Wingull
  face self player
  cry "Wingull"
  speaker "Wingull"
  say "Wingull: Pihyoh!"
  end
