# Phoebe's room, the second of Hoenn's Elite Four (plan 21 · H9). docs/scripts.md describes the language; the lines are
# our own, on Emerald's beats (data/maps/EverGrandeCity_PhoebesRoom/scripts.inc). As Sidney's room: spoken to, she
# battles; beaten, the door behind her opens onto the hall to Glacia's room (FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_PHOEBE).


script Phoebe
  face phoebe player
  speaker "Phoebe"
  if flag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_PHOEBE goto Beaten
  scene "Phoebe"
  music "common/eye_elite_four"
  say "Hi there! I'm Phoebe of the Elite Four. I trained at the top of Mt. Pyre, among the spirits who rest there."
  say "Up there, I learned to share my heart with Ghost-type Pokémon. We understand each other completely."
  say "Let's see if your bond with your Pokémon can match ours!"
  battle self as "hoenn_phoebe"
  speaker "Phoebe"
  say "Oh... we lost. Your bond with your Pokémon must run very deep."
  setflag FLAG_DEFEATED_ELITE_4_PHOEBE
  sound "door_open"
  say "Go on to the next room. I'll be cheering for you, quietly, the way the spirits do."
  endscene
  end
label Beaten
  say "The way on is open. Keep that bond strong, and you'll go far."
  end
