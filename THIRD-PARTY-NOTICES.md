## Pokémon Showdown

<https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown>. Read for numbers and names only: each move's power as a Z-Move and as a Max Move, what Z-Power adds to a status move, the Z-Moves, Max Moves and G-Max Moves, which species each Mega Stone and Z-Crystal belongs to, and the colour of each form. The rule that gives a move its default Z and Max power (`Importer.ZPowerOf`, `Importer.MaxPowerOf`) follows its `sim/dex-moves.ts`. Plan 06 names it as the reference for the rules of later generations; any logic or test ported from it in later sessions is covered by this notice too.

## PokeAPI

<https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi>. Its CSV files give the names, categories, generations and short effect texts of species, moves, abilities and items, and all the data of Generations 5 to 9. © Paul Hallett and PokéAPI contributors, used under the BSD 3-Clause licence: <https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/blob/master/LICENSE.md>.

## pret/pokeplatinum

<https://github.com/pret/pokeplatinum>. The decompilation of Pokémon Platinum is read for layouts and numbers only (stats, move and item values, tile behaviours, positions, short names), never for art, sound or text; `tools/DataImporter/README.md` and `tools/MapImporter/README.md` say exactly what.
